# Automation Bias and Legal Reasoning in Law Students: Experimental Dataset on Generative AI Errors

**著者**: Miluska Rodriguez-Saavedra
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

This dataset contains experimental data collected from 360 law students to examine automation bias and legal reasoning when using generative artificial intelligence systems that may provide erroneous legal information.The study includes 2,880 student-case observations generated under controlled experimental conditions. Participants were assigned to different AI-assistance conditions designed to evaluate how exposure to accurate or erroneous AI-generated legal recommendations influences legal reasoning, error detection, reliance on AI, and decision-making.The dataset supports research on automation bias, generative AI errors, legal reasoning, trust in artificial intelligence, human-AI interaction, and legal education.The files include participant-level and case-level information required for reproducible analysis of the experimental design. The dataset does not contain personally identifying information.Participants: 360 law students Observations: 2,880 student-case observations Research area: Artificial intelligence and legal education Study design: Controlled experimental study
