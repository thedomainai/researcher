# DIGITALIZING THE SPOKEN WORD : AI AND BIG DATA FRAMEWORKS FOR PRESERVING ORAL TRADITIONS IN RURAL DEVELOPMENT

**著者**: Maman Toukour Lawali, Esther Tontoh
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

Oral traditions constitute a foundational layer of cultural identity, indigenous knowledge, and community governance in rural societies worldwide, yet they remain acutely vulnerable to erosion through generational discontinuity, migration, and the displacement effects of globalization. This study presents an integrated framework combining artificial intelligence and big data technologies to systematically capture, classify, and preserve oral heritage as a driver of sustainable rural development. Drawing on a qualitative method, the research employs automatic speech recognition (ASR), natural language processing (NLP), and deep learning algorithms to transcribe, translate, and semantically annotate spoken narratives,folk knowledge, and traditional ecological practices in low-resource languages. A cloud-based big data architecture was designed to manage heterogeneous multimedia corpora at scale, ensuring interoperability with global digital heritage repositories.
