# Human–Generative Ai Coordination And The Dynamics Of Artistic Style Evolution

**著者**: Sepide Sadeghi
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

Generative artificial intelligence is changing the way visual art and illustration are produced.Prior research has focused primarily on individual-level human-AI collaboration, offering limited insight into how collective stylistic patterns emerge when many creators interact through shared generative tools and visible outputs.To address this gap, we develop an agent-based model that captures how creators balance exploration and AI-assisted exploitation while responding to visible styles.Our preliminary results suggest that the impact of generative AI on stylistic diversity depends critically on the level of human exploration: AI-driven convergence emerges primarily when exploration is limited, whereas higher levels of exploration sustain diversity over time.By shifting attention from individual outcomes to ecosystem-level dynamics, this study provides new insight into how widespread adoption of generative AI may reshape the diversity and evolution of artistic styles in digital environments.
