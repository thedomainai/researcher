# The Impact of Metaverse and Virtual Reality (VR) on Gamified Physical Education Learners' Outcomes

**著者**: Sohom Saha, Patcharavadee Sriboonruang, Pawan Deep, Himanshi Verma, SJ Singh
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

The Metaverse and Virtual Reality (VR) are reshaping physical education (PE) by providing immersive and interactive learning environments that enhance student engagement and motivation. This chapter presents a narrative review and content analysis of peer-reviewed literature to examine the impact of the metaverse, VR, and artificial intelligence (AI) on PE outcomes. The findings indicate that these technologies support motor skill development, simulate realistic learning experiences, and deliver real-time feedback despite physical or equipment limitations. Guided by Self-Determination Theory, Flow Theory, and the Cognitive Affective Model of Immersive Learning (CAMIL), the chapter highlights improvements in coordination, movement awareness, confidence, participation, and knowledge retention. It also addresses challenges such as cybersickness, cost, accessibility, and reduced social interaction, and proposes a hybrid instructional model that combines immersive technologies with traditional practical PE to support sustainable learning.
