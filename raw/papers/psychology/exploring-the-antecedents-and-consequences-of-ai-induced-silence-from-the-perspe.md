# Exploring the Antecedents and Consequences of AI-Induced Silence from the Perspective of Social Influence Theory

**著者**: Guan-Hong Liu, Wenfeng Su, Gary  Yu-Ho Yeh, Jack Hsu, Chao‐Min Chiu
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

This study examines how generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) influences communication behaviors in team decision-making contexts. Drawing on Social Influence Theory, we conceptualize AI-induced silence as reduced expressive engagement when GenAI-generated outputs enter team discussions. We propose that evaluation apprehension, silence climate, and cognitive congruence increase AI-induced silence, while GenAI domestication strengthens these effects. Using survey data from professionals who have participated in GenAI-supported team discussions, this research investigates the psychological mechanisms and consequences of AI-induced silence, including reduced knowledge sharing willingness and increased task apathy.
