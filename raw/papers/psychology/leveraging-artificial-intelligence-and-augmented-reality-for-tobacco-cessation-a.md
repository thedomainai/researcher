# Leveraging artificial intelligence and augmented reality for tobacco cessation: A review

**著者**: Sumeet Agarwal
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

Smoking is one of the most common problems worldwide with serious implications for health, both on an oral and systemic level. Traditional approaches towards quitting smoking are not always successful because of lack of personalization and proper adherence of patients and lack of follow-up. Thanks to the development of AI and AR technologies, it is possible to implement personalized smoking cessation programs that include adaptive conversational agents, digital therapeutics, predicting smoking behavior, wearable sensors and timely just-in-time support. The implementation of AR technologies allows enhancing the perception of risks, implementing cue exposure therapy and educating the patient thanks to the immersive visualization of the consequences of smoking. Thus, challenges such as varying designs of studies, lack of long-term evidence and ethical issues should be solved before the integration of such approaches in dentistry.
