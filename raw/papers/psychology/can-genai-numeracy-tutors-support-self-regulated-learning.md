# Can GenAI numeracy tutors support self-regulated learning?

**著者**: Lorenzo Gómez Camacho, Christopher Bridge, Birgit Loch
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

Generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) has been the focus of much attention in the higher education sector, both for its potential to support personalised and adaptive learning, but also due to the risk that students may outsource learning to it, leading to gaps in their professional or discipline competencies. This risk of cognitive offloading applies equally to learning how to learn, a skill set explored through studies of self-regulated learning (SRL). Two GenAI numeracy tutors were piloted at a regional Australian university as one component of a newly launched mathematics and statistics support centre, one focusing on medication calculations for Nursing students (NiNA), and the other on threshold numeracy skills for Education students (EdNA). Detailed analysis of students’ conversations with these agents using Zimmerman and Moylan’s 2009 SRL framework revealed extensive evidence of some elements of SRL such as strategic planning, task-specific strategies, and adaptive competence; trace evidence of other elements including self-efficacy and self-evaluation; and no evidence of the remaining elements. This study contributes an underrepresented qualitative perspective to the body of research into the crossover of GenAI and SRL and foreshadows avenues for further research and refinements in the design of GenAI numeracy tutors.
