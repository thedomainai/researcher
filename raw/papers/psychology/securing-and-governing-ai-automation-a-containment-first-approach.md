# Securing and Governing AI Automation: A Containment-First Approach

**著者**: Prabhuram Balaraman
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

AI is moving from assisting people to automating work: AI agents now call internal systems, run scripts and take actions on their own. This white paper brings together AI automation, AI security and AI governance. It argues that organizations should shift from asking “Is this AI model safe?” to “What can this AI model reach?” Because no one can perfectly guarantee an AI model’s behavior, it recommends containing agentic AI through sandboxing, least-privilege access and a Zero Trust mindset that treats model output as untrusted data. The paper reviews common threats to AI models (espionage, sabotage and fraud) and sets out a governance approach covering accountability, AI inventory, approval gates for high-impact actions, testing before and after change, audit trails and incident response. It explains the relevance to U.S. national interests, including critical infrastructure and its alignment with the NIST AI Risk Management Framework and America’s AI Action Plan, and closes with practical best practices. It is intended for quality engineers, software engineers, security practitioners and program managers working with AI-enabled applications.
