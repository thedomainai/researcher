# Engaged Or Anxious? The Dual Outcomes Of Job Crafting Using Generative Ai

**著者**: Akanksha Malik Jamwal, Swati Garg
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

This research-in-progress paper examines how employees’ job crafting using generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) shapes their engagement with GenAI and their GenAI-related anxiety. For this study, we conducted qualitative interviews with employees who actively use GenAI in their daily work, generating insights on how they experiment with, adapt to and integrate GenAI in their work. Building on these findings, we developed a conceptual model which would be tested using quantitative methods. Drawing upon social cognitive theory and self-determination theory, this model aims to test how job crafting using GenAI influences the outcomes of GenAI engagement and GenAI-related anxiety through psychological mechanisms of self-efficacy and meaningfulness of work. This study will contribute to the emerging scholarship on GenAI-enabled work by extending the work on job crafting using GenAI and offering practical guidance on fostering positive employee experiences in AI- integrated workplaces.
