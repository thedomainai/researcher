## Abstract

The following technologies are changing STEM education, Artificial Intelligence (AI), teamwork, and problem based learning (PBL) transform STEM education by providing interactive learning environments that are student centered. PBL assists learners to solve real life issues using teamwork, critical thinking and interdisciplinary learning. Personalized guidance, real time feedback, data analysis, simulations, and adaptive learning paths enhance this process with the help of AI. It enhances problem solving, creativity and independent learning besides equipping students in future careers in an AI driven world. The most important issues are equal access to technological assets, teacher training, data privacy, and algorithmic bias. Responsible and inclusive use of AI is essential for effective STEM learning.
