# School leaders' artificial intelligence self-efficacy: implications for workload and well-being

**著者**: Mustafa Özgenel, Mehmet Şükrü Bellibaş, Mahmut Polatcan
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

Purpose As school leaders face increasingly complex professional demands, the question of how artificial intelligence self-efficacy (AI-SE) is related to their workload and well-being has become increasingly important. This study aims to examine the relationships among AI-SE, workload, and well-being and to reveal the moderating role of AI-SE in the relationship between workload and well-being. Design/methodology/approach Data were collected from 415 school leaders working in Türkiye. We employed structural equation modeling to examine the relationships among AI-SE, perceived workload, and well-being, as well as the moderating role of AI-SE in the relationship between workload and well-being. Findings AI-SE is significantly associated with lower perceived workload and higher well-being among school leaders. Perceived workload is negatively associated with well-being. The negative association between workload and well-being is weaker among leaders with higher AI-SE. Originality/value This study extends the emerging literature on AI in educational leadership by showing that AI may function more as a resource than a demand, as AI-SE is associated with lower workload, higher well-being, and a weaker workload-well-being association.
