# The Paradox Of Optimal Experience: Navigating Flow-Induced Tensions In Software Development

**著者**: Saima Ritonummi, Valtteri Siitonen, Markus Salo, Henri Pirkkalainen
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

Motivated by the paradox of optimal experience, this research investigates how flow experiences in software developers' work can give rise to adverse outcomes and how developers navigate the resulting tensions.We use a qualitative approach to explore how flow episodes can become misaligned and, in doing so, generate tensions between individual engagement and either personal well-being or organisational expectations.The expected findings will illuminate trade-offs between immediate absorption and its longer-term consequences, clarifying how misaligned flow may inadvertently undermine developers' well-being or create friction with organisational demands.The study contributes to theory by advancing understanding of flow in IS research on software development and digital work practices, and offers practical implications for recognising and mitigating these tensions to support more sustainable engagement at work.
