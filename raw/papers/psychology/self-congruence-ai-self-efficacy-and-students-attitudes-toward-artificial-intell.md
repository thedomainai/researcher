# Self-congruence, AI self-efficacy and students’ attitudes toward artificial intelligence

**著者**: Piotr Mamcarz, Magdalena Kolańska
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence (AI) is now embedded in higher education, making it important to understand why some students embrace these systems and others do not. Integrating self-efficacy theory with self-congruence theory, we tested whether AI self-efficacy relates to attitudes toward AI through the perceived match between AI and the self, distinguishing congruence with the actual self (self-verification) from congruence with the ideal self (self-enhancement)
