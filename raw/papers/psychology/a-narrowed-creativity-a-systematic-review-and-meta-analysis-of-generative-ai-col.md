# A narrowed creativity? A systematic review and meta-analysis of generative AI collaboration and learning outcomes in art and design education

**著者**: Chungho Su, PY Su
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: meta_analysis | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

This systematic review and meta-analysis examines the effects of generative AI collaboration on learning outcomes among students enrolled in formal art, design and architecture programmes. Departing from existing syntheses, which analyse central tendency alone, the review quantifies effects at two levels: the mean, using Hedges' g, and the dispersion of outcomes, using the log variability ratio (lnVR) and the log coefficient of variation ratio (lnCVR). The review asks whether generative AI narrows the range of student creative output rather than, or in addition to, shifting its average, and which pedagogical design factors moderate that narrowing.
