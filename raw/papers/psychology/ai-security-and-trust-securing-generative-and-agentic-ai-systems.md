# AI Security and Trust: Securing Generative and Agentic AI Systems

**著者**: Kuladeep Sandra Koppuravuari Gurnadha Gupta
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

Artificial Intelligence has emerged as one of the most transformative technologies of the modern digital era, influencing industries, research, education, healthcare, finance, manufacturing, communication, and critical infrastructure. The rapid advancement of Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Generative Artificial Intelligence, Large Language Models (LLMs), and Agentic AI has introduced new opportunities for intelligent automation, autonomous decision-making, and data-driven innovation. However, these developments have also created complex security challenges involving data privacy, model integrity, adversarial manipulation, unauthorised access, misinformation, and the responsible use of autonomous systems. Establishing security, reliability, transparency, and trust has therefore become essential for the sustainable development and deployment of modern AI technologies. The book AI Security and Trust: Securing Generative and Agentic AI Systems is designed to provide a comprehensive understanding of the principles, technologies, vulnerabilities, and security mechanisms associated with contemporary artificial intelligence systems. It explores the relationship between artificial intelligence and cybersecurity, emphasising the importance of developing secure, reliable, privacy-preserving, and trustworthy intelligent applications. The book introduces fundamental concepts of AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Generative AI, Natural Language Processing, Transformer Architectures, and Foundation Models, while establishing the essential cybersecurity principles of confidentiality, integrity, availability, risk management, AI safety, and responsible governance. The first unit, Foundations of Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity and Trust, introduces the evolution, scope, and applications of artificial intelligence, together with the fundamentals of Machine Learning and Deep Learning. It examines Generative AI, Large Language Models, Natural Language Processing, Transformers, and Foundation Models. The unit also explains cybersecurity principles, information security requirements, AI safety, reliability, trustworthiness, fairness, transparency, accountability, and human oversight. The second unit, Generative AI Security and Large Language Model Vulnerabilities, focuses on the security challenges associated with generative intelligent systems. It discusses the architecture and working principles of Generative AI, security risks in Large Language Models, direct and indirect prompt injection attacks, jailbreaking techniques, and safety guardrail bypass mechanisms. It further examines data poisoning, training data manipulation, model extraction, model theft, intellectual property risks, sensitive information disclosure, privacy leakage, hallucinations, misinformation, and reliability challenges. The third unit, Agentic AI Security and Autonomous System Protection, explores the emerging field of autonomous intelligent agents capable of reasoning, planning, interacting with external tools, maintaining memory, and executing tasks. It examines agent architectures, decision-making processes, tool utilisation, autonomous execution risks, function-calling vulnerabilities, agent identity, authentication, and authorisation. The unit also addresses memory poisoning, context manipulation, persistent threats, multi-agent system security, inter-agent trust, human-in-the-loop control, and safe execution mechanisms. The fourth unit, Adaptive Defense and Intelligent Infrastructure Security, presents advanced approaches to strengthening cybersecurity through adaptive and intelligent technologies. It covers self-learning cyber defence architectures, reinforcement learning for autonomous cyber defence, security orchestration, automation, and automated incident response. Additional topics include cyber deception, honeypots, honeynets, moving target defence, Zero Trust Architecture, AI-driven identity security, cloud-native security, container and Kubernetes protection, and security challenges associated with IoT, edge computing, 5G/6G networks, cyber-physical systems, smart grids, industrial control systems, smart cities, and critical infrastructure. The fifth unit, Secure AI Engineering, Privacy and Trustworthy AI Architecture, focuses on the development and governance of secure artificial intelligence applications. It discusses the secure AI system development lifecycle, privacy-preserving Machine Learning, data protection, federated learning, secure collaborative AI, differential privacy, encryption, and confidential computing. It further examines secure Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures, knowledge base protection, AI guardrails, content filtering, policy enforcement, Explainable AI, model interpretability, transparency, monitoring, logging, incident response, and continuous AI governance. This book is intended for undergraduate and postgraduate students, research scholars, faculty members, cybersecurity professionals, AI developers, system architects, and technology practitioners seeking to understand the security and trust requirements of modern intelligent systems. The structured presentation of concepts across five units provides a foundation for academic learning, research exploration, professional development, and practical understanding of emerging AI security challenges. The overall objective of this book is to develop a comprehensive understanding of how artificial intelligence systems can be designed, implemented, evaluated, and governed securely. By connecting AI innovation with cybersecurity engineering, privacy protection, adaptive defence, and responsible governance, this book aims to encourage the development of intelligent systems that are not only powerful and efficient but also secure, transparent, accountable, and trustworthy. We sincerely hope that this book serves as a valuable academic and professional reference for readers and contributes to the advancement of research, education, and practical innovation in Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, Generative AI, and Agentic AI Security.
