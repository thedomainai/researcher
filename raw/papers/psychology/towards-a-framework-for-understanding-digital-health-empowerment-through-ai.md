# Towards A Framework For Understanding Digital Health Empowerment Through Ai

**著者**: Noushin Nazarian, Rm Lederman, Kathleen Gray, Tingru Cui
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

AI-enabled health tools proliferate, yet a fundamental question remains unresolved: do these systems genuinely empower consumers, or do they deliver the appearance of empowerment while bypassing the process through which genuine health decision capacity develops?Both Health Empowerment (HE) Theory and Self-Determination Theory (SDT) treat process and outcome as inseparable -that is, capacity is developed through its exercise.However, AI health tools appear to introduce a structural dissociation: the system performs the cognitive work of health decision-making, and the person experiences need satisfaction without having undergone the process that produces genuine capacity.We propose a framework that maps three psychological needs from STD onto three HE dimensionsparticipation, autonomy, and informed decision-making -identifying system-related factors that enable or inhibit each.A novel contribution is proposed; that deskilling and agency decay arise as consequences of this dissociation.Our goal is to theorise the dynamic, context-dependent nature of systems for health empowerment.
