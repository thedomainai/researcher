# Gamified Learning for Neurodivergent Students: Personalization vs. Over-Stimulation in ADHD and Autism Education

**著者**: Saima Akhtar
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

AbstractGamified learning has become a significant force in contemporary education, drawing on game-design elements to strengthen learner motivation, engagement, and academic outcomes. This approach holds particular promise for neurodivergent students — especially those with Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) and Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) — where conventional instructional methods frequently fall short in addressing challenges related to attention regulation, executive functioning, and emotional engagement. Drawing on Cognitive Load Theory, Self-Determination Theory, and Universal Design for Learning, this paper presents a conceptual review of gamified learning environments and their effectiveness for neurodivergent populations. The review examines how adaptive reward structures, interactive feedback mechanisms, and personalised learning pathways can foster autonomy and intrinsic motivation, while simultaneously identifying the risks of cognitive overload, sensory overstimulation, and anxiety when design principles fail to account for neurodivergent needs. The central argument advanced is that effective gamified learning must move beyond one-size-fits-all approaches, embedding personalisation through AI-driven adaptive systems, sensory-sensitive interface design, and flexible difficulty adjustment. The paper further considers emerging directions in immersive technologies, ethical AI governance, and inclusive design frameworks, advocating for gamified digital learning environments that are responsive, equitable, and cognitively sustainable for neurodivergent learners.Keywords: gamified learning, neurodivergent students, ADHD education, autism spectrum disorder, personalised learning, cognitive load, educational technology design
