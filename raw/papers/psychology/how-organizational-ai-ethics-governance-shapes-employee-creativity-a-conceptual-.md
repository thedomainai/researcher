# How Organizational AI Ethics Governance Shapes Employee Creativity: A Conceptual Analysis of Mechanisms and Boundary Conditions

**著者**: Bing Zhang, Linpei Song
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

As AI becomes embedded in organizational decision-making and knowledge production, employees gain new tools for accessing and generating information while also confronting concerns about algorithmic evaluation, data use, and accountability. Drawing on research on AI ethics, algorithmic management, and organizational creativity, this conceptual study defines organizational AI ethics governance as the rules, procedures, and allocation of responsibilities that guide the development, deployment, operation, and retirement of AI systems. We argue that such governance can shape employee creativity through several connected mechanisms. Fair and contestable procedures can strengthen procedural justice, while transparent system boundaries and meaningful human review can support calibrated trust. Tolerance for responsible experimentation, together with discretion over how AI is used, can protect psychological safety and job autonomy. Governance also determines whether human–AI collaboration broadens exploration or channels employees toward standardized outputs. These effects depend on task characteristics, the stakes of AI-assisted decisions, whether AI is used primarily for automation or augmentation, employees' AI literacy, and the consistency between formal rules and managerial conduct. This study suggests that organizations should translate ethical principles into risk-based review, purpose limitation in data use, effective contestability and appeal mechanisms, employee participation, and oversight across the AI system life cycle so that AI can serve as a resource for careful creative work rather than a substitute for judgment.
