# Antes do prompt: formulação de problemas e definição de objetivos no uso de inteligência artificial generativa por empreendedores

**著者**: Daniela Hayoshi
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

The diffusion of generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) tools among entrepreneurs has been accompanied by a practical emphasis on writing prompts. This theoretical essay argues that this emphasis diverts attention from an earlier and more consequential step: formulating the problem and defining the outcome one seeks to achieve. We distinguish two patterns of use. In task-first use, the entrepreneur asks the AI for an immediate deliverable (a text, a plan, an analysis) without making explicit the goal it serves. In outcome-first use, the entrepreneur first states the desired outcome, diagnoses the problem, defines success criteria and constraints, and only then delegates tasks to the AI. We integrate four bodies of literature: research on problem finding and problem formulation, goal-setting theory, research on entrepreneurial decision-making under uncertainty (effectuation, the scientific approach, and experimentation), and recent empirical evidence on productivity, human–AI collaboration, and overreliance on automation. Building on this integration, we propose a conceptual model of pre-prompt problem formulation, with mechanisms (goal fit, calibrated evaluation, and selective implementation) and boundary conditions (task structure, position relative to the AI capability frontier, degree of uncertainty, and the entrepreneur's baseline performance), and we derive eight propositions. We discuss implications for individual entrepreneurs, limitations of the argument, and a research agenda that includes a three-arm field experiment. The article presents no empirical data; its propositions are intended for testing.
