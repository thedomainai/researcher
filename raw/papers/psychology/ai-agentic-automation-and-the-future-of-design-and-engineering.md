# AI, Agentic Automation, and the Future of Design and Engineering

**著者**: Theodoros Galanos
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Agentic Automation represents a paradigm shift in design and engineering, leveraging advanced AI systems not to replace human expertise but to amplify it. This chapter explores how large language models and large reasoning models enable sophisticated AI agents that interpret complex tasks, make contextual decisions, and adapt workflows dynamically. Through agentic orchestration – the coordination of multiple specialised AI agents working synergistically – designers can tackle unprecedented complexity, performing multi-objective optimisation across diverse domains simultaneously. The chapter examines three critical layers: tools (simulation engines, databases, APIs), agents (specialised AI entities), and workflows (strategic coordination protocols). Effective implementation demands intuitive user experience design, transparent AI explanations, and careful attention to trust-building. This transformation fundamentally redefines expertise, shifting professionals from task executors to strategic orchestrators who guide AI systems ethically while focusing on creativity, judgment, and higher-order problem-solving. Success requires balancing technological enthusiasm with critical reflection, ensuring AI augments rather than diminishes human ingenuity.
