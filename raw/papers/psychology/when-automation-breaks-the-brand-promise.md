# When Automation Breaks the Brand Promise

**著者**: Brian Chama
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

This chapter examines how undisclosed AI authorship weakens audience trust and destabilizes the credibility of news organizations, using CNET's 2023 release of AI generated financial articles as a central case. It analyzes how readers react when content presented as expert journalism is revealed to be produced by automated systems, especially when errors and unoriginal phrasing emerge. Through qualitative review of newsroom statements, public responses, and industry commentary, the chapter shows how concealed automation intensifies concerns about editorial integrity, transparency, and accountability. It argues that reputational harm arises not from the use of AI itself but from the perception of deception, underscoring the need for clear disclosure, rigorous oversight, and ethical governance in hybrid human-machine news production.
