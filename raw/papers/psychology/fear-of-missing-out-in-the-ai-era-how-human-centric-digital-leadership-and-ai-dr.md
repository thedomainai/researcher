# Fear of missing out in the AI era: how human-centric digital leadership and AI-driven HRM shape employee digital performance

**著者**: Tran Duc Anh, Anh Thi Ngoc Giap, Dung Thuy Do, Duong Thuy Than, Thuy Thanh Pham
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

Purpose This study examines how AI-driven HRM (ADH) relates to Employee Digital Performance (EDP) through the mediating role of Fear of Missing Out (FoMO) and examines whether Human-Centric Digital Leadership (HCDL) moderates these direct and indirect relationships within Vietnam’s financial services sector. Design/methodology/approach A quantitative design was employed using survey data collected from a purposive sample of 368 full-time employees working in commercial banks, securities, insurance, and financial technology firms in Vietnam. The proposed research model was tested using partial least squares structural equation modeling (PLS-SEM) following established reliability, validity, and structural path assessment procedures. Findings Results reveal ADH is positively associated with EDP and negatively with FoMO, while FoMO relates negatively to EDP and partially mediates the ADH–EDP relationship. HCDL significantly moderates these relationships, making both the positive ADH–EDP and negative ADH–FoMO associations more pronounced at higher HCDL levels. Moderated mediation analysis confirms the conditional indirect relationship between ADH and EDP through FoMO is stronger under higher HCDL. Originality/value This study extends Self-Determination and Conservation of Resources theories by integrating FoMO as an anxiety-based psychological mediator and HCDL as a critical leadership boundary condition in AI-enabled workplaces. By combining organizational HR systems and leadership perspectives, the study provides a comprehensive explanation of digital performance in AI-enabled workplaces and offers empirical evidence from the underexplored context of Vietnam’s financial services sector.
