# Designing human-AI creative learning: A scoping review towards a human-centred framework for AI-assisted creativity in higher education

**著者**: Thomas Cochrane, Md Haseen Akhtar (24461553)
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) is rapidly transforming higher education by reshaping how learners generate ideas, solve problems, and engage in creative knowledge construction. While existing research has primarily examined the effectiveness of AI tools on learning outcomes, writing performance, and student engagement, comparatively little attention has been paid to understanding how educators intentionally design AI-assisted creative learning experiences. This paper presents a scoping review of 49 empirical and conceptual sources on AI-assisted creativity in higher education to identify recurring instructional design practices, human–AI interaction patterns, and pedagogical strategies. Rather than focusing on technological capabilities alone, the review synthesizes how creative learning activities are structured around collaboration between learners, educators, and AI systems, while also engaging directly with mixed evidence on AI’s creative value, including findings that AI-assisted ideation can expand idea novelty under some conditions, yet produce design fixation and narrowed divergent thinking under others. Building upon this synthesis, the paper proposes a preliminary Human-Centered AI Creativity Design (HACD) framework that positions AI as a collaborative creative partner while preserving learner agency, critical reflection, and ethical decision-making. The framework offers an initial conceptual model to guide educators in designing meaningful AI-supported creative learning experiences and provides a foundation for empirical validation.
