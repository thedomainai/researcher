# Combating Automation Complacency in Financial Due Diligence: A Behavioral Framework for AI-Augmented Investment Analysis

**著者**: Leigh Coney
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

As AI systems become increasingly integrated into financial due diligence workflows, a dangerous paradox has emerged: the more polished and confident AI outputs appear, the less likely experienced professionals are to scrutinize them. This phenomenon, Verification Atrophy, represents one of the most significant yet underappreciated risks in AI-augmented decision-making. Unlike technical failures that produce obviously erroneous outputs, verification atrophy operates invisibly. Senior professionals, trusting AI-generated analyses that appear thorough and well-structured, gradually reduce their independent verification efforts. This paper introduces a behavioral science framework for understanding and combating automation complacency in financial due diligence contexts. Dr. Leigh Coney is the founder of WorkWise Solutions (www.workwisesolutions.org), building AI systems for investment firms.
