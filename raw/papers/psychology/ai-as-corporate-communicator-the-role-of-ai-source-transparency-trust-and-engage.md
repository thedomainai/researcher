# AI as corporate communicator: the role of AI source transparency, trust, and engagement in brand advocacy

**著者**: Evi Chatzopoulou, Athanasios Poulis, Zoi Anagnostidou
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

Purpose This study investigates the role of artificial intelligence (AI) as a corporate communicator and examines how AI source transparency influences message credibility, employee trust, employee engagement and ultimately brand advocacy. Specifically, the study explores both cognition-based and affect-based dimensions of trust and evaluates the moderating role of AI literacy in shaping employees’ responses to AI-generated communication. Design/methodology/approach A quantitative research design was adopted using an online survey questionnaire. Data were collected from 317 employees across different organizations who had experience with AI-mediated communication in their workplace. Structural equation modeling (PLS-SEM) with SmartPLS 4.0 was applied to test the proposed relationships and evaluate the conceptual framework. Findings The findings indicate that AI source transparency positively affects message credibility. Message credibility significantly enhances both cognition-based and affect-based trust, which subsequently increase employee engagement. Employee engagement was also found to positively influence brand advocacy. However, AI literacy showed an unexpected negative moderating effect on the relationship between message credibility and cognition-based trust, suggesting that employees with greater AI knowledge become more critical and skeptical of AI-generated content, whereas its moderating effect on the relationship between message credibility and affect-based trust is not statistically significant. Practical implications Organizations should not use AI solely as an automation tool but also as a strategic communication instrument. Transparency regarding AI usage, combined with human oversight and quality assurance mechanisms, can strengthen credibility and trust. Companies should also invest in content governance and monitoring practices to ensure that AI-generated communication remains accurate, relevant, and aligned with organizational values. Originality/value This study contributes to the growing literature on AI-mediated corporate communication by developing and empirically testing an integrated model linking AI transparency, message credibility, trust, engagement and brand advocacy. It also provides novel insights into the complex role of AI literacy in influencing employee perceptions and trust toward AI-generated communication.
