# Quality Assurance Checklist for Human-AI-CPS Integration in Regulated Engineering Practice: Establishing 'Responsible Control' and the Centre of Focus (COF/0)

**著者**: Junbao Hao
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

This governance document presents the official Quality Assurance (QA) Checklist designed to establish and enforce "responsible control" in automated, agentic AI, and Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS) workflows within regulated, safety-critical civil engineering practice. Anchored in the triadic governance framework of the MIPR Institute, this checklist operationalizes the Centre of Focus (COF/0)—the unvarying coordinate origin where human professional responsibility, algorithmic assistance, and physical site telemetry reach static equilibrium. As AI language and parametric design models accelerate text and geometry production, they introduce severe cognitive risks, including automation bias, complacency, and the loss of a traceable design paper trail. These "black-box" deviations threaten the professional standard of care and non-delegable legal accountability carried solely by the human practitioner. To mitigate these risks, this checklist translates the Golden Equilibrium Model (GEM) and boundary-damping kinetics into five practical, interface-driven deployment phases: Pre-Prompting Analog Intent: Preserving sketching and traditional planning as cognitive constraints before computation. Boundary-Based Delegation: Restricting agentic AI permissions and establishing mandatory exception-routing thresholds to keep human supervisors in control. Interfacial Quality Controls: Instituting output classification (Draft vs. Proposal vs. Baseline Candidate), strict traceability links ("no source, no trace = no acceptance"), and the "Four-Eyes" verification principle. RACI Accountability Mapping: Assigning unambiguous acceptance authority under Singapore's regulatory engineering frameworks. Decision Logging and Auditing: Systematically archiving prompts, inputs, outputs, and sources to defend design authorship and professional originality. This checklist serves as an in-house operating playbook for Le Management (2011) Pte Ltd and acts as the practical companion to Paper 1 ("Human Decides, AI Assists, CPS Enables") of the 1st World LEM AI-Empowered Built Environment Convention (WABE 2026).
