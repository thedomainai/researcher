# Consumer Experience In Metaverse Environments

**著者**: Özlem Keloğlu, Mehmet Marangoz
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

The rapid advancement of digital and immersive technologies has transformed consumer experience, shifting marketing toward interactive and personalized service encounters. The metaverse represents a novel environment where consumers engage through avatar-mediated interaction and real-time participation. This chapter examines how consumer experience is formed in metaverse environments, with a focus on AI-driven hyper-personalization. It integrates experience economy and experiential marketing perspectives with immersive technology frameworks to explain key mechanisms such as presence, flow, and interactivity. The findings suggest that consumer experience is shaped by psychological, social, and technological factors, with artificial intelligence enabling adaptive, personalized, and engaging experiences in digital service contexts.
