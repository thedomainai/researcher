# Personal AI Systems Architecture: A Human Centered Framework for Integrated Work, Learning, Research, and Creativity

**著者**: Roman Shaban
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

The rapid development of generative artificial intelligence has created new opportunities for augmenting human intellectual activity. However, the adoption of AI tools frequently remains fragmented, consisting of disconnected conversations, isolated applications, temporary workflows, and poorly integrated information repositories. Such fragmentation limits continuity, knowledge reuse, transparency, and the systematic development of human–AI collaboration. This paper introduces Personal AI Systems Architecture (PAISA), a proposed conceptual and operational framework for designing integrated personal intelligence infrastructures that support professional work, education, scientific research, creative production, and everyday cognitive activities. Rather than treating artificial intelligence as a collection of independent tools, PAISA conceptualizes personal AI as a coordinated sociotechnical system combining human goals, generative models, structured knowledge, persistent memory, workflow orchestration, external applications, evaluation mechanisms, and governance procedures. The framework specifies seven interconnected architectural layers and introduces a human controlled operational cycle encompassing task formulation, contextualization, planning, execution, verification, knowledge integration, and reflective evaluation. Particular attention is given to cognitive autonomy, metacognitive regulation, information provenance, system interoperability, data protection, and the appropriate distribution of intellectual responsibilities between humans and AI. The paper also presents an implementation methodology, domain-specific application scenarios, and a multidimensional evaluation framework. It argues that the primary objective of personal AI infrastructure should not be maximum automation but the sustainable expansion of human intellectual capabilities while preserving independent judgment, competence, and accountability. PAISA is presented as a conceptual design proposal rather than an empirically validated architecture. Its principles and evaluation criteria provide a foundation for future prototype development and systematic investigation of personal human–AI systems
