# Artificial Intelligence in Uropathology: Current Applications and Clinical Implementations – A Narrative Review

**著者**: William Radu, Dinnur Mingaliev, Oleksandr Burov, Lilly Louise Hoffmann, Afschin Soleiman
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has gained increasing popularity as a tool for the histopathological assessment of urological cancers.The aim of this narrative review is to examine current applications of AI, its potential integration into clinical practice, and the challenges associated with its implementation.The publications used for this review were found on PubMed and included relevant English language publications published between 2020 and 2026, with earlier studies being used where necessary for context.This review describes the usage of deep learning and machine learning for various urological cancers such as prostate, renal, bladder and testicular cancers.Current evidence supports the fact that AI can be used as a supportive tool for selected diagnostic and prognostic tasks; however, clinical usage of AI remains challenging due to various factors including dataset shift, inconsistent validation, workflow requirements, regulatory issues, and the lack of prospective evidence demonstrating clinical benefit.Emerging technologies continue to offer further opportunities, with multimodal AI approaches and three-dimensional models, which may extend the prognostic and diagnostic capabilities.Although the use of AI is helpful and supports pathologists, further validation is still needed before a wider clinical adoption.
