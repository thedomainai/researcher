# The TRAIL Instrument: Development and Validation of a Diagnostic Measure of Educator Trust and Readiness for Artificial Intelligence in Learning

**著者**: Nikolas McGehee
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

Educators’ readiness to adopt generative artificial intelligence (AI) is increasingly important for K–12 systems, yet few validated instruments capture the beliefs that shape it. This study developed and provided initial validation evidence for the Trust and Readiness for AI in Learning (TRAIL) instrument, an adaptation of the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM), and tested predictions from a prior meta-analytic synthesis (McGehee, 2024) against primary data for the first time. Using a sequential explanatory mixed-methods design, we surveyed educators and leaders at a statewide virtual K–12 school and conducted follow-up interviews. Three constructs proved central to educator AI readiness. Perceived usefulness was the dominant determinant: it was the sole unique predictor of adoption intention and was strongly associated with active use, exceeding the synthesis’s predictions. Trust was empirically distinct from usefulness and made a modest but unique contribution to co-creative, thought-partner use of AI. Self-efficacy correlated substantially with every outcome but uniquely predicted the depth of use rather than whether use began. Most other synthesis predictions were confirmed or refined. Ethical concern was the most salient condition, subjective norm was unrelated to adoption, and cost, anxiety, and age operated indirectly or not at all. Facilitating conditions could not be tested as classically specified. The most-supported teachers adopted least, because pre-built curricula left few tasks suited to AI.
