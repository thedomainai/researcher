# Inclusive & Ethical Business Practices in the AI Era

**著者**: Shital Subhashchandra Nabariya
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has significantly transformed modern business practices by enabling automation, data-driven decision-making, and innovative digital solutions. Organizations across industries are rapidly adopting technologies such as machine learning, big data analytics, and generative AI to enhance operational efficiency and improve customer experiences. However, the widespread use of AI also rises important ethical concerns, including data privacy, algorithmic bias, transparency, accountability, and social inequality. As AI technologies become part of everyday business operations, companies must adopt inclusive and ethical practices to ensure responsible development and use of AI systems. This paper examines the importance of inclusive and ethical business practices in the AI era. The study reviews major AI ethics principles and global guidelines that organizations should consider while designing, implementing, and managing AI technologies. It also highlights the challenges businesses face in balancing innovation and profitability with ethical responsibilities. The research is based on a review of academic literature and policy documents related to AI ethics and business governance. The findings suggest that integrating ethical principles across the AI lifecycle and promoting inclusive decision-making can help organizations build trust, ensure responsible innovation, and contribute to sustainable business development.
