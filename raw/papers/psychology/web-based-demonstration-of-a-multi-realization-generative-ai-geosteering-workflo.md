# Web-Based Demonstration of a Multi-Realization Generative-AI Geosteering Workflow

**著者**: Sergey Alyaev, Kristian Fossum, and Hibat Errahmen Djecta
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 心理学

## Abstract

Abstract Recent advances in generative AI for geomodeling enable rapid creation of plausible geological realizations and show strong potential for real-time model updating. As these methods fundamentally alter workflows, they remain unvalidated for field use and are unfamiliar to operational experts. This work presents an interactive web-based prototype that bridges this gap by visualizing a complete AI-driven geosteering workflow combining generative geomodeling, uncertainty quantification, and optimization to provide decision support in a sandbox environment. The demo builds on a recent ensemble-based workflow with a multi-step AI-powered modeling sequence that combines generative geomodeling with machine-learning-based simulation of electromagnetic (EM) logs. A trained Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) produces multiple plausible geological realizations in a low net-to-gross geological setting. They are continuously updated using synthetic EM log data through ensemble data assimilation. Upon update an analytical dynamic programming (DP) module performs global optimization and recommends steering based on quantified uncertainty. The demonstration illustrates how generative AI can parameterize subsurface uncertainty and connect it to decision- making in geosteering. The interactive interface allows users to follow either system or expert recommendations. As drilling progresses, the user can see how new data affects model uncertainty and the subsequent decisions in real time. In our synthetic setup, we demonstrate how the system updates geological models in real time, identifies likely target pocket locations, and recommends optimal steering. The visualization component displays the subsurface uncertainty "curtain-map," providing decision explainability and showing how uncertainty evolves and how the AI adapts to new data. By interacting with the demo, users can see the system's reasoning process and test its sensitivity to data quality and target priorities. Although the workflow is not yet ready for field operations, it lays a foundation for human-AI interactions in geosteering, uncovering potential challenges of automation adoption. This work presents an open-source, web-based demonstration of a generative-AI- and data-assimilation-based geosteering workflow. Beyond algorithmic innovation, it addresses the human side of automation, letting experts interact with, understand, and build trust in AI-driven decision systems. The demo serves as both a modular workflow foundation and a discussion catalyst for future collaboration between AI researchers and geoscience professionals.
