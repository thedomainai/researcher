## Abstract

This chapter explores the concept of frontier leadership as a human-centric approach to managing hospitality organizations in the context of the integrationSpecial attention is paid to the Ukrainian hospitality sector, which operates under conditions of extreme uncertainty and constant disruption.The research results indicate that trust-based communication, flexible organizational structures, and employee-centered management significantly increase resilience to crises. Furthermore, the effective implementation of AI depends on its alignment with human values and leadership practices. The chapter contributes to the development of contemporary leadership models by positioning leadership at the edge of possibilities as a strategic response to a complex and volatile environment.
