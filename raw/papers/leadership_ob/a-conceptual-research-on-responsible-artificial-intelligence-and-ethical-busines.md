# A Conceptual Research on Responsible Artificial Intelligence and Ethical Business Practices

**著者**: Akanksha Sharma Mr. Shubham Sharma, Vaidehi Verma Nitish Kr. Maurya
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: リーダーシップ・組織行動

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence is becoming part of routine business judgment. Firms use it to identify customers, screen job applicants, anticipate demand, evaluate credit and prepare managerial reports. These applications promise speed and scale. They also create difficult questions about fairness, privacy, worker dignity, accountability and the public consequences of automated decisions. This conceptual paper examines how responsible artificial intelligence can be translated into ethical business practices rather than treated as a separate compliance exercise. Drawing on recent peer-reviewed research published primarily between 2024 and 2026, the discussion connects stakeholder responsibility, organizational governance and the practical management of AI systems. It identifies six business-facing ethical obligations and develops a proposed framework linking leadership commitment, risk-sensitive design, human oversight, stakeholder voice and continuing assurance to ethical outcomes. The paper argues that a responsible firm does more than publish AI principles. It determines where AI should be used, records who remains answerable for each decision and creates a workable route for challenge and correction. The framework is presented as a set of conceptual propositions for subsequent empirical testing. Implications are discussed for managers, auditors, policymakers and smaller firms with limited technical capacity. The central conclusion is that responsible AI is best understood as an ongoing organizational capability that protects human interests while enabling legitimate business innovation
