# Psychological Conditions, Transformational Leadership, and Change Oriented Work Engagement for Employee Adaptive Behavior during Public Sector Change

**著者**: Dinda Aulia Savira, Elok Savitri Pusparini
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: リーダーシップ・組織行動

## Abstract

This study examines the effects of change meaningfulness, psychological safety, change self-efficacy, and transformational leadership on proactive work behavior and championing behavior through the mediating role of change-oriented work engagement among employees in an Indonesian Government Secretariat Institution undergoing organizational transformation. Using a quantitative survey approach, 414 valid responses were analyzed with PLS-SEM through SmartPLS. The results show that transformational leadership is the strongest predictor of change-oriented work engagement, followed by change self-efficacy and psychological safety, while change meaningfulness has no significant direct effect. Furthermore, change-oriented work engagement significantly mediates the relationship between all predictors and both adaptive behaviors, particularly championing behavior, highlighting its important role in supporting organizational change in the Indonesian public sector.
