# An Auditable Aerobic Fermentor Simulator Tool for kₗa Determination and AI-Integrated Transport Phenomena Education

**著者**: José González‐Valdez
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: リーダーシップ・組織行動

## Abstract

First public release of the Aerobic Fermentor Simulator An interactive Gradio web application designed for teaching transport phenomena in bioprocess engineering. The simulator performs kₗa determination by the dynamic method and simulates full aerobic fermentation with coupled biomass, substrate, and dissolved oxygen dynamics. Beyond its computational use, the simulator is designed as an audit target: it embeds several undeclared assumptions that students must discover through code inspection with AI assistance and by contrasting the model against experimental datasets. This dual role (computational tool + object of epistemic scrutiny) supports an AI-integrated pedagogical framework developed in the context of the course "Análisis de Fenómenos de Transporte" (BT2026) at Tecnológico de Monterrey. This release includes: The Gradio application in English and Spanish (Jupyter notebooks ready to run on Google Colab). Eight synthetic experimental datasets (five team datasets and three quiz datasets), each designed with a specific physical perturbation that violates one of the simulator's implicit assumptions. MIT license. The pedagogical materials that reveal the simulator's assumptions (implementer manual, teacher annex, quiz answer key, rubric) are distributed privately through institutional channels and are intentionally not included in this repository.
