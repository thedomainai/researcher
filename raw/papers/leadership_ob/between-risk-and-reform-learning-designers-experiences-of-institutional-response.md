# Between risk and reform: Learning designers’ experiences of institutional responses to GenAI in assessment

**著者**: Nhung Nguyen, Michael Henderson, Keith Heggart, Homa Babai Shishavan
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: リーダーシップ・組織行動

## Abstract

Generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) has prompted universities to reconsider how assessment can remain meaningful, valid and fit for an AI-integrated future. While institutional responses to GenAI are often framed as technical, policy or academic integrity challenges, they are also deeply human organisational change challenges, shaped by competing logics of risk, reform, infrastructure and operational efficiency. Drawing on semi-structured interviews with 11 learning designers (LDs) from six Australian and New Zealand universities, the paper addresses the following research question: How do LDs experience and navigate institutional responses to GenAI in assessment in higher education? The analysis identified five interrelated themes: navigating strategic ambiguity and policy lag; mediating compliance-driven and integrity-focused responses; translating GenAI disruption into assessment reform; bridging AI infrastructure and pedagogical practice; and advocating for educational purpose within operational constraints. The paper contributes to current debates on GenAI and assessment by shifting attention from assessment tasks and technologies to the institutional conditions that shape assessment change. It argues that in addition to policy statements and enterprise AI platforms, sustainable responses to GenAI require coordinated professional development, curriculum governance processes enabling timely change, and stronger recognition of LDs as strategic partners whose pedagogical expertise, institutional knowledge and change leadership are central to assessment transformation.
