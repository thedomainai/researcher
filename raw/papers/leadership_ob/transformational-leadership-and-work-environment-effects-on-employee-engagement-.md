# Transformational Leadership and Work Environment Effects on Employee Engagement and Customer Service Quality

**著者**: Yuda Padriyadi, Andi Surya, Hasbullah, Yudhinanto Cahyo Nugroho, Megasari
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: リーダーシップ・組織行動

## Abstract

This study examines the relationships between transformational leadership, work environment, and employee engagement and customer service quality at PT Anak Tuha Abadi, including the mediating role of employee engagement. A quantitative cross-sectional study was conducted with 73 employees selected through saturated sampling. Eligible respondents were permanent employees with at least one year of service who were willing to participate; inactive employees and those submitting incomplete questionnaires were excluded. Data were collected using a five-point Likert-scale questionnaire and analyzed using multiple linear regression, path analysis, and the Sobel test. Transformational leadership (β = 0.303, p = 0.008) and work environment (β = 0.251, p = 0.028) were positively associated with employee engagement. Transformational leadership (β = 0.231, p = 0.044), work environment (β = 0.240, p = 0.034), and employee engagement (β = 0.232, p = 0.047) also showed significant positive direct associations with customer service quality. However, the indirect effects through employee engagement were not statistically significant for transformational leadership (0.070, Z = 1.629) or work environment (0.058, Z = 1.512). These findings distinguish significant direct relationships from unsupported mediation effects. Although the variables were positively associated, the cross-sectional design and self-reported data do not establish causality. The study provides context-specific evidence that employee engagement does not necessarily constitute a significant mediating pathway between organizational factors and customer service quality.
