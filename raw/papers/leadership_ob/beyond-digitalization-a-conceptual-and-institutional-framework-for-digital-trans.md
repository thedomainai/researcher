# Beyond Digitalization: A Conceptual and Institutional Framework for Digital Transformation in Higher Education

**著者**: H. Hamidi, F. Haji Seyed Javadi, Mahsa Zahedi
**年**: 2027 | **被引用数**: 1
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: リーダーシップ・組織行動

## Abstract

Digital transformation in higher education has emerged as a strategic priority for universities worldwide over the past decade. Despite the rapid growth of scholarly interest in this field, the existing literature remains conceptually fragmented, disproportionately focused on technological aspects, and limited in providing integrated frameworks to guide digital transformation processes. This study aims to systematically synthesize the digital transformation literature in higher education, identify dominant trends, key dimensions, challenges, and research gaps, and develop an integrated conceptual framework to address these limitations. Using a systematic literature review approach in accordance with the PRISMA guidelines, 145 peer-reviewed articles published between 2017 and 2026 were analyzed. Data were examined through systematic content analysis across multiple levels, including temporal and geographical trends, methodological characteristics, thematic classifications, and conceptual integration of findings. The results reveal a clear shift in the literature from technology-centered perspectives toward a multidimensional, institutional, and strategic understanding of digital transformation in higher education. Three interrelated dimensions, digital technologies and infrastructure, human resources and digital skills, and leadership, culture, and policy were identified as the core components of effective digital transformation. The findings further highlight persistent research gaps related to organizational readiness, institutional implications, change management, and the contextual localization of digital transformation frameworks, particularly in developing country contexts. In response to these gaps, this study proposes an integrated conceptual framework that conceptualizes digital transformation in higher education as a dynamic four-stage process encompassing readiness and assessment, pilot implementation, scaling and integration, and continuous optimization and innovation. The proposed framework offers both an analytical foundation for future research and a practical reference for university leaders and policymakers seeking to design and implement sustainable digital transformation initiatives.
