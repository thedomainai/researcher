## Abstract

This chapter discusses how human centred artificial intelligence and digital platforms are transforming the modern talent attraction practices. Since the use of AI in recruitment across organizations is on the rise, there is an increasing demand to bring a balance between the efficiency achieved through technology and human values like empathy, fairness and inclusiveness. The chapter focuses on the ways human centred AI can be used to improve candidate experience, data-driven decision-making processes, and decrease bias in the hiring process and stay ethical and responsible. It also examines how digital platforms, such as professional networking systems, AI-enhanced recruitment platforms, and employer branding platforms, are being used to establish dynamic talent ecosystems. The chapter emphasizes the significance of transparency, accountability, and human control. The chapter ends with the vision of the future and a changing role of HR leadership on the exploitation of AI and digital innovation in the attraction of diverse and meaningful talent.
