# The Effects of AI Readiness, HRM Practices, and Continuous Learning Culture on HR Analytics Capability in Public Service Organizations

**著者**: Basyirah, Susi Arijanti, Nunut Asniar, Muhammad Sabir, Ferra Eka Ramadhani

## Abstract

The study aims to investigate the effect of AI Readiness, HRM Practices and Continuous Learning Culture to HR Analytics Capability in public sector enterprises in Indonesia. The research was conducted based on a quantitative approach and the survey technique. The research participants consisted of the workers of public sector organizations who have expertise in the use of digital information systems and management of personnel data. The data were collected using an online questionnaire through Google Forms. The questions used a five-point Likert scale. The sampling technique used purposive sampling. Multiple linear regression was used to explore HR Analytics Capability in relation to the three independent variables. The proposed study model indicates that AI Readiness, HRM Practices and Continuous Learning Culture are positively related to HR Analytics Capability. AI Readiness is expected to have the highest relative impact. Artificial intelligence preparation may enhance the organizational capacity to integrate, analyze and use personnel data. Quality information is available with good human resource management practices. Moreover, a culture of continuous learning promotes the enhancement of employees’ analytical skills, teamwork and flexibility. The report provides practical implications for public service companies in terms of the value of strengthening AI infrastructure, data quality, digital competences, human resource management practices and promoting a culture of continuous learning for achieving HR Analytics Capability.
