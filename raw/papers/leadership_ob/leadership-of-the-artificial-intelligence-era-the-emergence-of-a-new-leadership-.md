# Leadership of the Artificial Intelligence Era: The Emergence of a New Leadership Style

**著者**: Elsy Sarkis-Garlough
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: リーダーシップ・組織行動

## Abstract

This chapter examines the intersection of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and leadership, exploring how technological transformation is reshaping leadership roles, skills, and paradigms across business, economic, and governmental contexts. It investigates the growing demand for agility, creativity, empathy, and ethical judgment among leaders who are navigating AI-driven disruption. Using a mixed-methods approach that integrates expert interviews, an organizational case study, and a cross-sectional survey, the study examines recurring AI-related challenges and the leadership responses they generate. The findings highlight challenges related to scaling AI solutions, managing unrealistic expectations, integrating legacy systems, solving complex problems, and translating innovative ideas into operational outcomes. They further indicate the emergence of a leadership style characterized by reflective judgment, continuous learning, cross functional collaboration, adaptability, and human accountability for AI supported decisions. Building on these findings, the study introduces a conceptual framework known as the “Hurotic” Leadership Style. This hybrid leadership model combines human judgment, emotional intelligence, and ethical reasoning with AI-enhanced systems referred to as Huros. The style emphasizes the synergy between human capabilities and intelligent machines, encouraging collaboration, adaptability, and responsible innovation. It also positions AI as a source of analytical and decision support rather than a substitute for leadership responsibility.The chapter contributes to leadership and digital transformation literature by proposing a conceptual framework for effective leadership in the AI era. The framework clarifies how conceptual, technical, and interpersonal skills can be integrated to guide human AI collaboration while preserving ethical evaluation, contextual interpretation, and decision accountability.
