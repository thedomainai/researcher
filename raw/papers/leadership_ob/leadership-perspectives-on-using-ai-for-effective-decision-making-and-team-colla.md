## Abstract

In the current scenario in the workplace, the incorporation of artificial intelligence (AI) has developed as an evolutionary force, reshaping the scenario of leadership and organisational dynamics. This research explores the effects of AI and its tools on the effectiveness of leadership, concentrating on how AI has enhanced decision making, communication, and employee engagement across various sectors in an organisational context. This chapter aims to explore the determinants of the application of AI and its impact on leadership in the workplace. The primary objective of this research is to explore the ways in which leaders across various industries utilise AI tools to enhance decision-making processes. The research design will funnel out the findings and provide a comprehensive understanding of the role of AI in leadership. The results of this study underscore the relevance of integrating AI tools into leadership frameworks to enhance organisational effectiveness through a culture of collaboration and engagement among teams.
