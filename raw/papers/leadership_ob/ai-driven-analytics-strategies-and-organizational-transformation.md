# AI-Driven Analytics Strategies and Organizational Transformation

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

This chapter presents an overview of the transition from traditional data analytics (DA) to AI-driven business analytics models, highlighting the strategic, operational, and ethical considerations for successful implementation. This chapter addresses (1) the increasing role of AI in modern business; (2) the shift from data analytics to AI through the emergence of AI technologies; (3) case studies of companies that successfully transitioned from DA to AI-driven business; and (4) business strategies for this transition, implementation guidelines, and challenges and solutions. Building a business analytics and AI-ready organization involves strategic alignment, robust data collection, assessment and governance, talent development, and a culture that embraces innovation and continuous learning and improvement. This chapter also presents key insights from the transition process and practical recommendations for businesses considering AI integration and the transition process. The effective implementation of these transitional strategic plans ensures businesses build a resilient, adaptable, and BA- and AI-ready organization to seize opportunities and sustain competitive advantage in an increasingly digital landscape.
