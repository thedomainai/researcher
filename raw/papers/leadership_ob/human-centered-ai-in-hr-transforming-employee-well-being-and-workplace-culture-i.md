# Human-Centered AI in HR Transforming Employee Well-Being and Workplace Culture in the Digital Era

**著者**: Preet Kanwal
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

AI is changing the employment relationships and is also offering efficiency, less bias, and personalized experiences. Nevertheless, these advantages require a human-focused design with the emphasis on the welfare of employees, their dignity, and inclusion. This chapter analyses the benefits of human-oriented AI in improving workplace culture and performance based on organizational psychology, human-computer interaction, HR technology, as well as AI ethics. It discusses its use throughout the employee life cycle and governs its responsible use. The major issues like prejudice, monitoring, privacy, and the imbalance are evaluated critically. Case studies emphasize the strategies, which provide a practical example that HR leaders can use to design and operate AI to address employee welfare and ethical cooperation.
