## Abstract

The incorporation of AI in universities is a significant transformational challenge for academic leaders. This chapter addresses the complexities of strategic leadership in the deployment of AI technologies. Moreover, the chapter investigates the development of adaptive organizational cultures that support strategic leadership; these cultures integrate the organizational frameworks necessary for the rapid deployment of technological innovations. The chapter starts with a description of the special context of the higher education sector, including multi-headed governance systems; a wide variety of stakeholder constituents; and long-standing traditions of academic and intellectual freedom and inquiry, which shape the sector's approach to the adoption of AI technologies. Educators, students and research, as the focus of higher education, the leadership should articulate a positive and exciting vision for the adoption of AI to enhance teaching and learning, research, and administration.
