# Responsible Artificial Intelligence Leadership as a Strategic Competence for Universities and Business Organizations

**著者**: Daniela Nikolaeva Ilieva
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: リーダーシップ・組織行動

## Abstract

The acceleration of generative and agentic artificial intelligence has shifted responsible digital transformation from technical adoption to leadership competence. This conceptual paper develops an integrative framework for responsible artificial intelligence leadership in universities and business organizations. Drawing on management theory, organizational learning, digital competence research, empirical evidence on generative artificial intelligence, and current governance instruments, the paper proposes a five-domain competence model and a five-stage maturity path. It argues that artificial intelligence literacy, governance discipline, communication, human oversight and learning culture are strategic capabilities for sustainable value creation, trust and regulatory readiness.
