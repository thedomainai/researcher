# Trust Recalibration in Human-AI Agile Teams: A Nested-Cycle Framework Linking Team Dynamics to Product Innovation

**著者**: Lalit Singh
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: リーダーシップ・組織行動

## Abstract

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is increasingly emerging as one of the most important tools for agile organizations, and Human and Artificial Intelligence is a new alliance for continuous innovation. Existing studies tend to focus on the individual constructs, such as trust, Human-AI teaming, team dynamics, and agile practices, without providing theoretical insights into the relationship between constructs. To address this gap, the present study proposes a Nested-Cycle Framework that, conceptually, links the Agile Team Environment and Human-AI Teaming, the Trust Recalibration Cycle, Team Dynamics and Innovation Outcomes in order to provide a comprehensive view of the theoretical framework. The framework establishes trust recalibration as a continuous process that is part of Human-AI teaming and Human-AI teaming is part of the larger Agile Team Environment that enables adaptive collaboration and innovation. Six theoretical propositions are formulated to develop the relationships among the constructs of the framework and to serve as a basis for future research. The proposed framework builds on the existing body of Human-AI teaming literature, offering a nested-cycle view that provides an understanding of how iterative trust recalibration, co-actions, and team dynamics all play a role in achieving innovation outcomes. The study provides a theoretical basis for further research and organizational approaches to the improvement of adaptive Human-AI collaboration in the agile context.
