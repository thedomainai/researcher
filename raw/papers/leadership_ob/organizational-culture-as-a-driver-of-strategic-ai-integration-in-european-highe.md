# Organizational Culture as a Driver of Strategic AI Integration in European Higher Education

**著者**: Oscar AGUADO-TEVAR, Luis Díaz-Marcos, María García de Blanes Sebastián
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: リーダーシップ・組織行動

## Abstract

The acceleration of artificial intelligence adoption in European universities between 2020 and 2025 has generated substantive transformations in teaching, research, and institutional management. However, technological uptake does not necessarily entail organizational strategic integration. This article develops a conceptual model from a strategic management perspective and introduces the construct Strategic Integration Level of Artificial Intelligence (NIE-IA) as the central explanatory variable. The model integrates institutional theory, organizational culture, digital leadership, technological investment, faculty resistance, and academic autonomy within a multilevel framework applicable to the European higher education system. Eight theoretical propositions are formulated to explain the conditions under which artificial intelligence achieves substantive strategic institutionalization. The work extends contemporary institutional theory to the digital university context and offers implications for academic governance and European public policy.
