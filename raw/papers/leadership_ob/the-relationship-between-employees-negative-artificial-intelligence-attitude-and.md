# The relationship between employees' negative artificial intelligence attitude and individual work performance: The moderating role of organizational support

**著者**: Yunus Emre Taşgit, Utku Can AYDIN, Yasemin Baykal Çetinkaya, Abdullah YAKUPOĞLU
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: リーダーシップ・組織行動

## Abstract

Purpose - The research aims to determine the moderating role of organizational support (supervisor and colleague support) in the relationship between employees' negative AI attitudes and individual work performance. Design/methodology/approach - Since the research aimed to determine the existence of co-variation among the variables, the relational screening model was preferred. Data were collected by questionnaire. The research model was tested on the data obtained from 573 participants. Findings - The effect of employees' negative AI attitude on the task performance is prevented by the colleague support rather than the supervisor support. If employees have a negative attitude towards AI, contextual performance is negatively affected, even if supervisor or colleague support is provided. However, supervisor support showed only a tentative buffering pattern in this relationship, as the interaction effect did not reach statistical significance at the conventional 5% level. Finally, both supervisor support and colleague support prevent negative attitudes of employees from turning into counterproductive work behavior, but supervisor support is more effective. Originality/value - Although there are comprehensive studies in the literature on each of the research variables, no study was found that examined employees' negative AI attitude, organizational support perceptions, and individual job performance variables together. In this context, the research is valuable because it provides findings that can fill the existing gap in the literature. Research Limitations/implications - The research could not be carried out on a homogeneous sample group. At the same time, the research reflects the perspective of a single culture (Turkish Culture). For more realistic findings, comparative studies with sample groups from different cultures are required. Practical implications - This research proposes specific solutions in the context of supervisor support and colleague support on how to prevent the negative reflection of technological developments on organizational atmosphere and individual work performance. Keywords: Negative AI Attitude; Individual Work Performance; Task Performance, Contextual Performance, Counterproductive Work Behavior; Organizational Support; Supervisor Support, Colleague Support.
