# Behavioral Biases in AI-Driven Pricing and Revenue Optimization

**著者**: Shyam Sunder Agrawal, Mohit Verma, Aishwarya Vashishta
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートファイナンス

## Abstract

The intersection of artificial intelligence and behavioral finance presents an underexplored but commercially significant terrain for reimagining how tourism firms design and implement their pricing strategies. While revenue management in travel and hospitality has long depended on demand-based algorithmic models a persistent gap between what these systems predict and what consumers actually do reveals a structural blind spot the systematic cognitive biases that shape purchase decisions in ways that rational utility models simply cannot capture. This chapter develops a behavioral-AI pricing framework by synthesizing prospect theory mental accounting reference price anchoring and social proof mechanisms within the architecture of modern machine learning revenue management systems. Drawing on secondary evidence from major online travel platforms and published booking behavior studies we demonstrate that tourism consumers exhibit consistent loss aversion effects when exposed to dynamic price fluctuations that reference price anchoring significantly modulates willingness to pay at different stages of the booking funnel and that AI pricing engines which ignore these behavioral dimensions routinely suppress conversion or trigger demand destruction. Our proposed framework integrates behavioral signals into AI feature engineering arguing that affective pricing cues cancellation-loss framing and social validation mechanisms can be systematically deployed to improve both conversion rates and revenue per available unit without exploiting consumers in ethically problematic ways. The chapter contributes to the growing literature on neuroeconomic applications in hospitality management while offering practical direction to revenue managers who seek to humanize not merely automate their pricing decisions.
