# Foreign Exchange (Forex) Market and Financial Technology

**著者**: Abdullah Mamoon
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートファイナンス

## Abstract

This chapter critically evaluates the structural transformation of the global foreign exchange market driven by Financial Technology (FinTech). It examines how digital trading platforms, algorithmic and artificial intelligence (AI)-driven execution systems, blockchain-based settlement mechanisms, and decentralised finance protocols are reshaping currency trading across spot, forward, futures, and options markets. By comparing traditional interbank-centred models with FinTech-enabled ecosystems, the chapter highlights improvements in execution speed, cost efficiency, pricing transparency, and market accessibility. It further explores institutional convergence, including application programming interface-driven treasury solutions and distributed ledger applications in cross-border settlement. While FinTech enhances liquidity management and operational resilience, it also introduces new systemic risks, including cybersecurity threats, smart contract vulnerabilities, leverage amplification, liquidity fragmentation, and regulatory arbitrage. The chapter concludes by assessing future trajectories in AI-powered personalised trading, interoperable infrastructures, and hybrid institutional–decentralised market structures, emphasising the need for adaptive regulatory frameworks to safeguard financial stability and market integrity.
