# Financial Technology and Profitability of Commercial Banks in Nigeria

**著者**: Biliqees Ayoola Abdulmumin
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートファイナンス

## Abstract

Financial technology has fundamentally transformed Nigeria’s banking sector, yet considerable uncertainty remains regarding its actual impact on commercial bank profitability. This Study analyze the impact of financial technology on the profitability of commercial banks in Nigeria from the p[eriod of 2012 to 2025. Focusing on ATM, POS, internet banking (INB), and mobile banking transaction (MB) values individually effect on EPS. The study employs Autoregressive Distributed Lag (ARDL) model. The finding reveal that ATM have a negative and statistically significant effect on EPS in both short and long run, MB, POS and bank size exert a positive and statistically significant effect on EPS in both short and long run however, INB exhibits a positive but statistically insignificant effect on EPS in a long run but statistical significance in short run The study concludes that fintech has a significant impact on the profitability of commercial banks in Nigeria. It was recommended that Commercial banks should strengthen the integration and coordination of their digital service channel in Nigeria. Keywords: Financial technology; bank profitability; commercial banks; digital banking channels; ARDL bounds testing; Nigeria
