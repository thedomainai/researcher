# An AI-Driven Behavioral Decision Support System for Adaptive Risk Management in Financial Trading

**著者**: Maria Abu Zollf, Sandra Gammoh, Sara Kharabsheh
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートファイナンス

## Abstract

Behavioral and psychological factors influence financial trading decisions and can increase risk exposure.Although most artificial intelligence (AI) trading applications focus on market prediction, they rarely incorporate trader behavior into risk assessment.This study aims to develop and evaluate an AI-driven behavioral decision-support system that identifies risky trading patterns and generates personalized risk-management recommendations.The novelty of the proposed system lies in integrating trading, financial, and psychological indicators within a unified behavior-aware framework rather than relying solely on market or technical data.A quantitative approach was applied to a behavioral trading dataset containing approximately 5,000 trader records.Following data preprocessing and feature engineering, Random Forest and XGBoost models were developed for trading-outcome prediction and behavioral-risk classification.A behavioral-risk scoring framework and assessment simulation were also developed to translate model outputs into interpretable recommendations.For trading-outcome prediction, Random Forest and XGBoost achieved accuracy of 55.5% and 57.5%, respectively.For behavioral-risk classification, the models achieved accuracies of 96.2% and 100%, respectively.Trading frequency, leverage use, sleep hours, emotional state, and revenge trading were identified as the most influential predictors of behavioral risk.The findings demonstrate the potential of integrating behavioral-finance concepts with machine-learning techniques to support early risk identification, strengthen risk awareness, and promote more disciplined trading decisions.
