# Money Market and Financial Technology

**著者**: Abdullah Mamoon
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートファイナンス

## Abstract

This chapter analyses the structural transformation of the global money market driven by Financial Technology (FinTech). It explores how artificial intelligence (AI), distributed ledger technology, tokenisation, and digital platforms reshape short-term funding instruments, liquidity management, and settlement architecture. The analysis contrasts traditional, institutionally dominated money markets with emerging digital ecosystems characterised by fractional access, near real-time settlement, programmable assets, and continuous trading. The chapter focuses on digital money market funds, tokenised Treasury instruments, AI-driven treasury optimisation, and the increasing role of stablecoins and central bank digital currencies in short-term finance. It also assesses systemic, regulatory, and operational risks associated with technological integration, including cyber vulnerabilities, legal uncertainties related to smart contracts, liquidity run dynamics, and cross-border regulatory fragmentation. The chapter concludes that although FinTech improves efficiency, transparency, and inclusion, the sustainable transformation of money markets necessitates coordinated regulatory adaptation, robust governance frameworks, and enhanced resilience mechanisms.
