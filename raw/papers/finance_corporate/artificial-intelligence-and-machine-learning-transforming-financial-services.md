# Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning: Transforming Financial Services

**著者**: Nagaraja R. C.
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートファイナンス

## Abstract

Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) have become big changes in the financial services industry, changing how things are done and making operations more efficient, accurate, and focused on customers. This study looks at how AI and ML are used in important areas of finance like credit scoring, fraud detection, algorithmic trading, risk management, and wealth management through robo-advisors. It also shows how using different types of data helps make lending decisions better and supports financial inclusion. The study uses a descriptive and analytical approach, based on information gathered from academic papers, reports, and other official documents. Comparing old ways with AI-based methods shows big improvements in how fast things are done, how accurate they are, how much they save in costs, and how well they can be tailored to individual needs. Looking at trends shows that AI is being used more and more quickly, with more investment and better results for productivity and financial performance. However, the study also points out some issues, like worries about keeping data private, unfairness in algorithms, problems with rules and regulations, and risks from cyber-attacks. The research also gives suggestions for successfully using AI and ML, stressing the need for good data management, fair practices, following the rules, and training a skilled workforce. The study concludes that AI and ML are not only improving how financial services work but also pushing for new ideas and helping companies stay competitive. Keeping up with these technologies is important for long-term growth and the future of the financial industry.
