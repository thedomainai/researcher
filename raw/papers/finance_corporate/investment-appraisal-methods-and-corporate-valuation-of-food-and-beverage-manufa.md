# Investment Appraisal Methods and Corporate Valuation of Food and Beverage Manufacturing Firms in Nigeria

**著者**: Joseph Adebayo Komolafe
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートファイナンス

## Abstract

This study examined the effects of investment appraisal methods on the assets valuation of quoted food and beverages manufacturing firms in Nigeria. Multiple regression models were formulated to examine the relationship between the dependent and the independent variables. Assets value were modeled as the function of accounting rate of return, internal rate of return, net present value, payback periods and profitability index. A pooled panel regression model was adopted to estimate the regression equation. After cross sectional validity of the models, the results validate the use of fixed effect model. The study found that investment appraisal methods explained 66.7% variations in assets valuation of the quoted food and beverage manufacturing firms. Net present value, pay back periods, and internal rate of return have positive effect on assets value while profitability index and accounting rate of return have negative effect on assets valuation of the quoted food and beverage manufacturing firms. From the findings, it concludes that investment appraisal methods have both positive and negative effect on assets valuation of the quoted food and beverage manufacturing firms in Nigeria. the study recommend that internal rate of return should well be applied in investment appraisal methods and Net present value approach should always be used in capital investment decisions to determine investment that add to valuation.
