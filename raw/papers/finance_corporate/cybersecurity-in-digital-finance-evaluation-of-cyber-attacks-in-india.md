# Cybersecurity in Digital Finance: Evaluation of Cyber-Attacks in India

**著者**: Bandita Gogoi, Chinmoyee Kalita
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートファイナンス

## Abstract

Digital finance is revolutionizing financial services access across India, from UPI payments to mobile/digital banking in semi-urban and rural areas, but it has also resulted in cyber-attack. There is a shift towards open Application Programming Interfaces (API), cloud services and AI-enabled decision-making systems that bring efficiency and inclusivity, yet expose financial institutions, businesses and individual users to data breaches, phishing, identity theft and algorithmic exploitation. This paper investigates cybersecurity challenges faced by banks, NBFCs and FinTech firms operating in Indian markets. Using an empirical approach, the study combines a review of regulatory guidance from RBI advisories and national data protection initiatives with important case studies of cyber-attacks in India. The paper highlights innovative practices that show promise. These include two-step authentication that balances usability with security for users, community-oriented cybersecurity awareness campaigns conducted via local language media and partnerships between banks and academic institutions to create cost-effective threat detection solutions. The paper concludes with policy suggestions and an operational checklist for financial institutions and other relevant sectors, aiming to promote safe and inclusive digital financial systems that can maintain trust and facilitate broader financial inclusion.
