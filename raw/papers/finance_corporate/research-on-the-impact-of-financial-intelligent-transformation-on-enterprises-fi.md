# Research on the impact of financial intelligent transformation on enterprises' financing constraints

**著者**: Shunmei Li
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートファイナンス

## Abstract

Against the backdrop of deep integration between the digital economy and artificial intelligence, enterprises' financial management models are undergoing profound changes. Financial intelligent transformation has opened up a new path for alleviating enterprises' financing constraints. This paper systematically analyzes the mechanism by which financial intelligent transformation mitigates financing constraints, and constructs a theoretical analysis framework from four dimensions: information mechanism, efficiency mechanism, risk control mechanism, and asset mechanism. The research finds that financial intelligent transformation effectively eases enterprises' financing constraints by improving information transparency, optimizing operational efficiency, strengthening risk management and control, and accumulating data assets. Combined with a case analysis of Midea Group, this paper proposes corresponding countermeasures, providing a reference for enterprises to solve financing problems in the process of digital transformation.
