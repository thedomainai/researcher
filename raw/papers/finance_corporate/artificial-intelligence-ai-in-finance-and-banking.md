# Artificial Intelligence (AI) in Finance and Banking

**著者**: Abdullah Mamoon
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートファイナンス

## Abstract

This chapter critically examines the transformative integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in contemporary finance and banking, with particular emphasis on machine learning, natural language processing, and robotic process automation as core enabling technologies. It analyses how these tools reshape high-impact financial functions, including algorithmic trading and predictive analytics, credit scoring, fraud detection, and regulatory compliance. The discussion highlights AI’s capacity to improve predictive accuracy, automate complex workflows, and enhance real-time risk management. Central attention is given to governance and accountability concerns, notably ethics, bias, and Explainable AI (XAI), to address the opacity of advanced models and regulatory demands for transparency. The chapter further explores the strategic evolution towards XAI frameworks, autonomous finance systems, and structured human-AI collaboration. It concludes that sustainable AI deployment requires integrating technical innovation with ethical safeguards, model interpretability, and regulatory alignment to ensure institutional trust, systemic resilience, and responsible financial transformation.
