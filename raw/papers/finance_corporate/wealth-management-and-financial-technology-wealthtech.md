# Wealth Management and Financial Technology (WealthTech)

**著者**: Abdullah Mamoon
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートファイナンス

## Abstract

This chapter explores the transformation of the wealth management industry by Wealth Technology. It first outlines traditional models of private banking, advisory firms, and family offices, including institutions such as UBS Group AG and Vanguard, which have historically emphasised personalised, relationship-driven advisory services. Building on this foundation, the narrative shifts to contrast these conventional models with digital platforms and robo-advisors that utilise automation, algorithmic portfolio construction, and streamlined digital onboarding processes. Subsequently, the chapter analyses how artificial intelligence, machine learning, big data analytics, and blockchain-based custody solutions are applied to transform core functions, including portfolio management, retirement planning, tax optimisation, and estate structuring. In addition, it highlights the emergence of hybrid advisory models that combine algorithmic efficiency with human judgement. The discussion then evaluates key opportunities, including cost reduction and expanded access to advice, alongside risks such as cybersecurity threats, regulatory complexity, and algorithmic bias. Finally, the chapter concludes by examining trends in hyper-personalisation, embedded finance, and Environmental, Social, and Governance investing within a broader governance framework.
