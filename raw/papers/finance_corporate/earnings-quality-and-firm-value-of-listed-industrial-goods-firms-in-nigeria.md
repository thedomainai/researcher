# Earnings Quality and Firm Value of Listed Industrial Goods Firms in Nigeria

**著者**: U. E. Eyo
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートファイナンス

## Abstract

In this study, we evaluated the influence of earnings quality on firm value of listed industrial goods firms in Nigeria from 2017 to 2022. Earnings quality is the ability of the company’s reported earnings to reflect the true earnings and to predict the future of the firm. Earnings quality was proxied by accruals quality, earnings persistence and earnings predictability and was measured using the modified Jones model. Firm value was measured using Tobin Q. The research design was ex-post facto. The population of the study were thirteen (13) listed industrial goods firms in Nigeria. The sampling technique used was census sampling method. The hypotheses were analyzed using descriptive and inferential statistics and regression analysis. The null hypothesis that accrual quality had no significant influence on earnings quality of listed industrial goods firms in Nigeria was rejected because accruals quality had a P-value of 0.008 which is less than critical value of 0.05. The null hypotheses earnings persistence, earnings predictability had no significant influence on firm value, failed to be rejected because they have positive and insignificant p-value of 0.647 and 0.615 respectively which are greater than the p-value of 0.05. The control variables financial leverage shows a positive significant p-value of 0.000 while firm size shows a positive and insignificant p-value of 0.763. The results revealed that earnings quality had positive influenced on firm value of listed industrial goods firms in Nigeria.
