# Unveiling the Dynamics of Super Apps: Adoption Factors and Strategic Insights across Key Industries

**著者**: Jan Budinský, Mohit Srivastava, Heřman Kopkáně, Ladislav Tyll, Ladislav Tyll
**年**: 2027 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートファイナンス

## Abstract

This study examined the emerging phenomenon of super apps, which are comprehensive platforms transforming mobile applications worldwide. It expanded the focus beyond mobility-based super apps to analyze their adoption across five critical sectors: mobility, food delivery and mobility, messaging, fintech, and e-commerce. Using logistic regression models on a dataset of 1,783 enterprises, of which 43 were classified as super apps, the study identified key factors that influence super app adoption. More specifically, this research fills a significant gap in the literature on platforms and multi-sided markets by evaluating the strategic development of super apps from a multisectoral perspective. In addition, it presents a conceptual framework for analyzing the technological infrastructure, regulatory landscape, market economics, and competition, as well as the network effects and multi-sided market dynamics that influence the growth trajectories of super apps.
