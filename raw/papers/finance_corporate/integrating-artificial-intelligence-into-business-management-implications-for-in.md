# Integrating artificial intelligence into business management: implications for intellectual capital development

**著者**: Dimitrie Stoica, Oana Pricopoaia, Nicoleta Cristache, Cezar Ionuț Bichescu, Camelia Mădălina Beldiman
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートファイナンス

## Abstract

Purpose This study explores the implications of artificial intelligence (AI) integration into business management practices, emphasizing its impact on intellectual capital components. Specifically, it examines how AI integration influences the interaction between human, structural and relational capital in managerial processes. Design/methodology/approach The study adopts a rigorous thematic analysis conducted in NVivo. Data collected through an online qualitative questionnaire administered to managers from multiple sectors were analyzed to identify key dimensions of AI integration in business contexts and to develop a conceptual model explaining their interactions. Findings The findings reveal four key dimensions: the role of AI in managerial processes, the challenges associated with AI, the strategies and best practices required to integrate AI into management and the strategies needed to adapt and train human resources in organizations integrating AI. While AI enhances efficiency, automation and decision-making processes, sustainable integration requires strategic planning, gradual implementation and investment in human capital. Overall, AI integration appears to enhance the interaction between human, structural and relational capital rather than radically restructuring intellectual capital. Practical implications The link between technological implementation and human-centered challenges provides practical guidance for organizations undergoing digital transformation. Gradual AI integration through pilot projects, employee training and governance structures supports responsible human–AI collaboration and strategic decision-making. Originality/value This research extends the traditional technology–organization–environment framework by conceptualizing AI integration as a dynamic socio-organizational process. Furthermore, it highlights how AI-driven managerial practices reinforce existing intellectual capital and provides a conceptual model explaining these dynamics.
