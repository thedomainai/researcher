# Capital Market Infrastructure, Regulation, and Financial Technology

**著者**: Abdullah Mamoon
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートファイナンス

## Abstract

This chapter provides a critical analysis of the transformation of capital market infrastructure (CMI) driven by Financial Technology (FinTech). It examines how distributed ledger technology, artificial intelligence (AI), cloud computing, and application programming interfaces are restructuring the core functions of exchanges, central counterparties, central securities depositories, and payment systems. By comparing traditional centralised architectures with decentralised and network-based models, the chapter identifies improvements in settlement efficiency, transparency, automation, and operational resilience. Additionally, it explores the concurrent evolution of regulatory frameworks through Regulatory Technology, Supervisory Technology, and the emerging concept of embedded supervision. Although FinTech-enabled CMI facilitates near-instant settlement, enhanced data integration, and predictive risk monitoring, it also introduces complex challenges related to legal finality, interoperability, cybersecurity, governance, and regulatory arbitrage. The chapter concludes by evaluating future developments, including tokenised assets, AI-driven predictive regulation, and integrated digital settlement assets, underscoring the necessity for adaptive, technology-native regulatory design.
