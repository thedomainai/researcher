# Reconfiguring Information Ecosystems: Referential, Curational, and Generative Intermediation

**著者**: Antti Lyyra, Roser Pujadas
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートファイナンス

## Abstract

Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) is transforming how information is accessed, produced, and distributed, with implications that extend beyond organizational contexts. This paper develops an information ecosystem perspective that foregrounds the processes of intermediation reshaping informational and economic flows. We theorize three modes of information intermediation, referential, curational, and generative, each characterized by distinct technological logics and processes for organizing access to information. These modes, which have emerged over time, are not mutually exclusive but layered, blending into hybrid forms in modern information services and ecosystems. We show how the emergence of generative intermediation can reconfigure information ecosystems by shifting information flows from traceable sources to synthesized patterns and associated economic flows from traffic redistribution to centralized value capture. In doing so, we highlight how evolving forms of intermediation drive structural transformation in information ecosystems.
