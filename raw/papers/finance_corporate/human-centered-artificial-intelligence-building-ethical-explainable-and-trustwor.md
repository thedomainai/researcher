# Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence: Building Ethical, Explainable and Trustworthy AI Systems

## Abstract

Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) are transforming healthcare, education, finance, agriculture, manufacturing, cybersecurity, and transportation by enabling technologies to learn from data, analyze information, make decisions, and solve complex problems. The Two-Day National Seminar on “Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Intelligence Today, Innovation Tomorrow” organized by K.R.T. Arts, B.H. Commerce and A.M. Science (K.T.H.M.) College, Nashik, sponsored under Quality Improvement Program of Savitribai Phule Pune University, emphasizes both the potential and responsibility of AI-led innovation. As AI systems influence critical human decisions, technical accuracy alone is insufficient. Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (HCAI) places human values, ethics, transparency, and trust at the core of the AI lifecycle. This paper presents a comprehensive study of HCAI focusing on ethical AI, explainable AI (XAI), and trustworthy deployment. It discusses algorithmic bias, opacity, privacy risks, and accountability gaps, and reviews frameworks from EU, UNESCO, and NITI Aayog, along with technical solutions such as LIME, SHAP, fairness-aware learning, federated learning, and human-in-the-loop architectures. Two architectural diagrams illustrate the HCAI framework and its implementation workflow.
