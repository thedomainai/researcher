# A Study of Innovative Financial Services in India – With Reference to Commercial Banks

**著者**: Bhautik S Ruparel
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートファイナンス

## Abstract

Abstract The Indian banking sector is undergoing a structural transformation driven by digital technology, platform-based finance, data analytics, artificial intelligence, cloud computing and interoperable payment infrastructure. This study examines the nature, adoption and implications of innovative financial services offered by commercial banks in India, with particular emphasis on digital payments, mobile and internet banking, UPI-linked services, biometric and Aadhaar-enabled services, video-based customer onboarding, digital lending, contactless payments, open banking interfaces, and emerging central bank digital currency ecosystems. The study adopts a descriptive and analytical approach based primarily on secondary data published by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) and the World Bank. Evidence indicates rapid expansion of digital financial infrastructure: UPI transaction volume increased from 4,59,561 lakh transactions in FY2021-2022 to 13,11,295 lakhs in FY2023-2024, while transaction value rose from ₹ 84.2 lakh crore to over ₹ 200 lakh crore. The RBI Digital Payments Index increased from 100 in March 2018 to 465.33 in September 2024. At the same time, innovation creates challenges involving cybersecurity, fraud, digital exclusion, data privacy, consumer literacy and operational resilience. The paper argues that commercial banks must combine technological innovation with customer-centric design, responsible digital lending, strong risk management and inclusive delivery to convert innovation into sustainable financial value.
