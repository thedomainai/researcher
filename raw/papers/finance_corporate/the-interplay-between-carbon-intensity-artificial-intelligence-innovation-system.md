# The Interplay between Carbon Intensity, Artificial Intelligence Innovation, Systematic Risk, and Unsystematic Risk

**著者**: Isnaini Nuzula Agustin, Jefry Jefry, Badriatul Mawadah
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートファイナンス

## Abstract

Carbon intensity has emerged as a critical financial risk factor in Indonesia amid the implementation of carbon economic policies, including Presidential Regulation No. 98 of 2021 and the launch of the Indonesia Carbon Exchange in 2023. This study examines the effect of carbon intensity on firm risk, measured through systematic risk and unsystematic risk, and investigates the moderating role of Artificial Intelligence (AI) innovation among companies listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange over the period 2018–2024. Using panel data regression analysis with year fixed effects on 394 firm-year observations across 74 companies, carbon intensity is measured as CO₂ emissions divided by firm revenue, systematic risk is represented by stock beta, unsystematic risk is measured through residual volatility, and AI innovation is proxied by the natural logarithm of AI-related patent counts. The results show that carbon intensity has a positive and significant effect on both systematic risk and unsystematic risk, indicating that firms with higher carbon exposure face greater market sensitivity and firm-specific volatility. Furthermore, AI innovation significantly weakens the positive effect of carbon intensity on unsystematic risk, suggesting its role as a firm-level risk-absorbing mechanism during the carbon transition process. However, AI innovation does not significantly moderate the relationship between carbon intensity and systematic risk, indicating that macro-level carbon transition risks remain beyond the reach of individual firm-level technological capabilities. This study contributes to the literature by providing empirical evidence from an emerging market setting and by highlighting the differentiated moderating role of AI innovation across two dimensions of firm risk.
