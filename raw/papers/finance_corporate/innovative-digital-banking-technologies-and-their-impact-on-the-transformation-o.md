# INNOVATIVE DIGITAL BANKING TECHNOLOGIES AND THEIR IMPACT ON THE TRANSFORMATION OF BANKING ACTIVITIES

## Abstract

The article examines innovative digital banking technologies as a key factor in the transformation of modern banking activities and the modification of traditional banking business models. The contemporary technological foundations of digital banking are considered, including artificial intelligence and machine learning, Big Data and data analytics, cloud technologies, APIs and Open Banking, robotic process automation, digital identification, modern payment technologies, and blockchain. Current trends in the digitalization of the Ukrainian banking sector are analyzed, including the development of cashless payments, the increasing adoption of contactless and tokenized payment instruments, the expansion of digital payment infrastructure, the formation of a regulatory framework for Open Banking, and the growing use of artificial intelligence technologies. It is substantiated that the digital transformation of banking activities is comprehensive in nature and involves a gradual transition from technological modernization to business process automation, personalized customer service, and the transformation of banks’ business models. The main transformational effects of innovative technologies are systematized, including increased operational efficiency, reduced service delivery time, expanded accessibility of financial products, improved customer experience, enhanced quality of managerial decision-making, and improved risk management systems. The key challenges of digital transformation are identified, including cyber risks, data protection, technological dependence, model risks associated with the use of artificial intelligence. The necessity of assessing the effectiveness of digitalization not only in terms of the scale of technology adoption but also according to its economic, customerrelated, operational, and risk-related effects is substantiated. The promising areas for the development of digital banking in Ukraine are identified, including the wider application of artificial intelligence, the development of Open Banking and Open Finance, the formation of digital ecosystems, the expansion of embedded finance, cloud technologies, and intelligent automation.
