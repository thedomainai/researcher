# Balance is bad? The structure of basic and applied research and firm financial performance

**著者**: Yutao Sun, Jia Chen, Yumei Liu
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートファイナンス

## Abstract

This study explores the impact of balancing basic and applied research on firm financial performance. By distinguishing knowledge complementarity from knowledge transformation, we propose a framework that explains the relationship between balanced research structure and firm financial performance based on knowledge-boundary view. Using a longitudinal panel dataset of Chinese technology-intensive firms listed on the STAR Market from 2016 to 2024, our findings indicate that balancing basic and applied research undermines firm financial performance. Specifically, because knowledge boundary frictions prevent basic research output from being fully absorbed into the applied domain, a balanced structure generates either redundant sunk costs, or an effective knowledge supply deficit when deep scientific input is required. Moreover, this negative effect is stronger for academic spin-offs but weaker for firms operating in science-based industries.
