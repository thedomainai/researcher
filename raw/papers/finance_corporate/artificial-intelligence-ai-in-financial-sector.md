## Abstract

The financial sector is under constant pressure to do the evolution of risk management techniques for discharge of financial services with the broad objectives of meeting the tenet of optimising cost and boost productivity with enlargement of the scope of inclusivity and financial outreach programme. This will be facilitated by a blending of financial services software interface, the facility that enables the nation to achieve an enviable record of ease of doing business, digitalisation of financial market through a safe and secure network portal. Artificial intelligence (AI) has become a game-changer in nearly all financial sectors. The use of AI in banking, insurance, stock market, quantitative finance has revolutionised the process of data analysis, predictions and risk management thereby improving the decision making process for investors. In this context an attempt has been made to focus on the application of AI in financial sector and analyse the global best practices of AI in banking, property and title insurance enforcement.
