# Algorithmic Governance And Predictive Artificial Intelligence In Hospital Workforce Management: A Narrative Review Of Burnout Prevention Strategies

**著者**: Ufuk Burak Karcıoğlu
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートファイナンス

## Abstract

Healthcare worker burnout remains a major organizational challenge that adversely affects employee well-being, quality of care, and workforce sustainability. Persistent staffing shortages, high workloads, unpredictable working schedules, and increasing administrative demands suggest that individual-level interventions alone may be insufficient to address the organizational determinants of burnout. This narrative review examines the potential role of predictive artificial intelligence and algorithmic governance in addressing organizational and workload-related factors associated with burnout in hospital workforce management and evaluates both the potential benefits and risks of these technologies. The available literature suggests that AI-enabled workforce management may improve workload predictability, reduce selected administrative burdens, and support workforce planning. Accordingly, the potential contribution of predictive AI to burnout mitigation should be understood not as a technological effect in isolation, but as part of a broader organizational approach supported by appropriate working conditions and robust algorithmic governance. Overall, predictive AI may serve as a tool for identifying burnout-related organizational risks at an earlier stage and supporting targeted workforce interventions; however, the current evidence does not justify a definitive causal claim that predictive AI prevents burnout. Future research should examine these relationships through longitudinal and empirical studies across different healthcare systems, professional groups, and organizational resource settings, while also evaluating potential unintended consequences of AI implementation.
