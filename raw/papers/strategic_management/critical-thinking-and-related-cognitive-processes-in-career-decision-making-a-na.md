# Critical Thinking and Related Cognitive Processes in Career Decision-Making: A Narrative Literature Review

**著者**: Natália Marková, Tomáš Sollár
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: 経営戦略

## Abstract

Career decision-making is a complex process involving the selection of educational and career pathways based on an individual’s values, interests, preferences, and available information. Critical thinking has been proposed as a potentially relevant cognitive competence in this process; however, direct empirical evidence of its role in career decision-making remains limited. This study aimed to synthesize empirical evidence on the relationship between critical thinking, related cognitive processes, and career decision-making. A narrative literature review was conducted using systematic search strategies across major bibliographic databases. Seven studies met the inclusion criteria. Only a small proportion of these studies directly examined critical thinking, whereas most investigated related cognitive constructs, including thinking styles, dysfunctional career thoughts, and other cognitive variables. Overall, the findings suggest that critical thinking and related cognitive constructs may be associated with career decision-making outcomes; however, the available evidence is limited, heterogeneous, and predominantly indirect. These findings highlight the need for further research directly examining critical thinking using validated measures and suggest that fostering critical thinking may represent a promising direction for educational and career guidance.
