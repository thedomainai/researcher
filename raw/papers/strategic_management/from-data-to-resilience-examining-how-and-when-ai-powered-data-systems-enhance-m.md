# From data to resilience: examining how and when AI-powered data systems enhance manufacturers' digital resilience

**著者**: Jieying Chen, Mingwang Cheng, Wenjie Xin
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経営戦略

## Abstract

Purpose This study investigates how artificial intelligence (AI)-powered data systems enhance manufacturers' digital resilience through dynamic knowledge management and under which contextual conditions these effects are strengthened or weakened. Design/methodology/approach Two-wave survey data from 331 Chinese manufacturing firms are analyzed using structural equation modeling and bootstrap procedures. Findings The AI-powered data systems significantly improve digital resilience both directly and indirectly through dynamic knowledge integration and dynamic knowledge reconfiguration. Moreover, AI strategic flexibility and environmental hostility strengthen the effects of the three AI-powered capabilities on dynamic knowledge management, thereby amplifying their indirect impact on digital resilience. Practical implications The findings highlight the need to treat AI-powered data systems as strategic, knowledge-centric infrastructures. Managers should invest in integrated AI architectures, cultivate dynamic knowledge processes, and purposefully build AI strategic flexibility, particularly in hostile environments, to unlock resilience gains. Originality/value By specifying dynamic knowledge integration and dynamic knowledge reconfiguration as mediating mechanisms and identifying AI strategic flexibility and environmental hostility as critical boundary conditions, it advances AI strategy, knowledge management, and dynamic capability research in emerging-market manufacturing.
