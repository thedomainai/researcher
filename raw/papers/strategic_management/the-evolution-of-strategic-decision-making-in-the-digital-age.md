# The Evolution of Strategic Decision-Making in the Digital Age

**著者**: Elena Gurgu, Sabyasachi Rath
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経営戦略

## Abstract

The rapid advancement of digital technologies has transformed the landscape of Strategic Decision-Making across industries. This chapter explores the paradigm shift in how organizations approach strategy formulation and execution in an increasingly data-driven, interconnected world. Drawing on a comprehensive review of literature and case studies, the study examines key factors which influence modern strategic decisions, including Big Data Analytics (BDA), Artificial Intelligence (AI), digital platforms, and automation. It highlights how these tools have not only enhanced decision-making agility, improved accuracy, and fostered innovation but also introduced new challenges, such as information overload, cybersecurity risks, and ethical concerns. The chapter traces the transition from traditional top-down, intuition-based decision-making models to more dynamic, decentralized approaches which leverage real-time data and predictive algorithms. Additionally, it assesses the role of leadership and organizational culture in adapting to these Digital Transformations, as well as the need for continuous learning and upskilling to maintain competitive advantage. The chapter concludes that the Digital Age demands a more agile, data-informed, and collaborative approach to Strategic Decision-Making, requiring organizations to balance technological innovation with human oversight and ethical considerations. This research contributes to a deeper understanding of how digitalization reshapes corporate strategy, offering valuable insights for business leaders, policymakers, and scholars in strategic management and organizational behaviour.
