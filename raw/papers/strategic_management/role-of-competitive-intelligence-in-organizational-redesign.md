# Role of Competitive Intelligence in Organizational Redesign

**著者**: Satya Subrahmanyam, Agatha Saber
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

In today's volatile business environment, organizational redesign is essential for sustaining competitiveness amidst technological disruptions, market shifts, and structural transformations. This chapter explores the strategic role of competitive intelligence (CI) in facilitating effective redesign, particularly during mergers, acquisitions, and restructuring. CI provides actionable insights into industry dynamics, market trends, competitor behavior, and internal capabilities. Using frameworks like the McKinsey 7S and Nadler-Tushman Congruence Model, the chapter illustrates how CI informs structural alignment and strategic decisions. Practical tools—SWOT, PESTEL, Porter's Five Forces, scenario planning—and technologies like AI and war-gaming are discussed. Emphasis is placed on ethical practices, leadership alignment, and embedding a CI-driven culture. Future trends, including predictive analytics and big data, are highlighted as essential for innovation, agility, and long-term resilience.
