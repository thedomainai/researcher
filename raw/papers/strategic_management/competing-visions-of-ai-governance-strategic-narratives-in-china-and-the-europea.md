# Competing Visions of AI Governance: Strategic Narratives in China and the European Union

**著者**: Dora Papadopoulou
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経営戦略

## Abstract

International actors use narratives to enhance their influence in the public sphere. Both China and the European Union (EU) construct strategic narratives regarding the role of artificial intelligence (AI) in pursuit of broader geopolitical objectives. Through AI governance, they develop policies, regulatory frameworks and technological strategies related to AI. This attempt focuses on examining the strategic narratives formulated by China and the EU regarding their governance models for AI. These narratives are reflected in policy, regulatory approaches and technology strategies that align with broader geopolitical aspirations. In addition, the study examines how China and the EU use AI governance as a tool of political and geopolitical influence, as well as how these strategic narratives shape perceptions of the international order and technological leadership. For this purpose, the period from 2022 to 2024 serves as the primary analytical timeframe, while selected policy texts from 2025 are incorporated as a bridge to future developments in AI governance. Although the two narratives are antagonistic, they address different audiences and project different governance priorities. The chapter contributes to broader debates on AI governance and geopolitical competition through a comparative analysis of EU and Chinese strategic narratives in the field of AI.
