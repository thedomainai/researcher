# Artificial Intelligence Driven Operational Excellence as a Catalyst for Sustainable Development in the Digital Economy

**著者**: nabila artika sari, Natannia Ericyant
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: 経営戦略

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence (AI) is now not only automating tasks but also transforming how organizations pursue both efficiency and sustainability. However, research on AI, operational excellence, and sustainability has largely remained fragmented. This study aims to analyze the role of AI-driven operational excellence in fostering sustainable digital transformation. A systematic literature review, following the PRISMA 2020 guidelines, was conducted on 20 publications from the 2018-2025 period, with data analyzed through thematic synthesis. The findings indicate that AI enhances operational excellence via process automation, predictive maintenance, and supply chain resilience while simultaneously supporting sustainability performance through energy and resource efficiency. These benefits are realized only when organizations possess sufficient dynamic capabilities and sociotechnical readiness. These findings provide a framework for managers to design efficient and sustainable AI strategies.
