# State-owned enterprises drive sustainable development of agricultural enterprises: supply chain spillover effects based on new quality productivity of enterprises

**著者**: Yutong Zhu, Wenjing Guo
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: meta_analysis | **分野**: 経営戦略

## Abstract

Introduction From the perspective of sustainable agricultural development, state-owned enterprises play a crucial role in ensuring the security and development of agricultural industrial and supply chains. They support productivity improvements among agricultural enterprises upstream and downstream in the supply chain, mitigate development risks for agricultural enterprises, and lay the economic foundation for sustainable agricultural development. Against the backdrop of global food system challenges, including supply chain disruptions, climate volatility, and resource constraints, Chinese agricultural enterprises face persistent dilemmas such as weak innovation capacity, high resource dependence, and low marketization. The strategic concept of “new-quality productive forces” has thus emerged as a key framework for driving technology-led and sustainability oriented agricultural transformation. Methods Drawing on the perspective of supply chain spillover effects and utilizing panel data from Chinese A-share listed agricultural companies from 2012 to 2024, this study employs a fixed-effects model, along with bootstrapped mediation analysis and instrumental variable approaches, to examine the impact of state-owned enterprises on the new-quality productive forces of agricultural enterprises upstream and downstream in the supply chain. Results The study finds that state-owned enterprises exert a positive spillover effect on the new-quality productivity of agricultural enterprises upstream and downstream in the supply chain; the higher the proportion of state-owned enterprises in the supply chain, the higher the level of new-quality productivity among agricultural enterprises. Mechanism tests reveal that state-owned enterprises primarily enhance the new-quality productivity of agricultural enterprises by promoting green technological innovation, actively strengthening internal control, and enhancing corporate governance efficiency. The results of the moderating mechanism analysis indicate that media supervision has a positive moderating effect on the supply chain spillover effects of state-owned enterprises. Heterogeneity analysis reveals that the spillover effects of state-owned enterprises on the new-quality productive forces of agricultural enterprises in the supply chain are more pronounced in the central and western regions. Specifically, the presence of state-owned enterprises on the supplier side has a more significant impact on private enterprises and those with closer regulatory proximity, while the presence of state-owned enterprises on the customer side has a more significant impact on state-owned enterprises themselves and those with more distant regulatory proximity. Discussion In summary, the presence of state-owned enterprises within the supply chain serves as a reserve force for agricultural enterprises, acting as a key driver for enhancing the level of new-quality productivity and achieving high-quality development in the agricultural sector. This finding offers a policy implication: leveraging SOEs as “anchors” in agricultural supply chains can complement direct subsidy policies to foster sustainable agricultural transformation.
