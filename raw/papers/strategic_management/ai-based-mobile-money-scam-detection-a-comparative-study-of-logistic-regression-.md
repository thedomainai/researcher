# AI-Based Mobile Money Scam Detection: A Comparative Study of Logistic Regression and LSTM Models

**著者**: Suleiman Ismail, Ibrahim Sani Lugga, Eli Adama Jiya
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経営戦略

## Abstract

The rapid proliferation of mobile money services across Sub-Saharan Africa and other developing economies has been accompanied by a corresponding surge in mobile money fraud, posing significant risks to financial inclusion and consumer welfare. This paper presents a comparative investigation of two machine learning approaches Logistic Regression (LR) and Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) neural networks for the automated detection of mobile money scams. Using a synthetically augmented transaction dataset of 1,048,575 records modelled on real-world mobile financial service patterns, we construct a comprehensive preprocessing pipeline encompassing missing-value imputation, feature engineering, class imbalance mitigation via Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique (SMOTE), and temporal sequence formatting for the LSTM model. The LSTM model, while competitive with an ROC-AUC of 0.9991, exhibits marginally lower recall, indicating sensitivity to sequential data representation. Logistic Regression, although interpretable and computationally efficient, yields comparatively lower performance, reflecting its linear decision boundary limitations in highly nonlinear fraud patterns. We discuss the implications of these findings for real-time fraud prevention systems in resource-constrained telecommunications environments, and identify avenues for future research including graph neural network integration and federated learning deployment.
