# DIGITAL ECONOMY DEVELOPMENT AND REGIONAL COMPETITIVENESS: ASSESSING THE SOCIO-ECONOMIC CONDITION OF REGIONS USING ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND SATELLITE DATA

**著者**: Zufarjon Toymasov
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経営戦略

## Abstract

This paper examines the interrelation between the development of the digital economy and the enhancement of regional competitiveness, focusing on the use of remote sensing and artificial intelligence (AI) to assess the socio-economic condition of regions, and poverty levels in particular. By integrating nighttime lights (NTL) imagery with geographic information system (GIS) technologies and machine learning, the study proposes data-driven analytical tools that complement demographic and economic statistics and support evidence-based regional policy.
