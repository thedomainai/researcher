# Tort Before Retrieval in Code: A Rail-Routing Architecture for Pre-Retrieval Legal Intake

**著者**: Yongzhi Huang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

This deposit provides a technical white paper and minimal executable prototype extending the Tort Before Retrieval thesis. The white paper develops a rail-routing architecture for pre-retrieval legal intake. It argues that Legal AI should not move directly from raw factual narrative to archive retrieval. Before retrieval begins, the system must first determine whether the material is procedurally ready: whether the basic factual structure is present, whether critical facts are verified, whether multiple legal routes are competing, and whether the matter may involve a novel or unclassified harm. The accompanying Python MVP, pure_procedural_pjls_mvp.py, implements a pure procedural Pre-Judgment Logic Shell. The prototype does not provide legal advice, does not determine legal merit, and does not classify legal liability. It only tests whether a matter is procedurally mature enough for targeted legal retrieval. The package’s central claim is that retrieval should be treated as a permissioned action rather than a default response. This shifts Legal AI governance from merely controlling hallucination at the output stage to controlling legal misrouting before retrieval begins.
