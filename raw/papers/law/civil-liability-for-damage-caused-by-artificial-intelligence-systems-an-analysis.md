# CIVIL LIABILITY FOR DAMAGE CAUSED BY ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEMS: AN ANALYSIS OF UZBEKISTAN'S LEGISLATION AND INTERNATIONAL EXPERIENCE

**著者**: Dilnura Akhmadjon qizi Abduvaliyeva, Worldly Knowledge Publishing Centre
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

This article analyzes the transformation of the civil liability framework regarding compensation for harm caused by artificial intelligence (AI) systems and autonomous algorithms to human life, health, property, and intangible rights. Current tort legislation is scrutinized through the lens of AI autonomy and algorithmic opacity. Drawing on a comparative legal approach, the paper examines modern practices and judicial case law from the European Union and the United States. The study concludes with practical and scholarly proposals for national legislation: categorizing high-risk AI systems as sources of increased danger, delineating liability between developers and operators, and introducing a rebuttable presumption of causality.
