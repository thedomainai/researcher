## Abstract

This Judicial Commentary establishes a groundbreaking doctrine for adjudicating algorithmic harm, building on Lex Aeternum Algorithmicum. It dismantles the "computer says no" defense via Strict Algorithmic Liability, Digital Causation, and the Chain of Command Test. Addressing epistemic asymmetry, it mandates reversed burdens of proof and secure code discovery. Featuring 15 practical chapters with model judgments, protective orders, and remedies like Kill-Switch Injunctions and Digital Termination, it equips judges to hold AI deployers accountable, ensuring human dignity prevails over autonomous code, bridging the gap between complex technology and global justice.
