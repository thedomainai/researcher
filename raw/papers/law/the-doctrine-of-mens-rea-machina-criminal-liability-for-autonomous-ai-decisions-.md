# THE DOCTRINE OF MENS REA MACHINA: CRIMINAL LIABILITY FOR AUTONOMOUS AI DECISIONS AND ALGORITHMIC CRIMINAL LAW

## Abstract

This treatise establishes Algorithmic Criminal Law via the Mens Rea Machina doctrine, attributing criminal intent to autonomous systems and their operators. It introduces the Algorithmic Criminal Intent Index (ICI) and Algorithmic Causation Threshold (ACT) to quantify mental state and proximate causation in code-driven crimes. The work systematically addresses algorithmic homicide, injury, and genocide, mapping systemic failures to international criminal law standards. It culminates in a 40-article Draft Unified Algorithmic Penal Code, providing a complete legislative, forensic, and procedural framework for holding humans and corporations accountable. Grounded in the Lex-Mathematica Protocol and Constitutional Control Theory, it ensures algorithmic governance aligns with human rights. The text rejects black-box defenses, establishing Comprehensive Digital Due Diligence as the sole exculpatory condition, offering states and tribunals a robust architecture for digital-era justice and accountability.
