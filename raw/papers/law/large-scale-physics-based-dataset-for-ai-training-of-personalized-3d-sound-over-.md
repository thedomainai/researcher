# Large-Scale Physics-based dataset for AI training of personalized 3D sound over headphones

**著者**: Luis Marcelino, Rui Rijo
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

The TNA project aims to create a large-scale, physics-based synthetic dataset for the study and personalisation of Head-Related Transfer Functions (HRTFs). Anatomically diverse virtual heads will be generated and simulated using the Boundary Element Method (BEM), including frequency-dependent surface absorption. The resulting data will support the investigation of individual and distance-dependent binaural cues and the training of AI models for estimating personalised HRTFs from images.Three interconnected types of data will be created: 3D head geometries (STL) produced through randomised virtual-head generation; HRTF acoustic data obtained from BEM simulations for both ears across 512 frequencies and multiple near-field source positions; and rendered head images (PNG) generated under diverse visual and rendering conditions. A unique subject identifier will link geometries, HRTFs and images, together with the relevant simulation and generation metadata. All subjects and associated data are synthetically generated and do not represent identifiable individuals.The primary scientific datasets and trained models are intended to remain restricted because of intellectual-property and commercial exploitation considerations.
