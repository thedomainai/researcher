# The accountability gap: Shadow digital labor and the crisis of accountability in automated welfare

**著者**: Viviana Ponce de León Solís
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

Automated welfare systems increasingly rely on complex assemblages of algorithms, administrative rules, and forms of digital labor that remain legally and institutionally invisible. This article develops a sociolegal analysis of what it terms the “accountability gap:” the structural disjunction between formal legal responsibility and the practical conditions under which automated welfare decisions are produced, maintained, and contested. Using the Chilean sociolegal and institutional landscape as a crucial case study, the article conceptualizes “shadow digital labor” as a constitutive yet unacknowledged component of contemporary welfare governance. It illustrates how “digital-by-default” welfare architectures clash with new GDPR-inspired data protection frameworks (Law No. 21.719), producing an asymmetrical distribution of compliance risk. Rather than proposing a comprehensive theory of automation, the analysis offers a jurisprudential typology of “law in action” to examine how accountability is displaced across human and technical actors.
