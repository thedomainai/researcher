# Datafication of Childhood in the AI Era

**著者**: Satyajeet Tukaram Chavan, Shivraj Vinayak Darekar, Anuradha Yenkikar
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

Children have become data-creating consumers in algorithmic markets due to the proliferation of artificial intelligence in digital markets. The interactions between children are being constantly tracked, scanned, and sold, be them smart toys and educational applications, biometric-enabled platforms, and so forth. The chapter is a critical analysis of the lifecycle of data of the child, starting at birth to adolescence, and contains ethical dangers of biometric surveillance, algorithmic non-transparency, and behavioral profiling. It assesses the shortcomings of the regulatory frameworks like the Children Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in terms of their effectiveness in the response to the consent issue and transparency issue. The chapter develops a child-centered AI data governance framework to safeguard the digital well-being through theoretical issues, case study evaluation, and balancing innovation and autonomy.
