# The PACE Firewall: A Diagnostic Account of the Legal ScreeningMechanism for Claims of Subjectivity in Artificial Intelligence andNeurotechnology

**著者**: Hanoi Towerz
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

This paper does not argue that law must first resolve the ontological question of consciousness raised by AI or neurotechnology. Instead, it proposes a conditional analytical frameworkshowing how legal systems process claims of subjectivity across divergent regulatory pathways, andreveals the structural vulnerabilities that arise whenever institutional design fixates on a single condition. These vulnerabilities trace back to a shared categorical presupposition: that “subject-status”(personhood), rather than the “screening mechanism” itself, is the basic unit of analysis. The paper’s contribution is not the discovery of a new fact about consciousness, but the identification ofa single recurring structure that law repeatedly uses when screening claims of subjectivity.
