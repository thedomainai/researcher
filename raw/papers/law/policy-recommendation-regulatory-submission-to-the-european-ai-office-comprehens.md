# Policy Recommendation & Regulatory Submission to the European AI Office: Comprehensive Governance of Generative Biometric Mimicry and Proposed Amendments to the GPAI Code of Practice under Regulation (EU) 2024/1689

**著者**: 洋子 長谷部
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

Description / Abstract(概要) This policy paper and regulatory submission to the European AI Office (DG CONNECT) formulates concrete statutory amendments and technical standards for the upcoming General-Purpose AI (GPAI) Code of Practice under the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689). Addressing the systemic exploitation risks posed by generative biometric mimicry (such as speech-driven facial and articulatory cloning), this document establishes four actionable pillars: Multi-Layer Machine-Readable Rights Reservation: Enforceable standards across HTTP headers, binary document XMP metadata, and C2PA provenance assertions under Article 53(1)(c). Biometric Exemption from TDM: Legal re-classification of dynamic articulatory kinematics as special category biometric data under GDPR Article 9, exempt from general TDM exceptions (CDSM Directive Art. 4). Zero-Knowledge Verifiable Training (zkVM): Mathematical non-ingestion proof protocols fulfilling Article 53(1)(d) while preserving trade secrets. Operational Continuity Licensing (OCL) & Fiduciary Board Liability: Pre-deployment liquidity escrow trusts coupled with director oversight obligations under CSDDD and Caremark principles.
