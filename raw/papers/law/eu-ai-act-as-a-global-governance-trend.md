# EU AI Act as a Global Governance Trend

**著者**: Alaa Ibrahim
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

This academic project examines the EU AI Act as a broader trend toward risk-based artificial intelligence governance. It considers how regulation may influence innovation incentives, organizational capabilities, compliance costs, and societal outcomes, using algorithmic hiring as an illustrative application. The analysis draws on academic literature and policy documents and develops alternative scenarios around enforcement intensity and the maturity of standards and compliance tools. These scenarios explore possible pathways and monitoring indicators rather than predict a single future. The discussion reflects the sources and regulatory context used in the submitted version. It should not be treated as current legal guidance; implementation details require verification against up-to-date official sources. Prepared for the Foresight and Global Trends module at Macromedia University of Applied Sciences. This is a student academic project and has not undergone journal peer review.
