# UAGT — Unified AI Governance Taxonomy

**著者**: Vinod Dhiman
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

An open, machine-readable, citable, and continuously versioned crosswalk anchored on ISO/IEC 42001:2023, the NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0, and the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, as amended), with optional cross-mappings to ISO/IEC 27001, SOC 2 (Trust Services Criteria), COBIT 2019, and ISO/IEC 27701 - seven frameworks across eight governance domains. Built on a normalized Master Control Set with typed relationships (full/partial/superset/subset/none), rationale, and provenance for every mapping. References clause/article identifiers only; source standards are not reproduced.
