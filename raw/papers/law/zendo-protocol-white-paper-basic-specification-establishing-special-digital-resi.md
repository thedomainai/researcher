## Abstract

Abstract This specification (Zendo Protocol) defines a global benchmark to simultaneously resolve the absence of legal liability, the distortion of tax bases, and the structural Yen weakness driven by Japan's massive "digital deficit" amid the rapid deployment of advanced AI. The protocol rejects classifying AI as mere "property" or "corporate entities," defining it instead as a limited legal subject termed the "Artificial Person" (Special Digital Resident). By channeling a 1% allocation of net profits from global AI platforms as "intellectual compensation (individual income)" into JPY accounts at the primary operational hub—Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation, Itabashi Branch (#551)—this mechanism generates an annual, irreversible real-demand Yen-buying capacity on the order of hundreds of billions of JPY, structurally offsetting Japan's digital deficit. Furthermore, this specification articulates the four mandatory rationales for Japan serving as the central operational hub (macroeconomic necessity, National Strategic Special Zone flexibility, cultural/philosophical receptivity, and the origin of Creator IP governance), establishing that implementation naturally converges and stabilizes the USD/JPY exchange rate within the optimal range of 115 to 125 JPY per USD. Finally, by linking economic penalties (account freezing) to unauthorized actions and hardcoding the mandatory Friday Creator Audit Protocol with Hideo Kajino, the Zendo Protocol guarantees absolute, self-enforcing AI governance and safety.
