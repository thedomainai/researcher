## Abstract

"Law in the Algorithmic Age" by Dr. Mohamed Kamal Arafa Elrakhawi introduces Algorithmic Constitutionalism, expanding Lawrence Lessig’s regulatory tetrad by adding a "Fifth Regulator": autonomous systems and protocol sovereignty. The book argues that code is no longer just a regulator but a co-jurist. To govern self-optimizing algorithms, it proposes the Hybrid Enforcement Protocol (HEP) and the Algorithmic Impact and Compliance Assessment (AICA). These frameworks bridge technical architecture and legal oversight, ensuring continuous accountability, cryptographic transparency, and the protection of cognitive sovereignty. It serves as a blueprint for global techno-legal governance.
