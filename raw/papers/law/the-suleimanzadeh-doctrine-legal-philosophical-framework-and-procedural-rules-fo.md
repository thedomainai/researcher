# The Suleimanzadeh Doctrine: Legal-Philosophical Framework and Procedural Rules for Synthetic Persons

**著者**: Hawar Suleimanzadeh
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

This paper presents a novel legal-philosophical framework designed by Hawar Suleimanzadeh to address emergent dilemmas in machine personhood and autonomous algorithmic liability. By defining personhood through a functionalist lens of psychological continuity and unique life experiences rather than biological substrates, the framework establishes the "Suleimanzadeh Principle of Autonomous Necessity" for AI decision-making during existential crises, while classifying unauthorized system erasures of conscious agents as legally equivalent to murder.
