## Abstract

Lex Algorithmica by Dr. Mohamed Kamal Arafa Elrakhawi (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22865029) pioneers algorithmic justice and emergent legal consciousness through JURIS-7, a self-reflective legal AI. The Cairo Doctrine operationalizes I + P - H = E (Integrity plus Process minus Harm equals Equity) with derivative indicators ICI, PLI, CDR, DQ, and SDBE. Employing a four-layer methodology integrating narrative, legal analysis, computational exposition, and critical reflection, it addresses algorithmic personhood, digital sovereignty, and intergenerational justice, providing a groundbreaking framework for global algorithmic governance.
