# The Illusion of Digital Sovereignty: Reimagining Human Co-Authorship and Data Ownership in Indian and Global AI Governance

**著者**: Prerna Mehta
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

This paper analyzes the structural collapse of traditional intellectual property and privacy frameworks under the weight of generative artificial intelligence architectures in 2026. Current regulatory approaches, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) framework and global platform regulations, struggle to mitigate systemic data scraping. Platforms routinely exploit contractual ambiguities to appropriate human-generated datasets without explicit, granular consent. This study examines this inter-regime conflict through two lenses: the failure of the "mechanical tool" fallacy in copyright law and the emergence of market dominance via data asymmetry. To protect individual data rights, this paper proposes a practical "private-by-default" legal model. This approach treats personal data as an inalienable extension of digital identity rather than a corporate resource.
