# Chasing the algorithm:A law and economics perspective on experimental AI governance

**著者**: Antonella Zarra
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

When should institutions step in and regulate novel technologies such as AI? Traditionally, scholars involved in the economic analysis of law would answer along the following lines: “when the benefits of regulation outweigh the costs.”24 This thesis examines the contributions of law and economics to addressing AI governance challenges by analyzing market failures, liability allocations, and the effectiveness of regulatory interventions in order to answer two research questions: How should AI rules be designed? Are standard regulatory tools sufficient? To accomplish this, the present thesis explores the extent to which existing legal frameworks can internalize (and mitigate) AI-related risks and how economic, design thinking, and behavioral principles can inform regulatory design in an effort to ensure optimal policy outcomes.
