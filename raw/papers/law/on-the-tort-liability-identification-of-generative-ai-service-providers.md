# On the Tort Liability Identification of Generative AI Service Providers

**著者**: Ziyu Li
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

Generative AI service providers should be positioned as indirect infringement subjects, not directly subject to product liability or traditional "safe harbor" rules. Liability attribution should be based on fault liability, with fault presumption introduced in scenarios such as the "algorithm black box" where decision-making processes are opaque. The duty of care must be dynamic, requiring a "type + hierarchy" system that distinguishes between basic obligations applicable to all providers and strengthened obligations for high-risk scenarios. The traditional notification-removal rule should be reshaped into a "notification-shielding" mechanism aimed at "preventing re-generation" of infringing content rather than mere post-hoc removal. Additionally, protective norms in public law should be identified to establish a linkage mechanism between public and private law. This approach balances technological innovation with rights protection, ensuring legal responses remain adaptive to generative AI's unique challenges while maintaining accountability and fairness. A reasonable duty of care applies across different service provider types and application scenarios, promoting responsible innovation without overburdening providers. The proposed framework avoids imposing strict liability while ensuring effective protection for infringed parties through fault-based attribution and dynamic obligations tailored to specific risk profiles.
