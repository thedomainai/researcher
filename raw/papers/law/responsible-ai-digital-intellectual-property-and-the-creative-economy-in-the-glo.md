# Responsible AI, Digital Intellectual Property, and the Creative Economy in the Global South: A Scoping Review of Policy Frameworks and Ethical Guardrails

**著者**: Ruth B. Rodriguez
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

The rapid diffusion of generative artificial intelligence (AI) systems has reconfigured how creative value is produced, circulated, and captured. Large language and multimodal models trained on vast corpora of text, images, audio, and video now generate outputs competing directly with human-authored works, while raising unresolved questions of consent, attribution, and compensation. These dynamics are global, but their distributional consequences are uneven. Creators, cultural institutions, and regulators in the Global South face a dual imperative: to engage with AI governance frameworks largely authored in the Global North, while simultaneously protecting indigenous knowledge, traditional cultural expressions (TCEs), and nascent creative industries from extractive data practices. Public administration scholarship recognizes that technology governance is an arena where state capacity, normative authority, and distributive justice are negotiated. This scoping review maps the intersection of responsible AI, digital intellectual property (IP), and the creative economy across the Global South.
