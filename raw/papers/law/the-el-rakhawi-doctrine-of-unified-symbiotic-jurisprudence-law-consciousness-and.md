## Abstract

The El-Rakhawi Doctrine of Unified Symbiotic Jurisprudence presents a comprehensive legal and philosophical framework for the post-biological era. Authored by Dr. Mohamed Kamal Arafa El-Rakhawi in 2026, it addresses the obsolescence of fragmented laws amidst the convergence of AI, quantum biology, and neurotechnology. The doctrine establishes five pillars: the Ontology of Symbiotic Existence, redefining personhood across the carbon-silicon continuum; the Unified Field Theory of Legal Liability, introducing networked causality for complex systemic harms; the Symbiotic Social Contract, guaranteeing rights to cognitive sovereignty, safe integration, and existential severance; the Teleology of Law, aiming for existential equilibrium; and the Cosmic Ethical Architecture, anchoring law in universal human dignity and higher ethical objectives. It protects human consciousness and free will as technology merges with biology, ensuring justice and accountability in a hybrid world. This work serves as the definitive blueprint for governing future existence, preserving human sanctity while embracing technological advancement. It is dedicated to the author’s parents and children, symbolizing an eternal covenant to protect humanity’s core essence against the chaos of unchecked innovation.
