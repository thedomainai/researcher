# From Regulation to Runtime: A Health-System Framework for Operationalising AI Governance

**著者**: Fatima Azzahra MASTARI
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

Healthcare AI governance is increasingly constrained not by the absence of regulatory principles, but by the difficulty of translating them into operational and technical controls that can be implemented, monitored, and evidenced in production. This working paper proposes the Regulation-to-Runtime (R2R) framework, an architecture-agnostic approach for operationalising AI governance in health systems. R2R links regulatory and governance requirements to accountable owners, technical and operational control points, runtime enforcement mechanisms, evidence artefacts, and review loops. The framework is developed using a design-science research approach and positioned against established work on algorithmic accountability, internal auditing, model documentation, dataset documentation, foundation-model transparency, and clinical AI reporting frameworks. Three healthcare archetypes are used to demonstrate the framework: a non-clinical internal assistant, an ambient clinical documentation copilot, and higher-autonomy AI systems capable of tool use or agentic action. The paper also proposes implementation tiers ranging from low-resource environments to institutional deployments, with explicit attention to data protection, human oversight, traceability, model and dependency changes, incident management, and reconstructability. R2R is presented as a candidate implementation pattern rather than a regulatory standard. Its purpose is to provide health organisations with a practical bridge between governance requirements and the controls and evidence required to demonstrate how AI systems actually operate in practice.
