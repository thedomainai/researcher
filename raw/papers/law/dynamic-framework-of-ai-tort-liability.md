# Dynamic Framework of AI Tort Liability

**著者**: Juan Diaz-Granados
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

Chapter 6 presents a novel model for reallocating and recalibrating tort liability across diverse types of AI systems. Recognizing that AI technologies differ in both the risks they pose and the benefits they deliver, the chapter proposes a dynamic, dual-axis framework—the tort liability matrix—that maps liability along two dimensions: the degree of risk and the scope of benefit, each ranging from private to public. This model establishes four normative “zones” of liability—green, yellow, orange and red—each reflecting a distinct balance between public and private risks and benefits. At its core lies a principle of proportionality: as risks become more public and severe, and the benefits remain predominantly private, liability mechanisms intensify, shifting from traditional fault-based regimes toward stricter forms of liability. To this end, and building on a functional taxonomy of AI actors, the chapter introduces dynamic liability mechanisms that allow tort law to evolve in alignment with technological change. These mechanisms include flexible approaches to traditional tort doctrines, ex ante and functional allocation of liability, industry best practices, safe harbors, evidentiary presumptions, risk mitigation measures, joint and several liability, insurance schemes, public compensation funds, strict liability and precautionary bans and moratoria. These mechanisms operationalize a responsive and proportionate system of AI tort liability that balances innovation, accountability and public protection.
