## Abstract

Lex Aeternum Algorithmicum (Articles 5-10) by Dr. Mohamed Kamal Arafa Elrakhawi proposes a supranational constitutional framework for algorithmic governance. It introduces three core mechanisms: Algorithmic Due Process, mandating pre-execution judicial review to prevent irreversible harm; the Cloud Judicial Officer, an independent entity empowered to cryptographically audit algorithms via zero-knowledge proofs without exposing trade secrets; and Cloud Sovereignty, a treaty-based framework enabling global enforcement and algorithmic embargoes against non-compliant jurisdictions. Grounded in international human rights law, this academic proposal transforms voluntary ethical AI guidelines into binding, enforceable constitutional law, ensuring algorithms remain strictly subordinate to human dignity, transparency, and legal accountability.
