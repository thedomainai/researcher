# Ethical considerations in AI-driven radiology

**著者**: Atul Kumar Singh
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

The integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Deep Learning (DL) into medical imaging marks a transformative era in modern medicine. Radiology, uniquely positioned as a highly technology-dependent specialty, increasingly relies on algorithms for automated nodule detection, anatomical segmentation, and clinical triage. However, the rapid advancement of these technologies has vastly outpaced the development of robust ethical guidelines. Shortcomings in radiological algorithms can lead to direct, measurable consequences for patient outcomes. This chapter explores the critical ethical dimensions of AI-driven radiology, analysing the challenges of algorithmic bias, data justice, transparency, and the "black box" dilemma. It further examines legal liability frameworks, patient privacy, risks of clinical de-skilling, and the shifting professional role of the radiologist from a technical image reader to a patient-centred communicator. Ultimately, this chapter outlines a comprehensive ethical framework designed to maximize clinical benefits while minimizing patient risk.
