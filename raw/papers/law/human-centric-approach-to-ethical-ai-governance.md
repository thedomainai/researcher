# Human-Centric Approach to Ethical AI Governance

**著者**: Ida Skubis
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

This chapter examines the concept of a human-centric approach in the European Union’s Artificial Intelligence Act (AI Act), a landmark legal framework addressing the ethical governance of AI systems. Through qualitative content analysis, the study identifies and evaluates the occurrence and contextual significance of key terms – human-centric AI, human well-being, social and environmental well-being, user-friendliness, inclusivity, gender balance, and human dignity – within the Act’s provisions. The findings highlight the Act’s emphasis on aligning AI systems with societal values, fundamental rights, and ethical principles, ensuring inclusivity, autonomy, and public trust. Additionally, the analysis underlines the Act’s focus on safeguarding public interests such as health, safety, democracy, and environmental sustainability while balancing innovation and regulation. By placing a strong emphasis on a human-centric approach and fostering accountability, transparency, and equity, the AI Act serves as a model for responsible AI governance. This research examines how the European Union’s (EU) regulatory approach, as reflected in the AI Act, seeks to mitigate ethical risks and promote societal well-being through a human-centric legal framework.
