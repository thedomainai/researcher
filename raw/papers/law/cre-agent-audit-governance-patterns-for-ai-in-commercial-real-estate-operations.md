# cre-agent-audit: Governance Patterns for AI in Commercial Real Estate Operations

**著者**: Kunjar Bhaduri
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

Reference governance patterns, schemas, and runnable implementations for audit-ready autonomous AI agents in commercial real estate operations. Ships nine governance primitives (a DEFCON state machine, a fail-closed un-self-clearable sovereign veto, a tamper-evident hash-chain audit ledger with witness-anchored regeneration detection, an Autonomy Ladder A0-A4 level-gate, a regulation loader, a shadow-mode rollout router, a lease-abstraction provenance check, a fair-housing pre-flight gate with an opt-in mutual-information learned-proxy detector, and a tenant-PII data-residency check) plus a vendor-output adapter (VendorScoreGate) for vendor-mediated AI scoring, persistence/trusted-timestamp/external-witness seams, a module-integrity verifier-attestation hook, and three runnable regulatory-incident replays (TransUnion, SafeRent, RealPage) that produce six-artifact audit-evidence bundles. Anchored to the Fair Housing Act, ECOA, the Colorado AI Act (SB 24-205 as amended by SB 26-189), FCRA, the Sherman Act, EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, and Treasury FS AI RMF. Reference IP for adoption, not a control operating in production; not legal or compliance advice.
