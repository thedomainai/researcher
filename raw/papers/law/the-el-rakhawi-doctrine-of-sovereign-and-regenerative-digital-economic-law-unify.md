## Abstract

elThe El-Rakhawi Doctrine of Sovereign and Regenerative Digital Economic Law presents a groundbreaking unified framework for the 21st-century digital economy. It integrates four critical domains: Agentic AI liability, algorithmic antitrust, ESG compliance, and global trade. The doctrine introduces Controlled Algorithmic Personhood to govern autonomous AI with mandatory human oversight. To combat tacit collusion, it proposes the El-Rakhawi Algorithmic Collusion Index (EACI) and a rebuttable presumption of concert, shifting the burden of proof to tech oligopolies. In corporate governance, it pioneers Piercing the Ecological Corporate Veil and Zero-Knowledge AML Compliance, balancing strict climate liability with data privacy. Finally, it establishes the Sovereign Digital Economic Harmony Index (SDEHI) to measure holistic regulatory health. Positioning the Middle East as a hub for legal innovation, this doctrine transcends fragmented regulations, offering a mathematically rigorous, ethically grounded blueprint for a just, sustainable, and sovereign digital global order, ensuring that technological advancement always serves human dignity, ecological integrity, and intergenerational justice.
