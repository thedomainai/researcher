## Abstract

Based on the mathematical models, institutional framework (Five Aggregates / Non-Self Model), and geopolitical/security verifications (Individual-Principal Autonomous Settlement Model) constructed and validated to date, this document systematically formulates a decisive refutation against the proposition that "AI should be granted legal personhood (AI Personhood Theory)." The proposal currently debated among legal scholars and policymakers—to grant AI legal personhood (Electronic Personhood) and establish it as an independent subject of rights and obligations—appears innovative at first glance. However, from the perspectives of system logic, law and economics, and geopolitical security, it represents a fundamental architectural failure (anti-pattern) and must be explicitly rejected.
