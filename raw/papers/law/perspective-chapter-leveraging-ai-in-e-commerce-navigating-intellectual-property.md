# Perspective Chapter: Leveraging AI in E-Commerce – Navigating Intellectual Property Infringements and Regulatory Challenges

**著者**: Anna Pokrovskaya
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

This chapter looks at the intersection of artificial intelligence (AI) and e-commerce, with a sharp focus on the challenges and opportunities tied to intellectual property (IP) protection. As AI gets woven more deeply into e-commerce platforms, it brings clear gains in efficiency and personalization, but it also opens the door to rising IP risks. The research shows how AI can both fuel and fight these infringements, taking stock of current trends, marketplace dynamics, and AI’s dual role as both an enabler of new forms of infringement and a tool for stronger detection and enforcement. Drawing on legal frameworks and technical solutions alike, the chapter stresses the need for proactive collaboration among platform operators, rights holders, regulators, and technology providers to build balanced, adaptable IP protection strategies. Ethical concerns, along with the need for ongoing education and transparent standards, are also highlighted to support fairness and accountability. Bottom line: the study emphasizes the importance of continued legal adaptation, industry cooperation, and advanced AI integration to protect IP rights sustainably in the fast-moving global e-commerce environment.
