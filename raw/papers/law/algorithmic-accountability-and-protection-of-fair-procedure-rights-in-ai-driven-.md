# Algorithmic Accountability and Protection of Fair Procedure Rights in AI-Driven Public Administration

**著者**: Muhamad Fajri Permana Haryanto, Binastya Anggara Sekti
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

This chapter analyzes the paradigm shift from human-based bureaucracy to data-based governance and the tensions between algorithmic logic and fair procedure rights. The authors argue that automated decision-making in public administration triggers algorithmic procedural erosion, where traditional legal protections fail to address “black box” opacity. By identifying research gaps in how administrative law handles algorithmic discretion, this chapter explores the realignment of state power from officials to code developers. The authors propose reconstructing legal norms through an “Accountability by Design” framework, integrating procedural rights such as notification, intelligible explanation, and human intervention directly into AI architecture. Furthermore, this chapter advocates for independent algorithmic audits as legal evidence in judicial reviews to bridge information asymmetry. Finally, the authors conclude that normative redesign is essential to ensure digitalization strengthens democratic sovereignty and the rule of law, rather than creating an unaccountable technocracy.
