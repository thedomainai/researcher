# Intellectual Property Rights and Cyber Forensics: Addressing the Emerging Challenges of Digital Era Through Empirical Analysis

**著者**: Chitra Shukla, Kaveri Shukla
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

In the digital era, there is rapid growth in technologies, which are accompanied with its challenges to the existing system. Earlier preserving of intellectual property was easy as technologies were not much developed. Traditional evidence system is no longer sufficient in encountering the emerging challenges created by technologies. Cyber forensics is a branch of forensic evidence that aims to analyze various digital evidences. They play a crucial role in investigating civil and criminal cases but if proper guidelines are framed then these evidences can also be used in investigation of infringement cases effectively. This research will explore the ways in which cyber forensic techniques can be effectively used in resolving complex IP cases and protect the rights of original owners. The main objectives of this research is identifying emerging challenges to intellectual property rights in the digital age and identifying reforms to these cyber threats with the help of digital forensic techniques. Empirical research methodology has been used in which direct interaction has been done with IP practitioners, legal professionals and cyber forensic experts to bring out ground realities and measures that experts suggest for improving the existing situation. Structured questionnaire has been used for gathering data from 100 experts from each professional category. Qualitative and quantitative data analysis have been used for identifying the thematic challenges and solutions proposed by experts. The research concludes with suggesting effective policies through the ground level survey carried out in this research.
