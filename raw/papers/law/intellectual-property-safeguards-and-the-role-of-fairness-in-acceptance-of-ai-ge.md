# Intellectual Property Safeguards and the Role of Fairness in Acceptance of AI Generated Content

**著者**: Kazi Abdul Mannan, Mikat Jahan Sony
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

Abstract Public endorsement of safeguards for AI-generated creative content does not establish how strongly fairness figures in personal acceptance of that content. This cross-sectional study distinguishes these judgements using 385 adult questionnaire records from Bangladesh. We analysed four statements on creator protection, transparent balancing rules, complaint access and transparency about data and content practices, and a fifth statement on the relevance of fairness to acceptance. We estimated full response distributions, joint endorsement and paired contrasts, followed by exploratory regressions of the original fairness-salience rating. Safeguard endorsement ranged from 78.2% to 80.3%, compared with 58.4% for the salience statement. Within-person differences ranged from 19.7 to 21.8 percentage points (all Holm-adjusted p < 0.001). Of the 301 respondents endorsing access to complaints, 176 also endorsed the salience statement. A copyright-oriented summary was associated with a higher salience rating in the adjusted model (b = 0.358, 95% CI 0.181 to 0.536; Holm-adjusted p < 0.001). Reported production precautions and the governance expectation summary did not meet the adjusted threshold. The association with copyright orientation persisted when the outcome was dichotomised and when the analysis was restricted to law students. The findings distinguish support for institutional protections from the reported place of fairness in personal judgement. They do not measure actual acceptance, legal compliance or the effect of changing a safeguard. The sample is concentrated in legal education and practice and does not establish national prevalence.
