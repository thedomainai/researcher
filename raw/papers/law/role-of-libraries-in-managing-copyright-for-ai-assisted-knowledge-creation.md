# Role of Libraries in Managing Copyright for AI-Assisted Knowledge Creation

**著者**: M Anjaneya Naik
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

Today, the integration of AI technology across all sectors has transformed content creation processes. As with all sectors, the usage of artificial intelligence (AI) in providing library facilities presents many challenges. While AI tools offer the opportunity to systematize processes and improve users' access to information, they simultaneously pose a risk of copyright infringement due to the unintended reproduction of copyrighted material in AI-generated content. This study examines how AI-powered tools are being advanced for creating educational materials in libraries and explores the crucial role of librarians in copyright management for AI-assisted knowledge creation in this technological revolution. This analysis includes an examination of the multifaceted challenges faced by libraries, a review of legal outlines such as India's Copyright Act, 1957, and relevant case law, and a proposal for a multi-pronged strategy to mitigate risks. This strategy recommends technical safeguards such as copyright detection tools, amendments to policies and laws, education on copyright and the responsible use of AI, and collaboration among stakeholders, enabling libraries to leverage the benefits of AI while balancing innovation with minimizing copyright risks and respecting intellectual property rights. This paper explores the complexities arising from this intersection.
