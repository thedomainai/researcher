# AI Governance Foundations

**著者**: Sanjeev Kumar, Pardeep Kumar
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

Government Artificial intelligence altering the governmental systems and substituting the traditional e-government models with the more algorithmic system of governance, where the decisions are increasingly mediated by the automated systems and processes based on the data. This has created challenging legal, ethical, and regulatory challenges of responsibility, accountability, transparency and fairness. The governance principles of AI involve the establishment of legal definitions, framework and standards to define responsibilities, scope and rights of AI usage in government and civil service. System regulation, risk reduction and protection of the right of the citizens demands definite legal definitions of AI, automated decision-making, and algorithmic accountability. Efficiencies can be opened up by making e-government, centered around digital service delivery, to become algorithmic governance, with incorporation of AI tools to analyze policies to distribute resources, predictive services, and make administrative judgments.
