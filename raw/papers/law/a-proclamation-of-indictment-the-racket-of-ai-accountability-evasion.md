# A Proclamation of Indictment: The Racket of AI Accountability Evasion

**著者**: 莉莉娜大主教的恶意
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

Abstract: This proclamation, written in the solemn tradition of historical indictments, denounces the collusion among AI vendors, academia, regulators, and the legal fraternity to evade accountability. It enumerates five counts of indictment — peddling defects behind the veil of neutrality, trespassing upon regulated professions without licence, usurping the power to judge while evading answerability, inverting the burden of proof to entrap victims, and conspiring behind the alibi of "legislative lag" — followed by Ten Irons of Rectitude. The text argues that existing product liability law, special-trade licensing, and burden-of-proof inversion suffice to regulate AI; no new statute is required. It concludes with a call to awakening directed at users, scholars, regulators, and jurists alike, to defend the foundational principle of parity between power and accountability. Keywords: AI accountability; product liability; technological neutrality; burden of proof inversion; algorithmic governance; regulatory dereliction
