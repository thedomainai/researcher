# Civil liability in a data driven economy: Proposal for a data-centric liability

**著者**: Michel Cannarsa
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

The topic of civil liability in the context of fast-spreading AI technologies can be taken from different angles. First of all, from a practical perspective, the fast development of stand-alone AI and embedded AI in more and more consumer goods requires to tackle several unresolved questions about grounds of liability, allocation of liability among several producers and developers, compensation of non-material damages, and many others as well as their fitness in the current digital revolution. Secondly, the new economic and societal context is an invitation to adapt and revisit traditional product liability and more generally tort law doctrines, which is the undergoing process at the EU level. Because AI technologies raise issues that were not previously addressed by domestic legislators, there is a genuine opportunity to set up a single European scheme to deal with damages caused by AI.
