# THE EL-RAKHAWI UNIFIED ANALYTICAL FRAMEWORK FOR CONSTITUTIONAL COMPETENCE A Comparative Critical Study Across Legal Systems

**著者**: mohamed kamal arafa el-rakhawi
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 法学

## Abstract

This research presents the El-Rakhawi Unified Analytical Framework for Constitutional Competence, a critical comparative study across major legal systems. It addresses modern challenges by critiquing the normalization of emergency powers in Arab states and proposing a dual-track accountability mechanism for independent regulatory authorities. It introduces "Pan-Arab Constitutional Stare Decisis" to harmonize fundamental rights jurisprudence without violating sovereignty. Finally, it establishes the Principle of Non-Delegation of Discretionary Competence to Black-Box Algorithms, mandating human ratification for AI-driven administrative decisions to preserve constitutional accountability and the rule of law.
