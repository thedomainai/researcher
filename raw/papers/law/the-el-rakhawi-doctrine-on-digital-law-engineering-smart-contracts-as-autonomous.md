## Abstract

THE ELRAKHAWI DOCTRINE redefines smart contracts as autonomous self-executing legal entities, establishing Algorithmic Jurisprudence. It introduces the Legal-Technical Isomorphism Protocol to align code with legal intent. It resolves critical paradoxes: Exploits via Emergency Patches; Oracle Manipulation via Liability Standards; the DAO Accountability Void by creating Digital Legal Entities; and Public Law collisions via Regulatory Kill Switches. Integrating these into an International Treaty and Diamond-tier Certification, the treatise provides the definitive blueprint for the Digital Uniform Commercial Code, ensuring legal certainty, compliance, and justice in decentralized transactions.
