## Abstract

IQLíder® System of Ethical Indicators for AI in Education 1. Purpose of the Indicator System 1.1. Conceptual nature The IQLíder® System of Ethical Indicators for AI in Education is a conceptual framework designed to describe ethical, pedagogical, and governance-related dimensions associated with the use of artificial intelligence in educational environments. Its function is strictly observational: it enables the identification of structures, trends, and relevant principles without performing evaluations, audits, diagnostics, or operational recommendations. The system is grounded in an analytical approach that prioritizes epistemic clarity and avoids instrumental interpretations. Its value lies in providing a shared language for examining educational phenomena mediated by AI without replacing national regulations, institutional policies, or international guidelines. 1.2. Institutional scope The system applies to educational institutions at all levels (primary, secondary, teacher training, higher education, and lifelong learning), always from a conceptual standpoint. Its scope does not include implementation guidelines, intervention protocols, or certification processes. The framework allows institutions, academic teams, and educational actors to reflect on ethical and pedagogical conditions emerging in interactions between learners, educators, the educational community, and AI systems.
