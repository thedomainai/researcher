## Abstract

We present GAUI (Governed Agentic User Identity), an architectural framework for a human-principal personal-AI substrate. GAUI is the designated technical authority for representing and preserving the continuity state of a human principal within the GAUI architecture, subject to human sovereignty and applicable law. GAUI does not own the human's legal identity and does not determine legal personhood. The architecture is structured into four planes (Identity, Continuity, Authority, and Personal Intelligence / Memory) bound by a 22-law constitution and integrated with the BIGMAE execution substrate, the GIoA governed network, and (optionally) the COAGRE federation governance. The continuity model is eight-dimensional (Identity, Memory, Authority, Behavioural, Embodiment, Intent, Provenance, Constitutional) and produces a vector of six discrete verdicts rather than a lossy scalar percentage. The False-Continuity Rate (FCR < 0.01) is proposed as a flagship engineering acceptance target. The 22 constitutional laws include ten prohibited manipulation vectors and an anti-capture principle that prevents operational control from yielding authority.
