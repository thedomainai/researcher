# Data Marauders and Organised Data Warriors for Hire in the Liquid Order: From Historical Private Force to Algorithmic Neo-Contractors and the Rise of Contestable Security Constructors

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

This paper examines the emerging possibility of organised data marauders and data warriors for hire as a structural feature of the liquid order. Drawing on the analysis of trans-sovereign force, algorithmic neo-contractors, cyber-mercenaries and private sector offensive actors, it traces historical precedents of private violence and information operations through to contemporary zero-day markets, commercial spyware, narrative laundering and LLM data poisoning. These actors operate across strategic, infrastructural, algorithmic and cognitive domains, exploiting the inverse law of sovereign accountability. The paper connects this development to the rise of independent security constructors who offer Dual-Core compatible, contestable services rather than pure extraction. Within the Flow Sovereignty and Reserve of Humanity framework, such constructors can be integrated as peripheral agents whose morphisms remain auditable, while unconstrained data marauders constitute a cohomology threat. The analysis closes with architectural propositions for containing the former and constraining the latter under Permanent Systemic Instability. KSCCN article (3 pages).
