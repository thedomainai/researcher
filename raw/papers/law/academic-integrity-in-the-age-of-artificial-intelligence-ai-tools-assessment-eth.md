# Academic Integrity in the Age of Artificial Intelligence: AI Tools, Assessment Ethics, and Intellectual Property Rights

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

The rapid proliferation of generative artificial intel-ligence (AI) tools—particularly large language models such asChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude—has fundamentally disrupted thelandscape of academic integrity and assessment design in highereducation. This paper examines the current state of AI-drivenacademic misconduct, evaluates evolving institutional and globalpolicy responses, and investigates the critical intersections ofassessment ethics with intellectual property rights (IPR) in anentrepreneurship education context. Drawing on empirical studies(2024–2026), institutional policy analyses, and legal developments—including the U.S. Copyright Office’s landmark 2025 Report oncopyrightability of AI-generated works—this paper argues thataddressing AI-era academic dishonesty demands a paradigmshift: from punitive detection toward proactive pedagogicalredesign, ethical AI literacy, and equitable policy frameworks. Thepaper introduces the Authorship–Disclosure–Ownership (ADO)model and an Entrepreneurship AI Provenance Matrix as originalcontributions connecting academic integrity to the downstreamownership and entrepreneurial reuse of AI-assisted work. Thepaper concludes with actionable recommendations for educators,institutions, and policymakers
