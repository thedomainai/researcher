# Research and Practice on the Construction of a Lightweight AI-Enabled Welding Experimental Teaching and Safety Intelligent Management System

**著者**: Haifeng Yang, Gang Chen, Meirong Wang, Xiongyao Wang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーションズリサーチ

## Abstract

Aiming at the four prominent problems existing in current multi-process welding laboratories, including difficult supervision of teaching processes, high pressure on safety risk prevention and control, fragmented integration of teaching and management, and difficult implementation of intelligent transformation, this paper takes an eight-category multi-process, multi-equipment, and multi-scene welding experimental teaching system as the research object. Adopting the methods of literature research, field investigation, pilot verification and closed-loop optimization, this study constructs an integrated intelligent management system with in-depth integration of teaching and safety, covering the whole experimental process of pre-class, in-class and after-class stages supported by lightweight artificial intelligence (AI) technology. Lightweight AI capabilities are deeply embedded in four key links: standardization of teaching procedures, standardization of operational behaviors, evaluability of experimental quality, and early warning of safety risks, forming a low-cost, easy-to-implement, replicable and scalable intelligent management paradigm for engineering experiments. Practical results show that the proposed system significantly improves the standardization, proceduralization and objectivity of welding experimental teaching, strengthens the capabilities of pre-event early warning, rapid disposal and closed-loop rectification of safety risks, and effectively reduces human supervision omissions. It provides a referable practical path for the digital transformation of university experimental teaching and the modernization of laboratory governance capacity.
