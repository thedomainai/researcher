# From Participation to Profit: Examining Vendor Development in Darknet Markets

**著者**: Delali Major-Setugah
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーションズリサーチ

## Abstract

Prior research on darknet cryptomarkets has largely examined users who are already vendors, leaving questions about how they become vendors. Drawing on social learning theory to explain the transition, this study examines whether pre-vendor activities, like forum participation and network, predict the transition from forum user to vendor. Using linked forum and marketplace data from the Evolution cryptomarket (2014–2015), this study employs a series of logistic regression models to compare 682 users who later became vendors with 19,148 who did not. Users who posted more over shorter periods were more likely to transition. Beyond posting volume, posting in the vendor discussion, fraud discussion, and information subforums, along with starting topics or posting early in threads, predicted transition. Betweenness centrality also predicted transition beyond these behaviors (OR = 2.11). The findings are consistent with a social learning account of vendor emergence and suggest that future vendors are distinguishable before they begin selling.
