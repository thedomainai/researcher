## Abstract

The rapid development of digital government services has increased the demand for intelligent, adaptive, and reliable decision-making mechanisms. Traditional digital government infrastructures often face challenges related to dynamic resource allocation, network management, service quality, security, and decision-making under uncertain conditions. To address these challenges, this paper proposes an intelligent hybrid framework that integrates Artificial Intelligence (AI), Fuzzy Logic, and Software-Defined Networking (SDN) to improve adaptive decision-making in digital government environments. The proposed framework combines AI-based predictive analysis, fuzzy inference mechanisms, and centralized SDN network control to support intelligent resource management and dynamic service optimization. AI techniques enable the analysis of operational data and identification of changing service requirements, while Fuzzy Logic handles uncertainty and imprecise information in complex decision-making scenarios. Furthermore, SDN provides flexible network configuration, traffic management, and resource allocation capabilities.
