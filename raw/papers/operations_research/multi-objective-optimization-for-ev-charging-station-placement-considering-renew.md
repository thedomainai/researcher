# Multi-objective optimization for EV charging station placement considering renewable energy integration and grid load management

**著者**: Deepa Somasundaram, V. Pramila, G. Ezhilarasi, D. Lakshmi, Pazhanisamy Kavitha
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーションズリサーチ

## Abstract

The rapid acceleration of electric vehicle (EV) adoption has intensified the need for urban charging infrastructures that are cost-efficient, renewable-supported, and grid-resilient. This study proposes a multi-objective optimization framework for EV charging station (EVCS) placement that jointly considers infrastructure cost, solar energy potential, and grid load stability, addressing a gap where integrated renewable-grid constraints are rarely modeled together. The problem is formulated with spatial, policy, and uncertainty constraints and solved using NSGA-II and MOPSO, enabling a comparative assessment of convergence speed, Pareto diversity, and robustness under variable solar and grid conditions. Experiments conducted on realistic urban datasets incorporating hourly load curves, rooftop solar irradiance, and zoning restrictions show that NSGA-II achieves superior solution diversity (hypervolume = 0.82) and higher robustness, while MOPSO exhibits faster convergence but greater risk of premature stagnation. A sensitivity and scalability analysis demonstrates the framework’s applicability for large urban grids and renewable-integrated smart city planning. The results provide concrete guidance for policymakers, including optimal zoning for EVCS deployment, priority regions for solar-integrated stations, and strategies to minimize grid congestion while supporting national e-mobility and clean energy missions.
