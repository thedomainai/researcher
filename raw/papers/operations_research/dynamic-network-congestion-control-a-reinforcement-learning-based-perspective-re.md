# Dynamic Network Congestion Control: A Reinforcement Learning-based Perspective Review

**著者**: Ehsan Abedini, Mohsen Nickray, Amir Jalaly Bidgoly
**年**: 2027 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーションズリサーチ

## Abstract

Congestion control is a fundamental challenge in modern computer networks, particularly in the transport layer of the TCP/IP protocol stack. While traditional algorithms such as TCP Reno and CUBIC have provided foundational approaches, their adaptability to dynamic and complex network environments remains limited. Reinforcement learning (RL) offers a promising solution due to its adaptive, data-driven nature and ability to optimize performance through interaction with the environment. This study presents a comprehensive survey of RL-based congestion control algorithms, categorizing and comparing existing research across four key dimensions: 1. the RL algorithms used (e.g., Q-learning, Deep RL, POMDP); 2. the parameters defined as state, action, and reward; 3. the simulation platforms employed (e.g., NS-3, Pantheon, Mininet); and 4. performance outcomes. The analysis highlights dominant trends in algorithm selection, parameter usage, and implementation environments. Our findings confirm that RL-based approaches often outperform traditional methods in terms of throughput, delay, fairness, and adaptability. Finally, we outline the current research gaps and propose directions for future work in this evolving field.
