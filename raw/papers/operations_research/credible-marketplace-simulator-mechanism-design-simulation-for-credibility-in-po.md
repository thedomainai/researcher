## Abstract

Simulator, analysis code, YAML experiment configurations, raw outputs and figure-generation scripts supporting Credibility Trilemma in Polymatroidal Service Markets (Lovén, Gujar, Timperi, Mehmood, Donta, Tarkoma, Dustdar), submitted to ACM Transactions on Economics and Computation.This version corresponds to git tag v1.2-teac-2a in the Future-Computing-Group repository and is the artefact matching the submitted manuscript: it includes the single-panel Figure 2 regenerated to the Paper-2A scope and the experiment-to-manuscript mapping updated for the 2A/2B split. Earlier version v1.0-teac-r1 archived the pre-split state.
