# A Machine Learning Approach for Cassava Leaf Disease Identification Using Constraint-Regularized Neural Networks and AJFO Meta-Optimization

**著者**: K. L. Devi, K. V. D. Kiran
**年**: 2027 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーションズリサーチ

## Abstract

Early diagnosis of cassava leaf diseases is paramount to enhance the productivity of cassava and to support sustainable agriculture. Traditional deep learning methods, however, are not very robust in complex field conditions, suffer from manual hyperparameter tuning, and are not fully leveraging the structural information from the disease, which leads to poor classification accuracy and poor generalization. To cope with these challenges, this paper proposes a unified machine learning framework for cassava leaf disease identification, which combines with enhanced image preprocessing, Residual Multi-scale U-Net (RMUNet) segmentation, Multi-Level Feature Extraction (MLFE), Constraint-Regularized Neural Network (CRNN), and Artificial Jellyfish Optimization (AJFO). The proposed approach is different from the conventional RMUNet-based disease detection techniques, which involve disease-oriented multiscale segmentation along with structural regularization in feature space to enhance the classification resilience in the presence of illumination variations, background clutter, and noisy farming ground. Moreover, AJFO uses to tune the learning rate, batch size, dropout rate, training epochs, and regularization coefficient in an automatic way, which removes the manual tuning of these parameters and increases the stability of convergence. A computational complexity analysis also shows that the extra cost of optimization is only during offline training, and inference complexity is comparable to the conventional deep learning models. Experimental results on the Cassava Leaf Disease dataset show the effectiveness of the proposed framework with 99% classification accuracy for healthy leaves, 97% classification accuracy for Cassava Mosaic Disease (CMD), 95% classification accuracy for Cassava Green Mite (CGM), and 93% classification accuracy for Cassava Brown Streak Disease (CBSD) and consistently better precision, recall, and F1-score than CNN-PLDD, SVM-PLDD, and DENN-PLDD. Overall, the proposed integrated framework demonstrates its capability of achieving effective convergence, better generalization performance, and effective disease classification performance under different imaging conditions, which is considered as a strong solution for intelligent precision agriculture and automated crop health monitoring.
