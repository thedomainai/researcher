# AI for Agriculture in China: Research Progress, Practical Dilemmas and Optimization Paths

## Abstract

AI for Agriculture is a crucial pathway for advancing agricultural modernization and fostering new quality productive forces in the agriculture sector. Based on a systematic review of existing research progress in this field, this paper finds that AI has effectively improved agricultural production efficiency and industrial chain resilience through data-driven models and intelligent decision-making. The large-scale implementation of AI for Agriculture still faces multiple bottlenecks, including core technological shortcomings, inadequate data governance systems, high application costs, a shortage of interdisciplinary talents, and insufficient institutional guarantees. Accordingly, this paper proposes targeted optimization pathways from the perspectives of technological innovation, data governance improvement, application cost reduction, talent cultivation, and multi-party policy coordination.
