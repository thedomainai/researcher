# AI-Enhanced Nodal Analysis for Intelligent Production Optimization

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Making timely and effective decisions has always been a challenge, particularly in complex production environments. Conventional approaches, such as Nodal analysis, have been widely applied to address this matter. Although these methods can provide reliable outcomes, they often require considerable time and extensive manual effort. With the rapid growth in AI model development, this matter can be addressed not only to make good decisions but also to make optimal production decisions in the shortest time, with minimal human intervention. In this study, an integrated production system was developed to determine the optimal oil rate and support decision-making through evaluating different scenarios and selecting the best operational decision. The model incorporates the complete set of production system components, such as wells, reservoirs, and surface facilities, and employs AI-based optimization to enhance adjustment and validation. This approach minimizes human intervention, reducing computational time and increasing operational efficiency to achieve the final target of real-time wellhead adjustment according to optimal oil rate. Data processing was carried out using an automatic system focused on delivering the results in minimal time, while enabling hypothesis testing and validation. In the wells models, the obstacle of the lengthy matching process was addressed by reducing the process time and increasing the analysis of well behavior via sensitivity analysis for the parameters affecting flow rate. While the pressure depletion through time in the reservoir has been built and modified to match the historical data by automatically adjusting the reservoir parameters, these changes and improvements contributed to enhancing the ability of real-time wellhead adjustment to reach the desired oil rate with an error percentage less than 10%, indicating the ability of AI to achieve reliable data with the lowest time. The model has been optimized using a dynamic analysis mechanism that allows adaptation to the operational changes immediately. Also, improve the accuracy of selection of the optimal scenario through continuous updating of the variables, to decrease the error percentage and improve results by combining artificial intelligence techniques to enhance the efficiency and the speed of decision-making.
