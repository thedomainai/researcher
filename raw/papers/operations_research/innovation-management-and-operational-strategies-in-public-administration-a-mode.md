# Innovation management and operational strategies in public administration: A model for implementing artificial intelligence in strategic decision-making

**著者**: Mohsen Arman, Davood Feiz, Abbasali Rastgar, Fatemehzahra Tabrizian
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーションズリサーチ

## Abstract

Purpose: The aim of this research is to design a conceptual model for the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in strategic decision-making within Iranian governmental organizations.Methodology: This is an applied research study, and from the perspective of its objective, it is exploratory. The research method is qualitative, based on Grounded Theory, specifically the Strauss and Corbin approach. Data was collected through semi-structured interviews with 26 university professors and experienced managers from governmental organizations. Theoretical saturation was achieved after 16 interviews.Findings: The findings revealed that the core phenomenon, the integration of human leadership with AI, is influenced by causal factors such as a lack of technical skills, employee resistance, ethical concerns, insufficient quality data, high costs, and legal issues.Contextual factors including supportive regulations, high-quality data, specialized personnel, financial support, international collaboration, and advanced infrastructure, along with intervening conditions like management support, a culture of innovation, technical infrastructure, training, academic cooperation, and ethical frameworks, all facilitate the adoption of AI.The proposed strategies include developing ethical frameworks, training employees, establishing monitoring systems, investing in infrastructure, collaborating with universities and the private sector, and improving data management.The outcomes of this integration are improved decision-making quality, increased citizen satisfaction, reduced costs, enhanced transparency, faster crisis response times, and optimized resource allocation.Originality/Value: By presenting a localized and systematic model for applying Artificial Intelligence within Iran's bureaucratic structure, this research takes an innovative step towards digital governance. It can serve as a valuable reference for policymaking and transformation in public administration.
