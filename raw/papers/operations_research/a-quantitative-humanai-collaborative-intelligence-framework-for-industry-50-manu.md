## Abstract

This dataset supports the manuscript “A Quantitative Human–AI Collaborative Intelligence Framework for Industry 5.0 Manufacturing: Systematic Evidence Synthesis, Mathematical Modelling, Multi-Objective Optimization, and Numerical Validation.” It contains the numerical data and supporting documentation used in the literature-calibrated evaluation of the proposed Quantitative Human–AI Collaborative Intelligence Framework (QHAIF). The dataset includes literature-derived calibration anchors for AI reliability, trust, reliance, workload, and Human–AI interaction; reported QHAIF model parameters; steady-state simulation results across four AI-reliability regimes (R = 0.50, 0.70, 0.80, and 0.90); sensitivity-analysis results for the trust-gain, trust-damage, and trust-decay coefficients; Pareto-analysis ranges describing the trust–productivity trade-off; and machine-learning internal-consistency results for Random Forest, multilayer perceptron, and persistence-baseline models. The accompanying files also include the final numerical-analysis figures, a data dictionary, provenance information, and repository documentation. The QHAIF numerical outputs are model-generated simulation results and not measurements from human participants. Published empirical studies were used as calibration and interpretation anchors rather than as direct experimental validation of the simulated outputs. The available working files retain the reported summary values and final figures; original point-by-point numerical arrays underlying some time-series and surface plots were not retained, and this limitation is documented transparently in the repository.
