# Digital Twin Simulation with Real-Time Data Feedback and Reinforcement Learning

**著者**: Jincheng Zhang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーションズリサーチ

## Abstract

This paper presents a novel approach to digital twin simulation that integrates real-time data feedback and reinforcement learning (RL) to achieve dynamic and adaptive behavior. Traditional digital twin simulations often operate with static models, failing to account for the inherent variability and changes within physical assets. This research addresses this limitation by constructing a digital twin system that continuously receives data from the physical asset and employs RL algorithms to optimize its operational parameters. The core mechanism involves a closed-loop system where the digital twin predicts the asset's behavior, compares it with real-time data, and adjusts its parameters through an RL agent, thereby minimizing discrepancies and maximizing performance. The resulting system offers a more realistic and responsive simulation, suitable for applications requiring continuous optimization and adaptation, such as predictive maintenance, process control, and autonomous operation. The paper details the system architecture, the RL algorithm employed (specifically, a Deep Q-Network), and demonstrates its effectiveness through a conceptual example. The key contribution lies in the synergistic combination of these three elements – digital twins, real-time data integration, and reinforcement learning – to create a truly dynamic and self-improving simulation environment.
