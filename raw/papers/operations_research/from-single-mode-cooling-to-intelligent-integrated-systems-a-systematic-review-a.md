# From single-mode cooling to intelligent integrated systems: A systematic review and performance evaluation of Lithium-ion battery thermal management technologies

**著者**: Mengxiang Yuan, Can Chen, Kunyu Wang, Shuaiwei Wu, Zhixin Wu
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: オペレーションズリサーチ

## Abstract

Lithium-ion batteries are widely used in electric vehicles and energy storage systems, but their performance, lifetime, and safety are strongly affected by temperature. Effective battery thermal management systems (BTMS) are therefore essential for high-power operation, fast charging, and thermal runaway prevention. This review discusses BTMS from heat-generation mechanisms and thermal runaway behavior to cooling technologies, system integration, intelligent control, and performance evaluation. Six single cooling technologies are compared, including air cooling, indirect liquid cooling, direct liquid cooling, refrigerant direct cooling, phase change material cooling, and heat pipe cooling. Hybrid and integrated systems are further analyzed in terms of cooling performance, temperature uniformity, complexity, energy consumption, cost, and safety. Recent progress in machine learning, reinforcement learning, and digital twin methods is also evaluated, with attention to data requirements, computational cost, implementation feasibility, and generalization. Finally, future research directions are proposed for high-energy-density batteries, all-climate operation, and standardized BTMS evaluation. Lithium-ion batteries are central to transportation electrification and large-scale energy storage, yet their strong temperature sensitivity and catastrophic thermal runaway risk remain major barriers to safe global deployment. Effective battery thermal management systems (BTMS) are therefore essential for electric vehicles, grid energy storage, and fast-charging applications. Existing reviews still show fragmented coverage of cutting-edge cooling technologies, insufficient analysis of multi-component system integration, inadequate treatment of intelligent control strategies, and limited adaptability to next-generation high-energy-density batteries. To address these gaps, this paper reviews BTMS from heat-generation mechanisms and thermal runaway behavior to cooling technologies, system integration, and performance evaluation. Using the Bernardi heat generation model and the thermal runaway energy balance equation, we elucidate coupled heat generation mechanisms under normal operation and the four-stage thermal runaway evolution under abuse conditions, and we quantify key thermophysical and thermal runaway parameters. The review compares single cooling technologies, hybrid cooling systems, vehicle-level thermal integration, and intelligent control methods. We comprehensively analyze six mainstream technical routes, detailing their working principles, reported performance, advantages, and bottlenecks. We further summarize performance evaluation metrics in four dimensions (temperature characteristics, heat transfer and energy consumption, comprehensive performance, and safety and environmental impact) and compare representative results reported in previous studies. Finally, we identify key challenges in materials, system coordination, algorithm implementation, and industrial standardization, and we propose future directions for high-energy-density batteries, all-climate applications, and low-carbon energy systems. This review provides a structured basis for BTMS design, technology selection, and performance evaluation.
