# A Game-Theoretic Approach for Probabilistic Reactive Power Management with Flexibility and Stability Assessment

**著者**: Majid Najjarpour, Behrouz Tousi, A. Ebadi Zahedan
**年**: 2027 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーションズリサーチ

## Abstract

The increasing integration of renewable energy sources and the inherent uncertainties in power systems have made reactive power management a critical challenge for maintaining voltage stability and system flexibility. This study proposes an innovative probabilistic reactive power optimization method based on game theory for the IEEE 57-bus system. This approach considers uncertainties in both load demand and wind power generation while modeling interactions among network players, including generators, transformers, and reactive power compensators. Simulation results indicate that the proposed method significantly reduces network losses (~65 MW), efficiently allocates reactive power from compensators, and improves voltage stability and flexibility indices. Furthermore, the approach enables multi-objective analysis by evaluating the impact of wind power variability on network losses and the probability of constraint violations. These findings demonstrate that the proposed framework provides a robust and effective tool for intelligent reactive power management in modern power networks with high renewable penetration.
