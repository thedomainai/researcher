# An Adaptive Feature-Weighting Mechanism in Antlion Optimization for Enhanced Machine Learning Classification

**著者**: Firman Tempola, A. Musdholifah, Wahyono Wahyono
**年**: 2027 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーションズリサーチ

## Abstract

This study proposes a novel feature-weighted Antlion Optimization approach (FALO) to enhance machine learning performance in clove quality classification. Conventional models often suffer from redundant and low-importance features, as well as sensitivity to noisy attributes, leading to unstable classification accuracy. To address these limitations, the proposed method introduces an adaptive feature weighting mechanism within the Antlion Optimization framework, which improves the exploration–exploitation balance by dynamically reducing trap size and guiding the search toward elite antlion solutions. The effectiveness of FALO is evaluated using five machine learning classifiers, including k-Nearest Neighbor (k-NN), Naïve Bayes, Decision Tree, Support Vector Machine (SVM), and Multilayer Perceptron (MLP). Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method significantly improves classification performance for several models, achieving accuracy increases of 5.52%, 8.98%, and 1.38% for k-NN, Naïve Bayes, and Decision Tree, respectively, with a maximum accuracy of 96.54%. Statistical analysis using paired t-tests confirms the significance of these improvements. Although SVM and MLP do not exhibit significant performance gains, the proposed approach maintains stable performance, indicating its robustness across different classifiers. Overall, FALO provides an effective and reliable feature weighting strategy and demonstrates strong potential for optimization-driven machine learning applications in agricultural product quality assessment.
