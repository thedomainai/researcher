# AI-Driven Business Process Reengineering Platform for Urban Home-Based Businesses (UHBB) Using Social Media Analytics and Use-Case Driven System

**著者**: Divya Joshi
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーションズリサーチ

## Abstract

It is a well-known fact that India is an emerging economy; there are many small-scale businesses that exist in varying areas and they make a significant contribution to economic growth in the country. Such businesses offer services to people using different strategies and means but, most of them use conventional approaches. Urban home-based businesses (UHBBs) form one of the most significant parts of the informal economy but, they are facing specific difficulties in operations and inefficiencies in the working processes. In this paper, the researcher suggests an AI-based Business Process Reengineering (BPR) platform which adopts uninterrupted social-media analytics and auto-activated procedures, based on the procedures of artificial intelligence, such as Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Machine Learning (ML). It aims to make the business more flexible and engaged with its customers by optimizing data-driven processes. The literature review will identify deficiencies in the current BPR solutions applied to UHBBs, specifically in terms of adding social-media feedback. The proposed system, which is represented by use-case-driven design, would overcome these constraints as it allows checking in, iterating, and managing the process. This study also includes a comparative analysis table highlighting related works and research gaps. This novel framework enhances and enlightens substantial improvements in UHBB operational efficiency and responsiveness. basically, this paper sheds lights on proposed system which can give optimizes solution on business process based on customer feedback. Machine learning is the way where we can identify the loophole of process so change can be made on bases of that. This research concentrated on proposed system and use case analysis.
