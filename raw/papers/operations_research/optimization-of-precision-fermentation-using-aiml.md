## Abstract

Precision fermentation represents a frontier convergence of synthetic biology and process engineering, employing genetically modified microbial systems to biosynthesize tailored biomolecules—ranging from proteins, amino acids, and fatty acids to pigments, vitamins, and therapeutic intermediates. The integration of artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) has transformed this biotechnology by enabling advanced process optimization, self-adapting controls, and predictive analytics. This chapter presents a comprehensive overview of AI/ML-assisted strategies for enhancing fermentation performance through computational modeling, sensor integration, and data-driven decision frameworks. Multivariate analysis of multi-omics datasets and in situ bioreactor signals facilitates accurate prediction of metabolic fluxes, enzyme kinetics, and optimal nutrient utilization. The discussion extends to emerging methodologies such as digital twin systems that replicate microbial bioprocesses in virtual environments, reinforcement learning approaches that iteratively refine bioreactor control parameters, and surrogate models that accelerate simulation and scale-up design. Together, these technologies support real-time monitoring, adaptive error correction, and automated anomaly detection, effectively bridging the gap between laboratory precision and industrial reproducibility. The chapter further examines challenges, including algorithm generalization, model interpretability, data standardization, and bioprocess scalability, while highlighting the pivotal role of AI in ensuring resource-efficient and environmentally sustainable biomanufacturing. Ultimately, the integration of AI and ML redefines precision fermentation as an intelligent, predictive, and sustainable toolkit—laying the foundation for next-generation biosynthetic industries aligned with global green economy objectives.
