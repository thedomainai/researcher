# From 4.5 Hours to 30 Minutes: The Fully Integrated Digital Drilling Ecosystem Delivering 35% NPT Reduction

**著者**: S. Rashidi Aghdam, D. Ignatov
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーションズリサーチ

## Abstract

Abstract Worldwide drilling operations continue to face heavy financial and operational strain due to excessive non-productive time (NPT) and fragmented, inefficient workflows. With daily rig operating expenses typically ranging from $120,000 to $250,000, NPT often consumes 20–40% of total rig hours. Additional losses stem from manual reporting practices, limited retention of operational knowledge, slow access to performance insights, and poorly coordinated rig scheduling sequences. This study offers an in-depth review of an end-to-end digital transformation platform built to dismantle information silos, automate repetitive processes, deliver instantaneous data analytics, and enhance scheduling efficiency across fleets of multiple rigs. At its core, the platform unites four tightly integrated modules: An automated digital daily reporting system for supervisors that consolidates scattered contractor reports into one consistent, standardized daily summary;A centralized knowledge base for capturing, reviewing, approving, and quickly distributing lessons learned along with safety alerts and preventive strategies;Flexible, real-time analytics dashboards tailored for management, featuring fleet-level KPI tracking, user-defined visualizations, and on-demand performance benchmarking;A sophisticated dynamic scheduling tool that enables intuitive drag-and-drop resequencing, side-by-side version comparisons, and automatic synchronization with actual daily operational advancements. The evaluation relies on field-collected data from more than 50 wells (onshore and offshore) operated by five leading companies, supported by side-by-side before-and-after implementation analyses. All quantitative metrics were calculated in accordance with SPE and API guidelines for NPT quantification, report generation cycle times, rig standby durations, repeat incident frequencies, and rig efficiency rates. The assessment incorporates detailed rollout examples from a 15-rig operation, ROI projections grounded in an average well construction cost of $250,000, and rigorous verification of data quality (exceeding 95% accuracy through open API integration), spanning matched six-month intervals before and after deployment. Key findings highlight powerful, interconnected performance gains: Automation of reporting cut daily report preparation time by 85% reducing it from roughly 4.5 hours to about 30 minutes while raising data accuracy to 98% via enforced standardization and built-in validation rulesAverage NPT fell by 35% against pre-implementation reference levels, corresponding to estimated cost savings on the order of $12–13 million annually for a 10-rig fleet (based on average day rates of $120,000–$250,000)Systematic collection and sharing of lessons learned through the central database lowered the recurrence of similar incidents by 61%Real-time dashboards accelerated cross-rig and cross-company benchmarking by 95%Optimized scheduling lifted average rig utilization from 70% to 95%, resulting in estimated additional annual savings on the order of $4 million per 10-rig fleet through reduced standby and mobilization time. The combined improvements resulted in a first-year ROI exceeding 400% under the documented conditions, with projected annual cost reductions on the order of $20 million for a 10-rig fleet. By removing conventional data barriers, the platform architecture enables forward-looking planning, continuous real-time optimization, and organization-wide scalability all without compromising data reliability or usability. In contrast to most earlier SPE publications, which tend to assess isolated tools separately, this research measures the combined, multiplicative benefits arising from unified data ingestion, live analytics, persistent knowledge management, and adaptive scheduling. The field-proven outcomes supply operators with a reliable, evidence-based model for unlocking major productivity advances in today's intensely competitive drilling sector. Incorporating future enhancements such as predictive modeling and machine learning algorithms could drive NPT reductions and utilization increases well beyond 50%. This work introduces the first fully documented, field-verified ROI framework for a comprehensively integrated digital drilling ecosystem that combines automated reporting, centralized knowledge and safety management, real-time analytics interfaces, and intelligent dynamic scheduling. Departing from the single-component focus typical in prior SPE studies, it demonstrates synergistic results including average NPT reduction of 35% in matched before-and-after comparisons under comparable geological and operational conditions, 85% reduction in reporting effort, 61% drop in repeat incidents, and 450% first-year ROI realized through fluid data connectivity and open API synchronization across over 50 wells and multi-rig fleets. It also contributes valuable enterprise-scale performance indicators and real-world deployment guidance that have been largely missing from the current body of published literature.
