# AI-Driven Multi-Criteria Scoring Framework for Chronic Asset Underperformance Identification - From Data Silos to Decision Intelligence

**著者**: I. Mathur, P. Fleitas, N. Karvekar, J. Olvera, B. Hewitt
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーションズリサーチ

## Abstract

Oil and Gas operators face persistent challenges in identifying underperforming assets due to fragmented data across maintenance, inspection, and operations domains. This paper presents an AI-enabled underperforming asset management system. The core objective was to apply a robust, multi-criteria scoring mechanism that dynamically ranks and prioritizes underperforming assets identified during qualification delivering real-time asset performance visibility and enabling proactive, data-driven maintenance decision-making at scale. The system consolidates structured and unstructured data from multiple sources including CMMS maintenance records, inspection reports, process digital twins, predictive analytics results, and operational KPIs into a unified industrial data platform. Eight evaluation categories tailored to the asset performance are defined for analysis. Advanced algorithms were developed to analyze these KPIs and detect patterns indicative of asset degradation. A hybrid scoring methodology combines KPI normalization, multi-parameter correlation, and analytic hierarchy process weighting to generate a composite underperforming asset index. Results are delivered through continuously updated real-time dashboards that surface asset rankings and trigger threshold-based alerts. Implementation of the solution on the assets transformed underperforming asset identification from a manual and reactive process into an automated, proactive workflow completing in under five minutes - a ~99.98% reduction in cycle time representing a ~4000× improvement in speed. This step-change directly enables near real-time decision-making and significantly improves detection of emerging asset risks. By integrating previously siloed heterogeneous data sources, the solution enables continuous monitoring and near real-time equipment scoring. The underperforming asset index provides a ranked, multi-dimensional prioritization of assets, highlighting the key drivers of performance degradation across reliability, integrity, and operational dimensions. Operations and maintenance teams can now visualize degradation trends, detect early-stage failures, and diagnose root causes through consolidated insights derived from both structured KPIs and AI-extracted findings from unstructured reports. Furthermore, integration of predictive analytics and digital twin outputs enables anticipatory failure detection, supporting a measurable shift from corrective to condition-based and predictive maintenance strategies improving overall asset reliability, reducing unplanned downtime, and optimizing maintenance resource allocation across complex asset portfolios. This work introduces a scalable framework that unifies multi-source data integration, AI-driven analytics, and analytical hierarchy decision modelling into a continuously updated underperforming asset scoring system. The novelty lies in dynamically synthesizing heterogeneous operational, maintenance, and predictive datasets into a single, ranked performance index moving beyond static periodic reviews. This approach delivers enhanced operational visibility, accelerates data-driven intervention decisions, and provides a replicable methodology for underperforming asset prioritization across complex industrial asset portfolios.
