## Abstract

Objective/Scope The primary objective of this research is to demonstrate how autonomous gas-lift optimization—powered by Industrial Internet of Things (IoT), edge computing, and machine learning (ML)—can significantly enhance oil and gas production efficiency. The study aims to validate real-time, intelligent control systems can reduce manual intervention, improve safety, and deliver measurable cost and time savings, aligning with the broader goals of Production 4.0.
