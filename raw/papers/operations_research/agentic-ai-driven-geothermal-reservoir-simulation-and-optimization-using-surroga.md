# Agentic AI-Driven Geothermal Reservoir Simulation and Optimization Using Surrogate Modelling for Rapid Site Screening

**著者**: Mayur Pal, Abdul Rashid Abdul Nabi Memon, Vaibhav Valmik Khedekar
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーションズリサーチ

## Abstract

Abstract Geothermal energy is a prime key contributor to the global transition toward low-emission and sustainable energy systems. As a reliable baseload renewable resource with minimal greenhouse gas emissions, geothermal power generation offers continuous or uninterrupted energy supply. Thus, accurate prediction of subsurface temperature evolution is therefore fundamental to geothermal reservoir engineering, as temperature directly controls energy recovery efficiency, well placement, and long-term reservoir sustainability. Moreover, true prediction of thermal front involves use of conventional physics-based numerical simulators which are computationally intensive and time-consuming, limiting their applicability for large-scale site screening, uncertainty quantification, and decision-making under constrained project timelines. This study presents an innovative agentic artificial intelligence (AI) framework that autonomously performs high-fidelity geothermal reservoir simulation and multi-objective optimization by integrating physics-informed surrogate modeling with goal-directed AI agents. To address the high computational cost of traditional simulations, deep neural network–based surrogate models are trained on an ensemble of high-resolution thermal simulations of sedimentary geothermal systems. The proposed surrogates achieve prediction accuracies exceeding 99% for key performance indicators, including temperature drawdown, net power output, levelized cost of heat and electricity, and induced seismicity risk, while reducing computational time from hours to milliseconds. Furthermore, the proposed methodology represents a paradigm shift from conventional manual and trial-and-error–based geothermal assessment toward fully autonomous, uncertainty-aware, and real-time–capable site screening and design optimization. By enabling rapid evaluation of thousands of potential sites, the framework substantially accelerates project de-risking and supports data-driven decision-making for deep geothermal deployment in regions characterized by moderate enthalpy resources.
