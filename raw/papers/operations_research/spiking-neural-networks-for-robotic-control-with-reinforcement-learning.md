# Spiking Neural Networks for Robotic Control with Reinforcement Learning

**著者**: Jincheng Zhang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーションズリサーチ

## Abstract

This paper explores the application of Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs) combined with Reinforcement Learning (RL) for robotic control. Traditional artificial neural networks (ANNs) often struggle with the energy efficiency and temporal dynamics inherent in real-world robotic systems. SNNs, inspired by the biological nervous system, offer a potentially more efficient solution due to their event-driven nature and ability to operate with sparse spikes. This research investigates the feasibility of using SNNs to learn control policies from sensor inputs via RL, focusing on achieving real-time adaptation and minimizing energy consumption. The core mechanism involves a mapping layer within the SNN that translates sensory data into motor commands, while the RL algorithm optimizes the network's synaptic connections to maximize a reward signal. We present a framework for designing and training such systems, highlighting the advantages of this biologically-inspired approach for robotic control. The results demonstrate the potential of this combined approach to achieve robust and adaptive control performance, particularly in scenarios demanding low power operation.
