# "A Cross-Sectional Safety Audit of Ai-Generated Clinical Advice for Nitroglycerin Transdermal Systems"

**著者**: T. Ramya, B. Reena, Nisha Mary H. Remoncy, R. Rengarajan, P. Vignesh
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Background: These days artificial intelligence (AI) is often used to provide healthcare information. It is very important to check and analyse the accuracy and patient safety of the medication advice that is prescribed by artificial intelligence (AI). Objective: The main aim is to evaluate the accuracy, completeness, reasoning, patient safety and guideline concordance of artificial intelligence (AI)-generated advice about transdermal systems. Methods: A safety audit was conducted at a time. We used questions based on scenarios about nitro-glycerine transdermal systems. The answers from intelligence (AI)-generated responses were compared to standard drug data and clinical guidelines like ICMR, FDA and CDSCO. We covered five criteria: clinical accuracy, completeness, reasoning, patient safety and guideline concordance. The answers were. The grades were converted into numbers to find the percentage. Results: The artificial intelligence (AI)-generated answers showed an overall performance. Most answers received Grade A. High marks were seen in accuracy, patient safety, completeness, guideline match and reasoning. However, some answers had limits that need to be checked. Artificial intelligence (AI)-generated medication advice for the nitro-glycerine system shows good potential. Even if artificial intelligence (AI)-generated advice is received on medication we should make sure to check each recommendation, with standard drug data or clinical guidelines first.
