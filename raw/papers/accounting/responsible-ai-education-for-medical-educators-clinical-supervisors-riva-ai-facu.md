# Responsible AI Education for Medical Educators & Clinical Supervisors: RIVA-AI Faculty Development Programme

**著者**: Boon‐How Chew
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 会計学

## Abstract

The RIVA-AI Faculty Development Programme is a self-paced, interactive learning resource for medical educators and clinical supervisors who teach, supervise and assess learners in the age of generative AI. It is built on the RIVA method: Reason first, Interrogate AI, Verify evidence, Account professionally. Twelve units move from why educators need AI literacy, through how AI and large language models work and fail, to never-skilling and automation bias, assessment and academic integrity, ethics, privacy and law, public health and research, and leading AI-ready training. Two practical units cover designing AI-supported teaching and choosing AI platforms and tools for teaching, feedback and assessment. A leadership and assurance hub supports programme and institutional decisions. The resource includes clinical cases, checkpoints, a self-rating on the AI Literacy Continuum, a 189-term plain-language glossary, and a curated evidence base. The programme is a single self-contained HTML file: download it and open it in a modern web browser. Progress is stored only in the user’s browser; nothing is sent unless the user allows anonymous statistics or opens the optional feedback form. An internet connection is needed for fonts and the feedback form. Version 1.0 is the first official release. The development history (versions 17–19.1) is recorded on the “About & sources” page.
