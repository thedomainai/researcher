# BUILDING TRUST THROUGH QUALITY INFRASTRUCTURE: ACCREDITATION IN THE AGE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

**著者**: Sonja Ćirić, Ana Rakić, Biljana Tošić
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 会計学

## Abstract

Considering the significance of Artificial Intelligence (AI) for ensuring reliability, traceability, and trust in the digital society, this study aims at exploring the key role of AI in Quality Infrastructure (QI), especially the role of AI within the accreditation process, and the role of the accreditation process within AI. The study explores the dual role of AI, especially AI as a process tool for transforming the operations of Conformity Assessment Bodies (CABs) and AI as an object of accreditation and conformity assessment. Compared to the traditional accreditation process, which usually relied on periodic retrospective inspections of static and deterministic systems, the contemporary AI-transformed accreditation process requires an entirely novel approach due to its operational autonomy, adaptability, and non-linear decision-making process. From an Operations Research (OR) perspective, these challenges can be framed as decision-support problems involving risk-based prioritization of activities, allocation of resources, scheduling of assessments, etc. Recent developments in AI auditing and trustworthy AI demonstrate the potential of multi-criteria decision-making, fuzzy decision models, optimization, and risk-based approaches to support such decisions. The study identifies the integration of AI, accreditation, and OR as a promising direction for developing efficient, risk-based, and transparent decision-support frameworks for the continuous assessment of AI systems.
