## Abstract

Sustainable soil management is fundamental to ensuring global food security, maintaining biodiversity, and mitigating climate change. However, soil health is constantly challenged by factors such as intensive agriculture, climate variability, and soil degradation. Traditional soil management approaches, while effective in many contexts, often fail to account for the complex and dynamic interactions that govern soil ecosystems. The advent of artificial intelligence (AI) has opened new frontiers in sustainable soil management by integrating vast datasets, optimizing decision-making processes, and enabling predictive modeling for improved agricultural practices. AI-driven techniques, including machine learning, deep learning, and data-driven decision-support systems, are reshaping the way soil resources are monitored, analyzed, and managed ( Zhang et al., 2022 ).
