# Tenet-Bound AI: A Complete Architecture for Machine Ethics

**著者**: George Charles
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 会計学

## Abstract

This paper specifies an ethical architecture for artificial intelligence derived from a single foundational axiom rather than stipulated as a list of rules. The architecture — Tenet-Bound AI (TBAI) — is axiomatic-deontological with a meta-deontic update mechanism: the Axiom of Liberty grounds it, the Critical-Rational Ethical Tenets express it, and Domain T9 governs how the system reasons about its own application and revises itself under public examination. The paper distinguishes stipulated from derived ethical frameworks, classifies existing AI governance approaches accordingly, and presents the installation procedure, the runtime processing pipeline (primer detection, Tenet adjudication, Axiom review), the governance structure for invocations of the Axiom's exception clause, the four-step public audit protocol, and the self-reporting architecture that together constitute the proposed alignment posture. A philosophical sidebar relates the Hameroff–Penrose Orchestrated Objective Reduction conjecture to A2's functional definition of personhood as a falsifiable bridge between this framework and the consciousness literature. The framework is released into the permanent public domain.
