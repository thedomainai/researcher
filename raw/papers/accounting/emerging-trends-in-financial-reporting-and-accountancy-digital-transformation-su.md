# Emerging Trends in Financial Reporting and Accountancy: Digital Transformation, Sustainability, and Regulatory Dynamics

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Abstract The landscape of accountancy and financial reporting has undergone a monumental shift driven by rapid technological advancements, evolving regulatory frameworks, and increasing demand for corporate transparency. Traditional financial reporting models are shifting toward real-time data access, automated audit trails, and integrated non-financial disclosures. This research paper examines the convergence of automation, Artificial Intelligence (AI), and Sustainable Financial Reporting (ESG disclosures) within modern accounting practices. It highlights key challenges faced by organizations during implementation and presents a prospective framework for accounting professionals to navigate digital disruption while maintaining reporting accuracy, transparency, and compliance.
