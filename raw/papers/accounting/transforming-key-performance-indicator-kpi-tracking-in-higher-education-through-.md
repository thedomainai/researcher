# TRANSFORMING KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATOR (KPI) TRACKING IN HIGHER EDUCATION THROUGH ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND MACHINE LEARNING: THE APPLICATION OF THE PREMIER SCORE INDEX (PSI) IN HIGHER EDUCATION

**著者**: Mohamad Rosni Othman, Nor Laili‐Azua Jamari
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 会計学

## Abstract

The rapid digital transformation in higher education has exposed a critical flaw in institutional governance: traditional Key Performance Indicator (KPI) tracking methods that reliant on manual data entry, siloed systems, and static reports are increasingly obsolete and lack the precision, adaptability, and responsiveness needed in today's data-intensive environments. To address this pressing need for data-driven evaluation tools, Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) are fundamentally transforming performance management by introducing real-time analytics, strategic alignment, and Data-informed decision-making. This study explores the development and application of the Premier Score Index (PSI), a novel, weighted composite metric assessing institutional performance across five strategic domains: Teaching, Research, Community Service, Professional Service, and Academic Reputation. Through a systematic literature review and bibliometric analysis from 2010 to 2024, the paper identifies how AI/MLenhance KPI tracking across its lifecycle: development, monitoring, and reporting. Findings reveal the potential of AI-driven KPI systems, such as the PSI, to revolutionize institutional planning and accountability, positioning academic institutions to thrive in a rapidly evolving digitallandscape.
