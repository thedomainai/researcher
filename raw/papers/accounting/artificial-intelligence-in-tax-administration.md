# Artificial Intelligence in Tax Administration

**著者**: K. Balaji
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 会計学

## Abstract

The rapid digital transformation of public finance has positioned Artificial Intelligence (AI) as a critical tool in modern tax administration.This chapter examines the transformative role of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in tax administration through a comparative case study of the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) in the United States, Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs (HMRC) in the United Kingdom, and the Australian Taxation Office (ATO).The study adopts a qualitative comparative research design using secondary data from official reports, policy documents, and OECD publications. Analytical tools include comparative framework analysis, thematic analysis, and cross-case evaluation to identify patterns and divergences in AI adoption.The findings reveal that AI significantly enhances fraud detection, enables real-time data processing, and supports risk-based auditing, thereby improving voluntary compliance and administrative efficiency. The chapter contributes to the literature by integrating tax policy, digital governance, and accounting information systems within an AI-driven framework
