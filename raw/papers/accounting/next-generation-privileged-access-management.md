# Next-generation privileged access management

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Privileged Access Management (PAM) has become an essential component of enterprise cybersecurity. A PAM system functions as a critical safeguard for protecting high-value accounts that introduce a significant level of risk when compromised. As modern infrastructures expand across cloud, hybrid, and distributed environments, traditional rule-based PAM approaches increasingly struggle to detect sophisticated threats, adapt to rapidly changing access patterns, and reduce administrative overhead. Recent developments in Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) offer a transformative opportunity to enhance PAM systems by enabling behavioral baselining, real-time anomaly detection, adaptive risk scoring, and intelligent automation of access decisions. Therefore, the main goal of this chapter is to present a comprehensive analysis of AI-driven PAM. For this purpose, the study establishes a conceptual foundation to outline the core PAM functions and the motivations for integrating AI-driven capabilities. Moreover, current academic and industrial advancements are examined to highlight the state-of-the-art techniques, such as ML-based anomaly detection and Large Language Model (LLM)-enabled policy analysis. Additionally, a structured SWOT analysis is conducted to evaluate the integration of AI within PAM infrastructures. The chapter further identifies open challenges, including explainability, adversarial robustness, data security, and regulatory constraints that must be resolved to enable the secure and responsible deployment of AI-enabled PAM systems. Finally, future directions are discussed to provide a guide for the research path toward autonomous, predictive, and context-aware PAM architectures. Consequently, the chapter aims to provide a strong foundation for researchers, practitioners, and product leaders seeking to advance the next-generation of AI-driven privileged access security.
