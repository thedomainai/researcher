# De-Risking Deepwater Gas Assets: An AI-Enabled Ensemble Framework for Probabilistic Asset Management.

**著者**: M. Bansal, R. Jain, M. Bandyopadhyay, U. Shankar, B. Deka
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 会計学

## Abstract

Abstract Asset management and production forecasting are critical for de-risking deep-water gas well operations, where subsurface uncertainty and limited data availability make decision-making challenging. To account for subsurface uncertainties and support effective de-risking in offshore asset development, a probabilistic modelling framework ‘Unified Ensemble Modelling’ was implemented. The realizations were built on Ensemble Kalman Filter Smoother (En-KF-S), and includes scope of implementing AI and specialized ML algorithms to automate complex subsurface workflows and reconcile patterns with subsurface models. This approach constructs an ensemble of equiprobable reservoir models that represent the unknown subsurface through probability distributions rather than a single deterministic interpretation. Multiple modelling ranges were incorporated to reflect key geological and dynamic uncertainty, including facies distribution, rock property variation, saturation modelling, and dynamic uncertainty parameters. The Gaussian process refined and updated geological concepts based on variogram ranges, to perform simultaneous conditioning of static and dynamic data. The initial ensemble sets were history matched to field data, to match the defined objective function, model uncertainty parameters, localization setup and scalar impacting different parts of objective function. By capturing ~100 realizations, the ensemble algorithms allowed for iterations and feature updates, significantly faster than simulations methods. The sparse regression also helped in identifying the most influential parameters affecting the asset performance. The method enabled managers to systematically evaluate project risks, make more robust reservoir development decisions and reduce uncertainty at the asset level. By effectively capturing and propagating uncertainty throughout the modelling and data assimilation process, the ensemble-based workflow proved successful in supporting offshore development by enhancing forecasting reliability, reducing subsurface risk, and enabling more informed and adaptive asset management decisions. Traditional prediction approaches suffer from workflow repetition, long processing times, and limited predictive accuracy, which increases operational risk when applied to data-sparse deep-water assets. In data-driven environments, unified ensemble-based modelling approach offers novel and quick optimization framework, by combining diverse predictors to enhance production forecasting and risk management. This Asset Management technique outperforms conventional workflows by applying advanced data analytics and real-time updates, essential for understanding gas field dynamics that guide offshore development strategies.
