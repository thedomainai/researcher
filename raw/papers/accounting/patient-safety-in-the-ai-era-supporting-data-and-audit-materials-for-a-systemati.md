# Patient Safety in the AI Era: Supporting Data and Audit Materials for a Systematic Review of Clinical Artificial Intelligence Across the Care Pathway

**著者**: Ekaterina Kldiashvili
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

This dataset contains supporting materials for the systematic review “Patient Safety in the AI Era: A Systematic Review of Clinical Artificial Intelligence Across the Care Pathway.” The review examined patient-safety evidence across clinical applications of artificial intelligence, including diagnostic and predictive systems, human–AI interaction, generative AI, patient-facing communication, workflow integration, and postdeployment monitoring. The final evidence corpus comprises 1,759 independent studies and 1,778 extraction components. The deposited materials document the review process and include the report-level eligibility dataset, report-to-study linkage and denominator-reconciliation files, search-update controls, appraisal and source-limited evidence handling, final cross-study synthesis, and PRISMA/search audit. The evidence was organized using an evidence-directness classification developed specifically for this review to distinguish direct patient or care-pathway outcomes from clinical-performance, human-factors/mechanistic, and implementation/workflow evidence. This classification was used to control the scope of inference and should not be interpreted as a validated quality or certainty scale.
