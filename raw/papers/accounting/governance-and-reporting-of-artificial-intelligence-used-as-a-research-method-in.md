# Governance and reporting of artificial intelligence used as a research method in suicide research: an AI-coded cross-sectional meta-research audit

**著者**: Matias Gay
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 会計学

## Abstract

This study is a cross-sectional meta-research audit of published empirical suicide and self-harm research in which qualifying artificial intelligence performs a substantive research function. The publication corpus is the object of study. The audit will evaluate how eligible studies report ethics oversight, consent and data-use governance, model identification and versioning, study-specific human verification of AI outputs, privacy and data governance, and applicable individual risk-management procedures. Eligibility is structured using an adapted Population/Corpus, Concept, Context framework and a prespecified R0/R2 distinction between AI used as a research method and AI used solely as the object of technical evaluation. The publication eligibility window is fixed at 1 January 2020 through 22 August 2026. The production database searches will be executed only after preregistration.
