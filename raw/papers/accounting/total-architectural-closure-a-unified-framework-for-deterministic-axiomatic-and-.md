## Abstract

This paper introduces Total Architectural Closure (TAC), a unified framework for engineering deterministic, axiomatic, and machine-verifiable artificial intelligence. It presents an integrated architecture combining Russell Recursive Refinement (R³), Meta-Dynamical Mathematics (MDM), WAD-18 integer-exact computation, executable invariants, cryptographically chained state records, and reproducibility capsules. The framework examines how architectural constraints, ontological grounding, deterministic execution, and independently replayable verification can work together to make AI systems more transparent, auditable, and accountable. It develops a formal approach to governing system state transitions, validating computational behavior, and preserving evidence across recursive refinement. Through architectural definitions, verification structures, and implementation case studies, the paper proposes Total Architectural Closure as an engineering paradigm for building advanced intelligent systems whose operations can be examined against explicit, executable criteria.
