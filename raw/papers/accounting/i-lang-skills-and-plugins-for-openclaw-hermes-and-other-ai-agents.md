# I-Lang skills and plugins for OpenClaw, Hermes and other AI agents

**著者**: Zhu Longquan
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 会計学

## Abstract

Ten instruction-only skills and two OpenClaw plugins written in the I-Lang protocol. The skills are plain text with no dependencies and work in any agent that loads a SKILL.md: text de-AI rewriting in four languages, Chinese public-account writing, dead-site recovery, prompt rewriting and compression, product research and SEO automation for cross-border sellers, and a US class-action settlement tracker. The plugins register tools and hooks inside the OpenClaw runtime.
