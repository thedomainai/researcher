# The Mediating Effect of Ethical Leadership on Organizational Performance of the Nigerian Industrial Landscape

**著者**: Aneozeng Awo Egbe
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 会計学

## Abstract

Effective leadership is a major determinant of organization success and one very crucial ingredient that makes leadership effective is ethical consideration and adherence in leadership. Therefore ethical leadership when appropriately established in an organization, leadership would experience enhanced accountability, empathy, transparency and ethical guidance. Conceptual and empirical reviews were made, as well as theoretical framework established. These reviews provides a framework and analytical insights into the state of the Nigerian Industrial Landscape using evidence from Larfarge Africa PLc, in Calabar, Nigeria. Conclusion and recommendations were advanced thereto.
