# Datasets: AI and NLP Approaches for Automating Data Extraction in Systematic Literature Reviews — extraction corpus and quality scores (144 studies, 2020 to June 2026)

**著者**: Aris Tsakpinis, Christian Wolff
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 会計学

## Abstract

Extraction dataset and quality-assessment scores for the systematic review of AI/NLP data-extraction automation (144 included studies, 2020 to June 2026). S1a: bibliographic and coding fields; S1b: models, evaluation, findings, limitations; S2: per-study quality scores; S3: the screening protocol with the per-record screening decisions; S4: the accounting of records removed before screening. Includes the codebook and the full database search-query strings. Records keyed by Corpus ID.
