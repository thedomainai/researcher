# AI-Enabled Perception, Decision-Making, and Trust Management

**著者**: Jialong Zhang, Yi He, Pu Zhang, Xiangke Wang, Jianan Zong
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 会計学

## Abstract

Researchers have studied the problem of formation reconfiguration for UAV swarms under failure conditions. However, while the proposed formation reconfiguration control schemes can ensure that the relative distance error of the UAV swarm converges to zero and meets formation maintenance requirements, they have not yet accounted for the impact of unknown wind fields on close-range, high-speed swarm formations. The effects of wind fields are primarily manifested in the fact that both their magnitude and direction cause changes in the aerodynamic forces and torques acting on the UAVs, which in turn lead to changes in the sideslip angle and angle of attack. This may result in collisions between the UAVs and ultimately prevent the maintenance of the desired formation. Therefore, in unknown wind field environments, ensuring that close-range, high-speed UAV formations can resist wind disturbances and reach a stable state within a limited time presents a significant challenge in the design of swarm cooperative control laws.
