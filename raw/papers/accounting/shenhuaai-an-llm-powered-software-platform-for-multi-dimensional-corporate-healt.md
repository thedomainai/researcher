# ShenhuaAI: An LLM-powered software platform for multi-dimensional corporate health assessment in FinTech analytics

**著者**: Ruiyuan Shi, Wen Zhu
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 会計学

## Abstract

We present ShenhuaAI (v1.0.0), an open-source software platform that integrates a locally deployed Gemma 3 27B large language model (LLM) with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), fuzzy set theory, and graph-based path analysis to assess corporate health (CH) across seven organizational subsystems. Grounded in an empirical study of 788 listed companies in China's Greater Bay Area (2023 fiscal year), the platform combines 34 quantitative indicators drawn from the Wind financial database with LLM-generated qualitative scores for strategic indicators that resist purely numerical measurement. ShenhuaAI correctly identifies 86.7% of high-risk (ST/*ST) enterprises and achieves an R² of 0.647 in a regression model capturing direct, mediation, and interaction effects. The codebase is publicly available under Apache 2.0 and ships with reproducible evaluation harnesses and Docker deployment recipes.
