# The Role of Artificial Intelligence in Accounting of Small Business Enterprises: An Empirical Study of Davanagere District, Karnataka

**著者**: Venkatesh Babu S
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 会計学

## Abstract

The rapid advancement of Artificial Intelligence (AI) has significantly transformed business operations worldwide. Accounting, being one of the most information-intensive functions, has witnessed remarkable changes through AI-enabled technologies such as machine learning, robotic process automation, predictive analytics, intelligent bookkeeping, and cloud-based accounting systems. Small Business Enterprises (SBEs), which constitute a major component of India's economic structure, are increasingly adopting AI tools to improve accounting efficiency and decision-making processes. The present study investigates the role of Artificial Intelligence in accounting practices among small business enterprises in Davanagere District, Karnataka. Primary data were collected from 100 small businesses operating in retail, manufacturing, service, hospitality, trading, and agro-based sectors. The study examines AI adoption levels, benefits derived from AI-based accounting systems, challenges encountered during implementation, and the impact on accounting performance. Statistical techniques including percentage analysis, mean score analysis, correlation, Chi-square test, and ANOVA were used to analyze the data. The findings reveal that AI significantly improves accounting accuracy, reduces processing time, enhances compliance, and supports strategic financial decision-making. The study further identifies technological awareness, infrastructure availability, and managerial attitude as key determinants of AI adoption among small enterprises.
