# On the Classification of Topological Field Theories

**著者**: Jacob Lurie
**年**: 2008 | **被引用数**: 567
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 数理物理学

## Abstract

Our goal in this article is to give an expository account of some recent work on the classification of topological field theories. More specifically, we will outline the proof of a version of the cobordism hypothesis conjectured by Baez and Dolan.
