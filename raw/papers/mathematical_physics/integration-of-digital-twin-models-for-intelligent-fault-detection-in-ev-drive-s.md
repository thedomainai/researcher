# Integration of digital twin models for intelligent fault detection in EV drive systems

**著者**: Donadi Ashok Kumar, Karthikeyan Dhandapani
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 数理物理学

## Abstract

The adoption of digital twin technology is on the rise for predictive maintenance and health monitoring in electric vehicles, facilitating real-time fault diagnosis, system optimization, and informed decision-making. This study offers an in-depth assessment of the latest developments in digital twin architectures aimed at fault detection within electric drive systems, classifying current methodologies into model-based, data-driven, and hybrid frameworks. The investigation centers on the diagnostic functionalities pertaining to frequently observed faults, such as inverter malfunctions, motor winding anomalies, and bearing deterioration. This study examines the integration of digital twins with enabling technologies, such as the internet of things (IoT), edge and cloud computing, and artificial intelligence and machine learning techniques, to enhance real-time execution and predictive capabilities. Significant challenges concerning computational efficiency, interoperability, and model fidelity have been recognized as critical research hurdles. This study emphasizes existing research deficiencies and proposes future pathways, intending to assist researchers and practitioners in creating scalable and intelligent diagnostic solutions for advanced electrified transportation systems.
