# Improved Experimental Test of Detailed Balance and Time Reversibility in the Reactions Al27+p⇆Mg24+α

**著者**: E. Blanke, H. Driller, W. Glöckle, H. Genz, A. Richter (+1)
**年**: 1983 | **被引用数**: 105
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 数理物理学

## Abstract

A new test of the principle of detailed balance in the nuclear reactions $^{27}\mathrm{Al}(p,{\ensuremath{\alpha}}_{0})^{24}\mathrm{Mg}$ and $^{24}\mathrm{Mg}(\ensuremath{\alpha},{p}_{0})^{27}\mathrm{Al}$ at bombarding energies $7.3 \mathrm{MeV}<~{E}_{p}<~7.7 \mathrm{MeV}$ and $10.1 \mathrm{MeV}<~{E}_{\ensuremath{\alpha}}<~10.5 \mathrm{MeV}$, respectively, is reported. Measured relative differential cross sections agree within the experimental uncertainty $\ensuremath{\Delta}=\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{} 0.51%$ and hence are consistent with time-reversal invariance. From this result an upper limit $\ensuremath{\xi}<~5\ifmmode\times\else\texttimes\fi{}{10}^{\ensuremath{-}4}$ (80% confidence) is derived for a possible time-reversal-noninvariant amplitude in the reaction.
