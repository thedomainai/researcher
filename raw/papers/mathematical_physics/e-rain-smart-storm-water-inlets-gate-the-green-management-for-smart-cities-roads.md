# E-Rain Smart Storm Water Inlets Gate The GREEN MANAGEMENT for Smart Cities Roads Drain Rain System

**著者**: Khaled Elnems
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 数理物理学

## Abstract

The E-Rain Smart Gate is a pioneering innovation in the newly defined "Civilectronics" category, integrating civil, electronic, and systems engineering to revolutionize urban drainage. Designed specifically for smart cities and arid environments like the Gulf region, this fully automated system addresses the critical challenges of infrastructure maintenance and street flooding caused by sand, dust, and debris accumulation. The system utilizes advanced rain and water sensors to automatically open inlet gates during rainfall and close them during dry periods to prevent blockages. A key technical feature is the inclusion of distance sensors that electronically measure sand accumulation within the inlets. This data allows utility providers to use specialized software to pinpoint exact maintenance needs, eliminating human error and the need for manual inspections. By integrating with GIS and CMMS, the system enables real-time asset management and predictive maintenance. Economically, the E-Rain system is a "Technology Enabler" within the Industry 4.0 framework. It reduces periodic maintenance costs for municipalities by over 65% and ensures 100% operational efficiency of the drainage network. To ensure financial feasibility, the project utilizes a strategic deployment model focusing on "Wet Spots"—urban zones prone to water accumulation—and offers tiered hardware solutions ranging from USB-connected devices to fully autonomous gates. The efficacy of this "Green Management" solution has been validated through a successful prototype in Shakhbout City, Abu Dhabi. Its technical excellence is further recognized by over 70 international innovation medals from 30 different countries, marking it as a sustainable solution for enhancing public safety and infrastructure longevity.
