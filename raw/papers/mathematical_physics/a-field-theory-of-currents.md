# A Field Theory of Currents

**著者**: Hirotaka Sugawara
**年**: 1968 | **被引用数**: 542
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 数理物理学

## Abstract

A new type of model field theory is constructed. Only currents appear as the coordinates. The canonical formalism is abandoned. The self-consistency, Lorentz covariance, energy-momentum conservation, etc., are checked.
