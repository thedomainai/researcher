# Political Restrictions on Human Rights in Iran: English Studies as a Case Study

**著者**: Aviva Pinchas
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 数理物理学

## Abstract

This article examines political restrictions on human rights in the Islamic Republic of Iran through the lens of English studies, using it as a case study. Drawing on modernization and democratization theory alongside empirical evidence of state control over knowledge production, the analysis reveals how the Iranian state restricts freedom of expression, academic freedom, and freedom of association within English language education—in school curricula, university departments, and private language institutes. The article argues that the regime’s intensive efforts to discipline English studies as an ideological and securitized field reveal both the depth of political control and the regime’s awareness of the democratizing potential of global linguistic and cultural linkages. Despite pervasive restrictions, English studies retain significant potential to contribute to processes of political modernization, as English-proficient youth use linguistic resources to access censored knowledge, build transnational ties, and frame local struggles in universalistic human rights terms. The case of Iran illuminates the tensions inherent in “modernization without democratization”. It demonstrates that the human rights situation cannot be understood without considering the politics of language, knowledge, and academic freedom.
