# Gauging Spatial Symmetries and the Classification of Topological Crystalline Phases

**年**: 2018 | **被引用数**: 306
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 数理物理学

## Abstract

Crystalline topological phases are phases of matter described by the interaction between quantum entanglement within a solid and the crystal symmetries that are present. A new analysis offers, for the first time, a systematic theoretical framework for describing these phases.
