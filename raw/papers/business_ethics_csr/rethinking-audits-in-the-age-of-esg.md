# Rethinking audits in the age of ESG

**著者**: Divya Soni, Amrinder Singh
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経営倫理・CSR

## Abstract

Purpose This study aims to systematically examine the core themes and boundaries of Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) assurance, identifying prevailing research hotspots and dominant scholarly conversations. Furthermore, this study seeks to uncover emerging areas and critical gaps that offer opportunities for future research to address current and evolving challenges in ESG assurance. Design/methodology/approach Based on 148 peer-reviewed articles (after rigorous screening), the authors use bibliometric techniques, including network analysis, clustering and co-citation analysis, to synthesise the field’s evolution and reveal underexplored areas. Findings The review identifies five priority directions for future inquiry: the professionalism and evolving role of ESG assurers, ESG assurance quality and firm performance, the influence of governance mechanisms and stakeholder pressures, the implications of Audit 4.0 technologies (e.g. artificial intelligence, blockchain and data analytics) for assurance legitimacy and impact of agentic AI on assurance providers and ESG reporting readability and its consequences for assurance outcomes. By highlighting persistent tensions, contradictions and unresolved debates, this study reconceptualises ESG assurance as an evolving, multidisciplinary domain. Research limitations/implications This study may fully rely on Scopus, which limits the scope by potentially excluding relevant studies from other databases. Further, this paper addresses the key challenges found by International Federation of Accountants (IFAC, 2021), in determining the scope of an assurance engagement, exercising professional scepticism and judgment and obtaining the necessary competency and skills. Practical implications This study advances the emerging field of ESG assurance by identifying underexplored research areas, highlighting key research gaps and outlining promising directions for future inquiry. Beyond mapping the existing literature, this study also discusses the possible theoretical contributions that can strengthen the conceptual development of this domain. The review highlights key practical issues, including the role of governance, AI adoption and assurer professionalism in enhancing the credibility and transparency of ESG assurance. This study offers useful insights for researchers, practitioners, standard setters and regulators aiming to strengthen the quality and reliability of ESG assurance. Social implications This study’s insights can enhance transparency and accountability in ESG reporting, fostering trust among stakeholders such as investors and regulators. Improving ESG assurance practices encourages companies to adopt more responsible and sustainable approaches, benefiting society at large. Originality/value To the best of the authors’ knowledge, this study is among the first multi-disciplinary systematic literature reviews to study potential future avenues in the ESG assurance area with a holistic view. This study introduces diverse significant areas to meet current challenges in ESG assurance practices.
