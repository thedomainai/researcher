# Digital Ecosystems and ESG-Driven Leadership

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Digital transformation and ESG expectations are reshaping leadership in the creative economy. This chapter examines digital business ecosystems as governance structures that connect organisations, platforms, technologies and stakeholders to support responsible innovation and sustainable value creation. It argues that digital ecosystems are not merely operational tools, but integrated systems that strengthen ESG transparency, accountability, coordination and stakeholder trust. Using a multi-theoretical approach, the chapter develops a conceptual framework linking digital ecosystem maturity, ESG leadership capability and creative economy outcomes such as performance, innovation, social impact and human-centred value creation. It highlights the role of ethical AI, blockchain, data governance and inclusive ecosystem design in enabling verifiable ESG commitments. The chapter offers insights for executives, policymakers and researchers seeking to align digital strategy with sustainability.
