# Business Ethics and Governance in Digital Transformation: Protecting Human Agency Through Inclusive Corporate Policies

**著者**: Palupi Pratiwi, Dewi Risyantika, Shalahudin Habibullah, Ruslan Abdul Ghofur, Hanif Hanif
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経営倫理・CSR

## Abstract

Corporate digital governance often fails to mitigate the social impact of digitalization due to short-term profit optimization, triggering AI black box phenomena that marginalize digital workers' welfare. This study explores the integration of ethical dimensions into corporate governance frameworks to safeguard stakeholder rights. Employing a qualitative literature review approach (2019–2026), this research analyzes technology corporations' accountability regarding automated work systems. The findings reveal that current platform governance prioritizes financial gains over social equity, leading to algorithmic opacity and worker vulnerability in Indonesia. Consequently, this research concludes that algorithmic transparency must become an integral part of corporate compliance. The study recommends mandating boards of directors to implement periodic ethical audits on artificial intelligence systems to ensure business sustainability and distributive justice.
