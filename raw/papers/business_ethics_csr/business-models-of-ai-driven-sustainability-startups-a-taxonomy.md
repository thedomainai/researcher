# Business Models of AI-driven Sustainability Startups: A Taxonomy

**著者**: Kathleen Liebe, Niclas Brune, Oliver Vetter, Jan Heidrich, Joshua Beck
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経営倫理・CSR

## Abstract

Rapid advancements in artificial intelligence (AI) and sustainability efforts are fostering innovative business models that integrate both domains. This paper develops a taxonomy to classify business models for AI-driven sustainability startups. Following the methodological approach of Nickerson et al. (2013), a taxonomy comprising of 43 characteristics across nine dimensions was developed through five iterations utilizing the Business Model Canvas as a foundational framework. This taxonomy was developed and validated by analyzing 90 European startups, resulting in the categorization and clustering of five types of business models. By establishing a common vocabulary, this research addresses fragmentation and creates a foundation for comparative research and theory building in the intersection of AI and sustainability. The final taxonomy provides a tool for evaluation, classification and communication of business model types for both researchers and stakeholders, while remaining adaptable of future extensions and specifications to reflect innovation and evolution of AI-driven sustainability startups.
