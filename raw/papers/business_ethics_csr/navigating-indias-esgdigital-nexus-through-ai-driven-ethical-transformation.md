# Navigating India's ESG–Digital Nexus Through AI-Driven Ethical Transformation

**著者**: Piali Haldar, Dev Kumar Mandal
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経営倫理・CSR

## Abstract

This chapter examines how India's creative economy might navigate the ESG–Digital Nexus via an AI-driven ethical shift, not just tech for tech's sake. It draws on Dynamic Capabilities Theory and Stakeholder Theory, and basically looks at how AI-Driven Digital Capability, Big Data and IoT Integration, Ethical Digital Transformation, Strategic Leadership Agility, ESG Performance, and Sustainable Creative Economy Development connect together. The study uses survey data from 412 professionals, and it then analyses the whole thing with PLS-SEM. The outcome of the study is that AI capabilities and digital integration really do improve ESG performance, but they do it through ethical digital transformation. Strategic leadership agility tends to reinforce these effects, while ESG performance in turn supports sustainable creative economy development. Overall, the chapter stresses that technological experimentation has to be matched with ethical governance and sustainability objectives if India wants long-term competitiveness and responsible digital expansion.
