# A Study of Financial Instruments for the Development of Primary Education in Kolhapur District: Integrating AI and Smart Technologies for Sustainable Development and Viksit Bharat 2047

**著者**: Ravi Shivaji Chavan, M. K. Kamble
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経営倫理・CSR

## Abstract

Abstract Primary education constitutes a fundamental component of human-capital formation, social inclusion and long-term economic development. The National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 gives particular importance to foundational literacy and numeracy, equitable access, appropriate use of technology and improvement of learning environments. These priorities are also closely connected with Sustainable Development Goal 4 and India's long-term vision of Viksit Bharat 2047 (Ministry of Education, 2020). This paper examines the role of financial instruments in supporting the development of primary education in Kolhapur District, Maharashtra, with particular emphasis on public expenditure, Samagra Shiksha, district-level development funding, Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR), community participation and technology-enabled financial governance. The study adopts a descriptive and analytical framework combining secondary educational and policy data with a proposed field-survey design covering selected primary schools across urban, semi-urban, industrial-agrarian and rural/hilly areas of the district. The paper argues that the effectiveness of educational finance depends not only on the volume of expenditure but also on the timeliness, transparency, geographical targeting and outcome orientation of fund utilisation. Rural and geographically difficult areas may require differentiated resource allocation because equal per-school or per-student allocations do not necessarily reflect differences in infrastructure, connectivity, terrain, maintenance requirements and access to private investment. To address these challenges, the paper proposes an AI-Driven Educational Resource Optimization System (AI-EROS). The proposed framework combines data-based needs assessment, a school-level deprivation index, digital fund tracking, CSR-school matching and technology-assisted monitoring. Blockchain or distributed-ledger mechanisms are presented as possible audit-support technologies rather than as immediate substitutes for existing public financial systems. The proposed framework can help district authorities move from conventional input-based budgeting towards evidence-based and equity-oriented educational finance. The paper concludes that an integrated approach combining public finance, CSR, community participation, digital public infrastructure and responsible AI could strengthen the efficiency and transparency of primary education financing in Kolhapur District. Such a model could also provide a scalable framework for educational governance in Maharashtra and contribute to the broader objectives of Viksit Bharat 2047.
