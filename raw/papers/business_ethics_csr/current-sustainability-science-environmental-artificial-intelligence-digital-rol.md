## Abstract

Abstract The study titled “Current Sustainability Science, Environment and Artificial Intelligence: Digital Role in Human Well-being” examines the growing role of science, digital technology, and artificial intelligence (AI) in promoting sustainable development and improving human well-being. It explores the applications of AI and digital tools in education, healthcare, agriculture, environmental protection, industry, business, transportation, public services, and economic development. The study employs survey and descriptive observation methods, drawing on online information, research articles, reference books, and other sources. It highlights how AI can support informed decision-making, improve access to information, enhance agricultural productivity, assist medical diagnosis, monitor environmental changes, and facilitate services for persons with disabilities. However, the study also emphasizes the limitations and potential risks associated with excessive or irresponsible use of digital technologies. It argues that artificial intelligence should complement, rather than replace, human intelligence and decision-making. The effective use of these technologies requires transparency, safety, ethical responsibility, and inclusiveness. The study concludes that the constructive and responsible integration of science, digital technology, and AI can contribute significantly to sustainable development, environmental protection, social welfare, and the well-being of present and future generations.
