# The Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Different Aspects of the Society

**著者**: Ramchandra Pandurang Pakhare
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経営倫理・CSR

## Abstract

Abstract In recent times, artificial intelligence (AI) is emerging as a revolutionary transformative force in various sectors of the contemporary society. It has rapidly becomes an integral part of our daily lives. Now a days AI plays crucial role in transforming various aspects of society and generate new possibilities and opportunities. The paper also present a dissection of an impact of AI on different aspects of the society including agriculture, industry, education, market, finance, business, transportation, employment, healthcare etc. This article is presented in a narrative review. The main object of this research paper is an attempt to find and to analyze the positive and negative impact of artificial intelligence on various field of the society. This research paper provides a comprehensive overview of the impact of AI on various aspects of the society in the form of dynamic nature of economy, transformation in education system, modern and advanced healthcare system, change in nature of employment and ethics etc. The collected literature for this research paper is focused on socio-economic influence as well as future fear and challenges in implementing of AI. The AI related literature shows that it has large potential to provide significant benefits for the present modern society but it has also aware us about challenges and risk in implementing of AI technology. Because of many scientists, technologists and others concerned with the welfare of society have started fearing about the extensive use of AI and its impacts on the society. The medical field is more affected by use of AI technology. It plays a crucial role in diagnosis, surgery, appropriate treatment methods and other related healthcare services. It can also play an important role in education transforming with student personalized learning experience for student and teacher also. However, we cannot ignore some problems about using of AI in the field of viability of employment, economic inequality, and ethical issues. In short, this research paper provides a comprehensive overview of the impact of AI on various aspects of the present society life.
