# The Impact of Artificial Intelligence on the Role of the Business Analyst in the Context of Enterprise Architecture

**著者**: Παναγιώτης Τσαρουχάκης Πολυζώης, Panagiotis Tsarouchakis Polyxois

## Abstract

The rapid advancement of Artificial Intelligence (AI) is profoundly reshaping numerous professional fields, with the Business Analyst (BA) role at the epicenter of this transformation. This thesis investigates how AI impacts the BA's role, tools, and practices within the Enterprise Architecture framework. Through a literature review and critical analysis, it examines how the BA evolves from a requirements 'translator' into a strategic facilitator who leverages AI to improve decision-making, requirements elicitation, and capability design. Key findings show that AI redefines the BA's daily activities, shifts analysis practices toward a data-driven, predictive model, strengthens the strategic relevance of Enterprise Architecture, and demands a new skill set centered on digital literacy, critical thinking, and ethical responsibility. As its primary contribution, the thesis proposes the "AI Augmented BA Framework", a practical five-phase model serving as a roadmap for the careful integration of AI by practitioners and organizations. The research concludes with a discussion of limitations and directions for future empirical investigation.
