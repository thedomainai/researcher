# AI-Enabled Decision Intelligence for Sustainable E-Commerce and Online Business Transformation

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Global market places, consumer behaviour and competitive dynamics have through significant changes due to the rapid emergence, advancements in e-commerce and digital business models. The requirement for a balanced strategy which helps in integrating environmental responsibility with technological progressions is increasing. This study is based on how e-commerce operations would make better demand forecasting and sustainable project suggestions are made possible through analytical tools via the context of online shopping. This study gives us a strategic model for sustainable digital practices that is based on four main domains: Strategic adaptability morally sound data practices, responsible customer interaction and operational efficiency. As the results of the finding indicates intelligent decision making systems is said to improve environmental responsibility and corporate efficiency. When sustainability is incorporated into strategic choices, balancing of both the expansion of e-commerce can be pursued simultaneously support climate goals like NET zero and SDGs of the UN.
