# When Business Ventures Scale Faster Than People Systems: HRD in the AI-Enabled Start-Up Boom

**著者**: Rob E. Carpenter
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経営倫理・CSR

## Abstract

The surge in U.S. business formation has been accelerated by generative artificial intelligence (AI) and is often interpreted as evidence of renewed economic vitality. This perspective argues that the same trend also creates a consequential challenge for human resource development (HRD). This is because new ventures may achieve commercial reach and technical capability before developing the human systems needed to support learning, judgment, coordination, and workforce development. This temporal imbalance is theorized as venture heterochrony . Its accumulated human and organizational consequences are conceptualized as organizational formation debt . These constructs explain how AI-enabled speed can obscure weakness in knowledge governance, organizational memory, employee development, and relational capacity until periods of increased organization demand make them visible. The paper positions HRD upstream of formal human resource functions and proposes a minimum viable learning architecture grounded in decision rights, AI verification, organizational memory, employee voice, and developmental work design. It further identifies an HRD commons through which entrepreneurial ecosystems can provide shared developmental infrastructure early in venture formation. The central thesis is that business formation does not necessarily produce organizational formation; sustainable growth requires the deliberate synchronization of technical-commercial advancement with human-system maturity.
