# Stakeholder Value Creation in International Business

**著者**: Muhammad Junaid Ahsan, Mashhood Ur Rehman
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

This chapter develops a conceptual model of stakeholder value creation (SVC) in international business through the lens of AI-powered whole-person leadership. It argues that responsible stakeholder value creation begins with inward leader formation and becomes visible through CSR implementation, responsible AI governance, stakeholder relationships, ethical judgement, trust, collaboration, proactive engagement, and continuous learning. Drawing on stakeholder theory, CSR scholarship, self-efficacy, servant leadership, reflective awareness, and human-centred AI literature, the chapter positions SVC as the outward organisational expression of inwardly grounded leadership. Rather than presenting the five SVC dimensions as empirically validated outcomes, the chapter offers them as a conceptual framework for interpreting cross-border stakeholder complexity, global supply-chain interdependence, AI-mediated decision-making, algorithmic risks, regulatory variation, and sustainability pressures. The chapter contributes by extending whole-person leadership into AI-mediated international business
