## Abstract

Interdisciplinary Dimensions of Contemporary Research – Marketing and Digital Business Perspectives is an edited book comprising 28 research chapters covering contemporary developments in marketing, consumer behaviour, digital business, artificial intelligence, fintech, entrepreneurship, business analytics, cybersecurity, and financial literacy. The volume presents conceptual, empirical, and review-based studies examining digital transformation, changing consumer behaviour, AI applications, social media, digital marketing, sustainable marketing, financial behaviour, startups, and emerging digital technologies. It provides valuable insights for researchers, academicians, students, and business professionals interested in contemporary marketing and digital business practices.
