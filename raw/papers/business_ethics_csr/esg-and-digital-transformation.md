# ESG and Digital Transformation

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Environmental Social Governance (ESG) merged with Digital Transformation has become one of the hottest research areas influenced by sustainability, technology, and business requirements. This paper aims to provide a comprehensive overview of ESG correlated with digital transformation through a systematic literature review and bibliometric analysis. Searches were conducted on the Scopus database using keywords associated with ESG, sustainability, AI, Industry 4.0, and digital keywords. Initially, 919 publications were found; after screening and eligibility assessment, 43 studies indexed in Scopus were included for final analysis. For bibliometric analysis, a three-stage thematic protocol consisting of assembling, arranging, and assessing was followed. Bibliometric analysis was performed by using Microsoft Excel, Bibliometrix (R Studio), and VOSviewer to demonstrate publication patterns, research areas, keywords with cluster analysis, research themes over time, and country-wise contribution to research.
