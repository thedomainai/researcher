# Collaborative Partnerships and Ecosystem Building

## Abstract

The movement toward a circular economy is inevitable globally; for this to be realistic, there is a need for innovative approaches in the form of value creation. This development is a move beyond conventional approaches driven by conventional efficiency driven approaches. However, the circular economy needs a collaborative approach that tales share responsibility, system thinking, and realistic governance. This chapter has shown that within energy industries and manufacturing chains, the development of an ecosystem that can connect suppliers, producers, consume and policy makers is the best pathway for closing. In addition, it has explained the roles of ecosystem building and collaboration in the advancements of the circular economy across all sectors of society. Other contributions of this chapter are the analysis of the contributions of artificial intelligence (AI), blockchain, Internet of Things (IoT), and digital twins towards the implementation of the circular economy. Furthermore, discussions on case studies from different countries on various circular economic integrated models have been provided, and eventually, lessons learned from them have been highlighted. Finally, various policy directions have been discussed that can enable all the stakeholders to be committed in order to provide many possible options for realistic implementations and development of the circular economy. It is expected that the chapter can assist policymakers, researchers, and all stakeholders in better understanding and possible options for the development of circular economic models across the globe. This can assist the developing nations to be more proactive in the circular economy, so that they will not be left behind in the scheme of things.
