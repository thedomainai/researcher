# Forecasting ESG Performance With Machine Learning Regression Models

**著者**: Kesavulu Poola, Sakthikamalnathan Sambasivam, B. S. Sahana, K. Gokul
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経営倫理・CSR

## Abstract

Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) performance has become a critical indicator of sustainable value creation in the digital economy. This chapter examines how machine learning regression models, including Decision Tree, Random Forest, Support Vector Regression, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost, and LightGBM, can support ESG forecasting and strategic leadership decision-making. Drawing on Resource-Based View, Dynamic Capability Theory, Stakeholder Theory, and Digital Leadership Theory, the chapter develops a conceptual framework linking predictive analytics capabilities, leadership effectiveness, ESG performance, organizational resilience, and sustainable value creation. It also discusses implementation challenges, model validation considerations, and future research opportunities. The chapter contributes to the ESG–Digital Nexus literature by highlighting the strategic role of AI-driven forecasting in proactive sustainability management and long-term organizational performance.
