# Building Sustainable Business Value through Human-Centred AI Transition Pathways

**著者**: Dimitrios Ziakkas, Debra Henneberry
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経営倫理・CSR

## Abstract

Successful AI implementation depends as much on organisational readiness as on technological maturity. This paper proposes a business-oriented transition framework linking Human Factors, competency development, AI governance and organisational transformation. The model aligns investment decisions with operational performance, workforce capability and long-term resilience rather than technology acquisition alone. Aviation case studies demonstrate how organisations can generate measurable business value through structured implementation pathways that integrate AI, Human-AI Teaming and competency assurance into everyday operations.
