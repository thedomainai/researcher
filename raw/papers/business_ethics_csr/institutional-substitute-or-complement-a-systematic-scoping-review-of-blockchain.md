# Institutional substitute or complement? A systematic scoping review of blockchain for carbon accounting and supply-chain environmental, social and governance data in emerging economies

**著者**: Nguyen Van Tho, Tuan Minh Nguyen
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: 経営倫理・CSR

## Abstract

Exporters in emerging economies face growing demands for verifiable greenhouse gas and environmental, social and governance (ESG) data from EU importers under the carbon border adjustment mechanism (CBAM), domestic carbon pricing schemes and investors. Blockchain is often proposed as the infrastructure for these data, sometimes as a substitute for weak institutions. This study examines that claim. Following the PRISMA extension for scoping reviews (PRISMA-ScR), 494 OpenAlex records were screened with AI assistance against predefined criteria, 239 studies were mapped and 53 empirical studies were synthesised using institutional theory, the natural-resource-based view and the technology-organisation-environment framework. A document analysis of Vietnam, Malaysia and Indonesia complemented the review. Models and technical designs dominate the field, empirical studies make up 22% of it, and none focuses on Vietnam. Adoption and outcomes are associated mainly with off-chain carbon data capability, cost and institutional conditions. Of 16 studies with findings relevant to institutions, 15 treat institutions as enabling conditions, and the only evidence resembling substitution concerns private governance by technology providers. Because the evidence is heterogeneous, mostly cross-sectional and coded largely from abstracts, this pattern is tentative. In the layered view proposed, blockchain tends to complement institutions in measurement and verification, may replace some intermediary functions in transactions and may support private governance where oversight is weak. Six propositions and steps for Vietnamese exporters ahead of the first CBAM declaration are derived.
