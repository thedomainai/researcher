# AI-Driven Business Promotion: Transforming Marketing, Advertising, and Customer Engagement in the Age of Artificial Intelligence

**著者**: Adan Moin
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経営倫理・CSR

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence has moved from a peripheral marketing tool to the operating layer beneath most modern promotional activity. This paper examines how AI technologies, recommendation engines, generative content tools, conversational agents, and programmatic advertising systems are reshaping how businesses attract, engage, and retain customers. Drawing on recent industry survey data from McKinsey, Salesforce, HubSpot, Gartner, and The CMO Survey, alongside established academic frameworks in marketing science, the paper traces the evolution of AI-enabled promotion from rule-based segmentation to generative and agentic systems, evaluates its documented benefits in personalization, cost efficiency, and customer engagement, and critically examines the ethical, regulatory, and labor-market challenges accompanying its adoption. Particular attention is given to the persistent gap between rapid adoption and measurable value realization, and to the emerging regulatory landscape shaped by the EU Artificial Intelligence Act and a growing patchwork of U.S. state legislation. The paper concludes that sustainable AI-driven promotion depends less on tool acquisition than on governance, human oversight, and the preservation of consumer trust.
