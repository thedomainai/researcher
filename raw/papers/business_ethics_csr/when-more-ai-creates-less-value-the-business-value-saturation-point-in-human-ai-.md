# When More AI Creates Less Value: The Business-Value Saturation Point in Human-AI Organizations

**著者**: DEVIN LAMBETH
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経営倫理・CSR

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence can scale computational output faster than organizations can scale the human work required to verify, interpret, govern, communicate, and act on that output. This working paper develops the Business-Value Saturation Point (BVSP), a conceptual threshold at which the marginal benefit of additional AI augmentation is approximately equal to the marginal organizational cost of absorbing the operational demand it creates. BVSP extends the Lambeth AI Augmentation Capacity framework, which relates AI-Generated Operational Demand (AOD) to Human Processing Capacity (HPC) through the Augmentation Load Ratio (ALR). The article proposes that AI augmentation and net organizational value may be nonlinear: early deployment can improve efficiency and coverage, but marginal returns can diminish as AOD consumes available HPC. Beyond BVSP, additional technical capability may produce slower decisions, service degradation, hidden labor, governance burden, risk exposure, and declining net value. The contribution is a business-oriented architecture connecting AI-generated work, sustainable organizational capacity, bottleneck migration, and marginal economic analysis. The article provides seven testable propositions, operational measures, and a five-step management process for locating and managing augmentation saturation. BVSP remains conceptual and requires longitudinal, cross-industry validation.
