# An examination of the benefits, challenges and critical success factors of generative AI in the agri-food supply chain

**著者**: Astha Agrawal, Srikant Gupta, Naveen Virmani
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経営倫理・CSR

## Abstract

Purpose The presented work aims to explore the application of generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) in the agri-food supply chain, thereby enhancing innovation and resilience. Further, research focuses on developing practical insights to enhance competitiveness, minimize wastages and enhance sustainability by means of analyzing challenges, benefits and critical success factors. Design/methodology/approach The interpretive structural modeling (ISM) technique was used to analyze the relationship among anticipated benefits, challenges and critical success factors. Moreover, by using MICMAC analysis, all these variables were categorized into autonomous, linkage, dependent and independent categories. Findings Among the benefits, precision agriculture, supply chain optimization and real-time monitoring are found to have the highest driving power. Likewise, high implementation costs, technical complexity, skill gap and vendor dependence are computed as the topmost challenges in respect of driving power. Practical implications The study translates the ISM–MICMAC hierarchy into a stakeholder-specific short, medium and long-term implementation agenda that specifies data-quality, system-integration, governance and regulatory requirements for farmers, cooperatives, agribusiness firms, technology providers and policymakers. Originality/value This research contributes to academic discourse by conceptualizing GenAI as a dynamic capability within agri-food systems and offers actionable knowledge for practitioners aiming to integrate AI for sustainable value creation. The study explicitly separates the generative layer of GenAI (advisory and content generation, synthetic data, scenario simulation and natural-language interaction) from the established AI/ML and IoT functions on which it builds.
