# Environmental Hustlers and Greenwashing in the Data Centre Economy

**著者**: Ramya Krishnappa, Rajiv Gurugopinath
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経営倫理・CSR

## Abstract

India's expanding data centre economy powers AI, cloud computing, e-governance and digital services, but also carries heavy environmental costs through electricity use, cooling, water consumption, diesel backup, land use and e-waste. This chapter examines greenwashing by “environmental hustlers” who use claims such as “green cloud,” “carbon neutral” or “renewable powered” without verifiable proof. It argues for data centre-specific sustainability disclosures, independent audits, clear legal standards for green claims and stronger enforcement under consumer, environmental, ESG and corporate laws.
