# DIGITAL FINANCE AS A CATALYST FOR SUSTAINABLE BANKING: DECONSTRUCTING THE NEXUS BETWEEN FINTECH ADOPTION AND ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL, AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) PERFORMANCE

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経営倫理・CSR

## Abstract

Abstract Fintech has transformed banking quickly, and it is now possible to meet the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and identify alternative means of covering the costs of initiatives that do not harm the environment. The paper explores how the fintech can generate green finance models based on digital banking applications, which involve the application of artificial intelligence (AI), blockchain technology, big data analytics, and technical developments in cybersecurity. The three large issues that sustainable digital banking addresses with the traditional banking system include excessive carbon emissions, inadequate product choices regarding environmentally friendly financing, and regulations that are not strict. Digital banking has the potential of providing financial services to all people to the availability of modern technology. It is also able to establish financing schemes and investment services that are environmentally friendly. The article describes the way fintech can facilitate green financial products through the use of digital payment systems, green investment platforms, and central bank digital currencies (CBDCs). Both works indicate major issues that will complicate the use of sustainable digital banking, including legal issues, cybersecurity, and technology. The article examines how quantum computing, Internet of Things (IoT), and the financial platforms of smart cities are all converging in order to make green finance a better thing. The research makes use of the available literature and business experience to provide a comprehensive insight into fintech banking innovations that can cultivate the development of sustainable financial systems. The findings of the research equip the researcher, banks, and legislators with valuable data they must use in generating ideas on how to improve the use of digital banking and regulations. The essay discusses improved regulations and new research projects that would allow to make banks more viable and open to all in a shorter period of time.
