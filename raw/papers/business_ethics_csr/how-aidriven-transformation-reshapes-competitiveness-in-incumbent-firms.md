# How AI–Driven Transformation Reshapes Competitiveness in Incumbent Firms

**著者**: Francisco J Navarro-Meneses
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経営倫理・CSR

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence (AI) is reshaping how firms compete, innovate, and coordinate value creation in increasingly complex economic systems. While contemporary discourse remains largely focused on automation and productivity gains, this chapter examines how AI-driven transformation alters the strategic foundations of incumbent organizations. It develops a framework that distinguishes three stages in the evolution of AI capabilities: algorithmic consolidation, anticipatory integration, and architectural transformation. A longitudinal comparative analysis of five multinational incumbents shows how machine learning, digital twins, and simulation platforms reshape decision-making, business models, and stakeholder coordination. The findings suggest that competitive advantage increasingly depends on embedding anticipatory capabilities into organizational governance and value architectures. In doing so, AI-driven transformation enables firms to align innovation, sustainability objectives, and long-term resilience in environments characterized by systemic uncertainty.
