# Agentic AI Systems for Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence: A Layered Survey of Architectures, Evaluation, and Safety

**著者**: Md Abul Bashar
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: HCI

## Abstract

Agentic artificial intelligence (AI) refers to foundation-model-based systems that do more than produce isolated predictions: they maintain state, select actions, invoke tools, plan over multiple steps, interact with external environments, coordinate with other agents, and operate under explicit or implicit oversight. This transition is central to pattern analysis and machine intelligence because emerging systems no longer stop at recognition, detection, captioning, or classification; they close the loop between perception, representation, reasoning, decision-making, and action in textual, visual, multimodal, software, and embodied environments. Yet the literature remains fragmented across natural language processing, machine learning, computer vision, robotics, software engineering, human--computer interaction, security, and AI governance, and the word \emph{agent} is often used for systems with very different degrees of autonomy. This survey provides a layered architectural taxonomy that treats agency as a graded systems property. The grounding layer covers tool use, computer use, and environment interaction; the cognitive core covers memory, planning, and reasoning; the behavioral layer covers autonomous goal pursuit; the system layer covers multi-agent coordination; and the deployment layer covers human oversight, safety, alignment, controllability, and evaluation. Across these layers, we review representative methods, benchmarks, design patterns, and failure modes, emphasizing architectural assumptions, trade-offs, and cross-layer dependencies rather than merely cataloging papers. We further synthesize open challenges in long-horizon reliability, secure tool use, governed memory, scalable oversight, benchmark validity, multimodal grounding, and safe autonomy. The result is a conceptual map for researchers and practitioners who study, build, evaluate, or govern agentic systems within modern machine intelligence.
