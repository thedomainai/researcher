# GiriNeoMyoIschaemia™: Neonatal Myocardial Ischaemia Clinical Decision Support System — ECG-Led Screening, Assessment and Management

**著者**: Girish Gupta
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: HCI

## Abstract

GiriNeoMyoIschaemia™ is a single-file, interactive clinical decision support system for recognising and managing myocardial ischaemia in neonates and young infants. It is designed for neonatologists, paediatricians, paediatric cardiologists and intensivists, trainee doctors, and NICU and cardiac ICU nurses. The tool takes an ECG-led screening approach complemented by biomarkers, echocardiography and coronary imaging. Drop-down menus open 41 independent pages: Home: a one-tap "Open bedside assessment" button inside a "Stabilise and escalate first" banner; injury, dysfunction and suspected ischaemia kept separate; three ways to use the tool; pathway and key numbers. Basics: why the newborn myocardium is vulnerable, scenario map with a presentation timeline, and who to screen. Screen (ECG): normal newborn ECG evolution, normal versus abnormal ST–T findings, Jedeikin grading, infarct patterns with an interactive lead–territory heart map, mimics, and nine synthetic teaching strips (exportable as labelled teaching drawings). Assess (injury, function and coronary cause): troponin and NT-proBNP against neonatal reference limits; echocardiography; coronary imaging and cardiac MRI; the Risk-Signal engine; and a published engine rule map. Risk-Signal engine: urgent clinical, ECG, echo, coronary and postoperative rules run first, independent of age; every field has an explicit not-recorded state; the result gives one leading action, its triggers, who to call and what is still unknown, separate status tiles, a post-asphyxia (Jedeikin) descriptor kept separate from urgency, a troponin unit converter, and a transfer handover (copy, download, print) that carries urgency, unknowns and the pre-thrombolysis checklist. Scenarios: mechanism comparison; perinatal asphyxia; coronary thrombosis or embolism; ALCAPA; after the arterial switch; PA-IVS with RV-dependent coronary circulation; critical left-heart obstruction; myocarditis; coronary anomalies and fistulae; other causes including septic cardiogenic shock — each with recognise, distinguish, act and reassess lines and a named owner. Treat: general principles, echo-guided haemodynamic support, thrombosis (bedside team activation, interactive pre-thrombolysis checklist, collapsed specialist evidence panel), surgery, ECMO and transfer. Also: follow-up, nursing (watch list, traffic-light escalation, SBAR), worked cases (including three where signals disagree), quiz and FAQs. 28 data-driven figures redraw the cited evidence. Evidence is current to 4 November 2026: 134 Vancouver references, 122 linked to their PubMed records by PMID, with an evidence map, a screened PubMed set (2021–2026) and a research agenda. No randomised trials of anti-ischaemic treatment and no neonatal infarction criteria exist; this is stated throughout. Five external critical reviews (Releases v1.1–v1.4) were appraised; responses and changes are published inside the tool. Software rule testing is complete; clinical review by neonatology, paediatric cardiology and haematology is pending and is required before bedside use. Developed under the GIRISH-M™ architecture. Part of the NeonatAIlogy™ GiriNeo Series, GAIR Innovation Registry. Supportive Risk Signal — not a directive. This is an educational and decision-support resource. It is not a substitute for a clinician-read 12-lead ECG, echocardiography, cardiology advice or local protocols. Inotrope doses are deliberately not given; thrombolysis doses appear only as reported ranges in a specialist panel. No patient data are stored or transmitted. Prepared with AI assistance (Claude, Anthropic; Consensus for literature discovery; PubMed for verification) under the author's editorial control.
