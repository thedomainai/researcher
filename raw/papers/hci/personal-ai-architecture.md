# PERSONAL AI ARCHITECTURE

**著者**: Roman Shaban
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: HCI

## Abstract

The increasing accessibility of generative artificial intelligence has enabled individuals to integrate sophisticated computational capabilities into everyday intellectual activities. Large language models can support information processing, research, knowledge organization, decision analysis, content creation, and workflow automation. However, the use of multiple AI applications without a coherent organizational architecture frequently produces fragmented knowledge, duplicated efforts, inconsistent context, limited traceability, and difficulties maintaining control over automated processes. This paper introduces Personal AI Architecture (PAIA), a conceptual and methodological framework for constructing an integrated, human-centered environment for AI-augmented intellectual work. Rather than treating artificial intelligence as an isolated conversational tool, PAIA conceptualizes it as a configurable component of a broader personal cognitive and operational infrastructure. The proposed framework consists of eight interconnected architectural layers: human governance, information intake, personal knowledge and memory, cognitive augmentation, model and agent coordination, tool integration and automation, verification and quality assurance, and adaptive system development. These layers are supported by cross-cutting principles of cognitive autonomy, information provenance, privacy, security, interoperability, and human accountability. The paper develops an operational model for translating individual goals into coordinated AI assisted workflows, distinguishes different levels of task delegation, and introduces mechanisms for managing persistent context, agent responsibilities, approval procedures, and knowledge continuity. Particular attention is devoted to avoiding uncontrolled automation, excessive cognitive dependence, erroneous information propagation, and the fragmentation of personal knowledge across platforms. An illustrative implementation protocol, evaluation framework, and set of empirically testable propositions are presented. The anticipated outcome is a modular Personal AI Architecture capable of supporting sustained intellectual productivity, knowledge development, and adaptive collaboration between human cognition and artificial intelligence
