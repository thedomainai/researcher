# Prompts to Impact: User Experience Design in AI-Enhanced Online Learning Environments

**著者**: Julián Galindo-Losada, Vilma Rodríguez, Viviana Parraga, Mayra Carrión, Marco Santórum
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: HCI

## Abstract

The growing integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) into higher education requires a human-centered perspective that extends beyond technical functionality to examine how learners perceive, understand, and engage with AI-generated instructional content. This study evaluates the effects of prompt-engineered educational resources on user experience in a Moodle-based Business Intelligence workshop conducted in Ecuador. The intervention involved 10 advanced students from Information Technology and Software Engineering programs who independently interacted with AI-generated videos, code examples, simulations, scripts, and dashboard-related materials, with tutor support. The workshop was structured according to the Plan–Do–Check–Act framework and assessed through a pre-test/post-test design focused on learning facilitation, content comprehension, and engagement through multimedia resources. Results showed statistically significant improvements in all three dimensions. Learning facilitation increased from 3.5 to 4.3 (p = 0.0107, Cohen’s d = 1.01), content comprehension rose from 3.5 to 4.4 (p = 0.0294, d = 0.81), and multimedia engagement increased from 3.4 to 4.6 (p = 0.0002, d = 1.89). These findings indicate that prompt-engineered instructional materials can enhance motivation, conceptual understanding, and participation in online learning. The study contributes a methodological foundation for positioning prompt engineering not only as a content-generation technique, but also as a user-centered instructional design strategy for AI-enhanced education. Future research should examine affective responses, collaborative learning, scalability, and explainability in larger and more diverse populations.
