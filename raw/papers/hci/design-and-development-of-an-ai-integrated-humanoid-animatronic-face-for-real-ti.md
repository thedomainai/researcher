# Design and Development of an AI-Integrated Humanoid Animatronic Face for Real-Time Perception and Human-Robot Interaction

**著者**: Stalin Syjan
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: HCI

## Abstract

This project presents the design and development of an AI-integrated humanoid animatronic face capable of real-time perception and human-robot interaction. The system combines computer vision, machine learning, and embedded systems to detect human facial expressions and respond dynamically using servo-based actuation. The proposed model utilizes real-time image processing techniques to recognize emotions and generate appropriate facial responses, enhancing human-robot communication. The integration of artificial intelligence enables adaptive behavior, improving interaction efficiency and user engagement. This work contributes to the advancement of assistive robotics, social robots, and intelligent human-machine interfaces.
