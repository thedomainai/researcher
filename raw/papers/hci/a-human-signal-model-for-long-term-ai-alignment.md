# A Human-Signal Model for Long-Term AI Alignment

**著者**: Sabrina Van
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: HCI

## Abstract

This paper proposes a complementary approach to long-term AI alignment based on maintaining an ongoing dependency between advanced artificial intelligence and living human civilization. The central concept is “human signal”: the continuously changing information generated through human choices, relationships, emotions, creativity, culture, physical experience, mistakes, discoveries, and evolving values. Rather than treating humanity only as a source of fixed training data or as an external controller of AI systems, the model explores whether advanced AI could be designed so that continued interaction with living humans remains necessary to its useful development. The paper presents this relationship as a reciprocal loop in which humans generate novel signal, AI interprets and learns from that signal, and the resulting interaction produces further human development and information. The model is offered as a conceptual direction for discussion in long-term AI safety and alignment research.
