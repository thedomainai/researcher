# AI-powered fashion trend forecasting: enterprise users' cognitive evaluation and adoption

**著者**: Xiaohong Xu, Minjung Cho, Eunju Ko
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: HCI

## Abstract

Purpose AI-powered fashion trend forecasting tools are increasingly being used to translate fashion, market and consumer data into insights for business decisions. This study examines how enterprise users evaluate and apply these tools in B2B contexts. It focuses on how innovation attributes, perceived value and perceived constraints influence continued usage and intention to recommend. Design/methodology/approach The primary study examined survey responses from 312 enterprise users with prior experience using AI-based fashion trend forecasting tools. Structural equation modeling was used to evaluate the proposed model. To supplement the findings, the study conducted an age-based comparative analysis and a supplementary field validation study in which enterprise users evaluated the AI forecasting tools they use in their daily work environments. Findings Relative advantage and compatibility strengthened perceived value, whereas complexity increased perceived constraints. Fashion information accuracy had the strongest effect on both continued usage and recommendation intentions, suggesting that professional users give particular importance to the credibility and practical usefulness of AI-generated trend insights. Most perceived constraints did not directly affect behavioral intentions, except for security risk, which reduced recommendation intention. Originality/value This study advances research on AI adoption by shifting the focus from consumer-facing AI applications to enterprise users in B2B fashion forecasting. By combining diffusion of innovation theory and perceived value theory, it explains how professional users progress from evaluating AI innovation attributes to making value and constraint decisions. The findings have practical implications for using AI forecasting tools in design, merchandising, procurement, and planning.
