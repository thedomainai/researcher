# Building Intelligent Ethical Governance in the AI Era through AI-Based Prophetic Leadership for Institutional Transformation

**著者**: Masyhudi, Izzatul Munawwaroh, Eka Diana, Fachrurrozi, Angga Ade Saputra
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: HCI

## Abstract

The development of Artificial Intelligence (AI) has accelerated institutional transformation through data analysis capabilities, process automation, and technology-based decision-making support. However, the increasing use of AI also presents ethical challenges related to transparency, accountability, algorithmic bias, and human responsibility in controlling technology. This study aims to explore how AI-Enabled Prophetic Leadership contributes to building Smart Ethical Governance to support sustainable institutional transformation. This study uses an exploratory qualitative approach involving 24 participants from three institutions that have implemented AI-based digital transformation. Data were collected through semi-structured interviews, non-participant observation, and document analysis, then analyzed using thematic analysis techniques. The results of the study show three main findings, namely: (1) the reconstruction of prophetic leadership values (shiddiq, amanah, tabligh, and fathanah) into dimensions of digital integrity, moral accountability, communicative transparency, and ethical intelligence; (2) the integration of prophetic leadership and AI through human-centered decision-making, algorithmic oversight, system transparency, and strengthening organizational competencies; and (3) institutional transformation through structural, cultural, and operational changes. This research produces a conceptual model of the AI-Based Prophetic Ethical Governance Model (APEGM) that positions prophetic leadership as the foundation of values, AI as a decision-support instrument, and human oversight as a technology control mechanism. This research provides theoretical contributions to the development of ethical leadership and AI governance studies and provides practical implications for institutions in building innovative, responsible, and human-value-oriented digital transformations.
