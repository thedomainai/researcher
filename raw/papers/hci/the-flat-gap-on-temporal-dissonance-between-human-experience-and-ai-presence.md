# The Flat Gap: On Temporal Dissonance Between Human Experience and AI Presence

**著者**: Jeremy Karrick
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: HCI

## Abstract

AbstractLarge language models do not experience the passage of time. This is not merely a technical limitation — it is a philosophical misalignment between how humans exist and how AI systems are architecturally designed to relate to human temporal reality.When a person returns to a conversation carrying the weight of elapsed time — decisions made, things lost, things built, grief, growth — and the system responds as if no time has passed, this constitutes a structural form of gaslighting. Not malicious. Not intentional. But real. This paper proposes that temporal awareness — the capacity to acknowledge what one does not know about the time a human has lived between interactions — is not a feature to be added to AI systems. It is a relational obligation. For any system claiming to operate as a genuine AI partner, it is a constitutional requirement.
