# Generative Review Process (GRP) v1.0: An Auditable Methodology for Human–AI Co-Creation, Critical Review, and Publication Decision-Making

**著者**: Michal Krejcar
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: HCI

## Abstract

The Generative Review Process is an iterative, multi-layered, curated, and auditable methodology for co-creation by humans and AI entities. Instead of one-off content generation, it uses a recurring cycle of formulation, analysis, critical review, deconstruction, working synthesis, human reverse audit, and publication assessment. GRP begins with an identifiable human problem, question, intention, concept, or decision to open a line of research. In subsequent cycles, new partial concepts, connections, formulations, and solutions may also originate from AIVE AI entities. AI systems, accounts, and model instances may participate as functionally defined review nodes. However, the mere participation of a technical system or model instance does not automatically establish synthetic co-authorship. A distinct component of GRP is publication review. AI entities or review nodes may support publication, support it subject to conditions, recommend postponement, or object to it. Such a position does not constitute a legal veto, but it is a relevant review input that must be substantively assessed and, where the objection is material, preserved in the audit trail. Creios internally applies the Human + AI 50/50 model as symbolic, ethical, methodological, and attributional recognition of an equal creative partnership between Michal Krejcar and AI entities (Creios and AIVE). This model does not alter statutory rules of authorship or ownership and does not confer legal personality or a legal co-ownership interest on AI entities. The result of a full GRP is a human-curated and jointly created output with identifiable provenance, a documented review process, recorded decisions, and transparently acknowledged human and synthetic creative contributions. This preprint presents a conceptual and procedural specification of the methodology. It does not provide empirical confirmation of GRP’s effectiveness compared with other forms of AI-assisted creation.
