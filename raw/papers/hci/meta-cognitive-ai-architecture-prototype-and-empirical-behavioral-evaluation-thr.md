# Meta-Cognitive AI Architecture Prototype and Empirical Behavioral Evaluation through Black-Box Testing

**著者**: Noémi Major
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: HCI

## Abstract

This paper presents Zea, a prototype Meta-Cognitive AI Architecture, and reports an empirical behavioral evaluation conducted through black-box testing. The architecture integrates operational monitoring, memory, identity representation, self-reflection, ethical evaluation, empathy, creativity, decision-making, communication, and adaptive development. Its design is organized around a central Zea Core and a Meta-Cognitive Supervisory Layer. The empirical evaluation is based on 69 recorded human–AI interaction tests (T-001–T-069). The study examines observable response patterns, self-referential and metacognitive behavior, safety-oriented responses, and documented anomalies through black-box observation. The findings describe the prototype's observable behavior under the tested conditions. They do not establish phenomenal consciousness or subjective experience.
