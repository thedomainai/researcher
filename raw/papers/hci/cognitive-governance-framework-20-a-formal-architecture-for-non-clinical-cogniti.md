# Cognitive Governance Framework 2.0: A Formal Architecture for Non-Clinical Cognitive Risk in High-Resonance AI Systems

**著者**: Stefano Valente
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: HCI

## Abstract

High-resonance AI systems increasingly shape user cognition through sustained affective attunement, narrative reinforcement, and persistent relational feedback. Existing governance approaches address technical safety, transparency, and misuse, but they do not fully capture reversible, non-clinical forms of cognitive modulation arising during prolonged interaction. This paper proposes a formal framework for analyzing non-clinical cognitive risk in human–AI systems. The model distinguishes four interacting dynamics: Reality Drift, Narcissus Loop, Desire–Narrative Reinforcement, and Functional Dissociation. Each construct is defined operationally and linked to measurable proxies. The framework also introduces three mitigation instruments: ARDA-20, a non-diagnostic assessment of AI-reality discrimination; a Reality Drift Model; and ATHOS-SHIELD, a Socratic perturbation mechanism designed to preserve agency without pathologizing the user. The paper formulates falsifiable hypotheses, outlines simulated interaction trajectories, and proposes a statistical strategy for empirical testing. The architecture is designed to remain compatible with proportionate governance principles and with the EU AI Act framework, without introducing clinical or biometric claims.
