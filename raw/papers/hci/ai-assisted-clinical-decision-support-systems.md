# AI-Assisted Clinical Decision Support Systems

**著者**: Anthony Blash
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: HCI

## Abstract

This chapter examines how artificial intelligence (AI) is transforming clinical decision support by enabling healthcare teams to interpret complex information, recognize subtle patterns, and make safer and more informed decisions. It traces the evolution from early rule-based systems to modern adaptive tools that learn from real-world data and support clinicians in areas such as diagnosis, treatment planning, risk prediction, and workflow optimization. The chapter explains the major technologies behind these systems, including pattern recognition, language processing, and deep learning, and illustrates their impact through examples in imaging, chronic disease management, emergency care, and oncology. Readers are guided through both the benefits and the challenges, including ethical concerns, transparency needs, equity considerations, and the importance of human oversight. By the end of the chapter, readers will understand how AI-assisted decision support can enhance accuracy, efficiency, and personalized care when implemented responsibly within clinical environments.
