# No Hat, No Play

**著者**: Zanetta Arnold
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: HCI

## Abstract

Schools around New Zealand are currently undergoing large changes in curriculum and teaching pedagogies as AI is becoming a more prominent teaching tool. There have been large changes to teaching pedagogies; however, this has not yet been reflected in the architectural environment of each school. There is a major disparity between what should be designed and what is designed in current educational facilities, resulting in environments that are not encouraging or stimulating for students to learn in. As technology continues to progress, the environmental needs and engagement of students will need to evolve as well. This thesis will look into designing learning modules within schools that integrate AI into the architectural environment and encourage social interaction between students. Since AI is already a prominent teaching tool in schools, it is predicted to become the primary teaching tool in the near future, with schools evolving to AI schools. The definition of an AI school in this context is based on research of existing schools where AI is used to teach students subjects through virtual reality (VR) such as maths and English, with a human teacher supervising. The architecture of these schools and sensory/ emotive driven designs have been investigated and used as a precedent to base the design from. This research will inform design options of AI learning modules that can be integrated into existing schools and help to accommodate future learning needs without compromising student needs. This thesis assesses the essentials for learning and proposes design options to help overcome the limitations of AI in a learning environment. These final outcomes will focus on creating spaces for students to learn through AI; however, it also acknowledges and allows space for human educators as well. Research has shown the benefit and need for human interaction and teaching, which will be reflected in the design choices and final outcome. This will guarantee the upcoming generations are supported and learn all the artistic, physical, social and critical thinking skills they need. The final outcome will present three different options that will range in scale, materials, and purpose. Each module option can work in different school environments, and their compatibility will be based on the school’s budget and the spaces they have available.
