# Ai-Driven Decision Support and Product Lifecycle Data Management for Digital Transformation in Service-Oriented Supply Chains

**著者**: Soumya Mazumdar, Angshuman Majumdar, Sukanta Kundu, Pratik Kumer Halder
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Digital transformation is reshaping service-oriented supply chains by enabling faster, data-driven, and lifecycle-aware decision-making across procurement, logistics, customer service, maintenance, and after-sales operations. This chapter examines how artificial intelligence-driven decision support systems and product lifecycle data management contribute to supply chain visibility, resilience, traceability, and service performance. AI-enabled decision support systems support demand forecasting, risk detection, inventory optimization, predictive service operations, and real-time logistics coordination. Product lifecycle data management strengthens cross-functional data integration by linking product, process, supplier, customer, and service information across the lifecycle. The chapter further discusses how digital technologies such as IoT, blockchain, and generative AI support agile and sustainable service supply chains. The chapter concludes by identifying implementation challenges related to interoperability, data governance, cybersecurity, explainability, and readiness.
