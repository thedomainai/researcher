# Speculative Design for AI-Enabled Animal--Robot Interaction: Exploring Cross-Category Design Dimensions in Multispecies Futures

**著者**: Haruka Kasuga, Masashi Takeshita, Sonia Yuhui Zhang, Hayate Shimizu, Yuichiro Kasuga
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: HCI

## Abstract

Advances in artificial intelligence are expanding the possible roles of robots in animal environments, from monitoring and care support to enrichment, training, and interspecies communication. These possibilities raise ethical and design questions about animal agency, welfare, surveillance, misinterpretation, human responsibility, and automated control. This three-hour, half-day workshop uses design fiction as a practical form of speculative design to explore future Animal–Robot Interaction (ARI) across animal categories, including home, farm, working, laboratory, zoo/aquarium, and wild animals. In groups of four to five, participants will select one animal–category card, define their own two axes for mapping robot cards, and create two design fiction artifacts: a one-day scenario and a speculative news article. These activities will reveal how participants classify robot roles, risks, and possibilities in relation to specific animal contexts. Through cross-group comparison, the workshop will identify candidate ARI design dimensions, including shared, category-specific, relationship-specific, and ethically contested considerations. The workshop will produce participant-defined mapping axes, robot-card maps, design fiction artifacts, reusable workshop materials, and open research questions. It also forms part of a broader research program that aims to run the format across multiple venues and build a corpus of approximately 50 participants. By connecting ARI, HRI, ACI ethics, AI ethics, and design fiction, the workshop aims to support more context-sensitive and ethically grounded approaches to AI-enabled animal–robot interaction.
