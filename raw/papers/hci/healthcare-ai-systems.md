# Healthcare AI Systems

**著者**: Wenke Du
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: HCI

## Abstract

Clinical decision-support and patient-safety prototypes evaluated on open clinical data. Includes a measurement of subject-level leakage on the MIMIC-IV open-access demo (row-level split AUROC 0.906 against subject-grouped 0.499), calibration and alarm comparison at matched sensitivity, a physiological waveform pipeline, and an empathy-lexicon study reported as a negative result. Validity claims that the data cannot support are withheld with a stated reason.
