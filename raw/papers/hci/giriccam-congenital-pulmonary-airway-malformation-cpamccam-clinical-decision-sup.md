# GiriCCAM™: Congenital Pulmonary Airway Malformation (CPAM/CCAM) Clinical Decision Support System — From Fetal Diagnosis to Adult Follow-up

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

GiriCCAM™ is an open, single-file interactive clinical decision support system (CDSS) on congenital pulmonary airway malformation (CPAM, formerly congenital cystic adenomatoid malformation, CCAM), from fetal diagnosis to adult follow-up, for doctors, nurses and parents. It is part of the GiriNeo Series under NeonatAIlogy™ in the GAIR Innovation Registry and is governed by the GIRISH-M™ architecture (Goal, Input, Role, Iteration, Safety, Human oversight, Miscellaneous). Content: incidence and classification (prenatal, Stocker, mechanistic KRAS/obstruction models) with a schematic atlas; aetiopathogenesis and routes to malignancy; prenatal assessment with a CPAM volume ratio (CVR) calculator that shows emergency alerts first and states which threshold applies to current, maximum or final values; fetal therapy (maternal betamethasone, thoracoamniotic shunt, EXIT/section-to-resection); delivery room and NICU care; workup and differential diagnosis, including red flags for pleuropulmonary blastoma and DICER1 testing; surgery (lobectomy versus segmentectomy, thoracoscopy, anaesthesia); a balanced presentation of the elective-resection versus structured-surveillance debate, including the ongoing CONNECT trial; a rule-based navigator; a printable "My agreed care plan"; genetic counselling; follow-up and lung function; immunisation against respiratory viruses (RSV, influenza, measles, COVID-19), with Indian (IAP ACVIP 2025) and international guidance; a nursing bundle; parent information with a Hindi summary; and an 11-item CME quiz. Evidence: literature search through 03 Nov 2026 (PubMed, Consensus AI, ClinicalTrials.gov, GeneReviews, national and professional guidance). 89 references; each DOI was queried in PubMed (74 of 78 matched; 4 non-indexed sources labelled), and every reference shows where it is cited. Guideline pages were accessed on 04 Nov 2026. Outputs are Supportive Risk Signals, not directives. The tool supports and does not replace clinical judgement, is not a medical device, and has not been externally validated. Subject-expert review and reconciliation with local protocols are required before bedside use.
