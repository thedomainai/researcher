# When Machines Become Friends: Evaluating the Impact of AI Companions on Human Well-being in the Digital Age

**著者**: Lokanath Patra
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: HCI

## Abstract

Abstract Human–machine interaction has changed from task-oriented help to emotionally engaging companionship due to rapid AI evolution. Conversational AI companions like Replika, Character.AI, XiaoIce, Nomi, and Kindroid are used worldwide for emotional support, companionship, mental wellness, and social engagement. They're becoming more popular as people seek connection and psychological comfort in a digital environment. These technologies may reduce loneliness, support emotional well-being, and improve conversational support. Still, they also raise concerns about emotional dependency, social isolation, privacy, manipulation, misinformation, and the gradual replacement of authentic human relationships. The growing role of AI companions in shaping human well-being is critically examined in this interdisciplinary study that draws on global literature, empirical research, market reports, and verified international case studies. The study examines the evolution of AI companions, global adoption, user behavior, and the psychological, social, and ethical ramifications. The study balances the pros and cons of emotionally intelligent AI systems, drawing on recent advancements in the US, China, Japan, South Korea, and selected European countries. The study draws on psychology, sociology, human-computer interaction, and digital ethics to examine the complex relationship between AI companionship and human flourishing. The study claims that AI companions are evolving socio-technical systems whose influence depends on use patterns, user vulnerability, social context, and design characteristics, rather than being substitutes for human relationships or destructive technologies. By synthesizing current evidence and identifying new research directions, the study contributes to the growing discourse on responsible, human-centered AI. It provides a comprehensive understanding of the opportunities and challenges of emotionally intelligent technologies in promoting human well-being in the digital age.
