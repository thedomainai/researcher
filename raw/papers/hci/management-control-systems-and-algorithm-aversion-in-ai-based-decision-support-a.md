# Management Control Systems And Algorithm Aversion In Ai-Based Decision Support: A Systematic Review Protocol

**著者**: Mario Richard Smeets
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: HCI

## Abstract

Management Control Systems (MCS) shape the organizational architecture within which managers engage AI-based Decision Support Systems (DSSs)-yet their role as antecedents of algorithm aversion remains theoretically unaddressed.Behavioral IS research has identified individual-and task-level drivers of algorithm aversion, while the management control literature has theorized how MCS configurations govern IS use contexts.No systematic review bridges both streams.This paper presents a protocol for a systematic literature review integrating these two bodies of research.Following PRISMA guidelines, the review addresses three questions: the current state of the MCS-algorithm aversion intersection, the mechanisms through which MCS characteristics promote or impede AI-based DSS reliance, and how findings can be integrated into a conceptual IS framework.Expected contributions include a structural mapping, a theoretically grounded framework linking Simons ' (1995) Levers of Control to behavioral reliance outcomes, and a prioritized empirical research agenda.
