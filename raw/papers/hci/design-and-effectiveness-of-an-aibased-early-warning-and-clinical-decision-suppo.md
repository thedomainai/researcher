# Design and effectiveness of an AI⁃based early warning and clinical decision support system for multidrug⁃resistant organism infections

**著者**: WU Xiaoqin, LI Jin, XUE Jing, Liran Shi, GUO Feng
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: HCI

## Abstract

ObjectiveTo explore the design and application effectiveness of an artificial intelligence(AI)⁃based early warning and clinical decision support system for multidrug⁃resistant organism(MDRO) infections.MethodsData of inpatients in a tertiary general hospital in Xuzhou from January 2022 to December 2024 were collected.According to the system implementation timeline,the study was divided into three phases:pre⁃implementation(2022),one year post⁃implementation(2023),and two years post⁃implementation(2024).Indicators including MDRO detection rate,immediate execution rate of early warnings,incidence of healthcare-associated infections, antimicrobial use rate,rate of pathogen detection before antimicrobial therapy,and compliance rate with core prevention and control measures were compared.ResultsAfter the AI system operation,the immediate response rate for MDRO early warning cases increased from 66.80% to 99.40%.The rate of isolation order issuance rose from 68.10% to 100.00%.The incidence of MDRO infections decreased from 0.197% to 0.073%(P<0.05).The therapeutic antimicrobial use rate,rate of pathogen detection before treatment, and compliance rate with core MDRO prevention and control measures all significantly improved(P<0.05).ConclusionsThe AI⁃based MDRO early warning and clinical decision support system enables more scientific,standardized, and efficient prevention and control of MDRO infections.It effectively reduced the incidence of MDRO healthcare⁃associated infections,and enhanced the rational use of antimicrobial agents.
