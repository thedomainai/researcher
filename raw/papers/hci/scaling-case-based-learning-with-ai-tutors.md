# Scaling Case-Based Learning With Ai Tutors

**著者**: Mila Jegerlehner, Nikolaus Obwegeser
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: HCI

## Abstract

Case based learning (CBL) is shown to significantly enhance critical thinking, problem-solving and decision-making skills.AI tutors offer a promising approach to scale CBL while preserving its interactive and dialogic nature.Yet, design guidance for AI-supported CBL remains underdeveloped.This paper addresses this gap by examining key design dimensions, including pedagogical effectiveness, engagement, feedback, the role of human tutors, scalability, and ethical considerations.Empirically, the study draws on Gaia, an AI-supported CBL environment developed within the ERASMUS+ project L2BGreen, where learners interact with an AI tutor while working on cases in green entrepreneurship.Gaia enables the collection of rich interaction data, providing insights into learning processes and the design of AI tutors for CBL.Based on this setting, the paper adopts a solution-driven perspective to advance the development of a systematic design theory for AI-supported CBL.
