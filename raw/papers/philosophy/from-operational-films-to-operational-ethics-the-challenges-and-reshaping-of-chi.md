# From 'Operational Films' to 'Operational Ethics': The Challenges and Reshaping of Chinese Film and Television Ethics and National Image Posed by AI-Generated Scripts

**著者**: Yuxuan Cao
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 哲学

## Abstract

With the deep integration of generative AI in the field of film and television creation, humanmachine collaborative AI-generated scriptwriting has become an irreversible trend. However, large models led by the West, such as OpenAI and Claude, have pre-training data and value alignment mechanisms that naturally carry Western centrist discourse encoding, resulting in frequent misexpressions such as cultural misinterpretation, ethical misconduct, and value misalignment in AI-generated "Chinese stories", which to some extent, hinder the international narrative dissemination of "telling good Chinese stories". While AIgenerated script tools increase efficiency and lower the threshold for screenwriters, their inherent technical logic and cultural biases pose systemic risks and the possibility of structural remodeling to the maintenance of Chinese film and television ethics and the construction of the national image. At present, the fundamental task for China in the "AIGC script generation race" is to break away from dependence on the European and American film ethics system, achieve independence from its ethical origins, and realize Chinese characteristics in the face of the European and American film system. This paper suggests that the fundamental task of China's film and television industry is to achieve a crucial leap from the technical level of "operational films" to the value level of "operational ethics". The research aims to construct a threedimensional ethical framework of "technology ontology - narrative ethics - image construction" based on Peking University's "Linghu" intelligent script creation, by "encoding" localized ethical alignment into the algorithmic core, to transform AIGC from a potential source of cultural risk into a controllable and reliable tool for shaping the national image.
