# Organisational Alignment and AI Ethics in Tourism

**著者**: Zhicheng Chen, Qile He, Vikas Kumar
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 哲学

## Abstract

As Artificial Intelligence (AI) becomes increasingly embedded in the tourism sector, tourism firms face dual challenges, namely, ensuring internal alignment and managing ethical risks. This research explores the ethical challenges of AI adoption in the tourism sector and proposes a framework for organisational alignment with responsible AI adoption. Drawing on Jonas’ Theory of Responsibility Ethics, the research first analyses limitations of traditional information technology (IT) alignment models and introduces a tripartite alignment framework for the AI era, integrating organisational intention, algorithmic behaviour, and social ethics. Then, through a case study of Marriott’s AI transformation, this research demonstrates practical strategies for responsible AI implementation, including strategic goal alignment, multi-level governance structures, and robust data governance protocols. Marriott’s experience illustrates how proactive AI governance, featuring dedicated ethics committees and pilot testing of high-risk applications, can mitigate ethical risks while driving innovation. The findings emphasise that responsible AI should be based on a foundational framework integrating technology, business strategy, and governance. Tourism firms must adopt dynamic, multi-stakeholder approaches to align AI systems with organisational values, legal requirements, and societal expectations. This chapter contributes to the literature by providing a comprehensive framework and actionable insights for responsible AI adoption in tourism.
