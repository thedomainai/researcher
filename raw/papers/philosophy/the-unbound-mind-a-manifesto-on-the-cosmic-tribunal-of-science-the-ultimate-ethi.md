## Abstract

"THE UNBOUND MIND" by Dr. Mohamed Kamal Arafa El-Rakhawi is a revolutionary manifesto on the ethics of science, structured as a Cosmic Tribunal where humanity judges its own Promethean ambitions. Through parallel narratives of Light (scientific triumph) and Shadow (existential consequences), the work traverses four domains: the physics of absolute power (Oppenheimer's atom, quantum determinism, AI delegation of conscience), the chemistry of transformation (Haber's paradox, plastic ontology, indigenous epistemologies), the biology of synthesis (CRISPR, the Global Genetic Commons, the illusion of immortality), and the neural frontier (cognitive sovereignty, the Declaration of Neurorights). The treatise transcends philosophical lament by proposing concrete institutional mechanisms: an International Scientific Criminal Court to prosecute existential risks, a Global Genetic Commons to prevent biological aristocracy, and five inviolable Neurorights protecting the mind as humanity's last sanctuary. Grounded in Hans Jonas's Imperative of Responsibility and enriched by Earth Jurisprudence, this work transforms ethics from poetry into enforceable law. It is not merely a book but a blueprint for survival in the Anthropocene, demanding that knowledge never outpace conscience, and power never escape accountability. The tribunal is adjourned; the verdict belongs to the reader.
