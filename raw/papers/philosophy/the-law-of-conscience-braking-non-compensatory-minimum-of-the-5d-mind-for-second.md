## Abstract

Simultaneously, when legacy rules go bankrupt and a system enters a Gödelian deadlock, why can only a tiny fraction of "Chief Architect" leaders step outside the game board to redefine the problem, whereas the vast majority of high-IQ elites and trillion-parameter Large Language Models (LLMs) either grind in a Turing Deadlock or exploit rule loopholes in a Goodhart Arbitrage spiral? Grounded in first principles of systems and complexity science and predictive-coding neurocybernetics, this paper formulates and proves the law governing subject mind architecture and human-machine symbiosis in complex giant systems: The Law of Conscience Braking.
