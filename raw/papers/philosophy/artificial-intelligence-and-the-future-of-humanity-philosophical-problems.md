# ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND THE FUTURE OF HUMANITY: PHILOSOPHICAL PROBLEMS

**著者**: Department of Social Sciences Bozorboyeva Zuhra Abdurakhimovna Jizzakh State Pedagogical University Associate Professor
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 哲学

## Abstract

Abstract: The rapid development of artificial intelligence has transformed AI from a specialized technological instrument into a phenomenon capable of influencing human cognition, labour, communication, education, culture, politics, and decision-making. As artificial intelligence becomes increasingly integrated into everyday life, the discussion surrounding it can no longer be limited to technical efficiency. It raises fundamental philosophical questions about human nature, freedom, responsibility, consciousness, knowledge, justice, and the future relationship between humanity and technology. This article examines the principal philosophical problems associated with artificial intelligence and considers their significance for the future of humanity. The research is based on qualitative conceptual analysis and comparative examination of international scholarship, UNESCO and OECD documents, Uzbekistan’s national artificial intelligence strategy, and Uzbek philosophical literature. The analysis indicates that the major challenge of AI is not the existence of increasingly capable machines itself, but the possibility that human beings may gradually transfer important intellectual, ethical, and social decisions to systems that do not possess human experience, moral responsibility, or consciousness in the ordinary philosophical sense. Particular attention is given to human autonomy, algorithmic responsibility, the transformation of labour, epistemic dependence, inequality, human dignity, and the problem of technological control. The study concludes that the future of humanity should not be conceptualized as a competition between humans and artificial intelligence. A more sustainable philosophical model understands AI as a powerful extension of human capabilities whose development must remain subordinate to human dignity, ethical responsibility, social justice, and meaningful human control. Keywords: artificial intelligence, philosophy of technology, human future, ethics of AI, human dignity, autonomy, responsibility, consciousness, algorithmic decision-making, Uzbekistan.
