## Abstract

We present our game zine “AI Rebellion” as an arts and culture-based artefact that invites technology activists, researchers and practitioners to engage in playful speculative storytelling to explore counternarratives to the dominant, institutional discourse on artificial intelligence (AI), and contribute to the cocreation of more responsible AI systems. Zines are self-published, small-circulation paper booklets or magazines characterized by creative freedom and independent spirit. They have been historically used as a low-cost, accessible artform for disseminating information for counter-movements (Duncombe, 1997). Game zines grew out of the science fiction ‘fanzine’ culture of the 1930s and spiked in popularity in 1975 with the creation of the first tabletop role-playing-games (RPG) that inspired a movement and formed the basis for our game zine AI Rebellion, an open-ended role-playing narrative game.
