# Governance Is Knowledge, Knowledge Is Governance: The Governance of AI Governance

**著者**: Oliver Krone
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 哲学

## Abstract

Abstract This paper argues that the contemporary AI governance debate has imported the term “governance” while narrowing its meaning to technical permissions, controls, and compliance mechanisms. This semantic migration obscures a foundational feature of governance in administrative and political science: governance rests on a socially shaped substrate of premises about what should be governed, what governance is meant to do, and which readings of reality are permitted to become institutionally binding. By reconstructing governance through institutional theory, systems theory, and epistemology, the paper shows that the core problem in AI governance is not only the regulation of models or outputs, but the disappearance of the premise layer through which governable objects are constituted in the first place.
