# THE NEW BABEL BUILDERS Artificial Intelligence and the Crisis of Human Centrality

**著者**: Nadji Belkheiri
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 哲学

## Abstract

This paper investigates the philosophical crisis arising from advances in Artificial Intelligence, moving beyond functional debates about machine automation and human replacement. It introduces the concept of "algorithmic anthropological reduction" to describe how measurable human capabilities are progressively transformed into implicit metrics of human worth. Drawing on philosophy of mind, philosophy of technology, and theories of personal identity, the article argues that defending human dignity in the algorithmic age requires rejecting the reduction of human value to optimizable performance metrics, calling instead for a reconstruction of human centrality rooted in normative agency and shared forms of life.
