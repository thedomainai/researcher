# Strategic Uncertainty Paradox Theory (SUPT)

**著者**: Shamiul Hoque Shan
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 哲学

## Abstract

Strategic Uncertainty Paradox Theory (SUPT) proposes a unified interdisciplinary framework explaining how rational strategic intent systematically diverges from emergent outcomes in complex adaptive systems due to uncertainty, information asymmetry, feedback loops, and system complexity. This paper integrates mathematical modeling, game theory, chaos theory, cognitive psychology, neuroscience, philosophy, political science, sociology, and artificial intelligence ethics into a single meta-theoretical structure. A quantifiable index (SUPI) is introduced to measure paradox likelihood across domains. The theory emphasizes that paradox is not an anomaly but a structural inevitability in high-complexity systems. This work formalizes SUPT into a predictive, falsifiable, and scalable framework applicable across geopolitical, economic, technological, and cognitive domains.
