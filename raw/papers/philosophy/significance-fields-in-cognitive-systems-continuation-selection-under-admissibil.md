# Significance Fields in Cognitive Systems: Continuation Selection under Admissibility Constraints

**著者**: Kostiantyn Osmolovskyi
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 哲学

## Abstract

This technical report introduces significance fields as a structural-regulatory layer within the General Theory of Cognitive Structuring (GTCS). While structural admissibility determines what can enter regulation or become structurally updateable, it does not by itself explain why one admissible continuation receives greater weight than another. The report defines a significance field as the structured distribution of continuation-weight across states, discrepancies, representations, trajectories, relations, and possible transformations. On this basis, continuation selection is introduced as the unequal regulatory prioritization of admissible continuations under significance constraints. The report distinguishes significance fields from attention, motivation, value, reward, affect, coherence, overload, and identity. It further shows how significance fields clarify manifestation, inter-system conflict, significance misreadability, and artificial agency. The account does not provide a theory of consciousness, emotion, moral value, or artificial personhood. Its role is to introduce a minimal architectural distinction needed to describe how cognitive systems organize continuation beyond admissibility alone.
