# YOUHENG ZHANG: A HISTORICAL INTERACTION BETWEEN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND PHILOSOPHY

**著者**: Qiyomova Rayhona Farxulloyevna
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 哲学

## Abstract

This paper explores the historical interaction between artificial intelligence and philosophy, highlighting how philosophical thought has shaped the development of AI and how AI, in turn, has influenced modern philosophical inquiry. From early reflections on logic and reasoning in ancient philosophy to contemporary debates on machine learning and machine consciousness, the relationship between these two fields has been deeply interconnected. The study examines key philosophical questions such as the nature of intelligence, consciousness, and reasoning, and how these concepts have been translated into computational models. It also discusses major milestones in AI development and the philosophical implications they raise regarding ethics, mind, and human identity. The paper concludes that artificial intelligence and philosophy continue to evolve together, each challenging and expanding the boundaries of the other in understanding intelligence and existence.
