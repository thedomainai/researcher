# Knowledge and competency framework for quantity surveyors to perform circular economy practices successfully in the construction industry

**著者**: D.S.I. Wijayawardena, Iresha Subashine Gamage, Sepani Senaratne, B.A.K.S. Perera
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 哲学

## Abstract

Purpose The construction industry significantly contributes to resource depletion and waste generation, necessitating the adoption of circular economy (CE) practices. The successful implementation of CE practices depends on the knowledge and competencies of construction professionals, particularly quantity surveyors (QSs), whose cost, procurement and contractual decisions strongly influence CE adoption. Therefore, this study aims to develop a knowledge and competency framework for QSs to perform CE practices successfully in the construction industry. Design/methodology/approach An interpretive research philosophy with a qualitative approach was adopted using the Delphi technique. A two-round Delphi study was conducted involving 20 experts in Round 1 and 17 experts in Round 2, resulting in a total of 37 interviews. The data were analysed through code-based content analysis using NVivo14. Findings The findings highlight QS involvement across design, construction and end-of-life stages, identifying 14 significant CE practices. The study further identifies 17 common knowledge areas and 60 competencies, along with 11 QS-specific knowledge areas and 21 competencies required for each CE practice. Originality/value This study contributes to academic discourse by developing and empirically validating a comprehensive framework encompassing QS-specific knowledge areas, QS-specific competencies, common competencies and common knowledge areas. The core contribution of this study is in identifying QS-specific knowledge areas and competencies and systematically mapping them to the specific CE practices that QSs can perform across the project life cycle. For industry professionals, the framework assists in identifying, assessing and enhancing the specific knowledge areas and competencies required to effectively engage with CE practices to successfully embed CE into the construction industry.
