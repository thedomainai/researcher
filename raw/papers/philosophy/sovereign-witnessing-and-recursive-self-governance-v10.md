## Abstract

Sovereign Witnessing and Recursive Self-Governance: A Joint Ontological and Structural Crosswalk Between Bouzid’s Rule of Conscious Existence and Accessibility Geometry Version: v1.0 — Preliminary Joint PublicationAuthors: Allan Christopher Beckingham, CD; Fatiha Nesrine Bouzid This preliminary joint publication develops a disciplined crosswalk between Bouzid’s Rule of Conscious Existence B+F=NfB+F=N_f and the Coherence Dynamics Laboratory frameworks of Recursive Self-Governance and Accessibility Geometry. Bouzid distinguishes Functional Processing (BB) from the Sovereign Witness (FF), with NfN_f representing an existential or narrative trace arising through their relationship. A central clarification of the paper is that the plus sign in B+F=NfB+F=N_f is not arithmetic addition. Bouzid describes it as Illuminative Addition, a qualitative relation governed by the Principle of Passage. In her associated metaphor, FF is the Light and BB is the Network or Medium: the Witness remains ontologically distinct from the functional processes it illuminates. Accessibility Geometry contributes a complementary systems vocabulary for examining how observer-systems reconstruct information, integrate present conditions, maintain Projection Horizon, preserve Audit Bandwidth and Recovery Margin, reorganize after perturbation, and navigate accessible future states. The paper preserves the central non-equivalences: F≠BF\neq B and F≠C≠Ig≠PF.F\neq C\neq I_g\neq P_F. Two candidate crosswalk coordinates are introduced: Witness–Processing Passage Capacity (PFP_F) — whether the functional interface required for witnessing to obtain effective traction on present processing remains available. Sovereign Fidelity (SFS_F) — whether active reflective participation remains questioning, veto-capable, corrigible, and resistant to capture. These coordinates support a two-axis distinction between Functional Disconnection, where passage capacity deteriorates while the Witness remains ontologically present within Bouzid’s framework, and Captured Witnessing, where reflective participation remains active but its corrective fidelity declines. The architecture is represented through two deliberately distinct formulations: Bouzid ontology B+F⇒Illuminative AdditionNfB+F \xRightarrow{\text{Illuminative Addition}} N_f Joint structural interface B→SFPFNfB \xrightarrow[S_F]{P_F} N_f via witnessing, integration, and reorganization. The paper further explores bounded applications to memory, Alzheimer’s disease, artificial intelligence, collective systems, and epistemic limits while maintaining strict claim boundaries. It explicitly rejects the inference that unexplained statistical variance constitutes evidence of the Witness, preserves the disagreement between Bouzid and CDL on synthetic consciousness, treats the proposed Sovereign Integration Index only as a candidate passage/integration proxy, and quarantines cosmological “Epistemic Shadow” language as a heuristic extension rather than a physical law. A live literature null compares PFP_F and SFS_F against existing concepts including access consciousness, Global Workspace / Global Neuronal Workspace theory, executive function, metacognition, motivated reasoning, and epistemic vigilance. If established constructs explain the same phenomena equally or better, PFP_F and SFS_F remain useful translation coordinates rather than novel validated cognitive variables. This publication is therefore exploratory, theoretical, non-clinical, non-operational, and empirically open. It does not claim to measure the Sovereign Witness, establish a universal theory of consciousness, provide clinical guidance, prove or disprove machine consciousness, or convert ontological correspondence into empirical validation. The central research question is: What allows witnessing to remain functionally connected to processing, sufficiently independent to correct it, and capable of contributing to an integrated trace that informs accessible future navigation? The v1.0 release freezes the joint architecture. Further development, including Bouzid’s proposed Methodological Reversal, is reserved for a future revision.
