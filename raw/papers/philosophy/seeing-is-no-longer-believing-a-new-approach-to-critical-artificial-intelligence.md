# SEEING IS NO LONGER BELIEVING A NEW APPROACH TO CRITICAL ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE LITERACY

**著者**: Paul Mercier
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 哲学

## Abstract

The proliferation of hyper-realistic artificial intelligence (AI) media, particularly short-form synthetic video and audio, has outpaced traditional media literacy education. While current pedagogical approaches largely emphasize AI creation and integration, there remains a critical deficit in teaching students how to recognize and critically evaluate AI-generated content. To address this gap, this project proposes a two-week experiential instructional module designed for upper-level higher education students. Grounded in Knowles’s Adult Learning Theory, Kolb’s Experiential Learning Theory, and the Media Literacy Theory of Change (MLTC), the curriculum utilizes collaborative “analytic teams” to transition students from intuitive media consumption to goal-oriented analytical thinking. Through hands-on evaluation, students will assess content authenticity, analyze agenda-setting and framing, and appraise potential sociopolitical harms, culminating in a summative Media Verification Portfolio. Ultimately, this equips adult learners with the critical AI literacy skills necessary to identify digital manipulation and mitigate the spread of misinformation.
