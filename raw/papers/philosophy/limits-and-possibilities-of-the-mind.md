# Limits and Possibilities of the Mind

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Some books are written as the result of years of scientific research within a particular discipline. Others emerge from a lifelong search for answers to questions that transcend the boundaries of any single field. The Limits and Possibilities of the Human Mind belongs to both categories. Throughout my academic and professional career, I have worked in diverse fields, including information technology, management, economics, digital transformation, and artificial intelligence. Regardless of the discipline, however, one fundamental question has consistently guided my research: What are the true limits of the human mind, and how far can its potential be developed? At first glance, this question appears to have already been answered. Psychology studies intelligence, neuroscience investigates the brain, philosophy explores consciousness, and artificial intelligence is reshaping our understanding of cognition itself. Yet, despite remarkable advances, these fields often evolve independently. Each provides valuable insights, but none offers a comprehensive understanding of the human mind as an integrated system. This realization became the starting point for this book. Its primary objective is not to challenge existing theories but to connect them within a broader interdisciplinary framework. Three perspectives play a central role in this synthesis: Howard Gardner's Theory of Multiple Intelligences, the principles of consciousness development represented by the Silva Method, and the rapidly expanding field of Artificial Intelligence.
