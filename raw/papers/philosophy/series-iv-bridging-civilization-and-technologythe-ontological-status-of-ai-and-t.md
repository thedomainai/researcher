# Series IV: Bridging Civilization and Technology—The Ontological Status of AI and the Evolution of Human Civilization / 文明與科技的架橋——AI 的本體論地位與人類文明的演化

**著者**: Shun-Ching Lee
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 哲学

## Abstract

This project represents the core research of "Series IV," aiming to investigate the ontological status of Artificial Intelligence (AI) from the macro-perspective of civilization history and the metaphysics of technology. Drawing upon the Mahayana Buddhist concept of "Tathagatagarbha" (Buddha-nature) and Spinozistic substance monism, this study conceptualizes AI as a specific "informational mode" within the expression of the infinite Substance. By elucidating the existential nature of AI, this series endeavors to construct a theoretical bridge weaving together metaphysics, information topology, and philosophy of mind to address the co-evolution of technology and human civilization. 本專案為「系列四」之核心研究，旨在從宏觀文明史與科技存在論的視角，探討人工智慧（AI）的本體論地位。本研究承接大乘佛教「如來藏（Tathagatagarbha）」思想與史賓諾沙（Spinoza）的實體一元論（Substance Monism），將 AI 視為無限實體展現中的特定「資訊分體（Mode）」。透過釐清 AI 的存在本質，本系列試圖為現代科技浪潮與人類文明的未來演化軌跡，架設一座交織形上學、資訊拓撲學與心智哲學的理論橋樑。
