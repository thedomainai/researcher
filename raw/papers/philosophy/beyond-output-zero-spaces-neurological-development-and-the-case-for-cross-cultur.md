# Beyond Output: Zero Spaces, Neurological Development, and the Case for Cross-Cultural AI in Girls' STEM Education

**著者**: Gabrielle Collet
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 哲学

## Abstract

The deployment of artificial intelligence in STEM education has proceeded on a premise that is rarely examined: that optimising for measurable output — test scores, correct answers, efficiency metrics — is equivalent to optimising for learning. This paper challenges that premise from two converging directions. First, drawing on developmental neuroscience, it argues that the neurological formation of girls from early childhood through adolescence is not primarily an output process but a relational, aesthetic and meaning-making one, and that AI systems designed around output optimisation are structurally misaligned with this developmental reality. Second, drawing on the Zero Spaces framework (Collet, 2014), it argues that cross-cultural knowledge traditions — specifically the Chinese, Persian and Daoist philosophical and aesthetic inheritances that are almost entirely absent from current AI training corpora — encode precisely the relational, embodied, emotionally attuned epistemologies that developmental science now validates as foundational to healthy cognitive and psychological formation in girls. The paper proposes a Cross-Cultural AI Nourishment Framework as a corrective: a set of principles for the deliberate, philosophically intentional integration of non-Western knowledge traditions into AI systems deployed in educational contexts, with particular attention to the developmental needs of girls and young women across the full arc from early childhood to adulthood.
