# Rebooting The Hindu Mind

**著者**: Subhas Mitra
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 哲学

## Abstract

This book explores the connection between ancient Hindu philosophy and the modern worlds of Information Technology and Artificial Intelligence. Using concepts such as the Trigunas—Sattva, Rajas, and Tamas—it examines human consciousness, conditioning, self-awareness, and the influence of history on our thinking. Through metaphors of data, malware, memory, and source code, the book encourages readers to examine their inner programming, question inherited beliefs, reconnect with Vedic wisdom, and discover a clearer sense of self. A thought-provoking journey into philosophy, spirituality, consciousness, and the “Human AI,” this book offers a distinctive perspective on the art of living and inner transformation.
