# SABRINAL EL-RAKHAWI DOCUMENT: CONSCIOUSNESS, ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE, AND LAW Foundations: Biology, Mind, and Legal Systems

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

This foundational document explores the intersection of consciousness, artificial intelligence, and law through three comprehensive books. Book One traces biological evolution from primordial replicators to human consciousness, examining genetic engineering and legal personhood. Book Two investigates artificial intelligence from neural networks to large language models, addressing consciousness, ethics, and accountability. Book Three analyzes legal systems, intellectual property, liability frameworks, and technology regulation. Dedicated to the author's daughter Sabrinal, this work bridges biology, neuroscience, computer science, law, philosophy, and ethics to navigate humanity's technological future. It provides policymakers with actionable recommendations for AI governance while raising profound questions about consciousness, rights, and what defines humanity in an age of artificial intelligence. This interdisciplinary resource serves researchers, legal scholars, and anyone concerned with ensuring technology remains a servant to human dignity.
