# Making Sense of Responsible AI: An Institutional Complexity Perspective

**著者**: Benjamin Brauer, Eryk Maciej Wiecki
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 哲学

## Abstract

Responsible AI has received increasing attention, yet its implementation in organizational contexts remains insufficiently understood. This study examines how organizations experience and respond to challenges in Responsible AI by conducting a structured synthesis of prior research. The findings reveal that Responsible AI is shaped by interdependent demands related to innovation, compliance, and ethics, which give rise to persistent challenges that cannot be fully resolved. Building on this, the study conceptualizes AI governance as a process of mediating competing demands rather than translating principles into practice, and shows that organizational responses are constrained to forms of ongoing balancing, such as alignment, compromise, and workarounds. By integrating these insights, the study advances Responsible AI research beyond principle-based discussions and provides a more grounded understanding of how organizations navigate complexity in the governance of emerging digital technologies.
