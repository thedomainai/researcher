# AI-Assisted Research Competency in Secondary Education: A Framework for Epistemic Agency, Authorship and Responsible Knowledge Production

**著者**: Mathieu Beau, Mehdi Lazar, Brice Flaquiere
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 哲学

## Abstract

Generative artificial intelligence (AI) can support student research while obscuring whether learners have performed the intellectual work an inquiry is meant to develop. This conceptual article proposes AI-assisted research competency (AARC): the integrated capacity to conduct inquiry with AI without surrendering authorship, judgment, verification, or intellectual responsibility. Grounded in virtue epistemology, AI literacy and AI intuition, AARC comprises seven teachable dimensions and develops from guided foundation through cross-domain transfer to autonomous inquiry. The framework translates the construct into an analytic rubric, a five-step decision routine, and three recurring commitments: verify, cite, and reflect. A design case across upper-school courses illustrates how teachers can scaffold tool selection, source verification, synthesis, disclosure, and reflective defense, culminating in a secondary STEM research seminar. We argue that assessing the research process, rather than only its final product, can cultivate epistemic agency and responsible authorship while establishing coherent institutional expectations for AI use.
