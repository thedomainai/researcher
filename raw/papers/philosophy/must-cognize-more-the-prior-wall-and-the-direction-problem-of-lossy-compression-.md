# Must-Cognize-More: The Prior Wall and the Direction Problem of Lossy Compression / 不得不认知更多:先验墙与有损压缩的方向问题

**著者**: Han Qin
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 哲学

## Abstract

Description This paper develops the second a priori condition of SAE epistemology: must-cognize-more. It argues that cognition cannot stop for two structural reasons — knowing accumulates past tolerance (intake compulsion) and lossy compression cannot cover the next novelty (output compulsion). The paper distinguishes "more information" from "more levels of lossy compression," identifies the prior wall as a structural bottleneck where chiseling occurs but its direction is locked by the old framework, and uses Meta as the paradigm case and the Aleph thought experiment (Borges, 1945) as the extreme counter-case.
