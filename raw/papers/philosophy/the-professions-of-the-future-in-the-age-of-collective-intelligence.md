# The Professions of the Future in the Age of Collective Intelligence

**著者**: Hüseyin Okan Durmuş
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 哲学

## Abstract

This paper explores how the rise of artificial intelligence and collective intelligence may reshape the professions of the future. Rather than focusing solely on which occupations will disappear or emerge, it argues that the fundamental transformation will occur through the redistribution of tasks between humans and intelligent systems. The paper examines the concept of collective intelligence, discusses how AI can enhance or undermine institutional decision-making, and proposes emerging professional roles such as Human–AI Workflow Architects, Collective Intelligence Architects, AI Validation and Assurance Specialists, Organisational Epistemology Specialists, Decision Auditors, and Algorithmic Metrology Specialists. It further argues that future economic competitiveness will increasingly depend on the ability of societies and institutions to integrate human expertise, organisational knowledge, and artificial intelligence into reliable systems of shared decision-making.
