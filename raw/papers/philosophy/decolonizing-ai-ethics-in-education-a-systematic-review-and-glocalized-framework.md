# Decolonizing AI Ethics in Education: A Systematic Review and Glocalized Framework (FGAIEE)

**著者**: Dwi Mariyono, Muhammad Yunus, Akmal Nur Alif Hidayatullah
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: 哲学

## Abstract

AI integration in education has intensified efforts to establish ethical guidelines, yet prevailing frameworks remain shaped by Global North epistemologies, universalist assumptions, and centralized governance. Consequently, ethical principles rarely translate into contextually legitimate or enforceable practices in Global South and postcolonial settings. This study critically examines how AI ethics in education is produced and operationalized across diverse contexts. Using a PRISMA-guided systematic review of 84 studies (2015–2025) with integrated PICo–Thematic synthesis, the analysis reveals three core findings: (1) AI ethics frameworks are centralized in Global North institutions with limited participatory governance and persistent algorithmic bias; (2) pluriversal practices grounded in Indigenous knowledge, communal ethics, and culturally embedded pedagogies have emerged as viable counter-models; and (3) ethical effectiveness correlates with governance mechanisms embedding ethics into participation, procurement, and accountability—not voluntary principle adoption. The study advances the Framework for Glocalized AI Ethics in Education (FGAIEE), reconceptualizing AI ethics as multi-scalar governance integrating epistemic pluriversality, participatory oversight, and structural enforceability. Rather than proposing another universal model, FGAIEE enables ethical coordination between global infrastructures and local values—moving AI ethics from symbolic compliance toward structural redress through epistemic justice and institutional design.
