# From the discovery of the neutron to Machine Learning: The scientific and technological evolution of nuclear engineering

**著者**: Carlos Mario Custodio-Vázquez, Youness El Hamzaoui, Juan Antonio Álvarez-Arellano, Zujey Berenice Cuevas-Carballo
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Nuclear engineering has evolved for more than a century through scientific discoveries that progressively transformed the understanding of atomic structure and the utilization of nuclear energy. The objective of this article is to present a historical and scientific perspective on the evolution of nuclear engineering, from the discovery of X-rays and radioactivity to the development of computational methods based on reactor physics, Monte Carlo simulations, and artificial intelligence. The methodology consisted of a documentary review of seminal scientific publications and recent specialized literature, analyzed chronologically to relate major scientific milestones with the evolution of computational tools. The review describes the transition from deterministic methods and Monte Carlo simulations to Machine Learning, Physics-Informed Neural Networks, Digital Twins, and Explainable Artificial Intelligence, highlighting that these technologies complement traditional physics-based models rather than replacing them. This review integrates historical, scientific, technological, and computational advances into a coherent narrative of modern nuclear engineering evolution.
