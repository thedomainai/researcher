# The Impact Of Artificial Intelligence (AI) On The Labor Market In The Banking And Finance Sector

**著者**: 
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 技術史

## Abstract

The article interviewed and discussed financial and banking business groups that use artificial intelligence (AI) and explored the impact of AI on the labor market. It also provided recommendations for business managers using AI and for state regulatory agencies to limit the negative effects of AI systems on Vietnam's labor market.
