# How GenAI Adoption Affects Job Demand: Evidence from India

**著者**: Derek Tran, Probal Mojumder, Sunil Mithas, Samadrita Bhattacharyya
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 技術史

## Abstract

How does Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) affect labor markets in emerging economies? We address this important question leveraging data from a leading job postings platform in India and exploiting ChatGPT’s launch in November 2022 as a quasi-natural experiment. The findings indicate that GenAI adoption reshapes labor demand through differential effects across GenAI-exposed versus non-exposed occupations. We document the effect of GenAI on jobs across IT outsourcing (ITO)/business process outsourcing (BPO) jobs versus other industries, and partial mediation through GenAI exposure. The study contributes to debates on technological displacement versus skill transformation, offering implications for policy design, and workforce reskilling strategies.
