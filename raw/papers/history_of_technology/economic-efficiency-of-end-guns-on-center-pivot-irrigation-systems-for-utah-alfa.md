# Economic Efficiency of End-Guns on Center Pivot Irrigation Systems for Utah Alfalfa Production

**著者**: Sophia Moss
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 技術史

## Abstract

This study looks at whether using an end-gun on a center pivot irrigation system is financially worthwhile for Utah alfalfa producers. End-guns are used to water extra acreage beyond the end of a pivot, they can also apply water less evenly, which may reduce production on those outer acres. To examine this tradeoff, the study compares farm returns with and without an end-gun using historical Utah alfalfa price and yield data along with estimates of irrigation cost, added acreage, and possible yield loss. The results suggest that end-guns can improve profitability in many cases, mainly because they bring more acres into production. However, the benefit depends heavily on how well those outer acres perform. When yield losses on end-gun acres are low, the end-gun is more likely to provide an economic advantage. These findings can help Utah alfalfa producers better evaluate whether end-gun use fits their irrigation and management conditions.
