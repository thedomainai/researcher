# Artificial Intelligence, Process Automation, Interoperability and Cost-benefit: Where Lies the Problem with the Effectiveness of Brazilian Justice?

**著者**: Valter Moura do Carmo, Jefferson Patrik Germinari and
Fernando Galindo Ayuda

**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 技術史

## Abstract

Numerous transformations have been recorded in the Brazilian Judiciary, whether regarding internal aspects of its structural system or exogenous reasons, as denoted by the advent of the digital era and the increase in judicial demands, a consequence of the ease with which personal transactions have come to be conducted; electronic judicial proceedings have become established in the Courts as an alternative mechanism to mitigate such difficulties. Notwithstanding the automation promoted, in view of the increase in pending cases, this study aims, through the deductive research method, to identify the inconsistencies existing in the current scenario, analyzing how artificial intelligence resources, automation, and interoperability can become interesting alternatives for the productivity of clerical tasks, contributing to greater procedural fluidity and guaranteeing the effectiveness of the delivery of justice desired by all, also verifying the cost-benefit of the modernization of the Judiciary in comparison to expenditures on human resources and the amortization of the budgetary impact, a reflection of the globalized economic crisis that has reached the Brazilian Courts.
