# Fortalecimiento institucional del Estado para la implementación de la política pública de Inteligencia Artificial en la administración de justicia

**著者**: Éddison David Castrillón García
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 技術史

## Abstract

This article aims to analyze how decentralization and territorial development, together with good governance and the selection of appropriate public policy instruments, contribute to strengthening the State's institutional capacity for the implementation of artificial intelligence (AI) policy in the digitization of justice in Colombia. To achieve this purpose, a qualitative research was conducted that included the hermeneutic paradigm and the historical neo-institutionalism approach, combined with the analytical-deductive approach. This research was carried out based on the following objectives: first, to review public policy approaches as tools for analyzing policies articulated to the territorial context; second, to study technology and AI policy in Colombia in light of the challenges of implementing AI in the Administration of Justice; and third, to analyze the most relevant aspects in strengthening the State’s institutional capacity for implementing AI public policy in the digitalization of justice in Colombia. Based on the research findings, it was concluded that the commitment to territorial development involves establishing guidelines on three specific fronts to strengthen State capacities: decentralization in the face of trends toward recentralization of the State in Colombia, governance, and the selection of appropriate public policy instruments aligned with the general guidelines for implementing AI policy in the administration of justice.
