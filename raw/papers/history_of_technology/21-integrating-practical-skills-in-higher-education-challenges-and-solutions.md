# 21. Integrating Practical Skills in Higher Education: Challenges and Solutions

**著者**: 
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 技術史

## Abstract

In the contemporary global and digital landscape, the objective of higher education is no longer confined to imparting theoretical knowledge alone; it is directly intertwined with a student's employability and practical skills.This research chapter presents a critical and pragmatic analysis of the widening gap between academic degrees and actual competence within the Indian higher education system.The primary objective of this study is to identify the fundamental challenges that impede the path of skill development at the college level.The study rev0eals that routine issues such as the 'rote learning culture' fostered by the traditional examination system, unavailability of resources, excessive administrative burden on teachers, and a lack of synergy between industry and academia are rendering students practically unequipped.To address these challenges, this chapter proposes highly practical and low-cost solutions that standard educational institutions can easily incorporate into their daily routines.Prominent among these are restructuring the evaluation system (emphasizing projects and presentations), organizing regular sessions for soft skills, establishing local-connect initiatives with regional industries and organizations, and involving alumni as active mentors.In conclusion, this chapter emphasizes that the core objectives of the National Education Policy (NEP 2020) can only be realized when we transition knowledge from textbooks into the tangible skills and behaviour of students.This study will serve as a practical guide for policymakers, faculty members, and institutional administrators alike.
