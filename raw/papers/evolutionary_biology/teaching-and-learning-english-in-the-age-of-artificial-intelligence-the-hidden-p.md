# TEACHING AND LEARNING ENGLISH IN THE AGE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE: THE HIDDEN POWER OF UNIVERSITY LIBRARY COLLECTIONS

**著者**: Tetiana Serhiienko, Olena Lysenko
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 進化生物学・文化進化

## Abstract

The article is devoted to the development of an effective strategy for teaching and learning English in the context of the active implementation of artificial intelligence technologies.The integration of AI tools into higher education makes it necessary to reconsider traditional approaches to language instruction and to determine effective ways of combining innovative digital instruments with established academic resources.The objective of the study is to develop and test the hybrid model "compass map", which combines the adaptive capabilities of artificial intelligence with authentic university library resources.The methodology of the research includes a theoretical analysis of scientific sources devoted to artificial intelligence in education and modern language pedagogy, a comparative review of the evolution of educational technologies, and a pedagogical experiment conducted in two higher education institutions of Ukraine.The empirical component involved standardized language proficiency tests to assess students' competence, questionnaires to evaluate their motivation and attitudes toward AIsupported learning, and statistical processing of the obtained results in order to ensure their validity and reliability.The data obtained showed that students who were taught according to the hybrid model demonstrated significantly higher indicators of language proficiency and learning motivation compared to the control group.The implemented model proved its effectiveness in combining a personalized AI-based approach with the cultural depth and reliability of university library collections.Such integration contributes to the formation of critical thinking, academic integrity, and improvement of the overall quality of English language training in higher education institutions.
