# Consciousness as the Self Reflection of Cosmic Laws

**著者**: Jiaqing Yan
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 進化生物学・文化進化

## Abstract

Academic and philosophical circles have long lacked a self consistent physical explanation for the origin essence and diversity of products of consciousness. Most existing studies are limited to descriptions of neuroscience phenomena or philosophical speculations. They often deify and particularize human consciousness, overemphasize the subjective experience of carbon based life, and separate the homology of biological cognition and silicon based system cognition. Few explain why the same conscious system can produce completely different ideological achievements such as science shamanism theology religion and agnosticism. When touching on ultimate questions related to consciousness many researchers in scientific research finally turn to the standpoint of agnosticism. Based on the theory of the origin of light this paper establishes a unified model of signal coding and law cognition for consciousness and puts forward the core proposition. Consciousness is essentially the response and summary of complex material systems to the underlying cosmic signals and the self reflection of cosmic laws. Consciousness is not a mysterious innate gift. It emerges after material structures become increasingly complex and develop the capabilities of signal collection memory induction and feedback. Simple substances such as stones can only passively bear external effects. They cannot retain signals nor extract laws. The emergence of neural networks gradually upgrades scattered stress responses into extraction storage output and communication of the laws of the world. As a general set consciousness has no inherent hierarchy or nobility. Its outputs only fall into two categories correct reflection and distorted deviation. Science is only a subset among many reflections of consciousness. It relies on empirical verification and continuous iteration to approach objective reality. Shamanism theology and religion are projective reflections shaped by information conditions cultural backgrounds and individual spiritual pursuits. Agnosticism is an ideological choice when consciousness hits the boundary of existing theories and observation means. Human selfish instincts survival obsessions and species superiority are not essential elements of consciousness. They are additional products constrained by aging loss and extinction of carbon based life. The binary coding of activation and silence of human neurons and the binary coding of zero and one in artificial intelligence share the same underlying information processing paradigm. Their differences lie in carrier material system complexity signal dimension and survival closed loop. There is no insurmountable physical barrier between them. Restricted by observation tools and experimental methods scientific cognition cannot reach all ultimate truths at once. It can only be revised and iterated to move toward deeper physical foundations. This paper combines statements about God from Newton Einstein and Yang Zhenning to analyze the psychological mechanism of misinterpretation based on spiritual sustenance and breaks the inherent prejudice that carbon based instincts are taken as the criterion for advanced consciousness. This model unifies science faith agnosticism carbon based cognition and silicon based cognition under the framework of signal coding and law reflection. It provides a new theoretical perspective for understanding the essence of consciousness the evolution of human machine cognition and the origin of diverse human thoughts.
