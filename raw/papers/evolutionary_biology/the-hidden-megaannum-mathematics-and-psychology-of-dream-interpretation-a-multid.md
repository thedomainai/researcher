# The Hidden Megaannum Mathematics and Psychology of Dream Interpretation: A Multidisciplinary Framework

**著者**: Ismail A Mageed, Amelia Mason
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 進化生物学・文化進化

## Abstract

For millions of years of human evolution, the phenomenon of dreaming reflects a deep confluence of evolutionary biology, cognitive psychology, and sophisticated systems mathematics. This study presents a multidisciplinary approach meant to decipher the psychology and maths of "megaannum" present in dream interpretation. We present a cohesive way to chart the unconscious mind by combining spatiotemporal fluid dynamics models, cognitive-phenomenological theories of intentionality, and bias-aware socio-technical approaches. While reducing cultural and subjective biases, our suggested design transforms the ongoing neural flow of the dreaming state into discrete, understandable psychological meanings. By means of this fresh synthesis, we show how sophisticated computer approaches may reveal the hidden semantic structures of nighttime awareness, therefore opening new avenues for medicinal intervention and artificial intelligence design.
