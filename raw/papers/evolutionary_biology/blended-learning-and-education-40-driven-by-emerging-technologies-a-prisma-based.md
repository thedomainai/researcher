# Blended learning and education 4.0 driven by emerging technologies: A PRISMA-based bibli-ometric review and future research directions

**著者**: Mst. Nowshin Tabassum, Fahim Hossain, S. M. Ashraful Alam, Md. Mehedi Hasan Mredul, Ahona Talukder
**年**: 2027 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

This study acknowledges the fragmented nature of blended learning and Education 4.0 research across various disciplines, technologies, and educational contexts, and aims to provide a bibliometric overview of publication trends, thematic evolution, collaboration patterns, and future research directions. To do this, PRISMA-based bibliometric techniques using Biblioshiny and RStudio were applied, analyzing 2,155 Scopus-indexed publications published between 2022 and 2025 to identify publication trends, leading journals, country and institutional contributions, collaboration networks, conceptual structural map, keyword cloud, and keyword co-occurrence patterns. It found a steady growth in publications, as research numbers were rising from 576 in 2023 to 605 in 2024, indicating increasing interest among scholars in this area. The findings also reveal the dominance of China, the USA, and the UK in publication volume and global collaboration, while blended learning, higher education, and e-learning were the most prominent themes. This study offers implications for policymakers and educators to strengthen digital infrastructure, institutional readiness, and inclusive pedagogical design. Finally, this study acknowledges it’s limitations of Scopus-only coverage, English-language bias, and keyword restrictions, and suggests multi-database, multilingual, and mixed-method approaches for future research.
