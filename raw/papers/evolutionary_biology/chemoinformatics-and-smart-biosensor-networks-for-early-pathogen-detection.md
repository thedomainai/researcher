# Chemoinformatics and smart biosensor networks for early pathogen detection

**著者**: Karina Amerbek
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 進化生物学・文化進化

## Abstract

Clinically, diagnosis based on purulent exudate, deepening probing depths, and radiographic radiolucency remains critically lagged: pathological changes do not appear until there is irreversible compromise of the tissues, with the patient often being asymptomatic and radiographs unremarkable. The delayed response of molecular level dysbiosis compared to clinical presentation is the main diagnostic failure in today's implant care. Peri-implant crevicular fluid, saliva, volatile organic compounds and microbial metabolites contain extensive molecular profiles of the early stages of peri-implant infection, but there is no current clinical platform that integrates these signals in real-time. This article suggests a thorough review to investigate the potential of convergent smart nanobiomaterials based biosensor networks and AI supported chemoinformatics as a 'molecular detective' that can identify the chemical signature of evolving microbial communities and the imminent immune rejection before any clinical symptoms are observed. Therefore, through synthesizing recent studies, this article presents the need for the construction of a diagnostic investigation model. This is a framework that will move implantology from the retro diagnosis to the predictive, preventive, personalized and dynamically monitored precision care.
