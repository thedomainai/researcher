# Who Stays When AI Answers? Core-Periphery Resilience in Online Knowledge Communities

**著者**: Leping Li, Ruoxi Zhang, Shitao Zeng, Yash Raj Shrestha
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 進化生物学・文化進化

## Abstract

Generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) offers a low-cost substitute for community-based knowledge exchange, but its disruptive effects may vary across users and task contexts. Drawing on social exchange theory, embeddedness, and task-technology fit, we examine how ChatGPT’s public release reshaped participation and contribution in online knowledge communities. Using 52-week user-level panels covering 558,980 users on Stack Overflow and Mathematics Stack Exchange, we estimate difference-in-differences and triple-differences models. Peripheral users experienced sharper participation declines than core users, with the relative decline 15.4% greater on Stack Overflow than on Mathematics Stack Exchange (p = .009), consistent with stronger GenAI substitutability for programming tasks. Among users who remained active, peripheral users elaborated their contributions further, with average post length rising by 40.7 more characters, an increase unchanged when the sample is restricted to users active in both periods. These findings show that GenAI reconfigures who participates and how remaining users contribute, revealing differential resilience shaped by embeddedness and task-technology fit.
