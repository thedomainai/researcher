# Concept OF Creating A French-Uzbek Dictionary OF Chemical Terminology

**著者**: B. O. (Bobokalonov) Ostonovich, M. M. (Madinakhon) qizi, H. N. (Hasanova) Allokulovna
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 進化生物学・文化進化

## Abstract

This scientific article explores the conceptualization of chemistry terminology in the French and Uzbek languages, aiming to shed light on the linguistic and cultural nuances that shape the understanding of scientific concepts in these two languages. The study employs a comparative analysis to examine how key chemistry terms are translated and conceptualized in French and Uzbek, considering the influence of linguistic structures, cultural contexts, and historical developments on the representation of scientific ideas.
