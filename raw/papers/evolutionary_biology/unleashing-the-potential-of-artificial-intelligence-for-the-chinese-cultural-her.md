# Unleashing the Potential of Artificial Intelligence for the Chinese Cultural Heritage of the Forbidden City in China

**著者**: Poshan Yu, Jiawen Guan
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 進化生物学・文化進化

## Abstract

As technology rapidly advances, artificial intelligence (AI) is increasingly used in cultural heritage. Using CiteSpace, this study analyzes three - decade - long domestic and international literature on cultural heritage and AI, uncovering knowledge evolution, paradigm shifts, and research gaps. With the Forbidden City as a case, it examines two decades of AI - related practices and their effects on cultural heritage's production, communication, and identity. AI drives collaborative co - creation in production, enhances cultural - centered communication, and enables participatory cultural identity construction. The research concludes that for successful AI - based cultural heritage transformation, a balance between technological innovation and cultural essence is essential. Also, an inclusive “production - communication - identity” system must be established to meet stakeholder needs and safeguard cultural heritage in the digital age.
