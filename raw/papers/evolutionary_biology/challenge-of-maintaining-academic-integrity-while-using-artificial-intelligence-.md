# Challenge of maintaining academic integrity while using Artificial intelligence in research: Potential, pitfalls, and accountability

**著者**: Avinash Supe, Chinmay Shah
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 進化生物学・文化進化

## Abstract

Introduction: Artificial intelligence (AI) is revolutionizing research methodology across all stages of the research process, from hypothesis generation to dissemination of findings. However, the integration of AI tools in research requires careful consideration of benefits, limitations, and ethical implications.Objective: To provide a comprehensive overview of AI applications in research, examine selection criteria for appropriate tools, identify potential pitfalls, and review current guidelines for responsible AI use in academic research.Methodology: This narrative review synthesizes current literature on AI applications in research methodology, examines available AI tools across different research phases, and analyzes existing guidelines from major journals and organizations including COPE and EQUATOR. It also discussed various practical ways of mainating academic integrity while using artificia intelligence in researchResults: AI significantly enhances research efficiency through automated literature searches, hypothesis generation, study design optimization, data collection and analysis, and manuscript preparation. Key applications include semantic literature analysis using tools like Semantic Scholar and Consensus, automated data entry with OCR/NLP technologies, predictive modeling with platforms like Tableau and RapidMiner, and plagiarism detection systems. However, AI implementation presents challenges including algorithmic bias, lack of standardized guidelines, authenticity concerns, and potential authorship disputes. There is need for proper disclosure of AI tools for maintaining academic integrity while presenting and publishing.Conclusions: While AI offers substantial benefits for research productivity and quality, responsible implementation requires human oversight, critical evaluation, and adherence to emerging guidelines. Researchers must balance AI's capabilities with traditional scientific rigor to maintain research integrity and academic standards.
