TRI-COUPLING SYSTEM EVOLUTION THEORY II is a high-density research monographand the second evolutionary generation of Evolutionary Coupling Systems Science. Volume I formalized the tri-coupling as a versioned research object with variables,relationship structure, evidence state, evolutionary history, mutation, branching,selection, coupling ecology, Research Gates, AI-assisted evolution, governance,and successor handoff. Volume II begins where a single triad becomes insufficient. It asks how many locally testable couplings can form modules, networks, branches,and research ecologies while keeping evidence, failure, topology change,transfer, and handoff scientifically legible. Its flagship Three-Coupling System is: Network Legibilityx Evolutionary Adaptabilityx Successor Reproducibility The proposed Adaptive Coupling Network Integrity Index (ACNII) is: ACNII =(Cross-Coupling Traceabilityx Modular Adaptationx Failure Localizationx Successor Reproducibility)/(1 + Network Opacity+ Cascade Risk+ Complexity Debt+ Lock-In) ACNII is a proposed research heuristic and hypothesis-generating metric. It is not a validated scientific law, universal knowledge score,intelligence metric, psychometric scale, optimization target,or regulatory standard.
