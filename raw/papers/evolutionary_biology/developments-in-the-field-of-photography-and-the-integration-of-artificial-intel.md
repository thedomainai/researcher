# Developments in the Field of Photography and the Integration of Artificial Intelligence and Smart Technologies in a Developed India

**著者**: Mangesh Krishnarao Raut
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 進化生物学・文化進化

## Abstract

Abstract Developments in the field of photography and the contribution of artificial intelligence have been remarkable in a developing India. The journey from early optical and chemical processes to digital visual communication has been significant. Technologies ranging from everyday applications to film, digital photography, smart photography, cloud platforms, machine learning, generative AI, drones, and IoT devices (such as Alexa) have vastly expanded the possibilities for capturing, processing, interpreting, and sharing images. Modern photography is no longer limited to just cameras, lenses, digital sensors, and photographers; smart technologies are increasingly playing a pivotal role in processes such as focusing, exposure, color correction, noise reduction, dynamic range enhancement, subject recognition, image segmentation, autofocus, image enhancement, and post-production. Research into AI and photography indicates that machine learning and computational processing have transformed the traditional boundaries between image capture and image processing. In India, these changes are unfolding within a digital framework. The Government of India’s 'IndiaAI' mission received approval in March 2024, aiming to strengthen computing infrastructure, data availability, AI innovation, and world-class development. This emerging technology ecosystem directly impacts photography and visual media education, as well as professional fields such as journalism, advertising, fashion product imaging, scientific documentation, wildlife and heritage conservation, and the creative industries. Furthermore, generative AI is driving a profound transformation; images can now be enhanced, recreated, modified, or generated from scratch without every visible element being directly linked to an optical phenomenon. This opens up new creative opportunities. However, issues regarding authenticity, the author's copyright, privacy, consent, employment, cultural representation, and public trust also arise. This research paper examines the historical evolution of photography, the opportunities for India in the field of photography alongside smart technology, the challenges faced by photographers and educators, and a proposed framework for responsible photography in a developing India.
