# Physical Processes in a Convergent Theory of the Weak and Electromagnetic Interactions

**著者**: Steven Weinberg
**年**: 1971 | **被引用数**: 606
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 素粒子・場の量子論

## Abstract

A previously proposed theory of leptonic weak and electromagnetic interactions is found to be free of the divergence difficulties present in conventional models. The experimental implications of this theory and its extension to hadrons are briefly discussed.
