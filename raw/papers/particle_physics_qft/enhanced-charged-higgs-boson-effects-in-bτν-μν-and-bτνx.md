# Enhanced charged Higgs boson effects in B−→τν¯, μν¯ and b→τν¯+X

**著者**: W.-S. Hou
**年**: 1993 | **被引用数**: 496
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 素粒子・場の量子論

## Abstract

Charged Higgs boson effects are not helicity suppressed in the decays ${B}^{\ensuremath{-}}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{\tau}\overline{\ensuremath{\nu}}, \ensuremath{\mu}\overline{\ensuremath{\nu}}, \mathrm{and} e\overline{\ensuremath{\nu}}$, and could enhance or suppress these modes without violating lepton universality. The present limit of $B({B}^{\ensuremath{-}}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{\mu}\overline{\ensuremath{\nu}})<2.0\ifmmode\times\else\texttimes\fi{}{10}^{\ensuremath{-}5}$ is more stringent than the measurement $B(b\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{\tau}\overline{\ensuremath{\nu}}+X)=({4.20}_{\ensuremath{-}0.68}^{+0.72}\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}0.46)%$, while a factor of 2 improvement in the present limit of $B({B}^{\ensuremath{-}}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\ensuremath{\tau}\overline{\ensuremath{\nu}})<1.2%$ would be comparable. There is much room for discovery as one pushes the latter limit down to the standard model expectation of order ${10}^{\ensuremath{-}4}$. A value below this may be explained without requiring ${f}_{B}|{V}_{\mathrm{ub}}|$ to be unduly small.
