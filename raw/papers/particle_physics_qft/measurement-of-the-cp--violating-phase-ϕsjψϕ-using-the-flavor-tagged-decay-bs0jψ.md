# Measurement of the CP -violating phase ϕsJ/ψϕ using the flavor-tagged decay Bs0→J/ψϕ in 8 fb−1 of pp¯ collisions

**著者**: V. M. Abazov, B. Abbott, B. S. Acharya, M. R. Adams, T. Adams (+95)
**年**: 2012 | **被引用数**: 220
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 素粒子・場の量子論

## Abstract

We report an updated measurement of the $CP$-violating phase, ${\ensuremath{\phi}}_{s}^{J/\ensuremath{\psi}\ensuremath{\phi}}$, and the decay-width difference for the two mass eigenstates, $\ensuremath{\Delta}{\ensuremath{\Gamma}}_{s}$, from the flavor-tagged decay ${B}_{s}^{0}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}J/\ensuremath{\psi}\ensuremath{\phi}$. The data sample corresponds to an integrated luminosity of $8.0\text{ }\text{ }{\mathrm{fb}}^{\ensuremath{-}1}$ accumulated with the D0 detector using $p\overline{p}$ collisions at $\sqrt{s}=1.96\text{ }\text{ }\mathrm{TeV}$ produced at the Fermilab Tevatron collider. The 68% Bayesian credibility intervals, including systematic uncertainties, are $\ensuremath{\Delta}{\ensuremath{\Gamma}}_{s}={0.163}_{\ensuremath{-}0.064}^{+0.065}\text{ }\text{ }{\mathrm{ps}}^{\ensuremath{-}1}$ and ${\ensuremath{\phi}}_{s}^{J/\ensuremath{\psi}\ensuremath{\phi}}=\ensuremath{-}{0.55}_{\ensuremath{-}0.36}^{+0.38}$. The $p$-value for the Standard Model point is 29.8%.
