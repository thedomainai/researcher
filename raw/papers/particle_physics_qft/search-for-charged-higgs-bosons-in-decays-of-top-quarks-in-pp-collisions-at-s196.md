# Search for Charged Higgs Bosons in Decays of Top Quarks in pp¯ Collisions at s=1.96 TeV

**著者**: T. Aaltonen, J. Adelman, Takashi AKIMOTO, B. Álvarez González, S. Amerio (+95)
**年**: 2009 | **被引用数**: 189
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 素粒子・場の量子論

## Abstract

We report on the first direct search for charged Higgs bosons decaying into $c\overline{s}$ in $t\overline{t}$ events produced by $p\overline{p}$ collisions at $\sqrt{s}=1.96\text{ }\text{ }\mathrm{TeV}$. The search uses a data sample corresponding to an integrated luminosity of $2.2\text{ }\text{ }{\mathrm{fb}}^{\ensuremath{-}1}$ collected by the CDF II detector at Fermilab and looks for a resonance in the invariant mass distribution of two jets in the $\mathrm{\text{lepton}}+\mathrm{\text{jets}}$ sample of $t\overline{t}$ candidates. We observe no evidence of charged Higgs bosons in top quark decays. Hence, 95% upper limits on the top quark decay branching ratio are placed at $\mathcal{B}(t\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{H}^{+}b)<$ 0.1 to 0.3 for charged Higgs boson masses of 60 to $150\text{ }\text{ }\mathrm{GeV}/{c}^{2}$ assuming $\mathcal{B}({H}^{+}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}c\overline{s})=1.0$. The upper limits on $\mathcal{B}(t\ensuremath{\rightarrow}{H}^{+}b)$ are also used as model-independent limits on the decay branching ratio of top quarks to generic scalar charged bosons beyond the standard model.
