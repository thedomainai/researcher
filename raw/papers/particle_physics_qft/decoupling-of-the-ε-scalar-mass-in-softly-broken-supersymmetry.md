# Decoupling of the ε-scalar mass in softly broken supersymmetry

**著者**: I. Jack, D.R.T. Jones, Stephen P. Martin, Michael T. Vaughn, Youichi Yamada
**年**: 1994 | **被引用数**: 200
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 素粒子・場の量子論

## Abstract

It has been shown recently that the introduction of an unphysical \ensuremath{\epsilon}-scalar mass m\ifmmode \tilde{}\else \~{}\fi{} is necessary for the proper renormalization of softly broken supersymmetric theories by dimensional reduction (DR). In these theories, both the two-loop \ensuremath{\beta} functions of the scalar masses and their one-loop finite corrections depend on m${\mathrm{\ifmmode \tilde{}\else \~{}\fi{}}}^{2}$. We find, however, that the dependence on m${\mathrm{\ifmmode \tilde{}\else \~{}\fi{}}}^{2}$ can be completely removed by slightly modifying the DR renormalization scheme. We also show that previous DR calculations of one-loop corrections in supersymmetry which ignored the m${\mathrm{\ifmmode \tilde{}\else \~{}\fi{}}}^{2}$ contribution correspond to using this modified scheme.
