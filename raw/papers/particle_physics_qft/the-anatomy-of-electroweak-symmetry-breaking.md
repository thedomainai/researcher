# The anatomy of electroweak symmetry breaking

**著者**: Abdelhak Djouadi
**年**: 2007 | **被引用数**: 1788
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 素粒子・場の量子論

## Abstract

This review is devoted to the study of the mechanism of electroweak symmetry breaking and this first part focuses on the Higgs particle of the Standard Model. The fundamental properties of the Higgs boson are reviewed and its decay modes and production mechanisms at hadron colliders and at future lepton colliders are described in detail.
