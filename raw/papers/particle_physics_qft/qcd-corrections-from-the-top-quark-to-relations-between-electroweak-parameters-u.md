# QCD Corrections from the Top Quark to Relations between Electroweak Parameters up to Order αs2

**著者**: K.G. Chetyrkin, Johann H. Kühn, Matthias Steinhauser
**年**: 1995 | **被引用数**: 173
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 素粒子・場の量子論

## Abstract

The vacuum polarization functions $\ensuremath{\Pi}({q}^{2})$ of charged and neutral gauge bosons that arise from top and bottom quark loops lead to important shifts in relations between electroweak parameters that can be measured with ever-increasing precision. The large mass of the top quark allows approximation of these functions through the first two terms of an expansion in ${M}_{Z}^{2}{/M}_{t}^{2}$. The first three terms of the Taylor series of $\ensuremath{\Pi}({q}^{2})$ are evaluated analytically up to order ${\ensuremath{\alpha}}_{s}^{2}$. Results for the subleading contributions to $\ensuremath{\Delta}r$ and the effective mixing angle ${sin}^{2}\overline{\ensuremath{\Theta}}$ are presented.
