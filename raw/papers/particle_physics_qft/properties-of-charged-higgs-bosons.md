# Properties of charged Higgs bosons

**著者**: John F. Donoghue, Ling-Fong Li
**年**: 1979 | **被引用数**: 359
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 素粒子・場の量子論

## Abstract

In theories with more scalar multiplets than the minimal Weinberg-Salam model, there will exist charged physical Higgs particles, in addition to the neutral Higgs particle. We examine the theory and phenomenology of such a situation as a guide for future experimental searches.
