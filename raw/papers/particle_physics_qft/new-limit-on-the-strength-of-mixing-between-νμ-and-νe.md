# New limit on the strength of mixing between νμ and νe

**著者**: L. A. Ahrens, S. H. Aronson, P. L. Connolly, Bruce Gibbard, M. J. Murtagh (+26)
**年**: 1985 | **被引用数**: 93
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 素粒子・場の量子論

## Abstract

Measurements have been made of the reactions ${\ensuremath{\nu}}_{e}$n\ensuremath{\rightarrow}${e}^{\mathrm{\ensuremath{-}}}$p and ${\ensuremath{\nu}}_{\ensuremath{\mu}}$n\ensuremath{\rightarrow}${\ensuremath{\mu}}^{\mathrm{\ensuremath{-}}}$p in a detector located an effective distance of 96 m from a neutrino source. These measurements yield directly the energy-dependent ratio of the neutrino fluxes [\ensuremath{\Phi}(E(${\ensuremath{\nu}}_{e}$))/\ensuremath{\Phi}(E(${\ensuremath{\nu}}_{\ensuremath{\mu}}$${))\phantom{\rule{0ex}{0ex}}]}_{\mathrm{obs}}$ incident on the detector. When combined with an estimate of the flux ratio emanating from the source [\ensuremath{\Phi}(E(${\ensuremath{\nu}}_{e}$))/\ensuremath{\Phi}(E(${\ensuremath{\nu}}_{\ensuremath{\mu}}$${))\phantom{\rule{0ex}{0ex}}]}_{\mathrm{calc}}$, the measured ratio provides an upper limit on the strength of mixing between ${\ensuremath{\nu}}_{\ensuremath{\mu}}$ and ${\ensuremath{\nu}}_{e}$. We obtain ${\mathrm{sin}}^{2}$2\ensuremath{\alpha}3.4\ifmmode\times\else\texttimes\fi{}${10}^{\mathrm{\ensuremath{-}}3}$ (90% C.L.) in the limit of large mass difference \ensuremath{\Delta}${m}^{2}$ (\ensuremath{\equiv}\ensuremath{\Vert}${m}_{1}$${}^{2}$-${m}_{2}$${}^{2}$\ensuremath{\Vert}) between neutrino mass eigenstates, ${m}_{1}$ and ${m}_{2}$; and an upper limit on the product \ensuremath{\Delta}${m}^{2}$ sin2\ensuremath{\alpha}0.43 ${\mathrm{eV}}^{2}$ in the limit of small mass difference.
