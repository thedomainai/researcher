# Basis-independent methods for the two-Higgs-doublet model. II. The significance of tanβ

**著者**: Howard E. Haber, Deva O’Neil
**年**: 2006 | **被引用数**: 240
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 素粒子・場の量子論

## Abstract

In the most general two-Higgs-doublet model (2HDM), there is no distinction between the two complex hypercharge-one $\mathrm{SU}(2{)}_{\mathrm{L}}$ doublet scalar fields, ${\ensuremath{\Phi}}_{a}$ $(a=1,2)$. Thus, any two orthonormal linear combinations of these two fields can serve as a basis for the Lagrangian. All physical observables of the model must be basis-independent. For example, $\mathrm{tan}﻿\ensuremath{\beta}\ensuremath{\equiv}⟨{\ensuremath{\Phi}}_{2}^{0}⟩/⟨{\ensuremath{\Phi}}_{1}^{0}⟩$ is basis-dependent and thus cannot be a physical parameter of the model. In this paper, we provide a basis-independent treatment of the Higgs sector with particular attention to the neutral Higgs boson mass-eigenstates, which generically are not eigenstates of $CP$. We then demonstrate that all physical Higgs couplings are indeed independent of $\mathrm{tan}﻿\ensuremath{\beta}$. In specialized versions of the 2HDM, $\mathrm{tan}﻿\ensuremath{\beta}$ can be promoted to a physical parameter of the Higgs-fermion interactions. In the most general 2HDM, the Higgs-fermion couplings can be expressed in terms of a number of physical ``$\mathrm{tan}﻿\ensuremath{\beta}$-like'' parameters that are manifestly basis-independent. The minimal supersymmetric extension of the standard model provides a simple framework for exhibiting such effects.
