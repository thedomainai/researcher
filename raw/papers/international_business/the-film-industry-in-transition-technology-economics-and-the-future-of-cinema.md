# THE FILM INDUSTRY IN TRANSITION: TECHNOLOGY, ECONOMICS, AND THE FUTURE OF CINEMA

**著者**: YuHan Xiao
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 国際経営

## Abstract

The motion picture industry has undergone a profound transformation over the past three decades, evolving from a predominantly theatrical business into a multi-platform, globalized entertainment ecosystem. This article examines the structural forces reshaping cinema: the disruption of the traditional production–distribution–exhibition value chain by streaming platforms, the changing economics of blockbuster filmmaking, the accelerating adoption of digital and AI-driven production technologies, and the growth of international markets, particularly Asia. Drawing on industrial organization theory, media economics, and recent industry data, it argues that the industry is not declining but diversifying, and that its long-term health depends on balancing technological innovation with the preservation of the theatrical experience, cultural diversity, and sustainable labor practices.
