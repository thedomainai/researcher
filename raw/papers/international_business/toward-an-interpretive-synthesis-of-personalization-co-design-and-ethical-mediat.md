# Toward an Interpretive Synthesis of Personalization, Co-Design, and Ethical Mediation in AI-Supported Higher Education: An Integrative Review of Agentic, Generative, and Adaptive Tutoring Systems

**著者**: Xuao Li, Rafiza Abdul Razak
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: meta_analysis | **分野**: 国際経営

## Abstract

Generative artificial intelligence (AI) and adaptive learning technologies are expanding in higher education, raising questions about their alignment with pedagogical goals. This qualitative-integrative review synthesizes 57 publications (21 empirical, 25 conceptual or framework, 9 systematic reviews or meta-analyses retained for triangulation, and 2 design/development papers) from 2024 to 2026, employing Whittemore and Knafl’s methodology to examine AI-supported tutoring, implementation strategies, and learner/instructor experiences. Three interpretive themes organize this heterogeneous evidence: (1) personalization as a recurring target, from micro-level adaptation to curriculum design; (2) technology-pedagogy co-design as a recurring implementation pattern; and (3) contextual and ethical conditions surrounding adoption, including culture, policy gaps, well-being, and academic integrity. These themes are interpretive, not a validated framework, and draw on evidence of uneven strength. The present review does not estimate effect sizes or pool outcomes across studies; g = 0.70, reported by X. Wang et al. in a separate meta-analysis of 45 studies (2010–2022) of pre-generative adaptive systems, which is cited only as background, not as a finding of this review. Implications are framed as evidence-tiered hypotheses, while the layered model is offered as an interpretive scaffold for future research, not a confirmatory model.
