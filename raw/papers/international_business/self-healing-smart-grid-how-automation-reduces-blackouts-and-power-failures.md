# Self-healing smart grid: how automation reduces blackouts and power failures

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Modern societies depend heavily on a continuous and reliable supply of electricity, yet traditional power grids are increasingly vulnerable to outages caused by equipment failures, extreme weather, cyber threats, and rising demand. Blackouts and power failures not only disrupt daily life but also lead to significant economic losses and safety risks. In response to these challenges, self-healing smart grids have emerged as a transformative solution. By integrating advanced automation, sensors, real-time data analytics, and intelligent control systems, self-healing smart grids can detect, isolate, and respond to faults automatically—often within seconds. This capability greatly reduces the frequency, duration, and impact of power outages. As automation becomes more sophisticated, self-healing smart grids are redefining how electrical networks operate, shifting from reactive systems to proactive and resilient infrastructures that ensure greater reliability and efficiency in power delivery.
