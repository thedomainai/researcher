## Abstract

Geopolitical risks have become a major issue of concern to governments, multinational corporations, financial institutions and policy-makers in an increasingly interconnected and volatile world. An accelerated pace of change brought by technological innovation and the alteration in balance of power as a result of heightened globalization has extensively increased the complexity, as well as unpredictability of geopolitical occurrences. Conventional methods of risk assessment, mostly focused on historical track record, subjective judgment and qualitative assessments, often fail to address the pace, magnitude and interdependent nature of the current geopolitical upheavals. The chapter examines the disruptive nature of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in improving the mapping of risk of geopolitics and its use as a support to strategy.
