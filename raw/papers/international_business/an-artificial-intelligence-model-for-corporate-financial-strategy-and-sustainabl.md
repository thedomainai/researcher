# An Artificial Intelligence Model for Corporate Financial Strategy and Sustainable Global Business Growth

**著者**: Jonathan Jemine Medon
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 国際経営

## Abstract

In an era of increasing global economic complexity, corporations face unprecedented challenges in aligning financial strategy with sustainable business growth. This paper proposes an artificial intelligence (AI) model designed to enhance corporate financial strategy by integrating predictive analytics, real-time data processing, and advanced decision-support frameworks. The model leverages machine learning algorithms to forecast revenues, optimize capital allocation, and assess risk exposure across multiple operational and market scenarios. By providing accurate, dynamic, and actionable financial insights, the AI framework enables organizations to make informed strategic decisions, enhance operational efficiency, and anticipate market shifts proactively. The study emphasizes the role of AI in promoting sustainability, highlighting how predictive financial planning can align corporate objectives with environmental, social, and governance (ESG) considerations while supporting long-term value creation.
