# High-Growth Economy Context and Research Design

## Abstract

This chapter bridges the theoretical foundations of strategic renewal and business model innovation from Chap. 2 with an empirical examination grounded in the Polish post-transition context. It unpacks how Poland’s unique institutional evolution—from a centrally planned economy through a turbulent transition to full European Union integration—may shape firms’ strategic renewal processes and business model transformations. The chapter explores possible effects of institutional imprinting across firms founded in different eras, the tensions arising from institutional dualities, and the opportunities fostered by regulatory harmonization and EU support. A mixed-methods research design is introduced, combining qualitative multiple case studies and a large-scale quantitative survey to capture the complexity of renewal strategies within Poland’s dynamic high-growth economy. The empirical findings, presented in subsequent chapters, illuminate how Polish firms navigate inherited constraints to seize new institutional and technological opportunities.
