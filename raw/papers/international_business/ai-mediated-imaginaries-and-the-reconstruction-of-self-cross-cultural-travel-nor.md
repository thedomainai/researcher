# AI-Mediated Imaginaries and the Reconstruction of Self: Cross-Cultural Travel, Norm Negotiation, and Identity Transformation

**著者**: Yuanjia Guo
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 国際経営

## Abstract

This article develops a reflexive and recursive model of how algorithmically mediated cultural imaginaries interact with cross-cultural mobility to reshape understandings of home norms and the self. Bringing tourism imaginaries, possible selves, transformative travel, intercultural communication, and algorithm studies into dialogue, it argues that digital platforms and generative AI do more than influence destination perceptions: by organizing cultural visibility, they can make alternative ways of living - and therefore alternative selves - imaginable before mobility occurs. Lived cross-cultural encounters then confront these mediated imaginaries with heterogeneous social realities, creating expectation-reality gaps that may denaturalize previously taken-for-granted norms. Rather than assuming linear adaptation or cultural replacement, the model conceptualizes subsequent identity work as reflexive negotiation through reaffirmation, selective incorporation, rejection, hybridization, and contextual switching. A qualitative, biographical, process-oriented design is proposed to trace trajectories from pre-mobility media exposure through lived encounter, post-mobility reflection, and digital re-narration. The article's central contribution is a recursive account of mediation, mobility, and reflexivity in which individuals are simultaneously recipients, interpreters, and producers of cultural imaginaries. The resulting theoretical movement is summarized as Other -> Home -> Self, embedded within a broader algorithmic circulation of cultural visibility and possible selves.
