# DIGITAL TRADE AND AI INTEGRATION IN UZBEKISTAN: STRATEGIC OPPORTUNITIES, SYSTEMIC RISKS, AND STRUCTURAL CHALLENGES

## Abstract

In the contemporary digital economy, artificial intelligence (AI) and digital trade have become critical instruments for enhancing economic competitiveness and fostering global economic integration. For Uzbekistan, a developing economy with limited direct access to international markets, AI-driven digital trade represents a strategic opportunity to reduce logistical disadvantages and expand engagement in global value chains. This paper investigates the opportunities, challenges, and risks arising from the integration of AI technologies into Uzbekistan’s digital trade landscape. It also assesses the effectiveness of current government initiatives, particularly Presidential Decree No. PQ-358 and Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 885, while drawing on macroeconomic evidence to formulate recommendations for long-term and inclusive digital development.
