# Leveraging Artificial Intelligence in Citizenship by Investment Programmes: A Framework for Minimum Due Diligence Standards to Counter Financial Crime

**著者**: Jeiel Joseph, Lucas Millan-Narotzky, Umut Türkşen
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 国際経営

## Abstract

This chapter examines the integration of artificial intelligence (AI) into due diligence (DD) processes within the Investment Migration (IM) sector, focusing on Citizenship by Investment (CBI) programmes. CBI programmes grant citizenship to qualified individuals in return for investment in a country’s economy and are increasingly recognized as a significant source of foreign direct investment (FDI). However, concerns around misuse—such as money laundering and tax evasion—highlight the urgent need for robust DD frameworks. The chapter introduces a framework that leverages established AI technologies, including machine learning (ML) and Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT), to standardize DD procedures across specific CBI programmes. This framework draws heavily on historical CBI data, which often involves personal information held in private databases. As such, legal safeguards and policies must ensure that access to and processing of this data remain lawful and ethical. These processes must also be cross verified with independent, external data sources to meet DD requirements effectively. A critical component of this AI integration is the development of “legally explainable” AI, enabling transparency for stakeholders regarding how automated decisions and risk profiles are generated. Furthermore, mechanisms for auditing AI decision-making must be established to uphold transparency, accountability, and trust in CBI administration. By embedding AI in DD procedures, CBI programmes can significantly enhance operational integrity, efficiency, and resistance to abuse, ultimately strengthening their global legitimacy and their role in combatting financial crime.
