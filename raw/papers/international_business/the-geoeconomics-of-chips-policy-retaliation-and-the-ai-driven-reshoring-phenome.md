# The Geoeconomics of Chips: Policy, Retaliation, and the AI-Driven Reshoring Phenomenon

**著者**: Christina Tay, Duong Minh Tran, Thanh Thuy Nguyen
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 国際経営

## Abstract

Over the past two decades, global production strategies have undergone a structural paradigm shift, transitioning from a cost-centric focus toward supply chain resilience and national security. In the early 2000s, deep offshoring and hyper-lean, just-in-time supply chains maximized cost efficiency and economies of scale across global value chains (GVCs). However, compounding shocks—including the U.S.–China trade war, the COVID-19 pandemic, geopolitical conflicts in Eastern Europe, and the rapid rise of Artificial Intelligence (AI)—have exposed the systemic vulnerabilities of hyper-globalized production networks. These disruptions have compelled states and multinational enterprises to reassess their strategic priorities, particularly in the semiconductor sector, where the rise of AI has elevated advanced chips to a critical pillar of technological sovereignty. In this emerging era of resilience, firms and state actors are strategically reconfiguring sourcing networks, strengthening domestic production capabilities, and embracing more regionalized supply-chain frameworks. This study demonstrates that, rather than triggering full-scale deglobalization, these forces are transforming global value chains into policy-driven, security-oriented regional production blocs, while substantial cross-border interdependencies and international economic linkages continue to persist.
