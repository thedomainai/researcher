# AI in Real Estate: From Experimentation to Enterprise Transformation

**著者**: Sonal Tripathi
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 国際経営

## Abstract

Abstract Artificial intelligence is moving from a technology experiment toward a potential operating-model capability for real estate and construction. This research paper examines the transition from AI experimentation to enterprise-scale value creation, with particular focus on data foundations, workflow integration, enterprise architecture, AI governance, agentic AI and measurable business value. Drawing on recent research and industry evidence, the paper examines the gap between widespread AI experimentation and limited enterprise-scale deployment. It identifies key AI opportunities across customer experience, sales, design and planning, construction, procurement, finance, property operations and investment decisions. The paper proposes a five-stage AI maturity model for real estate: Awareness and Experimentation, Controlled Pilots, Integrated Workflows, Enterprise Intelligence and Intelligent Enterprise. It also presents an illustrative AI value chain connecting governed data, AI and analytics, workflows, human decision-making and measurable outcomes. Particular attention is given to data quality, systems integration, human oversight, responsible AI, privacy, cybersecurity and governance. The paper argues that sustainable AI value depends not simply on adopting AI tools, but on redesigning selected business processes around reliable data, intelligent automation and human judgement. The geographic perspective is Global + India, with specific consideration of the Indian real-estate and construction context.
