## Abstract

This repository provides a balanced panel dataset comprising 60 global economies observed annually from 2011 to 2025, yielding 900 total observations. It is structured to investigate the interrelationships among digital financial inclusion, green innovation, artificial intelligence readiness, and inclusive green growth for empirical publication in peer-reviewed journals like the International Review of Economics & Finance (IREF).File Structure (Digital_Finance_Inclusive_Green_Growth_Dataset_2011_2025.xlsx):The uploaded multi-tab Excel workbook contains four dedicated sheets:Panel_Dataset_2011_2025: The primary raw and structured panel matrix (900 rows, 11 columns) capturing the dependent variable ($\text{IGG}$), independent variable ($\text{DF}$), mediator ($\text{GI}$), moderator ($\text{AI}$), and macroeconomic controls ($\text{HC}$, $\text{TO}$, $\text{FDI}$, $\text{INF}$).Variable_Metadata: Full documentation dictionary detailing column codes, measurement units (indices, percentages, patents per capita), mathematical transformations (logarithmic scaling for patent data), and official international sources (World Bank WDI, IMF Financial Access Survey, WIPO, OECD, Oxford Insights, ITU, Penn World Table).Country_Sample_60: Complete inventory of all 60 cross-sectional sample countries categorized by geographical region and economic development status.Submission_Workflow_Steps: Procedural roadmap outlining the 5 consecutive research phases:Dataset auditing and cleaning.Mendeley Data repository deposit and DataCite DOI generation.Descriptive and diagnostic statistical testing.Advanced econometric estimation using Stata / R / Python (two-step System GMM dynamic panel models and moderated-mediation interaction regressions).Final IREF manuscript compilation and submission.
