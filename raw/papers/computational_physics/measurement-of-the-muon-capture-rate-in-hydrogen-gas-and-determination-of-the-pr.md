# Measurement of the Muon Capture Rate in Hydrogen Gas and Determination of the Proton’s Pseudoscalar Coupling gP

**著者**: V. Andreev, T. I. Banks, T. A. Case, D. B. Chitwood, Steven M. Clayton (+31)
**年**: 2007 | **被引用数**: 102
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 計算物理学

## Abstract

The rate of nuclear muon capture by the proton has been measured using a new technique based on a time projection chamber operating in ultraclean, deuterium-depleted hydrogen gas, which is key to avoiding uncertainties from muonic molecule formation. The capture rate from the hyperfine singlet ground state of the $\ensuremath{\mu}p$ atom was obtained from the difference between the ${\ensuremath{\mu}}^{\ensuremath{-}}$ disappearance rate in hydrogen and the world average for the ${\ensuremath{\mu}}^{+}$ decay rate, yielding ${\ensuremath{\Lambda}}_{S}=725.0\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}17.4\text{ }\text{ }{\mathrm{s}}^{\ensuremath{-}1}$, from which the induced pseudoscalar coupling of the nucleon, ${g}_{P}({q}^{2}=\ensuremath{-}0.88{m}_{\ensuremath{\mu}}^{2})=7.3\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}1.1$, is extracted.
