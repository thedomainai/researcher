## Abstract

What happens to nucleon-nucleon interactions at very short distances, below the radius of the proton? Does the structure of a nucleon bound in a nucleus differ from that of a free nucleon? The answer to both questions can be provided in terms of the internal quark structure of a nucleon. This article reviews current experimental and theoretical developments providing the high-resolution picture of correlated nucleonic pairs at a short distance scale.
