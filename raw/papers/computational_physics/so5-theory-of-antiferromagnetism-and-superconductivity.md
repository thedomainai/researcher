# SO(5) theory of antiferromagnetism and superconductivity

**著者**: Eugene Demler, Werner R. Hanke, Shoucheng Zhang
**年**: 2004 | **被引用数**: 367
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 計算物理学

## Abstract

Antiferromagnetism and superconductivity are both fundamental and common states of matter. In many strongly correlated systems, including the high-${T}_{c}$ cuprates, the heavy-fermion compounds, and the organic superconductors, they occur next to each other in the phase diagram and influence each other's physical properties. The $\mathit{SO}(5)$ theory unifies these two basic states of matter by a symmetry principle and describes their rich phenomenology through a single low-energy effective model. In this paper, the authors review the framework of the $\mathit{SO}(5)$ theory and compare it with numerical and experimental results.
