# Stochastic series expansion method with operator-loop update

**著者**: Anders W. Sandvik
**年**: 1999 | **被引用数**: 906
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 計算物理学

## Abstract

A cluster update (the ``operator loop'') is developed within the framework of a numerically exact quantum Monte Carlo method based on the power series expansion of $\mathrm{exp}(\ensuremath{-}\ensuremath{\beta}H)$ (stochastic series expansion). The method is generally applicable to a wide class of lattice Hamiltonians for which the expansion is positive definite. For some important models the operator-loop algorithm is more efficient than loop updates previously developed for ``worldline'' simulations. The method is here tested on a two-dimensional anisotropic Heisenberg antiferromagnet in a magnetic field.
