# Infrared Probe of Transition from Superconductor to Nonmetal in YBa2(Cu1−xZnx)4O8

**著者**: D. N. Basov, Bogdan Dabrowski, T. Timusk
**年**: 1998 | **被引用数**: 118
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 物性物理

## Abstract

We report on a study of the optical conductivity of single crystals of the high- ${T}_{c}$ superconductor ${\mathrm{YBa}}_{2}{\mathrm{Cu}}_{4}{\mathrm{O}}_{8}$ in which superconductivity is suppressed by random substitution of Cu with Zn. The $a$-axis infrared spectrum, which is a direct measure of the charge dynamics in the ${\mathrm{CuO}}_{2}$ planes, is dominated by a bound state at $\ensuremath{\approx}120{\mathrm{cm}}^{\ensuremath{-}1}$. This indicates that the low-energy electronic states are on the verge of localization driven by the Zn impurities.
