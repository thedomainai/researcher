# Band structure of Bi2 Sr2 CaCu2 O8 studied by angle-resolved photoemission

**著者**: T. Takahashi, H. Matsuyama, Hiroshi Katayama‐Yoshida, Yutaka Okabe, S. Hosoya (+4)
**年**: 1989 | **被引用数**: 218
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 物性物理

## Abstract

Angle-resolved photoemission measurement with synchrotron radiation has been performed on single-crystal ${\mathrm{Bi}}_{2}$${\mathrm{Sr}}_{2}$${\mathrm{CaCu}}_{2}$${\mathrm{O}}_{8}$. Two dispersive bands intersecting the Fermi level midway between the \ensuremath{\Gamma} point and the Brillouin-zone boundary were observed. The other bands with higher binding energy are almost dispersionless in contrast with the band-structure calculation. The present experimental result indicates the existence of a Fermi surface and Fermi-liquid states in the high-${T}_{c}$ superconductor as well as band renormalization due to the strong electron correlation.
