# Upward curvature of H c 2 in high- T c superconductors: Possible evidence for s - d pairing

**著者**: Robert Joynt
**年**: 1990 | **被引用数**: 113
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 物性物理

## Abstract

A mechanism is proposed to explain the upward curvature of the plot of thermodynamic ${\mathit{H}}_{\mathit{c}2}$ versus temperature in the high-${\mathit{T}}_{\mathit{c}}$ superconductors, as observed in dc magnetization measurements. If a multicomponent order parameter is assumed, the internal degrees of freedom of this parameter change along the ${\mathit{H}}_{\mathit{c}2}$(T) curve. In a superconductor with s-d mixing, a pure d wave at low fields will turn into a superposition of s and d at high fields, with a corresponding change in slope of ${\mathit{H}}_{\mathit{c}2}$. In a pure d-wave superconductor belonging to a two-dimensional representation, an orthorhombic distortion can produce the curvature. At low fields the slope of the curve is dominated by coupling to the orthorhombic distortion; at high fields the coupling of the supercurrent to the field is more important. Thus the curvature in ${\mathit{H}}_{\mathit{c}2}$(T) can be interpreted as evidence for d-wave pairing or for a mixture of s and d. The consequences of these two possibilities for other experiments are discussed. In particular, the mixed s and d picture can be reconciled with penetration-depth measurements, and therefore seems favored.
