# Topological phases and the quantum spin Hall effect in three dimensions

**著者**: Rahul Roy
**年**: 2009 | **被引用数**: 1071
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 物性物理

## Abstract

We show the existence of topological phases of Bloch insulators with time-reversal symmetry in three dimensions. These phases are characterized by topological ${Z}_{2}$ invariants whose stability is studied using band-touching arguments. Unlike insulators which break time-reveral symmetry, some of these topological phases are intrinsically three dimensional. The number of invariants (four) needed to specify the phase of these insulators also differs from the time-reversal-breaking case. The relation between these phases and the quantum spin Hall effect in three dimensions is investigated.
