# First-Principles Study of the Structural, Mechanical, Electronic, Magnetic, and Optical Properties of Pd2MnZ (Z = Al, Ga, and In) Heusler Alloys

**著者**: Murasa Baraki, Zulfiqar Ali Shah, Uzma Nosheen, Syed Zafar Ilyas, Hareem Mufti
**年**: 2027 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 物性物理

## Abstract

Heusler alloys have gained considerable attention due to their tunable electronic, magnetic, mechanical, and optical properties, making them promising candidates for spintronic, magnetic-storage, and optoelectronic applications. Pd-based Heusler alloys, in particular, offer interesting combinations of metallic, magnetic, and mechanical characteristics. Therefore, investigating the fundamental properties of Pd2MnZ (Z = Al, Ga, and In) alloys is important for evaluating their potential multifunctional applications First principles calculation has been used to calculate the structural, electronic, mechanical, magnetic, and optical properties of Palladium-based Pd2MnZ (Z = Al, Ga, and In) Heusler alloys by the WIEN2k method with an HSE06 hybrid functional. In electronic properties, the calculated band gaps for all alloys show overlapping of valence and conduction bands, which confirmed its metallic nature. The bulk modulus (B) was calculated to be 104.03, 105.00, and 112.51 GPa, while the shear modulus (G) was calculated to be 47.04, 48.00, and 40.00 GPa. The elastic anisotropy factor (A) was calculated to be 8.95, 9.00, and 5.00, respectively, to understand the mechanical behavior of the Pd2MnZ compounds. The Young’s modulus (E) was found to be 122.63, 125.00, and 110.00 GPa, while the B/G ratios were 2.21, 2.18, and 2.81, respectively. Poisson’s ratios were 0.30, 0.31, and 0.25, respectively. The Cauchy pressure values were positive, indicating that all the compounds exhibit ductile behavior. The calculated magnetic moments were 4.12, 4.29, and 4.19 μB for Pd2MnAl, Pd2MnGa, and Pd2MnIn, respectively, demonstrating that these compounds are magnetic and metallic in nature. Therefore, they may have potential applications as ferromagnetic materials in data-storage devices. The refractive index reaches a maximum at low photon energies (0.9–1.25 eV) and then decreases. The maximum absorption peaks occur at approximately 21.0, 17.5, and 7.5 eV, while the reflectivity peaks occur at approximately 22.5, 25.0, and 8.0 eV. The principal peaks in the loss function occur at approximately 4.15, 4.35, and 3.95 eV.
