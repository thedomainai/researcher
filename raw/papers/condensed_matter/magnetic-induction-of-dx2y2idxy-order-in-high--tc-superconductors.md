# Magnetic Induction of dx2−y2+idxy Order in High- Tc Superconductors

**著者**: R. B. Laughlin
**年**: 1998 | **被引用数**: 315
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 物性物理

## Abstract

I propose that the phase transition in ${\mathrm{Bi}}_{2}{\mathrm{Sr}}_{2}{\mathrm{CaCu}}_{2}{\mathrm{O}}_{8}$ recently observed by Krishana et al. [Science 277, 83 (1997)] is the development of a small ${d}_{\mathrm{xy}}$ superconducting order parameter phased by $\ensuremath{\pi}/2$ with respect to the principal ${d}_{{x}^{2}{\ensuremath{-}y}^{2}}$ one to produce a minimum energy gap $\ensuremath{\Delta}$. The violation of both parity and time-reversal symmetry allows the development of a magnetic moment, the key to explaining the experiment. The origin of this moment is a quantized boundary current of ${I}_{B}\phantom{\rule{0ex}{0ex}}=\phantom{\rule{0ex}{0ex}}2e\ensuremath{\Delta}/h$ at zero temperature.
