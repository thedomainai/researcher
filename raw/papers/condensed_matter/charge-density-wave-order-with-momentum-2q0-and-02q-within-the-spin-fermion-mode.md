# Charge-density-wave order with momentum (2Q,0) and (0,2Q) within the spin-fermion model: Continuous and discrete symmetry breaking, preemptive composite order, and relation to pseudogap in hole-doped cuprates

**著者**: Yuxuan Wang, Andrey V. Chubukov
**年**: 2014 | **被引用数**: 240
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 物性物理

## Abstract

The authors show that cuprate pseudogap physics can be well understood within the same spin-fermion model which yields $d$-wave superconductivity and non-Fermi-liquid physics in the normal state. Thus, the spin-fermion model re-emerges as a strong candidate for the theoretical model of the cuprates.
