# In-Plane Anisotropy of the Penetration Depth in YBa2Cu3O7−x and Y Ba2 Cu4 O8 Superconductors

**著者**: D. N. Basov, Ruixing Liang, D. A. Bonn, W. N. Hardy, B. Dąbrowski (+5)
**年**: 1995 | **被引用数**: 431
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 物性物理

## Abstract

The superfluid response of single-crystal $\mathrm{Y}{\mathrm{Ba}}_{2}{\mathrm{Cu}}_{3}{\mathrm{O}}_{7\ensuremath{-}x}$ and Y${\mathrm{Ba}}_{2}$${\mathrm{Cu}}_{4}$${\mathrm{O}}_{8}$ shows a strong anisotropy in the $a\ensuremath{-}b$ plane as revealed by far infrared spectroscopy. The value of the London penetration depth in both systems is considerably smaller in the direction along the Cu-O chains, suggesting that a substantial portion of the superconducting condensate is on the chains.
