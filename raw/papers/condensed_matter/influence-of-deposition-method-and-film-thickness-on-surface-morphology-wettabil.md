# Influence of Deposition Method and Film Thickness on Surface, Morphology, Wettability and Photoresponse Behavior of Copper Oxide Thin Films

**著者**: Vadim Morari, D. BRAGARI, Victor Suman, Daniel Ilie Timpu, E. I. MONAICO
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 物性物理

## Abstract

This study presents a comparative investigation of copper oxide (CuO and Cu2O) thin films deposited by spray pyrolysis and RF magnetron sputtering, examining the relationship between surface morphology, wettability, and electrical properties. AFM analysis reveals distinct growth characteristics: spray pyrolyzed films show granular and columnar structures with height variations of 71–116 nm, while RF-sputtered films exhibit dense, uniform morphologies with 9–17 nm variations. Increasing film thickness reduces RMS (Sq) and mean (Sa) roughness for both methods, reflecting grain coalescence and densification. Water contact angles correlate with topography: spray pyrolyzed films show increasing hydrophobicity (108°–118°) with thickness, consistent with the Cassie–Baxter model, whereas RF-sputtered films display slightly decreasing angles (106°–103°) according to the Wenzel model. X-ray diffraction confirms cubic Cu2O in RF films and monoclinic CuO in spray pyrolyzed films. Electrical measurements under dark and 20 mW/cm2 illumination indicate ohmic behavior. Spray pyrolyzed films exhibit higher resistance and larger photo-to-dark current ratios (Ilight/Idark), highlighting stronger photoresponse, while RF-sputtered films have higher conductivity but weaker photoresponse. Correlation between RMS roughness and Ilight/Idark underscores the role of surface morphology in light absorption and charge dynamics.
