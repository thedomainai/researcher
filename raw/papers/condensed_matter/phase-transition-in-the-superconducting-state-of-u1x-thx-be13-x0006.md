# Phase transition in the superconducting state of U1−x Thx Be13 (x=0–0.06)

**著者**: H. R. Ott, Helmut Rudigier, Z. Fisk, J. L. Smith
**年**: 1985 | **被引用数**: 288
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 物性物理

## Abstract

We report results of specific-heat measurements and the observation of a new phase transition in the superconducting state of ${\mathrm{U}}_{1\mathrm{\ensuremath{-}}\mathrm{x}}$${\mathrm{Th}}_{\mathrm{x}}$${\mathrm{Be}}_{13}$ compounds for a narrow range of x values. Although the nature of this transition cannot be unambiguously established, its occurrence is important with regard to the characterization of the superconducting state in these compounds.
