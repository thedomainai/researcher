# Sr2RuO4: an electronic analogue of3He?

**著者**: Thomas Maurice Rice, Manfred Sigrist
**年**: 1995 | **被引用数**: 883
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 物性物理

## Abstract

Sr 2 RuO 4 is a superconductor with a similar structure to a high-T c cuprate superconductor. Nevertheless, the superconducting state may have different symmetry than that of cuprate superconductors. Strong Hund's rule coupling favours triplet over singlet pairing, similar to 3 He. A strong candidate is the odd-parity pairing state which is the two-dimensional analogue of the Balian-Werthamer state of 3 He. Various experimental consequences and tests are analysed.
