# Critical Current in Superconductors with Strong Pinning of Abrikosov’s Vortice

**著者**: O.L. Kasatkin, V.S. Flis, O.A. Kalenyuk, V.Ye. Moshchil, V.P. Tsvitkovskyi
**年**: 2027 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 物性物理

## Abstract

One of the most important challenges in applied superconductivity is increasing the current-carrying capacity and, accordingly, the critical current of superconducting materials used in modern electrical engineering and electronics. To solve this problem, it is necessary to create conditions for the effective pinning of Abrikosov’s vortices and impede their movement under the influence of the Lorentz force generated by the transport current. Numerous research groups try to solve it in an optimal way by choosing the relevant artificial defect nanostructure implanted inside the superconductor material in order to prevent magnetic-fluxons’ motion and the corresponding resistive state of the superconductor caused by this motion. In the present work, we review mechanisms of effective vortexes’ pinning in moderately anisotropic 3 D superconductors produced by nanosize structural defects of different dimensionality, as well as the so-called ‘magnetic’ mechanism of pinning in the hybrid superconductor/ferromagnet ( S/F ) structures, and estimate their effect on the critical-current value and its dependences on the external parameters, such as temperature and applied DC magnetic-field value and orientation with respect to crystalline axes. We also present some experimental results on critical-current measurements in high- T c superconducting materials: pulse-laser deposited thin Y–Ba–Cu–O films, as well as bulk MgB 2 samples and films of them with different defect nanostructures.
