# Surface effects in layered semiconductors Bi2Se3 and Bi2Te3

**著者**: Sergei Urazhdin, Daniel Bilc, S. D. Mahanti, Stuart Tessmer, Theodora Kyratsi (+1)
**年**: 2004 | **被引用数**: 125
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 物性物理

## Abstract

Scanning tunneling spectroscopy of ${\mathrm{Bi}}_{2}{\mathrm{Se}}_{3}$ and ${\mathrm{Bi}}_{2}{\mathrm{Te}}_{3}$ layered narrow gap semiconductors reveals finite in-gap density of states and suppressed conduction in the energy range of high valence-band states. Electronic structure calculations suggest that the surface effects are responsible for these properties. Conversely, the interlayer coupling has a strong effect on the bulk near-gap electronic structure. These properties may prove to be important for the thermoelectric performance of these and other related chalcogenides.
