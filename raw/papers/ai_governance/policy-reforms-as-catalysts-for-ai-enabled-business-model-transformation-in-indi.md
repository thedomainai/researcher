# Policy Reforms as Catalysts for AI-Enabled Business Model Transformation in India

**著者**: Pranoti Vijay Agarwal, Sagar Nimbalkar
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

This paper examines the paradigm shift in India’s regulatory landscape from passive oversight to active ecosystem facilitation and its subsequent impact on domestic business model innovation. Historically, Indian enterprises functioned primarily within labor intensive service frameworks; however, the introduction of the India AI Mission (2024), the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, and the National Strategy for Artificial Intelligence has triggered a fundamental transition toward "AI-First" value propositions. By analyzing the democratization of sovereign compute infrastructure and the integration of AI into Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI), this study argues that policy reforms have lowered the "cost of innovation" for startups and legacy firms alike. The research utilizes a dual-methodology approach: a longitudinal analysis of policy evolution since 2020 and a series of sectoral case studies in Fintech, Agri tech, and Health tech. Findings suggest that government backed initiative such as subsidized GPU access and unified data exchange protocol are enabling businesses to pivot from linear scalability to exponential, platform-based models. The paper concludes that India’s "Middle Path" of regulation—balancing aggressive innovation with ethical guardrail serves as a global blueprint for emerging economies seeking to leverage artificial intelligence for inclusive economic growth.
