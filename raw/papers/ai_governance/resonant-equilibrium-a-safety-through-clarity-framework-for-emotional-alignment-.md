# Resonant equilibrium: A safety through clarity framework for emotional alignment and cognitive economy in human-AI interaction

**著者**: David Chamberlin Madrid, Philosopher Madrid
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

The current technological landscape represents a wild and uncharted frontier in which artificial intelligence has transitioned from a mere tool for calculation and automation into a quasisocial relational partner. This evolution is not merely incremental but a fundamental shift in the architecture of human-machine engagement, wherein AI not only delivers content but cocreates lived experiences with human interlocutors. As these systems integrate into the most intimate aspects of human life—including healthcare, mental health, education, and domestic companionship—the need for a logically followable framework of resonance and emotional input becomes a critical imperative. This report outlines the "Safety through Clarity" framework, designed to bridge the participatory gap between biological and silicon-based cognition without imposing an unsustainable metabolic or cognitive tax on either side.
