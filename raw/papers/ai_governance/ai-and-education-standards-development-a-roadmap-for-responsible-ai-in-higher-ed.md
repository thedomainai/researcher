# AI and Education Standards Development: A Roadmap for Responsible AI in Higher Education

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) has entered higher education with unprecedented speed, offering transformative possibilities while simultaneously challenging the foundational principles of academic integrity, critical thinking, and institutional governance. This white paper argues that the current "governance gap", where institutions and staff are forced to navigate these technologies without clear standards, which is unsustainable. To safeguard the future of the sector, we must move from reactive, fragmented policies toward a coordinated, standards-based framework that prioritises human expertise and ethical safety. In this regard, this document is guided by the investigation of the current literature and workshops conducted at the University of Southampton. The findings aim to inform institutions, policymakers and technology companies the extent to which AI, especially GenAI, affects the educational environments. Consequently, it can be appropriate for more policy responses and drive technology towards the responsible GenAI development and use in education.
