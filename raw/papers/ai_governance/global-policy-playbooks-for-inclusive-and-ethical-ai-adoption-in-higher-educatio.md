# Global Policy Playbooks for Inclusive and Ethical AI Adoption in Higher Education

**著者**: Rohmatul Fitriyah Dewi, Saifurahman Rohi, Elva Retnawati
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

This chapter explores how artificial intelligence is reshaping higher education and why existing governance approaches are struggling to keep pace with this transformation. This chapter examines the opportunities associated with AI adoption in teaching, learning, research, and administration, while critically addressing the ethical, social, and institutional challenges that accompany these developments. Key issues discussed include equity and access, academic integrity, algorithmic bias, transparency, data governance, and the fragmentation of AI policies across institutions. Drawing on global policy debates and comparative examples from the European Union, the United States, China, and Singapore, the chapter highlights how national contexts shape differing approaches to AI regulation and institutional practice.
