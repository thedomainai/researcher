# Generative AI in Multimodal Biological Data

**著者**: Tomojit Ghosh, Caleb Hendren
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

Generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) has reshaped analysis of biological data analysis by enabling the synthesis, integration, and enhancement of complex multimodal datasets. This chapter provides a comprehensive overview of how generative models—such as generative adversarial networks (GANs), variational autoencoders (VAEs), and diffusion models—are applied to high-dimensional biological data, with a focus on addressing challenges related to dimensionality, data sparsity, and cross-modality alignment. We highlight recent advances in synthetic data generation, data imputation, and signal enhancement across omics domains, including single-cell and bulk RNA-seq data. Through representative case studies, we illustrate how these models have been used to predict cell fate, infer disease progression, and improve drug response modeling. The chapter concludes with a discussion of the current limitations, ethical considerations, and future research directions shaping the responsible deployment of generative AI in biology. Overall, this chapter aims to equip readers with a clear understanding of the principles, applications, and emerging frontiers of generative AI in data-driven biological discovery.
