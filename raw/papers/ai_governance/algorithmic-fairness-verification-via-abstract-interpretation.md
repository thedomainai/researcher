# Algorithmic Fairness Verification via Abstract Interpretation

**著者**: Jincheng Zhang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

Algorithmic fairness is a critical concern as machine learning systems increasingly influence our lives. However, verifying fairness – ensuring that algorithms don't systematically disadvantage specific groups – remains a significant challenge. This paper proposes a novel approach to algorithmic fairness verification using abstract interpretation. We argue that abstract interpretation, a powerful technique for reasoning about program behavior, can be applied to systematically identify and mitigate potential sources of bias within machine learning algorithms. Our core claim is to leverage abstract domains to represent the essential properties of data and algorithms, enabling us to analyze algorithm behavior and pinpoint bias without the need for exhaustive testing. This approach provides a systematic, formal framework for ensuring algorithmic fairness, moving beyond intuitive notions and offering a concrete methodology for verification. The paper details the framework, focusing on defining appropriate abstract domains, employing abstract interpretation techniques, and demonstrating their application to a simplified algorithmic scenario.
