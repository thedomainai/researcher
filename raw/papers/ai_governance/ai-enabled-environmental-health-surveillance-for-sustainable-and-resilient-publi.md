# AI-Enabled Environmental Health Surveillance for Sustainable and Resilient Public Health Systems: A Systems Thinking and Governance Framework

**著者**: Jyothi Kurapati
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Environmental pollution is a growing public health challenge linked to respiratory, cardiovascular, neurological, and climate-sensitive diseases worldwide. Traditional monitoring systems often rely on fragmented data and delayed reporting, limiting timely responses to environmental risks. This chapter explores how artificial intelligence (AI) can strengthen environmental health surveillance and governance through a systems-thinking perspective. It examines key environmental health challenges, limitations of conventional monitoring approaches, and the potential of AI-enabled surveillance technologies. The chapter proposes the AI-Enabled Environmental Health Action Framework (AIEHAF), integrating environmental monitoring, AI analytics, public health action, and governance processes. Drawing on lessons from low- and middle-income countries, the chapter highlights environmental justice, digital inclusion, and ethical governance considerations. The framework offers a practical approach for developing resilient, equitable, and evidence-informed environmental health systems.
