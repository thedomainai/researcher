# AI governance through the eyes of users: a large-scale study of online reviews

**著者**: Stefan Pasch
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

Purpose With the growing importance of artificial intelligence (AI) governance, numerous high-level frameworks have been articulated by policymakers and expert communities to guide the development and application of AI. While such frameworks offer valuable normative orientation, they may not fully capture the practical concerns of those who interact with AI systems in organizational and operational contexts. This study aims to address that gap by exploring bottom-up, user-centered perspectives on AI governance. Design/methodology/approach This study adopts a bottom-up approach to explore how governance-relevant themes are expressed in user discourse. Drawing on over 100,000 user reviews of AI products from G2.com, we apply Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers for Topic Modeling (BERTopic) to extract latent themes and identify those most semantically related to AI governance. Findings The analysis reveals a wide range of governance-relevant topics spanning technical and non-technical domains. These include concerns across organizational processes, such as planning, coordination, and communication, as well as stages of the AI value chain, including deployment infrastructure, data handling, and analytics. The findings show overlap with institutional AI governance frameworks on issues like privacy and transparency, while surfacing overlooked areas such as project management, strategy development, and customer interaction. Originality/value This paper advances AI governance research by introducing a scalable method for identifying governance-relevant topics using semantic similarity and unsupervised topic modeling. It offers a novel, bottom-up mapping of governance concerns as expressed in user discourse, highlights both alignments and blind spots in existing frameworks, and demonstrates how bottom-up user perspectives complement existing AI governance frameworks by revealing governance dimensions that are salient in practice.
