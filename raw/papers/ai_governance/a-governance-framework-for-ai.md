# A Governance Framework for AI

**著者**: Ryan McCarthy
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

AI companies write, enforce, and revise their own safety standards, and nothing independent checks whether those standards are followed. This framework proposes four tiers so that no one party checks itself: (1) the AI companies; (2) an independent Watcher on every frontier system, made of a narrowly trained security AI that catches problems in real time and security built into the chip that can freeze them; (3) national governments that certify the Watchers and may not punish a company for holding an ethical line; and (4) an international body of randomly selected citizens and technical experts that checks the governments. One rule holds it together: no company may own or be tied to the provider that checks it. The checkers are checked too: providers rotate, are audited quarterly, and are tested with planted faults. It is a proposal built with transparency and humanity in mind, and it says plainly where it does not have the answers.This record contains the framework and a companion Process document showing how it was built. It was developed in collaboration with Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic; the disclosure and a tagged record of who originated each idea are in the documents.
