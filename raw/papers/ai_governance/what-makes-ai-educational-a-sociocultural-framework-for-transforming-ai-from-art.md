# What makes AI educational? A sociocultural framework for transforming AI from artefact to mediating tool

**著者**: Sara Mashayekh, Ali Darejeh
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

Generative artificial intelligence (AI) is increasingly embedded in higher education, yet much of its use positions AI as a private productivity tool for generating answers, feedback or text. This paper argues that AI is not educational simply because it produces outputs. From a sociocultural perspective, its educational significance emerges when pedagogical design transforms it from an artefact into a mediating tool for participation in disciplinary learning. The paper introduces the AI Mediated Participation Framework, grounded in mediated learning, appropriation and participation. It proposes three design principles: preserving learner ownership, scaffolding thinking through dialogue, and developing AI literacy through disciplinary practice. These principles operate through questioning, dialogue, reflection, critical evaluation and decision making to support learner agency, evaluative judgement, responsible AI use, critical thinking and knowledge construction. The framework is illustrated through AI learning activities in undergraduate and postgraduate educational research courses, where educator modelling, peer discussion and disciplinary expectations repositioned AI from a private feedback service to a mediating tool for methodological reasoning and researcher judgement. The framework shifts attention from what AI can do to how pedagogical design determines what AI becomes educationally.
