# ML Foundations: A Sequential Analogy Approach to Teaching Machine Learning from Gradient Descent to Constitutional AI

**著者**: Roopesh Aithal
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

We present a five-chapter ML curriculum structured around sequential analogy chains — a pedagogical approach in which each technical component maps to the next step of one continuous narrative analogy, making formula sequences memorable through story rather than isolated mnemonics. The curriculum spans gradient descent through Constitutional AI, covering: (1) gradient descent and linear regression, (2) logistic regression and classification metrics, (3) neural networks and backpropagation, (4) convolutional neural networks and transfer learning, and (5) transformer architecture, RLHF, and AI safety alignment. Each chapter introduces a master analogy chain — for example, the transformer is a collaborative research library in which tokenisation assigns catalogue numbers, embeddings are 768-page profile folders, queries are job descriptions, keys are CV covers, values are knowledge files, and the causal mask covers future folders with black paper. Components build physically on each other so each formula feels structurally inevitable. A care robot fall detection system threads through all five chapters, grounding abstract concepts in a concrete deployment goal. Pre and post chapter assessments with full explanations measure actual learning gain. The curriculum was developed iteratively through extended dialogue between a human ML engineering candidate and an AI assistant, with the human directing conceptual depth and the AI generating structured explanations.
