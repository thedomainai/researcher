## Abstract

This repository contains the full research package for SRFM Quantum Phase12. Phase12 extends the SRFM Quantum program from fixed coherent transport structures toward adaptive transport topology formation. Previous phases established that instability is better interpreted as sustained coherent transport rather than peak activation magnitude alone. Phase12 introduces adaptive coupling dynamics: dC_ij/dt = alpha * M_i * M_j - gamma * C_ij where transport pathways evolve according to mode synchronization and adaptive reinforcement. The central question investigated in Phase12 is: Can coherent transport reorganize the topology that carries it? The project explores: adaptive coherent transport self-reinforcing instability dynamics latent transport activation synchronization island formation transport fracture precursors evolving transport topology adaptive transport phase selection Key Phase12 findings include: emergence of self-reinforced coherent transport latent transport island activation adaptive amplification vs suppression regimes synchronization-supported transport survival coupling concentration fronts adaptive topology regime separation Most importantly, weak fixed transport structures can transition into sustained coherent transport states under adaptive reinforcement. This suggests that instability propagation may dynamically reorganize the topology that carries it.
