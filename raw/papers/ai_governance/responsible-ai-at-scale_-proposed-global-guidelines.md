# Responsible AI at Scale_ Proposed Global Guidelines

**著者**: Athanasios-Apollon Nikolaou
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

Global issues need global solutions, and AI is one of them. These proposed guidelines give organisations that train, develop or use AI at scale one practical, voluntary baseline for every market. They draw on what the EU AI Act, the GDPR and the leading international frameworks have in common: the OECD AI Principles, the UNESCO Recommendation, the Council of Europe AI Convention, the G7 Hiroshima Code of Conduct, the NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001. The approach is risk-based and works through existing privacy, security and risk processes, without extra bureaucracy. It covers red lines, governance, training, deployment, AI agents, transparency, people’s rights, security, incidents and cross-border cooperation. Its practical tools include: control questions and suspension triggers that keep models and agents within their set scope a 90-day starting plan an integrated impact assessment a protocol for sharing incidents and lessons worked examples This record contains the full Guidelines and a two-page Essentials version.
