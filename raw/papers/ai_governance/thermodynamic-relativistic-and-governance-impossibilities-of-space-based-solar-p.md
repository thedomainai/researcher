## Abstract

Recent sovereign budget allocations and corporate capital expenditures have increasingly targeted Space-Based Solar Power (SSPS) and Lunar AI Data Centers as purported solutions to terrestrial grid constraints and AI compute scaling limits. This monograph provides a rigorous mathematical, thermodynamic, relativistic, and corporate governance refutation of both architectures, proving their structural infeasibility ("Physical-Layer Impossibility").
