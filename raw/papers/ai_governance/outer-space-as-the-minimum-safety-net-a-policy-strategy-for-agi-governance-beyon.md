## Abstract

Current AGI governance is trapped in a zero-sum dynamic of great-power competition. This paper proposes a concrete policy strategy: establish outer space exploration as the minimum safety net for AGI governance. Space exploration is a non-zero-sum domain where cooperation is unambiguously beneficial. By building collaborative space programs--joint deep-space AI development, shared astronomical data infrastructure, coordinated planetary defense--nations can accumulate the trust needed to eventually address AGI governance. v2: Expanded with rebuttal section addressing 5 anticipated objections (space militarization, AGI timeline mismatch, free-rider problem, technological asymmetry, institutional design).
