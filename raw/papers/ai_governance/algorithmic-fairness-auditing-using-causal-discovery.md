# Algorithmic Fairness Auditing using Causal Discovery

**著者**: Jincheng Zhang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

Algorithmic fairness is a critical concern as algorithms increasingly influence decisions across various domains. Traditional methods for assessing fairness often rely on statistical correlations between protected attributes (e.g., race, gender) and outcomes, which can be easily confounded by other factors. This paper proposes a novel approach to algorithmic fairness auditing that leverages causal discovery algorithms. We argue that identifying the underlying causal relationships between variables is crucial for accurately assessing and mitigating bias. By employing causal discovery, we can pinpoint confounding variables – those that relate to both the protected attribute and the outcome, leading to spurious correlations – and thus provide a more robust and interpretable evaluation of algorithmic fairness. This approach enables the design of targeted interventions to address bias at its root cause, rather than simply masking statistical disparities. The core of this methodology lies in transforming the fairness auditing process from a correlation-based analysis to a causal inference one, leading to a significantly improved understanding of algorithmic bias and its potential solutions. ---
