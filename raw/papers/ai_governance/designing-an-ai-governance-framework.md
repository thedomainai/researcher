# Designing an AI Governance Framework

**著者**: Barry A. Salas
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

Twelve months after the initial AI inventory had surfaced fourteen systems across the institution, the ISPM sat down to take stock. The program had come a long way. There was a current inventory. Risk assessments existed for every Level 3 and Level 4 system. The vendor questionnaire had been updated. An acceptable use policy was published. A monthly monitoring report went to the Risk Committee. The gaps that had been invisible a year ago were now documented, tracked, and being closed.
