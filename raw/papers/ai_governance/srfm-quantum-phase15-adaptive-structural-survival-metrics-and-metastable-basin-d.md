## Abstract

SRFM Quantum Phase15 develops phenomenological diagnostic metrics for adaptive structural survival in metastable basin space. Building on: - Phase13: topology-only memory- Phase14: adaptive basin geometry this phase converts metastable basin behavior into measurable structural-survival diagnostics. The central result is: Persistent structural memory degradation is dominated by metastable redistribution and gradual erosion rather than abrupt collapse. Phase15 introduces: - Structural Survival Score (SSS)- Erosion Risk Index (ERI)- Basin Competition Index (BCI)- Redistribution Profiles (RP)- metastable diagnostic regime classification Main findings include: - metastable redistribution dominates the perturbation grid- partial retention is the dominant intermediate memory state- survival and erosion form a strong negative relation- no sharp collapse-dominant regime was observed This phase intentionally focuses on diagnostic-theory construction before IBM Quantum hardware validation planned for Phase16. The repository includes: - reproducible analysis scripts- perturbation-grid diagnostics- structural survival metrics- redistribution analysis- diagnostic regime classification- publication figures- Markdown/PDF summaries Safe claims: This work does not claim: - quantum error correction- topological quantum protection- quantum advantage- universal physical laws The introduced metrics are phenomenological diagnostic composites derived from Phase14 perturbation-grid outputs.
