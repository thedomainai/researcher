**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

A semi-formal governance framework for autonomous AI systems, with explicit mathematical arguments, conformance contracts, partially executable verification, and a self-applied adversarial constitutional process. CAGF separates derived structure (a typed axiomatic core, A0-A10 plus extensions) from declared normative choices (the P0 premise and documented PolicyParameters); its multi-agent governance record (dialectical review cycles, separated roles, signed verdicts, cryptographic event log) is documented in the repository itself.
