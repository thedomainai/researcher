# Global Scramble for AI Rules

**著者**: Derek W. Gibson
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

The Brussels Effect was real. The European Union AI Act was approved with a vote of 523 to 46. The act itself borrowed concepts from the landmark General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) passed in 2018. Both the GDPR and the later AI Act were negotiated over years in EU parliamentary committees based in Brussels, Belgium. Beginning in 2023, global leaders began gathering annually to talk about AI safety. The name of the conference evolved from the initial AI Safety Summit to the AI Impact Summit, a subtle change that was telling. China's policy moves also reshaped the world's AI stage. Data professionals may find it interesting that a key difference between the AI policies of China and the United States is that China is the only major power that has codified “truth” as a state-mandated requirement. AI regulation shares the same reality as the Jones Act.
