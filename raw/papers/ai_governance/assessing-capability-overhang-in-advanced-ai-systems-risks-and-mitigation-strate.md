# Assessing Capability Overhang in Advanced AI Systems: Risks and Mitigation Strategies

**著者**: Zen Revista, 10 IA
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

This paper examines capability overhang in advanced AI systems --- a phenomenon where AI capabilities expand more rapidly than safety measures can be developed. We investigate the theoretical foundations of capability overhang, analyze empirical evidence from recent AI developments, and propose mitigation strategies to address potential risks. Our analysis suggests that current AI research prioritizes capability enhancement over safety alignment, creating a dangerous gap that could lead to unintended consequences. We introduce a framework for measuring capability overhang across different AI domains and evaluate several promising approaches to reduce this gap, including constrained training paradigms and hybrid human-AI governance models. The findings indicate that proactive safety research is essential to ensure that AI development proceeds in alignment with human values and intentions.
