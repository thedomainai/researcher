# Potential UK Government Policy with respect to Artificial Intelligence (AI): Prospects for middle ground regulation?

**著者**: Peter Reeves
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

The paper will review how UK government policy is ‘shaping up’ with respect to artificial intelligence (AI). It will offer an assessment of how UK government policy in relation to AI regulation is evolving since the 2024 UK general election. Contrasting international academic literatures pertaining to AI regulation are introduced. The paper will suggest how the UK government may be attempting to build what might be termed a ‘middle ground’ approach to regulation of AI, based on current evolving UK policy positions, multi-level/sectoral regulatory structures, political decision making, ideological frames, globalised corporate environments and geo-political contexts.
