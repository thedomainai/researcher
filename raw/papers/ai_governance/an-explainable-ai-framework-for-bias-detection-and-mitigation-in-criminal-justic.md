# An Explainable AI Framework for Bias Detection and Mitigation in Criminal Justice Systems

**著者**: Vaishnavi Janardhan Gawand
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

This paper presents a conceptual Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) framework for bias detection and mitigation in criminal justice decision support systems. The proposed framework integrates data quality assessment, proxy-variable auditing, fairness evaluation, model explainability, bias mitigation, human oversight, and continuous monitoring. It incorporates LIME and SHAP to interpret model predictions and uses fairness metrics such as demographic parity, equalized odds, false-positive rates, and false-negative rates to examine disparities across groups. The COMPAS risk-assessment controversy is discussed as a case study highlighting the challenges of algorithmic fairness. The framework emphasizes transparency, accountability, privacy, and responsible AI-assisted decision-making. It provides a structured methodology for future implementation and evaluation without claiming experimentally validated improvements.
