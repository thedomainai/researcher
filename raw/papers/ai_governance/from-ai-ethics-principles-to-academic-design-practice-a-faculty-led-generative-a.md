# From AI ethics principles to academic design practice: a faculty-led generative AI case in doctoral education

**著者**: Scott A. Courtney, Bridget K. Mulvey
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

Abstract This article examines how principles of responsible generative artificial intelligence (AI) use can inform faculty-led academic design in higher education. Using the development of the Doctoral Scholar Development Framework for Education (DSDF-E) as a design case, the article documents how generative AI supported organization, comparison, redundancy review, terminology refinement, and language revision while faculty retained responsibility for values, content, institutional fit, scholarly warrant, and final decisions. The case contributes to AI ethics by illustrating one institutional example of how transparency, accountability, human oversight, verification, contextualization, and bounded use can be enacted in program-level academic design. Rather than treating AI ethics as abstract principles, the article shows how ethical safeguards may be incorporated into everyday institutional work. The resulting descriptive model identifies seven phases of faculty-led AI-supported framework development and six practical design principles for responsible use: bounded use, provisionality, contextualization, verification, transparency, and data protection and confidentiality.
