# Predict, Prevent, Protect: Transforming Onshore Road Safety by Leveraging Data and Technologies

**著者**: K. Q. Al Hammadi, M. F. Al Mansoori, A. A. Al Ebri, N. A. Al Blooshi
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

Abstract ADNOC Onshore operates across a large and dispersed onshore environment where road transport is a daily operational exposure for employees, contractors, logistics teams, project personnel and heavy-vehicle movements. This paper presents ADNOC Onshore's development and early implementation of an AI-enabled road safety transformation aimed at progressing toward zero motor-vehicle incidents. The program is organized around a six-pillar strategy—driver compliance and capability; people, compliance and engagement; infrastructure and vehicle movement control; technology and AI enablement; awareness, enforcement and culture; and joint partnerships and alignment—delivered through a dedicated Road Safety Taskforce and a structured Motor Vehicle Crash (MVC) reduction framework. Within that strategy, the paper examines the technology and AI enablement pillar, delivered through an initiative under the iASPIRE program that combines In-Vehicle Monitoring System (IVMS) data, Anti-Fatigue Driver Detection (AFDD), AI-camera pilots, drone-surveillance pilots and AI agents. The Road Safety Command Center (RSCC) follows a prevention loop of monitor, detect, intervene and learn, supported by a workflow of detect, assess, act, verify and close. Early results show improvement despite rising exposure: driving exposure increased by around 22%, while incidents decreased from two to one and the incident ratio improved from 0.013 to 0.0053 per million km. Because the incident count is small, greater emphasis is placed on leading indicators, including a 33% reduction in IVMS violations and a 77% reduction in unknown drivers. The paper concludes that the early improvements reflect the combined contribution of all six pillars, with technology and AI enablement making road safety risks more visible and actionable, and human coaching and field intervention converting digital insights into safer driving practices.
