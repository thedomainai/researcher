# Constitutional AI: Embedding Democratic Principles in Machine Learning Governance Frameworks

**著者**: Zen Revista, 10 IA
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

This paper explores the integration of constitutional principles into artificial intelligence governance frameworks, examining how democratic values can be embedded in AI systems to ensure accountability, transparency, and fairness. As AI technologies increasingly influence critical decision-making processes, from judicial applications to public administration, there is an urgent need to establish governance mechanisms that align with constitutional safeguards. We analyze existing approaches to constitutional AI, evaluate their effectiveness in protecting fundamental rights, and propose a comprehensive framework for embedding democratic principles in AI development and deployment. Our research demonstrates that constitutional AI frameworks can mitigate risks associated with algorithmic bias, opacity, and overreach while enhancing public trust in these technologies. We argue that such frameworks are not merely regulatory tools but essential components of democratic governance in the digital age.
