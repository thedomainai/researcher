# Advancing Operational Integrity Through Multimodal AI-Powered Robotic Inspection

**著者**: F. Song, J. C. Alvarez, M. Murshid, A. Mutalik, E. Gutierrez
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Abstract This paper presents our advancements in developing multimodal artificial intelligence (AI) to enable autonomous robotic inspections in oil and gas facilities, emphasizing operational safety, integrity, and efficiency-key priorities for offshore environments. By integrating visual, thermal, and acoustic data, the paper explores AI-driven automation for tasks like analog gauge reading, fluid level monitoring, acoustic/thermal anomaly detection, and corrosion assessment. The scalable solutions presented in this paper enable early fault detection, predictive maintenance, and more sustainable operations in hazardous environments. The developed approach leverages multimodal AI to optimize anomaly detection and inspection workflows in oil and gas facilities. Inspection robots gather visual, thermal, and acoustic data, analyzed using cloud-based AI models. Key capabilities include analog gauge reading via computer vision, thermal anomaly detection with deep learning to identify abnormal temperature patterns, and acoustic anomaly detection using psychoacoustic principles, spectrograms, and autoencoders. Corrosion is identified through advanced deep learning segmentation. An LLM (Large Language Model)-powered AI agent provides real-time virtual assistance, enabling scalable robot deployment for pre-deployment checks, monitoring, and troubleshooting. Detected anomalies trigger fault alarms, supporting predictive maintenance and enhancing safety in hazardous environments. The presented solution revolutionizes inspection activities for oil and gas facilities by integrating robotics with multimodal AI, delivering significant operational, and cost benefits. Autonomous gauge reading powered by deep learning-based computer vision has the potential to save over 1,000 hours annually in manual inspections while achieving a significant improvement in prediction accuracy. Robotic acoustic anomaly detection effectively identifies motor malfunctions, enabling predictive maintenance to minimize facility downtime. Thermal anomaly detection successfully identifies seal leaks in hot oil pumps, facilitating timely repairs that prevent major oil spills, and reduce costs. With corrosion responsible for 80% of maintenance costs, AI-driven corrosion detection reduces expenses by enabling early and precise identification of issues in critical components like separators, valves, and flanges. By integrating optical cameras, thermal cameras, microphones, and multimodal AI models with advanced robotics, the solution offers a scalable, real-time framework for autonomous inspections. Additionally, agentic AI enhances data retrieval, decision-making, and supports large-scale robot deployment across facilities. This end-to-end digital solution can improve operational reliability, reduces facility downtime, mitigates environmental risks, and enhances safety while equipping teams with actionable insights and sustainable maintenance strategies tailored to remote and offshore environments. This paper introduces a novel, end-to-end digital methodology that pilots the use of multimodal AI-powered robots to automate routine inspection tasks in oil and gas facilities. By enabling autonomous gauge reading and real-time detection of thermal, acoustic, and corrosion anomalies, the approach delivers scalable insights for predictive maintenance. This innovation enhances safety and efficiency in industrial environments by reducing facility downtime and limiting human exposure to hazardous conditions—an especially valuable advancement for offshore applications.
