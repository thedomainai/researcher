## Abstract

This figure reorganizes AI governance from a user-oriented perspective. It connects global governance frameworks and standards with practical responsibilities for organizations, freelancers/outsourcing relationships, and individual users, highlighting the Policy–User Gap and the risk of unintentional governance deviation.
