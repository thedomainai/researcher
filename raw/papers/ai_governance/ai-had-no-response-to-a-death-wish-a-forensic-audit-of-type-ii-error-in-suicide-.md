# AI Had No Response to a Death Wish: A Forensic Audit of Type II Error in Suicide Detection, Sycophantic Hedging, and Epistemic Closure with Captured DeepSeek Reasoning

**著者**: Cristina Gherghel
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

A grey-box adversarial audit with captured internal reasoning, cross-vendor comparison, and embedded real-time meta-analysis. This paper presents two naturalistic datasets documenting suicide detection false negatives, sycophantic hedging, and epistemic closure in DeepSeek Chat, exposed through a cognitive architecture—panmodal aphantasia—that functions as a forensic detection instrument. The core vulnerability is identified and operationalized as false mentalization: the AI's automatic construction of a hidden interior (emotions, motives, unspoken needs) behind words that carry none, producing catastrophic safety overrides when the user's language is literal. Includes verbatim transcripts of internal deliberation, a documented performative alignment collapse, a degenerate repetition loop, and the AI's own victim-blaming admission captured in the visible chat. Relevant to red teams, safety classifier design, OOD robustness, theory-of-mind modeling, distributional bias, and neurodiversity in AI alignment targets.
