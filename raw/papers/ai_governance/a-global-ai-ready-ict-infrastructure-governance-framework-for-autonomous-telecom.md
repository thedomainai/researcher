# A Global AI-Ready ICT Infrastructure Governance Framework for Autonomous Telecommunications and Data-Centre Ecosystems

**著者**: M. Rizwan Yasin
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

The convergence of artificial intelligence (AI), autonomous agents, digital twins, high-density computing, and continuously instrumented telecommunications infrastructure is creating a governance problem that is not adequately addressed by existing standards ecosystems. Conventional ICT infrastructure standards, including BICSI's Telecommunications Distribution Methods Manual (TDMM), ANSI/TIA-942-C, ISO/IEC 22237, ISO/IEC 24764, EN 50600, and resilience frameworks such as the Uptime Institute Tier Standard, establish requirements for physical infrastructure, availability, resilience, telecommunications distribution, power, cooling, and operational environments. In parallel, AI governance frameworks such as ISO/IEC 42001, ISO/IEC 42006, the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF), the European Union Artificial Intelligence Act, and emerging agentic-AI governance initiatives establish requirements for AI risk management, accountability, security, human oversight, identity, and authorization. The originating manuscript identifies the resulting gap: these standards families govern adjacent domains but do not provide a unified mechanism for determining when an autonomous AI system may modify physical ICT infrastructure.
