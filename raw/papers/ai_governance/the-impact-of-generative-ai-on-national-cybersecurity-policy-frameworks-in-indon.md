# The Impact Of Generative Ai On National Cybersecurity Policy Frameworks In Indonesia

**著者**: Chon Lap Wong, Santoso Wibowo, Rongbin Yang, Sameera Mubarak, Nurul Hidayah Ab Rahman
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

The rapid advancement of Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) is reshaping the global cybersecurity landscape by amplifying both defensive capabilities and cyber threats.In Indonesia, where digital transformation is accelerating across government, industry, and critical infrastructure sectors, existing national cybersecurity policy frameworks face significant challenges in addressing AIdriven threats and risks.This study examines the impact of GenAI on Indonesia's national cybersecurity policy frameworks, focusing on regulatory readiness, institutional capacity, and the adequacy of existing standards in responding to emerging AI-enabled threats such as automated cyberattacks, deepfake-based social engineering, and autonomous malicious agents.The study employs a qualitative research approach using semi-structured interviews with a sample of 50 respondents drawn from both public and private organizations involved in cybersecurity governance and implementation.The findings will inform policy recommendations for developing an adaptive, risk-based national cybersecurity framework that integrates GenAI governance, ethical principles, and multi-stakeholder collaboration.
