# Building Ethical AI Governance Frameworks

**著者**: Holly Kathleen Hall
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is no longer a distant concept. It is a driving factor shaping decisions impacting individuals, institutions, and societies. While the capabilities of AI tools have accelerated rapidly, frameworks for governing AI have not kept pace, leaving organizations vulnerable to ethical and legal difficulties. Direction around AI governance (structures, policies, and practices that guide the selection, development, and use of AI) is still in its infancy. And, while the establishment of responsible and trustworthy AI governance frameworks offers significant advantages to organizations, the field of AI governance scholarship remains in its early stages. But, in high-impact areas, such as healthcare, human resources (HR), and finance, the need for robust governance is urgent. This chapter highlights some of the areas, especially in HR, that are impacted by AI, the risks and concerns, as well as emerging best practices for organizations drafting ethical AI governance frameworks.
