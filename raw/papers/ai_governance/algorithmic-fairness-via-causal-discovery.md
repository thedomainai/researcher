# Algorithmic Fairness via Causal Discovery

**著者**: Jincheng Zhang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

Algorithmic bias poses a significant challenge to the responsible development and deployment of machine learning models. Traditional approaches to fairness often rely on identifying correlations between sensitive attributes (e.g., race, gender) and outcomes, which can be misleading and fail to address the root causes of discrimination. This paper proposes a novel framework for achieving algorithmic fairness by leveraging causal discovery algorithms. The core idea is to move beyond correlational analysis and explicitly uncover causal relationships within the data. We argue that biases often arise from spurious correlations that are influenced by confounding variables. By employing techniques like the PC algorithm, we can identify these causal pathways and design targeted interventions to mitigate bias. This approach offers a more robust and transparent way to ensure fairness in machine learning systems. The paper details the methodology, including the application of PC algorithms, and discusses the potential impact of causal discovery on fairness interventions.
