# The Role of Generative AI in Shaping Multimodal Experiences

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

This chapter explores the implications of generative AI on multimodal artificial intelligence, focusing on its strengths, uses, and limitations. The objective of this research is to offer an in-depth insight into how generative AI is reshaping creative processes, especially in multimedia fields where the integration of image, video, text, and image is required. This chapter delves into the primary methodologies, including generative adversarial networks (GANs), variational autoencoders (VAEs), and diffusion models, investigating how these technologies are redefining the capabilities of AI-driven content generation. The structure of this chapter is streamlined into four sections. The first section provides an introduction to the basic principles and types of generative AI, laying the groundwork for an analysis of its multimodal applications. Following this, an examination of practical applications, including the generation of digital art, audio generation, and interactive storytelling. The vital ethical considerations are also explored, which include intellectual property rights, trustworthiness, and ethical implications of deepfake generation. Eventually, the chapter concludes with an overview of forthcoming trends and expected challenges in generative AI, drawing attention to the necessity for responsible AI practices with the advancement of this technology. This research offers an understanding of the revolutionary role of generative AI within the expanding field of multimodal AI.
