# Bridging institutional AI strategy and subject-level teaching practice: A school-level design framework for responsible AI-supported learning

**著者**: Tommy Huynh, Eric Pardede, Leanne Ngo
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

The rapid growth of generative artificial intelligence (AI) in higher education has accelerated institutional investment in AI infrastructure, yet many universities continue to face difficulties translating institutional strategy into consistent subject-level teaching practice. This paper presents a School-level framework developed at an Australian university. The paper reports the design rationale, outputs and approved deployment model of the framework, including staff capability resources, configurable AI templates, disclosure mechanisms and governance processes. Informed by Educational Design Research and scholarship on organisational change in education, the paper argues that Schools can serve as meso-level environments that enact institutional AI strategy through resources and structures designed to support subject-level teaching practice. It contributes a meso-level governance model, six transferable design principles, and an operational framework intended to support responsible and human-centred AI-supported learning. Formal evaluation is planned following the pilot phase.
