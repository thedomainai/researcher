# Algorithmic Fairness Auditing via Bayesian Networks

**著者**: Jincheng Zhang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

Algorithmic bias poses a significant challenge to the responsible deployment of machine learning systems. Traditional methods for detecting and mitigating bias often rely on post-hoc statistical analysis, which can be insufficient in capturing the underlying causal mechanisms driving unfairness. This paper proposes a novel approach to algorithmic fairness auditing leveraging Bayesian Networks (BNs). BNs provide a framework for explicitly modeling causal relationships between protected attributes (e.g., race, gender) and outcomes, allowing for a more rigorous and interpretable assessment of fairness. We outline a methodology for constructing a BN that represents the causal pathways within a specific application domain. This BN is then used to simulate the impact of various interventions designed to reduce bias, providing a proactive means of identifying and addressing unfairness before deployment. The core claim is that BNs offer a significantly improved approach compared to traditional statistical methods, fostering greater transparency and accountability in algorithmic decision-making. This work introduces a new paradigm for fairness auditing, moving beyond correlational analysis to a causal understanding of bias.
