## Abstract

This record preserves Public Release 1.0 of the POD AI SOLO Constitution v2, an internal constitutional governance framework developed for a one-Founder + AI-native organizational model. POD AI SOLO is an independent initiative exploring how a single responsible human Founder can use artificial intelligence to expand organizational capability while retaining meaningful human authority, accountability, and final decision-making. The Constitution establishes principles and boundaries concerning human dignity, human rights, non-weaponization, authority and delegation, AI admission and re-certification, meaningful human review, constitutional memory and provenance, succession and recovery, responsible evolution, and lawful continuity. This Constitution is not public law, Thai law, governmental regulation, or a democratic public mandate.
