# EVALUATING BIAS DETECTION METHODS TO IMPROVE FAIRNESS IN MARKETING ALGORITHMIC DECISION SYSTEMS

**著者**: Dr. V. Antony Joe Raja
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: AIガバナンス

## Abstract

Purpose - This paper examines bias detection methods in marketing algorithmic decision systems, focusing on how fairness can be systematically improved in automated decision-making processes that influence consumer targeting, segmentation, and personalization. Methodology - A conceptual and analytical approach is adopted, synthesizing prior scholarly work on algorithmic bias detection and fairness-aware machine learning. The study categorizes bias detection methods into statistical, causal, and model-based techniques, and evaluates their applicability in marketing systems. Findings - The analysis reveals that hybrid bias detection frameworks combining statistical parity metrics with causal inference techniques yield more robust fairness outcomes. Additionally, real-time monitoring mechanisms significantly reduce discriminatory outcomes in dynamic marketing environments. Practical Implications - Organizations can enhance fairness in marketing algorithms by integrating bias auditing tools into model pipelines, adopting transparent evaluation metrics, and implementing continuous fairness monitoring strategies. Originality - This paper contributes by bridging fairness research with marketing algorithmic systems, proposing an integrated evaluation framework tailored to marketing applications.
