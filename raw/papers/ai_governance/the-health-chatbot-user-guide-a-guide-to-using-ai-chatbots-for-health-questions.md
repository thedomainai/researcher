# The Health Chatbot User Guide: a guide to using AI chatbots for health questions

**著者**: Elinor Laws, Duaa O. Khair, Aditya U. Kale, Ariel Ong, Danica Baluyot
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

The Health Chatbot User Guide is free, evidence-based guidance for members of the public on using general-purpose AI chatbots, such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Copilot, to ask health questions. Use of these tools for health is already widespread: in national polling commissioned for this project, 43.7% of UK adults reported asking a chatbot a health question in the previous year. Yet most of the existing guidance on how to use AI chatbots safely is written for a technical audience. The guide has a simple message: AI chatbots can be useful for health questions, but they can also get things wrong. Ask SMART: Slow down and think before asking health questions to a chatbotMake your questions clearAsk yourself "what am I sharing?"Remember to check the factsTalk to a real person when you need to The guide was developed using multiple research methods, including an online scoping survey, a rapid evidence review, national UK polling and patient and public involvement, followed by an international e-Delphi study and consensus meeting with 13 members of the public. The work was steered by an international group of patients and public representatives, clinicians, AI researchers, ethicists, and experts in health policy, regulation and public health. This Zenodo record contains the PDF version of the guide (version 1.0). An interactive version is available at www.healthchatbotguide.org. A scientific report describing the guide development and reseach findings will be submitted for peer review in the autumn of 2026.
