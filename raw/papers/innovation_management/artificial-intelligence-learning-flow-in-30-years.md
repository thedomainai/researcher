# Artificial Intelligence: Learning Flow in 30 Years

**著者**: Otacílio Antunes Santana
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: イノベーション管理

## Abstract

The rapid ubiquity of generative artificial intelligence (AI) has fundamentally disrupted traditional pedagogical frameworks in engineering education, necessitating a critical examination of its impact on cognitive development and knowledge retention. This longitudinal study spans 30 years (1996–2025) to analyse shifts in student learning curves within an AI curriculum, testing the hypothesis that reliance on automated tools exacerbates a ‘J-curve’ learning style, characterised by delayed engagement and terminal production spikes, at the expense of fundamental understanding. By comparing weekly topic acquisition and final assessment data across cohorts, the research reveals a distinct morphological transformation from the classic sigmoidal progression (‘S-curve’) of the baseline 1996 class to an exponential ‘J-curve’ in the 2025 cohort. While modern students demonstrate high procedural efficiency in project delivery, this shift correlates strongly with a significant decline in independent examination performance, where mean scores fell from 9.50 to 7.75. Although a gradual decline in performance was observed prior to 2020, the disruption caused by the COVID-19 pandemic and the subsequent ubiquity of generative AI tools may have intensified this trend (main finding). The data indicate that students utilise AI to bypass the cognitive friction necessary for deep learning, resulting in a failure to exit the assisted phase of skill acquisition. These findings suggest that current AI integration acts as a mechanism for cognitive offloading rather than scaffolding, creating a trade-off between immediate output and long-term declarative competence. The study concludes that engineering curricula must rigorously re-evaluate assessment strategies to ensure that the efficiency of automated generation does not erode the foundational logic required for sustainable innovation.
