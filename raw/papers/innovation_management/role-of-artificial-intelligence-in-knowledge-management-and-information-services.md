# Role of Artificial Intelligence in Knowledge Management and Information Services

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has emerged as a transformative technology that is revolutionizing the management, organization, retrieval, and dissemination of knowledge and information. In the digital age, organizations generate vast amounts of data and knowledge assets that require efficient management for informed decision-making and innovation. AI technologies, including machine learning, natural language processing, expert systems, deep learning, and generative AI, are enhancing knowledge management processes and redefining information services across libraries, educational institutions, businesses, healthcare organizations, and government agencies. This chapter examines the role of Artificial Intelligence in knowledge management and information services, highlighting its applications, benefits, challenges, and future prospects. The study emphasizes how AI contributes to knowledge creation, organization, storage, retrieval, sharing, and utilization while improving the quality, accessibility, and effectiveness of information services
