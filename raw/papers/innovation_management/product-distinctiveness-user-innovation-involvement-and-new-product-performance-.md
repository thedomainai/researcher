# Product Distinctiveness, User Innovation Involvement, And New Product Performance: Evidence From Multimodal Ai–Driven Analysis

**著者**: Fuxin Lin, ZHI CHEN, D Q Wang, Yiyue Shao
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: イノベーション管理

## Abstract

Digital platforms face challenges in extracting innovation-relevant insights from massive user-generated content, limiting the effective use of user involvement in new product development. We construct a multimodal AI–based index of product distinctiveness and find an inverted U-shaped relationship between distinctiveness and new product performance. Focusing on 79 games released between 2023 and 2024, we apply multimodal AI to classify 218,655 user comments and identify exploratory and exploitative user innovation involvement. A panel dataset of 1,619 game–month observations shows that exploratory involvement strengthens the performance of highly distinctive products, whereas exploitative involvement exerts greater influence when developer–user interaction is stronger. This study demonstrates how multimodal AI can support digital platforms in capturing user innovation signals and highlights the joint effects of product positioning and user involvement on new product performance.
