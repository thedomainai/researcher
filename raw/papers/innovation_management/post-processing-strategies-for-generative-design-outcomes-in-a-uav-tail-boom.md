# Post-processing strategies for generative design outcomes in a UAV tail boom

**著者**: Michał  Piotr Kowalik, Piotr Stapinski, Mateusz Papis
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: イノベーション管理

## Abstract

Abstract Generative design produces structurally efficient but organically shaped geometries that require post-processing before Fused Deposition Modeling (FDM) manufacturing. This paper compares two post-processing strategies applied to the same generative design outcome for an Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) tail boom: a complex variant faithful to the raw topology, and a simplified variant with symmetric arms, self-supporting geometry, and uniform cross-sections oriented toward FDM manufacturability. Both variants were evaluated using linear static and buckling finite element analysis underidentical loading conditions. Scaled prototypes of the simplified variant were FDM-printed at two infill configurations and physically load-tested. The manufacturing-oriented simplification reduced both mass and print time while eliminating support structures; peak stress remained well below the material limit. FEA-predicted stress concentrations correlated with observed fracture locations in physical tests. For lightly loaded polymer FDM structures, these results suggest that manufacturing-oriented simplification of generative design outcomes incurs a negligible structural penalty while improving printability and reducing production cost.
