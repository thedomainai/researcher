# Openness of Public Data and Corporate Artificial Intelligence Innovation

**著者**: Junyan Hui
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: meta_analysis | **分野**: イノベーション管理

## Abstract

High-quality data serves as a prerequisite for the continuous innovation of artificial intelligence. Scientifically evaluating the impact of public data openness on AI innovation is of great significance for unlocking data value and optimizing openness policies. This paper takes the launch of local public data open platforms as an exogenous shock for a quasi-natural experiment. Based on the matched data of more than 980,000 AI patents and listed companies from 2010 to 2023, it finds that public data openness increases the number of corporate AI patents by an average of 0.25 units. Parallel trend tests and various robustness checks all support this conclusion. Mechanism analysis shows that public data promotes AI innovation by expanding the demand for digital talents and broadening the scope of application scenarios. Heterogeneity analysis indicates that this effect is more prominent in AI hardware and general technologies, state-owned enterprises, and the central region. Further research reveals a synergistic effect between computing power and public data, and enterprises engaged in data center businesses benefit more significantly. This study provides empirical evidence for tapping the value of data factors and supporting the development of AI technologies.
