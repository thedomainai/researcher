# Framing Uniqueness-Focused Leadership in AI Adoption: A Structure–Process–Effectiveness Model

**著者**: Huey Chuan “Linda” Huang, Eric Canny
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: イノベーション管理

## Abstract

Abstract As organizations adopt artificial intelligence (AI) to enhance innovation efficiency, reliance on AI recommendations has led to performance homogeneity, creating an AI-generated status quo that stifles innovation competitiveness. In response, this chapter explores how leaders can inspire employees to think boldly, take risks, and develop innovative solutions beyond the AI-generated status quo. It advances a human-centered approach to innovation management by emphasizing leaders’ influence on innovation attention and individuals’ perceptions of their unique ideas, distinct from AI recommendations, as drivers of innovative behavior. Using the integrated structure-process-effectiveness (SPE) model, this chapter proposes a uniqueness-focused inclusive leadership implementation plan that aligns intended outcomes with structural factors throughout the change process. Although conceptual rather than empirical, the plan illustrates how the SPE model can guide organizations in fostering uniqueness-focused inclusive leadership and cultivating employees’ personal sense of uniqueness to strengthen innovation during AI adoption.
