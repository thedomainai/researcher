# Digital Transformation and Market Disruption in India

**著者**: Shridhar Laxmeshwar, Saraswati H. Bammanal
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

In recent times, digital transformation has been a major driver of economic growth and structural change in India. Technologies such as artificial intelligence, cloud computing, big data analytics, and advanced digital innovations are significantly disrupting traditional markets and their operations. The primary objective of this study is to analyse the impact of digital transformation on India’s market and examine sectoral changes. identify the challenges and opportunities associated with digital adoption. It also aims to evaluate how digital tools contribute to economic growth by providing competitive advantages. The research adopts a qualitative and descriptive methodology, based on secondary data collected from industry reports, research articles. The study concludes that digital transformation has become a key driver of economic progress in India’s market operations. However, it also creates challenges such as unequal access to technology between urban and rural areas. While digitalization enhances market innovation and introduces advanced platforms, it also brings cybersecurity risks. Therefore, effective policies and strategic investments are essential to address these challenges and ensure secure and inclusive growth.
