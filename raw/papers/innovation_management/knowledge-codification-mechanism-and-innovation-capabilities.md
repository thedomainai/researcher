# Knowledge Codification Mechanism and Innovation Capabilities

**著者**: Ebenezer Adewale
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: イノベーション管理

## Abstract

This study examined the relationship between knowledge codification mechanisms and innovation capabilities. Drawing on the Knowledge-Based View (KBV), the study conceptualized knowledge codification as the process through which tacit organizational knowledge is transformed into explicit and transferable formats that enhance organizational learning and innovation. Innovation capabilities were examined through technology adoption, new service development, and service quality enhancement. Using a conceptual and empirical review approach, the study found that effective knowledge codification mechanisms significantly improve firms’ capacities for technology adoption, service innovation, and quality service delivery. The study further revealed that organizations with robust knowledge management systems, documentation processes, and knowledge-sharing infrastructures demonstrate stronger innovation performance and competitive advantage. However, excessive codification may also constrain flexibility and creativity if not balanced with adaptive learning practices. The study concludes that knowledge codification mechanisms are critical strategic resources for enhancing innovation capabilities and sustaining organizational competitiveness. The study recommends continuous investment in dynamic knowledge management systems, digital knowledge infrastructures, and organizational learning practices to improve innovation outcomes.
