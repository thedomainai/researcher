# Preparing Organizations for the AI Era: Organizational Readiness, Capability Building, Digital Transformation, and Business Performance

**著者**: Joni Wilson Sitopu, Maiza Fikri, Andi Riwanto
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: イノベーション管理

## Abstract

The rapid development of artificial intelligence (AI) has encouraged organizations to move beyond technology adoption toward broader organizational preparation and transformation. This study aims to examine how organizational readiness and capability building support AI-driven digital transformation and how these processes contribute to business performance. A Systematic Literature Review (SLR) was conducted using the PRISMA 2020 framework. Relevant studies published between 2021 and 2026 were identified through SpringerLink and ScienceDirect. The selection criteria included English-language, open-access research articles related to organizational and business contexts. Following the screening and eligibility process, 20 studies were included in the final synthesis. The selected studies were analyzed thematically and grouped into four major themes: organizational readiness, capability building, digital transformation, and business performance. The findings show that successful AI adoption depends not only on technological infrastructure but also on leadership, data readiness, employee capabilities, organizational culture, governance, and strategic alignment. Capability building plays a critical role in strengthening AI capability, digital capability, absorptive capacity, intellectual capital, organizational agility, and AI literacy. Digital transformation subsequently acts as an organizational process that embeds these capabilities into business processes, innovation, learning, and value creation. The reviewed studies further indicate that AI-related performance is multidimensional, including productivity, innovation, resilience, ESG performance, environmental performance, and sustainable business outcomes. This review concludes that business value from AI is generated through the interaction of organizational readiness, capability development, and digital transformation rather than through technology adoption alone.
