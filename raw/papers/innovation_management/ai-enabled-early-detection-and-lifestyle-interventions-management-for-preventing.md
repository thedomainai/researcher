# AI-Enabled Early Detection and Lifestyle Interventions Management for Preventing Neurodegenerative Disorders

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Neurodegenerative disorders such as Alzheimer’s disease and Parkinson’s disease present escalating clinical and economic challenges for healthcare systems worldwide. Recent advancements in artificial intelligence (AI) have significantly enhanced early detection capabilities through multimodal machine learning models integrating neuroimaging, motion analytics, cognitive assessment data, wearable monitoring, and biomarker prediction systems. Drawing exclusively on recent Scopus-indexed research, this chapter examines how AI-enabled early detection can be strategically integrated into preventive healthcare frameworks, with particular emphasis on commerce and management implications. While existing studies demonstrate high diagnostic accuracy and predictive performance, gaps remain in long-term validation, economic evaluation, governance structures, and scalable implementation. This chapter synthesizes methodological approaches used in AI-based neurodiagnostics and extends the discussion toward managerial integration, cost-effectiveness, digital infrastructure requirements, and preventive lifestyle intervention models. It argues that AI should not be viewed solely as a technological innovation but as a strategic management instrument capable of reshaping healthcare planning, resource allocation, and value-based care delivery. By aligning clinical evidence with organizational and economic considerations, the chapter provides a structured framework for embedding AI-enabled early detection systems within sustainable healthcare management models. The findings suggest that when predictive technologies are supported by robust governance, interdisciplinary coordination, and preventive lifestyle strategies, they can shift neurodegenerative care from reactive treatment toward proactive, economically responsible prevention.
