# AI Governance Maturity and Enterprise Adoption of Agentic AI: Evidence from a Mixed-Methods Study

**著者**: Sergei Emelianov
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: meta_analysis | **分野**: イノベーション管理

## Abstract

Background: Agentic AI is diffusing rapidly across enterprises, yet Gartner forecasts that more than 40% of agentic AI projects will be cancelled by the end of 2027, primarily for organizational rather than technical reasons. Research gap: Industry reports show roughly one in five firms with mature governance for autonomous agents, but the empirical link between governance maturity and downstream adoption outcomes—including HR-relevant workforce and organizational dimensions—remains under-tested in the management literature. Novelty and contribution: We extend management-innovation theory to the agentic AI context and provide firm-level empirical evidence that governance is associated with three operational outcomes: (H1) self-assessed adoption maturity; (H2) projected time-to-production; and (H3) the diffusion of formal ROI tracking. Methods: Mixed-methods design combining a PRISMA-ScR scoping review (47 academic papers, 12 industry reports; 2023–2025) with an original cross-sectional survey of 136 IT professionals across 14 industries and 9 countries (Cronbach’s α = 0.81). Findings: All three hypotheses are supported with moderate-to-large effect sizes (Cramér’s V = 0.45 for H1; Cohen’s d = 0.70 for H2; 62.5% vs 3.1% formal ROI tracking for H3); 73% of respondents identify an organizational factor as the single greatest barrier. Practical implications: Managers should prioritise governance and independent ROI measurement—together with workforce reskilling and clearly defined human-in-the-loop governance roles—before scaling agentic deployments, repositioning AI governance from compliance overhead to enabler of management innovation in enterprise digital transformation.
