## Abstract

Abstract Financial management is an important exposure of a business. To establish and run a business there is a need of well knowledge of financial management. It includes supply of money, well profit, proper utilization of resources and creating safe investment chances. Innovations like FinTech, data driven decision making, AI in financial practices, increased Environmental, Social and Governance deposits, in addition organisations contributing to risk management, real time reporting, sustainability to maintain competitiveness in creating long lasting value. The article is related to Recent Innovations and Strategic Shifts in Financial Management. Important trends are AI, Machine and Advanced Analysis, Digital Transformation and Cloud-Based Systems, Sustainability, ESG and Ethical Investing, Open Banking, DeFi and Blockchain, Regulatory and Risk Management Pressures, Alternative Investments and Illiquid Assets. Article is purely theoretical and informative. the article is based on purely secondary data collected from various websites relating to financial management. Recent trends of financial management are moving towards increasing operational productivity but also delegates financial managers to play enhanced strategic role in organizational growth. Adoption of the recent trends are very essential for professionals to forward looking in economic landscape. It focuses on working capital, capital budgeting and revenue cycle.
