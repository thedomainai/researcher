# Ethics and Privacy in AI Marketing

**著者**: N. Rajkumar, Saranya Vijay, V. Gopi
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: イノベーション管理

## Abstract

Marketing operations have improved significantly through artificial intelligence (AI) implementation, which enables organizations to deliver personalized services and automation, while also helping with behavior analysis. Modern technological progress produces substantial ethical problems concerning privacy violations. AI marketing technologies produce various privacy and ethical effects on customers by creating problems with consent and algorithmic discrimination while monitoring activities and diminishing user control. The chapter investigates the moral complexities resulting from invisible algorithms together with fraudulent advertisement methods and multi-platform data monitoring while emphasizing necessary standards of openness alongside responsible management of information assets. The chapter examines the limitations of General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) regulatory structures because they fail to resolve the sophisticated challenges in AI marketing operations. The research has identified ethical-by design procedures and powerful privacy protections and consumer control as mandatory elements for developing responsible AI marketing practices. The study shows how maintaining innovation with ethical responsibility enables trust between public and digital marketing entities and protects individual rights within this domain.
