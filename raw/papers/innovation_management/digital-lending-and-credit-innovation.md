# Digital Lending and Credit Innovation

**著者**: Abdullah Mamoon
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: イノベーション管理

## Abstract

This chapter examines how Financial Technology is transforming lending from a traditional bank-intermediated model to data-driven, platform-based ecosystems that directly connect borrowers and lenders. It explains how artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning, blockchain, and Open Banking Application Programming Interfaces are reshaping credit assessment, risk pricing, and customer engagement. Emerging models, including peer-to-peer lending, embedded finance, and decentralised finance, are compared with conventional bank lending structures. The chapter also highlights key benefits, including faster processing, improved efficiency, broader financial inclusion, and personalised credit products enabled by alternative data analytics. At the same time, it addresses critical risks, including algorithmic bias, opaque decision-making models, cybersecurity threats, and regulatory fragmentation across jurisdictions. It emphasises governance priorities such as explainable AI, consumer protection, and cross-border regulatory compliance. The chapter concludes that sustainable digital lending depends on balancing technological innovation with ethical data management, effective regulation, and long-term financial stability in an increasingly interconnected credit market.
