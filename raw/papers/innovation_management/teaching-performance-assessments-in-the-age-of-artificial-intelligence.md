# Teaching performance assessments in the age of artificial intelligence

**著者**: Hayden Park, Aristidis Galatis, Grace; id_orcid 0000-0002-1177-1738 Oakley
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: イノベーション管理

## Abstract

Generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) is disrupting education and presents both opportunities and challenges in teacher education. The opportunity to develop pre-service teachers’ (PSTs) AI literacy through Teaching Performance Assessments (TPAs) is explored in this chapter, as are challenges associated with learning assurance. Mounting research suggests the need to prioritise assessment practices that verify student learning throughout the teaching and learning process and, where possible, in authentic settings, to ensure assessment is not compromised by GenAI. In this chapter, we draw on the authors’ combined experiences through a perspective methodology to critically examine current and future opportunities, along with the challenges that GenAI presents for TPAs. We conclude with four recommendations that could assist in designing TPAs in the age of GenAI. By adopting both a focus on PST AI literacy and certain measures to further assure learning, TPAs could not only safeguard professional standards for teachers; they could drive innovation and foster ethical leadership in initial teacher education.
