# A Multi-stage Framework for Plant Disease Diagnosis with Prototype-based Severity Estimation and Treatment Recommendation

**著者**: Mariam Hussein Raheem, Nadia F. AL-Bakri
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: イノベーション管理

## Abstract

Plant leaf diseases are among the major challenges affecting agricultural productivity and economic stability, making early detection and accurate severity estimation essential for reducing losses and improving disease treatment.This paper proposes a comprehensive AI-driven framework integrating disease classification, severity estimation, and treatment recommendation within a multi-stage pipeline, The framework utilizes an image dataset containing 37 classes, including 26 disease and 11 healthy classes, in addition to a newly developed treatment recommendation dataset named PLDK-TR.For classification, a hybrid architecture combining EfficientNet-B0 and Swin Transformer achieved 99.85% accuracy.Disease severity estimation was performed using two approaches.The first is a segmentation-based method using two RF-DETRSegNano models for leaf and lesion segmentation, with a semiautomatic annotation strategy applied for leaf segmentation, achieving mAP@50 99.01% and 94.08% respectively.The second is a prototype-based severity estimation approach measuring deviation from healthy plant references.TF-IDF and Cosine Similarity generated treatment recommendations, while an interactive GUI supported practical agricultural applications.
