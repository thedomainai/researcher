# Resilient Institutions in the Age of Artificial Intelligence

**著者**: Deepak Gupta
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: イノベーション管理

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence (AI) is rapidly transforming economic governance, democratic institutions, and public administration. This chapter examines how institutions can cultivate resilience amid accelerating technological disruption. Drawing on political economy and public administration literature, it analyzes the intersection of AI-driven automation, market-shaping policy, algorithmic accountability, and democratic legitimacy. The chapter argues that resilient institutions must integrate proactive regulatory design, transparent governance architectures, and adaptive policymaking frameworks that balance innovation with equitable outcomes. Policy implications for governments navigating AI deployment are discussed.
