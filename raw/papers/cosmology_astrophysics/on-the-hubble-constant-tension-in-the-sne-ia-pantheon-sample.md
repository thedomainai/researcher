## Abstract

The Hubble constant (H 0) tension between Type Ia supernovae (SNe Ia) and Planck measurements ranges from 4 to 6σ. To investigate this tension, we estimate H 0 in the ΛCDM and w 0 w a CDM (cold dark matter) models by dividing the Pantheon sample, the largest compilation of SNe Ia, into 3, 4, 20, and 40 bins. We fit the extracted H 0 values with a function mimicking the redshift evolution: g (z) = H 0 (z) = H ˜ 0 / (1 + z) α, where α indicates an evolutionary parameter and H ˜ 0 = H 0 at z = 0.
