# Competitive advantage in biodiesel production: conversion of glycerol and phospholipid-rich gum into value-added streams

**著者**: Rodney Helder Miotti Junior, Emily Vivanco, Diego Yamir Ocán-Torres, Leonardo José Duda, Natália Paes de Oliveira

## Abstract

The rapid expansion of biodiesel production has reinforced its relevance in low-carbon energy transitions while exposing a critical structural limitation associated with high production costs and narrow profit margins. Under these conditions, the effective utilization of by-products such as crude glycerol, phospholipid-rich gum, and soapstock emerges as a key factor influencing the overall performance and viability of biodiesel systems. This review repositions biodiesel by-products from residual liabilities to strategic resources by examining their conversion into value-added products, including biofertilizers and biogas, with particular emphasis on phosphorus recovery from degumming gum and carbon recovery from crude glycerol. These pathways offer opportunities to enhance resource efficiency and support integrated biorefinery configurations, although they also face key limitations related to process instability, feedstock variability, pretreatment requirements, and regulatory constraints that impact scalability and environmental performance. Overall, by-product valorization can play a central role in improving both the sustainability and the economic performance of biodiesel systems, supporting the transition toward more integrated and resilient biorefinery configurations.
