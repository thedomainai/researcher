# The Cosmic Microwave Background Spectrum from the FullCOBEFIRAS Data Set

**著者**: Dale J. Fixsen, E. S. Cheng, Joel Gales, John C. Mather, R. A. Shafer (+1)
**年**: 1996 | **被引用数**: 2364
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 宇宙論・宇宙物理

## Abstract

We have refined the analysis of the data from the FIRAS (Far-InfraRed Absolute Spectrophotometer) on board the COBE ( COsmic Background Explorer ). The FIRAS measures the difference between the cosmic microwave background and a precise blackbody spectrum. We find new, tighter upper limits on general deviations from a blackbody spectrum. The rms deviations are less than 50 parts per million of the peak of the cosmic microwave background radiation. For the Comptonization and chemical potential, we find | y | < 15 × 10 –6 and |μ| < 9 × 10 –5 (95% confidence level [CL]). There are also refinements in the absolute temperature, 2.728 ± 0.004 K (95% CL), the dipole direction, (1, b )/(264 14 ± 0.30, 48 26 ± 0.30) (95% CL), and the amplitude, 3.372 ± 0.014 mK (95% CL). All of these results agree with our previous publications.
