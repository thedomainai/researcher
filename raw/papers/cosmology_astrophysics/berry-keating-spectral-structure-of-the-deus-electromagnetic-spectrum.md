## Abstract

This analysis presents the rigorous derivation of the link between the Berry-Keating dilation operator and the logarithmic dynamics of the DEUS (Dimension Embedded in Unified Symmetry) model, demonstrating how its energy spectrum can be mapped onto the zeros of the Riemann zeta function. The article provides theoretical background on the Berry-Keating operator, its relevance within the Hilbert-Polya hypothesis, and its role as a bridge between scale symmetry and spectral theory. This work also integrates an explicit verification of the relationship between the electromagnetic spectrum predicted by the DEUS model and the eigenvalues of the logarithmic evolution operator, confirming that the model’s mathematical structure is isomorphic to the spectral system underlying the Riemann Hypothesis.
