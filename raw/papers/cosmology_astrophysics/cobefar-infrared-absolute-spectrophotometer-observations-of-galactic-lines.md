# COBEFar Infrared Absolute Spectrophotometer Observations of Galactic Lines

**著者**: Dale J. Fixsen, C. L. Bennett, John C. Mather
**年**: 1999 | **被引用数**: 240
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 宇宙論・宇宙物理

## Abstract

The COBE Far Infrared Absolute Spectrophotometer (FIRAS) observations constitute an unbiased survey over the wavelength range from 100 μm to 1 cm over 99% of the sky. Improved calibration of the FIRAS instrument and the inclusion of all of the FIRAS data allow an improved signal-to-noise ratio determination of the spectral lines by a factor of ~2 over our previous results. The resolution is low (0.45 cm -1 ), so only the strongest lines are observable. The CO chain from J = 1-0 to J = 8-7 is observed toward the Galactic center. The line ratios are roughly consistent with a 40 K excitation temperature. The 157.7 μm C II and 205.3 μm N II lines are observable over most of the sky. The 370.4 and 609.1 μm lines of C I and the 121.9 μm line of N II are observed in the Galactic plane. The line ratios at the Galactic center are consistent with a density of n 0 ~ 30 cm -3 and a UV flux of G 0 ≈ 15 μW m -2 sr -1 (10 Habing units). The 269 μm H 2 O line is observed toward the Galactic center in absorption.
