# Toward A Technocultural Perspective On Technology And Society: Reintegrating Technology Into Social Theory

**著者**: Gustaf Juell-Skielse
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 社会学

## Abstract

Contemporary research agendas increasingly prioritize mission-driven technological domains such as AI and digital infrastructures, leading social science researchers to perceive marginalization despite technology's growing societal centrality.This paper argues that this tension reflects not an overemphasis on technology, but a limitation within social theory, where technology is often treated as external to social life.We propose a technocultural perspective that conceptualizes technology as a collective, cultural, and evolutionary practice, and introduce a four-layer conceptual framework, materiality, practices, institutions, and transmission, to explain how sociotechnical systems emerge and stabilize over time.The paper contributes by reintegrating technology into social theory and outlining how social science can engage with the analysis and design of contemporary technological systems.
