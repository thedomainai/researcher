# Layered Intelligence Theory in the Logic of Reality: A Process-Logical Case for Deeply Human and Deeply AI Cognition

**著者**: Richard Dobson
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 1
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 社会学

## Abstract

Layered Intelligence Theory (LIT) is introduced as a process-logical framework grounded in Logic in Reality. The paper presents a dual Deeply Human–Deeply AI programme that unifies deeply human and deeply AI cognition through five nested, dialectically interdependent layers (physiological, emotional, associative, symbolic, integrative). It derives eight architectural design principles and four falsifiable predictions for cognitive systems that preserve human creativity markers while avoiding narrow expertise and taming.
