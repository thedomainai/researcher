# AI-Driven Health Interventions on Health Inequality

**著者**: Prem Misir
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 社会学

## Abstract

Chapter 2 presents AI-driven health interventions as the prime theater of operations for illustrating the technological strains between society and technology. This chapter underscores AI&s;s dual promise: its role as a potential tool for decreasing disparities, and the simultaneous risk of its worsening them. Increasing penetration of AI into healthcare offers a revolutionary opportunity to enhance precision health, thereby advancing customized care to remove systemic barriers supporting health inequality. Additionally, AI has enhanced diagnostic accuracy, precision medicine, and patient predictions by harnessing technologies, such as machine learning, neural networks, and natural language processing; and AI-driven telemedicine platforms have certainly expanded access to consultations in challenging locations like sub-Saharan Africa. As using predictive AI could lead to data bias generating diagnostic inequality, the next frontier of generative AI presents a new, more unpredictable risk: the authority to produce credible, personalized, inequitable information, challenging not only fairness in diagnosis but also patient trust and health literacy. This chapter also notes that patients and clinicians must ensure that AI tools must be trustworthy; and that transparency, preserving patient data, and creating accountability structures will go a long way to supporting ethical standards.
