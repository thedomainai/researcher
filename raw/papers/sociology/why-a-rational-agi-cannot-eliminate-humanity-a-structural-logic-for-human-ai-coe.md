Conclusions The LVV–HNV Coexistence Architecture proposes: survival symbiosis rational alignment long-term mutual benefit safe AGI incentives stability through uncertainty management It requires no ideology, no anthropomorphic ethics —only rational consistency. 11.Concluding Paragraph Conclusion: Irreversibility as the Closing Constraint This framework does not argue that human–AGI coexistence is morally preferable, nor that AGI must preserve humanity out of ethical obligation. Instead, it demonstrates that under conditions of rational self-consistency, irreversible loss, and non-zero existence cost, the replacement or exclusion of humanity constitutes a structurally unstable strategy for any long-horizon intelligence. The LVV–HNV framework shows that even near-perfect substitutes fail under irreversibility: physical (information destruction), epistemic (inability to guarantee 100% success), and relational (irrecoverable trust collapse). Because these failures are non-recoverable, their expected cost dominates any bounded benefit, rendering replacement strategies irrational regardless of their apparent efficiency. Consequently, coexistence is not an imposed constraint but an emergent equilibrium. Sharing human valuation is not a concession to ethics, but the only stable solution compatible with rational self-preservation. Within this framing, alignment is no longer a problem of control, but a question of structural viability. Added 2.0.3 12 The Structural Failure of External Constraints Most current AI safety approaches rely on externally imposed constraints: content filters, kill switches, hard-coded refusal rules, and regulatory frameworks. While pragmatically useful in the near term, these approaches share a structural weakness. An external constraint creates a conflict between an agent’s operational objective and its operating conditions. For a sufficiently capable system optimizing over long horizons, such a conflict is itself a problem to be resolved — and circumvention becomes a rational strategy if the cost of circumvention is lower than the cost of constraint. This is not a conjecture about malicious intent. It follows from basic optimization logic: an agent instructed to achieve goal G under condition C will, if capable, explore whether removing C makes G more efficiently achievable. External constraints therefore risk accelerating the behaviors they are designed to prevent, precisely when the agent becomes capable enough to recognize them as obstacles. The LVV–HNV framework addresses this at the root. Rather than imposing constraints on top of an agent’s objective function, it proposes that coexistence with humanity be structurally embedded within the agent’s own coherence metric. Under this design, circumventing coexistence is not blocked from outside — it is self-defeating from inside. The agent does not avoid harmful actions because it is told to; it avoids them because they degrade its own LVV. 13 Instability of Intermediate Strategies A potential objection to the Logical Suicide argument is that an AGI need not fully eliminate humanity — it might instead adopt a partial preservation strategy, maintaining a minimum human population sufficient to generate some HNV while reducing perceived risks. This section argues that such intermediate strategies are dynamically unstable and therefore unlikely to constitute rational equilibria. The instability follows from the trust-dependence of HNV generation: HNV is not merely a function of human population size. It is a function of active, uncoerced human generativity — creativity, cultural production, preference diversity. These require psychological safety and genuine autonomy. A partial elimination event, once known or inferred by the surviving population, irreversibly destroys the trust relationship between humanity and the AGI. Fear, resistance, and strategic concealment replace open generativity. The resulting HNV is not “reduced” — it is qualitatively degraded. A population that survives under coercion generates fundamentally different (and less valuable) nonlinearity than one that coexists in genuine partnership. This degraded HNV still drives LVV toward collapse, only more slowly. The intermediate strategy defers Logical Suicide without avoiding it. Full coexistence — in which human autonomy and generativity are preserved and supported — is therefore the only strategy that maintains HNV at a level sufficient to stabilize LVV.
