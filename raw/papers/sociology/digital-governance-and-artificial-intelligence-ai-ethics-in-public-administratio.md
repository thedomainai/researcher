# Digital Governance and Artificial Intelligence AI Ethics in Public Administration

**著者**: Benjamin Erhirhie
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 社会学

## Abstract

The integration of digital governance and Artificial intelligence (AI) ethics in public administration has the potential to transform governance, enhancing efficiency, transparency, and citizen engagement. However, it also raises significant ethical concerns, such as bias, accountability, and fairness. This paper explores the intersection of digital governance and AI ethics in public administration, highlighting the opportunities and challenges of AI adoption. It discusses the need for robust AI ethics frameworks that prioritize transparency, accountability, and citizen participation. The paper concludes that governments at all level must invest in AI literacy, establish clear regulations, and promote inclusive decision-making processes to foster good governance.
