# Social displacement and relational andragogy in generative artificial intelligence-enhanced learning and teaching

**著者**: Joseph Crawford, Michael Cowling
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 社会学

## Abstract

Generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) is changing how students learn and how they seek support. This conceptual paper develops social displacement theory to explain how GenAI may replace interpersonal encounters that would otherwise contribute to social learning, belonging, and access to future support. It proposes the ABCs of Human-GenAI Support Decision-Making, in which (A) achievement, (B) burdens, and (C) connections shape whether students rely on independent effort, human support, GenAI, or a combination of these resources. It then advances relational andragogy as an institutional response suited to connected adult learning in a digital society. Relational andragogy intentionally cultivates trust, compassion, mattering, psychological safety, and mutuality while preserving learner agency. Through these relational conditions, institutions can support achievement over time, respond compassionately to external burdens, strengthen students’ anchors of connection, and develop more informed human-GenAI support choices. The framework does not assume that human support is always preferable or that GenAI use is inherently harmful. Instead, we distinguish augmentative uses that expand access or prepare students for human interaction from substitutive patterns that may progressively weaken learning communities. Practical and theoretical implications are offered for educators, student support services, institutional policy, and future empirical testing.
