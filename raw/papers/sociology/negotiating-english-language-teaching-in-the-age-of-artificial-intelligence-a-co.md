# Negotiating English Language Teaching in the Age of Artificial Intelligence: A Constructivist Grounded Theory of Secondary School Teachers’ Pedagogical Adaptation in Philippine Public Schools

**著者**: Clarice Daryll Silang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 社会学

## Abstract

This qualitative study examined how secondary school English teachers in Philippine public schools negotiate and reconstruct pedagogy in response to artificial intelligence (AI). Using Constructivist Grounded Theory, the study drew on intensive semi-structured interviews with seven English teachers from selected public secondary schools under the Schools Division Office of Batangas City. Initial and focused coding, constant comparison, memo writing, and theoretical integration generated seven interrelated categories: continually recalibrating pedagogical practice; protecting the learning work from technological substitution; reconstructing assessment to make learning and authorship visible; negotiating ethical boundaries through contextual professional judgment; building adaptive capacity through collegial and institutional support; cultivating critical AI literacy while preserving human voice and communication; and mediating AI access to preserve equity and inclusive participation. Findings indicate that responsible adaptation involves recursive, context-sensitive professional judgment. Teachers evaluated whether AI supported intended competencies, preserved valid assessment and authorship, maintained ethical safeguards, and enabled equitable participation. The categories informed the emergent AI-Responsive English Pedagogy Framework. The study concludes that responsible integration requires bounded technological augmentation, professional learning, flexible institutional guidance, assessment redesign, and protection of authentic language learning.
