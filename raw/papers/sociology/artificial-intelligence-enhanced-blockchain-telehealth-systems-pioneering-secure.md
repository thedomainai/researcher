# Artificial Intelligence-enhanced Blockchain Telehealth Systems: Pioneering Secure and Intelligent Remote Healthcare Delivery

**著者**: Waseem Ahmad, Sana Shahab, Tauseef Ahmad, Mohd Anjum, Mohd Sadim
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 社会学

## Abstract

The artificial intelligence (AI)-enhanced Blockchain telehealth framework proposes a new architecture that addresses remote healthcare delivery challenges through AI and Blockchain technologies, making services secure, intelligent, and transparent. Traditional telehealth delivery models face significant data security, privacy, and trust concerns. This study addresses these issues by applying AI predictive analytics to assess service delivery risk among patient populations through patterns of telehealth utilization and accessibility across demographic, ethnic, and geographic cohorts. After collecting and preprocessing patient information from the Telehealth Services for Medicaid and CHIP Population dataset, a Gradient Boosting Machine model was developed, achieving 99.96 percent accuracy, 99.89 percent precision, 99.98 percent recall, and a 99.93 percent F1-score. The experimental evaluation showed promising performance, with very low gas consumption of about 22,500 units per transaction, a latency of 0.013 seconds, and a strong encryption throughput of 57.75 MB/s. These results confirm that the framework is scalable and highly efficient. Overall, the proposed framework would improve security, reliability, and interoperability in the telehealth ecosystem, supporting more robust, resilient, and patient-centered sustainable digital health initiatives.
