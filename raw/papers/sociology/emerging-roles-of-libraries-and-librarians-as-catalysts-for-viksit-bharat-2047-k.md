# Emerging Roles of Libraries and Librarians as Catalysts for Viksit Bharat 2047: Karnataka as A Scalable Model

**著者**: Afzal G. Jamadar
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 社会学

## Abstract

Viksit Bharat @2047, launched on 11 December 2023, envisions India as a developed nation powered by economic growth, innovation, and a robust knowledge society. Karnataka’s integrated library ecosystem encompassing 1,335 public libraries, 5,884 Arivu Kendras (rural knowledge centres) serving over 50 lakh children and adults, and academic libraries in government and government-aided colleges, positions the state as a scalable model contributing to this national vision. A significant recent stride is the March 2026 expansion of the British Council partnership, increasing dedicated Library Corners from 10 pilot centres (launched in 2025) to 70 across Arivu Kendras, thereby enhancing English language resources and digital access for rural lifelong learning. From a Library and Information Science perspective, librarians across public, rural, and academic settings are evolving from traditional custodians into dynamic catalysts facilitating digital inclusion, AI-enabled services, preservation of Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS), and community skill development aligned with NEP 2020. While Karnataka’s unique features like early library legislation, high rural digitization, and innovative community centric models set it apart through extensive rural knowledge networks, challenges such as funding gaps and capacity building persist. This paper analyses the emerging roles of libraries and librarians in Karnataka as information hubs and innovation enablers. It proposes a strategic roadmap encompassing digital transformation, public-private partnerships, and professional development. Strengthening Karnataka’s library network will advance Viksit Karnataka while offering adaptable models for other states to realise a knowledge-driven, inclusive Viksit Bharat by 2047.
