# Digital Transformation in Education Through Artificial Intelligence (AI)

**著者**: Dr. Shambhulingappa. F. Nallanavar
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 社会学

## Abstract

Digital transformation has emerged as a significant force in reshaping educational systems, with Artificial Intelligence (AI) playing a central role in enhancing teaching, learning, assessment, and administration. AI technologies such as machine learning, natural language processing, predictive analytics, and intelligent tutoring systems provide personalized learning experiences and improve educational effectiveness. This study examines the role of AI in driving digital transformation in education and explores its applications, benefits, challenges, and future prospects. AI-enabled tools support personalized learning, automated assessment, learning analytics, virtual assistants, content development, and administrative automation, thereby increasing efficiency and student engagement. Furthermore, AI contributes to inclusive education by facilitating assistive technologies, multilingual learning, adaptive instruction, and remote access to educational resources. Despite its numerous advantages, the adoption of AI in education faces challenges related to data privacy, algorithmic bias, the digital divide, teacher preparedness, ethical concerns, and implementation costs. The study emphasizes the importance of responsible and human-centered AI integration supported by strong governance frameworks and digital infrastructure. The findings indicate that AI has the potential to create more accessible, equitable, and future-ready educational systems while complementing the role of teachers in fostering meaningful learning experiences.
