# Theoretical and methodological foundations of organizing personalized english language education using artificial intelligence

**著者**: G.LOLAYEVA
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 社会学

## Abstract

This thesis highlights the theoretical and methodological foundations of individualizing the English language teaching process in higher education institutions using artificial intelligence technologies. The conceptual connection between individualized education and differentiated, adaptive, and personalized education is clarified, and the diagnostic, adaptive, formative-corrective, and reflexive capabilities of artificial intelligence tools are analyzed. The theoretical and methodological basis of the research consists of B. Bloom's concept of mastery learning, C. A. Tomlinson's theory of differentiated education, L. S. Vygotsky's views on the zone of close development, D. Wood, J. Bruner and G. Ross's scaffolding model, B. Zimmermann's concept of self-regulated learning, as well as the theoretical views of M. Long, M. Swain and R. Schmidt on second language acquisition.
