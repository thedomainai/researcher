# Artificial Intelligence-Driven Digital Twin for Disease Progression Prediction: A Comprehensive Review

**著者**: Asaad Abd-Alsada A., Zainab Najem Nemer
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 社会学

## Abstract

Purpose: This study aims to integrate machine learning and deep learning techniques with Digital Twin technology to improve the accuracy of disease progression prediction and support personalised clinical decision-making. Design/Methodology/Approach: The study reviews existing Digital Twin frameworks in healthcare and proposes an Artificial Intelligence-driven Digital Twin paradigm based on temporal electronic health record data. The proposed approach develops dynamic digital representations of patients that reflect their current health conditions and predict their future clinical evolution. Machine learning and deep learning techniques analyse large-scale medical data, identify hidden patterns, and provide early predictions of patient deterioration, particularly in intensive care environments. Research Limitation: Further validation using diverse, multi-centre clinical datasets and real-time patient information is required before the model can be widely implemented in clinical practice. Findings: The findings indicate that AI-powered Digital Twins can improve the accuracy of disease progression prediction, facilitate early identification of patient deterioration, and support clinical treatment decisions. The proposed approach may also enable proactive, personalised, and data-driven disease management. Practical Implication: The proposed Digital Twin paradigm can help healthcare professionals monitor patients, identify high-risk cases, select appropriate interventions, and allocate critical care resources more effectively. Social Implication: Improved early prediction and personalised treatment may reduce mortality rates, prevent avoidable complications, enhance healthcare quality, and reduce the clinical and economic burden on patients and healthcare institutions. Originality/Value: This study highlights the value of combining temporal electronic health record data, Artificial Intelligence, and Digital Twin technology within a unified framework for predicting disease progression. It contributes to the development of intelligent and patient-specific clinical decision-support systems for critical healthcare environments.
