# When Chatbots Become Companions: Generative AI, Loneliness, and Adolescent Well-Being

**著者**: Restu Bilal
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 社会学

## Abstract

Generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) has quickly moved from a school andproductivity tool into the social and emotional lives of adolescents. Young people nowuse systems such as ChatGPT to ask for advice, explain difficult feelings, manageconflicts, and find a responsive presence when no trusted person is immediatelyavailable. This paper examines how these interactions may relate to loneliness andadolescent well-being. It brings together research on loneliness, social pain, regionaland interpersonal influences on loneliness, studies of conversational agents, and recentevidence on adolescents’ spontaneous use of GenAI for emotional support. The centralargument is that a chatbot can reduce the immediate feeling of being alone by offeringattention, language, and a low-judgment space for self-expression. That short-termexperience should not be confused with the formation of a reciprocal humanrelationship or with lasting protection from loneliness. GenAI may support reflectionand help-seeking when it is used as a supplement to human care, but its apparentcompanionship also creates risks. Adolescents may anthropomorphize the system,disclose sensitive information, trust inaccurate or overly agreeable answers, and delaycontact with people who can provide responsibility and care. Future research shoulduse longitudinal designs, measure both loneliness and the quality of adolescents'human relationships, and examine how different patterns of AI use affect well-beingacross developmental and cultural contexts
