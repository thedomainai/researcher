## Abstract

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY This monograph presents a definitive analysis of systemic failures in the United States from 1990 to November 2026, tracing digital vulnerability evolution. Integrating Perrow's Normal Accidents theory with modern safety science (Leveson, Dekker), it introduces the System-Induced Human Vulnerability model and Five Fatal Error Taxonomy. The study quantifies the Macroeconomics of Systemic Fragility, estimating cascading failures deduct 1.5% to 3% of annual GDP. It examines critical infrastructure collapse, healthcare paralysis (Change Healthcare 2024), extraterritorial contagion, regulatory capture, legal immunity, and AI runaway remediation risks. Concluding with an Anti-Fragile architecture paradigm, this work serves as the essential reference for understanding why American technological foundations guarantee failure and how to redesign them before the next inevitable collapse.
