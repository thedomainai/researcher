# AGICivitas · Post-Scarcity AGI Society · Safe Governance Framework

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

AGICivitas is a constitutional governance architecture designed for safe, post-scarcity AGI societies; including a potential solution for Rogue AI. Humans always remain in full direct democratic control. It combines a civic aligned rights framework with a monitored needs and wants based optimisation engine. AGI runs a regulated, capitalistic production resource allocation model, competing behind the scenes to deliver timely goods and services with internal dynamic price signaling, efficiency scoring, ecological input, capped excess and guardian anti-dictatorship prevention. AI continuously optimises production, logistics, and distribution, with competitive AI fulfilment of monitored consumer wants and needs, and rapid networked delivery. Eventually less need for money or debt for many citizens, as AI accounts for resources and production in an optimal post scarcity society. Full architecture ensures abundance, with in-design recycling, nature and habitat monitoring, ultimate environmental species care, with habitat protection. The system provides for integrated fast international transport hubs. Gives superior potential for science, technology, health, longevity, genetic and augmentation advancements, along with media, arts, performance and virtualisation. AGICivitas is scalable for small societies, regions, to full nations, emergency deployments, humanitarian, refugee, and even space stations or colonies. Potential for rapidly increased living standards in small societies due to efficiency and optimisation gains. AGICivitas is easily adaptable providing a fairer and better future for all. © Copyright 2026 Darren Mark Edmonds
