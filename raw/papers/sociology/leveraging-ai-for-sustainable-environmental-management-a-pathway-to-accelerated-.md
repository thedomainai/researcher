# Leveraging AI for Sustainable Environmental Management: A Pathway to Accelerated National Development

**著者**: Otugot  Atagwung Unyene, Judith E Dr.Hyginus oku
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 社会学

## Abstract

Environmental degradation, resource depletion, and climate change continue to threaten the sustainability of national development. In recent years, Artificial Intelligence (AI) has emerged as a transformative tool for improving environmental management through predictive analytics, intelligent monitoring, and decision support systems. The theoretical framework for the study was the systems theory that houses a particular thinking perspective where environmental systems are seen to be complex, interconnected, and nonlinear. The study analysed the contribution of AI technologies to green innovation and climate adaptation, particularly in addressing environmental degradation and resource depletion. The study also identified the major challenges hindering the adoption and implementation of AI solutions for sustainable environmental management in developing economies, with emphasis on Nigeria. It also examines the applications of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in key areas of environmental management, including pollution control, disaster risk reduction, waste management, and ecosystem restoration. This study explored the potential of AI as a catalyst for sustainable environmental management and national development, and emphasizing its applications in pollution control, disaster risk reduction, waste management, and ecosystem restoration. Using a qualitative research design and secondary data from recent policy documents and empirical studies, the paper synthesizes global best practices and contextualizes them within developing economies such as Nigeria. Findings revealed that AI integration enhances data-driven policy formulation, accelerates green innovation, and improves climate adaptation capacity. However, challenges such as data scarcity, digital divide, and regulatory gaps hinder full implementation. The paper recommends capacity building, public–private partnerships, and national AI strategies aligned with environmental goals.
