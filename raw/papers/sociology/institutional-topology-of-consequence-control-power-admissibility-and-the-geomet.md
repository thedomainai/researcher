# Institutional Topology of Consequence Control: Power, Admissibility, and the Geometry of Non-Consequence in the Age of Artificial Intelligence

**著者**: Yochanan Schimmelpfennig
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 社会学

## Abstract

Institutional Topology of Consequence Control develops a formal-political framework for analyzing how institutions govern the conditions under which claims can bind as consequence. The central argument is that political and legal theory have too often begun too late: with subjects, claims, institutions, and norms already in place. This book instead formalizes the prior field in which harms may or may not become claims, in which claims may remain locally visible while structurally incapable of binding, and in which defeat can be preserved and accumulated under adaptive reconfiguration. The manuscript introduces and connects the following layers: harm space, the formation operator and formation kernel, the admissibility topology, the chronotopological triple of windows, routes, and delays, the defeat surface, the central spinal theorem, Recursio Intensitatis as monodromy, and the sheaf-theoretic realization of the jurisdictional graph. The result is not simply a critique of opacity, bias, or institutional legitimacy, but a geometry of the field in which consequence is governed. The book is philosophical in stake, formal in construction, and political in consequence. Although Artificial Intelligence provides a concentrated domain of legibility, the architecture is intended for broader application across distributed apparatuses of governance.
