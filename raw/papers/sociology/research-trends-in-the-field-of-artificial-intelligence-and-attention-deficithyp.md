# Research trends in the field of artificial intelligence and attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder: A bibliometric analysis

**著者**: Yong Liao, San-Jin Zeng, Cheng-Gui Xu, Liu Rui-lu, Jia-Yu Wang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 社会学

## Abstract

BACKGROUND Attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) is a common neurodevelopmental disorder among children and adolescents. Artificial intelligence (AI) can provide novel quantitative technical means for the objective diagnosis and precise treatment of ADHD. AIM To adopt multi-tool bibliometric visualization to map the global knowledge landscape of AI research for ADHD diagnosis and treatment, clarify evolutionary stages, core hotspots and unresolved research gaps. METHODS Literature was retrieved from the Web of Science Core Collection (WoSCC), with the retrieval cutoff date formally set to July 1, 2026. Search the WoSCC from January 1, 2016, to July 1, 2026, integrating search terms related to ADHD and AI. Combine CiteSpace, VOSviewer, and Scimago Graphica to visualize the annual publication trends, national/institutional/author collaboration networks, journal distribution, highly co-cited foundational literature, disciplinary citation correlations, and focused diagnostic direction multi-layer keyword maps. Calculate total correlation strength, total citations, modular Q, and silhouette coefficient and other quantitative indicators for quantitative analysis. The analysis was limited to WoSCC and Englishlanguage records. RESULTS In total, 151 relevant articles were included in the analysis. Annual publications maintained steady growth after 2020 and reached a peak in 2025 (with partialyear 2026 data). China topped publication volume, whereas Australia took the lead in international collaboration and cumulative citation influence. Two distinct journal clusters were formed: IEEE Access focused on diagnostic algorithm development, and Frontiers in Psychiatry centered on clinical therapeutic research. Keyword clustering and timeline graphs divided the whole field into two major research branches. For intelligent diagnosis, four successive technological phases were identified: Single electroencephalogram-based screening, multi-modal deep learning modeling, explainable AI clinical validation and integrated multi-modal comprehensive assessment, with neuroimaging and computer vision as mainstream technical pipelines. For individualized treatment, research split into two parallel directions: Machine learning-aided stimulant efficacy prediction and humanoid robot non-pharmacological rehabilitation. It should be emphasized that bibliometric metadata cannot verify realworld clinical translational superiority of any intervention modality. CONCLUSION Bibliometric mapping demonstrates that global research integrating AI with ADHD diagnosis and treatment has formed a relatively complete research framework from the perspective of publication metadata. Intelligent diagnostic technology constitutes the longstanding core of this field, while robot-assisted rehabilitation emerges as a fast-growing translational frontier. This bibliometric panorama systematically unpacks the developmental trajectories of diagnostic and therapeutic research branches and provides quantitative evidence-based references for subsequent clinical translational exploration.
