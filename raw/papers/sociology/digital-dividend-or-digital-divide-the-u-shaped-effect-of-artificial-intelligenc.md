# Digital Dividend or Digital Divide? The U-Shaped Effect of Artificial Intelligence on the Urban-Rural Income Gap

**著者**: Weiping Wang, Jianping Jing, Jingjing Ma
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 社会学

## Abstract

Whether artificial intelligence narrows or widens the urban-rural income gap in developing economies remains contested. We build a dual-sector general-equilibrium model in which AI enters as a continuous task-autonomy parameter and derive a closed-form marginal effect on the urban-rural wage ratio. The effect crosses zero once, yielding a U-shaped relationship with a unique minimum. Using panel data from 288 Chinese prefecture-level cities over 2012–2023 and a Bartik shift-share design, we confirm the prediction: the Lind-Mehlum test rejects monotonicity at the 5 percent level, and an AI-patent proxy reproduces the same U-shape. On the convergence arm, regional innovation mediates 31.8 percent of the total effect, while government science-and-education expenditure shifts the turning point outward. The U-shape vanishes in resource-dependent cities, where resource rents disable urban capital deepening. Policy should target the structural shifters of the turning point rather than average AI exposure.
