## Abstract

The El-Rakhawi Doctrine on Algorithmic Ecological Justice and Planetary Trusteeship is the first framework auditing the hidden environmental and social costs of digital systems. It uniquely integrates environmental science (ISO Life Cycle Assessment, Landauer’s Principle), algorithmic fairness (NIST AI RMF), and comparative law (Islamic Khilafa, Civil Law Precautionary Principle, Common Law Public Trust Doctrine). The doctrine introduces legally actionable tools: the Justice Equilibrium Quotient (JEQ) Master Equation, the Planetary Trusteeship Index (PTI), the Ecological Redress Function (ERF), and the Algorithmic Environmental Impact Assessment (AEIA). Supported by an open-source Python protocol (ElRakhawi_EcoJustice_Auditor), it transforms abstract stewardship into measurable, enforceable obligations, ending digital greenwashing and holding tech operators scientifically and legally accountable for planetary and intergenerational justice.
