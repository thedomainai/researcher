# Designing Integrated Human-AI Systems: A Socio-Technical Approach to Knowledge Management in Healthcare

**著者**: Kristin Weger, Vineetha Menon, Jennifer Bail
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 社会学

## Abstract

The integration of artificial intelligence (AI) into healthcare knowledge management (KM) offers opportunities to improve knowledge flows, decision-making, and clinical workflows. However, current AI applications remain fragmented and often address isolated tasks rather than supporting system-wide integration. This conceptual paper examines AI-enabled KM through a socio-technical systems perspective and proposes a collective adaptive socio-technical framework comprising four interdependent dimensions: the KM process, social, technological, and relationship contexts. The framework emphasizes interactions among healthcare professionals, AI systems, organizational infrastructure, and regulatory environments, supported by continuous learning and feedback. The conceptual analysis suggests that effective AI integration requires more than technological capability; it also depends on usability, trust, human-AI collaboration, interdisciplinary cooperation, a knowledge-driven culture, and strategic leadership. By positioning AI as an embedded component of an interconnected healthcare system, the framework advances a human-centered approach intended to support collaborative intelligence and continuous adaptation.
