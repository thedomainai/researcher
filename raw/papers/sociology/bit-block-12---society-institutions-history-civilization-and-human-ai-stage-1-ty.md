## Abstract

English abstract Stage 1 of BIT Block 12 consolidates multiple research lineages on persons, relations, social networks, collectives, institutions, society, history, civilization, and Human-AI systems. The dossier provides a five-tier, twenty-branch typed formal architecture; an institutional grammar; collective, authority, and delegation gates; history-bearing state; resource, viability, culture, and platform layers; observation and identifiability mathematics; and a scoped theorem/no-go and negative-knowledge portfolio. The block emerged through repeated runs: branch mapping, formal integration, typed-architecture audit, world-anchor mathematics, the institution-observable adapter, partial or held data pilots, and a comprehensive A-T mathematical atlas. The result closes with scope at the formal and mathematical-foundation level. It does not claim universal social laws, empirical validation, causal effects, policy validity, legitimacy, justice, or verified novelty.
