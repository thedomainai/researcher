# Artificial Intelligence, Economic Development, and Human Rights

**著者**: Jipson Joseph
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 社会学

## Abstract

Over the last century, the world witnessed unprecedented technological advancements and economic growth. Artificial Intelligence (AI) has been a major technological breakthrough, decisively transforming economic interactions in various sectors. With their unique characteristics of speed, accuracy, and efficiency, AI-enabled systems enhanced innovation, increased productivity, and advanced economic growth. However, current experience affirms that the benefits of AI-empowered economic development are not equitably shared. It results in rising poverty, economic inequality, and human insecurity worldwide. Developing countries experience them most severely due to the increased digital divide. The human rights of millions of people are directly or indirectly denied on account of the biased interplay between AI and economic development that primarily favors advanced countries. From this perspective, this chapter, using the qualitative methodology's narrative synthesis and critical analysis tools, examines the correlation between AI and economic development through the lens of human rights.
