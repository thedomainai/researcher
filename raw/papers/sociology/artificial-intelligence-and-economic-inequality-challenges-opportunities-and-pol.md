# Artificial Intelligence and Economic Inequality: Challenges, Opportunities, and Policy Responses for Inclusive Growth

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming economies, industries, and labor markets at an unprecedented pace. While AI offers immense potential for productivity, innovation, and economic growth, it also raises concerns about widening economic inequality within and across nations. This paper examines the dual role of AI as both a driver of inequality and a tool for inclusive development. It highlights key challenges such as job displacement, digital divide, and concentration of wealth, while exploring opportunities in public services, financial inclusion, and innovation. The study also suggests policy responses to ensure equitable distribution of AI benefits. The findings emphasize that inclusive governance, skill development, and ethical AI frameworks are essential for sustainable and inclusive growth.
