# What Is Real in Social VR? Exploring AI-Based Privacy Obfuscation in Social Virtual Reality

**著者**: Marvin D. Strauss, Jonas Keppel, Niklas Pfützenreuter, Stefan Schneegaß
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 社会学

## Abstract

Social Virtual Reality (VR) platforms enable users to share embodied representations, media, and voice, creating privacy risks when other users access personal information. We present a multiplayer VR chatroom that integrates AI-based obfuscation techniques for avatars, shared media, voice, and spoken content. Rather than masking sensitive information with visually disruptive filters, our system replaces it with plausible, contextually coherent alternatives. We evaluated the system in a paired VR study with 24 participants, in which one participant disclosed personal information across predefined channels during a structured VR interaction while the other attempted to infer as much information as possible. Participants were initially unaware that selected information had been obfuscated and were debriefed afterward. Interviews indicate that users value such privacy protection and perceive AI-based obfuscation as aesthetically convincing and largely unnoticed. However, this seamlessness also raised concerns about authenticity, transparency, and trust in social VR interactions.
