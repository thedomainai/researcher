# Bridging the Digital Gap: Ensuring Equity and Access to AI in Education

**著者**: Sali Mohammed Bobboi
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 1
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 社会学

## Abstract

The integration of artificial intelligence (AI) in education presents substantial opportunities to transform learning, enhance personalized instruction, and streamline administrative processes. However, the persistent digital divide remains a critical barrier, amplifying disparities in access to AI-powered educational resources. Socioeconomic inequalities, geographic limitations, and insufficient infrastructure continue to restrict equal learning opportunities, particularly in less developed regions. While AI tools—such as adaptive learning platforms, intelligent tutoring systems, and automated assessment technologies—can help close learning gaps, their effectiveness relies on broad and equitable accessibility. Addressing these challenges requires strategic policy measures, targeted investment in digital infrastructure, and inclusive AI models that accommodate linguistic and cultural diversity. Collaboration among governments, educational institutions, and technology developers is crucial to ensure ethical, equitable, and responsible deployment of AI in education. This paper explores the intersection of AI adoption and the digital divide, emphasizing the need for ethical AI frameworks, affordability initiatives,and comprehensive teacher training programs. It also considers the role of open-source AI platforms and public–private partnerships in expanding access. By mitigating digital inequities, AI has the potential to advance inclusive, high-quality education, align with global goals such as the United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 4 (SDG 4), and foster a more equitable, knowledge-driven society.
