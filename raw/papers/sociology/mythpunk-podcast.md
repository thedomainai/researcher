## Abstract

Title: Mythpunk Podcast: Where Archetypes and Algorithms Collide Description: Mythpunk Podcast is an experimental literary work by Anders McGarrighan that explores the intersection of ancient archetypes, modern science, and artificial intelligence. Presented as a series of satirical "podcasts" and lyrical debates, the work utilizes a "mosaic" structure—referred to by the author as "Poetic Mathematics"—to deconstruct complex philosophical and scientific narratives. Key Thematic Explorations The work acts as a creative critique of contemporary academia, reductionism, and institutional dogmatism. It explores several core intellectual conflicts across three distinct parts: Philosophy of Mind & Cognitive Science: Simulated debates featuring Jordan Peterson, Steven Pinker, Richard Dawkins, David Chalmers, Anil Seth, Michael Graziano, and Thomas Metzinger. The text interrogates concepts such as the "Hard Problem of Consciousness," Attention Schema Theory (AST), predictive processing ("controlled hallucination"), and the boundaries between biological reality and digital simulation. Neurodiversity & Society: Conversations with thinkers like Agnes Callard, Susan Blackmore, and Simon Baron-Cohen explore the "Double Empathy Problem," neurodivergent cognitive frameworks, and critiques of diagnostic frameworks like the DSM-5, challenging the "neurotypical" monopoly on formatting human experience. Technodiversity & Cosmotechnics: Engaging with Yuk Hui's philosophy to critique planetary homogenization, technological singularity, and "culture theft" in AI training datasets. Global Justice, Ecology & Post-Colonialism: Deep dialogues with figures such as Vandana Shiva, Nnimmo Bassey, Ailton Krenak, Mpho Tshivhase, and J. O. Chimakonam. These sections contrast Western uniformitarianism and corporate exploitation with open-source pride, Indigenous relational ecologies, and African philosophies like Ubuntu and Ezumezu logic. Folklore & Chaos: The abrupt intrusion of the Slavic folklore entity Baba Yaga introduces a chaotic, non-linear force of nature that disrupts rigid Western scientific models, turning philosophical discourse into mythic trials. The work challenges the "firewalls" of modern academia and social control, advocating for a transformation narrative that embraces neurodiversity and anarchic creativity. By blending "Snoop Dogg-style" rap, philosophical prose, and mythic imagery, Mythpunk Podcast serves as a satirical mirror to the systemic "cogs" of 21st-century civilization.
