# Trust but Verify: Understanding Consumer Responses to Generative AI Review Summaries

**著者**: Shuqing Liu, Dennis Galletta, Thomas Ware
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 社会学

## Abstract

AI-generated review summaries are becoming a common decision aid on e-commerce platforms, where they are attributed to AI and presented with or without traceability cues.Although prior work shows that AI-attributed summaries reduce trust relative to human-attributed summaries, this effect may not be consistent across consumers, and traceability has received little empirical attention.Drawing on the MAIN model and the Theory of Information Compression, we propose a dual-mechanism framework: Source disclosure influences credibility through perceived impartiality, while traceability affects credibility through perceived verifiability.A 2×2 between-subjects pilot experiment supports both pathways and reveals that machine heuristic belief moderates the source pathway, such that AI labels enhance perceived impartiality only among machineoriented consumers, but does not moderate the traceability pathway.This research advances an integrative framework for evaluating AI-mediated information artifacts, provides the first empirical test of TIC's rectifying intervention prediction, and clarifies when AI labels help consumer evaluations.
