# Leveraging Artificial Intelligence for Language Education in Nigerian Educational Institutions

**著者**: Aishatu Shehu, Comfort Eke
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 社会学

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence (AI) is reshaping language teaching and learning, yet the research that informs this change comes largely from outside Africa. This conceptual paper examines how AI can support language education in Nigerian educational institutions, where large classes, limited instructional resources, uneven digital access and multilingual learner populations shape classroom practice. The analysis is guided by an integrated theoretical framework that combines Vygotsky’s sociocultural theory, the technological pedagogical content knowledge (TPACK) framework and the task-and-conditions perspective of computer-assisted language learning (CALL). Drawing on international systematic reviews, empirical studies of AI-supported writing feedback, foundational scholarship on technology-enhanced language learning, UNESCO guidance and Nigerian policy documents, the paper discusses applications in reading, writing, listening, speaking, grammar and vocabulary, and in teachers’ planning, assessment and professional development. It also analyses challenges relating to infrastructure, teacher preparation, accuracy, academic integrity, data protection, linguistic bias and sustainability. The paper argues that AI should complement, not replace, language teachers, and proposes a six-step implementation framework built on clear learning objectives, small-scale pilots, teacher competence, inclusive design, ethical rules and continuous evaluation. Because the paper is a literature-based review, its claims are evidence-informed possibilities that require local testing. It concludes with recommendations for institutions, policymakers and researchers.
