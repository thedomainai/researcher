# AI Systems for Filmmaking: A Survey of HCI Research from a Labor-Centered Perspective

**著者**: Yuying Tang, Lin Gao, Haotian Li, Baiqiao Zhang, Rebecca Fiebrink
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence is integrated into filmmaking and has attracted attention in human-computer interaction (HCI). Existing studies have primarily characterized AI systems for filmmaking by their capabilities and supported tasks, with limited attention to the organisation of filmmaking labor during system use. We review 31 HCI system papers and develop a three-layer taxonomy of labor sites, labor types, and human-AI allocation, examining where AI systems are positioned, what types of labor are involved during system use, and how those labor types are allocated between humans and AI. Our analysis reveals a concentration on individual below-the-line contexts, increasing attention to structural and meaning-making labor beyond execution, comparatively limited relational labor, and substantial variation in human-AI allocation across labor types. We further identify opportunities for the design and evaluation of AI systems that account for the broader organization of filmmaking labor. This review provides a labor-centered taxonomy for synthesizing AI systems for filmmaking and a basis for examining how AI may become involved in broader organizations of creative labor.
