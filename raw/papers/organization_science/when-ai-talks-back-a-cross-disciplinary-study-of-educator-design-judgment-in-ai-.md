# When AI talks back: A cross-disciplinary study of educator design judgment in AI-assisted case development

**著者**: Xuanxuan Zhang, Mary Jesselyn Co
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

Educators are increasingly turning to generative AI when developing teaching materials, yet most research to date has centred on student-facing applications. This paper presents a work-in-progress study that asks a different question: what happens to an educator’s design judgment when AI enters the case-writing process? Two business educators – one in accounting, one in management – will each develop a teaching case study using AI-assisted workflows, documenting their prompts, editorial decisions, and reasoning through structured process logs. Drawing on Schön’s (1983) reflective practitioner framework and Abell’s (1997) 10 criteria for effective teaching cases, the study examines where human expertise proves essential and where AI contributes meaningfully. The pairing of two sub-disciplines, management accounting and management, allows us to examine whether disciplinary knowledge shapes these design decisions in distinct ways, although the study’s central concern remain the design judgement itself. Early observations from the instrument development phase point to a useful distinction: AI appears better suited to qualities that require evaluative judgment (structure, data accuracy, length) than those demanding generative judgment (ambiguity, controversy, authentic voice). We expect further patterns to emerge once data collection begins. This paper outlines the research design and its anticipated contributions.
