# Based on Information Bottleneck: A Neural System Model and Artificial Intelligence

**著者**: Jincheng Zhang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

This paper proposes a novel approach to artificial intelligence (AI) based on the information bottleneck (IB) principle, arguing that the fundamental nature of neural system learning lies in identifying and exploiting information bottlenecks. We introduce a computational model designed to mimic the formation and elimination of information bottlenecks within a neural system. This model leverages the core IB theory, aiming to achieve AI learning and reasoning by actively seeking and resolving these bottlenecks. The model's architecture emphasizes the dynamic adjustment of representations to minimize information loss while retaining only the most relevant features for a given task. We present a mathematical framework for describing this process, focusing on variational inference and Kullback-Leibler divergence. The primary contribution of this work is a practical, computationally tractable method for implementing the IB principle in AI, offering a potential pathway towards more efficient and robust learning algorithms. Furthermore, this model's design provides a theoretical basis for understanding how biological neural systems achieve their remarkable learning capabilities. ---
