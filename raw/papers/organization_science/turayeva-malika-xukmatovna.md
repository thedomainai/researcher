## Abstract

The rapid development of artificial intelligence (AI) is transforming traditional accounting and financial reporting practices. AI-based technologies, including machine learning, intelligent automation, expert systems, natural language processing, and advanced data analytics, provide new opportunities for increasing the accuracy, timeliness, relevance, and reliability of accounting information. This study examines the role of artificial intelligence in improving accounting processes and the quality of financial reporting. Particular attention is paid to the impact of AI on accounting process automation, internal control, error detection, financial data analysis, forecasting, and managerial decision-making. The study applies systematic analysis, comparative analysis, synthesis, induction, and deduction to examine recent theoretical and empirical research. Based on the findings, an integrated “AI–Accounting Quality” framework is proposed, linking AI adoption with accounting process automation, data accuracy, internal control effectiveness, financial reporting quality, and decision usefulness. The study argues that AI should not be considered merely as a tool for reducing routine accounting work. When combined with effective internal controls, professional judgment, ethical standards, and appropriate data governance, AI can become an important mechanism for improving the overall quality of accounting information. At the same time, algorithmic bias, cybersecurity risks, data privacy, lack of transparency, and excessive reliance on automated decisions require continuous human oversight. The paper provides practical recommendations for organizations seeking to integrate AI technologies into their accounting and financial reporting systems.
