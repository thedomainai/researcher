## Abstract

Supporting materials for a focused secondary qualitative analysis of practitioner perspectives on explainability in AI-enabled cyber alert triage. This deposit contains an aggregate, de-identified HCI coding summary and design implications associated with the manuscript “Designing Explainability for AI-Enabled Cyber Alert Triage: Practitioner Perspectives on Uncertainty, Progressive Disclosure, and Evidence Access.” The underlying interview records are not included in this public deposit. Participant-confidentiality and data-security procedures under Liberty University IRB protocol IRB-FY26-27-79 do not authorize public distribution of the complete interview notes. The deposited materials contain no participant identifiers, employer or organizational identifiers, classified information, controlled unclassified information, proprietary information, live operational data, or other non-public organizational information. The materials are provided to document the analytic structure and aggregate HCI findings while preserving the confidentiality requirements of the approved study.
