# Moving from Human + Machine to Human x Machine

**著者**: Instituto Tecnológico de Santo Domingo
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

Focus on the shift from simple automation to human-AI synergy. Key Idea: Instead of machines working "beside" humans to do routine tasks, explore how they can work "with" humans to multiply creativity and decision-making speed. Discussion Point: How can organizations move beyond 2025-era "check-the-box" AI features to deliver daily utility that actually improves human tasks? The shift from simple automation to human-AI synergy marks a transition from "doing things for us" to "thinking with us." While 2025-era AI often focused on offloading repetitive tasks (the "check-the-box" phase), true synergy integrates AI into the flow of work to act as a cognitive force multiplier. Organizations achieve this by moving away from static tools toward context-aware collaborators that refine human intuition, accelerate complex decision-making, and unlock creative possibilities that neither could achieve alone. https://interconnectd.com/forum
