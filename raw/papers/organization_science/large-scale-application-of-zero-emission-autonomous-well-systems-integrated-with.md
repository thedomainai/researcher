# Large-Scale Application of Zero-Emission Autonomous Well Systems Integrated with Artificial Intelligence and Renewables

**著者**: yi peng, Pei Yan, Yuke Wang, Yizhou Zhu, C. Li
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

Abstract In remote, uninhabited off-grid oilfields, well systems dependent on traditional fossil fuels impose operational costs that are dozens of times higher than those linked to grid-connected counterparts. The integration of solar energy and energy storage—key elements of sustainable energy infrastructure—into remote well operations provide an innovative pathway to significantly reduce operational expenditures, mitigate carbon emissions, and curtail the consumption of produced hydrocarbons. This study proposes a holistic microgrid solution that combines solar power, energy storage systems, and sophisticated optimization algorithms with autonomously operating well systems, specifically designed in remote uninhabited off-grid oilfield environments for further large-scale application. A series of artificial intelligence (AI)-based predictive models were constructed to estimate well electric loads and daily solar energy output, establishing a solid basis for accurate energy system scheduling. In addition, a set of optimized operational protocols for energy storage and solar power integration was developed to enhance the microgrid's resilience and minimize operational costs. For wells equipped with artificial lift technologies, production plans and operational parameters were further optimized in accordance with predicted solar energy generation, maximizing the utilization of green electricity and accomplishing the dual objectives of low-carbon operation and cost reduction. Field validation of the newly developed microgrid solutions has been carried out across several remote uninhabited off-grid oilfields located in desert, plateau, and mountainous regions. The results confirm the successful deployment of the off-grid microgrid integrated with solar energy, energy storage, and autonomous well operations. Both single and multi-well autonomous systems operated stably under the established optimization strategies, which maximize the utility value of solar energy, with the proportion of green electricity exceeding 90%. The optimized production plans for autonomous wells also demonstrated outstanding performance during daytime operations, dynamically aligning with the output capabilities of solar power and energy storage systems based on machine learning forecasting models. Moreover, the integrated solution has proven to be feasible and robust in harsh environmental conditions, including high-altitude and high-temperature areas characteristic of remote uninhabited off-grid oilfields. These machine learning-driven models and optimization-based solutions will act as crucial components in advancing the low-carbon energy transition of remote uninhabited off-grid oilfields. The operational insights gained from autonomous well applications can be extended to more complex upstream oil and gas operations, thereby accelerating the decarbonization of the entire industry and offering a scalable model for the integration of sustainable energy in energy-intensive remote off-grid sectors.
