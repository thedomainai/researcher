# “I’ll Ask AI First”: How GenAI Disrupts Collaboration in Agile Teams

**著者**: Jan Henrik Gundelsby, Marthe Nordengen Berntzen, Camilla Grøneng, Dan Vigeland
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

Abstract Agile teams depend on collaboration, collective learning, and shared accountability to function effectively. As team members increasingly use generative AI (GenAI) tools, these foundational dynamics may be disrupted. In this study, we investigate how individual human–AI collaboration affects agile teamwork through thirteen interviews with software and technology professionals. Our findings show that GenAI reshapes team collaboration and learning while enabling individuals to work across traditional role boundaries, but also reinforces the continued necessity of human oversight and team accountability. We argue that these dynamics challenge the notion of agile teams, and call for updated models of agile teamwork.
