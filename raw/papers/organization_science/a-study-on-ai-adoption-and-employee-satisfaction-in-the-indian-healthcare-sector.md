# A STUDY ON AI ADOPTION AND EMPLOYEE SATISFACTION IN THE INDIAN HEALTHCARE SECTOR

**著者**: D. RAKESH .
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

The use of artificial intelligence (AI) in healthcare systems is rapidly increasing in India, with the market estimated to grow from $0.83 billion in 2023 to $17.75 billion by 2032. Employee satisfaction remains a major concern that relevant studies in the healthcare sector have yet to address. This article explores how AI integration is affecting employee satisfaction in India’s healthcare industry. The healthcare industry faces a 60% shortage of digital skills; 47% of Indian healthcare workers are not digitally literate, and only 34% of organizations are advancing toward piloting AI projects, despite 86% having adopted AI. Implementation challenges include limited technical expertise among clinicians, the psychological burden of increased monitoring, poor rural infrastructure, data privacy concerns, and workforce constraints. For AI to succeed, it must be presented as a supplement to workers, not a replacement. This shift should include targeted training to address digital literacy gaps, governance that incorporates clinician input in system design, and ongoing staffing support. Existing evidence suggests that AI adoption may be associated with burnout and reduced employee satisfaction when it is not coupled with these integrated efforts. Beyond technical expertise, research highlights human-centered design, thorough training for healthcare professionals, and maintaining autonomy as key drivers of effective AI integration in healthcare in India. To benefit from AI, healthcare facilities in India must adopt modern technology. They must also prioritize staff well-being.
