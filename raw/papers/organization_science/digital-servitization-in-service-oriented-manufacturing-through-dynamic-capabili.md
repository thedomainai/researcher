# Digital Servitization in Service-Oriented Manufacturing Through Dynamic Capabilities and Supply Chain Transformation

**著者**: Thinh Gia Hoang, Tien Tran Thuy Dang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

This chapter examines how Industry 4.0 technologies reshape servitization and service-oriented manufacturing within digitally transforming supply chains. While digital servitization is often associated with technologies such as IoT, artificial intelligence, cloud platforms, analytics, and digital twins, the chapter argues that technology adoption alone is insufficient to generate service-oriented performance. Drawing on service-dominant logic, business model innovation, supply chain integration, socio-technical systems thinking, and dynamic capabilities theory, the chapter develops a theory-informed model explaining how manufacturers sense digital service opportunities, seize them through Industry 4.0-enabled service offerings, and reconfigure supply chain relationships around lifecycle value creation. The model is applied to an anonymous industrial equipment manufacturing case that illustrates the transition from product sales and reactive maintenance toward predictive maintenance, remote diagnostics, lifecycle service agreements, and recurring service revenue. The chapter contributes by positioning digital servitization as a capability-driven supply chain transformation and highlights practical conditions related to data governance, supplier readiness, customer trust, cybersecurity, and service-oriented performance measurement.
