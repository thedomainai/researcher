# Conversational AI Capability and Organizational Agility

**著者**: Zahoua Selmi, Moez Ltifi
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

Conversational artificial intelligence (AI) has emerged as a strategic capability for enhancing customer interactions and supporting organizational processes. However, the mechanisms through which conversational AI capability contributes to organizational agility remain underexplored. Drawing on the Dynamic Capabilities View, Service-Dominant Logic, and Information Processing Theory, this study examines the mediating roles of customer experience and decision-making efficiency. A quantitative, cross-sectional design was adopted using perceptual survey data collected from managers in highly digitalized companies. The proposed model was tested using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM). The findings indicate that conversational AI capability is positively associated with organizational agility, both directly and indirectly through customer experience and decision-making efficiency. The study contributes to the literature by integrating complementary theoretical perspectives to explain how conversational AI capabilities foster organizational agility and offers practical implications for organizations seeking to strengthen their adaptive capabilities in dynamic environments. Because the study relies on cross-sectional perceptual data, the findings should be interpreted as evidence of associations rather than causal relationships.
