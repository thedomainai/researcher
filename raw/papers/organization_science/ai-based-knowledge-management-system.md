# AI Based Knowledge Management System

**著者**: Kimberly Jane E. Litera, Rolaida L. Sonza
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

Higher education institutions face persistent challenges in delivering timely, accurate, and accessible information, particularly during peak academic periods. At the Nueva Ecija University of Science and Technology (NEUST), repeated inquiries, reliance on physical office visits, and fragmented information sources contribute to inefficiencies in service delivery. This study aimed to design, develop, and evaluate an AI-Based Knowledge Management System with a chatbot-enabled smart assistance desk to support student-related and general university inquiries. The study employed a descriptive–developmental research design guided by the Agile Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC). Verified institutional documents, including policies, FAQs, directory data, and academic program information, were used as primary knowledge sources. The system adopts a hybrid AI approach, utilizing a pre-trained SentenceTransformer (MiniLM) model for semantic similarity matching combined with structured knowledge retrieval. User queries are processed through normalization, synonym expansion, and vector-based comparison using cosine similarity to generate context-aware responses. Ethical considerations were guided by the Microsoft Responsible AI Standard and the Purdue AI Evaluation Framework. Evaluation involved IT experts and end users from NEUST Sumacab Campus. Results indicated that the system achieved a Highly Acceptable rating across all dimensions, including functionality, accuracy, usability, and ethical AI use. Findings demonstrate that the system provides reliable, consistent, and context-aware responses while enhancing accessibility and user experience. Overall, the system is a technically sound and ethically aligned solution that improves information access, supports administrative efficiency, and contributes to student-centered digital transformation in higher education.
