# THE IMPACT OF THE HUMANIZATION OF GENERATIVE AI ON ORGANIZATIONAL CHANGE AND TALENT MANAGEMENT

**著者**: Noé Chávez Hernández, Samuel Garrido Roldán, Gisela Janeth Espinosa Martínez, Verónica Velázquez Romero
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) has impacted organizations. Sin embargo, aún existe una brecha en la comprensión de cómo su humanización afecta los procesos organizacionales. The purpose of this study was to examine the potential effect of the humanization of GenAI on organizational transformation and to clarify the consequences of such humanization on talent management. A review of the scientific literature was conducted between 2023 and 2025, using AI-assisted tools and a Boolean search equation, in Spanish and English. It was determined that the humanization of GenAI promotes adaptability to change, optimizes the work experience, and fosters innovation through a balance between technological and human capabilities. Moreover, it influences talent management through the automation of routine tasks, the personalization of professional development, and the promotion of human skills. It was concluded that the success of humanized GenAI is achieved by balancing technology and human competencies, through talent management with proactive leadership and continuous training strategies to build organizational cultures oriented toward innovation.
