# KNOWLEDGE MANAGEMENT CHALLENGES IN BRAZILIAN HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS AMIDST THE RISE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

**著者**: Thiago Henrique Almino Francisco, Giancarlo Moser, Jeanderson Domingos Minotto Bombazar, Mouhamadou Moustapha Seck
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

This study examines knowledge management (KM) challenges in higher education institutions (HEIs) in the context of artificial intelligence (AI), focusing on Brazilian universities. Adopting a qualitative multiple-case study approach (Yin, 2018), data were collected through interviews, documents, and observations. The findings identify four interconnected challenges: technological limitations, human and cultural resistance, ethical and governance dilemmas, and academic integrity concerns. Interpreted through neo-institutional theory (DiMaggio &amp; Powell, 1983; Oliver, 1991) and organizational ambidexterity (Tushman &amp; O’Reilly, 1996), the results reveal tensions between innovation and academic values. The study contributes by conceptualizing AI integration as a socio-technical transformation that requires alignment between infrastructure, governance, human capabilities, and pedagogical practices.
