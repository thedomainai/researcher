# Turning the Threat Around: Restoring Online Community Engagement with Generative AI

**著者**: Luke Goh, Shi-Ying Lim
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

Online knowledge communities face a threat from Generative AI (GenAI): Users can obtain answers without engaging with the communities that produced the underlying knowledge. We therefore ask: Can GenAI be designed to restore community engagement, and if so, under what conditions and with what constraints? Through a Design Science Research study of IKEAhackers, an online creative knowledge community for DIY furniture modification, we develop a domain-specific Retrieval-Augmented Generation chatbot and evaluate it with 20 users. We identify generative concretization as a mechanism by which GenAI helps users articulate underspecified problems and connect them to potential solutions, exposing questions about enactment. Users may then return to community sources for verification, particularly when they need experiential evidence of a solution's feasibility. We derive three design tensions and principles for restoring engagement with community knowledge. We also discuss boundary conditions related to the creative problem-solving process and the type of knowledge required.
