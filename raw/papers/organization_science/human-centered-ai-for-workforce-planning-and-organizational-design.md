# Human-Centered AI for Workforce Planning and Organizational Design

**著者**: Manolo Logrono Anto
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

HR leaders need to develop both flexible and strategic competencies because modern workplaces require this combination of abilities. AI has achieved widespread adoption across multiple fields, including workforce planning, recruitment, predictive analytics, and data-driven support. The implementation of AI solutions brings numerous benefits to organizations, yet these solutions raise concerns about issues related to ethics, equity, system transparency, accountability, employee health impacts, and the preservation of human decision-making authority. The research investigates Human-Centred AI applications in HR management, which use AI technology to assist professionals who need to develop their abilities to use specialized knowledge and situational awareness. The study conducts a comprehensive analysis of existing AI research in HR workforce planning studies by using the PRISMA research framework. The results show that organizations must develop ethical guidelines, inclusive methods, and responsible AI deployment to establish reliable, resilient, environmentally sustainable workplace.
