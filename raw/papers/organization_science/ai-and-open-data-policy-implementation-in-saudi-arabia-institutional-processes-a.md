# Ai And Open Data Policy Implementation In Saudi Arabia: Institutional Processes And Organizational Practices In Emerging Economies

**著者**: Mona Abusurrah
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

Governments increasingly bundle AI adoption, data governance, and open-data obligations within single national strategies, yet more is known about policy design than about implementation.This TREO paper examines Saudi Arabia's National Strategy for Data and AI (NSDAI) as an integrated policy bundle and asks how organizational processes shape enactment in an emerging economy.Drawing on 27 interviews and 284 documents from 2020-2024, the paper develops a preliminary process model open to refinement with three interrelated mechanisms: institutional mandate construction, capacity building through organizational learning, and transparency-control negotiation.Using Technology Enactment Framework as the core lens, complemented by process theorizing and a selective street-level bureaucracy perspective, the paper argues that AI amplifies rather than replaces established implementation dynamics.
