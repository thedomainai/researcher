# Information Entropy and Cognitive Architecture: Assessing Artificial Consciousness and Learning

**著者**: Jincheng Zhang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

This paper explores the intersection of information entropy and cognitive architecture, investigating how this framework can be used to evaluate the self-awareness and learning capabilities of artificial systems. We propose a novel information entropy model for assessing these crucial properties and design a learning algorithm based on this model. The core claim centers on the ability to detect self-awareness through the dynamic assessment of information entropy within a cognitive architecture. This research utilizes entropy as a key metric to determine the system's ability to self-regulate and iteratively improve. The study focuses on the potential for this approach to provide a more nuanced and objective evaluation of artificial intelligence.
