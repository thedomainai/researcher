# Blame the Prompt: How Experts Attribute AI Errors in Hybrid Teams

**著者**: Andre Govorusic, Philipp Wunderlich, Dennis M. Steininger
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

As Agentic AI integrates into knowledge-intensive workflows, organizations anticipate unprecedented productivity, yet hybrid teams frequently underperform. To explore this collaborative paradox, we conducted an inductive qualitative study with management professionals to investigate how experts dynamically negotiate trust following critical AI failures. Our preliminary findings suggest a psychological defense mechanism termed the Internalization of Machine Error. Instead of abandoning an erring AI, experts tend to blame their own prompts, cognitively framing the system as a mere reactive tool. When subsequent relational trust repair fails, users appear to establish rigid cognitive boundaries that manifest as a Knowledge Paradox, in which experts micromanage highly verifiable tasks while risking overreliance in unfamiliar domains. Ultimately, our emerging model suggests that such behavioral adaptations deliberately silo the AI, potentially hindering the successful integration of Transactive Intelligent Memory Systems in professional practice.
