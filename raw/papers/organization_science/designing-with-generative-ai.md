# Designing with generative AI

**著者**: Geordie Fung
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

This chapter examines how generative AI (GenAI) was applied to design a Monitoring, Evaluation, and Learning Framework for a policy think tank. The case demonstrates that GenAI can accelerate literature synthesis, generate draft frameworks, and analyze large volumes of organizational outputs, but its results require extensive correction to ensure conceptual clarity and contextual fit. The lessons for evaluators are threefold: GenAI is most valuable as a design aide rather than an architect, evaluator competencies in reasoning and contextualization remain essential, and future design processes must safeguard critical human functions such as defining value, engaging stakeholders, and surfacing trade-offs.
