# Coupled Human-Artificial Team Cognition: A Restricted Methodological Archive (2026-08)

## Abstract

This restricted deposit archives a research framework for describing persistent human-artificial cognitive teams. The unit of analysis is the coupled arrangement of a human operator, several general-purpose models, external memory, tools, and environmental feedback. Capability is treated as a property of that system boundary, not of any single component. The archive describes tacit coordination skill, path-dependent integration, persistent records, differentiated model roles, and a closed loop from observation through representation and action back to an independent environment. The person remains the sovereign layer. The principle is augmentation without abdication of judgment. The described method is formulated for all entities and for teams of entities. It is not limited to one person, one model, or one domain. The same descriptive apparatus applies to individual operators, organisations, and mixed human-artificial teams. The deposit contains a conceptual position paper, a German working paper that frames the claim as a testable measurement question, and supporting notes on prior literature and study design. It does not claim a settled empirical result. It asks where capability should be attributed before it is measured. File access is restricted.
