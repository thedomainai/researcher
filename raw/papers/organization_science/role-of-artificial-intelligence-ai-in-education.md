# Role of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in Education

**著者**: Tidake Kiran Subhash
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

Abstract Artificial Intelligence (AI) is rapidly becoming an important component of the contemporary education system. The application of AI has introduced new possibilities in the areas of individualized learning, academic assessment, teaching support, educational research, accessibility, and institutional management. AI-enabled systems can process large volumes of learner-related information, identify patterns in academic performance, suggest appropriate learning materials, and provide timely feedback. The development of Generative AI has further expanded the scope of educational technology by allowing learners and educators to obtain explanations, examples, questions, summaries, and other educational resources through natural-language interaction. The significance of AI in education extends beyond the automation of routine activities. It can contribute to a shift from a largely uniform teaching model towards a more learner-oriented and flexible educational environment. Students with different levels of prior knowledge, learning abilities, and educational requirements may benefit from adaptive learning systems and AI-supported educational resources. At the same time, the growing use of AI presents important concerns concerning academic integrity, privacy, reliability of information, algorithmic discrimination, unequal access to technology, and overdependence on automated systems. The present paper examines the changing role of Artificial Intelligence in education with particular emphasis on its major applications, advantages, limitations, ethical concerns, and future possibilities. The study is descriptive and analytical and relies mainly on secondary sources such as books, academic studies, institutional publications, policy documents, and reports of international organizations. The paper emphasizes that AI should be regarded as a complementary educational technology rather than a substitute for teachers. Meaningful integration of AI requires appropriate teacher training, digital and AI literacy, protection of learner data, ethical standards, and continuous human supervision.
