# AI-DRIVEN CONSTRUCTION QUALITY MANAGEMENT AND INTELLIGENT INFRASTRUCTURE SYSTEMS

**著者**: Syed Qalab Abbas Syed
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

Quality failures in construction are expensive to put right, and in infrastructure they can linger for decades. Artificial intelligence (AI) is now used both to check quality while assets are built and to monitor their condition in service, yet these two bodies of research are usually studied in isolation. This paper reviews AI for construction quality management and for intelligent infrastructure systems, and proposes a framework that links the two. The corpus holds 30 peer-reviewed journal articles, the earliest from 2020 and the latest from 2025. The search was documented and rerun in OpenAlex on 16 November 2026 with eleven stated search strings, and the selection flow is reported in full. Studies were coded by phase, task, data, technique and type of evidence, and appraised with a short checklist. During construction, deep learning finds surface defects, comparison of scans with building information models measures deviations to within a millimetre, knowledge-based methods automate parts of code checking, and sensing combined with machine learning tracks concrete strength. In operation, machine learning supports damage diagnosis, drones and convolutional networks speed up inspection, and digital twins bring data together for maintenance decisions.
