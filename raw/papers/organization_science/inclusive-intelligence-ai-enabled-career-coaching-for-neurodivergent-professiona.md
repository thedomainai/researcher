# Inclusive Intelligence: Ai-Enabled Career Coaching For Neurodivergent Professionals With Adhd

**著者**: Kaveh Abhari, Reut Segal, Deepika Kanakamedala, Hossein Shirazi, Morteza Safaei Pour
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

This study addresses a core Information Systems design challenge: how to configure AI-enabled support systems for professionals whose cognitive patterns diverge from dominant organizational norms. Using ADHD as a boundary case, it develops and evaluates a design logic for inclusive AI-mediated career coaching. A progressive three-study design moves from problem specification to design articulation to artifact evaluation. Study I identifies ADHD-specific ad hoc support contexts. Study II derives communicative heuristics that make guidance cognitively accessible, actionable, and affectively attuned. Study III instantiates these heuristics in a prompt-conditioned, LLM-based coaching artifact and evaluates it against highly endorsed human responses. This work contributes in two ways. First, it develops design knowledge for configuring inclusive AI support systems by linking support contexts, communicative affordances, and artifact configuration. Second, it shows that AI-generated guidance can approximate perceived helpfulness in task-focused contexts while remaining constrained in emotionally sensitive ones, highlighting cognitive fit, trust calibration, and perceptual alignment.
