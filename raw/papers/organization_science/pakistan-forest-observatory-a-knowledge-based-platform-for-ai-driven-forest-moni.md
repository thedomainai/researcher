# Pakistan Forest Observatory: A Knowledge-Based Platform for AI-Driven Forest Monitoring, Risk Assessment, and Operational Management

**著者**: Muhammad Sohaib Ayub, Sikander Bakhat, Muhammad Awais, Aqeela Mobeen Akhter, Umair ul Hassan
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

Effective forest management requires a knowledge-based approach: the ability to integrate satellite data, sensor readings, and field observations into timely, actionable decisions. In Pakistan, this knowledge infrastructure does not exist. The country loses roughly 27,000 hectares of forest annually to fire, deforestation, and drought, yet forest departments still operate on paper inventories, disconnected spreadsheets, and post-event field reports. This paper presents the Pakistan Forest Observatory (PFO), an end-to-end digital platform integrating satellite remote sensing, ground-based IoT sensors, and machine learning into a unified operational environment. The system delivers three core capabilities: real-time fire risk and drought assessment using NDVI, NDMI, and NASA FIRMS hotspot data; a forest inventory layer driven by a Mask R-CNN microservice that estimates tree diameter at breast height, aboveground biomass, and carbon stock from stereo smartphone images; and an operational planning and field-tracking layer with GPS-bounded task assignment via a Flutter mobile application. A React.js administrative dashboard ties these modules together through a role-based interface designed for non-specialist government forestry staff. Validated across 12 forest plots in the Islamabad and Rawalpindi Forest Divisions, the platform reduces per-tree assessment time from roughly three minutes to under 30 s, achieves NDVI-based drought classification consistent with ground-truth biomass measurements, and had 85% of trial users report higher confidence in their management decisions.
