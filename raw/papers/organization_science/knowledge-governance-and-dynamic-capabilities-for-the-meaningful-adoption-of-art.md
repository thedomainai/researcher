## Abstract

This conference presentation examines how universities in emerging economies can meaningfully adopt artificial intelligence through knowledge governance, dynamic capabilities, and institutional transformation. Using a qualitative, interpretive, phenomenological, and emergent design, the study analyzes faculty perspectives, organizational learning, knowledge transfer, and technological change. Findings emphasize continuous faculty development, ethical and participatory governance, communities of practice, and the integration of tacit and explicit knowledge. The study warns against ceremonial AI adoption and argues that technology alone is insufficient. Meaningful transformation requires universities to develop institutional capacities to learn, adapt, regulate, share knowledge, and align innovation with educational purposes and the common good.
