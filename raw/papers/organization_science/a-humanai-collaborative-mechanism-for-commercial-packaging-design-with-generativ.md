# A Human–AI Collaborative Mechanism for Commercial Packaging Design with Generative AI

**著者**: Zibin Liu
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

This study The rapid adoption of generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) has significantly expanded its role in commercial packaging design, enabling designers to produce visual concepts with unprecedented speed. However, existing studies have primarily emphasized generation efficiency and visual quality, while paying limited attention to the stability of generated results, their commercial applicability, and the evolving relationship between designers and AI throughout the design process. This study investigates the aesthetic generation characteristics and practical limitations of GenAI in commercial packaging design and proposes a designer-centered human–AI collaborative creation framework. A mixed-method approach combining literature review, controlled experiments, and case analysis was employed. Under identical generation conditions—including the same model, prompts, and generation parameters—commercial packaging images were repeatedly generated and comparatively analyzed in terms of visual style, packaging structure, brand consistency, cultural expression, and commercial feasibility. The results indicate that GenAI can efficiently produce visually coherent design concepts while maintaining a consistent overall stylistic direction. Nevertheless, substantial variations remain in packaging structure, typographic legibility, brand identity, material organization, and cultural interpretation due to probabilistic sampling, training data distribution, and model-specific aesthetic preferences. These findings suggest that current GenAI systems are effective for early-stage ideation but remain insufficient for directly producing commercially deployable packaging solutions. Based on these findings, this study proposes a designer-led human–AI collaborative workflow in which designers retain responsibility for brand strategy, cultural interpretation, aesthetic judgment, and final decision-making, while GenAI supports concept exploration, design expansion, and iterative refinement. The proposed framework provides both theoretical insights and practical guidance for integrating generative AI into commercial packaging design while preserving design quality, brand coherence, and commercial viability.
