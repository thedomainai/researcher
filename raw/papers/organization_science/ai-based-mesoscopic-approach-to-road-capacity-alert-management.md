# AI Based Mesoscopic Approach to Road Capacity Alert Management

**著者**: Renáta Bordás
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 組織科学

## Abstract

This research aims to support urban traffic management and planning by identifying interventions for characteristic traffic congestion hotspots using crowdsourced data from the Waze platform. User alerts and delay data of segments are aggregated over defined temporal intervals, serve as the primary input. The analysis employs unsupervised machine learning to group road segments based on congestion patterns. The resulting clusters are interpreted through comparison with visual patterns from a curated traffic issue catalogue. This catalogue contains traffic incidents and typical congestion profiles that are associated with a predefined set of potential interventions, derived from expert knowledge in traffic engineering. By matching clusters to catalogue profiles, context-sensitive intervention strategies can be systematically proposed. Although the catalogue matching process currently relies on expert interpretation, it offers a structured, reproducible alternative to ad hoc hotspot identification. Moreover, the methodology provides a scalable framework for integrating AI-supported clustering with practical traffic engineering solutions. This semi-automated pipeline, from raw crowdsourced data to targeted urban mobility interventions, demonstrates the potential of combining machine learning with qualitative pattern recognition. Future work may involve the development of automated cluster-to-pattern matching models, enabling a fully data-driven decision support system for traffic management and planning.
