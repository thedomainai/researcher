# Teaching Reform of Civil Engineering Construction Course under the Background of AI

**著者**: Huaken Zhang, Xiaoxiao Sun, Qingchi Zhang, Zhe Yang, Qun Liu
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人的資源管理

## Abstract

With the large-scale implementation of generative artificial intelligence, digital twin, BIM and other technologies in the construction industry, the construction industry is transforming from the traditional manual construction mode to the intelligent construction mode, and the demand for industry post ability has changed significantly. As a core compulsory course of civil engineering undergraduate major, civil engineering construction undertakes the task of imparting professional knowledge such as construction technology, construction organization and site management. The traditional classroom teaching mode has practical contradictions such as lack of practical scenes, lagging update of teaching content, insufficient individualized training of students, and single assessment method. The teaching practice shows that the reasonable introduction of AI auxiliary tools can make up for the shortcomings of traditional teaching field conditions, enrich classroom case resources, and strengthen students’ engineering practice thinking. However, it also faces practical challenges such as students’ excessive dependence on AI output content, professional information distortion, and teachers’ teaching ability can not keep up with technical iteration. This study can provide reference for the digital teaching reform of construction courses in similar engineering majors, and help to cultivate compound civil engineering talents with both traditional construction skills and intelligent construction literacy.
