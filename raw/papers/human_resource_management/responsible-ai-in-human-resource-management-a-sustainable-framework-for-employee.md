# Responsible AI in human resource management: a sustainable framework for employee trust and organisational performance

**著者**: Azzam Altawil, Asad Ur Rehman, Ayesha Nawal, Ihtisham Ullah, Waris Ali Khan
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人的資源管理

## Abstract

Purpose This paper examines how responsible artificial intelligence (AI) governance in human resource management (HRM) may support employee trust and sustainable organisational performance. Design/methodology/approach A conceptual model, developed through purposive theoretical synthesis, connects employee-visible governance with justice, focal trust, employee responses and organisational outcomes, while specifying contextual conditions and learning feedback. Findings The framework proposes that employee-visible responsible AI governance informs justice perceptions, which inform trust in organisationally governed AI-enabled HRM decision processes. Trust may support calibrated reliance and constructive responses; individual performance and collective emergence may then contribute to sustainable organisational performance. Socio-technical alignment, contextual conditions and organisational learning delimit this pathway. The six governance dimensions and twelve propositions are conceptual and have not been empirically validated. Practical implications The conceptually derived guidance links accountable decision ownership, meaningful human review, accessible explanation and appeal, and lifecycle learning to employee experience. Originality/value The framework specifies an employment-system unit of analysis and distinguishes technical trustworthiness from employee trust. Its bounded contribution connects employee-visible governance with justice-mediated trust, cross-level emergence and governance reconfiguration.
