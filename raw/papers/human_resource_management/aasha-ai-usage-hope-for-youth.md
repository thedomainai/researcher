## Abstract

Through the diptych artefact “Aasha - AI Usage and Hope in Youth,” we invite public engagement with and reflection on the digital collaboration of youth and artificial intelligence (AI)-based tools for job search. We explore how this usage allows youth and AI to coexist while positively influencing youth’s level of hope. Thus, the artefact directly aligns with the conference theme “Digital Collaboration and Coexistence.” Information Systems research has traditionally examined digital technologies in terms of efficiency, decision quality, adoption, and performance impacts. More specifically, information systems (IS) research has largely examined AI systems through lenses of productivity, decision support, or user acceptance (Venkatesh, 2022; Benbya et al., 2021). More recent work has extended this focus to human-centred outcomes, including trust, well-being, and human–AI collaboration (Berente et al., 2021; Glikson & Woolley, 2020).
