# Designing For Deception: A Design Science Approach To Human-Centered Defences Against Ai-Driven Vishing

**著者**: Michael Lapke, Dr. Anila Mjeda
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人的資源管理

## Abstract

While phishing remains a persistent threat, the emergence of multimodal Large Language Models, such as OpenAI's GPT-5, has fundamentally altered the voice phishing (vishing) landscape.This research explores the arms race between automated AI-driven social engineering and human-centered detection.We have developed web-based autonomous vishing software capable of conducting real-time deceptive conversations.As the technical architecture is complete, the project is currently navigating the ethical review process in preparation for human-subject testing.The core objective is to evaluate how users interact with and potentially detect AI-synthesized voices in a controlled environment.By presenting in the TREO Emerging Ideas track, we seek to engage the IS community on two fronts: (1) refining experimental methodologies for testing live AI deception without compromising ethical boundaries, and (2) feedback on preparing for the next stage in research which will involve a Design Science study with the software artifact within an organization.
