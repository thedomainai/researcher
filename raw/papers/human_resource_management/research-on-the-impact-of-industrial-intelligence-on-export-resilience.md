# Research on the Impact of Industrial Intelligence on Export Resilience

**著者**: Xinyu Huang, Lingzhi Li
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人的資源管理

## Abstract

With the continuous progress of the new round of the technological revolution and the increasingly complex international environment, industrial intelligence and export resilience have become hot topics for scholars at home and abroad. This paper uses provincial panel data from 2011 to 2022 to examine the impact of industrial intelligence on export resilience. The research findings are as follows: First, the development of industrial intelligence has significantly enhanced export resilience. Second, from a regional perspective, the impact of industrial intelligence on export resilience is highest in the central region, followed by the western region, and is not significant in the eastern region. Third, industrial intelligence affects export resilience by promoting technological innovation.
