Detailed Description of the Research Theme The proposed research investigates the rapidly changing relationship between Artificial Intelligence and human employment. The central premise is that AI should not be understood exclusively as a replacement technology. Instead, the emergence of Generative AI creates a new paradigm in which intelligent computational systems can function as digital co-workers, assisting humans in information processing, analysis, content generation, programming, design, research, education, and decision support. The study adopts a task-based perspective because an occupation is normally composed of multiple activities. Some activities may be highly suitable for automation, while others require human creativity, contextual knowledge, ethical judgment, interpersonal interaction, physical presence, or accountability. Therefore, AI exposure should not automatically be interpreted as complete occupational replacement. The research introduces the concept of an AI-Augmented Occupation, in which AI performs or assists selected tasks while human workers retain responsibility for higher-level objectives, validation, interpretation, ethical reasoning, and final decisions. 7.
