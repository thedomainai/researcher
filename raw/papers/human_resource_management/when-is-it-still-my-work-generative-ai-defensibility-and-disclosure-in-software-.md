# When Is It Still My Work? Generative AI, Defensibility, and Disclosure in Software Product Managers’ Work

**著者**: Phong Trang, Nguyen Tran Dieu Hieu, Soumitra Chowdhury
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人的資源管理

## Abstract

As AI assistance becomes part of everyday work, managers increasingly produce roadmaps, decision rationales, and stakeholder messages with generative AI (GenAI). Yet, prior work offers little discussion on how managers keep such AI-assisted work defensible as their own, or about what colleagues see of the AI use behind it. Semi-structured interviews with 21 product managers in an automotive software organisation show a recurring Ask-Tweak-Share routine. Managers request a draft, rework it against their product knowledge, and share it. The reworking settles whether the result can be defended under a colleague's questioning (artefact-level defensibility). The choice of whether to mention the AI, made at sharing, settles how much of that use can be shown to colleagues (disclosure-mediated visibility). Settled at different moments, defensibility and visibility can decouple. Where no formal policy governs AI use, each disclosure choice helps set what a team comes to accept as legitimate practice.
