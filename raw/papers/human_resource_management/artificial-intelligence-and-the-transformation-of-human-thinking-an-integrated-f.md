Central Research Theme The entire paper can be organized around this central idea: “Artificial Intelligence should function as a cognitive amplifier rather than a cognitive replacement.” The transformation can be represented as: Traditional Model Human → Tool → Result AI-Assisted Model Human → AI → Human Evaluation → Revised Thinking → Result Proposed Model Human Problem Definition ↓ Independent Thinking ↓ AI Assistance ↓ Critical Verification ↓ Human Synthesis ↓ Human Judgment ↓ Responsible Outcome 10. Proposed Novel Framework HAICF Human–AI Cognitive Interaction Framework The framework consists of six major stages: Stage 1 — THINK Human independently understands and frames the problem. ↓ Stage 2 — ASK AI is used to generate information, alternatives, explanations, or possibilities. ↓ Stage 3 — ANALYZE Human evaluates AI-generated material. ↓ Stage 4 — CHECK Evidence, logic, calculations, assumptions, and sources are verified. ↓ Stage 5 — REFLECT Human determines what was actually learned. ↓ Stage 6 — DECIDE Final judgment remains with the human. 11. Proposed Core Principle THINK → ASK → CHECK → REFLECT THINK Attempt the problem independently. ASK Use AI to expand or challenge your thinking. CHECK Verify the AI response. REFLECT Understand what the interaction has taught you. This can become one of the paper's major conceptual contributions. 12. Proposed Quantitative Concepts A. Human Cognitive Autonomy Index — HCAI [HCAI=w_1I+w_2V+w_3R+w_4J+w_5L] where: (I) = independent reasoning (V) = verification ability (R) = reflective thinking (J) = independent judgment (L) = learning retention (w_i) = weighting coefficients Interpretation Higher HCAI: Strong human intellectual autonomy Lower HCAI: Potential cognitive dependence This is a conceptual index requiring empirical validation. 13. AI Dependence Index — ADI A complementary concept can be introduced: [ADI=\frac{T_{AI}}{T_{total}}\times100] where: (T_{AI}) = time or cognitive-task proportion delegated to AI (T_{total}) = total task time However: AI usage does not automatically equal AI dependence. The critical factor is whether the human continues to: Understand → Evaluate → Verify → Decide 14.
