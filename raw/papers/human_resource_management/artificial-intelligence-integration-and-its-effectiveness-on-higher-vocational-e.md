# Artificial Intelligence Integration and Its Effectiveness on Higher Vocational Education Management and School–Enterprise Talent Training

**著者**: Jianfeng Zhang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人的資源管理

## Abstract

This study examined artificial intelligence (AI) integration, management effectiveness, and school–enterprise collaborative talent training at Guangzhou Huali Vocational College of Science and Technology, China. A descriptive-correlational quantitative design involved 159 students and 101 teachers during the second semester of academic year 2025–2026. Students were included through total enumeration and teachers through purposive sampling. A researcher-developed questionnaire was validated by five experts and pilot-tested with 30 individuals; Cronbach’s alpha was 0.96. Analysis used frequencies, percentages, weighted means, t tests, one-way analysis of variance, and Pearson correlations. Both groups rated AI integration as high, management effectiveness to a high extent, and training success to a high degree. Students’ assessments differed significantly by enterprise exposure across all three constructs; teachers’ AI integration assessments differed by position. AI integration correlated positively with management effectiveness for students (r = 0.8839) and teachers (r = 0.9225). Management effectiveness correlated positively with training success for students (r = 0.9399) and teachers (r = 0.9586). The findings support coordinated curriculum, assessment, supervision, faculty development, and workplace learning. A Comprehensive AI Integration Framework was proposed to strengthen institutional practices and collaborative training. The relationships indicate association rather than causation.
