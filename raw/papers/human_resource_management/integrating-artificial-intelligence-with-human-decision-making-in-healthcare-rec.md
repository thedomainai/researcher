# Integrating Artificial Intelligence with Human Decision-Making in Healthcare Recruitment: Evidence from Health Care Centre

**著者**: Samreen Monga
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人的資源管理

## Abstract

The rising use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in recruitment has changed the way talent acquisition in healthcare sector used to work, but there are still fears of the automation and the human factor. This study focuses on the use of AI in conjunction with human decision-making in healthcare recruiting, which is supported by evidence of Health Care Centre. The main objective is to determine how AI-human cooperation affects the efficiency, accuracy, and equity of recruitment and to determine the effect of AI-aided recruitment on the quality of the candidates, fit to the job, and organizational performance. The quantitative research methodology was utilized and primary data were used as the source of information as a structured questionnaire was administered to HR professionals and recruitment managers. However, data analysis was done to include descriptive statistics (mean and standard deviation), reliability testing based on Cronbach alpha, hypothesis testing through regression analysis. The findings support the idea that AI-human partnership affects the efficiency of recruitment and the accuracy of decisions in a very positive manner and positively influences the perceived fairness. It also indicates that recruitment with the help of AI drastically enhances the quality of candidates and their job-role fit, which leads to a positive outcome in organizations. The study has concluded that a balanced Human-AI recruitment model improves the quality of decisions and promotes strategic human resources management within health care institutions.
