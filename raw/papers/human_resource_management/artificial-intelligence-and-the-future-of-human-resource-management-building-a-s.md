# Artificial Intelligence and the Future of Human Resource Management: Building a Skilled Workforce for Viksit Bharat @2047

## Abstract

Abstract Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming Human Resource Management (HRM) through AI-enabled recruitment, workforce analytics, employee development, performance management and talent management. In the context of Viksit Bharat @2047, AI can play a significant role in developing a skilled, adaptable and future-ready workforce. This paper examines the role of AI in HRM and its contribution to workforce reskilling, digital literacy, analytical skills and human-AI collaboration. The study adopts a descriptive and analytical research approach based on secondary literature, policy reports and an illustrative quantitative framework. The paper proposes an AI–HRM–Skills–Viksit Bharat framework, highlighting the importance of continuous learning, ethical AI governance and human-centred skills. It concludes that responsible integration of AI with effective reskilling and upskilling strategies can strengthen workforce productivity, employability, innovation and India's journey towards Viksit Bharat @2047.
