# A Human-Centered Framework for AI-Wearable Integration in Automotive Assembly Lines

**著者**: Xing Yao, Yehong Zhou, Qiheng Peh, Yanhao Tan, Xiaoqing Yu
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人的資源管理

## Abstract

Industry 5.0 shifts manufacturing from automation-centred production toward human-centred collaboration between operators and intelligent systems. Although artificial intelligence (AI), augmented-reality (AR) head-mounted displays, sensor-equipped wearables, and digital twins can support assembly quality, safety, training, and process optimisation, these technologies are often developed as isolated applications. This study proposes a process-oriented framework for integrating AI-assisted wearable systems into automotive assembly operations. The framework is developed through a structured synthesis of Industry 5.0, human–AI complementarity, wearable technologies, digital twins, Value Stream Mapping, Design for Assembly, concurrent engineering, and ergonomic system design. Process mapping and task decomposition are used to identify candidate operations characterised by bottlenecks, quality risks, repetitive activity, cognitive complexity, or ergonomic demands, while stakeholder analysis captures requirements from operators, quality, safety, maintenance, supervision, and management. The resulting framework combines a three-layer system architecture, a human–AI service blueprint, and a four-phase implementation roadmap that links wearable sensing and guidance with AI processing and backend systems such as digital twins, Manufacturing Execution Systems, and Quality Management Systems while retaining human decision authority. A structured evaluation plan is further proposed to assess operational performance, AI and sensing accuracy, system responsiveness, human-factors outcomes, and technical feasibility through controlled pilots, comparative testing, robustness analysis, and expert review. The framework provides a practical pathway for future evidence-based deployment of AI-assisted wearables in automotive and other complex manual-assembly environments.
