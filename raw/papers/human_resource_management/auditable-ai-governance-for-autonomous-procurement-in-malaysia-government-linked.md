# Auditable AI Governance for Autonomous Procurement in Malaysia Government-Linked Supply Chains

**著者**: Wai Yie Leong
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人的資源管理

## Abstract

Malaysia&s;s government procurement system is increasingly embracing AI-based automation for supplier vetting, risk assessment, tender evaluation, and payment tracking in government-linked supply chains. However, this also presents substantial governance challenges, including transparency issues in decision-making processes, audit difficulties, bias amplification, and reduced accountability. This paper outlines an Auditable AI Governance framework for the Malaysian government-linked procurement system, ensuring alignment of AI lifecycle risk management with procurement system transparency, data protection, and integrity requirements. The Auditable AI Governance framework is based on structured documentation such as datasheets and model cards, tamper evidence for AI decision logging, accountability via human-in-the-loop principles, and monitoring based on existing standards. The results show substantial improvements in traceability, audit reconstruction time, and governance key performance indicators without compromising AI system performance.
