# Omanization Long-Term Sustainability: Building Internal Technical Experts

**著者**: Ahmed Al Maqbali, Rahma Al Sibani
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人的資源管理

## Abstract

Abstract The Energy sector is one of the most competitive industries in attracting and retaining skilled and professional. Sustaining its technical capabilities is considered a main challenge for companies operating in the field. Organizations in the field rely heavily on its experienced technical experts, whose knowledge and experience has been built over years in the operations. Where the traditional career path usually pushes the technical experts away from the field to a leadership role, resulting in getting limitation into advancing their technical experiences and potentially losing the know-how and getting the knowledge to fade away by time, which also provides further time-away from the field and the chance to share this knowledge, that becomes a key challenge for organizations. This paper presents Oxy Oman's integrated strategy for Omanization Long-Term Sustainability, focused on the capability development of its internal technical experts. Instead of treating Omanization as if it is hiring target, the strategy is designed to be a full journey from hiring to retirement. The strategy incorporated a full talent management framework that develops Omani employees from entry-level positions to experienced technical experts capable of supporting both local operations and international business units. The framework brings together technical career pathways, capability development, structured mentoring, knowledge transfer, trainer certification, cross functional mobility, and technology enabled learning, all under one governance model meant to keep technical excellence steady and to protect organizational knowledge. A key component of the strategy was the introduction of a dedicated technical career pathway under (STEP) program that enables employees to progress as technical experts as an alternative to a supervisory/leadership career. Supporting initiatives include the Technical Trainers Development Program, Accelerate Program for trainers and SMEs, EmPower Mentoring Program, cross-posting opportunities, and the integration of artificial intelligence (AI), Virtual Reality (VR), simulators, and other digital learning technologies (Percipio) into technical development journey. A comprehensive quality assurance framework governs program design, implementation, assessment, and continuous improvement to ensure retaining technical expertise and maintain long-term sustainability. After 10 years of introducing the strategy and proven data, the strategy has clearly proven its practicality, where the number of qualified Omani Technical Trainers has increased from zero (in 2015) to 11 (by the end of 2025), while certifying more than 75 Omani Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) to deliver internal technical training. Currently, a majority of the technical trainings are conducted by Omani SMEs, and the key technical roles across core operational functions (including specialist, advisor, and chief), have reached 100% Omanization. The Omani technical experts now also help in the development of global technical training programs for other Oxy Business Units. P report an average participant satisfaction score of 94% and recorded a 20% year-on-year improvement in instructional design and delivery effectiveness based on internal assessments. This has contributed to retaining technical experts, knowledge, and enhance knowledge sharing transfer within Oxy Oman and other Oxy Business Units while maintains a high employment and career satisfaction.
