# WellBase: The Bedrock for AI-Driven Reservoir Insights Through Integrated Asset Modelling Optimization

**著者**: Sukilesh Anbalagan, Olivier Pierre Pippi
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人的資源管理

## Abstract

Abstract This paper presents WellBase, a centralized digital platform designed for integrated reservoir management. By consolidating simulation data with measured production, injection, and pressure data, the platform improves data quality control, keeps information consistent, and makes cross-functional integration far easier. It also gives simulation and surveillance engineers a common footing to work from, which supports proactive reservoir management and enables systematic reservoir and asset model optimization across producing assets. The platform was validated through a phased pilot deployment on a single reservoir (RESERVOIR-A) within a producing carbonate asset in an Operating Company, targeting automated data ingestion, classification, and quality-control workflows. At its core, the solution harmonizes simulation data with measured production, injection, and pressure datasets through a standardized well master model. Automated reconciliation, multi-scale mapping across well, sector, and reservoir levels, and continuous data refresh are embedded natively rather than added afterwards, which is what keeps asset-scale model calibration and optimization consistent. This robust framework ensures reliable, consistently classified data and establishes a scalable digital foundation for advanced analytics and future AI-driven applications. The pilot demonstrated measurable improvements in data reliability, automation, and reservoir diagnostics. Standardized, automatically quality-controlled well data eliminated inconsistencies between simulation and measured datasets and substantially reduced manual reconciliation efforts. The platform also enabled automated, multi-level analysis and diagnostics, which made three kinds of problems easier to identify early: (1) database anomalies such as abnormal water cut, gas-oil ratio (GOR) deviations, or pressure readings; (2) real data trends and reservoir behaviors; and (3) deviations between simulation model outputs and observed field performance. By delivering consistently classified, high-quality data with embedded domain validation, the platform strengthened confidence in both surveillance and simulation workflows while preserving full asset team oversight. Access to more than five decades of historical well data enabled robust trend-based analysis and supported proactive reservoir management decisions. Taken together, these results confirm that automation and data quality are critical enablers for asset-scale model optimization, enhanced reservoir performance, and scalable AI adoption across producing assets. In short, the platform introduces a fully automated, multi-scale framework for reservoir management and asset modelling optimization, anchored in standardized, accurately classified well data. By embedding continuous data quality control and keeping simulation and surveillance datasets tightly integrated, it establishes a robust foundation for asset-level modelling optimization and scalable analytics, and it directly addresses a critical industry gap in AI-ready upstream data infrastructure.
