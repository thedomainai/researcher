# Digital Transformation of Human Resource Management: A Systematic Literature Review

**著者**: Sravanthi Gali
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人的資源管理

## Abstract

Digital transformation has moved Human Resource Management (HRM) from a largely administrative function toward a technology-enabled, data-informed and increasingly strategic organizational capability. This systematic literature review synthesizes research on how digital technologies reshape HR processes, employee experience, decision-making and the strategic role of HR professionals. The review focuses on e-HRM and HR information systems, cloud platforms, people analytics, artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning, automation, digital learning, employee self-service, remote and hybrid work technologies, and emerging generative-AI applications. Recent literature indicates that digital HRM can improve administrative efficiency, information quality, responsiveness, personalization and evidence-based decision-making. At the same time, benefits are not automatic. Adoption is influenced by organizational resources, user knowledge, perceived usefulness, social influence, digital skills, leadership support and system integration. The literature also identifies important risks involving privacy, cybersecurity, algorithmic bias, explainability, surveillance, employee resistance and unequal digital capability. The review therefore adopts a socio-technical perspective in which successful transformation depends on the alignment of people, processes, technology, governance and organizational capabilities. The paper develops an integrative framework linking digital technologies to transformed HR practices and, through mediating mechanisms such as automation, analytics, employee experience and digital capability, to organizational outcomes. The review concludes that the future of HRM is not simply the replacement of human work with technology; rather, it involves redesigning HR work so that technology handles repetitive and analytical tasks while HR professionals concentrate more strongly on judgment, relationships, ethics, workforce strategy and change leadership.
