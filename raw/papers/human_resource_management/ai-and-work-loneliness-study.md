# AI and Work Loneliness Study

**著者**: Sarah Wright, Constance Hadley
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人的資源管理

## Abstract

This study investigates how the integration of AI into daily workflows influences employees’ social experiences, emotional well-being, and workplace dynamics. As AI becomes increasingly embedded in the modern knowledge economy, it raises important questions about how it changes communication patterns, social connections, and perceptions of managerial support and organizational care. The study is based on a cross-sectional survey administered online to 1,545 full-time U.S.-based knowledge workers aged 25–54. Participants used AI for work at least monthly and worked for an organization (they were not freelancers, gig workers, or entrepreneurs). Data were collected through Dynata’s survey platform. The 10-minute survey includes both standardized and custom-designed measures capturing AI, human, and organizational factors. This study aims to advance academic knowledge about how technology reconfigures workplace dynamics and contribute to the growing literature on the emotional and relational consequences of AI at work. The result will also provide practical recommendations for organizations navigating AI adoption, especially in relation to employee engagement and social well-being. Findings can inform leaders and HR teams on how to implement AI in ways that protect social connection, promote psychological safety, and maintain healthy workplace relationships.
