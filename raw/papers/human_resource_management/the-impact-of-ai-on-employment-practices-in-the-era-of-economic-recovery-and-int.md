# The Impact of AI on Employment Practices in the Era of Economic Recovery and Intelligent Transformation

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

AI is changing the way businesses hire, train, keep an eye on, and manage their workers, which is changing the way people work in many fields. AI-powered solutions make hiring more efficient by using automated screening, predictive hiring, and data-driven talent analytics. AI-driven automation and augmented intelligence are changing job responsibilities in the workplace, leading to the creation of new digital professions and the increase of remote and gig-based work patterns. Also, AI-powered technologies for performance monitoring and personalised skill development are changing the way people manage their workforces. This chapter critically analyses the influence of AI on employment practices, emphasising its revolutionary potential and the necessity for responsible deployment to guarantee equitable, inclusive, and future-oriented work settings
