# A Gated Digital Maturity Framework for Governing AI Adoption in the Built Environment

**著者**: Bhekisipho Twala
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人的資源管理

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence is reshaping project planning, construction monitoring and asset management, yet technical performance is often mistaken for organisational readiness and realised value. This study develops and computationally validates a Digital Maturity Framework (DMF) for governing AI adoption in the built environment. The framework comprises five capability dimensions (data infrastructure, AI capability, automation, governance and ecosystem integration) and five maturity levels, with non-compensatory governance gates that prevent technological strength from masking weaknesses in data, governance and human oversight. Its indicators and level definitions are grounded in a structured synthesis of 50 sources and an audit of seven frequently repeated benefit claims, and the framework is triangulated against four published construction maturity models. Four pre-specified, seeded Monte Carlo experiments tested the classification architecture. The gated rule recovered 86.6% of 10,000 synthetic maturity archetypes (weighted κ = .965; all within one level), assigned no high-technology, low-governance profiles to Level 4 or above, compared with 96.7% under compensatory averaging, and remained stable under threshold, weighting and missingness perturbations. These results demonstrate internal coherence rather than field validity, and the paper sets out a Delphi and multi-organisation survey pathway for empirical validation.
