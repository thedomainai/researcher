# Reframing Human Resource Leadership Through Human-Centred AI

**著者**: Akankshya Panda, Ipseeta Satpathy, Vishal Kumar Jain
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人的資源管理

## Abstract

The integration of artificial intelligence (AI) into Human Resource Management is reshaping organizational practices particularly in the Information Technology sector where employees face high digital intensity and constant technological change. While AI-driven HR systems enhance efficiency and decision-making, they also contribute to technostress, reduced autonomy and psychological strain. Existing research largely overlooks the role of leadership in addressing these challenges. This study proposes the Human-Centred AI Knowledge Resilience Model, integrating Technostress Theory, the Knowledge-Based View and Socio-Technical Systems Theory. Using a qualitative literature-based approach, the chapter examines how human-centred HR leadership can leverage AI and digital knowledge systems to mitigate stress and enhance employee well-being. The findings suggest that responsible AI implementation, supported by ethical leadership, can foster psychological safety, reduce cognitive overload and promote resilience enabling the creation of sustainable and human-centric digital workplaces.
