# Why Do Employees Hide Their AI Use? A Disclosure Threat Model of Shadow AI Use

**著者**: Jinyu LIU, Heshan Sun
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人的資源管理

## Abstract

Shadow AI use has emerged as a defining feature of generative AI, where employees increasingly conceal AI use. As generative AI takes on cognitive work that signals employees’ competence and effort, disclosing AI use has become a distinct interpersonal risk. When employees hide their AI use, organizations lose visibility into where AI adds value and introduces risk. Understanding why employees conceal AI use is therefore important. Grounded in psychological safety theory, our model identifies three individual-level perceived disclosure threats arising from distinct sources: AI competence attribution threat, AI-induced role threat, and AI governance uncertainty threat. These threats reduce psychological safety to disclose AI use and, in turn, drive shadow AI use. We also distinguish routine and cognitive tasks. To test the model, we plan a scenario-based experiment and a two-wave field survey within an organization. Upon completion, we expect the findings to yield significant implications for research and practice.
