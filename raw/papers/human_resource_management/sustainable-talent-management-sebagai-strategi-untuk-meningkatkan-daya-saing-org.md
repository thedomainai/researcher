# Sustainable Talent Management sebagai Strategi untuk Meningkatkan Daya Saing Organisasi di Lingkungan Kerja yang Dinamis: Systematic Literature Review

**著者**: Farah Luthfitah, Sabatini Tisya Devianti, Wasiatur Rizqiyyah, Mochammad Isa Anshori
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: 人的資源管理

## Abstract

The development of digital technology, artificial intelligence, environmental concerns, and changing workforce needs has encouraged organizations to adopt more sustainable approaches to talent management. This study aims to examine the development of sustainable talent management, identify its key practices, and analyze the role of digitalization, artificial intelligence, employee skill development, motivation, and retention in supporting organizational sustainability. This study employs a qualitative approach using a Systematic Literature Review (SLR) based on the PRISMA 2020 guidelines. Relevant studies were identified through systematic searches of selected databases, screened according to predetermined inclusion and exclusion criteria, and analyzed using content analysis and thematic synthesis. The findings indicate that sustainable talent management has evolved from a short-term, performance-oriented approach toward a more strategic and long-term perspective that emphasizes organizational sustainability and employee development. Its key practices include talent attraction and selection, competency development, employee motivation, and retention, supported by digital technologies and artificial intelligence. However, the implementation of these technologies requires continuous employee skill development and a human-centered approach to ensure that technological advancement remains aligned with employee and organizational needs. The study concludes that sustainable talent management integrates technological, human, social, and environmental aspects to support organizational sustainability and long-term organizational resilience.
