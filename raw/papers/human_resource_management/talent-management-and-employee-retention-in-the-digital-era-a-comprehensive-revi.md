# Talent Management and Employee Retention in the Digital Era: A Comprehensive Review

**著者**: Muzaffar Ahmad Dar, Sravanthi Gali, Mohd Iqbal Dar
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: 人的資源管理

## Abstract

In the contemporary volatile business environment, talent management has emerged as a critical strategic imperative for organizational sustainability,particularly in the digital era characterized by remote work, artificial intelligence, and changing employee expectations. The objective of this comprehensive review is to systematically synthesize the extant literature on talent management practices and their influence on employee retention from 2015 to 2026. The study adopts a Systematic Literature Review (SLR) methodology following the PRISMA guidelines. A total of 54 peer-reviewed articles were selected from Scopus, Web of Science, and ABDC-listed journals after rigorous inclusion and exclusion criteria. The data analysis includes descriptive analysis based on year-wise publication trends, geographical distribution, methodological approaches, and theoretical foundations, along with thematic content analysis to identify key determinants. The review identifies five major thematic clusters: (1) Digitalization of talent acquisition and talent analytics, (2) Employee engagement, employer branding, and retention, (3) Learning and development, career growth and upskilling in the digital era, (4) Leadership, organizational culture, and work-life balance, and (5) Artificial Intelligence, remote work, and new challenges in talent retention. Findings reveal that organizations integrating digital tools with human-centric practices such as personalized career paths, flexible work arrangements, and continuous learning opportunities exhibit significantly higher retention rates, especially among millennials and Gen Z. Despite its contributions, the study highlights research gaps including limited empirical work in developing economies, lack of longitudinal studies, and insufficient integration of AI-based retention models. The paper proposes a comprehensive conceptual framework linking digital talent management practices to employee retention and offers a future research agenda. The insights provide valuable implications for HR practitioners, policymakers, and researchers aiming to design effective retention strategies in the digital age.
