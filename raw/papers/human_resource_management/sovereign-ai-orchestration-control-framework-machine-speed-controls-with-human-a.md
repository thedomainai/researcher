# Sovereign AI Orchestration Control Framework: Machine Speed Controls with Human Accountability for Agentic AI

**著者**: Muhammad Amirrul Alhafiz Bin Mohd Zukry
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

The Sovereign AI Orchestration Control Framework (SAOCF) is a proposed implementation-oriented framework for governing the authority delegated to agentic AI systems. It defines an operating architecture through which an accountable organisation can define, enforce, observe and withdraw AI authority across identity, data, tools, execution, evidence and independent containment. SAOCF Version 1.0 introduces a bounded authority envelope, six sovereignty invariants, seven control domains and 35 core controls. A six-dimensional Action Risk Vector informs A0-A5 authority classes, while a nine-step controlled execution loop connects policy, human authorisation, resource enforcement, outcome verification and protected evidence. The framework draws on established AI risk management, zero-trust, identity, incident-response and assurance practices. It is intended for enterprise and public-sector executives, governance leaders, security architects, platform teams and assurance professionals designing bounded agentic-AI deployments. Version 1.0 is a design synthesis and normative control proposal intended for implementation, evaluation and technical review. It does not report deployment experiments or measured risk reduction and does not constitute an industry standard, certification scheme or legal compliance determination.
