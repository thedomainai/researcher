# Artificial intelligence and the future of youth employment in a developing economy: skills mismatch, early-career transitions and labour market vulnerability

**著者**: Ouissal Chichi, Mohamed Mamad
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人的資源管理

## Abstract

Purpose This study aims to examine how artificial intelligence (AI) is reshaping youth employment in Morocco, focusing on labour market vulnerability, skills mismatch and early-career transitions. Design/methodology/approach This study adopts a mixed-methods approach combining a task-based quantitative analysis of secondary labour market data with qualitative evidence from 12 semi-structured expert interviews. Findings The findings indicate that AI adoption in Morocco remains limited and uneven, with applications concentrated mainly on basic functions. The quantitative analysis highlights the structural characteristics of youth employment, while the interviews reveal concerns about skills mismatch, access to early-career opportunities and potential AI-related job displacement. A central finding is the youth paradox: young people are perceived as digitally adaptable but continue to face structural barriers to quality employment and AI-complementary opportunities. The findings also highlight the importance of education and skills development, stronger school-to-work transition mechanisms and institutional coordination in supporting inclusive adaptation to technological change. Originality/value Despite growing concern about AI’s labour-market implications, youth remain underrepresented in the literature, particularly in developing economies. Drawing on the routine-biased technological change and skill-biased technological change perspectives, the study explains how technological change interacts with labour-market structures and institutions to shape youth vulnerability. It further proposes a three-pillar AI adaptation framework centred on education and skills transformation, labour-market transition mechanisms and institutional and ecosystem capacity.
