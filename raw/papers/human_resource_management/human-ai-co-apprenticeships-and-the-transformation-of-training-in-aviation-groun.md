# Human-AI Co-Apprenticeships and the Transformation of Training in Aviation Ground Handling

**著者**: Hayat El Asri, Serafeim Tsiakalos
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Aviation ground handling is a time-critical and safety-sensitive domain in which safe performance depends heavily on tacit knowledge, situated judgement, and apprenticeship-based learning. As airports and airlines deploy artificial intelligence across apron operations, training practices are being reshaped. These technologies can enhance learning and safety, but they also introduce risks of deskilling, over-reliance on automation, and erosion of experiential knowledge. This chapter presents a framework for designing and governing Human-AI co-apprenticeships in aviation ground handling and introduces the Human-AI Co-Apprenticeship Model. The model positions AI as an augmentative pedagogical partner that supports situational awareness, guided reflection, and risk understanding while preserving human oversight and contextual judgement. The chapter maps AI-related risks to training design levers and governance safeguards and illustrates the framework through scenarios. Implications for competency-based training, safety management systems, and regulatory governance are discussed.
