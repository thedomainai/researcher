# Exploring the Role of AI in Hybrid Work Within Software Engineering

**著者**: Fateme Broomandi, Maria Paasivaara
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人的資源管理

## Abstract

Abstract This paper reports insights from the 4th International Workshop on Global and Hybrid Work in Software Engineering (GoHyb 2026), in which workshop participants, including Agile researchers and practitioners, explored positive and negative future scenarios of AI-supported hybrid and remote work. Our analysis of the discussion shows that participants viewed AI as especially consequential for hybrid work because it can support task review, synthesis, summarization, and asynchronous coordination across distributed teams. At the same time, they expressed concerns that increased AI mediation may weaken human communication, reduce critical discussion, and undermine psychological safety and social connection. Overall, the findings suggest that the central challenge is not simply adopting AI tools, but shaping human–AI collaboration in ways that strengthen coordination without endangering the human capabilities on which software teams rely. The paper closes with implications for future research on AI-enabled hybrid work in software engineering.
