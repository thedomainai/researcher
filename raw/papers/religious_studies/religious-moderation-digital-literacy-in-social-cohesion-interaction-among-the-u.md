# Religious Moderation, Digital Literacy in Social Cohesion Interaction among the University Students in Indonesia

**著者**: Abdul Azis Muslimin
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 宗教学

## Abstract

This paper investigates the interplay between digital literacy, religious moderation, and social cohesion among university students in Indonesia, a context marked by increasing digital religious consumption and concerns about polarization. We propose and test a mediation model wherein digital literacy—particularly algorithmic awareness and critical source evaluation—predicts religious moderation, which in turn predicts social cohesion. The study employs a multi-method design integrating a cross-sectional survey of 1,200 students across STEM, social sciences, and Islamic studies, semi-structured interviews with 48 participants, content analysis of 200 student-curated religious items on WhatsApp and Instagram, and simulated platform analysis of TikTok and YouTube recommendation systems over 14 days. Our results demonstrate that the relationship between digital literacy and religious moderation varies significantly across academic disciplines due to distinct epistemic cultures; for instance, STEM students emphasize procedural verification, while Islamic studies students combine textual authority with interpretive tradition. Furthermore, peer-to-peer digital sharing networks function as informal curation systems that can either strengthen or weaken institutional moderation efforts. The evidence supports a mutually reinforcing pathway rather than a unidirectional causal chain: digital literacy enables moderation indirectly through source criticism and resistance to algorithmic reinforcement, while moderation is associated with stronger social cohesion when supported by dialogue and institutional framing. The strongest protective factor across disciplines is the combination of epistemic culture, digital literacy, and institutional support for moderation. This research contributes a multi-level framework that integrates individual competencies with structural factors, offering actionable insights for designing targeted interventions in Indonesian higher education that strengthen both digital competencies and social harmony.
