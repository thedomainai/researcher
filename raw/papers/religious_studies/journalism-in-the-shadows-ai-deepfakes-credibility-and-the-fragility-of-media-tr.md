# Journalism in the Shadows: AI Deepfakes, Credibility, and the Fragility of Media Trust

**著者**: Moses Ubaka Okocha
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 宗教学

## Abstract

In today's world, artificial intelligence (AI) has become a central topic across various disciplines, transforming fields such as journalism and mass communications. This study investigates the experiences of journalists regarding the impact of AI deepfakes on their credibility and the public's trust in their reporting. Using qualitative interviews with 20 Nigerian journalists who have at least two years of professional experience, the study reveals that AI deepfakes pose a significant threat to journalists’ credibility and public trust, exacerbate societal divisions, spread visual misinformation, undermine the broader ecosystem of trust essential to democratic media, and inflict a considerable psychological toll marked by anxiety, fear, and professional vulnerability. Anchored in the framework of trust as infrastructure, the study shows that trust in journalism is not merely an individual attribute but a collective resource sustained through daily practices and institutional norms.
