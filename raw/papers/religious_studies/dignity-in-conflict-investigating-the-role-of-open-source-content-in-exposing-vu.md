# Dignity in Conflict: Investigating the role of open-source content in exposing vulnerable persons to public curiosity during armed conflict

**著者**: Tatjana Grote, Matthew Gillett, Gisela Archibol, Fanny-Anne Champenois, Oliver Goldsmith
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 宗教学

## Abstract

The University of Essex Digital Verification Unit (DVU) has conducted a structured analysis of 77 pieces of open-source content exposing vulnerable individuals to public curiosity during armed conflict. This report provides a detailed analysis of patterns identified both in the conduct depicted in the content as well as in how it is disseminated and reacted to online.
