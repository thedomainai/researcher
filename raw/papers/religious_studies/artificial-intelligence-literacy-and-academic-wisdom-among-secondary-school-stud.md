# Artificial intelligence literacy and academic wisdom among secondary school students: a structural equation modelling study in Indonesia

**著者**: Reva Riani Putri Asyrofi, Imam Suyitno, Gatut Susanto
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 宗教学

## Abstract

The rapid integration of artificial intelligence (AI) in secondary education has created a paradox between high adoption and limited critical-ethical readiness, raising concerns about students’ academic integrity and cognitive autonomy. This study addresses the lack of empirical evidence on how multidimensional AI literacy contributes to the development of academic wisdom. Using a quantitative explanatory design, survey data were collected from 396 secondary school students in Indonesia and analyzed using generalized structured component analysis-structural equation modeling. AI literacy was operationalized into three dimensions: technical, critical, and ethical literacy. The results reveal that all dimensions significantly influence academic wisdom, with ethical literacy emerging as the strongest predictor (β=0.354), followed by critical literacy (β=0.322) and technical literacy (β=0.170). The model explains 43.9% of the variance and demonstrates good fit indices (goodness of fit index=0.986; Std. root mean square residual=0.041). These findings indicate that academic wisdom is primarily shaped by students’ ability to critically evaluate and ethically regulate AI use rather than by technical competence alone. The study extends the data information knowledge wisdom framework by positioning ethical literacy as a key mechanism transforming knowledge into wisdom and offers implications for curriculum design emphasizing ethical and critical competencies in AI-integrated learning.
