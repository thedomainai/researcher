# AI-Based Learning in Islamic Religious Education: Ethics, Effectiveness, and Challenges Among Islamic Religious Education Students

**著者**: Muhammad Nasyikhul Ulul, Muhlisin Muhlisin, Rahmi Anekasari Rahmi
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 宗教学

## Abstract

The development of Artificial Intelligence (AI) has significantly impacted Islamic Religious Education (PAI) learning. This study analyzes AI implementation, the ethical aspects of its use, and the challenges PAI students face. The study used a descriptive qualitative approach, collecting data through semi-structured interviews and open-ended questionnaires and analyzing it using triangulation techniques. The findings indicate that AI helps students understand PAI material more effectively by simplifying complex concepts, providing interactive explanations and relevant examples, and offering rapid feedback tailored to learning needs. AI also improves access to Islamic information, supports independent learning, and helps complete academic assignments. However, its use requires verifying information accuracy, upholding academic integrity, protecting personal data, and aligning with Islamic values. Challenges identified include limited access to technology, low digital literacy, concerns regarding the validity of religious information, potential over-reliance on AI, and reduced lecturer-student interaction. The study is limited by its qualitative approach and limited informant pool, so the results cannot be broadly generalized. Future research should use quantitative or mixed-methods approaches with a more diverse range of respondents to empirically measure AI's effectiveness on PAI students' understanding, learning outcomes, and critical thinking skills. Thus, AI can enhance the quality of PAI learning if used critically, ethically, and responsibly.
