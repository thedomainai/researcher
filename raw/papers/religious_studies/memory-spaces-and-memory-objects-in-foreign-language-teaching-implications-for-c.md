# Memory Spaces and Memory Objects in Foreign Language Teaching: Implications for Cultural Intelligence

**著者**: Gonca KİŞMİR, Nurda Karadeniz Kayali
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 宗教学

## Abstract

This study explores the role of memory spaces and objects in the development of Cultural Intelligence (CQ) within foreign language learning. Memory, as a process that preserves and transforms experience, provides a link between remembering and forgetting and creates a meaningful context for the integration of language and culture. In this action research, 18 B1 level English learners at Gazi University participated in classroom tasks incorporating personal and collective memory objects, supported by the Skillful (Macmillan) Listening & Speaking coursebook. Data were collected through pre- and post-questionnaires and learner feedback forms and analyzed using quantitative and thematic methods. The findings indicated that the intervention did not result in statistically significant improvements in students’ Cultural Intelligence scores. However, descriptive results and qualitative feedback suggested increased awareness and engagement with cultural perspectives. The findings suggest that memory-based activities can help students reflect on cultural issues and become more aware of cultural differences. Further studies involving larger groups of participants and longer intervention periods are needed to evaluate their influence on Cultural Intelligence.
