# Forming discernment in the age of algorithms: artificial intelligence, moral authority, and pedagogy in religious education

**著者**: Ivan Efreaim A Gozum
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 宗教学

## Abstract

Religious Education (RE), particularly within the Catholic tradition, is fundamentally concerned with the formation of hearts and minds so that learners develop the capacity for moral reasoning, discernment, and faithful participation in the common good. As artificial intelligence (AI) becomes increasingly embedded in educational environments, RE faces a pressing pedagogical challenge: how to harness AI as a learning resource without allowing algorithmic systems to become formative authorities that shape students’ moral imagination, values, and religious understanding. This article offers a critical, library-based exploration of AI’s growing influence on religious learning, focusing on its implications for moral formation, epistemic trust, and discernment. Based on Antiqua et Nova and Magnifica Humanitas, the article argues that AI is not a value-neutral technology but a cultural force that influences how learners perceive truth, exercise judgment, and cultivate virtues. While AI can enhance access to religious resources, personalise learning, and enrich classroom engagement, its outputs also risk encouraging passive acceptance of algorithmically generated interpretations and diminishing the dialogical and relational dimensions essential to Catholic RE. In response, the article proposes a pedagogical framework of critical digital discernment that integrates theological reflection, ethical evaluation, and dialogical pedagogy to ensure that AI serves, rather than supplants, the formative mission of RE. By reasserting the primacy of human formation over technological efficiency, the study contributes to contemporary discussions on values-based education and offers practical guidance for Catholic educators seeking to cultivate students who engage AI critically, responsibly, and in light of the Gospel.
