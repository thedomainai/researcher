# Artificial Intelligence and Automated Decision-Making under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023: Evaluating the Adequacy of Nigeria’s Data Protection Framework

**著者**: G. Esq
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 宗教学

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence (AI) has transformed the collection, processing, and use of personal data across Nigeria’s public and private sectors, and AI-driven automated decisions now shape outcomes in employment, credit, insurance, healthcare, and other domains. The Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 introduced a comprehensive framework for personal data processing, including a right in section 37 against decisions based solely on automated processing. This paper asks a narrower and more precise question than is often posed in the existing literature: not whether the NDPA regulates automated decision-making, but whether the operative detail of its provisions — as supplemented by the General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID) 2025 — gives data subjects protection that is comparable, in specificity and enforceability, to that available under the European Union’s General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Adopting a doctrinal and comparative legal research methodology, the paper argues that the NDPA and GAID already recognise the principal safeguards relevant to AI, including transparency, fairness, and a right to human intervention, but leave the content of these safeguards substantially underspecified. The paper concludes that the most immediate reform need is not the invention of new rights but detailed regulatory guidance operationalising the rights that already exist, alongside more modest legislative and institutional reforms addressing algorithmic impact assessment and the independence of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission
