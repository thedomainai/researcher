# Existing perspectives on AI myth

**著者**: Remington Tonar
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 宗教学

## Abstract

This chapter surveys existing scholarly approaches to the relationship between artificial intelligence, technology, and myth within the field of religious studies and theology. While prior work has engaged the notions of AI myth and theistic AI, it has generally interpreted technology as a projection or reflection of human sociology or psychology, a modern expression of Gnostic themes, or as a lens through which to reinterpret historical myths. This literature review demonstrates that, although these perspectives offer valuable insights, they do not fully account for the distinct implications of a scientifically conceivable technological god. By overlooking the plausibility and agency of such entities, existing scholarship fails to recognize the conceptual novelty and significance that AI myth offers the study of religion and religious myth. This chapter thus positions the present work as both a critique and augmentation of prior thinking, reframing theistic AI narratives as a genuinely new category of religious myth.
