# Moral cognition and ethical perceptions in business: psychological insights from Gilgit-Baltistan’s cultural context

**著者**: Talib Hussain, Tong Zelin, Seung Won Lee, Manzar Rehman
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 宗教学

## Abstract

Abstract: This study examines the influence of ethical company practices, sustainability, and community involvement on consumer perceptions and the cultivation of trust. As demand for openness and accountability rises, consumers anticipate that firms will function in accordance with their values, emphasizing honesty, fairness, and ethical behavior. The study conducts a thorough qualitative analysis of consumer responses, identifying essential thematic areas such as trust and transparency, ethical practices, community engagement, sustainability, cultural values, and employee welfare, which are crucial for fostering enduring relationships between businesses and their stakeholders. The results underscore the importance of cultural respect, environmental stewardship, and customer-focused tactics in improving firm reputation and consumer loyalty. The study shows that businesses adhering to these ethical values not only attract and keep loyal customers but also enhance societal wellbeing. The report presents actionable advice for organizations to incorporate ethical principles into their fundamental strategies, including transparency, equity, and sustainability. This research enhances the existing literature on corporate ethics, offering significant insights for enterprises seeking success in a socially aware market.
