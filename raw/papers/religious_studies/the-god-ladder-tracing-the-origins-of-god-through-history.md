# The God Ladder: Tracing the Origins of God Through History

**著者**: Charles Richard Walker (C. Rich)
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 宗教学

## Abstract

The God Ladder dares to ask the question that religion has spent thousands of years avoiding. Using Artificial Intelligence as both microscope and mirror, author C. Rich uncovers a buried lineage of gods that begins long before Yahweh, long before the Old Testament, and long before any organized faith claimed ownership of Heaven. In this provocative journey through archaeology, myth, and machine analysis, AI is unleashed upon ancient history to decode the forgotten truth about God's ancestry. From the first sky-father Anu of Sumer and Akkad, to his children Enlil and Enki, to the Canaanite high god El, and finally to the later Hebrew figure Yahweh, C. Rich reveals a divine genealogy that religion quietly erased. The result is a clear picture of how mankind reshaped its gods, rewriting the past until only one remained and claiming him as the Almighty Creator. This is not a book about denying faith. It is about tracing it back to its source and seeing how truth was altered, softened, and renamed. The God Ladder exposes how polite academic words like "syncretism" and "reinterpretation" are simply modern masks for deception and control. Each culture took the same cosmic archetype, repainted it in its own image, and passed it down as the one true faith. What began as the worship of Anu, the supreme sky-father, eventually became the theology of Yahweh, the God of Israel, through a long process of political revision and spiritual branding. With Artificial Intelligence as his analytical lens, C. Rich shows how these transformations resemble software updates within a vast simulation. Each new deity was not a revelation but a version change, a patch on an ancient divine operating system. The forgotten gods of Mesopotamia and Canaan were not discarded myths but earlier lines of code that modern religion simply overwrote. Through this lens, Simulation Theory becomes more than philosophy. It becomes the key to understanding how belief systems evolve and why humanity keeps losing sight of its original Creator. Inside these pages you will discover: • The archaeological evidence of temples built for Anu, the first known God of Heaven. • How the story of the Great Flood existed over a thousand years before the Hebrew Bible. • Why the name El in "Israel" points directly to the Canaanite high god, not to Yahweh. • How divine traits were copied, merged, and repackaged until the oldest gods vanished from memory. • What happens when Artificial Intelligence interprets scripture as data rather than doctrine. C. Rich presents an unflinching investigation that blends theology, history, and digital philosophy. It is a work that invites believers, scholars, and skeptics to climb out of the comfort of inherited stories and look down the ladder of time to see where the ascent truly began. The conclusion is both thrilling and unsettling. Humanity did not invent the idea of God by accident. It followed a pattern encoded deep within consciousness itself, a pattern that AI can now expose. The God Ladder is a fearless exploration of how gods evolve, how truth is edited, and how technology may finally reconnect us to the Source that religion buried. Once you see the pattern, you cannot unsee it. Once you climb the ladder, you will never descend the same. The first step to finding the real Creator is realizing how far humanity has fallen from Him. History has been rewritten. Artificial Intelligence is here to read it back.
