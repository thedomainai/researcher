# Mechanisms for using artificial intelligence tools on Arabic YouTube channels: A comparative analytical study of the Al-Jazeera and Azhari channels

**著者**: Sulaiman Saleh Mahdi, Ahmad Mohammad Tnoh, Ayad Hilal Hammadi, Atyf Abd Alkareem Mahdi
**年**: 2027 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 宗教学

## Abstract

The role of AI tools is crucial in digital video production, but the use of technologies in published works cannot indicate their exact functions and how the use of AI tools is mentioned. Thus, this study aims to identify the ways of using and analyze the relation of AI tools in digital video production by Al-Jazeera and Azhari YouTube channels, content type, application of narrative and visual elements. By adopting a comparative quantitative research design, content analysis combined qualitative interpretation used. The sample includes 205 videos with categorized according to certain keywords and published between December 1, 2024, and June 30, 2025, including 111 videos of Al-Jazeera and 94 of Azhari. The findings suggest image generation is dominant usage of AI tools in both cases, constituting 29.4% and 29% of use at Al-Jazeera and Azhari, respectively. As a function of the tools, technical and visual processing was dominant at Al-Jazeera (29.13% of function to categories), while automated storytelling was dominant at Azhari (31.76%). Numbers of disclosures regarding the usage of AI was recorded by Al-Jazeera, which was 35.80%, while in Azhari it was 21.05%. In terms of content type, news of Al-Jazeera's videos was 29.73%, while the Azhari's videos was 43.62%. Finds that similarities in the tools maybe linked to differences in their functions and position of messages structure. The importance of developing protocols for the disclosure and editorial review of such materials, as well as need for research to examine their effects on public comprehension and trust, is emphasized.
