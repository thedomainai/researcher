# Unified Theistic Naturalism™

**著者**: Patrick Carodine, Patrick Carodine, Unified Theistic Naturalism, UTN
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Opening The Checkpoint There are moments in the development of an idea when the individual writings cease to appear as separate works. A paper becomes a doorway into another paper. A book gathers several discoveries that were first encountered independently. A podcast gives voice to a structure that had already been written, tested, observed, and carried through life. Concepts that once appeared local begin connecting across relationships, theology, philosophy, memory, suffering, biology, justice, artificial intelligence, and the ordinary decisions of a human day. Eventually, the shelf changes. What first looked like a collection begins to reveal an architecture. Unified Theistic Naturalism™ has reached such a moment. By this checkpoint, the work has expanded into approximately fifteen books, more than sixty white papers, and more than twenty podcast works. Yet those numbers alone say almost nothing about what has occurred. The significance is not volume. It is convergence. The white papers have served as the primary ground of discovery. They are where observations were isolated, questioned, named, tested against lived experience, and given increasingly precise language. The books have allowed those discoveries to breathe across larger landscapes—to enter love, death, prayer, suffering, philosophy, justice, scripture, identity, sexuality, memory, civilization, and the human search for meaning. The podcasts have given the work another kind of body: cadence, silence, emphasis, story, tension, and spoken encounter. They are not three separate projects. They are three expressions of the same unfolding inquiry. At the center of them is a proposition so simple that the expanding corpus has never managed to escape it: The Line does not move. Everything else is movement. In Principia Communio, this foundational architecture is stated directly. Oscillation is movement away from the Unwavering Line of the Divine. Communion is resonance with that Line. The Line is expressed through Seven Attributes: Freedom, Unconditional Love, Compassion, Stillness, Truthfulness, Non-duality, and Wholeness. The Four Pillars—Truth, Observation, Perfect Action, and Movement—describe the living structures of communion, while the Five Gold Coins of the Captive name recurring human captivities: suffering, death, identity, injustice, and distorted love. That architecture became a reference point from which an extraordinary number of apparently unrelated human questions could be examined. Why does love become possession? Why can two people experience the same event and inhabit entirely different realities? Why does prayer so often become negotiation? Why does suffering continue after an explanation has been accepted? Why can a religion preach peace while preserving fear? Why can philosophy clarify identity without ending loneliness? Why can a political victory fail to free the victor from the enemy still living inside the mind? Why can generations repeat emotional and behavioral patterns no one consciously chose? Why can the future feel present even though it has not happened? Why does memory sometimes educate the present and sometimes occupy it? Why can an algorithm predict a person's next movement while remaining unable to occupy the place from which conscious participation begins? Why can a human being know everything about yesterday and still fail to see what is happening now? These questions began appearing across the work, but they did not require a new foundational principle each time. They kept returning to the same one. The Line did not move. The subject changed. The movement changed. The vocabulary became more precise. The field widened. But the reference remained. That continuity matters because UTN did not begin as an academic attempt to construct a comprehensive theory. It began in lived experience—in communion, observation, relationship, suffering, silence, and the recognition that the Divine did not appear as distant as inherited explanations had suggested. Before there was language such as Oscillatory Velocity, there was stillness. Before there was Triangulation, there was observation of repetition. Before there was Somogenic Transmission™, there was the recognition that yesterday continued appearing through bodies, families, relationships, reactions, and inherited patterns. Before there was Homo Praesens, there was the human possibility of seeing again. Before Communiphysis was named as a field, participation was already occurring. The language arrived after the encounter. That distinction is essential to understanding this corpus. Unified Theistic Naturalism is not held together because each later work has been retrofitted to protect the earlier one. The later terminology has instead given increasing resolution to observations that were already present in simpler form. The vocabulary became more exact as the field of observation widened. This is especially visible in Principia Communio. The work formalizes what had become the central grammar of UTN: communion requires no effort, practice, or prayer; Perfect Action arises naturally in communion; oscillation becomes visible because the Line remains constant; the Four Pillars are not behavioral techniques but what remain when interference ceases. Later works did not replace that grammar. They carried it elsewhere. The Speed of Presence carried it into physiology and internal acceleration. It described presence not as mystical achievement but through the relationship between stimulus, reactivity, regulatory movement, residue, and stabilization. Matter moves. Physiology fluctuates. Environments shift. Yet the work argues that presence itself need not accelerate. Paradise is therefore not the elimination of motion but non-reactive alignment within motion. Triangulation carried the architecture into conditioned trajectory. Interpretation, Continuity, and Projection became three observable coordinates through which movement could be recognized before it completed itself. The future was not treated as destiny. Rather, conditioned continuation became visible enough to reveal direction—and communion became the interruption that prevents a recognized trajectory from being mistaken for fate. Homo Praesens carried the work into memory, civilization, culture, science, inheritance, and the freedom to observe again. Memory remained valuable—indispensable, even—but it was no longer granted sovereignty over the present. The past could remain a library without becoming the steering wheel. Observation precedes explanation. Reality remains larger than the inherited description of it. Dear Plato, After Twenty-Four Centuries, the Conversation Is Not Over carried UTN into sustained conversation with one of Western philosophy's foundational inquiries. Justice, political degeneration, appetite, reason, education, power, appearance, wisdom, and the Good were no longer examined as isolated historical questions. They were placed beside the developed UTN architecture of conditioned movement, communion, Perfect Action, Somogenic Transmission, Triangulation, and participation. The monograph itself identifies Principia Communio, Communiphysis, Triangulation, Three-Fifths of Justice, Time Travel, and related works as part of the intellectual foundation beneath that conversation. Communiphysis then widened the frame again. UTN was no longer required to occupy the entire intellectual field. Communiphysis was proposed as the interdisciplinary study of participation in reality itself. Its starting point is strikingly simple: before a person becomes religious or irreligious, philosophical or anti-philosophical, scientific or skeptical, traditional or revolutionary, the person is already participating. Participation precedes explanation. Every perception, silence, conflict, relationship, discovery, fear, act of compassion, and movement of attention is participation unfolding. This gave UTN a new location. UTN became the first comprehensive framework formally proposed within Communiphysis rather than the total boundary of inquiry. That distinction is enormous. It means the question can remain larger than the framework attempting to answer it. Physics may examine matter and energy. Biology may examine living systems. Psychology may examine mind and behavior. Theology may examine the Divine. Philosophy may examine knowledge, meaning, and ethics. Communiphysis asks something that passes through all of them: How does a conscious being participate in reality? And UTN answers that question through its own architecture of communion and oscillation. The result is a corpus that can now be viewed from two directions at once. From within UTN, the Unwavering Line supplies the reference. From within Communiphysis, participation supplies the field. One asks whether movement remains in resonance with the Line. The other asks what participation itself is doing. Together, they make visible a larger map. That map now reaches into artificial intelligence as well. In the most recent work, the question of participation has moved beyond the human mind's relationship with memory and projection and entered a world increasingly shaped by systems capable of learning astonishing amounts about observable behavior. An algorithm can record movement. It can compare movement. It can detect recurrence. It can infer likelihood from enormous stores of prior behavior. It can chart a person through clicks, pauses, purchases, searches, locations, relationships, language, rhythms, and choices. It can become extraordinarily good at the geometry of what has already moved. But this produces a new question: Can the chart occupy the participant? The recent AI work extends the logic of Triangulation into a technological environment.
