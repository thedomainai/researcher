# POST-SECULAR CIVIL RELIGION: A Theological Investigation

**著者**: Allan M. Savage
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 宗教学

## Abstract

Civil religion appears differently to sociologists, anthropologists and phenomenological philosophers. It looks different in that it possesses no creed since it is highly individualized and focuses on an individual experience which is at the centre of a person's spiritual life. The experience of universal community in civil religion is not of primary significance. It focuses on an explanation of inherited concepts and theories and on relationships which emphasize real-world encounters as opposed to theoretical beliefs. Civil religion does not tell the faithful what to believe. Civil religion does not omit transcendence but relocates it as a characteristic of this world, that is, transcendence becomes immanence. Unlike most traditional religions, the origins of civil religion are not geographically confined. It is becoming increasing clear that in the 21st century and beyond, use of the Internet and Artificial Intelligence generate developments not native to the traditional religions in whose cultures they were born.
