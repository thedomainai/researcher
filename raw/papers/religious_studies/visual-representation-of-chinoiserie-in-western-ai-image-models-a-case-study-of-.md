# Visual Representation of "Chinoiserie" in Western AI Image Models: A Case Study of Midjourney

**著者**: Shurong Wang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 宗教学

## Abstract

Using visual grammar as its theoretical framework, this study adopts a comparative experimental approach to perform a multimodal discourse analysis on 240 "Chinoiserie" (Chinese style) images generated by Midjourney. The analysis evaluates baseline and professionally constrained prompt groups across three dimensions: architectural space, costumed portraiture, and landscape atmosphere. Results show that in representational meaning, the model exhibits attribute ambiguity, structural dislocation, and cultural semantic rupture, achieving high adaptability only with abstract landscape styles. In interactive meaning, it demonstrates a spectacularized and Othered perspective, constructing a Eurocentric narrative through low-angle shots and demand-gaze interaction. In compositional meaning, stereotypical symbols occupy an exceptionally high proportion; professional constraints paradoxically exacerbate symbol stacking, and the aesthetic of negative space (Liu Bai) is entirely erased during entity generation. The study indicates that the "Chinoiserie" generated by Western AI is not an authentic cultural representation but a de-historicized digital simulacrum marked by significant cultural bias and aesthetic homogenization. Finally, governance recommendations regarding dataset construction and prompt optimization are proposed to serve as empirical references for the cross-cultural communication of Chinese culture and algorithmic bias correction in the AI context.
