# Psychic Algorithms: Digitalization of the Rituals of Coffee Divination

**著者**: Dicle Berfin Köse
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 宗教学

## Abstract

This study explores the digitalization of coffee divination, also known as tasseography, an intangible cultural heritage recognized by UNESCO. As cultural practices become digitally mediated, questions arise regarding their authenticity, symbolism, and social value. Therefore, drawing on the experiential computing perspective, this study examines users’ experiences with AI-based mobile coffee divination apps compared with in-person coffee divination, focusing on perceived differences, effects, benefits, and concerns. To this end, we analyzed two data sources: trace data from public discussion forums and interviews with 30 participants. Preliminary findings indicate that digital coffee divination transforms the practice along four dimensions—space, time, actors, and artifacts—while providing many of the same benefits as in-person coffee divination. However, users emphasize the importance of physical privacy and physical materiality for continued engagement with these apps. These initial findings extend experiential computing by introducing boundary conditions for digital transformation at the service layer.
