# Technology Meets Theology: Understanding AI-Driven Wellness in Muslim Prayer Apps

**著者**: Ahmed Alrawi, Mustafa Öz
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 宗教学

## Abstract

Abstract This study examines the experiences and perceptions of Muslim users regarding the integration of artificial intelligence (AI) in prayer apps and its impact on their religious and online communication activities. Through in-depth online interviews with a diverse group of participants, this study reveals that AI-driven prayer apps foster a sense of virtual community, bridging geographic distances, and enabling meaningful interactions among Muslims. AI tools like chatbots and reminders support emotional well-being by offering comfort, guidance, and companionship. Further, these tools increase religious awareness and empower users to customize their spiritual practices and access educational resources independently. However, perceptions of AI vary, with trust influenced by transparency, privacy concerns, and past experiences. This study emphasizes the importance of transparent and reliable AI tools to build user confidence. Lastly, the findings contribute to theories on digital religion and wellness, offering practical insights for developers, users, and religious organizations.
