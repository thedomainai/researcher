# Reconstructing Christian Religious Education through Digital Ethics: Forming Generation Z's Religious Identity and Moral Responsibility

**著者**: Liston Sihombing, Andrea Elfata Ratulangi
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 宗教学

## Abstract

Generation Z grows up in a digital ecosystem that shapes how young people understand themselves, relate to others, and practise their faith. Christian Religious Education has often responded to this condition mainly at the level of media, tools, and teacher competence rather than identity and moral formation. This study aims to reconstruct this media-oriented approach into a digital-ethics-based framework for forming the religious identity and moral responsibility of Generation Z. It is a conceptual study based on an integrative literature review of 22 peer-reviewed articles published between 2021 and 2026, combined with theological reflection. The analysis indicates that existing studies address digital literacy, Christian ethics, Generation Z spirituality, and digital discipleship largely in separate clusters, while moral responsibility is rarely theorised as a distinct educational outcome. The study proposes a theological foundation for digital ethics in the image of God, the incarnation, the love commandment, and the glory of God. On this basis, it proposes a conceptual model that integrates three formative axes, namely faith formation, religious identity, and moral responsibility for the self, others, and truth, through four pedagogical movements adapted from shared Christian praxis: discerning, grounding, embodying, and witnessing, supported by the collaboration of school, family, and church. The model contributes a theoretical framework that treats digital space as a formative environment and offers direction for curriculum design, teacher practice, and further empirical testing.
