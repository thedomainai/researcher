# Governance Maturity Mapping of 131 Agentic AI Incidents

**著者**: Shivaraman Parthasarathy
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 情報システム

## Abstract

A coded dataset mapping 131 documented agentic AI incidents from the AI Incident Database (AIID) to the six capability dimensions of the enterprise agentic-AI governance maturity model introduced in S. Parthasarathy, "Toward a Governance Maturity Model for Enterprise Agentic AI Systems," Proc. IEEE UEMCON, 2026. Each incident carries a primary (and optional secondary) governance-capability gap code (D1–D6), or an explicit NONE / UNCLEAR residual, with a confidence grade, a one-sentence evidence note tied to the documented record, and a re-review flag. Headline distribution (v1.0): D1=5, D2=23, D3=6, D4=23, D5=19, D6=4, NONE=24, UNCLEAR=27. Of 104 assessable incidents, 80 (76.9%) map to a governance capability gap; D2+D4+D5 jointly account for 81.2% of gap-coded incidents (81.7% under medium/high-confidence codes only). Contents: coded dataset (CSV + workbook), full codebook with decision rules R1–R13a, framework-level traceability table for the elaborated D6 criteria, and reproducibility scripts that regenerate every reported statistic and figure from the CSV. Incident records and OWASP/NIST annotations are frozen from the prior corpus release (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21542027); the five governance-coding columns are new. Method: AI-proposed, author-adjudicated coding with a full verification pass — see CODEBOOK.md. Released under CC BY 4.0.
