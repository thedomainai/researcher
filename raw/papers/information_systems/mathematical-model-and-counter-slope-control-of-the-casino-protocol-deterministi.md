# Mathematical Model and Counter-Slope Control of the Casino Protocol: Deterministic Governance and Early Warning Thresholds for Upper-Tier Governance AI

**著者**: Hideo Kajino
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 情報システム

## Abstract

Abstract This paper presents a rigorous mathematical and control-theoretic framework for the "Casino Protocol," a deterministic governance model designed to address structural imbalances between cryptocurrency node power consumption and regional feedback. By utilizing a foundational quadratic penalty function (y = x^2 structure), we establish an exact analytical break-even point (E_D^* \approx 3.8616 years) that delineates sustainable operations from exploitative states. Furthermore, to overcome the limitations of reactive evaluations, we formulate a piecewise derivative-based counter-slope control mechanism that introduces an early warning threshold (E_{D,\text{counter}}^* = 2.5000 years). This dual-layer approach separates formal break-even judgments from dynamic degradation alerts. Parameter-based scenario simulations across diverse regional settings demonstrate the effectiveness of this deterministic governance model as an upper-tier normative layer (Governance AI) free from probabilistic volatility and political interference.
