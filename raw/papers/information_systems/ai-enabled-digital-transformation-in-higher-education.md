# AI-Enabled Digital Transformation in Higher Education

**著者**: Merve Aksu
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 情報システム

## Abstract

This chapter examines how artificial intelligence is reshaping digital transformation in higher education, with particular attention to workforce roles, skills development, and institutional governance. It argues that AI adoption in universities should not be understood only as a technological upgrade, but as a broader organizational change process affecting academic work, administrative services, decision-making, and human–machine collaboration. The chapter first discusses the conceptual background of digital transformation, AI literacy, and institutional readiness. It then analyzes the changing responsibilities of academic and administrative staff and compares policy and implementation approaches in Türkiye, Finland, Canada, and Australia. Finally, it considers emerging skill gaps, reskilling needs, and the importance of ethical and human-centered AI governance. The chapter offers practical recommendations for university leaders, policymakers, and researchers seeking to manage AI transformation in a sustainable and responsible way.
