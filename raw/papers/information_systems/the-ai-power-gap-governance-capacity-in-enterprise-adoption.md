# The AI Power Gap: Governance Capacity in Enterprise Adoption

**著者**: Alexis S. Smith
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 情報システム

## Abstract

Organizations have substantially increased Artificial Intelligence (AI) investment without a corresponding increase in governance capacity. This paper argues that the resulting condition, which the author terms The AI Power Gap™, is not primarily a funding problem, a talent problem, or a technology problem. It is a capacity problem: the measurable distance between what an organization has purchased and what it can govern. The paper identifies 3 structural mechanisms that permit accountability to fail inside well-funded AI programs: the treatment of policy as a documentary artifact rather than as an operating control, the unexamined threshold between defined governance and operational governance, and the velocity differential between the rate of AI adoption and the rate at which oversight expands to cover it. It then presents the Ascend AI NOW Framework for Responsible AI™, a 5-phase maturity model (Diagnose, Govern, Architect, Deploy, Scale), as a corrective structure, and describes the AI Power Gap™ Readiness Index, a 50-control instrument cross-walked to NIST AI RMF 1.0, ISO/IEC 42001:2023, the EU AI Act, and SOC 2, as its measurement layer. An illustrative application of the instrument to publicly available federal documentation demonstrates a recurring signature in which early-phase maturity substantially exceeds late-phase maturity. The paper closes with implications for boards, executives, and resource-constrained institutions, and states explicitly what the framework does and does not claim. Working paper. Not peer reviewed.
