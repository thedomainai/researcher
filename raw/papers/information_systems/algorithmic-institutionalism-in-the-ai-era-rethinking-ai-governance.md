# Algorithmic Institutionalism in the AI Era: Rethinking AI Governance

**著者**: Virgílio Almeida
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 情報システム

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence is transforming not only technology but also the institutions that shape social, economic, and political life. This talk introduces the concept of Algorithmic Institutionalism, which views algorithms and AI systems as emerging social institutions that influence human behavior, allocate power, and govern public life, often without the democratic legitimacy and accountability expected of traditional institutions. The presentation argues that the rise of autonomous AI agents marks a new stage in this transformation. Rather than simply automating human tasks, AI agents increasingly act as institutional actors that make decisions, coordinate actions, and reshape governance processes. As these systems become embedded in governments, markets, and organizations, they create a new layer of algorithmic governance that challenges established principles of transparency, accountability, participation, and democratic legitimacy. The talk concludes by discussing the governance frameworks and institutional innovations needed to ensure that AI strengthens democratic values and human agency rather than undermining them. The central argument is that the future of AI depends not only on building more capable technologies, but also on building the institutions required to govern them responsibly.
