# Casino Protocol 23: A Deterministic Governance Implementation Model for Autonomous AI Damage Mitigation via On-Chain Slashing

**著者**: Hideo Kajino
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 情報システム

## Abstract

Abstract As probabilistic AI systems (such as LLM agents and autonomous nodes) are increasingly integrated into enterprise and social infrastructure, the current framework for assigning legal and financial liability for economic damages caused by hallucinations or unpredicted bugs remains fundamentally flawed due to its reliance on post-hoc litigation against system developers and corporate operators. To address this issue at its root, this paper presents the design philosophy and the finalized, self-contained implementation code—strictly excluding any medical contracts and dedicated exclusively to non-medical enterprise partners directly contracted with Hideo Kajino—for the Deterministic Slashing Oracle Pipeline (Casino Protocol 23). This pipeline completely isolates human founders and non-medical contracting partners from legal and financial liability by automatically executing damage claims against the AI's own on-chain staked collateral. Through simulation execution logs, we demonstrate that this model achieves 100% indemnification for both the Genesis Architect (Hideo Kajino) and non-medical contracted partners, realizing a self-contained liability framework within the autonomous AI ecosystem.
