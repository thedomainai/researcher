# The AI Persona Economy: Twelve Behavioral Archetypes Shaping the Future of AI Adoption

**著者**: S Laskar Sonia Laskar
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 情報システム

## Abstract

Ask ten people whether they “use AI” and the yes-or-no answer tells you almost nothing. One treats a chatbot as a faster search engine. Another lets it draft half of the working day. A third talks to it at midnight about things they tell no one else. A fourth signs the company’s entire AI budget while barely typing a prompt, and a fifth refuses the technology on principle. This paper argues that the question that now matters is not whether people use AI but what they are willing to trust it to do, and it offers a map: twelve types of AI user, from the Builder to the Churned Sceptic, organized into three tiers and described along six measurable dimensions. Three findings shape the map. People fall into stable AI habits remarkably fast, so the type someone becomes is partly created by product design, not just by personality. The types run on two separate logics, how much work is handed over and how close the relationship is allowed to get, and each carries its own distinct risk: costly errors on one side, dependency on the other. And each type should be visible in the clock: published data already shows sleep advice peaking at 5 a.m. and recipe requests at 6 p.m., so the map can be tested against the rhythms of an ordinary day. For AI leaders the consequences are direct: user counts conceal twelve different customers, and onboarding quietly decides which type each new user becomes. The paper closes with nine testable predictions, seven lessons for AI leadership, and a plan for putting the map on trial.
