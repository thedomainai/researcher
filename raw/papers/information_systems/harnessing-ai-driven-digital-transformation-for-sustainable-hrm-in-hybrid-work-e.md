# Harnessing AI-Driven Digital Transformation for Sustainable HRM in Hybrid Work Ecosystems

**著者**: Vinitha Saravanan
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 情報システム

## Abstract

In the modern era we are living in, Artificial Intelligence in Human Resource management made a revolution of how organizations manage employees, identify their talent and helping to manage work Stress and mitigate bias in the hybrid environment. As organizations embrace AI-based tools are transforming the recruitment and performance management and employee relations digital transformation. Nevertheless, the increased pace of using these technologies. poses highly important questions regarding sustainability in the sphere of HRM, especially in respect of balancing technology efficiency with values the human being. This paper examines the process of AI-based digital. change is capable of instilling sustainable HRM activities that boost the welfare of employees, resilience, and work ecosystems in the hybrid work environment.
