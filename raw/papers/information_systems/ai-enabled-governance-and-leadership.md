# AI-Enabled Governance and Leadership

**著者**: Amit Verma, Pragathi Kodali, Anita Saxena, Veena Veena
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 情報システム

## Abstract

Governance systems of the future: AI is changing leadership styles, institutional structures, and how strategic change happens in business and government. AI-enabled businesses are not just digital, they are predictive, adaptive and data-driven institutional ecosystems. These ecosystems can leverage both — especially algorithmic intelligence and real-time data — to create far more dynamic feedback loops that shift decision-making. Drawing on conceptual frameworks from institutional theory, adaptive systems theory and cybernetic governance through to algorithmic accountability, we develop a framework for understanding AI-enabled governance and leadership across multiple levels. We present a multi-layer approach to understanding institutional design enabling use of AI, stress the importance and implications of ceding control over the organizational processes to algorithms through transitions in leadership, and consider different models for ecosystem governance that can help reconcile these hybrid public-private ecosystems.
