# Real-World Impact: Transforming Agriculture with Technology A Multi-Stakeholder Review of Smart Monitoring, Precision Irrigation, and Digital Value-Chain Integration

**著者**: A. Shaji George, Dr.T.Baskar, P. Balaji Srikaanth
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 情報システム

## Abstract

There's a deliberate and significant transition from input to information. This article combines peer-reviewed evidence and public-sector data on six interrelated technology clusters that are fundamentally changing on-farm and off-farm decision making artificial intelligence (AI) based Farm Management Platforms, Internet of Things (IoT) smart irrigation, aerial and satellite crop monitoring, blockchain-enabled supply-chain digitisation, sustainability and carbon-tracking instruments, and farm data analytics dashboards. In the literature examined, precision tools have been correlated with 15% to 30% gains in crop yields, 30% to 55% of water saved during irrigation, and 10% to 20% of operational costs reduced, all with a range of variability based on the crop, the geographical region, and the size of the farm. It examines the mechanisms of these effects on the one hand and the differential rates of benefit, on the other, between large commercial farms and smallholders, and the policy and infrastructural conditions that drive actual adoption. It also highlights areas of current evidence which are lacking, such as limited long horizon field trials, poor interoperability standards, and lack of social outcome data. The article ends with a discussion on how to turn pilots into lasting, system-level change in food production, both from the government and enterprise side, as well as from the perspective of small and medium producers.
