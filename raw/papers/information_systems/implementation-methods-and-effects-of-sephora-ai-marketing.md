# Implementation Methods and Effects of Sephora AI Marketing

**著者**: Qiaoyu Zhu
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 情報システム

## Abstract

Nowadays, using AI technology to improve efficiency is one of the most important research subjects. Researchers found that Sephora use AI and AR technology to improve the efficiency of shopping. However, the reason behind efficiency improvements and their broader implication is not been unified. Therefore, this study collects data, including sales growth and so on, and compare it with initial data to examine how the adoption of these technologies enhancing Sephora's operational efficiency. According to the survey, AI technology has as well as enhanced sales efficiency and the experience of consumers transformed the structure of rights and the relationship between AI and consumers. These positive impacts demonstrate that Sephora must continue to use these technologies and refine them further. Besides, these advanced technologies may lead to data privacy leakage and declining consumer trust issues as well. Therefore, solving these issues has also become part of Sephora's efforts to refine these technologies.
