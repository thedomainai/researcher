# Human-Centred AI Enterprise

**著者**: BRITANNIA GLOBAL CERTIFICATION SERVICES LTD
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 情報システム

## Abstract

Human-Centred AI Enterprise™ is a BGC standard developed by the Centre for Research, Standards & Human Integrity of Britannia Global Certification Services as a practical, evidence-based framework within the category “Enterprises”. It is designed to demonstrate, strengthen and continuously improve the capabilities associated with Human-Centred AI Enterprise. Assessment is carried out through evidence, interviews and sampling, with emphasis on verifiable evidence, practical implementation and measurable improvement. The standard follows the seven BGC pillars—Understand, Lead, Identify, Prevent, Act, Verify and Improve—and the BGC I-MAP™ pathway: Discover, Map, Build, Act and Improve. Results are classified through the BGC TIER model, from Development Stage to Global Leadership. The scheme is private, voluntary and independent and must be applied in accordance with applicable law. Su finalidad es demostrar, fortalecer y mejorar de manera continua las capacidades vinculadas con Empresa de Inteligencia Artificial Centrada en las Personas. La evaluación se realiza mediante evidencia, entrevistas y muestreo, con énfasis en evidencias verificables, aplicación práctica y mejora medible. El estándar se estructura a partir de los siete pilares BGC —Comprender, Liderar, Identificar, Prevenir, Actuar, Verificar y Mejorar— y de la ruta BGC I-MAP™: Discover, Map, Build, Act e Improve. Los resultados se clasifican mediante el modelo TIER de BGC, desde Development Stage hasta Global Leadership.
