# How Digital Information Systems Enable Sustainable Procurement: A Systematic Literature Review

**著者**: Gökhan Cenk, Tobias Engel
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: 情報システム

## Abstract

Digital technologies, including artificial intelligence, machine learning, blockchain, and e-procurement systems, are transforming procurement practices. Firms increasingly prioritize economic efficiency while also adhering to environmental requirements and social responsibility to ensure long-term value creation. Despite this, the literature remains fragmented across the domains of purchasing, sustainability, and information systems. This systematic literature review, conducted using the PRISMA method, synthesizes evidence that digital technologies facilitate sustainable procurement across the Triple Bottom Line. Four primary enabling mechanisms are identified: process integration and efficiency, visibility and traceability, supplier assessment and governance, and lifecycle intelligence. Economic and environmental outcomes are more thoroughly addressed than social outcomes, revealing a structural gap in the literature. The review adopts a mechanism-based approach and develops a conceptual research model grounded in socio-technical systems theory and information technology (IT) business value logic, providing guidance for practitioners to align digital investments with procurement sustainability objectives.
