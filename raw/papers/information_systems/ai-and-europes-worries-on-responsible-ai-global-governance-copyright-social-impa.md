## Abstract

The European Parliament has stated its position: Europe needs responsible, ethical, and fair AI, with global governance, stronger copyright protections for journalists, artists, and creators, and concrete measures to address its social impact. https://www.threads.com/share/BASyuK5NzI/ Technological progress must go hand in hand with social and moral progress. AI must servehumanity, not the other way around. This paper takes those concerns seriously. It states them precisely. It then shows that thecurrent stochastic architecture cannot satisfy them — not because the regulators lack will,but because the architecture cannot be specified, verified, or attributed. The impossibility is not a policy failure. It is a mathematical result. The paper then presents the architecture that can satisfy them: ST-T1024, a deterministicstandard that is bounded, specified, verified, and attributable. It maps each of Europe'sconcerns to a specific architectural mechanism. It shows that responsible AI is not a promise. It is a property of the architecture. The paper does not argue that Europe is wrong to worry. It argues that the worries cannot beanswered by regulating an unregulatable object. They can be answered by building adifferent one.
