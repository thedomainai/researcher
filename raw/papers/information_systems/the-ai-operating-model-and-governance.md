## Abstract

With the human dimensions of AI adoption addressed in the preceding chapter, we now turn to the organizational structures and governance frameworks that sustain AI at enterprise scale. This chapter covers the executive roles, cross-functional team architectures, data strategies, and ethical guardrails that transform AI from a set of isolated projects into a governed enterprise capability.
