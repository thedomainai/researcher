# "Digital Sustainability and Foundation Model Adoption in Egyptian Organizations: A 347-Organization Dataset for PLS-SEM Analysis"

**著者**: Wael Badawy
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 情報システム

## Abstract

"The DS-FMA-347 dataset provides empirical data for investigating the organizational relationships among foundation model adoption, digital transformation capability, artificial intelligence governance, cybersecurity readiness, and digital sustainability performance. The dataset was developed in the context of research examining AI-enabled digital sustainability and its alignment with Egypt Vision 2030.The dataset contains 347 anonymized organizational observations representing six sectors: government, banking and finance, healthcare, education, information and communication technology (ICT), and manufacturing. Each observation is represented by a qualified organizational respondent and contains responses to 25 measurement indicators organized into five latent constructs: Foundation Model Adoption (FMA), Digital Transformation Capability (DTC), AI Governance (AIG), Cybersecurity Readiness (CSR), and Digital Sustainability Performance (DSP). Each construct is operationalized using five indicators measured on a five-point Likert scale.The accompanying codebook provides the indicator identifier, construct abbreviation, construct name, questionnaire wording, and permissible response range. The dataset also contains supporting structural-model information, including the hypothesized relationships reported in the associated study and respondent-level OLS robustness estimates with 95% confidence intervals.The dataset can support replication and methodological research involving Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM), covariance-based SEM, regression modeling, mediation analysis, measurement-model validation, reliability and validity analysis, common-method-bias diagnostics, sector comparisons, and alternative machine-learning or statistical investigations of organizational digital sustainability.Potential applications extend beyond replication of the associated study. Researchers may use the data to investigate responsible AI adoption, organizational digital capabilities, AI governance, cyber-resilience, sustainable digital transformation, emerging-economy technology adoption, and relationships among technological resources, organizational capabilities, governance mechanisms, and sustainability outcomes.All observations are anonymized, and the released dataset contains no directly identifying personal information."
