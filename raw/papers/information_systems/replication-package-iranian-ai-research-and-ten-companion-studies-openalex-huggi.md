# Replication package: Iranian AI research and ten companion studies (OpenAlex, Hugging Face, Wikimedia)

**著者**: P. Ghorbani
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 情報システム

## Abstract

Code, derived data, figures and results files for eleven scientometric and language-technology studies of Iranian AI research (bibliometric trajectory, researcher mobility, Persian open models, tokenisation, OpenAlex coverage break, Persian Wikipedia, frontier-topic adoption, spatial concentration, author productivity, Persian NLP community, topic breadth). Raw OpenAlex responses are not included; data were retrieved on 1-2 October 2026 and can be re-collected with the included scripts.
