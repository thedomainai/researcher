# Knowledge Graphs as Boundary Objects: A Sociotechnical Framework for Data Governance in Resource-Constrained Organizations

**著者**: Bushra Al Sulayyim, Madhushi Bandara, Asif Gill, Ghassan Beydoun
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 情報システム

## Abstract

More than ninety percent of organizations operate with limited resources, yet mainstream data governance frameworks assume enterprise-scale capacity. Drawing on the boundary-object concept (Star & Griesemer, 1989; Carlile, 2002, 2004), this paper proposes a sociotechnical framework that positions the knowledge graph as a shared artefact mediating between three governance communities: regulators and experts, ontology engineers, and organizations. A systematic review of forty-three studies shows that existing approaches treat knowledge graphs as technical artefacts, leaving resource-constrained organizations underserved along eight recurring dimensions. Reframing these as boundary-crossing needs, the framework explains how a knowledge graph transfers, translates, and transforms governance knowledge, making governance explicit and traceable while decisions remain with the communities. Nine design principles operationalize the framework. The paper offers researchers a new theoretical perspective, vendors an underserved market, and practitioners a feasible, constraint-aware approach.
