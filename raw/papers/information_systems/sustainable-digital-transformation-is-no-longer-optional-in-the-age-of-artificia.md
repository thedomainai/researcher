# Sustainable Digital Transformation Is No Longer Optional In The Age Of Artificial Inteligence

**著者**: Karim Feroz
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 情報システム

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence has become the defining force of digital transformation, reshaping organizational processes, structure, leadership decision-making, supply chain, and value creation.AI intensifies environmental, social, and ethical challenges related to energy consumption, algorithmic opacity, and societal impact.Despite these tensions, Information Systems research continues to privilege efficiency, scalability, and innovation speed, often treating sustainability as a secondary or downstream concern.This paper argues that sustainable digital transformation is a strategic imperative in the age of AI.We leverage sustainable digital transformation as a sociotechnical approach that integrates economic, environmental, and social considerations into AI-enabled transformation from inception rather than as an afterthought.Aligning with the ECIS 2026 theme, this paper articulates why sustainability must be foundational to AI-driven transformation and outlines implications for IS research, practice, and education aimed at shaping responsible and resilient digital futures.
