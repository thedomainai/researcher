# FACTORS INFLUENCING THE ADOPTION OF AI-ASSISTED UI/UX DESIGN TOOLS AMONG SOFTWARE PROFESSIONALS: AN EXTENDED TECHNOLOGY ACCEPTANCE MODEL APPROACH

## Abstract

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has transformed software engineering by introducing intelligent systems that support automation and creativity throughout the development lifecycle.AI-assisted UI/UX design tools, in particular, can generate interface components, create prototypes, and recommend design improvements, offering opportunities to enhance productivity and accelerate development.However, adoption among software professionals remains uncertain, as AI-based creative systems introduce unique challenges related to trust, reliability, transparency, and ethics.This study investigates the factors influencing adoption intention of AI-assisted design tools among software professionals using an extended Technology Acceptance Model (TAM).The proposed model integrates Perceived Usefulness, Perceived Ease of Use, and Trust in AI, Creativity Support, and Ethical Concerns, with Attitude Toward AI-Assisted Design Tools as a mediating construct.A quantitative, cross-sectional survey will collect data from software engineers, UI/UX designers, developers, and computing students.Analysis will employ descriptive statistics, reliability and validity tests, correlation, and PLS-SEM.This research aims to contribute empirical insights into AI adoption behaviour, supporting organizations and educators in developing strategies for integrating AI-assisted design technologies into modern software workflows.
