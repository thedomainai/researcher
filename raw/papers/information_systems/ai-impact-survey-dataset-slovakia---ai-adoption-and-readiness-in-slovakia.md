# AI-IMPACT Survey Dataset (Slovakia) - AI adoption and readiness in Slovakia

**著者**: Matej Černý, Baziuk Andriana, Slavka Šagátová, Robert Hanak, Peter Štetka
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 情報システム

## Abstract

This repository provides the AI-IMPACT organisation-level survey dataset from Slovakia, together with the documentation required for reuse, including a data dictionary/codebook, Slovak and English questionnaires, and a survey flow and skip-logic map. The dataset provides micro-level survey data on the adoption and use of artificial intelligence (AI) in companies operating in Slovakia and offers an empirically grounded perspective on the organisational aspects of digital transformation. The repository contains a public-release raw dataset with 1,086 complete screened responses and a deduplicated analytical dataset containing 816 unique organisational records. Both datasets comprise 621 variables organised into thematic blocks reflecting the questionnaire logic, including organisational and respondent characteristics; multidimensional indicators of AI maturity; companies’ attitudes towards AI; AI adoption status and intensity of use; functional areas of current or planned AI application; and perceived barriers to implementation. The dataset integrates multiple variable types, including categorical indicators, ordinal maturity items, Likert-type scale items, binary variables derived from multiple-choice questions, and technical metadata describing the response process and data structure. Missing values in content variables arise primarily from the questionnaire’s programmed branching and skip logic, whereby certain items were displayed only to relevant respondents, and therefore generally do not represent conventional item non-response. The publicly released datasets have been de-identified. We removed direct organisational identifiers and reviewed and processed free-text responses to minimise re-identification risk while preserving substantive information and the data's analytical usability. The dataset is distributed in CSV and SAV formats and includes a structured data dictionary, technical README documentation, Slovak and English questionnaire versions, and the survey flow and skip-logic map. These materials support transparent interpretation of the data structure and facilitate secondary research on AI adoption, organisational readiness, technology diffusion, and corporate digital transformation. Version 1.1 includes documentation and data-harmonisation updates. No new respondents were added, and no additional data collection was conducted. The final analytical sample remains unchanged at N = 816 unique organisations. See README.md for the detailed version history.
