# Reducing barriers to inclusive tertiary STEM education: opportunities for AI

## Abstract

Australia’s growing demand for science, technology, engineering and mathematics (STEM) skills contrasts with stagnant tertiary participation and persistent inequities. This brief review examines barriers to inclusive tertiary STEM education and the potential for artificial intelligence (AI) to support Universal Design for Learning. AI can provide multimodal content, personalised scaffolding, flexible participation and assessment, and support for self-regulated and collaborative learning. Effective adoption therefore requires institutional governance, AI proficiency and sustained educator judgement. Further longitudinal research in Australian tertiary STEM settings is needed to identify which tools reduce barriers, for whom and under what conditions.
