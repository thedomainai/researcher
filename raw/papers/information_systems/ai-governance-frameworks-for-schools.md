# AI Governance Frameworks for Schools

**著者**: Clara Hawking, Darren Coxon
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 情報システム

## Abstract

This chapter develops a practical framework for governing artificial intelligence in schools. It argues that the adoption of AI systems is not a purely technical procurement decision but an institutional decision that redistributes authority within educational organisations. Drawing on international governance principles from the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development, UNESCO, and emerging regulatory models such as the European Union Artificial Intelligence Act, the chapter proposes a multi-level governance architecture that clarifies roles and responsibilities among teachers, school leaders, education authorities, technology providers, parents, and students. The chapter translates ethical principles into operational design. It outlines how schools can establish oversight structures, classify AI systems according to decision impact, integrate governance into safeguarding frameworks, and develop procurement and contractual standards that ensure vendor accountability. Particular attention is given to professional capacity building, institutional codes of conduct, and iterative review mechanisms that allow AI use to be continuously evaluated and corrected. Rather than framing governance as a constraint on innovation, the chapter presents it as a condition for legitimate and sustainable AI integration in education. By embedding responsibility, transparency, and human oversight into institutional practice, schools can retain professional authority while responsibly engaging with rapidly evolving AI technologies.
