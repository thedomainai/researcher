## Abstract

SΔϕ-82 — Philosophical Force Matrix introduces an AI-native framework for comparing philosophical, ideological, constitutional, and governance systems according to how strongly they establish foundational claims and how strongly they intervene in practical action. The framework distinguishes two primary axes: M — Metaphysical / Ontological / Foundational Commitment P — Explicit Practical Intervention and introduces three modifying dimensions: W — Indirect World-Binding R — Re-entry / Self-Audit Capacity F — Final-Arbiter Tendency The resulting four-quadrant matrix distinguishes: Q1 — Strong Normative Architecture Q2 — Speculative System Q3 — Operational Critique Q4 — Suspensive Inquiry SΔϕ-82 does not treat quadrant placement as a moral ranking. High metaphysical commitment is not automatically dogmatic, low metaphysical commitment is not automatically neutral, strong practical intervention is not automatically authoritarian, and weak explicit prescription does not imply weak real-world impact. The framework therefore separates authorial prescription from historical World-Binding, and distinguishes declared openness from executable re-entry. A philosophical system may issue few direct prescriptions while substantially reshaping culture, institutions, identity, education, policy, or later political interpretation. SΔϕ-82 also introduces Final-Arbiter Tendency as an audit dimension for systems that increasingly absorb counterexamples, competing vocabularies, and external criticism into their own interpretive framework. Its central question is whether the world or another subject can still say “No” in a form that survives the framework’s own translation machinery. The package explicitly applies the same audit to SΔϕ itself. Low metaphysical commitment must not be used as evidence of neutrality, and an audit framework must remain auditable. The AI-Native package contains a canonical English paper, Korean companion document, AI-readable core, formal specification, force-matrix protocol, re-entry and final-arbiter audit, quadrant-specific failure modes, misclassification rules, worked philosophical cases, AI-governance cases, machine-readable evaluation cases, JSON Schema, evaluator instructions, provenance and limitation notes, metadata, citation information, and an integrity manifest. The central protective proposition is: “Quadrant location is a structural description, not a moral verdict.” A second canonical question is: “Can the world or another subject say ‘No’ to this framework in a way that survives the framework’s own translation machinery?” SΔϕ-82 is intended as a comparative philosophy and meta-governance instrument rather than a final taxonomy of philosophical truth. Classifications may vary by text, historical period, interpretation, institutional implementation, and later appropriation.
