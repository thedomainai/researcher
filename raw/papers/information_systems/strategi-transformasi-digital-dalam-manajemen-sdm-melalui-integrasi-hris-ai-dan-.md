## Abstract

This research is motivated by the increasing demand for digital transformation in human resource (HRM) management to improve operational efficiency and organizational competitiveness. This study examines the integration of digital technologies such as Human Resource Information Systems (HRIS), Artificial Intelligence (AI), and e-learning in HR management. The research uses the theoretical framework of Digital Transformation, Resource-Based View (RBV), Technology Acceptance Model (TAM), and Human Capital Theory as the basis for analysis. The method used is a descriptive qualitative approach, with data collection techniques consisting of a literature review of 25 scientific articles from 2020–2025. Data analysis was carried out through systematic data reduction, presentation, and drawing conclusions. The results show that the implementation of digital transformation contributes significantly to increased operational efficiency and employee satisfaction. However, its effectiveness is influenced by organizational readiness, the level of technology acceptance, and the ability to manage changes in work culture. Furthermore, digital competence and employee perceptions are key factors in successful implementation. In conclusion, digital transformation in HR management is a multidimensional process that requires the integration of technology, people, and organizational strategy to achieve optimal and sustainable performance.
