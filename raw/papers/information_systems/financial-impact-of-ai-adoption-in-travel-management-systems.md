# Financial Impact of AI Adoption in Travel Management Systems

**著者**: Manju Dagar, Mohit Yadav, Perfecto Gatbonton Aquino Jr., Arvind Goswami
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

The rapid integration of artificial intelligence (AI) into travel management systems is reshaping the financial landscape of the global travel industry. This chapter examines the multifaceted financial implications of AI adoption, encompassing cost reduction, revenue optimization, operational efficiency, and return on investment. Drawing on recent empirical studies and industry analyses, the chapter evaluates how AI-driven tools-such as dynamic pricing engines, predictive analytics, chatbots, and fraud detection algorithms-generate measurable economic value for travel enterprises. It further explores the financial barriers to AI implementation, including high upfront capital expenditure and workforce transition costs, while assessing long-term profitability gains. The chapter also addresses risk management and regulatory compliance cost savings enabled by intelligent automation. By synthesizing evidence from academic journals and industry reports, this chapter provides a comprehensive financial perspective on AI adoption as a strategic imperative for competitive travel management.
