## Abstract

This archive preserves nine original research manuscripts by Vladisav Jovanović on AI governance, human oversight, accountability, autonomy, and embodied AI safety. The archive is organized into two parts: Part 06A — AI Governance and Meaningful Human Control: Seven original manuscripts addressing human oversight, shared responsibility, bounded delegation, corrective control, clinical decision support, and human autonomy. Part 06B — Embodied AI: Two original manuscripts addressing embodiment, robotic agency, physical safety, runtime assurance, and persistent correction. The manuscripts have been consolidated into two academic publications: AI Governance and Meaningful Human Control: Oversight, Autonomy, and Accountability. Embodied AI: Agency, Responsibility, and Robot Safety. The original manuscripts are preserved without modification. Both archives include source maps, documentation, and SHA-256 checksums. The purpose of this archive is to preserve the original research, maintain citation continuity, document the development of the research program, and provide transparent access to the sources underlying the consolidated publications.
