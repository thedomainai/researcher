# How AI is Impacting Corporate Governance

**著者**: Michal Harcej
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 情報システム

## Abstract

Artifcial intelligence is often presented as a technological revolution. In reality, it is a governance revolution.For decades, organizations have invested heavily in building more capable software, stronger cybersecurity, and increasingly intelligent systems. Yet, a critical execution gap remains: traditional governance mechanisms (policies, compliance checklists, and human-in-the-loop approvals) govern people and documentation, not the actual runtime execution of AI decisions.In high-risk industries, the fundamental question is no longer, "Can AI make good decisions?" The imperative question is: "Can the organization prove that every AI-assisted action was constitutionally authorized before it was executed?"Intelligence alone does not create trustworthy systems. When governance is treated as a retroactive compliance activity rather than an architectural property, organizations expose themselves to catastrophic legal, fnancial, and operational risks. This book provides a blueprint for closing the execution gap. It argues that governance must exist before intelligence. Authority must be deterministic, policies must be machine-enforceable at runtime, evidence must be generated natively and immutably, and systems must possess the capacity for structural refusal when constitutional boundaries are tested.Where intelligence proposes, governance decides. This is the new fduciary reality.
