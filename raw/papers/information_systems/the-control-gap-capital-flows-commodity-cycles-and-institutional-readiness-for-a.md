# The Control Gap: Capital Flows, Commodity Cycles, and Institutional Readiness for AI-Enabled Macroprudential Policy in Emerging Economies

**著者**: Alfredo Merlet
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 2
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 情報システム

## Abstract

This book examines a systematic gap in three decades of research by Chilean economist Andrés Fernández (IMF, former Head of Research at the Central Bank of Chile) on capital controls, commodity-driven business cycles, and firms' financial vulnerability in emerging economies. While this body of work has produced rigorous measurement tools — including the widely used FKRSU capital control dataset and, more recently, the use of large language models to reconstruct historical capital-flow restrictions — it does not address the institutional capacity of regulators to execute policy using equivalent AI-enabled tools in real time. The book proposes a four-dimension AI-Governance Readiness framework (data infrastructure, internal analytical capacity, enabling regulatory framework, and institutional response speed) specific to the macroprudential and capital-flow-management function, extending the institutional-readiness research program developed in the author's previous volumes (The Compliance Gap; The Adaptation Gap). This is a conceptual and methodological volume; empirical validation with real-world data is left to a planned companion volume.
