# AI Tokenomics: The New Economic Language of Enterprise Intelligence

**著者**: A. Shaji George
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 情報システム

## Abstract

The arrival of generative artificial intelligence has quietly rewritten the financial grammar of enterprise technology. While traditional software consumption models are based on users, seats or servers, the modern stack is based on tokens, the smallest unit of linguistic and computational work that large language models process. This article looks at AI tokenomics as a formal discipline to manage the cost, value, and scale of intelligence within the organization. It explores the history of the token as the leading measure of information technology, how organizations are developing observability and governance systems to track it and why traditional budgeting methods break down in the recursive requirements of autonomous agents. The three pillars of a mature Tokenomic framework are attributed, predicted and optimized, and the analysis discusses the structural challenges that hinder each of the three steps hidden reasoning tokens, data gravity, and lack of pricing standards among providers. The study also considers the path of hybrid architectures, the emergence of autonomous budget agents, and economic aspects related to the forecasted twenty fourfold increase in the use of tokens by 2030. The findings indicate that those organisations that will see tokens as a strategic currency not a line item will shape the next decade of competitive advantage in digital transformation.
