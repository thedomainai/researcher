# The Future of AI Governance in Financial Services

**著者**: Barry A. Salas
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 情報システム

## Abstract

Two years had passed since the ISPM had first presented a half-finished AI inventory to the Risk Committee and watched the room go quiet. The program that had grown from that moment bore almost no resemblance to the ad hoc collection of activities it had started as. There was a current, maintained inventory. There were risk assessments for every system in it. There was a functioning governance committee with documented meeting records and a board that received annual briefings it could genuinely engage with. There were vendor contracts with AI-specific provisions, a monitoring program that caught anomalies before they became incidents, and an acceptable use policy that staff had actually read.
