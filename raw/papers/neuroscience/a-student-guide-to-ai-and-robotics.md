## Abstract

This book provides a practical and accessible introduction to AI and robotics, presenting the foundational technologies driving the development of intelligent systems. It covers key topics such as machine learning, perception, control, and autonomous decision-making, balancing theoretical concepts with practical applications. Through selected examples and case studies, the book explores robotics platforms and applied AI technologies used across industries, offering insights into their transformative potential in real-world scenarios. With a focus on hands-on learning, it introduces programming languages and tools commonly used in AI and robotics, empowering readers to apply concepts and techniques effectively. It also addresses the importance of building secure, responsible systems and examines the future of human-AI collaboration. An essential resource for introductory AI and robotics courses, this book provides the foundational knowledge needed to advance in these dynamic fields.
