# Project LINGXI: The Implicit Archive of Human Civilization — White Paper v1.0

**著者**: J Y Zhao
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 脳科学

## Abstract

This white paper proposes Project LINGXI, a universal archival framework for tacit knowledge—the embodied skills and cognitive styles that have always vanished with their bearers. It introduces the .hha format protocol, a multimodal container specification for recording and reproducing the processes behind human creativity, not merely their products. The document defines a nine-layer data structure (L1–L9) spanning kinematics, kinetics, electromyography, audio, olfaction, thermal, visual, environmental, and cognitive dimensions; a dual-path acquisition architecture combining deep-scanning studios for craftspeople (LINGXI Studio) with lightweight eyewear terminals for longitudinal cognitive acquisition (LINGXI Lite); and open execution interfaces for robotic reproduction, AI-mediated cognitive dialogue, and haptic skill transmission. Grounded in Polanyi's theory of tacit knowledge, embodied cognition research, information theory, and learning from demonstration, the project proposes a paradigm shift from product archiving to process archiving. A core theoretical contribution is the concept of process fidelity—the degree to which an archived process preserves the temporal, sensorimotor, and behavioral structure necessary for future reproduction or learning. The .hha format is permanently open, unbound to any commercial entity, and released as a public intellectual commons.
