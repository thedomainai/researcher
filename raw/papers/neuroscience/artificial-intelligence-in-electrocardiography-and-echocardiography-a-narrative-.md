# Artificial Intelligence in Electrocardiography and Echocardiography: A Narrative Review

**著者**: Amara Liaqat, Samman Rose, Ammar Farook Chapra, Hassan M Al Tamimi, Awad Al Qahtani
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: 脳科学

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence (AI) is increasingly being used in electrocardiography (ECG) and transthoracic echocardiography (TTE).This narrative review discusses its current applications, with particular attention to myocardial infarction (MI) detection, signal and image quality, clinical benefits, and limitations.We reviewed selected original research articles, clinical trials, published reviews, and relevant clinical guidelines.The review focused on the practical use of AI in ECG and echocardiography, including its diagnostic accuracy and potential role in patient care.As this was a narrative review, no systematic search or meta-analysis was performed.In ECG, AI has shown promising results in detecting abnormal heart rhythms, identifying patients at risk of atrial fibrillation (AF), detecting reduced ventricular function, predicting cardiovascular risk, and improving the quality of noisy recordings.In echocardiography, AI can help identify standard views, guide image acquisition, measure cardiac chambers and ejection fraction, assess diastolic function, and detect regional wall-motion abnormalities.Some clinical trials have shown improvements in specific diagnostic tasks and clinical workflows.However, results vary between studies.AI-based MI detection using ECG and echocardiography also shows promise, but further studies involving larger patient populations and different clinical settings are needed.AI has the potential to improve the accuracy, efficiency, and consistency of ECG and echocardiographic assessment.However, it should support rather than replace clinical judgment.Further research, particularly large multicenter and prospective studies, is needed to confirm its reliability, safety, and benefits for patient care.
