# Harnessing Brain‐Inspired AI for Healthcare's Digital Future

**著者**: Bittoo Kumar Sharma
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 脳科学

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence (AI) changed many industries, and now health care is no exception. Still, the most traditionally designed AI systems do not attain similar adaptability and efficiency like a human brain. Brain-inspired AI, or neuromorphic computing, attempts to bridge this gap by mimicking the structure and functionality of biological neural networks. This allows AI to process information more efficiently, make decisions in real time, and learn from minimal data, making it highly suitable for the evolving landscape of digital healthcare. In this book chapter, we study how brain-inspired AI is being used to reconstruct healthcare in support of faster and more accurate diagnostics, personalized prescription plans, intelligent automation, as well as robotic surgeries, computer-aided medical imaging analysis, and clinical predictive analytics through neuromorphic models that better emulate human brain cognition. Neomorphic AI machines can process unlabeled medical information with minimal retraining, increasing the accuracy as well as performance of healthcare-decision-making tools, unlike general machine learning setups that need voluminous labeled inputs. In addition, the brain-inspired AI facilitates remote patient monitoring, wearable health devices, and real-time decision support systems, making health care more accessible and proactive. It also mitigates challenges in high computational costs, data security, and interpretability, allowing for ethical and scalable AI applications in medicine. Integrating principles from neuroscience with AI-driven healthcare solutions can then make them more adaptive, autonomous, and human-centric. This chapter delves deeper into the technological advancements, challenges, and future possibilities of harnessing brain-inspired AI for the digital transformation of healthcare. In addition, we discuss real-world case studies, industry applications, and the potential impact of this technology on patient care and medical research. With the healthcare industry embracing digital transformation, brain-inspired AI is leading the charge towards intelligent, efficient, and personalized healthcare solutions.
