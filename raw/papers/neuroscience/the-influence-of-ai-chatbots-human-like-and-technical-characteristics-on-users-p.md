# The influence of AI chatbots’ human-like and technical characteristics on users’ perceived addictiveness

**著者**: Jing Yuan, Hengyi Shi, Yongjun Li, Tiantian Zhao
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 脳科学

## Abstract

With the widespread adoption of generative AI chatbots, their human-like and technical characteristics may enhance user experience while also increasing the risks of overdependence and potential addiction. However, how these two categories of characteristics jointly shape users’ perceived addictiveness remains insufficiently understood. Drawing on the Stimulus–Organism–Response (S–O–R) framework, this study developed a model integrating perceived empathy, social presence, perceived usefulness, perceived ease of use, trust, emotional attachment, functional dependence, and perceived addictiveness. Data were collected from 345 Chinese AI chatbot users through an online survey and analyzed using partial least squares structural equation modeling. The results showed that perceived empathy, social presence, perceived usefulness, and perceived ease of use all positively influenced trust, which in turn strengthened emotional attachment and functional dependence. Both emotional attachment and functional dependence increased perceived addictiveness, with emotional attachment exerting the stronger effect. Mediation analysis further indicated that chatbot characteristics indirectly influenced perceived addictiveness through two parallel pathways: trust–emotional attachment and trust–functional dependence. These findings reveal the emotional and functional mechanisms underlying perceived AI chatbot addictiveness, extend the S–O–R framework, and provide implications for responsible design, calibrated trust, and dependence-risk governance.
