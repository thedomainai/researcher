# Beyond AI Adoption: Trust as a Task-Level Appraisal in Academic LLM Use

**著者**: Yvonne Chang, Jon Mella, Anna Dowthwaite, Yoana Ahmetoglu, Anna Louise Cox
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 脳科学

## Abstract

Large language models (LLMs) have been positioned as tools that could reduce workload and support productivity in academic work, yet adoption remains selective. Prior research attributes this to differences in trust but offers limited insight into how trust is enacted in practice. We report a qualitative study with 18 UK academics, examining how they decide when and how to trust LLMs in their work. Trust is not a stable attitude but a situated, task-level appraisal, governed primarily by whether outputs can be verified and at what cost. We identify verification burden to describe how, in academic knowledge work, the effort required to validate LLM outputs can exceed the benefit of using them, making restraint the reasonable choice. LLMs also serve as emotional infrastructure for managing the psychological demands of academic work, while creating tensions with academic identity. At the institutional level, we identify institutional paralysis: unclear policies, absent norms, and a lack of endorsed tools constrain adoption even among willing users. Together, these reframe cautious LLM use as a nuanced interplay of constrained trust calibration, emotional ambivalence, and institutional paralysis. We translate these findings into design concepts that make verification effort legible and treat emotional support as core function, as well as describing implications for institutional policy
