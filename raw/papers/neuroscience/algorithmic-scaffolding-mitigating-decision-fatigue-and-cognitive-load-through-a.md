# Algorithmic Scaffolding: Mitigating Decision Fatigue and Cognitive Load through AI Chatbots to Prevent Occupational Burnout

**著者**: Adhilla Salsabila Putri Artha, Ly Hoang Nguyen
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 脳科学

## Abstract

The contemporary digital workplace imposes unprecedented information density on employees, rapidly depleting working memory and executive functioning, which accelerates occupational burnout. While artificial intelligence (AI) tools are frequently deployed to manage this burden, poor implementation often shifts the cognitive demand from task execution to a continuous algorithmic monitoring, thereby exacerbating mental fatigue. The current burnout interventions remain largely reactive, addressing symptoms rather than structural causes. To address this, we propose a conceptual "dual-pathway model" that integrates AI chatbots as proactive cognitive scaffolding. Pathway 1 (The Cognitive Load Reduction) argues that AI must absorb low-level administrative friction to preserve working memory for deep work. Pathway 2 (The Decision Fatigue Mitigation) frames AI as a cognitive heuristic that synthesises complex data to narrow choices, shielding executive function from micro-decision exhaustion. By strategically integrating AI as a structural cognitive support, organisations can cultivate structurally brain-friendly work environments that foster sustainable resilience.
