# Multimodal Reasoning for Intent-Aware Human–Robot Collaborative Disassembly

**著者**: Rebecca Nekou; id_orcid 0009-0003-7815-8262 Malihi, Dimitrios Chrysostomou
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 脳科学

## Abstract

Abstract—The transition toward circular manufacturing neces-<br/>sitates highly adaptive disassembly processes, where the cognitive<br/>flexibility of humans is combined with the precision and payload<br/>capacity of robots. However, current human–robot collaboration<br/>(HRC) frameworks often lack contextual awareness, relying on<br/>rigid, reactive control rather than proactive intent recognition.<br/>This paper introduces a multimodal reasoning framework for<br/>intent-aware collaboration by integrating visual, depth, and<br/>surface electromyography (sEMG) signals within a Vision language<br/>model (VLM), enabling real-time adaptation of robot behavior<br/>based on observed human activity.<br/>The approach will be evaluated in a Lego-based disassembly<br/>scenario using a UR5 robot, an Intel RealSense camera, and<br/>a MindRove sEMG armband. Preliminary experiments aim to<br/>demonstrate that integrating physiological signals with visual data<br/>reduces intent-recognition latency and improves the fluency and<br/>safety of collaborative disassembly tasks.<br/>Index Terms—Human–Centered Automation, Physical Human–<br/>Robot Collaboration, Intent Recognition, Multi-modal Large<br/>Language Models, Collaborative Disassembly, sEMG
