# Cognitive Liberty and the Law of Torts

**著者**: Jubaer Shah
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 脳科学

## Abstract

Innovative neuroscience technologies, artificial intelligence, and algorithmically driven neural feedback systems are revolutionizing human cognition and autonomy. Although these advancements offer improved cognitive performance, they simultaneously introduce new hazards of psychological damage. This study examines the convergence of cognitive liberty, tort law, and algorithmic interference, specifically with AI systems that influence brain feedback loops, resulting in anxiety, compulsion, or compromised decision-making. The paper presents frameworks for tortious accountability, risk mitigation, and policy regulation by analyzing doctrinal foundations, liability models, and comparative legal methods. The article advocates for the acknowledgement of cognitive liberty as a legal interest, necessitating the modification of current tort doctrines and the establishment of regulatory protections to guarantee mental integrity in the era of neuro-algorithmic technologies.
