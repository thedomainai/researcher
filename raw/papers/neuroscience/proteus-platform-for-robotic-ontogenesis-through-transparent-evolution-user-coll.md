# PROTEUS: Platform for Robotic Ontogenesis through Transparent Evolution, User-collaboration & Synthetic consciousness

**著者**: Sergio Noe Lara Soriano
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 脳科学

## Abstract

PROTEUS is an independent research project to create Saga — the first developmentally grown artificial being. Saga is built on the ENCELADUS architecture: a neuroevolutionary, embodied, socially-raised artificial cognition system with digital dream consolidation. This project studies the emergence of cognition through developmental neuroevolution, embodied interaction, and sleep-driven consolidation.
