# AlphaCast: A Human Wisdom-LLM Intelligence Co-Reasoning Framework for Time Series Forecasting

**著者**: Xiaohan Zhang, Tian Gao, Mingyue Cheng, Bokai Pan, Ze Guo
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 脳科学

## Abstract

Time series forecasting is typically treated as a single-pass numerical prediction task based on historical observations. However, real-world forecasting often requires expert-like reasoning, where temporal patterns, contextual cues, case-based references, and domain knowledge are jointly considered and iteratively revised. Existing statistical, deep learning, foundation, and LLM-based forecasting models mainly rely on pattern fitting, direct prompting, or static context augmentation, leaving interactive and reflective forecasting insufficiently explored. In this work, we propose AlphaCast, a human wisdom-LLM intelligence co-reasoning framework for interactive time series forecasting. AlphaCast does not require real-time human intervention; instead, it operationalizes human wisdom as structured forecasting support, including time-series features, contextual cues, case-based references, and domain knowledge. Built upon an expert-like think-forecast-reflect workflow, AlphaCast comprises three agents: an Investigator that constructs forecasting context from available evidence, a Generator that produces context-grounded forecasts, and a Reflector that evaluates plausibility and provides feedback for refinement. Extensive experiments demonstrate that AlphaCast improves forecasting accuracy and produces interpretable forecast explanations, offering a promising step toward interactive and context-aware time series forecasting.
