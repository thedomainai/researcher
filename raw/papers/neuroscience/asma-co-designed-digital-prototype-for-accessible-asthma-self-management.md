## Abstract

Aims : To review current challenges in cystic fibrosis management across diverse patient populations and healthcare settings, with a focus on unmet clinical needs, access to care, and strategies for delivering equitable and effective care globally Chairs : Andrea Gramegna (Milano (MI), Italy), Chair to be confirmed 11:15 When modulators are not an option: caring for patient's outside beyond precision therapy Speaker to be confirmed 11:30 Global cystic fibrosis care -the need for a unified and equitable response Speaker to be confirmed 11:45 Discussion and Q&A All speakers CC5.1 Session 13:00 -13:45 EU project: IDERHA: How better access to lung cancer patient data can have a major impact for clinical research & patient outcome IDERHA Public Forum event Aims : Our aim is to demonstrate to clinicians how they can access, integrate and analyse health data across institutions to maximize their value for patient care and medical research by using the IDERHA data space.We will touch on the following aspects: -What types of data we work with -How data can be securely analyzed in the federated setting -Which technical and legal requirements need to be fulfilled to work within IDERHA solutions -How data security and the privacy of the patients are addressed by IDERHA -What are the benefits when working with IDERHA for data holders and data analysers A case study involving several ERS partners will be highlighted Disease(s) : Thoracic oncology Method(s) : Imaging Public health
