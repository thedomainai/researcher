# The Power of Didactic Thinking: Frameworks, Cognitive Architecture, and Computational Paradigms in Modern Pedagogy and Artificial Intelligence

**著者**: Ismail A Mageed
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 脳科学

## Abstract

Didactic thinking is the intentional and systematic management of knowledge transformation, which involves addressing structural translation of complex information for optimal cognitive assimilation. This concept goes beyond content delivery. Classical pedagogy has been the foundation of didactic thinking, but contemporary cognitive science and computational paradigms have exposed the more structural aspects of this thinking. The paper provides a thorough examination of didactic thinking, emphasizing its relevance in human cognitive structures and autonomous artificial intelligence (AI) systems. We trace the epistemological roots of didactics, operationalize its cognitive underpinnings (working memory constraints, cognitive load theory, and schema construction) and analyze its structural instantiation in machine learning through curriculum learning, prompt engineering, or chain-of-thought prompting. Moreover, we expose the open issues that hinder the widespread systematization of didactic systems, such as the conflict between structured supervision and constructivist investigation, the problem of algorithm alignment in automated education, and systemic inequalities in adaptive tutoring implementation. Our proposal is for future research to harmonize neural-symbolic architectures, investigate the neurobiological implications of structured instruction, and create cross-cultural didactic ontologies.
