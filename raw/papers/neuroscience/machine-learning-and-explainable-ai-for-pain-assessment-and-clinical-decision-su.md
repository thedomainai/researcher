# Machine Learning and Explainable AI for Pain Assessment and Clinical Decision Support

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

This doctorate investigates the application of Virtual Reality (VR), Machine Learning (ML), and Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) in pain management, pain assessment, and clinical decision support. Pain is a complex and multidimensional phenomenon that remains difficult to assess and manage effectively, particularly in vulnerable populations and chronic conditions. The overarching aim of this dissertation is to explore how immersive technologies and intelligent computational methods can enhance non-pharmacological pain management, enable objective pain assessment, and support transparent clinical decision-making.
