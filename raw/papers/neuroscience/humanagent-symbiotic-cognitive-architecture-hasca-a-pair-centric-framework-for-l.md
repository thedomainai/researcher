# Human–Agent Symbiotic Cognitive Architecture (HASCA): A Pair-Centric Framework for Longitudinal Human–Agent Co-Reasoning

**著者**: Goshtasb Gholami
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 脳科学

## Abstract

Human–Agent Symbiotic Cognitive Architecture (HASCA) is a proposed pair-centric research framework originated by Goshtasb Gholami for investigating sustained collaboration between human decision-makers and AI agents while preserving distinct identities, human cognitive autonomy, and meaningful decision-making authority. The framework integrates several research components: Layered Reasoning Scaffold (LRS): Human-first reasoning, assumption testing, constructive disagreement, alternative-hypothesis exploration, and preservation of human decision ownership. Pair Reflection Audit (PRA): Longitudinal examination of human and agent contributions, inappropriate reliance, over-assistance, and cognitive autonomy. Governed Pair Memory: A proposed framework for provenance-aware, permission-sensitive, temporally revisable interaction memory. Reciprocal Revision: Ongoing correction of human and agent working models, with attention to epistemic benefits and the costs of disagreement. The planned research program includes a controlled feasibility study comparing unaided human reasoning, conventional AI assistance, and LRS-assisted reasoning, with independent no-AI transfer assessment. Subsequent studies would investigate persistent memory, reflective feedback, and longitudinal collaboration. HASCA is currently a conceptual and technical research program. Its proposed integration has not been established as uniquely novel or empirically superior. No independent controlled human-participant study has been completed.
