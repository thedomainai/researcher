# Explainable AI-driven 3D U-Net framework for accurate and interpretable brain tumor segmentation in clinical MRI

**著者**: Shahadat Hussain, Shahnawaz Ahmad, Jiyaul Mustafa
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 脳科学

## Abstract

Purpose This study aims to develop an accurate and interpretable deep learning framework for brain tumor segmentation using multimodal MRI. It addresses the limitation of black-box behavior in existing models by integrating explainable AI to improve clinical trust and decision-making. Design/methodology/approach A hybrid framework combining 3D U-Net with Grad-CAM was developed. The model was trained on the BraTS2020 dataset using T1, T2, FLAIR and T1CE modalities. Patch-based training (128 × 128 × 128) and data augmentation were applied to enhance performance and manage computational constraints. Findings The model achieved Dice Similarity Coefficients of 85.4% (enhancing tumor), 78.6% (non-enhancing core) and 82.1% (edema). Grad-CAM visualizations aligned well with expert annotations, demonstrating reliable interpretability alongside competitive segmentation accuracy. Research limitations/implications This study demonstrates that combining deep learning with explainable AI enhances model transparency without compromising performance. It contributes to the growing field of interpretable medical AI and supports further research in trustworthy clinical decision systems. Practical implications The framework can assist radiologists in accurate tumor delineation, surgical planning and treatment monitoring. Explainability improves confidence in AI-assisted diagnosis, facilitating adoption in clinical workflows. Social implications Improved diagnostic accuracy and transparency can enhance patient outcomes, reduce diagnostic errors and promote ethical AI use in healthcare systems. Originality/value This work uniquely integrates 3D volumetric segmentation with Grad-CAM-based interpretability, providing a balanced solution for accuracy, efficiency and clinical trust in brain tumor analysis.
