# Embodied Cognition-Driven Robot Control

**著者**: Jincheng Zhang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 脳科学

## Abstract

This paper proposes a novel approach to robot control based on embodied cognition – the idea that cognition is fundamentally shaped by interaction with the environment. Current robotic control methods frequently rely on pre-programmed models and precise sensor data, often struggling with the inherent uncertainties and complexities of real-world environments. We argue that a more effective strategy involves mimicking the human experience of embodied cognition, where the body and the environment are inextricably linked through perception and action. This paper outlines a framework for building a robot control system that integrates body models, environmental models, and cognitive models, allowing the robot to learn and adapt to its surroundings through simulated embodied interaction. The core claim is that by simulating human embodied cognition, we can significantly enhance robotic movement control and environmental understanding. We present a detailed description of the system architecture, key components, and potential benefits, emphasizing the shift from purely data-driven control to a more holistic, cognition-inspired approach. The proposed method offers a promising route towards creating robots that are not just responsive, but truly intelligent and adaptable in complex, dynamic environments.
