# Cognitive Architectures for Embodied AI – Predictive Coding of Sensorimotor Experience

**著者**: Jincheng Zhang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 脳科学

## Abstract

This paper proposes a novel cognitive architecture for embodied artificial intelligence (AI) grounded in the principles of predictive coding. Current approaches to embodied AI often struggle to replicate the seamless integration of perception and action observed in humans. This architecture addresses this limitation by implementing a continuous predictive-corrective loop, mirroring the human sensory-motor system. The core mechanism involves the agent generating predictions about its sensory input and updating these predictions based on the discrepancy between predicted and actual sensory data. This process is formalized through a series of equations, establishing a framework for dynamic modeling of embodied experience. The architecture aims to bridge the gap between symbolic AI and connectionist approaches, offering a biologically plausible foundation for intelligent embodiment. Specifically, the approach utilizes a hierarchical predictive coding framework, where higher-level predictions are generated based on lower-level sensory data, and these predictions are then refined through feedback loops. The iterative process of prediction and correction leads to a continually updated internal model of the agent's body and its surrounding environment. The architecture's potential impact includes improved sensorimotor coordination, robust adaptation to novel environments, and a more human-like understanding of embodied experience.
