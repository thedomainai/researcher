# Decoupled Thinking, Learning, and Action for Continually Growing Autonomous Robots

**著者**: Bing Guo
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 脳科学

## Abstract

Thinking, learning, and action are fundamental capabilities for autonomous robots to continuously adapt and improve through long-term interaction with changing environments. However, tightly coupled architectures often make learning and action dependent on continuous reasoning, while isolated modules cannot effectively guide or constrain one another. This paper proposes a Decoupled Thinking--Learning--Action (D-TLA) framework in which thinking, learning, and action operate as independent modules while influencing one another through requirement-mediated interactions rather than direct control. Thinking can guide what and how to learn, actively acquire informative experience, and constrain unsafe actions without blocking routine behavior or background learning. Learning continuously converts experience into reusable capabilities and can further modify the robot's thinking repertoire, thinking-selection strategy, and action behavior. Action connects internal cognition to the environment and generates new experience for subsequent learning and reasoning. Experimental results show that D-TLA maintains 100% routine completion under prolonged thinking occupation, achieves 97.2% rare-transition Macro-F1 versus 70.4% for passive learning, and increases novel-task success from 13.9% to 73.9% while preserving approximately 95.8% success on known situations.
