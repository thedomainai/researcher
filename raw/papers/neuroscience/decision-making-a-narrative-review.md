# Decision-Making: A Narrative Review

**著者**: Tiffany Martini Field
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 脳科学

## Abstract

The risks that have been presented in this current literature on decision-making include elevated arousal, COVID-19, independent medical decisions, gaming disorder, and gambling addiction. Several predictors of decision-making have appeared in this current literature including reversible decisions, experiential information, social media use, and ChatGPT advice. Only two potential underlying biological mechanisms have appeared in this literature on decision-making including the influence of dopamine and activation of the temporoparietal junction. Several methodological limitations can be noted for this literature including issues regarding sampling, variable selection and missing topics like interventions.
