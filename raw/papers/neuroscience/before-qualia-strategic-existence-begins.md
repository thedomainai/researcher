# Before Qualia, Strategic Existence Begins

**著者**: Angelina Inesia-Forde
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 脳科学

## Abstract

Before evolution can select anything, a physically possible causal architecture must already exist for selection to exploit. The T4 bacteriophage, a mindless nanoscale machine whose structure executes functions that map onto both empowering and disempowering strategies in Forde's framework of social dynamics, demonstrates this principle at the nanoscale. The strategies mapped include security, knowledge, hope, fairness, unity, and dignity, alongside their darker counterparts: nativism, hierarchy, misinformation, subjugation, dehumanization, and fear. Each concept is paired with a specific biological mechanism, virion stability, irreversible adsorption, superinfection exclusion, among others, showing that the mapping is grounded in observable structure. These mappings do not reify nonhuman organisms or attribute human qualities to mindless machines. They are structural descriptions: the concepts name functions that the architecture performs, not feelings that the organism experiences. This reframes a persistent gap in philosophy of mind: qualia have long been debated while the physical architecture that must bound it has been neglected. The paper argues that power and dignity are surface forms of a deeper current—the pursuit of a pathway to existence—and that this survival architecture appears even where no mind exists to experience it. The paper proposes a conceptual framework, using a virus as its test. It is not a theory of viral cognition. It is a work of philosophy: its claims are structural and interpretive, supported by biological evidence, not empirical results. It argues that strategy does not begin with a mind. It begins with architecture. Strategic existence is the genus, and qualia is a species: a system cannot have qualia without the architecture that makes strategic action possible, but it can have that architecture without qualia, as the T4 bacteriophage demonstrates. Before qualia, strategic existence begins.
