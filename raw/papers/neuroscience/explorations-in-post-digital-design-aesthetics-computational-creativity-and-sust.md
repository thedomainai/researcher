## Abstract

"Explorations in Post-Digital Design: Aesthetics, Computational Creativity, and Sustainability" critically explores how post-digital conditions are transforming the ways we create, curate, and design. This book foregrounds critical, experimental, and socially engaged practices that leverage digital technologies not simply for innovation, but for impact — aligning with principles of “design for good.” Covering topics such as authorship and creative agency, non-fungible tokens (NFTs), and sustainable design futures, this book is an excellent academic resource for graduate and doctoral students, educators, and professionals working across design, technology, curation, sustainability, and more.
