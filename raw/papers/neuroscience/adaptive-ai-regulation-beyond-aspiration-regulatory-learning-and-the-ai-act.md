# Adaptive AI Regulation Beyond Aspiration: Regulatory Learning and the AI Act

**著者**: Charuni Attanayake, Arto Lanamäki, Karin Väyrynen
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 脳科学

## Abstract

Information Systems (IS) research has predominantly treated regulation as stable and top-down: rules are set, and actors comply. This framing is inadequate for governing AI. Rapid technological change outpaces regulatory updates, rendering frameworks obsolete. Adaptive regulation addresses this by designing frameworks that learn from the actors they govern. The central mechanism is regulatory learning: generating and integrating evidence from practice to trigger updates. Drawing on the AI Act, where adaptive ambitions are explicitly codified in law, we show that the Act's adaptive infrastructure remains largely incomplete. The metrics, knowledge infrastructures, and coordination required to make regulatory learning operational do not yet exist. This gap opens three paths for IS research: (1) designing knowledge infrastructures that support regulatory learning; (2) empirically studying regulatory learning under ambiguity and resource constraints; and (3) engaging in IS policy research.
