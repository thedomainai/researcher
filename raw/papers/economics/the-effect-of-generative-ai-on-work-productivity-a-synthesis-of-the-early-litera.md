## Abstract

This chapter examines the emerging evidence whether and how generative AI (GenAI) affects work productivity. It focuses on careful empirical identification, task-level mechanisms, and the challenges of aggregating micro-level effects into macro outcomes. GenAI technologies including large language models and diffusion models have moved rapidly from research labs into everyday economic activity. GenAI appears capable of raising productivity across a broad range of tasks, consistent with its characterization as a general-purpose technology. Automation, augmentation, and early evidence that generative AI boosts task productivity. Granted, the literature reveals a range of dimensions of heterogeneity in productivity gains. As models improve and the frontier of AI capability shifts, sustained empirical attention across a broader range of industries, geographies, and worker populations will be essential for building a fuller understanding of GenAI's role in economic life.
