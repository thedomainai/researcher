# AI-Augmented Agile Teams: A Research Agenda and Summary of the Human-AI Collaboration Workshop

**著者**: Nils Brede Moe, Viktoria Gulliksen Stray
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経済学

## Abstract

Abstract Agile teams are rapidly adopting AI agents in everyday work, reporting increased productivity and faster feedback cycles. Simultaneously, the integration of AI raises new socio-technical issues concerning coordination, trust, roles, quality, and governance. To better understand these challenges, we organized the first International Human–AI Collaboration in Agile Teams workshop at XP 2026, with more than 40 participants from academia and industry. This paper reports on the outcomes of the workshop, synthesizing insights from interactive group discussions and collaborative analysis. The findings highlight how AI reshapes agile teamwork and workflows, introducing new human–AI collaboration patterns, shifting skills and roles, and raising challenges related to leadership, governance, and value assessment. A key emerging concept is cognitive debt, referring to the gradual loss or weakening of shared understanding in teams relying on AI-generated outputs. Based on these findings, we present a research agenda structured around eight key themes, including teamwork, workflows, autonomy, skills, value measurement, and adoption practices. The results emphasize that integrating AI into agile software development teams is not only a technical challenge but a socio-technical transformation requiring new practices, competencies, and organizational support.
