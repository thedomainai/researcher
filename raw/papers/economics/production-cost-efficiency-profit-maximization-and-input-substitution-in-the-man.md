# Production Cost Efficiency, Profit Maximization, and Input Substitution in the Manufacturing Industry: A Narrative Review

**著者**: Lintang Dwi Mayanti, Wuku Astuti, Bhenu Artha
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経済学

## Abstract

Global manufacturing industries face profound structural transformations driven by robotic automation, relative factor-price volatility, rising product-market markups, and stringent decarbonization mandates. Although extensive empirical and theoretical literatures have investigated production cost efficiency, profit maximization, and input substitution individually, these research streams remain largely fragmented across industrial organization, macroeconomics, and productivity econometrics. This article presents a comprehensive narrative review synthesizing foundational duality axioms and recent empirical breakthroughs published between 2014 and 2026 across Capital–Labor–Energy–Materials (KLEM) manufacturing systems. Following a structured narrative review protocol, we critically evaluate four thematic domains: the shift from Cobb–Douglas and symmetric Constant Elasticity of Substitution (CES) functions to flexible translog and task-based automation architectures; micro-versus-macro empirical estimates of input substitution elasticities (where plant-level capital–labor complementarity, 0.40 ≤ σ ≤ 0.50, coexists with higher industry reallocation elasticities, σ ≈ 0.70); stochastic frontier decompositions of technical and shadow-price allocative cost inefficiency; and the divergence between cost minimization and variable profit maximization under rising market power and financial frictions. We conclude with an integrative methodological synthesis and a future research agenda for industrial economists.
