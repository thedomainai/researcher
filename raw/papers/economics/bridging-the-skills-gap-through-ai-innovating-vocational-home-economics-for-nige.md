# BRIDGING THE SKILLS GAP THROUGH AI: INNOVATING VOCATIONAL HOME ECONOMICS FOR NIGERIA'S KNOWLEDGE ECONOMY`

## Abstract

Nigeria is currently confronted with a widening skills gap that undermines the employability of its graduates and the productivity of its economy. Vocational education, particularly Home Economics, was designed to equip learners with practical skills for self-reliance, entrepreneurship, and sustainable living. However, the discipline has become outdated due to obsolete curricula, inadequate teacher capacity, limited infrastructure, and declining student engagement. These challenges have reduced its relevance in addressing unemployment and poverty in Nigeria. This paper examines how Artificial Intelligence (AI) can be harnessed to reimagine Home Economics as a future-ready discipline aligned with Nigeria’s transition into a knowledge economy. Using Technological Determinism, Experiential Learning, and Constructivist Learning theories as conceptual lenses, the study explores how AI applications—such as virtual simulations, adaptive learning platforms, and intelligent tutoring systems—can modernize the curriculum, personalize instruction, and foster entrepreneurship. It also highlights AI’s potential to promote inclusivity through assistive technologies and strengthen digital literacy among learners. The analysis identifies systemic barriers to AI adoption, including infrastructural deficits, funding limitations, and cultural perceptions of vocational education. In response, a multi- pronged strategic framework is proposed, emphasizing curriculum redesign, teacher professionalization, infrastructure development, industry partnerships, and phased implementation. The expected impacts include improved student outcomes, enhanced teacher effectiveness, job creation, gender inclusivity, and alignment with Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) 4, 5, and 8. The study concludes that integrating AI into vocational Home Economics is not merely an educational innovation but a strategic necessity for Nigeria’s sustainable development. By equipping students with entrepreneurial, digital, and practical skills, AI-enhanced Home Economics can bridge the skills gap, reduce unemployment, and position Nigeria as a competitive player in the global knowledge economy. Keywords: Artificial Intelligence, Vocational Education, Home Economics, Knowledge Economy, Skills Gap, Nigeria, Sustainable Development
