# Artificial Intelligence (AI) In Commerce and Business Development

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has become a powerful force driving innovation and transformation in commerce and business development. By integrating advanced technologies such as machine learning, predictive analytics, natural language processing, and automation, organizations are improving operational efficiency, enhancing customer experiences, and strengthening strategic decision-making. AI applications are increasingly utilized in customer relationship management, marketing and sales, e-commerce, supply chain management, financial services, human resource management, and business intelligence. These applications enable businesses to analyze large volumes of data, predict market trends, personalize services, and optimize resource utilization. The study examines the concept of AI in commerce, reviews relevant literature, and highlights its major applications, benefits, challenges, and future trends. While AI offers significant advantages in terms of productivity, innovation, scalability, and competitive advantage, organizations also face challenges related to data privacy, ethical concerns, skill shortages, regulatory compliance, and workforce displacement. The findings suggest that responsible and strategic adoption of AI can contribute substantially to sustainable business growth and economic development. As AI technologies continue to evolve, they are expected to play an increasingly central role in shaping the future of commerce and business ecosystems.
