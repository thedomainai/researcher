# Fair Use Boundaries for AI Training Data: A Comparative Study of Chinese and U.S. Copyright Law

**著者**: Xurong Sun
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経済学

## Abstract

With the advent of generative artificial intelligence, there has been a significant amount of legal limbo surrounding the use of copyrighted material for training purposes. This paper explores the parameters of acceptable AI training as outlined by both Chinese and U.S. copyright law by analyzing recent court rulings. It highlights three situations: Kadrey v. Meta Platforms, Inc. and Bartz v. Anthropic PBC in the United States, and the Ultraman AIGC platform case in China. The method used is doctrinal legal analysis, comparative legal analysis and case study. It claims that the two countries have different institutional approaches to the training of AI: the United States with the flexible fair use doctrine of 17 U. S. C. § 107, China with the enumerated limitations and general conditions of Article 24 of the Copyright Law. However, it can be challenging for both systems to adapt the market impacts of AI to copyrightable standards. Judgements on the competition of AI-generated output or AI services to the market for original copyrighted work(s) are scarce. This paper does not suggest a new legal test, but rather that the test of market substitution should be given more explicit and structured consideration in the existing copyright doctrines. This may help create more consistency in analysis and allow for more flexibility in technology innovation.
