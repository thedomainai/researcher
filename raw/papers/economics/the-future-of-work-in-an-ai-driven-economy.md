# The Future of Work in an AI-Driven Economy

**著者**: Lata Shrikant Koparde
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経済学

## Abstract

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is increasingly recognized as a general-purpose technology with far-reaching implications for economic systems and labour markets. Its growing integration into production and service sectors is reshaping how work is organized and how skills are valued. On one hand, AI enables the automation of repetitive and rule-based activities; on the other, it facilitates the creation of new occupations and enhances human productivity. This dual dynamic introduces significant concerns, including occupational displacement, widening skill gaps, and unequal distribution of economic gains. Existing evidence suggests that without targeted interventions, the benefits of AI may disproportionately accrue to capital-intensive sectors and highly skilled workers (Brynjolfsson & McAfee, 2014; McKinsey Global Institute, 2023). Addressing these challenges requires a policy framework cantered on inclusive growth, continuous skill development, and responsible governance. This paper examines the evolving relationship between AI and employment, with particular emphasis on skill transformation, inequality, and institutional responses.
