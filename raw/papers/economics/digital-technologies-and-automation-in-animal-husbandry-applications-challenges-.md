# Digital Technologies and Automation in Animal Husbandry: Applications, Challenges, and Future Trends

**著者**: Pinqiao Huang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経済学

## Abstract

This study aims to examine how the application affects the animal husbandry industry across three specific areas: production, management and sales. This study discusses the development of animal husbandry. It divides it into 5 stages: learning to tame animals, developing techniques, achieving large-scale, high-density animal husbandry, the initial use of computers, and the current situation. By efficiently collecting and processing data, computers help farmers breed and manage animals and sell livestock. It can be shown that the use of computers and automation in animal husbandry has already become a trend. In the future, AI can also be used in this industry. However, the high cost of the device limited its widespread dissemination in order to build modern automated farms. Plus, most current research on AI-based precision livestock farming is applicable only to laboratory simulation environments. New technological development is necessary in the AI industry to enable AI to be used in complex real-world contexts.
