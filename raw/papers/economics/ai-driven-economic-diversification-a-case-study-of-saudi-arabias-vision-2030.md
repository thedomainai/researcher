# AI-Driven Economic Diversification: A Case Study of Saudi Arabia’s Vision 2030

**著者**: Kamal Tasiu Abdullahi
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経済学

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence is playing a critical part in Saudi Arabia’s transition from an oil-based to a knowledge-based economy under Vision 2030. AI is revolutionizing industries such as manufacturing, logistics, healthcare, and finance, making it a vital driver of innovation, productivity, and employment. This study investigates national strategies, investment objectives, and institutional reforms that facilitate AI integration, employing a qualitative policy analysis of secondary data through comparative and thematic evaluation of government reports, international publications, and academic literature. Preliminary results indicate that AI, adoption, including generative AI, is already improving sectoral efficiency, process optimization, and knowledge-based decision-making. The study also identifies implementation issues, such as regulatory preparation and workforce adaptation, and makes concrete recommendations to improve AI-driven development and maintain long-term economic resilience.
