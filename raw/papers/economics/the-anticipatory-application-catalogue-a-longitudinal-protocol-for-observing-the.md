# The Anticipatory Application Catalogue: A Longitudinal Protocol for Observing the Generativity of a Technological Primitive

**著者**: Sébastien FAVRE-LECCA
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経済学

## Abstract

This paper introduces the concept of the Anticipatory Application Catalogue as a longitudinal instrument for observing the generativity of a technological primitive. Rather than retrospectively identifying a technology as generative once an ecosystem has already emerged around it, the proposed approach consists of publicly mapping, at a given point in time, the functions and applications considered derivable from a common technological core, distinguishing their actual level of maturity, and then tracking how this application space evolves. The protocol is deliberately simple: anticipated applications are dated, documented, and compared over time with those that are actually implemented, abandoned, transformed, or unexpectedly discovered. This makes it possible to observe whether functional diversity progressively emerges while the underlying technological core remains relatively stable. The paper positions this proposal in relation to existing work on technological generativity, General Purpose Technologies, and platform ecosystems, while distinguishing the Anticipatory Application Catalogue by its prospective and longitudinal character. NeoMundi is presented as the first experimental instantiation of the protocol, using a common runtime measurement layer for AI systems and observing the diversity of downstream functions that may emerge from it. This Version 0.1 establishes the initial public state of the catalogue and the associated longitudinal protocol. Future versions may document new applications, external implementations, failed derivations, unanticipated uses, and changes in the underlying technological core.
