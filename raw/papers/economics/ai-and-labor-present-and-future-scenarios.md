## Abstract

This chapter reviews current evidence on the labor market impacts of artificial intelligence (AI) and provides guidance for thinking about future impacts. It discusses what AI exposure means, how to measure it, and how it may change going forward. The effects of AI on jobs can be complex. Projecting future impacts is even more difficult because it depends on factors like growth in AI capabilities and how quickly humans adjust. A useful rule of thumb is to think of job impacts in terms of three key ideas: scale effects, automation, and augmentation. These three factors are a useful rule of thumb for thinking about the effect of technology on work. There are many challenges to predicting the effects of AI on the labor market. New data collection methods may allow for closer to real-time measures of changes in tasks, which have broad uses beyond measuring AI exposure.
