# Beyond the average association between artificial intelligence and sales performance

**著者**: Cüneyd İkbal SARIOĞLU, Chané  of de Bruyn, Rıdvan Karacan, Eda Yalçın Kayacan, Sema Yılmaz Genç
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経済学

## Abstract

The assumption that AI-driven marketing technologies uniformly improve sales performance remains insufficiently tested. Using an exploratory synthetic firm-level dataset (n = 50), this study estimates OLS and quantile regressions at tau = 0.10, 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, and 0.90, with 2000-replication pairs-bootstrap inference. A data-integrity audit showed that marketing efficiency and revenue per salesperson are deterministic functions of revenue growth and were therefore excluded from the inferential models to prevent target leakage. In the corrected specification, automated lead generation is negatively associated with revenue growth in OLS and at Q75 and Q90, with 95% bootstrap confidence intervals excluding zero. AI usage, marketing budget, sales-team size, and personalized campaigns have wide bootstrap intervals that include zero at every reported quantile. Huber and ridge estimates preserve the OLS coefficient signs but differ in magnitude, underscoring the exploratory nature of the results. The study contributes a cautious, distribution-sensitive assessment and identifies priorities for validation with larger, real-world data.
