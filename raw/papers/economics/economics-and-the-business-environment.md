# ECONOMICS AND THE BUSINESS ENVIRONMENT

**著者**: Sixbert Sangwa, Placide Mutabazi
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経済学

## Abstract

Economic decisions made within an enterprise are constrained by markets, resources, institutions and macroeconomic conditions that no individual manager controls. This chapter establishes a critical and applied foundation for interpreting those forces without mistaking abstract equilibrium models for comprehensive accounts of economic reality. It connects scarcity and marginal choice to demand, supply, production, market power, welfare and policy, then examines growth, inflation, employment, monetary conditions, fiscal choices and institutional development. Particular attention is given to the transmission pathways linking economic indicators to pricing, cash flow, capacity, wages, procurement and investment. A purposive synthesis of foundational scholarship and official secondary data is illustrated with Rwanda’s 2026 national accounts, consumer prices and labour statistics, alongside the 2024 enterprise and financial-inclusion surveys. The chapter integrates competing neoclassical, Keynesian, institutional, structuralist and behavioural interpretations, noting their identifying assumptions and the contextual limits of transferring results across economies. Original analytical tools include a macro-to-firm transmission map, market-sensitivity calculations, an indicator-response matrix and a scenario-planning instrument. Numerical business cases are expressly hypothetical and reproducible. The central conclusion is that economically defensible management requires triangulating market signals, institutional feasibility, distributional consequences and uncertainty, rather than reducing decisions to forecasts or single performance ratios.
