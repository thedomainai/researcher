# The Impact of Artificial Intelligence Technology Applications on the Employment Structure of the Labor Force

**著者**: Haoyiran Wang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経済学

## Abstract

Advancements in artificial intelligence (AI) have occurred alongside digitization, big data, and computing power and have significantly impacted how the global labor market works. This paper examines the effects of AI applications on the labor market and the structure of the workforce. The main method of research is literature review; academic articles on the implementation of AI and its effects on the workforce are reviewed and analyzed to understand skill-biased and task-biased technological changes along with displacement, reinforcement, and productivity effects of AI. This paper finds that AI contributes to increasing the demand for highly skilled workers who would be able to complement intelligent systems while reducing the demand for simple professions and jobs that can be automated. However, the overall impact on employment is weak so far due to the offset of job losses resulting from automation by the emergence of new job positions at more productive companies using AI.
