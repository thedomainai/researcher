# Teaching Economics in the 21st Century in Developing Countries: Challenges, Opportunities, and Solutions

**著者**: CHEKWUBE UDEH PRECIOUS
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経済学

## Abstract

Economics education in developing countries faces a critical juncture as digital transformation, labour-market change, and demands for critical thinking reshape what and how the subject should be taught. This paper examines the challenges, opportunities, and solutions associated with teaching economics in the 21st century, drawing on a structured survey dataset of 10,000 educators, learners, and administrators across ten African countries (Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana, Uganda, Tanzania, South Africa, Zambia, Rwanda, Zimbabwe, and Ethiopia). Descriptive analysis shows that respondents rate infrastructural and pedagogical constraints—unreliable internet, limited digital training, and examination-focused instruction—as persistent barriers, while digital platforms, artificial intelligence, and locally grounded case studies are viewed favourably as opportunities. Respondents converge strongly on solutions centred on continuous teacher professional development, infrastructure investment, and curriculum revision. The paper argues that bridging the gap between potential and practice requires coordinated policy, institutional, and pedagogical action tailored to local realities.
