# The concept of financial models for scaling artificial intelligence systems for development and security

**著者**: Ю.Г. Лещенко, Mikhail Yu. Lev, Marina Medvedeva
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経済学

## Abstract

Subject. The concept of financial models for scaling artificial intelligence (AI) systems in the context of development and security. Objectives. Suggest and justify new financial models for scaling AI systems that provide companies and organizations with safe development and effective investment in AI technologies. Methods. An interdisciplinary approach is applied, combining elements of risk management theory, AI economics, and security studies. Results. The challenges and risks associated with the process of scaling AI systems are detailed. The Comprehensive System for Secure Scaling of AI Systems is proposed and argued. The specific features of financial planning for the secure scaling of AI systems are substantiated. Based on existing financial mechanisms, alternative models of this process are proposed. Methods for evaluating the efficiency of investments in this process are identified and grouped by functional criterion. Conclusions and Relevance. The concept of financial models for the secure scaling of AI systems, as a comprehensive and multi-level approach, is essential for expanding the practical capabilities of AI and guaranteeing its security, ethics, and sustainability. The proposed security system encompasses risk management, continuous monitoring, and adaptation of advanced technologies. Evaluating the efficiency of investments in the secure scaling of AI systems goes beyond traditional financial metrics and therefore requires consideration of quantitative and qualitative aspects, economic benefits, innovation potential, competitiveness, pricing, and ethical factors. The results can be applied in managing resources and capital when scaling AI platforms, assessing related financial risks, ensuring security, as well as in developing regulatory frameworks and attracting investments.
