## Abstract

Narrative Capitalism, Algorithmic Governance and Psychic Integrity investigates one of the most consequential transformations of the twenty-first century: the conversion of human imagination, emotion, and attention into monetizable assets within the digital economy. Drawing upon contemporary cultural phenomena such as Dark Romance, Romantasy, Mafia Romance, Fantasy, LitRPG, True Crime, Self-Help, Mental Health content, and conversational artificial intelligence systems like ChatGPT, this book examines how digital platforms, recommendation algorithms, and generative AI increasingly shape human behavior, identity, and decision-making. Combining insights from law, political philosophy, psychology, neuroscience, economics, and technology studies, Northon Salomão de Oliveira demonstrates how modern platform architectures employ advanced behavioral profiling, algorithmic recommendation systems, emotional targeting, and predictive analytics to influence cognitive processes on an unprecedented scale. Through a comparative analysis of regulatory frameworks in the United States, the European Union, the United Kingdom, Brazil, Australia, and South Korea, the work explores critical issues including Artificial Intelligence, Algorithmic Governance, Digital Privacy, Cognitive Liberty, NeuroRights, Mental Health, Consumer Protection, Attention Economy, Surveillance Capitalism, Platform Regulation, and Digital Rights. The book argues that traditional legal concepts of autonomy, consent, and consumer choice are increasingly inadequate in an era where algorithms continuously learn, predict, and influence human behavior. In response, it proposes a groundbreaking regulatory framework centered on the protection of Psychic Integrity, establishing new legal safeguards for cognitive freedom, psychological autonomy, and human dignity in the age of artificial intelligence.
