# A Cluster Study on the Translation of Teaching Functions in the Generative Artificial Intelligence Tool Ecosystem

**著者**: Na Zhao
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経済学

## Abstract

As generative artificial intelligence enters the field of university teaching, most existing studies explain its educational significance from the perspectives of platform differences, task structures or risk classifications, while rarely examining how the technology is named, and paying little attention to the connection between such references and the positioning of teaching functions. This paper uses de-identified public page semantic data from six platforms provided by the research team. From 32,500 main corpus entries, the study identifies 17,380 records that contain both teaching tasks and artificial intelligence function expressions. Based on the specificity of lexical items, four mutually exclusive reference modes are established: situational, conceptual, role-based and product-based. Methods including equal-weight description, 2000-round platform-stratified cluster bootstrap, and cluster generalized estimating equations are adopted to control for platforms, five types of teaching tasks, and text length. The results show a stable positive correlation between explicit technology references and teaching assistance expressions. The adjusted odds ratios of conceptual, role-based and product-based modes are 4.622, 6.014 and 5.927 respectively. These three types of explicit references are negatively correlated with task substitution, efficiency saving and process automation. The correlation between conceptual references and content generation is sensitive to single lexical item restrictions. On this basis, the study proposes an industry-education integration curriculum path, which includes tool identification, competency audit, real task translation, process evidence design, dual-tutor review, and curriculum iteration.
