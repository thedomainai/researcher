# Democracy Without Borders? Global Citizenship Between Platform Power, AI and the Crisis of Knowledge

## Abstract

Is the idea of global democracy over? Or has the time now come for more realism, following the democratic gains of recent decades? Ten years after Ingolf Pernice's visionary speech at the Congreso del Futuro in Santiago de Chile, it is worth looking back, and above all forward. What became of the idea of governing the 21st century democratically? And what new questions do platform economics, artificial intelligence and the role of knowledge raise for democracy today?
