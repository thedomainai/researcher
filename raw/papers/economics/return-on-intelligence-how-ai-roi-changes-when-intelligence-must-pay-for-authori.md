# Return on Intelligence: How AI ROI Changes When Intelligence Must Pay for Authority, Risk, Attention, and Recovery

**著者**: Rogério Figurelli
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経済学

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence is increasingly evaluated through the language of ROI. Yet most AI ROI discussions still inherit a narrow investment grammar: model cost, platform spend, seat licenses, token usage, automation volume, saved hours, productivity claims, and dashboard activity. These measures are necessary, but they are not sufficient when intelligence becomes persistent, agentic, tool-using, decision-shaping, and action-capable. This paper proposes Return on Intelligence as a broader field-economic architecture for AI ROI. The central thesis is that artificial intelligence produces real return only after its outputs survive the path from capability to accepted value, including the costs of evidence, integration, supervision, correction, exception handling, authority, risk, attention, governance, recovery, trust, and consequence. The paper synthesizes the AI ROI series, including Field Economics, AI Economy, AI ROI Is Not a Dashboard, The Real Cost of AI Agents, and The Autonomy Budget, and translates their shared thesis into one pre-publication research architecture. It introduces the Intelligence Return Unit, Work Path Cost, Full Intelligence Cost, the Autonomy Budget, the Return on Intelligence formula, and the ROI Gate. The paper argues that generated output is not accepted value, capability is not permission, and cheap autonomy is not profitable autonomy until the full cost of making intelligence admissible has been charged to the system that created it. The contribution is conceptual and architectural. It does not claim to provide a universal financial accounting standard, a regulatory compliance framework, or investment advice. It proposes a bounded research and management object for distinguishing apparent AI productivity from intelligence that returns as governed, recoverable, accountable value.
