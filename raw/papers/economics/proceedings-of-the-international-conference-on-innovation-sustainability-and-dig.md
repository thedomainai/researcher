## Abstract

The International Conference on Innovation, Sustainability and Digital Transformation in Education, Science, Economics, Medicine, and Artificial Intelligence is an interdisciplinary academic event dedicated to exploring innovative approaches, emerging technologies, sustainable development strategies, and digital transformation across key areas of contemporary society. The conference provides an international platform for researchers, academics, educators, scientists, healthcare professionals, economists, and technology experts to present original research, exchange scientific perspectives, and discuss practical solutions to current global challenges. The main areas of discussion include educational innovation and digital learning, scientific discoveries and technological development, sustainable economic systems, medical innovation and digital healthcare, and the responsible application of artificial intelligence. The conference aims to strengthen interdisciplinary collaboration, promote research excellence, and encourage the development of sustainable, evidence-based solutions through international scientific cooperation.
