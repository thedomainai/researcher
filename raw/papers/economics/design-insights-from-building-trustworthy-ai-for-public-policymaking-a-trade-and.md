# Design Insights From Building Trustworthy Ai For Public Policymaking: A Trade And Nutrition Project

**著者**: Angelina Chen, Raffaele Ciriello, Anne-Marie Thow, Sabrina Chakori, Kelly Garton
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経済学

## Abstract

Governments are increasingly adopting AI to support public services, yet general-purpose tools introduce risks such as unverifiable sources, limited transparency, and governance outside institutional control.This paper draws on an industry-academia project that built a domain-specific AI knowledge platform for policymakers working at the intersection of trade and nutrition.The goal was not a perfect product but replicable protocols and practical knowledge about what trustworthy public sector AI requires.Two prototypes were developed through iterative co-design with public health experts, IS scholars, and policymakers across four countries, within four months and under tight budget constraints.Three insights emerged: trustworthiness must be built into system architecture from the outset; real-world constraints can produce more equitable and sustainable design decisions; and documenting design rationale is itself an accountability requirement.
