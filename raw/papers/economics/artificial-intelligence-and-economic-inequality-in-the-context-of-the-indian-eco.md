# Artificial Intelligence and Economic Inequality in the Context of the Indian Economy: A Sectoral and Institutional Analysis

**著者**: Christopher Manohar
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経済学

## Abstract

The rapid proliferation of Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies presents a paradoxical challenge for emerging economies. While AI promises productivity gains and innovation-led growth, it also poses significant risks of exacerbating structural economic inequalities. This paper investigates the dual impact of AI on economic inequality within the distinctive framework of the Indian economy, characterized by a dualistic labor market, a dominant informal sector, and a nascent welfare state. Drawing on task-based models of automation and institutional economics, we analyze three transmission channels: labor market polarization, capital-skill complementarity, and digital infrastructure asymmetries. The study finds that while India’s IT services sector stands to gain from AI complementarities, the majority of its workforce—particularly in agriculture, manufacturing, and informal services—faces wage suppression and job displacement risks without adequate institutional buffers. We conclude by proposing a policy framework combining universal basic services, AI-specific reskilling, and progressive data taxation to mitigate rising inequality.
