# Patraraksha AI: An Offline-Enabled, Illumination-Adaptive Yolov8 Framework for Crop Disease Detection and Intelligent Treatment Guidance

**著者**: Apurba Nandi, Sai Prasanna Arutla, Lalith Naga Vaishnav G, Ramana P
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経済学

## Abstract

Crop diseases significantly affect agricultural productivity and can lead to considerable economic losses when not identified at an early stage. To address this challenge, this paper presents PatraRaksha AI, an offline-enabled intelligent framework designed for crop disease detection and treatment guidance using artificial intelligence. The system employs the YOLOv8 object detection model to analyse uploaded leaf images and classify crop conditions into three categories: Healthy, Powdery Mildew, and Rust. A detection-level weighted-voting mechanism is incorporated to determine the final disease classification based on the model's detections. The framework operates locally, enabling disease identification without continuous internet connectivity. In addition to disease detection, it provides information about possible causes and severity, along with multilingual home-remedy recommendations to support farmers in understanding appropriate treatment options. An area-based dosage calculator is also integrated to estimate treatment quantities according to the specified land area. The system uses a FastAPI backend for image processing and prediction handling, while OpenCV and Pillow support image processing and the generation of annotated prediction outputs. By integrating offline disease diagnosis with multilingual and treatment-oriented agricultural assistance, PatraRaksha AI aims to provide an accessible decision-support solution for farmers and promote timely crop-health management. A separately developed illumination enhancement technique is also considered as a proposed extension to improve image visibility under varying lighting conditions, with integration and performance evaluation planned for future work.
