# The Invisible Architecture of Social Media

**著者**: Hüseyin Okan Durmuş
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 経済学

## Abstract

This paper explores the invisible architecture that shapes modern social media platforms and examines how digital environments influence individual behaviour, collective thinking, and societal development. Rather than viewing social media merely as communication technology, it analyses the underlying roles of algorithms, attention economics, recommendation systems, network effects, artificial intelligence, and platform design in determining what people see, believe, and share. The paper discusses how these invisible mechanisms affect public opinion, collective intelligence, information quality, social trust, political polarization, and democratic processes. It argues that understanding the architecture of social media is essential for building healthier digital ecosystems and ensuring that technology strengthens rather than weakens human knowledge, cooperation, and social resilience.
