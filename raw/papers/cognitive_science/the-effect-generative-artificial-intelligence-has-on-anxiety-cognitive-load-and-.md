# THE EFFECT GENERATIVE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE HAS ON ANXIETY, COGNITIVE LOAD, AND LEARNING

**著者**: Eldred Bryan Jones
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

Generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) has rapidly become integrated into educational settings, yet its effects on learning, cognitive processes, and psychophysiological functioning remain poorly understood. The present study proposes an experimental investigation comparing GenAI-assisted learning with traditional textbook-based learning. Participants will be randomly assigned to use either GenAI or a textbook while completing learning tasks followed by assessments of learning outcomes. Primary outcomes include task accuracy, delayed recall, perceived cognitive load, state anxiety, and physiological indices of stress, including blood pressure and pulse rate variability. Learning performance will be evaluated across multiple levels of Bloom's Taxonomy to examine whether the effects of GenAI vary as a function of cognitive complexity. We hypothesize that GenAI will produce comparable accuracy to textbook learning but poorer delayed recall. The predicted effects GenAI has on accuracy, and recall will be moderated by Bloom’s level in which higher level questions will correspond to poorer accuracy and recall. We further predict that participants using GenAI will report lower cognitive load and state anxiety and exhibit lower blood pressure and higher pulse rate variability than participants using traditional textbooks. In addition, associations among learning outcomes, cognitive load, anxiety, and physiological measures will be examined. By integrating behavioral, psychological, and psychophysiological measures within a preregistered experimental design, this study aims to provide a comprehensive evaluation of how GenAI influences learning and the learner experience.
