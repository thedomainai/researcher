# Adoption Of Artificial Intelligence And Machine Learning Into The Criminal Justice System: An Overview

**著者**: Prashant Prabhakar Mulay
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

Over the course of the last several years, artificial intelligence (AI) has become the focus of a significant amount of discussion, especially in relation to the breadth and depth of its possible applications across a variety of sectors. Indian judicial system is facing huge pendency of the cases in different courts. Legal system is encouraged with the results of the broad application of AI ecosystem technologies which are helping to reduce the pile of pending cases by providing necessary assistance to the human resource working in judicial system. Integration of ICT is playing an important role the Indian legal system. Apart from the adoption of ICT in day-to-day work, the incorporation of AI into the judicial system at various levels will undoubtedly improve the efficiency of service delivery. Sensing this situation, the Vidhi Institute for Legal Policy in collaboration with TCG-Crest have published an independent research study analyzing the possible influence and implications that AI can have on the judicial system. This report has provided a larger framework, which addresses both immediate and prospective challenges and it also proposes different solutions that to be implemented to address the issues faced by the judicial system. The ultimate conclusion of the discussion is that the artificial intelligence (AI) may act as a driving force to revolutionize the entire system.
