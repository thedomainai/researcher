## Abstract

A Hypothetical approach to distinguish objective from subjective issues with uses of AI, which will change with complexity. Goal of this study: Identify fears about AI interference with set systems, like work. Identify changes in systems as AI advancements. Identify and distinguish observed versus assumed problems with use of AI Observe issues as a result of using sophisticated AI-related tools (post-grammar, pro-writing aid, quilbot) as an example of writing changed because of AI. Results: Findings are attached in the point slide below. Each question and set of (multiple choice, likes scales, and check all that apply) probable answers is displayed as a chart from Google Sheets. Responses were given in surveys in Google forms. These were distributed in online alcohol media groups and community forums. The number of respondents was 46. Parameters: Q1-3 Demographic questions Q4 - losing career perception Q5 - challenges to established systems due to AI Q6 - systems impacted by AI advances and growing/diversifying usage Q7 - impact on human cognition and critical thinking Q8 - relationship to value - authentity Q9 relationship to value - privacy Q10 - relationship to vice - theft identity, creativity, etc Q11 - valid obstacles in interacting with AI advancement Limitations: Aggregated Data Only Exploratory Research Results from pilot case studies Designed to show evolution of thought processes This survey is not intended to test a hypothesis.
