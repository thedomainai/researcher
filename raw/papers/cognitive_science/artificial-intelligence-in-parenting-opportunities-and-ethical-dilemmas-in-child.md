# ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN PARENTING: OPPORTUNITIES AND ETHICAL DILEMMAS IN CHILD DEVELOPMENT

**著者**: Angela Osas Isibor
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

This study examines the growing integration of artificial intelligence (AI) into parenting and its implications for child development, family dynamics, and ethical responsibility. AI technologies—ranging from adaptive learning platforms and smart home devices to health monitoring systems and parenting applications—are transforming traditional child-rearing practices into a hybrid model of human–machine interaction. The paper highlights key opportunities, including personalized learning, early detection of developmental challenges, enhanced safety monitoring, and real-time parental support. These innovations can improve cognitive outcomes, increase accessibility to educational and health resources, and assist parents in making informed decisions. However, the study also critically explores significant ethical dilemmas associated with AI-driven parenting. Issues such as data privacy, algorithmic bias, inequality, over-reliance on technology, and reduced parent–child interaction pose risks to children’s autonomy, social development, and emotional well-being. Continuous data collection and surveillance further complicate concerns about consent and children’s rights in digital environments. The paper emphasizes the need for balanced and responsible AI integration, where technology complements rather than replaces human engagement. It underscores the importance of robust regulatory frameworks, transparency in AI systems, inclusive design, and active parental oversight. Ultimately, the study concludes that while AI holds transformative potential for enhancing parenting and child development, its benefits can only be fully realized through ethical deployment, safeguarding children’s rights, and maintaining the central role of human relationships in nurturing development. Keywords: Parenting, opportunities, ethical dilemmas, child development
