# Cognitive Resonance Networks (CRN) for Algorithmic Bias Mitigation

**著者**: Jincheng Zhang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

This paper introduces Cognitive Resonance Networks (CRN) – a novel distributed network architecture designed to mitigate algorithmic bias in machine learning models. Current bias mitigation strategies often rely on simplistic statistical measures, failing to capture the complex interplay of internal representations. CRN utilizes a dynamic feedback mechanism predicated on the concept of 'cognitive resonance' – the similarity of internal states across data points – to proactively adjust network activation patterns, fostering balanced representation and reducing bias. This paper details the CRN's architecture, the core mechanism involving similarity analysis and state adjustment, and preliminary experimental results demonstrating its effectiveness in mitigating bias across a range of datasets.
