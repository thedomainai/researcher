# Artificial Intelligence and Learning in High School Education: A Literature Review

**著者**: Yudan Zhao
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence has recently become an important part of high school learning, which facilitates the learning process by providing students with viable alternative learning sources, feedback, and news. Today, the issue of the impact of artificial intelligence-based learning units, along with rapidly evolving learning-unit systems and content, on adolescent cognitive skills is emerging. This paper reviews literature on artificial intelligence-based high school learning, discussing the effects of these learning units on cognitive off-loading, motivation to learn, and self-regulated learning in order to bring out both the advantages and disadvantages associated with high school education. It maintains that artificial intelligence contains advantages of optimising learning and coming out with useful information when utilised as an assistant to a human being, however, the danger of human-critical thought development, built-in intrinsic motivation, and depending on the AI to provide solutions to social issues is there because of extreme reliance on artificial intelligence. In a nutshell, this review implores the stakeholders- high schools and students- to provide the requisite guidelines for AI exploration, and critical thinking development.
