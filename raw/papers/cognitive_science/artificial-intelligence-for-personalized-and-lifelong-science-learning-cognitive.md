# Artificial Intelligence for Personalized and Lifelong Science Learning: Cognitive and Pedagogical Implications

## Abstract

Chapter Highlights The chapter discusses AI in personalized and lifelong science learning, focusing on its cognitive and pedagogical implications in science education. It explains AI-supported personalized learning through adaptive systems, real-time feedback, and tailored learning pathways that improve understanding of scientific concepts. It highlights cognitive effects of AI, including cognitive load management, scaffolding, and metacognitive support for better learning of complex ideas. It discusses AI in lifelong learning through just-in-time learning, microlearning, and adaptive career pathways for continuous skill development. It outlines pedagogical implications such as flipped classrooms, inquiry-based learning, and teacher–AI collaboration to improve engagement and instruction. The chapter concludes that effective AI integration requires ethical use, teacher readiness, and balanced application to ensure meaningful and equitable science learning.
