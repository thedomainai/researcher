# Effects of artificial intelligence-based personalized learning on STEM reading comprehension

**著者**: Samsu Somadayo, Mohamad Jamil, Rohati Hi Halil
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

Science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM)-based reading comprehension is an important skill in modern education, yet many students still experience difficulties in understanding scientific concepts, technical terminology, and complex STEM texts. Although artificial intelligence (AI)-based personalized learning has been increasingly implemented in education, limited studies have explained how this approach improves STEM reading comprehension (SRC) through cognitive and self-regulation processes. Therefore, this study aims to examine the effects of AI-based personalized learning (AIPL) on SRC by investigating the mediating roles of cognitive engagement (CE) and self-regulated learning (SRL). A quantitative approach using partial least squares structural equation modeling (PLS-SEM) was employed. Data were collected through questionnaires from 250 students from several schools in Ternate City, Indonesia. The findings revealed that AIPL significantly influenced CE, SRL, and SRC. SRL was identified as the strongest factor affecting SRC. Furthermore, CE and SRL significantly mediated the relationship between AIPL and SRC. The study concludes that AIPL can effectively improve SRC by promoting CE and independent learning processes.
