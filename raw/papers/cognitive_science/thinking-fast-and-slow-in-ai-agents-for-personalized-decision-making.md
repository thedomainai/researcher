# Thinking, Fast and Slow in AI Agents for Personalized Decision-making

**著者**: Hao Frank Yang, Yang Zhao, Yichen Lin, Takeru Igusa
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

Human decisions are shaped by the interplay between fast intuitive judgments and slower deliberative reasoning, yet current AI agents rarely capture this fundamental dual-process structure in a personalized and transparent way. To ensure AI agents make decisions aligned with human values, we present DualMind. This adaptive computational framework discovers individualized decision strategies by automating the co-evolution of cognitive theories and language models. Inspired by dual-process theories of human decision-making, DualMind harmonizes numerical and textual gradients within a unified reflective loop, mirroring the psychological transition from cognitive dissonance to consonance. We show that, across six large-scale personalized choice tasks involving 5,734 individuals in transportation, job, and health choices, DualMind outperforms state-of-the-art utility theory and AI methods by 43.6% on average in predictive performance, while improving model consistency and reducing output variance by 47.7%. Furthermore, DualMind can automatically discover decision utilities, reasoning processes, and semantic preferences, making them explicit and transparent while revealing systematic demographic shifts in behavioral patterns. By unifying the precision of cognitive theories with semantic richness in human language, our framework provides a foundation for developing personalized AI decision companions and understanding the mechanisms that shape human choice.
