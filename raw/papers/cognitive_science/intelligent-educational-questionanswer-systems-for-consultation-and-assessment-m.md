# Intelligent educational question–answer systems for consultation and assessment: methods, challenges, and prospects

**著者**: Rakhila Turebayeva, Rozamgul Niyazova, Alimbubi Aktayeva, Denis Pleskachev, Zhanyl Sarsenbayeva
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

Automated assessment of open-ended responses is challenging in multilingual and low-resource educational settings because annotated corpora and language-specific semantic resources are limited. Educational question–answer systems often separate psychometric modelling from natural language processing (NLP), restricting the integration of ability estimation and semantic interpretation. This study developed and evaluated an interpretable hybrid framework that combines three-parameter item response theory (IRT) adaptive testing with contextual semantic evaluation using language embeddings and cosine similarity. The framework integrates adaptive item selection, response interpretation, ability estimation, and diagnostic feedback. KazQAD, a Kazakh question-answer dataset comprising over 12,000 annotated pairs, was developed to support this study. An evaluation involving 350 undergraduates compared fixed-form computer-based testing (CBT), standalone IRT-based adaptive testing, and the hybrid framework. Among the three approaches, the hybrid framework showed the strongest correlation with expert assessment (r=0.91), the lowest mean standard error of ability estimation (SE=0.08), and the shortest average assessment duration (23 minutes vs 42 minutes for fixed-form CBT), a 45.2% reduction. These findings support integrating psychometrically calibrated estimation with semantic interpretation within a unified adaptive workflow for Kazakh-language assessment. However, this study did not directly benchmark contemporary transformer-based semantic graders, and the findings remain specific to the evaluated conditions and do not establish universal superiority.
