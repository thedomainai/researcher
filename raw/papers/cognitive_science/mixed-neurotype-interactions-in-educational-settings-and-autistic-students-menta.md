# Mixed-Neurotype Interactions in Educational Settings and Autistic Students’ Mental Health: A Systematic Review of Qualitative Findings

**著者**: Shachar Ophir, Einat Keren, Nitsan Almog, Yonat Rum
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

Abstract Qualitative studies on social interactions between autistic and non-autistic students (Mixed-Neurotype interactions) provide rich insights but have limited generalizability. The objective of this review was to review and synthesize qualitative findings on Mixed-Neurotype interactions and autistic students’ mental health from students’ and teachers’ viewpoints. One hundred and eighteen papers were reviewed, and their findings were thematically analyzed. Themes identified: Main features (bullying, friendship, loneliness); Identity (camouflaging/disclosing autism); Support (teachers’ scaffolding, peer interventions); Key environments (Physical Education classes, online interactions, unstructured settings). The most prevalent sub-theme was loneliness. A twofold camouflaging pattern was identified: concealing autism and associated mental health outcomes. Support was described to decrease over time. Findings flag support reduction, loneliness, and camouflaging as harming autistic students’ mental health.
