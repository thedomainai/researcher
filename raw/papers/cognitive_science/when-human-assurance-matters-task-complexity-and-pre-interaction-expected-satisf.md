# When Human Assurance Matters: Task Complexity and Pre-Interaction Expected Satisfaction in Human-AI Hybrid Service Systems

**著者**: Lingyi Zhou, Xixian Peng
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

Consumers frequently disengage from AI service encounters because they doubt whether chatbots can resolve their requests. Although human–AI hybrid systems provide human backup, this capability is often concealed. Drawing on Uncertainty Reduction Theory and Cognitive Appraisal Theory, we examine when human assurance—an upfront statement that users may transfer to a human if the AI cannot resolve the request—improves pre-interaction expectations. A between-subjects online experiment (N = 217) and a within-subject EEG experiment (N = 35) show that assurance increases expected system capability and expected satisfaction, with larger effects under high task complexity. Expected system capability mediates this relationship. EEG results reveal early frontal theta synchronization followed by later frontal alpha desynchronization under high complexity, a temporal pattern consistent with affective engagement and cognitive elaboration. These findings identify human assurance as a task-contingent cue that makes hybrid systems’ recourse capability visible.
