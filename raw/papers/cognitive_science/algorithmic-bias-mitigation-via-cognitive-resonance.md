# Algorithmic Bias Mitigation via Cognitive Resonance

**著者**: Jincheng Zhang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

This research investigates the pervasive issue of algorithmic bias within complex machine learning models, focusing on the development of a novel mitigation strategy leveraging 'cognitive resonance.' We propose an algorithm that continuously evaluates the model's internal representation against a simulated cognitive landscape, encouraging a more balanced and diverse data reflection. The core mechanism centers on establishing a feedback loop that promotes a holistic understanding of the training data, ultimately reducing the impact of inherent biases. This work offers a fundamentally new approach to bias mitigation, moving beyond traditional statistical methods and embracing a cognitive perspective on model behavior.
