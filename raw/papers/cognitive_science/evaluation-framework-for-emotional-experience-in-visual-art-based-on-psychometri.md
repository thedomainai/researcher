# Evaluation Framework for Emotional Experience in Visual Art Based on Psychometric Feedback

**著者**: An Zhao
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

With the development of digital art exhibitions, intelligent display platforms, and interactive art systems, visual art experiences have gradually shifted from static viewing toward dynamic interaction based on user-state feedback. However, conventional art evaluation methods mainly rely on subjective questionnaires after viewing, which makes it difficult to capture emotional changes during the viewing process and support real-time visual optimization. To address this issue, an adaptive intervention evaluation framework for emotional experiences in visual art based on psychometric feedback is proposed. The framework integrates psychometric data, viewing behavior features, and facial behavior information to construct an emotional-state recognition model. Based on recognition results, visual factors including color, visual complexity, motion rhythm, and narrative prompts are dynamically adjusted. Sixty participants were recruited for experiments comparing static presentation and adaptive presentation conditions. The results show that the multimodal random forest model achieved superior emotional-state recognition performance, with an 85.00% accuracy in cognitive-load classification and a Macro-F1 score of 0.843. The adaptive presentation condition improved emotional valence, aesthetic engagement, immersion, and artwork understanding. This study provides a computational approach for emotional computing and personalized experience optimization in digital art environments, promoting the integration of artificial intelligence and visual art evaluation.
