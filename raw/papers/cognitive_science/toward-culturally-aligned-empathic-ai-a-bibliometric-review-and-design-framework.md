# Toward Culturally Aligned Empathic AI: A Bibliometric Review and Design Framework for Organizational Readiness

**著者**: Marcel R. Sieber, Thomas Keller
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

Empathic AI systems are increasingly integrated into organizational workflows, yet no comprehensive framework aligns their design with organizational culture. This paper presents a systematic bibliometric review of three key areas: empathic human-AI collaboration, trust calibration in automation, and the integration of organizational culture and technology. Analyzing 555 documents from an initial collection of 13,265 records across five databases, the study identifies four structural gaps: cultural fit, trust calibration, empathic interaction style, and ethical boundary conditions. These gaps inform the design requirements for a future diagnostic readiness instrument. The proposed conceptual model enables organizations to create differentiated readiness profiles by culture type rather than a single aggregate score. This paper offers a framework and a multi-phase validation roadmap as its main contribution to information systems research and practice.
