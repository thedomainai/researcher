# THINKING BEFORE PROMPTING: LINKING SPATIAL REASONING, CRITICAL THINKING AND GENERATIVE AI THROUGH THE SPARC FRAMEWORK

**著者**: Ergi BUFASI
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

Generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) produced explanations, diagrams and worked solutions in seconds, which raised a pedagogical dilemma: better task completion did not show that learners had practised the reasoning needed for independent performance. This conceptual paper examined the intersection of spatial reasoning, scientific reasoning and critical thinking in AI-supported higher education. A focused, illustrative synthesis of research on spatial cognition, cognitive offloading, human-AI interaction and educational scaffolding identified a plausible mechanism of concern: when AI supplied both a representation and its interpretation, students could bypass the construction, transformation and checking of mental models. The paper distinguished supportive from substitutive offloading and proposed SPARC (Sketch, Predict, Audit, Reconstruct, Calibrate), a five-stage instructional sequence that preserved learner-generated spatial and causal reasoning while using AI as a fallible comparison partner. Two teaching vignettes from introductory physics, the vertical motion of a tossed ball and work and power in stair climbing, showed how the sequence could be implemented with brief written or visual artefacts and assessed with an illustrative rubric. Four falsifiable propositions and a feasible pilot design were formulated for classroom evaluation. SPARC was not presented as an empirically validated intervention, and the available studies did not establish that GenAI necessarily caused durable declines in critical thinking. The paper concluded that educational evaluation should examine unaided transfer, reasoning quality and confidence calibration, rather than the quality of AI-assisted products alone.
