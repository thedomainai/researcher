# Artificial Intelligence in Higher Education: Opportunities, Challenges, and Future Directions

**著者**: Shivashankar H R, Varsha U
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence is transforming education and becoming a more strategic object as educational technology. These days universities are using AI-enabled tutoring systems, learning analytics, automated assessment, writing assistants, research support, student-advising platforms and administrative decision systems. This review paper studies the opportunities, challenges, and way forward on AI in higher education, with a spotlight on generative AI, learning analytics, academic misconduct, data governance, faculty readiness, and ethical adoption. The paper employs a design of narrative review woven into a systematic style search logic. The evidence on pedagogical, institutional and governance implications was synthesized from recent peer-reviewed literature and policies. According to the review, AI can be effective in personalized learning, timely feedback, accessibility, student retention, administrative processes, and evidence-informed decision-making. Despite their benefits, these cautions must be followed to ensure qualitative data, strong institutional capacity, human oversight, transparency, and the safeguard of student autonomy. The main risks of AI include algorithmic bias and surveillance and loss of privacy. Uneven access, academic cheating and overreliance on outputs. The suggested framework for Adopting Responsible AI links AI applications, institutional readiness, systems and processes, governance, behaviours and learning outcome. It concludes that the use of AI can result in a better higher education system but this is only possible if it is not treated merely as a technological enhancement but as a socio-technical governance challenge.
