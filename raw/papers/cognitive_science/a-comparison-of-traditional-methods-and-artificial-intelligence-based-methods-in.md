# A Comparison of Traditional Methods and Artificial Intelligence-Based Methods in the Education and Learning of Students with Special Needs

**著者**: Shiva Yarijani
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

Educating students with special needs presents numerous challenges to educational systems due to the wide range of variations in their cognitive, sensory, motor, and behavioral abilities. Despite their educational functionality, traditional teaching methods often lack the capacity to effectively address individual needs, provide immediate feedback, or facilitate personalized learning. Consequently, the integration of emerging technologies particularly Artificial Intelligence (AI) has emerged as an innovative solution in special education. The present study aims to examine the role and functions of AI in improving the educational process for students with special needs and to identify the associated opportunities and challenges. This research was conducted using a descriptive-analytical method based on a review of library resources, reputable scientific articles, and domestic and international research documents. The findings indicate that AI-based technologies, including adaptive learning systems, social robots, speech-to-text systems, and augmented and virtual reality, can increase educational engagement, strengthen cognitive and social skills, and reduce the cognitive load for students with special needs. However, issues such as data privacy, algorithmic bias, implementation costs, and the necessity of teacher training remain critical challenges in the adoption of these technologies. The results of this study emphasize the necessity of formulating intelligent educational policies and addressing ethical considerations in the development of AI-based special education.
