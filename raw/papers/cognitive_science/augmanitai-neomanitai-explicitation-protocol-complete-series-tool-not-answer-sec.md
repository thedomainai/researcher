## Abstract

Extended restricted snapshot (v4) of the AUGMANITAI / NEOMANITAI research bundle. Author: Andreas Ehstand, independent researcher, Starnberg, Germany. ORCID 0009-0006-3773-7796. Wikidata Q138634675. This version extends the v3 snapshot with three further companion papers that deepen specific aspects of the Explicitation Protocol and its surrounding theory. All three are full-length restricted research papers with the complete 26-paragraph bilingual disclaimer (V4.0) appended. Paper D — The Tool, Not the Answer: Distinguishing Concept Elicitation from Response Generation. 16 pages, 6,800+ words. Addresses a frequent misreading of the explicitation protocol by specifying the four registers in which concept elicitation categorically differs from ordinary response generation: direction of search (inward vs outward), form of output (structured primitive vs unstructured response), persistence (accumulating primitive layer vs single-use artefact), and role of the human interlocutor (framing agent vs consumer). Synoptic table, four-question practical guide for mode selection, consequences for evaluation. Paper E — Second-Order Emergence: The Generative Dynamics of Named Concepts Within Accumulating Primitive Layers. 16 pages, 6,900+ words. Characterises the phenomenon by which primitives within a maturing primitive layer produce further content through combination, attraction, and perceptual reframing. Three-level taxonomy of accessible content (unnamed known, unnamed unknown, newly emergent). Three structural limits (training-bound yield, framing-dependent quality, verification requirement). Practical implications for layer-building. Honest acknowledgement of open questions. Paper F — Multi-Perspective Analysis: Ontological, Epistemological, Systems-Theoretic, Phenomenological, Linguistic-Philosophical, and Cosmological Readings. 17 pages, 7,300+ words. Examines the explicitation protocol through six disciplinary perspectives, each contributing a distinct structural observation. The protocol produces a new category of entity (articulated forms); generates structured hypotheses with calibrated confidence; operates as a structurally-coupled self-elaborating process; enriches practitioners' self-access without replacing practice; extends the boundaries of the structurally expressible; belongs to a family of instruments that extend articulated access to structural regularities in the world. Synoptic conclusion; stated boundaries of the analysis. Central claim, carried across all three papers. The explicitation protocol is a categorically distinct mode of using learning systems, whose outputs interact generatively within accumulating primitive layers, and whose structural significance spans ontological, epistemological, systems-theoretic, phenomenological, linguistic-philosophical, and cosmological dimensions. The papers develop these claims structurally, without speculation about historical adoption, without recommendation for particular uses, and without evaluative framing. Methodological provenance. The underlying decomposition discipline originates in Leistungsfaktorenanalyse (performance-factor analysis) as practiced at ITF-tour and Bundesliga-level tennis coaching and has been transferred to representation-capable artificial systems under the wider research designation PERMANITAI.
