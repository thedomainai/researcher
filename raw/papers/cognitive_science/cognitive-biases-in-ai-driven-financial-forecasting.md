# Cognitive Biases in AI-Driven Financial Forecasting

**著者**: Mansi Panwar, Manish Dubey, Neha Malik, Maheswaran Muthuraman
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

Financial forecasting in travel and tourism organizations has undergone a dramatic technological transformation with the widespread adoption of artificial intelligence and machine learning tools. Yet the persistent underperformance of forecast-driven financial plans in the sector especially during demand discontinuities such as the COVID-19 pandemic, geopolitical disruptions, and fuel price shocks raises a question that purely technical explanations cannot fully address: are the cognitive biases of the human actors who commission, interpret, and act on AI-generated forecasts systematically corrupting the quality of financial decisions? This chapter answers that question in the affirmative.
