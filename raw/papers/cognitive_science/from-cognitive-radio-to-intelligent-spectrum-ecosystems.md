# From Cognitive Radio to Intelligent Spectrum Ecosystems

**著者**: Vinod Patil, Prof. Sudhir Adhikrao Kadam, Amol Kadam, Asheesh Tripathi
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Spectrum management is in an awkward position. On the one hand, the statistics of measurements constantly indicate that licensed bands of spectrum are occupied with significant proportions of each hour. On one side, the operators of networks are grappling with capacity constraints on an exponentially increasing load of mobile data, and in much of a large section of the developing world, rural subscribers are still underserved despite decades of policy work. The difference between these two realities has its roots in an institutional and technological failure: failure to reconcile the way spectrum is assigned to the way it is being used. The chapter contends that it will not suffice to fill this gap by making small changes to the policies. It needs the combination of three technologies artificial intelligence, machine learning, and blockchain to create spectrum management fast, smart, and trustworthy enough to serve the 5G age and beyond. It follows the intellectual path of early work in cognitive radio development into the current deep learning and reinforcement learning algorithms, why blockchain gives the governmental framework that the AI spectrum sharing needs and builds on testbed experience to connect these concepts to engineering reality. It ends by sketching the most difficult unsolved issues that the research community must address.
