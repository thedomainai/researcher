# MindTrap - Cognitive Bias Detection App Using Rule-Based Bias Profiling and Weighted Confidence Scoring Algorithm

**著者**: Manivannan D, Tharunika G, Pooja Nayak S, K Antony Kumar, Kishore Kumar K
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

The cognitive biases have an insidious, but a powerful effect on the decision-making process of a human being, and they often work beneath the level of conscious consciousness. The existing tools to study these biases are either mainly theoretical or rely on obscure machine-learning models that provide low interpretability, and scarcity of user-specific feedback. The current paper presents MindTrap, a scenariodriven cognitive bias-detecting system, combining rule-based bias profiling and confidence-weight behavioral scoring, which will improve awareness in users and reflective cognition. The respondents are presented with real-life situations of making decisions and do not know which biases are being tested so that they can naturally react. The system executes the participant decisions and demeanor clues through a comprehensible detection pathway and provides outputs in the shape of an interactive dashboard that outlines bias tendencies, consciousness, and scam-risk metrics. The assessment was conducted through a controlled user experiment using scenario-labeled ground truth and confusion-matrix evaluation. The overall accuracy of the system was about 150, which is what one would expect when dealing with multi-bias behavioral domains where cognitive patterns often overlap. Instead of focusing on the maximum accuracy of the prediction, the results are based more on interpretability, transparency, and educational value. MindTrap shows the example of the explainable, scenario-based system which can lead to the development of cognitive self-awareness and make informed decisions.
