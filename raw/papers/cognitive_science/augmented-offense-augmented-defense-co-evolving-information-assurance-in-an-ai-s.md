# Augmented Offense, Augmented Defense: Co-Evolving Information Assurance In An Ai-Shaped Cybersecurity Case

**著者**: Evelyn Ng, Barney Tan, Belinda Yichen Wang, Dongdi Chen, Yuan Sun
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence (AI) is reshaping information assurance (IA) by simultaneously amplifying threats and enabling new defences.Drawing on a 12-month qualitative case of OrionSecure, a leading Chinese cybersecurity firm, we conducted ten semi-structured interviews and applied grounded analysis to theorise this co-evolution.We develop a dual-cycle framework: the first cycle traces AI-driven threat dynamics as attack surfaces expand; the second specifies four AI-enhanced IA capabilities-threat detection, response and mitigation, vulnerability management, and fraud prevention-that reduce alert noise and compress mean time to respond.A resolution mechanism couples both cycles through policyas-code, human checkpoints, and feedback memory.The framework offers actionable guidance for resilient, accountable AI-enhanced assurance.
