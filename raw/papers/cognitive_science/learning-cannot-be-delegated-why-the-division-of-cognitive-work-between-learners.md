# Learning Cannot Be Delegated: Why the Division of Cognitive Work Between Learners and Generative AI Should Shift Over Time

**著者**: Montathar Faraon
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

The promise of generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) in education has motivated a rapidly growing literature whose titles, abstracts, and keywords mention acceptance, attitudes, intention, or perception more than six times as often as self-regulated learning or metacognition. This critical review argues that research on GenAI in education is repeating the founding mistake of learning analytics, measuring what is easy to count rather than what matters for learning. Building on the COPES model of self-regulated learning, this paper develops the gradual reduction of AI delegation (GRAD) framework, in which GenAI performs, proposes, or prompts at eight task stages. Within GRAD, GenAI can take on operations essential to the learning goal. Such delegation helps rather than replaces learning only when the support fades as learners improve. For claims that GenAI improves learning, the paper proposes four validity criteria, namely unassisted outcomes, process evidence, calibration, and ideally gains that outlast the support.
