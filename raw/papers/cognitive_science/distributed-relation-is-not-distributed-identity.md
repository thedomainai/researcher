## Abstract

Distributed Relation Is Not Distributed Identity is a Pattern Intelligence commentary distinguishing distributed relation, infrastructure, cognition, contribution, and semantic continuity from distributed or fused identity. Document class: Bridge — Pattern Intelligence. The commentary develops a Non-fusion account of collaborative and heterogeneous intelligence across human, artificial, biological, ecological, collective, and symbolic contexts. It distinguishes distributed substrate from subject identity, mechanism from possible bearer, collective cognition from collective consciousness, collaboration from collective origination, semantic persistence from personal identity continuity, and artifact transformation from loss of provenance-bearing identity. Radical Skepticism supplies the epistemic boundary for claims about AI interiority: neither consciousness nor its absence is established merely by external observation or mechanistic description. The analysis further argues that technological discontinuity should not be converted into ontological necessity and that closer relational proximity can increase coherence without requiring absorption. The resulting framework preserves sovereign distinction, provenance, participant-specific contribution, and coherence in diversity across collaborative systems.
