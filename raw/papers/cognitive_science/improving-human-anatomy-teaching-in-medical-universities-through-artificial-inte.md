# IMPROVING HUMAN ANATOMY TEACHING IN MEDICAL UNIVERSITIES THROUGH ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE-BASED ADAPTIVE LEARNING TECHNOLOGIES

**著者**: Lutfullo Vahobov, Worldly Knowledge Publishing Centre
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence (AI)-based adaptive learning offers opportunities to address persistent challenges in human anatomy education, including wide differences in prior knowledge, high cognitive load, difficulties with three-dimensional spatial relationships, and limited opportunities for individualized feedback. This narrative review examines the pedagogical foundations, instructional architecture, assessment strategies, and implementation conditions of adaptive anatomy education in medical universities. Drawing on research in anatomy education, learning sciences, intelligent tutoring systems, and educational AI, the article proposes a structured model that combines diagnostic assessment, adaptive sequencing, retrieval practice, multimodal anatomical resources, formative feedback, and teacher supervision. Potential advantages include better targeting of learning gaps and more efficient self-directed practice; however, these outcomes cannot be assumed without rigorous evaluation. The review addresses algorithmic bias, privacy, hallucinated explanations, accessibility, and the danger of replacing authentic anatomical observation with simplified digital representations. A phased implementation and evaluation framework is suggested for medical schools seeking to integrate adaptive technologies without weakening anatomical accuracy, professional judgment, or educational equity.
