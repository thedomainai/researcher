# Neurocontinuum Science Ω — Continuity Science of Living Cognition

**著者**: Kelly Rhys
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

Summary This work introduces Neurocontinuum Science Ω, a novel Continuity Science framework that reconceptualizes cognition as the lawful passage of signal across coupled neural, bodily, relational, and environmental continua rather than as isolated brain activity. The brain is treated as a node within a multi-scale neurocontinuum, where cognition emerges from the survival, distortion, repair, and replay of signal across interconnected pathways spanning molecule to environment. The framework establishes core laws governing signal survival, salience-gated passage, scar persistence, repair dynamics, and consciousness as a continuity regime, alongside a layered architecture (micro, meso, somatic, relational, ecological continua). It reframes memory as lineage, learning as scar-mediated route adaptation, and failure as continuity collapse rather than isolated dysfunction. A formalized mathematical and computational layer defines measurable variables (e.g., neurocontinuity coherence κₙ, salience gate strength S_G, replay-lineage fidelity R_L, scar load Σ_scar) and introduces a court-based runtime system for adjudicating signal passage, repair, and action admissibility. This enables translation into executable architectures for AI and continuity operating systems, where cognition is modeled as a governed, receipt-bound process.
