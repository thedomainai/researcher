# The Cognitive Exoskeleton: Artificial Intelligence as Mechanical Load-Bearing for Human Thought

**著者**: Michael Aksman
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

The cognitive exoskeleton does not portend the obsolescence of the human intellect; it marks the transition of theoretical research from manual artisanal crafting to heavy industrial precision.By relieving the biological mind of the crushing thermodynamic burden of syntactic serialization, algebraic expansion, and literature retrieval, the exoskeleton allows the human researcher to operate purely at the summit of intellectual capacity: framing the questions, defining the ontological boundaries, and judging what survives.Do not build machines to replace the thinker; build the exoskeleton so the thinker can move mountains.
