# Understanding Users’ Trust Calibration Journeys in Human-Generative AI Interaction

**著者**: Ana-Maria Sîrbu, Ulrich Gnewuch
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

As organizations increasingly adopt generative AI (GenAI) assistants, understanding when and to what extent users should trust them becomes critical. Although prior research has established that trust is dynamic and shaped by various factors, little is known about how the process of trust calibration unfolds over time at the individual level. To address this gap, we conducted a qualitative study based on interviews with 23 industry professionals and developed the Trust Calibration Journey (TCJ) framework. The framework conceptualizes trust calibration as an individual, dynamic, and episode-based journey, in which different types of trust calibrators trigger changes in users’ trust. These changes can improve or worsen calibration depending on whether they move users’ trust closer to or further away from the GenAI assistant’s actual capabilities. We contribute to IS research by providing novel insights into how users’ trust changes over time and how these changes affect trust calibration in human-GenAI interaction.
