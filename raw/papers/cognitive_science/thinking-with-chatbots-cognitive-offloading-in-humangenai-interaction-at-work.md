# Thinking with Chatbots: Cognitive Offloading in Human–GenAI Interaction at Work

**著者**: Jacopo Valentini, Haiat Perozzo, Aurelio Ravarini
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

GenAI chatbots are increasingly embedded in organizational work, yet we know little about how workers rely on them when performing cognitive tasks. Drawing on cognitive offloading theory, this study examines how workers engage with GenAI chatbots in work-related contexts. Based on 20 semi-structured interviews, we identify four recurring uses: preparing written outputs, organizing task-related information, retrieving or checking information, and comparing possible options. Building on these findings, we develop a framework of GenAI-enabled cognitive offloading based on two dimensions: perceived mental effort and perceived knowledge sufficiency. The framework distinguishes four modes of offloading: routine output offloading, knowledge-gap offloading, ambiguity-resolution offloading, and augmentation-oriented offloading. Our study contributes to research on cognitive offloading and human–technology interaction by showing how GenAI chatbots shift offloading from bounded cognitive functions toward broader task components, including writing, structuring, clarification, comparison, and elements of judgment.
