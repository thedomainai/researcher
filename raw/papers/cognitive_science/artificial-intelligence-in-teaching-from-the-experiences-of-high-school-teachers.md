# Artificial Intelligence in Teaching: From the Experiences of High School Teachers

**著者**: Emevie R. Pelicano-Abiero, Maedel Joy V. Escote
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 認知科学

## Abstract

The increasing integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in education has transformed teaching practices by enhancing instructional delivery, streamlining administrative tasks, and supporting professional responsibilities; however, concerns regarding accuracy, ethical use, curriculum alignment, technical limitations, and teacher dependence on AI remain significant challenges. This study aimed to explore the experiences and challenges of high school teachers in integrating artificial intelligence into their teaching profession. Employing a qualitative phenomenological research design, the study involved eight purposively selected high school teachers from four public secondary schools in New Bataan District, Davao de Oro, Philippines. Data was collected through in-depth interviews and analyzed using thematic analysis. Findings revealed that AI supports teachers by generating ready-made activities, assessments, instructional materials, and lesson plans while improving efficiency, communication, workload management, and access to innovative teaching strategies. AI also enabled teachers to devote greater attention to student learning and monitoring. However, participants encountered challenges related to ensuring the accuracy and appropriateness of AI-generated content, aligning outputs with curriculum standards and learner needs, managing excessive AI-generated suggestions, overcoming technical and knowledge limitations, addressing ethical concerns, and preventing overreliance on technology. These challenges often required additional time for verification, revision, and contextualization of AI-generated outputs. The study concludes that AI serves as a valuable complementary tool that enhances teaching effectiveness and professional productivity but cannot replace the pedagogical expertise, critical judgment, and human interaction provided by teachers. The findings further emphasize the need for continuous professional development, strengthened AI literacy, improved technological infrastructure, and the establishment of clear ethical guidelines to support the responsible, effective, and sustainable integration of AI in education.
