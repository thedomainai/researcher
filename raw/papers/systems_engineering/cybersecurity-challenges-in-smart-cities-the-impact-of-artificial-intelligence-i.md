# Cybersecurity Challenges in Smart Cities: The Impact of Artificial Intelligence Integration on Urban Digital Infrastructure Security and Resilience

**著者**: Prajakta Kulkarni, Asmita Bhagat, Rahul K. Shah
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

Artificial Intelligence (AI) plays a major role in modern digital systems and smart environments, improving efficiency, automation, and decision-making. However, the use of AI also introduces many cybersecurity challenges that affect the safety and reliability of these systems. This study focuses on key security threats in AI-based ecosystems, including adversarial attacks, data manipulation, privacy risks, and system vulnerabilities. It can also analyze how these threats can disturb critical services and reduce the effectiveness of AI technologies. To convey these issues, the paper highlights the need for strong and adaptive security strategies such as secure system design, continuous monitoring, and effective governance. This study highlights that traditional cybersecurity methods are insufficient for AI systems. Advanced and flexible approaches are required to ensure secure and reliable AI-driven environments.
