# 17) Resilience-Oriented Power and Cooling Architecture for High-Density Military AI and C5I Data Centers

**著者**: M. Rizwan Yasin
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

ABSTRACT This study develops a defense-oriented framework for resilience-oriented power and cooling architecture for high-density military ai and c5i data centers within an integrated military C5I environment. The research addresses the engineering problem of maintaining mission information advantage when physical infrastructure, optical transport, IP/MPLS connectivity, compute, data, cybersecurity, and operational services become dynamically constrained. The proposed method combines systems engineering, dependency modeling, quantitative resilience metrics, AI-assisted analytics, and controlled degradation/reconfiguration. To support reproducibility, the manuscript defines a synthetic 2026 experiment model, explicit assumptions, baselines, sensitivity analysis, and a laboratory validation pathway. The study is deliberately structured so that synthetic results can later be replaced by measured testbed results without changing the research architecture.
