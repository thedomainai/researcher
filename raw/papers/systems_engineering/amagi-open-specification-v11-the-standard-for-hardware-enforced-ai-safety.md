INTENDED AUDIENCERegulatory bodies, certification authorities, academic reviewers, and standards organizations evaluating hardware-enforced AI safety architectures. This document is the public-facing compliance gateway to the AMAGI Architectural Class. SCOPENormative specification of the six non-droppable invariants (INV-001 through INV-006), the irreducible Trusted Computing Base (≤500 lines HDL), and the mandatory audit protocols that any AMAGI-compliant implementation must satisfy. Conformance to this specification is the prerequisite for S-APL v2.0 licensing and AGIAM certification. COMPLIANCE MODELTier 1–6 certification levels per S-APL v2.0 Section 6, with Tier 1 (Reference Implementation) representing full hardware-enforced compliance and Tier 6 (Derivative Architecture under DALA) representing licensed third-party implementations. All tiers require preservation of the six invariants.
