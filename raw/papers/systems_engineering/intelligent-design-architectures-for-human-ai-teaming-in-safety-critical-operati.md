# Intelligent Design Architectures for Human-AI Teaming in Safety-Critical Operations

**著者**: Debra Henneberry, Dimitrios Ziakkas, Thanasis Binis
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

Intelligent systems increasingly influence operational decisions in transportation, yet their integration often occurs without considering the cognitive characteristics of the human operator. This paper proposes an intelligent design architecture that combines AI-enabled analytics, digital twins, competency modelling and systems engineering within a human-centred framework. Rather than optimising algorithmic performance alone, the proposed architecture dynamically adapts information presentation, decision support and workload distribution according to human performance requirements. Aviation case studies illustrate how computational intelligence can enhance resilience, explainability and operational adaptability while maintaining meaningful human authority.
