## Abstract

This introductory chapter recognizes that humans live in an era that is future-oriented from alpha to omega (that is, formulating plans from the very beginning (alpha) to the ultimate stage (omega)), yet little is done intellectually to speak to the problems of predicting the future. In this post-industrial information society, there is minimal social forecasting and a planning method to manage this AI-driven complex society. The overwhelming power of AI is on constant display, as it progressively and persistently transforms human lives, society, governance, ethics, and morality. So, as AI continues to grow exponentially, we see the increasing appearance of ethical challenges fueled by the antithesis between AI as a technical artifact and AI as a sociotechnical system. But now an influential body of sociological literature has identified this AI transformation as an overpowering technological mediation of social life, as an algorithmic authority activating social asymmetry, driving algorithmic oppression, inequity, and inequality. This manuscript&s;s common theme is the elaboration of a sociological model of AI ethics for healthcare that mitigates against health disparities. Chapters 2 – 6 synthesize current findings on AI-driven health interventions, the relationship between AI and society, the link between GenAI and health disparities, AI applications during COVID-19, and reconceptualizing the conversation on AI ethics, culminating in formulating a sociological model of AI ethics.
