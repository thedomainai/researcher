# Advancing Multimodal Freight Automation through Digital Twins and Decision Support: the AUTOSUP Framework

**著者**: Ilias Gkotsis, Dirk Staelens, Giuseppe Luppino, Luca Lucietti, Stéphane Kündig
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

The European freight transport sector is undergoing a profound shift through automation and digitalisation, with potential to enhance efficiency, sustainability, and resilience. Yet persistent barriers remain, including fragmented interoperability, regulatory uncertainty, cybersecurity risks, and workforce adaptation challenges. This paper outlines a methodological framework towards multimodal automation that integrates technical, business, and governance perspectives in line with the Physical Internet vision. The framework combines technology classification, stakeholder requirements, business model methodologies, and policy alignment, supported by Digital Twins and a Decision Support System for simulation-based validation and decision-making. Findings from stakeholder engagement in two Living Hubs (Antwerp-Bruges and Trieste) identify interoperability, certification, human–machine interaction, and cybersecurity as key enablers. These insights inform the validation of use cases such as predictive maintenance and automated transhipment. The study concludes with the next steps focusing on the finalisation of tools, integration of cost–benefit analysis, and validation of operational models to accelerate adoption.
