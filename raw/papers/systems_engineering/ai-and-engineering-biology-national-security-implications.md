# AI and Engineering Biology: National Security Implications

**著者**: Hannah Hunter
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

Intersections between AI and engineering biology are enabling a new era of advances in the life sciences, accelerating the analysis, design and optimisation of biological systems. However, the dual-use nature of AI-enhanced engineering biology (AI-bio) is introducing new risks. This CETaS Snapshot Report assesses the opportunities and national security implications of AI-bio. It calls for a coherent UK approach to AI-bio that combines innovation, resilience and risk mitigation, and proposes a ‘Soft-Sovereignty Framework’ that targets the highest-risk, highest-reward areas of AI-bio, focused on standardisation, assurance and coordination. This approach would help the UK to translate AI-bio R&D into greater public value, while shaping standards and norms for the responsible development, deployment and use of these technologies.
