# Immersive Technologies as Catalysts for Workforce Resilience: ASUSLA-WFD Framework for Talent Development, HSSE Performance, and Complex Troubleshooting in EPC and Gas Processing Systems

**著者**: John K. Galiotos
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

Abstract This manuscript presents the Southern University at Shreveport Workforce Development (SUSLA-WFD) framework for integrating immersive simulation, competency-based assessment, AI-assisted learning analytics, digital twin concepts, and health, safety, security, and environment (HSSE) instruction in engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) and gas-processing education. The framework is grounded in peer-reviewed research and recognized standards rather than unverified performance projections. Verified SUSLA institutional records are limited to an established 28-participant industry cohort that completed 84 hours of Fourth-Class Power Engineering instruction; all 28 candidates subsequently passed the National Institute for the Uniform Licensing of Power Engineers examination. That result demonstrates program delivery and certification readiness but is not presented as evidence of a VRD treatment effect. The manuscript defines a six-layer training architecture and a mixed-methods protocol for future evaluation through pre/post testing, scenario observation, comparison-group instruction, and 30- and 90-day retention assessment. Synthetic response times, simulated p-values, provisional demographics, and modeled HSSE gains from the earlier draft have been removed. The resulting framework offers a cautious, scalable approach for connecting regional workforce development with Energy 4.0 competency requirements while preserving process-safety rigor, responsible AI governance, and operational technology security.
