# Designing Authority for AI Enabled Administration: A Framework for Delegation, Oversight, and Execution Time Governance

**著者**: Michael McGuinness
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

Public administrations increasingly rely on artificial intelligence to support or structure core decision processes, yet existing governance frameworks do not provide the conceptual tools needed to understand how authority is exercised within these systems. This article develops a framework for designing and governing delegated authority in AI enabled administration, with a particular focus on execution time processes where public power becomes operational. The analysis shows how authority can drift across institutional, procedural, and computational layers, producing outcomes that diverge from legislated purpose while appearing compliant at design time. Using the Dutch childcare benefits scandal as an illustrative case, the article demonstrates how implicit delegation, embedded reasoning, and nominal oversight can create unbounded operational spaces that exceed statutory limits. The framework provides policymakers and institutional designers with a method for locating execution time authority, identifying points of drift, and evaluating the reasoning embedded in sociotechnical systems. It supports more precise delegation design, clearer allocation of responsibility, and more reliable oversight as governments integrate artificial intelligence into administrative practice.
