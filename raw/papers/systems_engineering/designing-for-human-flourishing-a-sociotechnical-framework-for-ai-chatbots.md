# Designing for Human Flourishing: A Sociotechnical Framework for AI Chatbots

**著者**: Louis Tay, Karina Vold, Gloria Liou, Harsh Kumar, Daniel Harris
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

The rapid adoption of AI chatbots - ranging from purpose-built systems to general-purpose large language models (LLMs) - for supporting human well-being and broader human flourishing requires an integrative interdisciplinary framework. In this article, we advance a sociotechnical framework for designing AI chatbots that support human flourishing, synthesizing four essential disciplinary perspectives (philosophical, behavioral, interactional, and technical) and articulating how their interplay shapes both the promises and risks of AI-based support. Central to our framework is the identification of two fundamental tensions that structure the design and evaluation of such systems: the temporal (i.e., immediate vs. longer-term benefits) and self-community (i.e., individual vs. collective outcomes) tensions. By making these tensions explicit, the framework offers an integrative lens for organizing disparate literatures on AI and human flourishing, generating novel hypotheses about system design and its effects on individual users and society more broadly. The framework guides both developers seeking to align AI system objectives with human values and policymakers governing the deployment of AI in mental health, education, and social welfare. We advance the field by equipping researchers, practitioners, and policymakers with a roadmap for the responsible design and regulation of AI chatbots that enable, rather than diminish, human flourishing.
