# Preventing Major Road Transportation Incidents Through ADAS and AI-Enabled Fleet Intelligence: A Framework for Safer and More Resilient Energy Supply Chains

**著者**: Sandeep Rameshbhai Thawani, Doli Sandeep Thawani
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

Abstract Objectives/Scope Road traffic crashes cause approx. 1.19 million fatalities and 20-50 million injuries globally each year (WHO 2023), while imposing economic losses approaching 3% of GDP in many countries. Transportation of fuels, chemicals, LNG, industrial gases, and other hazardous materials represents one of the most critical and risk-intensive links in global energy supply chains. Human factors—including fatigue, distraction, speeding, impaired judgement, and blind-spot errors—contribute to nearly 80-90%of severe transportation incidents. While telematics-based fleet monitoring has improved operational visibility, they remain largely reactive. This paper evaluates how Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) with AI-enabled fleet intelligence and Driver Monitoring System (DMS) can transform commercial fleet safety by shifting risk management from reactive monitoring to proactive accident prevention Methods, Procedures, Process This study utilizes a multi-evidence triangulation methodology integrating four streams: (1) forensic reconstruction of 60 severe tanker crashes (including 25 internal accident investigation); (2) physiological survey of 500 active drivers; (3) a 90-day pilot of 155 tankers using AI telematics and infrared DMS; and (4) Level 0–2 ADAS testing via an LCV-to-HCV validation bridge. The Safety Cocoon fuses forward 77 GHz radar, forward optical camera, and 24 GHz side radars with an in-cab DMS to continuously assess risk. Results, Observations, and Conclusions Forensic analysis identified rollovers (35%), rear-end impacts (28%), and sideswipes (18%) as dominant crash typologies, driven primarily by fatigue and distraction. The 155-tanker pilot revealed a 12-to-1 Detection Gap where DMS captured behavioral precursors invisible to passive GPS. Triangulated findings indicate the Safety Cocoon is projected to prevent 70–75% of forward and lateral collisions. Economic modelling projects a 17.12-month payback period on an upfront CapEx of ₹285.90 Lakh, generating ₹17.19 Crore in 10-year fleet lifecycle net returns. Novel Approach This paper presents an integrated Accident-to-ADAS Risk Mitigation Framework developed from real-world crash forensics, driver behavioural analytics, operational AI-enabled Fleet Intelligence pilot data, and an LCV-to-HCV ADAS Validation Framework. Rather than positioning ADAS as an optional vehicle feature, the research establishes an engineering and economic blueprint for integrating AI-enabled Fleet Intelligence, active ADAS, and Journey Risk Management 2.0 into a multi-layered Safety Cocoon for hazardous-material transportation. The proposed framework bridges the gap between passive behavioural monitoring and active physical intervention, providing energy executives, fleet operators, and regulators with a technically rigorous and economically justified pathway for strengthening the safety and resilience of global energy logistics
