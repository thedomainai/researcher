# Professionals in AI/ML Operations Deploying Trustworthy and Resilient AI Hardware Infrastructure

## Abstract

The large-scale deployment of AI and ML systems has transformed AI into mission-critical infrastructure across healthcare, finance, autonomous systems, and national security. Despite advances in algorithmic performance, the trustworthiness of AI remains fundamentally constrained by the reliability and security of the underlying hardware. As AI workloads increasingly execute on heterogeneous accelerators distributed across cloud, edge, and cyber-physical environments, hardware integrity, resilience, and provenance emerge as decisive factors for dependable AI behavior. This chapter advances a trust-centric perspective on AI/ML operations, arguing that software-focused MLOps practices are insufficient for ensuring reliable AI execution. By integrating hardware trust engineering, deterministic inference theory, distributed systems resilience, and socio-technical governance, the chapter proposes a unified operational framework that treats trust as a continuous, lifecycle-wide property.
