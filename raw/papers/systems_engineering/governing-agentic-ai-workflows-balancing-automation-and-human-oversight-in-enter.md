# Governing Agentic AI Workflows: Balancing Automation and Human Oversight in Enterprise Practice

**著者**: Natsumi Takahashi, Indranil Bhattacharjee
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

Agentic AI is extending organizational automation beyond predefined tasks, requiring organizations to reconsider what should be delegated to AI and what forms of human involvement should remain. This study compares three enterprise AI development cases. One sought to reduce cost by substituting human work. Another made AI easier to use by letting practitioners assemble assistants through natural-language instructions. A third treated continued interaction as a way for an AI agent to become aligned with actual work. Greater AI capability did not lead to a stronger expectation of removing people from the workflow. In the learning-oriented case, evaluation, correction, and contextual input were not merely safeguards against AI failure. They became part of the mechanism through which the agent and the workflow were adapted. Human-centered design for agentic AI should therefore keep delegation adjustable and treat human correction as a productive component of AI-enabled work.
