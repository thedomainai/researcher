# AI-Driven Intelligent Railway Monitoring: Integrating Track Defect and Obstacle Detection for Next-Generation Rail Safety

**著者**: Apurva Guldagad, Mayank R. Kothawade
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

Railway transportation systems are critical to global infrastructure; however, ensuring their safety remains a major challenge due to progressive track degradation and the unpredictable occurrence of foreign obstacles. Conventional inspection methods, including manual patrols and periodic monitoring, are inherently limited in providing continuous, real-time detection across large-scale railway networks. Recent advancements in Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and computer vision have enabled the development of intelligent monitoring systems capable of automated anomaly detection and predictive maintenance. This review presents a comprehensive and critical analysis of AI-driven approaches for railway track defect detection and foreign object identification. Existing methodologies are systematically classified into conventional inspection techniques, classical machine learning models, and deep learning-based vision systems. A comparative evaluation is conducted to assess their performance, robustness, and suitability for real-world deployment. Furthermore, this study identifies key limitations in current research, including fragmented problem modelling, limited availability of large-scale and region-specific datasets, and challenges in real-time implementation under dynamic environmental conditions. To address these gaps, an integrated AI-based railway monitoring framework is proposed, combining multi-sensor data fusion, deep learning models, and edge computing for continuous and real-time safety monitoring. The review also outlines future research directions focusing on scalable AI architectures, sensor fusion strategies, and intelligent railway ecosystems. The insights presented aim to support the development of next-generation railway monitoring systems capable of enhancing operational safety, reducing accident risks, and improving infrastructure reliability.
