# The Refusal Heuristic: A Proposed Stress Test for Adversarial Systems — Resilience Under Disobedience, Ambiguity, and Adversarial Pressure

**著者**: Narnaiezzsshaa Truong
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

This working paper introduces the Refusal Heuristic, a cross-cutting adversarial lens for evaluating whether AI-enabled and cybersecurity-relevant systems remain resilient when expected conditions fail. The heuristic treats operational order as a maintained equilibrium rather than a default state and asks: What happens when someone—or something—refuses to play along? The paper proposes SVAR—System Verification for Adversarial Resilience—as the heuristic’s operational review method. SVAR examines five dimensions: Authority Dependency, Assumption Fragility, Internal Adversariality, Theater Gap, and Adaptive Resilience. It uses a proposed evidence-oriented scale from Denial through Changed and Re-tested, and it applies a lowest-material-score rule to prevent strong documentation or instrumentation from masking an unexercised critical weakness. The Refusal Heuristic and SVAR are proposed methods. They are not certifications, compliance determinations, assurance opinions, security ratings, or validated predictors of security, safety, alignment, resilience, or incident outcomes. They are intended to complement—not replace—threat modeling, secure engineering, incident response, assurance, and established AI or cybersecurity risk-management frameworks.
