# AI Adoption and its Role in Enhancing Employee Psychological Safety and Innovative Behavior

**著者**: Vinod Patel, Parulkumari Bhati, Priyanka Sikarwar, Poonamben Pravinbhai Radadiya, Abhishek Singhal
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

The research work explores how the adoption of Artificial Intelligence (AI) can predetermine the psychological safety and innovation behaviour of employees working in AI-enabled workplaces. With organizations becoming more exposed to intelligent technologies in their daily operations, it is necessary to know the human-centric experience of AI. The study uses a sample of 276 employees who represented different industries and is based on the scale of Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM) to investigate the correlations of AI Adoption, Psychological Safety, and Innovation Behavior. The results also indicate that AI Adoption is positively related to psychological safety and behavior towards innovation, which means that workers who feel that AI tools can help them are safer to participate in risk-taking and creative tasks. Psychological Safety proves to be the most important predictor of Innovation Behavior, and it is very important to nurture idea generation, experimentation, and collaborative innovation. Though the model has a small explanatory value, the findings indicate the main psychological routes by which AI systems can affect behaviors at the workplace. The paper will add to the literature on the topic of human-AI interaction because it shows that technological progress encourages innovation not only through efficiency gains but also by enhancing the feeling of safety and confidence in employees. Conclusions about organizations focus on the critical role of nurturing psychologically safe environments in order to optimize the gains of utilizing AI.
