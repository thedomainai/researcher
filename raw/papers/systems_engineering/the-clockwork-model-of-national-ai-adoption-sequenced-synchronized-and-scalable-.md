# The Clockwork Model Of National Ai Adoption: Sequenced, Synchronized, And Scalable Transformation

**著者**: Ekaterina Glebova, Agnieszka Rzepka, Faranak Farzaneh
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

National AI strategies are increasingly setting ambitious goals for innovation, competitiveness and responsible deployment.However, the adoption of AI on a large scale remains uneven and pathdependent, often hindered by misalignment between policy design, infrastructure investment, data readiness, organisational capabilities and governance mechanisms.While prior research has explained adoption through diffusion and ecosystem perspectives, there has been a lack of explicit temporal logic to explain how these interdependent components evolve across institutional layers.This paper therefore proposes the Clockwork Model of National AI Adoption, which conceptualises AI transformation as a multi-level ecosystem orchestration challenge.The model represents national AI systems as interdependent sociotechnical 'gears' -policy, infrastructure, data, talent, use cases, governance and capital -whose synchronised progression determines scalable, ethical outcomes.Using the UAE as an illustrative context, the model links the macro, meso and micro levels in order to coordinate national AI diffusion.
