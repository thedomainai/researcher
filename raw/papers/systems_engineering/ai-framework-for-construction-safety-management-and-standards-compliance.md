# AI framework for construction safety management and standards compliance

**著者**: Atharva Kulkarni, Pravinchandra Gaikwad, Harsh Srivastav, snehjit kumbhar, Santosh B. Rane
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

The construction industry in India's fast-developing Metropolitan Regions experiences high accident rates despite mandatory regulatory frameworks. A large gap exists between standard safety procedures and their on-site implementation due to complex project interfaces and dynamic risks. The objective of this study is to systematically assess localised construction safety conditions and develop an integrated, technology-enabled framework for continuous statutory compliance. Three construction typologies (residential building, road construction, and a water supply project) were audited, revealing critical gaps in safety management. An artificial intelligence-driven framework is proposed to counter these vulnerabilities. Considering a sample of 75 professionals, the study establishes a human-assessed risk baseline using the Relative Importance Index (RII: 0.244–0.781) and Cronbach’s alpha (α = 0.714–0.827). To automate safety enforcement, a pre-trained You Only Look Once version 8 (YOLOv8) pipeline detects field hazards, algorithmically mapping them to Indian Standards and Occupational Safety and Health Administration regulations to generate a dynamic Risk Priority Number (RPN: 1–64). A Random Forest Regressor performs predictive risk modelling to identify key site activities influencing high RPN scores. This automated system meets legal standards, aligns with UN SDG 8 to improve social sustainability, and enables objective risk measurement.
