# DECISION-CENTRIC PROCESS MINING AND AUTOMATION PRIORITISATION FOR SAUDI PUBLIC AND FINANCIAL SERVICES UNDER VISION 2030: INTEGRATING BUSINESS PROCESS MANAGEMENT, ROBOTIC PROCESS AUTOMATION, AND BENEFIT-RISK ANALYSIS

## Abstract

Digital transformation programmes increasingly possess event traces but lack defensible methods for deciding which workflows should be automated, redesigned or retained under human control. This critical review examines how business process management, process mining, robotic process automation and benefit risk analysis can jointly improve prioritisation in Saudi public administration and financial services, while extending the analysis to electricity utility interfaces. Thirty DOI-identified scholarly works published between 2021 and 2025 are synthesised through an explicit thematic appraisal covering data quality,decision discovery,technical suitability,value measurement, governance and contextual transferability. The proposed decision-centric framework separates evidence about process behaviour from normative decisions about public value, legal authority and operational risk. It introduces auditable eligibility gates, scenario-sensitive prioritisation, human decision rights and post-deployment monitoring. Application pathways are developed for permits, banking onboarding, electricity connections, metering disputes and grid-related service requests.
