# BulliShon Bullion™: The Attention Tutoring Machine™ and the Educational Arcade — A Human-Centered Infrastructure for Reciprocal Tutoring, Curriculum-Native Gaming, Inclusive Learning, and Independent Mastery

**著者**: Rashon Rahming
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

BulliShon Bullion™: The Attention Tutoring Machine™ and the Educational Arcade presents a comprehensive, human-centered educational technology framework designed to integrate personalized tutoring, curriculum-native video gaming, artificial intelligence, accessible learning, financial literacy, and physical educational infrastructure into a unified system for K–12 education and childhood development. Authored by Rashon Rahming, this third volume of the BulliShon Bullion™ white paper series introduces the Attention Tutoring Machine™ (ATM), a proposed educational platform inspired by the service accessibility of banking ATMs and the interactive engagement of arcade gaming. Its companion environment, ATM Arcade™, envisions a connected ecosystem of educational games in which academic knowledge becomes an essential mechanism of gameplay rather than an interruption to entertainment. Central to the architecture is APPRECIATED INTELLIGENCE™, a child-centered artificial intelligence framework integrated with THE GOLDEN GLYPHS™, original educational characters representing distinct intellectual and instructional perspectives. Through reciprocal tutoring, children can alternate between learning, teaching, questioning, investigating, collaborating, and independently demonstrating their understanding. The proposed Human Attention Dividend further establishes an operational principle: technological automation should strengthen the educational contributions of teachers, parents, caregivers, and qualified human tutors rather than displace them. The paper also introduces the ATM Learning Game Engine, ATM Game Studio™, and Genesis One™, a proposed physical educational terminal combining arcade-inspired interaction with secure, accessible institutional functionality. Together, these components establish a framework for developing and distributing educational games, simulations, instructional experiences, and transferable learning activities across academic disciplines and practical life skills. Special attention is given to inclusive and special education, nonvisual accessibility, student agency, disability accommodations, financial empowerment through the FIVE SHONS (SPEND, SAVE, INVEST, GIVE, GROW), child privacy, human oversight, and independently verified learning outcomes. New York City is proposed as the initial cultural and educational context, with a staged development pathway toward broader institutional deployment. Rather than claiming to have established a new educational industry or demonstrated superior learning outcomes, this publication defines a proposed technological architecture, original product portfolio, testable educational hypotheses, implementation requirements, and independent evaluation framework. Its central proposition is that educational technology can unite rigorous instruction, imaginative gameplay, accessible physical interaction, and meaningful human participation while measuring success through children's demonstrated understanding, knowledge retention, transferable competencies, and increasing intellectual independence.
