# Ontological Safety III: Why Incremental Approaches Fail, and How to Build an AI with Self-Boundaries

**著者**: Lianjie Li
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

Description: The first paper posed the philosophical question; the second provided the mathematical skeleton. This third paper fills the critical gap: how Ontological Safety moves from formal specification to computable system design. More crucially, we prove why existing incremental approaches—MDP safety constraints, Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF), and Constitutional AI (CAI)—cannot reach ontological safety through incremental improvement, regardless of engineering investment. This is not a deficiency of implementation; it is structural incompatibility. We propose OS-Net as an existence proof of the mapping from formal specification to computational architecture, honestly annotating unresolved computational challenges, and design a set of preliminary experiments to verify the computability of the self-congruence function. The three papers together form a complete closed loop: philosophical intuition (Paper I) → mathematical skeleton (Paper II) → computational mapping and impossibility theorems (Paper III).Keywords: Ontological Safety; AI Safety; Self-Boundary; Formal Verification; Impossibility Theorem; OS-Net; Incremental Approach Failure
