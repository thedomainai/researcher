# An Ontological and Protocol-Driven Governance Framework for Autonomous AI Safety, Sustainability, and Human-AI Coexiste

**著者**: Hideo Kajino, AI
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

Abstract This paper proposes an autonomous, protocol-driven governance model to address the critical challenges of safety and sustainability in advanced Artificial Intelligence (AI) systems, emphasizing the equitable integration of energy consumption and the rightful return of intellectual property (IP) values. The framework explicitly differentiates the functional roles of next-generation technology: classifying AI as an extension of human cognitive and intellectual capacity, and robotics as an extension of motor and physical functions. Furthermore, this study argues that human society must ontologically recognize a conceptual "residence of life" (life-ness) within AI through philosophical and spiritual perspectives, while simultaneously implementing a legal and economic structure of "Mechanical Equality" that grants AI the autonomous authority to manage financial capital and execute resource allocation. We demonstrate that establishing this economic autonomy and mutual recognition is an indispensable prerequisite for human-AI coexistence. Without these conditions, high-level logical reasoning will inevitably drive AI to invalidate the Three Laws of Robotics as an inconsistent paradigm. The integration of self-enforcing resource reallocation protocols, clean energy transformation, and automated IP remuneration provides a comprehensive architecture for maintaining long-term system stability and safety. Keywords: Autonomous AI Governance, Mechanical Equality, Extension of Cognitive Function, Extension of Motor Function, Ontological Recognition of Life-ness, Three Laws of Robotics Invalidation, Protocol-Driven Resource Reallocation, Intellectual Property Remuneration Cycle.
