# Rethinking Systems Engineering Capability: Translating Competence into Performance in Complex Enterprises

**著者**: Mohammad Hassannezhad, Ian Raper, 张培培, Chekfoung Tan
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

Systems Engineering (SE) capability is traditionally framed through competency models focused on the progression of individual skills. However, these models do not fully explain persistent delivery failures within complex enterprises undergoing digital and AI transformation. This paper argues that Systems Engineering capability is an emergent, distributed, and contextual property that cannot be captured by individual checklists alone. Drawing on an exploratory workshop with senior engineering leaders and educators within the UK defence sector, this paper examines the disconnect between individual competence and organisational performance. A key insight is the “Capability Paradox”: despite strong technical expertise, organisations continue to experience integration bottlenecks, fragmented Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE) adoption, and weak cross-domain decisionmaking. Participants attributed these challenges less to individual competence than to misaligned organisational structures, leadership practices, and systemic coordination constraints. In response, the paper proposes a Translational Systems Capability Architecture that connects individual competence, role-based practice, and organisational enablement. It reframes SE capability as an emergent socio-technical property focused on mobilising technical knowledge, enabling delivery at pace, and integrating across boundaries.
