# ROLE OF CIVIL ENGINEERING IN SUSTAINABLE INFRASTRUCTURE DEVELOPMENT AND NATION BUILDING

**著者**: Gautam Bondyopadhyay
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

Civil engineering is one of the basic disciplines of the nation building process, as it translates public policy, economic hopes and social demands into physical systems - transport systems, water and sanitation, housing, energy-supporting systems, systems of the city, industrial facilities and infrastructure to protect from climate change. This article explores how to move the discipline away from the traditional project delivery system to sustainable stewardship of infrastructure. A systematic review protocol is created based on the PRISMA 2020 guideline to systematically review the literature in Scopus-indexed peer-reviewed journals related to sustainable infrastructure, life-cycle assessment (LCA), circular economy, Building Information Modelling (BIM), digital twins, artificial intelligence, climate resilience and sustainable urban development. We have limited the number of studies used for synthesis to 40 candidate peer-reviewed studies. The article also creates a VOS viewer-style thematic co-occurrence map, a comparative case-study matrix as well as an SDG alignment heat map. Four propositions are made: sustainability should be part of project decisions made at the front end; life-cycle performance must not be based on the performance of the buildings alone, but must also consider the performance of the city; digital engineering should include BIM, GIS, sensing, AI and digital twins; and resilience, equity and governance should be understood as fundamental engineering performance aspects. These principles can be translated into action as seen in the Indian projects such as the Delhi Metro, Kochi Metro, Namami- Gange and smart-city. The article suggests a Sustainable Infrastructure – Nation Building Framework for incorporating the environmental, economic, social, resilience and digital aspects, throughout the lifecycle of infrastructure. Since it is a live Scopus subscription/export that is not available for this drafting environment, the draft does not create record counts, but there is an explicit verification lock for the exact identification, duplicate, screening and eligibility counts in the PRISMA flow.
