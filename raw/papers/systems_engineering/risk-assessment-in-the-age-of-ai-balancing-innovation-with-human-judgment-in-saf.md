# Risk Assessment in the Age of AI: Balancing Innovation with Human Judgment in Safety-Critical Environments

**著者**: Abdelsalam Yasseen, Lamberto Nonno
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

Abstract Effective risk assessment is fundamental to protecting personnel, assets, and the environment across oil and gas operations. Traditionally, this process relies on collaborative judgment informed by field experience and operational context. With the emergence of artificial intelligence (AI) tools such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, questions are surfacing about the role these technologies may play in shaping future safety practices. This paper presents a comparative study evaluating AI-generated risk assessments against conventional team-based assessments. The analysis examines structure, depth, clarity, and operational relevance, with the goal of identifying both synergies and limitations. Subject matter experts, blinded to the origin of each assessment, evaluated paired examples developed through standard HSE processes and corresponding assessments generated using AI. Preliminary findings indicate that AI-generated assessments offer strong structure, consistency, and speed, but can underrepresent situational nuance and tacit field knowledge. Conversely, traditional assessments benefit from contextual insight, experience-driven detail, and practical relevance. The study outlines a structured methodology for comparing both approaches and proposes a hybrid model in which AI accelerates early hazard identification while human expertise ensures accuracy and applicability. The results provide valuable insights for operators seeking to enhance safety performance, improve clarity, and streamline planning in safety-critical environments.
