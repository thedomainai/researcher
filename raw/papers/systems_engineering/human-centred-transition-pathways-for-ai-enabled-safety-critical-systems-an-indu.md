# Human-Centred Transition Pathways for AI-Enabled Safety-Critical Systems: An Industrial Systems Engineering Perspective

**著者**: Dimitrios Ziakkas, Debra Henneberry
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence is transforming safety-critical industries from technology-centred automation towards integrated human-AI work systems. However, many organisations continue to implement AI through isolated technical projects rather than coordinated socio-technical transformation. This paper proposes a Human-Centred Transition Pathway that combines systems engineering, Human Factors Integration, Safety-II, STAMP and Human-AI Teaming into a structured implementation framework. The proposed methodology aligns operational objectives, competency requirements, organisational processes and AI capabilities throughout the system lifecycle. Aviation provides the principal case study through recent developments in EASA AI 3.0 and competency-based operations, while lessons are transferable to rail, maritime and healthcare systems. The framework demonstrates how industrial organisations can move beyond regulatory compliance towards resilient, human-centred AI ecosystems that support sustainable operational performance.
