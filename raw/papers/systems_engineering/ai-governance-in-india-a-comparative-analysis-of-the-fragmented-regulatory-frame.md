# AI Governance in India: A Comparative analysis of the Fragmented Regulatory Framework and the Need for Comprehensive AI-Specific Legislation in Light of the EU AI Act

**著者**: Deepshikha
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is rapidly growing and transforming governance, business, finance, and other sectors but it is also raising concerns regarding privacy, transparency, bias, accountability, safety. The increasing growth of AI has created an urgent need for a comprehensive law for AI governance. This paper critically examines how AI governance is needed in India and whether the existing frameworks are adequate to address the challenges posed by evolving AI. The study analyses India's current framework in relation to AI Governance including all existing laws, data protection laws, privacy policies and approaches adopted by institutions like RBI and SEBI. The gap research that this paper is trying to address is that despite existence of various laws, guidelines, frameworks that India has to apply to AI related activities, it lacks a comprehensive AI specific law which can establish clear classification, obligations, accountability standards. Currently the existing laws through which AI Governance is addressed is uncertain and inconsistent across different sectors The research paper is based on doctrinal and comparative legal methodology. It criticality analyses the existing legal framework in India and compare it with other countries AI acts like EU AI Act. The research conclusion is that the development of a comprehensive AI governance framework is needed in India. Instead of replicating the EU model, the proposed framework should address India's specific constitutional, economic, social, and technological need while ensuring innovation, accountability, and the protection of fundamental rights.
