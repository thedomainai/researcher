# AI for Verification and Validation of Safety-Critical Systems: reproducibility package (bibliometric data, code, and AVAF coding, 2016–2026)

**著者**: Fatma Dhaou
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

Reproducibility package for a bibliometric science-mapping study of artificial intelligence for the verification and validation (V&V) of safety-critical systems (2016–2026), and for the AI-Verification Assurance Framework (AVAF). Contains the raw Scopus and Web of Science exports, a single commented R script reproducing the full pipeline (import, merge, de-duplication, programmatic eligibility screening with per-reason exclusion counts, controlled thesaurus and stop-terms, co-word network, consensus community partition over 30 restarts, sensitivity/ARI grid, thematic map, and trend topics), the derived outputs (PRISMA counts, descriptor tables, cluster and sensitivity results, figures), the AVAF coding workbook (codebook, coded subsample, and intra-coder test–retest reliability), a README, and R session information. The analysed corpus comprises 425 documents (search date: 4 July 2026). The random seed is fixed for deterministic reproduction.
