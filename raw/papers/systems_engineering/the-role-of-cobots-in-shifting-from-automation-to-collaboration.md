# The Role of Cobots in Shifting from Automation to Collaboration

**著者**: Rajpreet Singh, Aashna Sinha, Vivek Kumar Singh, Praveen Kumar Malik
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: システム工学

## Abstract

Collaborative Robots (Cobots) are deployed in Industry 4.0 to enhance human-machine interactions in manufacturing. Cobots boost the flexibility of industrial processes, and this study investigates how they affect get-together and dismantling lines. A Systematic Literature Review analyzes existing contributions depending on subject matter, method presentation standards, and category of Human–Cobot collaboration. Decision-making views and research viewpoints are provided, emphasizing the potential benefits of cobot integration in manufacturing. Cobots are becoming an essential component of modern production, providing a relatively inexpensive alternative to traditional industrial robots. They offer more safety, flexibility, and cost-effectiveness, making them a more affordable and flexible choice for enterprises. As more organizations see the value of cobots, their position in industrial automation is projected to grow. This study looks at the current status of cobot technologies and anticipated future advances in the field, emphasizing their ability to transform the industrial sector and build ecologically conscious enterprises. This study explores computational strategies for analyzing and supporting the choice to purchase and install an individual robot for a single workstation and assembly line position. It explores the differences between these two scenarios and gives related models with computational examples, emphasizing the significance of weighing the economic benefits of obtaining and deploying a given cobot. The study highlights the possibilities of Cobots, a technology meant to handle activities such as managing toxic chemicals, attaching screws to automobiles, and preparing meals. It distinguishes Cobots from robots and analyzes their characteristics, capacities, collaboration, and industrial applications. Cobots are utilized in a diversity of ways and claims, which improve production and operations. They collaborate with humans to strike a balance between safety and flexibility, ensuring that the production process is efficient and flexible. The paper also emphasizes the important uses of Cobots in various industries.
