## Abstract

Artificial Intelligence (AI)-powered self-healing databases are advanced database systems that can automatically detect, analyze, and resolve problems without continuous human intervention. These databases use AI, machine learning, and automation to monitor system health, optimize performance, improve security, and recover from failures. They reduce downtime, increase reliability, and simplify database management. This paper explains the concept, working process, key features, benefits, challenges, applications, and future scope of AI-powered self-healing databases in simple and easy-to-understand language
