# MAGR-TCM: a knowledge graph-powered multi-agent model for intelligent home health consultation and triage in TCM gynecology

**著者**: Ming Lv, Xu Pan, Yishan Li, Yalan Hu, Wei Xu
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

BACKGROUND: Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) demonstrates distinct efficacy in managing gynecological disorders. However, accessing this specialized care in a home setting remains challenging due to the high cost of frequent hospital visits, privacy concerns, and a lack of immediate professional guidance. While general-purpose artificial intelligence offers a convenient alternative, the semantic gap between a patient's colloquial symptom descriptions and formal TCM diagnostic logic-compounded by the inherent 'hallucination' risks of large language models-prevents these tools from providing safe, actionable advice. To bridge this gap, this study proposes MAGR-TCM, an intelligent home health consultation and triage model. METHODS: Under expert supervision, a domain-specific knowledge graph was constructed, encompassing 10,231 entities and 32,051 relationships extracted from 741 high-quality clinical case records. Based on this foundation, we developed a Multi-Agent Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) framework comprising four specialized agents designed to reconstruct the complete TCM 'Disease-Pattern-Therapeutic Principle-Formula-Medicinal' reasoning chain from informal user inquiries. This collaborative architecture simulates the expert diagnostic process to generate logical, evidence-based health guidance. RESULTS: Experimental results demonstrate that MAGR-TCM significantly outperforms all baseline models on both RAGAS automatic evaluation and expert human evaluation. The model achieved 100% sensitivity and specificity on the safety test set, further validating its practical utility in home health settings and its ability to distinguish self-treatable conditions from those requiring urgent hospital care. CONCLUSIONS: By transitioning from black-box predictions to transparent, safe, and explainable reasoning logic, MAGR-TCM offers a technological paradigm for patient-centered home health management. It effectively mitigates generative hallucinations, making professional TCM expertise safely accessible for women's daily self-care and preliminary triage.
