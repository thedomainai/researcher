## Abstract

FEEDBACK LOOPS IN CYBERNETIC LAW AND SELF EVOLVING LEGISLATION: FROM STATIC RULES TO LIVING LEGISLATIVE SYSTEMS This groundbreaking work establishes Cybernetic Legislative Engineering as an entirely new legal science, designed to transform static legal rules into living legislative systems capable of self evolution. In an era where technological change occurs at exponential speed while legislative processes remain trapped in linear cycles, the traditional model of law making has become fundamentally inadequate. Structured across five major parts and eighteen chapters, the book introduces the paradigm of Self Evolving Legislation, where legal rules incorporate feedback loops, vital sign indicators, and adaptive mechanisms that allow them to evolve automatically in response to changing conditions. Part One establishes theoretical foundations from Norbert Wiener's cybernetics to modern legislative applications. Part Two explores the engineering of self evolving legislation, covering programmable legislative protocols, legislative vital signs, self adjustment mechanisms, and the balance between positive and negative feedback. Part Three examines practical applications across traffic law, financial regulation, environmental standards, and smart contracts. Part Four addresses risks and safeguards, including the Cybernetic Stop Principle, democratic legitimacy, and algorithmic transparency. Part Five concludes with the vision of living constitutions and digital parliaments. The five appendices provide specialized practical tools: a bilingual cybernetic legal glossary, model legislative clauses for dynamic technical delegation and data driven mandatory review, system architecture descriptions for feedback loop design, a comparative case studies matrix covering the European Union AI Act, Rules as Code initiatives, and FinTech sandboxes, and keywords in ten global languages. This work introduces ten original intellectual frameworks protected under international intellectual property law, including Cybernetic Legislative Engineering, Self Evolving Legislation, the Cybernetic Stop Principle, Legislative Vital Signs methodology, and Executable Legal Language. It is written for legislators, regulators, protocol engineers, artificial intelligence researchers, legal theorists, and digital governments seeking complete transformation. The vision of living law challenges us to reimagine legislation for the cybernetic age, to build legal systems that learn, adapt, and evolve rather than decay and obstruct.
