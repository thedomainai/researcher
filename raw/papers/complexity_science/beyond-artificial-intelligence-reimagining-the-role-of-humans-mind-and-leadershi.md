# Beyond Artificial Intelligence: Reimagining the Role of Humans, Mind, and Leadership in the Future of Educational Systems

**著者**: Golnaz Jahani
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

The rapid expansion of artificial intelligence has confronted educational systems with fundamental changes in the definition of learning, the role of teachers, decision-making processes, and leadership models. Although artificial intelligence can enhance analytical capacities, personalized learning, content generation, and data-based management, the future of education does not depend solely on technological progress. Rather, it depends on how the human role is redefined alongside technology. This analytical review examines the need to reimagine the role of humans, the mind, and leadership in the future of educational systems. From this perspective, humans are not competitors of artificial intelligence but the primary source of meaning, ethics, creativity, empathy, and professional judgment. The learner’s mind, as the central element of transformation, requires the development of cognitive and metacognitive skills, critical thinking, self-regulation, and adaptability. In addition, educational leadership in the age of artificial intelligence should move from traditional administration toward transformational, data-informed, ethical, and human-centered leadership. The analysis indicates that artificial intelligence can improve educational quality only when its implementation is accompanied by the development of human competencies, the redesign of professional roles, and the creation of learning-oriented organizational cultures. Therefore, the desirable future of educational systems is not the elimination of humans but the formation of intelligent human–technology coexistence in which technology serves human growth, educational equity, meaning, and sustainable development.
