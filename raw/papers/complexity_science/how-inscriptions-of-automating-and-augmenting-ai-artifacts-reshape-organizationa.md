# How Inscriptions of Automating and Augmenting AI Artifacts Reshape Organizational Routines

**著者**: Salar Abaspur, Richard Guse, Christoph Rosenkranz, Ali Sunyaev
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

Artifacts based on artificial intelligence are reshaping organizational routines, but their effects on routines vary markedly within the dominant framings of automation and augmentation. We argue that the inscriptions AI artifacts carry drive this variation. In a longitudinal qualitative case study at a German radiology department, we examine two AI artifacts introduced concurrently into the same prostate MRI diagnostic routine, one used to automate visual prostate segmentation, and one used to augment lesion assessment. The automating artifact carried a result inscription that enabled recognition-based verification and a compressed routine performance, while the augmenting artifact carried a proposal inscription that required reconstructive verification and produced a recursive routine performance. We extend artifact-centered routine theory by specifying the function-specific inscriptions across five action dimensions through which automating and augmenting AI artifacts reshape the ostensive and performative aspects of organizational routines.
