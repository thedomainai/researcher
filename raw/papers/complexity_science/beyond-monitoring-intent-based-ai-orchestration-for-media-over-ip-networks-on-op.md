# Beyond Monitoring: Intent-Based AI Orchestration for Media over IP Networks on Open Network Infrastructure

**著者**: Kenichi I
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

The broadcast industry's transition from SDI to SMPTE ST 2110 has stalled for the majority of organizations, not due to cost but due to the complexity of network construction — 40% of studios report skill gaps, 30% of early deployments suffer PTP failures. The rapid advancement of AI — from automation tools to autonomous agents capable of real-time optimization and self-healing — is fundamentally rewriting the assumptions of network control engineering. MoIP's core construction challenges — PTP synchronization, multicast routing, NMOS interoperability — are precisely the problem classes where this new generation of AI delivers the greatest advantage. Realizing this advantage requires network infrastructure designed for programmatic AI control, not for human CLI interaction. Open NOS platforms — SONiC foremost among them — provide this foundation: all network state in structured databases, all configuration through native APIs. This paper proposes an Intent-Based AI Orchestration architecture built on SONiC and NMOS that translates broadcast production intent into fully configured, verified, and self-healing ST 2110 networks. We argue that the convergence of AI orchestration with open network infrastructure is a necessary architectural direction for the broadcast industry as it navigates both the MoIP transition and the broader transformation of network control by AI. Keywords: Media over IP, ST 2110, PTP, NMOS, SONiC, Open NOS, Intent-Based Networking, AI Orchestration, Broadcast Engineering, Network Automation, Open Infrastructure
