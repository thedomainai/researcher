# AI-AI Scarcity (AASc)

**著者**: guoguang gan
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

Scarcity serves as the meta-presupposition of economics, classically defined as "limited resources and unlimited desires". Departing from the disciplinary boundary of economics, this paper explores the constituent components and structural origins of this presupposition from the perspectives of cybernetics and information theory.Chapter One extracts seven core elements constituting scarcity from the definitions of five canonical economists, and demonstrates that none of these elements have received rigorous structural definition within traditional economics. Starting from the Human-Human Fundamental Axiom (HHFA), Chapter Two introduces Gan's Black-Box Structure Model (GBS) and the Pointing Alignment Model (PAM) to redefine the seven elements one by one, and reconstructs the theoretical framework of Human-Human Scarcity (HHSc). Chapter Three migrates the seven elements from human systems to artificial intelligence systems, and proposes the core formulation of AI-AI Scarcity (AASc): "limited resources and unlimited internal states".This paper argues that AASc may function as the meta-presupposition of AI economics. Scarcity is not a numerical contrast between resources and desires, but a structural product of signal exchange and ownership assertion in multi-system interactions.
