# Transcending the Weberian Paradox: Fluid Institutions, Agentic Artificial Intelligence, and the Evolution of Post-Bureaucratic State Systems

**著者**: Syed Muntasir Mamun
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

The modern democratic state is characterised by a structural anomaly that has long confounded political sociology and institutional theory: populations of advanced, highly complex societies live under the continuous, dense administration of unelected specialists, yet they periodically delegate supreme formal authority to a single individual selected on the basis of campaign performance and diffuse public trust. Max Weber diagnosed this condition, recognising the permanent, rationalised bureaucracy as the most efficient and indestructible organisational form in human history, while warning of its tendency to become an "iron cage" devoid of ultimate values. To prevent this, he prescribed the charismatic leader, empowered by plebiscitary democracy, who would supply the state with ends and hold the experts in check. This article argues, following Berkovski (2026), that the remedy is itself a paradox: the more specialised the administrative machine, the more the leader depends on the bureaucracy's monopoly over knowledge. Berkovski's Oakeshottian alternative — officialdom checked by rival officialdom — is sound but remains analog, slow and exposed to ossification, capture and the Schmittian critique of liberal parliamentarianism. Drawing on polycentric governance, agile public administration and agentic artificial intelligence, the article proposes a framework of fluid institutions organised on Adaptive, Agentic and Agile (AAA) principles. It maps Ostrom's eight design principles onto algorithmic governance, confronts the documented failures of automated administration, and concludes that the structural permanence of bureaucracy is no longer an insurmountable precondition of state capacity, provided that files are kept open, algorithms auditable and human accountability intact.
