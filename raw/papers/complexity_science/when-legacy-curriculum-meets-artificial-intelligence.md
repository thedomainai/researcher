# When Legacy Curriculum Meets Artificial Intelligence

**著者**: Viktor Wang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence (AI) is transforming occupational tasks, workforce competencies, and relationships between humans and technology faster than many career and technical education (CTE) curricula are changing. This theoretical article introduces the Adaptive CTE Renewal Framework to address the growing mismatch between legacy curriculum and AI-transformed work. Drawing on human capital theory, sociotechnical systems theory, institutional theory and path dependence, and adult/workforce learning theory, the framework conceptualizes curriculum renewal across five interacting dimensions: occupational transformation, curriculum currency, faculty capability alignment, program architecture, and external alignment. Six leadership tests are proposed for evaluating whether courses should be retained, revised, integrated, replaced, or retired. The article argues that conventional periodic curriculum review is increasingly inadequate under conditions of continuous technological disruption and proposes continuous curriculum intelligence as an alternative institutional paradigm. Ultimately, CTE relevance depends on institutions' capacity to continuously align curriculum, faculty expertise, program structures, stakeholder evidence, and workforce competencies with evolving occupational realities.
