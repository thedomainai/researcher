# Probabilistic Differential Geometry in Artificial Intelligence

**著者**: Jincheng Zhang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

This paper explores the application of probabilistic differential geometry (PDG) within the field of artificial intelligence (AI). The core argument is that PDG provides a novel framework for quantifying and processing uncertain information, a critical challenge in modern AI systems. We propose constructing PDG models that represent data as probability distributions and then leveraging the analytical tools of differential geometry for data analysis and learning. This approach aims to move beyond traditional methods reliant on deterministic models, offering a more robust and nuanced understanding of complex datasets. We investigate key concepts like Riemannian manifolds, geodesics, and curvature, adapted for probabilistic contexts, and demonstrate their potential in tasks such as dimensionality reduction, clustering, and learning complex functions. The paper highlights the unique contributions of PDG to AI, focusing on its ability to represent and reason with uncertainty inherent in real-world data. ---
