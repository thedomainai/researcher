# Leverage Points, Paradigms, and Grounded Action: Intervening in Educational Systems

Mitchell M. Olson, Michael A. Raffanti (2006) cited:29

## Abstract

This article discusses connections between grounded action, leverage points, and paradigm transformation and transcendence. Part one provides background on CitationMeadows' (1999) approach to leverage points as well as the concept of paradigms. Part two argues that grounded action is inherently suited for leverage points analysis and paradigm-based interventions. Part three offers an example of individual paradigm transformation in the educational context vis-à-vis CitationOlson's (2006) grounded theory of driven succeeding. The article concludes with considerations of how the application of grounded action in one classroom might leverage system-wide paradigm transformation.
