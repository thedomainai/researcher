# WP3 — The Limits of Systems Structural Instability in Artificial Intelligence

**著者**: Qingyun Hu-Yang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

This collection presents three papers that examine artificial intelligence through the limits of system-based approaches. Rather than treating instability, misalignment, or failure as isolated technical problems, the analysis identifies recurring patterns that persist across architectures, methods, and domains. These patterns include deviation without convergence, the instability of system behavior across contexts, and the inability of alignment mechanisms to sustain consistency at scale. The papers approach these phenomena at the level of system structure. Contemporary systems are typically constructed through the separation of technical, human, and institutional dimensions, followed by their subsequent coupling. While this approach enables functionality, it does not produce coherence. Instead, it requires continuous adjustment, without achieving stable convergence. Across the three papers, system failure is reframed. It is not presented as a breakdown of otherwise stable systems, but as an expression of the conditions under which systems are formed. Under these conditions, instability is not an anomaly but a structural property. This collection does not propose specific technical solutions.
