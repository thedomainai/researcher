# Reimagining Mathematics Education in the Age of Artificial Intelligence: From Problem Solving to the Development of Algorithmic Thinking and Student Creativity

**著者**: Ali Khalil
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

Mathematics education is one of the most important areas of modern educational systems, playing a fundamental role in developing students’ cognitive abilities, logical reasoning, and problem-solving skills. Rapid technological advancements, particularly the emergence and expansion of artificial intelligence, have created an urgent need to reconsider traditional approaches to mathematics education. Today, the purpose of teaching mathematics extends beyond memorizing formulas and performing calculations and increasingly emphasizes the development of problem-solving abilities, algorithmic thinking, creativity, and higher-order cognitive skills. Artificial intelligence offers new opportunities for personalized learning by adapting educational content and feedback to students’ individual needs, abilities, and learning pace. Furthermore, algorithmic thinking enables learners to analyze complex problems systematically and develop logical, step-by-step solutions. Creativity also plays a significant role in encouraging students to explore multiple strategies and innovative approaches to mathematical problems. This study aims to examine the transformation of mathematics education in the age of artificial intelligence, with particular emphasis on problem solving, algorithmic thinking, and student creativity. The research adopts a descriptive-analytical approach and is based on a review of relevant educational and scientific literature. The findings suggest that the effective integration of artificial intelligence into mathematics education can contribute to more personalized, engaging, and meaningful learning experiences. However, technology should function as a supportive tool rather than a replacement for teachers. Therefore, the future of mathematics education requires a balanced integration of human expertise, innovative pedagogy, and intelligent technologies to prepare students for the challenges of an increasingly complex digital world.
