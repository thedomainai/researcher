# Enabling Resilient and Adaptive Production Operations Through AI-Enabled Production System Optimization at Scale

**著者**: R. Mohan, S. Al Muhairi, M. Al Hashemi, A. Alsereidi, Mohamed Khaled Almenhali
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Abstract ADNOC has deployed an AI-enabled Production System Optimization (AiPSO) solution to strengthen the resilience, flexibility, and responsiveness of production operations under increasing global supply demand uncertainty. This paper presents the value outcomes from the Phase-1 implementation across eight producing fields, demonstrating how real-time production intelligence and rapid scenario planning enable proactive, compliant, and value-driven production operations at scale. AiPSO is implemented as an integrated Production System Digital Twin spanning reservoirs, wells, and surface production systems, combining physics-based engineering models with AI-driven analytics. A scalable Industrial Data and AI Foundation automates real-time data ingestion, validation, contextualization, and model orchestration through a centralized DataOps pipeline. Core workflows include exception-based surveillance, automated diagnostics, scenario-aware production optimization, and execution tracking. Generative AI capabilities evolve from an engineering copilot to domain-specific advisory services and AiPSO Agents, accelerating scenario evaluation and shortening the decision-to-action cycle. Real-time engineering operating envelopes enable safe, constraint-aware execution and controlled autonomy. The Phase-1 deployment of AiPSO across eight producing fields during 2025–2026 delivered measurable business value by enhancing ADNOC's ability to anticipate, evaluate, and respond to production variability and operational uncertainty. More than 25 advanced digital workflows were operationalized, enabling real-time surveillance, automated diagnostics, and intelligent optimization, resulting in a sustained 1–2% production uplift with no incremental CAPEX. Key workflows, including exception-based well surveillance, artificial-lift diagnostics, multiphase flowmeter validation, and integrated production monitoring, significantly improved data confidence, asset visibility, and decision quality. The integrated digital twin enabled earlier identification of reservoir, well, and surface constraints, reducing time-to-intervention and improving production stability. Operations shifted from reactive issue resolution to proactive, scenario-driven constraint management. GenAI-enabled workflows accelerated root-cause analysis and response planning, while real-time operating envelopes supported safe closed-loop adjustments, strengthening operational resilience and supply reliability. This paper presents a multi-field, enterprise-scale application of AI-driven production system digital twins focused on operational resilience and rapid scenario planning. The integration of Generative AI evolving from engineering copilots to action-ready AiPSO Agents represents a novel advancement beyond traditional digital oilfield practices, enabling proactive, adaptive, and increasingly autonomous production operations across large, complex upstream assets.
