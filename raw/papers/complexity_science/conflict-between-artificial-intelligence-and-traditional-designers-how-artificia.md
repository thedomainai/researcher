# Conflict Between Artificial Intelligence and Traditional Designers: "How Artificial Intelligence Transforms the Design Process"

**著者**: 
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is rapidly changing the field of design.Today, tools like Midjourney, DALL•E, and Adobe Firefly are becoming common in design studios.This change has created a serious debate between AI and traditional designers.Many designers fear that AI will replace their jobs and take away their creativity.This research paper studies the relationship between AI and human designers and tries to find a practical solution.The paper has three main goals.First, it examines how AI has evolved in the design process and what specific tasks it has replaced.Second, it discusses the future impact of AI on design practice and the professional identity of designers.Third, it proposes a new model called the Human-AI Collaborative Design (HACD) Framework, which shows how humans and AI can work together effectively.The HACD Framework has five stages: Context Definition, Concept Exploration, Creation & Iteration, Curation & Refinement, and Completion & Delivery.In each stage, the leadership ratio between human and AI changes.The main idea of this model is simple: AI generates possibilities, while humans decide purpose.AI is good at speed and scale, but humans are good at judgment, emotion, and responsibility.The paper argues that the future of design is not about choosing between human and machine.Instead, it is about structured collaboration, where both work together as partners.This research gives designers, educators, and organizations a practical roadmap to use AI without losing creative control, ethical values, or professional identity.
