# Contemporary perspectives in social science commerce and physical education: Issues, Challenges and Future Direction

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Contemporary perspectives in social science, commerce, and physical education reflect the dynamic interplay of globalization, technological advancement, and evolving societal needs. Social sciences today emphasize interdisciplinary approaches to understand complex human behavior, social structures, and policy challenges in an increasingly digital and interconnected world. Issues such as inequality, cultural diversity, and data-driven decision-making continue to reshape research priorities and methodologies. In commerce, rapid digital transformation, e-commerce expansion, and globalization have introduced both opportunities and challenges. Businesses face concerns related to sustainability, ethical practices, consumer behavior shifts, and technological disruptions such as artificial intelligence and big data. The need for innovative strategies, adaptive leadership, and responsible governance is central to addressing these challenges and ensuring long-term growth. Physical education has also undergone significant transformation, with increasing recognition of its role in holistic development, health promotion, and social inclusion. However, challenges such as lack of infrastructure, insufficient funding, limited curriculum standardization, and inadequate teacher training persist. These issues are compounded by declining student participation and competing academic priorities. Future directions emphasize integrating technology, promoting inclusive and flexible curricula, and strengthening policy support to enhance engagement and effectiveness. Overall, the convergence of these fields highlights the importance of interdisciplinary research, policy innovation, and sustainable practices. Addressing current challenges requires collaborative efforts among educators, policymakers, and industry stakeholders to create resilient systems that are responsive to future societal and economic demands.
