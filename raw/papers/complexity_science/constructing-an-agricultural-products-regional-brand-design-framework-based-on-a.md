# Constructing an Agricultural Products Regional Brand Design Framework Based on Artificial Intelligence and Digital Twins Technology

**著者**: Peiyun Cheng, Yang Wang, Zixuan Hu
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

Brand building for agricultural products in the mountainous regions of southwest China faces significant challenges, including complex terrain, fragmented industrial-chain data, and low market recognition. Addressing these issues requires technological innovation to enhance brand value. This study proposes a novel regional brand design paradigm that integrates Digital Twins (DT) technology with Artificial Intelligence (AI) to create a brand empowerment system capable of dynamic mapping, intelligent decision-making, and full-chain traceability. A dual-driven DT framework was established, encompassing both physical entities and virtual models. Specifically, a terrain-adaptive perception network was developed to tackle fragmented data in complex mountainous environments. AI algorithm clusters were implemented, including an improved ResNet50 model for appearance defect detection, achieving 95.2% accuracy, and a coupled Graph Neural Network–Deep Q-Network (GNN-DQN) model for supply chain risk scheduling, reducing response time by 67.4%. Pilot studies in the Zunyi chili and Pu’er tea regions validated the methodology using rigorous quantitative metrics. This study develops a pilot-validated closed-loop digital branding system that transforms ecological, quality, logistics, and consumer data into measurable outcomes, including brand awareness, perceived quality, consumer trust, willingness-to-pay, and consumer engagement. The proposed system provides a technology-enabled pathway for regional agricultural product branding, precision agricultural management, and rural industrial development.
