# Enhancing Global Resilience Through Collaborative Artificial Intelligence Initiatives

**著者**: Amrik Singh
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has emerged as a transformative force, capable of strengthening global resilience in the face of complex, interconnected challenges such as climate change, pandemics, economic instability, and technological disruption. As societies increasingly rely on data-driven decision-making, collaborative AI initiatives offer significant opportunities to enhance crisis preparedness, response, and recovery at global, national, and organizational levels. This chapter examines the role of AI collaboration in building global resilience by integrating technological innovation with international cooperation. It discusses how AI applications in disaster management, healthcare, supply chain systems, and environmental sustainability contribute to adaptive and resilient systems. The chapter also highlights the importance of multi-stakeholder collaboration involving governments, industry, academia, and international organizations in developing responsible and inclusive AI frameworks.
