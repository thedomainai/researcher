# AI-Powered Iso 14224 Chatbot for Intelligent Reliability and Maintenance Data Classification

**著者**: S. B. Al Hajri, A. S. Al Araimi, S. Mohammed
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

Abstract This paper presents the development and pre-production validation of an artificial intelligence (AI) system designed to automate maintenance work order classification in accordance with ISO 14224 standards and to enable intelligent reliability analytics for oil and gas operations. The system supports all major equipment classes —including rotating equipment, mechanical systems, instrumentation, and safety-critical assets—across upstream, midstream, and downstream facilities. A Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architecture integrates open-source reasoning models (Qwen3-30B-Thinking and K2-Thinking) with a Milvus vector database containing embedded ISO 14224 taxonomies covering 64 standardized failure modes. A domain-specific knowledge graph links equipment hierarchies, failure modes, process units, trains, and operational contexts to enable context-aware classification and analytical queries. Initial system validation was performed on a dataset of 300 historical work orders, achieving an average classification accuracy of approximately 70% against a ground truth established by reliability engineers and without any supervised model training. A pre-production improvement phase is now underway using approximately 1,000 work orders, with a target accuracy of 95% within one to two months of active use by reliability engineers. Beyond classification accuracy, the system delivers substantial operational value by reducing ISO 14224 data preparation time from approximately six months of manual effort by two reliability engineers for 10,000 work orders to less than seven days, while targeting an error rate below 5%. In addition, the system enables interactive reliability queries to be answered within seconds rather than weeks, fundamentally transforming how failure data is accessed and used for engineering decision-making.
