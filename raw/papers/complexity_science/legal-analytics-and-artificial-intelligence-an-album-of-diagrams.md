# Legal analytics and artificial intelligence. An album of diagrams

**著者**: Vladimir Isakov
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

The textbook includes diagrams and drawings developed in the course "Legal Analytics and artificial intelligence" and linked to the textbook by a system of links. Drawings and diagrams are based on imaginative thinking, which is important for analyzing legal information, evaluating documents, and drawing conclusions. They help to actively involve students in the learning process, and ensure effective interaction between natural and artificial intelligence. Visual materials are especially useful for organizing students' independent work, as well as for distance learning, as they allow for a clear structure of the educational material and ensure its visibility. The album of diagrams is intended for students, undergraduates and postgraduates of law schools and faculties, as well as for teachers, methodologists, experts and analysts in the field of law.
