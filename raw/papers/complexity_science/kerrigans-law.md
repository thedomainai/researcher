# Kerrigan's Law

**著者**: Mercy Kerrigan
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

Description: Temporal synchrony and spatial self-organization are often conflated in studies of complex systems. I demonstrate that these phenomena are controlled by independent parameters. My v1 established Kerrigan Baseline: 1-tick delay coupling produces 100% global synchrony independent of N. v1 used a 71x71 world, leaving spatial dynamics unexplored. This work, v2.2: Kerrigan's Law, addresses spatial dynamics. Using NetLogo simulations of delay-coupled agents, I show that while temporal synchrony is density-invariant, spatial topology emerges only above a critical density threshold ρ_c. At N=1000, sustained flock polarization of 0.97-0.98 is maintained for >300,000 ticks while flash sync remains at ~14%. This defines a novel polarized incoherent flock phase where collective motion order is decoupled from pulse synchrony. I propose Kerrigan's Law: delay coupling + ρ > ρ_c → spatial self-organization. Implications extend to morphogenesis, disorders of consciousness, and decentralized AI. Dedicated to the 5 year old on the grass who first saw the lace.
