# Perceptual assessment of artificial intelligence assisted learning systems among students

**著者**: Paul Danquah, Henry Matey Akwetey, Michael L. Wilson
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

Incorporation of artificial intelligence (AI) in educational settings has transformed education through adaptive systems and intelligent tools. However, students’ perceptions remain critical for understanding the adoption and utilization of AI-powered learning platforms. This research examines determinants of student perceptions by extending the technology acceptance model (TAM) to include trust in AI (TRUSTIA), perceived usefulness (PU), ethical concerns (EC), and perceived risk (PR). Partial least squares structural equation modeling (PLS-SEM) with bootstrapping 5,000 subsamples was used to assess the quantitative data gathered from university students. Research findings indicate that PU significantly influences both behavioral intentions towards AI assisted learning (BIASL) (β=0.240, p<0.001) and TRUSTIA (β=0.222, p<0.001). TRUSTIA shows a negative but minimal significant effect on BIASL (β=−0.197, p=0.050). EC strongly and positively affect BIASL (β=0.695, p<0.001) and significantly increase PR (β=0.162, p<0.001). However, PR does not significantly affect BIASL (β=0.118, p=0.227). The findings highlight PU and EC as key drivers of behavioral outcomes, with trust playing a mitigating role. The findings emphasize the essence of ethical considerations in adoption of AI in education.
