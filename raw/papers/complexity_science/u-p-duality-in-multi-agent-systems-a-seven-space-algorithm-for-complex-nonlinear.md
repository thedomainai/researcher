# U-P Duality in Multi-Agent Systems: A Seven-Space Algorithm for Complex Nonlinear AI (Corrected Version)

**著者**: Xie Meng
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

Multi-agent systems are a core research area in distributed artificial intelligence, where algorithm design faces challenges due to complex nonlinear environments. This paper presents a seven-space algorithm based on U-P duality, including U-space for stabilization, P-space for asynchronous processing, V-space for exploration, Z-space for adaptive regulation, Q-space for anomaly detection, R-space for redundancy backup, and S-space for scene understanding. Each space is derived from a common fixed-point relation, with behavior regulated by a parameter s. Numerical experiments in autonomous driving perception, distributed training, and reinforcement learning show that near the critical line, P-space achieves accurate multi-sensor fusion, P+R combination achieves robust fault tolerance, and V-space provides balanced exploration-exploitation. Derivative relations provide closed-form expressions for gradient-based optimization. The seven spaces constitute a set of algorithms for nonlinear AI domains, with mathematical guarantees from fixed-point theory. Users can select parameters according to specific scenarios to obtain adapted behaviors.
