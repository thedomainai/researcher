# ASSESSMENT AND EVALUATION IN EDUCATION IN THE AGE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

**著者**: Hacı Hasan Yolcu
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

As artificial intelligence technologies take over tasks we regard as drudgery, they open up an unprecedented realm of freedom for humankind in history. In this new era, qualities unique to humans such as creativity and critical thinking are becoming more important than ever. In this context, it is an important matter for educators to redesign teaching processes, especially assessment and evaluation processes, in a way that centers human-specific skills. If education systems cannot structure artificial intelligence as a tool that supports human-specific skills, human-specific attributes may face the risk of atrophying in the shadow of technological comfort. Educational institutions that do not take this transformation and its risks into account, or are late in doing so, may over time become dysfunctional by losing their impact on the development of the human mind. This study offers concrete suggestions for designing assessment and evaluation processes in the age of artificial intelligence in a way that supports human-specific skills and aims to provide educators with new perspectives regarding assessment and evaluation processes.
