# Intelligent Frontiers in Emerging Technologies: AI, Sustainability, and Multidisciplinary Innovations

## Abstract

Intelligent Frontiers in Emerging Technologies: AI, Sustainability, and Multidisciplinary Innovations is a multidisciplinary edited research volume that brings together contemporary research and emerging developments across Artificial Intelligence, engineering, sustainable technologies, energy systems, computer science, healthcare, cybersecurity, education, and advanced technological applications. The book highlights the growing importance of intelligent and sustainable approaches in addressing complex challenges in science, technology, and society. The volume presents research chapters covering a diverse range of emerging topics, including AI-driven 6G telecommunications, intelligent resource optimization, renewable energy integration, smart grids, digital twin technologies, adaptive control systems, cyber-physical security, machine learning, deep learning, smart healthcare, natural product chemistry, and sustainable concrete technology. It also includes perspectives on educational innovation and the integration of heritage and the Indian Knowledge System. A major focus of the book is the convergence of Artificial Intelligence and sustainability. The chapters demonstrate how intelligent computational methods, advanced engineering techniques, automation, data-driven decision-making, and innovative technologies can contribute to improved efficiency, resilience, resource management, and sustainable development. The multidisciplinary nature of the volume encourages the integration of knowledge from different fields to develop practical and innovative solutions for real-world problems. The book is intended for research scholars, academicians, faculty members, students, engineers, technology professionals, and researchers who are interested in emerging technologies and their applications. It provides a useful reference for understanding current research directions, technological challenges, innovative approaches, and future opportunities across diverse disciplines. Through its collection of research-oriented chapters, this volume aims to promote interdisciplinary knowledge exchange, technological innovation, sustainable development, and intelligent solutions for future societal and industrial needs. It reflects the rapidly changing technological landscape and emphasizes the role of multidisciplinary collaboration in shaping a more intelligent, resilient, and sustainable future.
