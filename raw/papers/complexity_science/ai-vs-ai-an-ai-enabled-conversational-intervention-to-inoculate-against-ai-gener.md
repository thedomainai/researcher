# AI vs. AI: An AI-Enabled Conversational Intervention to Inoculate Against AI-Generated Fake News

**著者**: Mohammad S. Najjar, Murtaza Nasir
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

Abstract Generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) is a double-edged sword that can be used to amplify the proliferation of highly sophisticated fake news as well as to fight it. In this work, we explore a new route: using one AI to inoculate humans against fake news generated by another AI. Motivated by psychological inoculation theory, we created ‘Alex’, a conversational AI agent that engages users in an adaptive conversational dialogue designed to build cognitive resilience against AI-generated, AI-related fake news. We tested its efficacy in a Solomon four-group experiment with 542 participants. The results of a 2 × 2 ANOVA show a significant main effect of the intervention on participants’ discrimination of true and false headlines, with no significant evidence of pretest sensitization. We show that AI can be designed as a scalable, interactive conversational intervention for delivering psychological inoculation against AI-generated, AI-related fake news.
