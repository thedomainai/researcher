# ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE – COMPETITOR OR PARTNER? THE FUTURE OF THE LIBRARY INSTITUTION

**著者**: Naimova Binafsha Sharif qizi, Worldly Knowledge Publishing Centre
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

The rapid advancement of artificial intelligence (AI) has profoundly reshaped contemporary knowledge ecosystems, transforming how information is accessed, processed, and internalized. As algorithmic systems increasingly mediate learning and information retrieval, traditional reading practices and library-centered knowledge cultures appear to be diminishing. This study critically examines whether artificial intelligence constitutes a disruptive replacement or a complementary force for library institutions. Through analytical and philosophical reflection, the paper argues that while AI enhances efficiency, personalization, and accessibility, it lacks the cultural depth, epistemological responsibility, and ethical mediation inherent to libraries. Consequently, the future sustainability of libraries depends not on resisting AI, but on integrating it as an intellectual partner that reinforces libraries’ role as guardians of critical thinking, cultural memory, and knowledge integrity in the digital age.
