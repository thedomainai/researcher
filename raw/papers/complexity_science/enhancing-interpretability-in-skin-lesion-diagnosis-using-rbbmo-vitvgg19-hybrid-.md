# Enhancing Interpretability in Skin Lesion Diagnosis Using RBBMO-ViT–VGG19 Hybrid Framework with Intra-class Consistency and Inter-class Discrimination

**著者**: Praveena S, T. Veeramakali
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

The skin lesions are need to be accurately and promptly recognized for an effective treatments and favourable prognoses.Nevertheless, the common of existing deep learning model only focus on lesion classification, leaving out the simultaneous prediction of important morphological and colorimetric characteristics that are essential for interpretability.To enhance the interpretability of automated skin lesion analysis, this paper develops an improved hybrid framework that incorporates optimization-driven deep learning.An Adaptive Non-Local Mean (ANLM) filter is exploited to reduce image noise to maintain structural details.Consequently, the Type-2 Fuzzy C-Means (T2FCM) clustering is exploited to segment lesion zones for accurate boundary identification.The incorporation of Vision Transformer (ViT) and VGG19 is deployed for feature extraction to capture both global contextual patterns and hierarchical texture representations.To enhance feature separability in the latent space, the framework maximizes inter-class discrimination while encouraging intra-class consistency.A sparse auto encoder compresses the highdimensional perturbation space to lessen duplication while preserving meaningful representations.Moreover, t-SNE visualization latent space eliminates unnecessary perturbation samples and enhances visualization clarity.Red-Billed Blue Magpie Optimization (RBBMO) is employed for parameter refinement to enhance classification boundaries and stabilize convergence.Local Interpretable Model Agnostic Explanations (LIME) is utilized to develop perturbation based interpretations that guarantee explainability.When compared to traditional explainable AI techniques, experimental outcomes implemented in Python tool show better diagnostic accuracy of 99.02% in single run evaluation and 99.44% ± 0.82% across five random seeds.Thus, the suggested method enhances automated dermatological diagnostic systems' transparency, dependability and clinical effectiveness.
