# Patient Trust In Ai Self-Diagnosis

**著者**: Vishal Uppala, Supavich Pengnate
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

The use of artificial intelligence (AI) for self-diagnosis is widespread and continues to grow.Effective adoption of AI depends not only on its accuracy but also on patient trust.This study examines trust in AI healthcare platforms, focusing on two key antecedents: AI benevolence (empathy) and competence.We propose a model in which both factors influence trust, with the effect of competence moderated by task complexity.A quasi-experimental methodology is planned to test how users' perceptions of AI benevolence and competence shape trust.The findings aim to inform the safe and effective integration of AI in healthcare, highlighting trust as a critical factor beyond functional performance.
