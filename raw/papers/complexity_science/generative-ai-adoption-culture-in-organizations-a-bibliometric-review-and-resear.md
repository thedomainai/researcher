# Generative AI adoption culture in organizations: A bibliometric review and research agenda (2022–2026)

**著者**: Rini Fatmawati, Amelia Salsabila Khairunnisa
**年**: 2027 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

Generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) has reshaped organizational work since the release of ChatGPT in late 2022, yet research on the cultural dimensions of its adoption remains fragmented. This study synthesizes the emerging field through a bibliometric review combining performance analysis, science mapping, and the Theory–Context–Characteristics–Methodology (TCCM) framework. A Scopus search executed on 5 May 2026 yielded 257 records, refined to 128 by automatic filters, and reduced to 69 articles after PRISMA 2020 screening. Analysis employed bibliometrix (R 4.3.0) and four VOSviewer 1.6.20 networks. The field exhibits an annual growth rate of 114.07%, with publications rising from one in 2022 to 36 in 2025 and 21 in early 2026 across 65 sources and 23 countries (China leads with 12 articles), anchored by Ng et al. (2025), Jo and Bang (2023), and Lee and Park (2024). The keyword map yields four thematic clusters; the co-citation map reveals three intellectual sub-structures (Technology Acceptance, Methodological and Applied GenAI, and GenAI in Management). Both layers converge on a technology-acceptance dominant, methodologically homogeneous, and culturally thin configuration: classical culture references (Schein, Cameron, and Quinn) and dedicated AI-ethics works do not reach the co-citation core. To redress this asymmetry, we propose an integrative framework: Leadership Vision → AI Literacy and Trust → Psychological Safety → Ethical Climate → GenAI Adoption Culture → Productivity, Innovation, and Well-being. The agenda outlines five priorities, including cross-cultural instruments, longitudinal multi-wave designs, and emerging-market studies foregrounding Indonesia and ASEAN.
