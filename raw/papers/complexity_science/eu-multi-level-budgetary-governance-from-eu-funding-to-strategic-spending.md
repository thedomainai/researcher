# EU multi-level budgetary governance: from EU funding to strategic spending

**著者**: Maria Patrin
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

Abstract In the emerging paradigm of “integration through funding” the budget is increasingly becoming an instrument of EU governance. In this context, cohesion policy is evolving from a funding scheme to a form of strategic spending, leading to the emergence of a new EU budgetary governance, whose key features are the multi-level nature and the conditionality regimes. The paper investigates these emerging trends in EU budgetary governance from a legal perspective, focusing on the centralisation of decision-making, the relationship between levels of government and the Union’s democratic legitimacy. First, building upon theories of multi-level governance, the paper argues that, whereas cohesion policy has traditionally favoured decentralised decision-making, the redirection and repurposing of funding as a form of EU strategic spending tends to recentre decision-making around the relationship between the EU and national executives. Second, the shift towards a more strategic use of spending has enhanced the potential of cohesion funds to serve EU enforcement and compliance purposes, yet it carries two significant accountability and legitimacy concerns: a risk of permanent executive dominance and the stretching of EU enforcement channels beyond the power allocation of the Treaties.
