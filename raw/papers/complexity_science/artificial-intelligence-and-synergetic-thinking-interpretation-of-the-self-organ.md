# ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND SYNERGETIC THINKING: INTERPRETATION OF THE SELF-ORGANIZATION PHENOMENON IN COMPLEX SYSTEMS

**著者**: Tajibayev Muxiddin Abdurashidovich
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

This article analyzes the relationship between Artificial Intelligence (AI) systems, the cornerstone of modern technological progress, and synergetics, an interdisciplinary science studying the laws of non-linear complex systems. Unlike traditional cybernetic approaches, the author interprets AI as a dynamic, self-organizing, and evolving open system. The paper provides a philosophical and technical justification for the emergence of order out of chaos in Big Data streams, self-adaptation of neural networks, and the concept of cognitive synergetics. The research demonstrates that the integration of human intelligence and AI will shape a non-linear synergetic thinking culture in the future.
