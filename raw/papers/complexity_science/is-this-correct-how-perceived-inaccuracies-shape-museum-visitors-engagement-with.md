# Is this correct? How Perceived Inaccuracies Shape Museum Visitors' Engagement with a Virtual Expert

**著者**: Marcus Winter, Phil Blume
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

This paper examines museum visitors' perceptions of inaccuracies in the responses of a conversational agent, cast as a virtual expert on the history and architecture of a heritage building, and how they shape their engagement and learning. It discusses the curatorial need addressed by the agent, its technical design and implementation, and its empirical evaluation with visitors to the building. Findings show that visitors engaged extensively with the agent and appreciated the direct access to expertise, despite expressing a preference for human interaction. Most participants reported that the agent helped them to learn about the building's history and architecture. While many qualified this by expressing doubts about the provided information, perceived inaccuracies also provided opportunities for deeper engagement and independent inquiry. The highly rated user experience, potential for learning, and open, low-energy, privacy-preserving technical design strongly align with curatorial goals. The paper's main contribution, empirical findings on how perceived inaccuracies affect visitor's engagement and learning, is relevant for practitioners and researchers designing conversational agents in heritage contexts.
