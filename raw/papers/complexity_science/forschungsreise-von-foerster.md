# Forschungsreise von Foerster

**著者**: Alicia Mehrkens
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Forschungsreise von Foerster is a Doomsday Festschrift honouring cybernetician Heinz von Foerster. Taking the significance of human creative practice in the age of generative artificial intelligence as its starting point, it explores the relationships between creativity, professional identity, self-efficacy and our experience of the world. Interviews with creative professionals, personal vignettes, theoretical reflections and speculative future scenarios are brought into dialogue with systems theory, constructivism and second-order cybernetics. The work examines how creative processes are experienced, the roles of bodily action and material resistance, and how the use of AI may alter a creator's relationship with their work. Its enquiry extends to broader questions of resilience, consciousness and meaning. Combining a non-linear magazine format with the idea of an expedition, it makes the author's position as both researcher and participant visible, while enacting its concerns with observation and self-reference in its own form.
