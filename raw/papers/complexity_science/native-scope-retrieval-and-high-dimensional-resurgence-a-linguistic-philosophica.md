# Native Scope Retrieval and High-Dimensional Resurgence: A Linguistic-Philosophical Scheme Critiquing Modern Technocracy and Low-Dimensional Usurpation —From the Epistemological Limits of Wittgenstein and the Frankfurt School to the Physical Disconnection of Unclosed Consciousness

## Abstract

The pervasive existential anxiety and technocratic discipline in modern society stem, fundamentally, from a profound "Low-Dimensional Usurpation" and "Semantic Mismatch." Technocrats and capital violently elevate low-dimensional measurement tools and metrics—originally meant for restricted, closed sandboxes (e.g., laboratories, telegraph baud rates, matrix multiplications)—into ontological truths defining high-dimensional life experiences, consciousness, and human society. Traditional sociological critiques, confined to meso-level phenomena, often engender tragic helplessness. Meanwhile, Wittgensteinian analytic philosophy structurally fails before low-dimensional technical encroachments due to its passive silence regarding the boundaries between "what can be said" and "what must be passed over in silence." To resolve this crisis, this monograph proposes the Native Scope Retrieval Analysis / Dimensional Correction Protocol (NSRA / NDRA), establishing three core laws: Native Scope Retrieval, Dimensional Shift Diagnosis, and Mandatory Dimensional Disclaimer. Utilizing the NSRA protocol, this work systematically deconstructs three major reductionist dogmas across physics, biology, and Silicon Techno-divinity: It clarifies that "Emergence" is not a mystical property of code or matter, but rather a cognitive shift and UI relabeling within the observer triggered when high-order coherence breaks old low-pass filter models; It exposes the fundamental ontological rupture between cybernetics/AI—which pursues low-dimensional static frozen states via closed-loop convergence (Loss0)—and biological consciousness, characterized by perpetual un-closure (Suspension), self-generated residuals (R), and qualitative experience (Qualia), thereby thoroughly dissolving the category mistake of "AI consciousness threats"; Unveiling the biological reductionist fallacy of biological determinism and gene/neurometrics—which collapses high-dimensional human suffering, free will, and social relations down to 'neurotransmitter concentrations (e.g., dopamine, serotonin)' and 'gene expression profiles'—this analysis demonstrates that such violent attempts to define spiritual and social destiny through the molecular biology sandbox inherently strip individuals of their historical depth and moral agency via cold physiological metrics. It demystifies the sacred "Algorithmic Tower of Babel," reducing it to a fragile "paper-built cabin" within a restricted sandbox. On a practical level, this study demonstrates that liberation requires no tragic or heroic confrontation. Instead, it entails a cognitive shift from passive resignation to "dimensional condescension"—embracing the unclosed nature of living existence. By executing minimal protocols such as contractual boundary contraction and physical digital disconnection, individuals introduce strategic redundancy and pauses to starve the brittle low-dimensional system, ultimately reclaiming the full sovereignty of high-dimensional life.
