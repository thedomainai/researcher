# Educating Inferential Thinking in the Age of Artificial Intelligence

**著者**: Antonella Nuzzaci
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

In an era increasingly mediated by artificial intelligence (AI), education must move beyond a purely technical view of digital literacy to foster the inferential and epistemic capacities underlying critical and autonomous thinking. This theoretical-conceptual contribution argues that inferential thinking—the ability to draw conclusions, formulate hypotheses, and construct meaning from incomplete information—represents a key competence for responsible learning in hybrid human–AI environments. Drawing on cognitive and educational theory, the paper examines how deduction, induction, and abduction are reshaped through interaction with AI systems and proposes a pedagogical framework to promote epistemic responsibility, metacognitive awareness, and reflective judgment. By integrating Explainable AI (XAI), abductive reasoning, and metacognitive practices, it reconceptualizes AI literacy as inferential literacy and outlines a pedagogy of human–AI co-inquiry aimed at fostering epistemic responsibility, ethical awareness, and cognitive autonomy in complex learning contexts.
