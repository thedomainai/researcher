# Digital Twin-Driven Optimization of Mechanical Manufacturing Systems Using Artificial Intelligence

**著者**: Bipul Tuhin, Susmita Shammy, Lipi Akter, Hussam Elddin Nabieh Khasawneh, Joytun Sujon
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

Industry 4.0 integrates cyber–physical systems, industrial Internet of Things technologies, artificial intelligence (AI), and real-time analytics to improve manufacturing performance. However, small- and medium-scale manufacturing facilities frequently face fragmented automation, reactive maintenance, and limited integration between production data and operational decision-making. This study developed and experimentally evaluated an AI-enabled Digital Twin (DT) framework for optimizing a multi-station mechanical manufacturing system in Bangladesh. The manufacturing cell comprised six interconnected stations: raw-material inspection and sorting, automated loading and orientation, CNC machining, drilling and cutting, AI-assisted quality inspection, and automated storage and dispatch. A synchronized DT represented machine states, production flow, queue formation, operating parameters, energy consumption, tool condition, vibration, temperature, and quality outcomes. An AI layer supported anomaly detection and cycle-time prediction, while a multi-objective optimization mechanism balanced throughput and Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE) against cycle time, energy consumption, defect rate, and unplanned downtime. Three operating configurations were evaluated: conventional programmable logic controller (PLC)-based operation, DT-assisted operation, and DT–AI optimization. During the six-hour production period, the DT–AI configuration increased throughput from 338 to 405 parts, reduced average cycle time from 63.9 to 53.3 s/part, and increased OEE from 67.6% to 84.6%. Energy consumption decreased from 54.8 to 47.6 kWh, the defect rate declined from 5.9% to 2.6%, and unplanned downtime decreased from 38.4 to 17.9 min. The anomaly classifier achieved an accuracy of 94.6%, while the cycle-time prediction model achieved a coefficient of determination (R²) of 0.94. These findings demonstrate the potential of DT–AI integration to improve productivity, energy efficiency, and operational reliability under the evaluated conditions. The framework offers a practical basis for progressive digital transformation in Bangladeshi mechanical manufacturing facilities, with further validation needed to establish its applicability across other production environments.
