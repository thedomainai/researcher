# PSYCHOLOGICAL CHARACTERISTICS OF THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND CRITICAL THINKING

**著者**: Mehrigul Abdurasulova
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

The rapid development of artificial intelligence (AI) technologies has significantly transformed the ways individuals access, process, and evaluate information. While AI systems provide unprecedented opportunities for learning, decision-making, and problem-solving, concerns have emerged regarding their potential influence on independent thinking and critical reasoning. This article examines the psychological characteristics of the relationship between artificial intelligence and critical thinking. Particular attention is given to cognitive processes, metacognition, reflection, information evaluation, and decision-making mechanisms. Drawing on contemporary psychological theories and empirical findings, the paper argues that artificial intelligence can serve both as a facilitator and a constraint on critical thinking, depending on the manner in which it is utilized. A conceptual psychological model is proposed to explain the pathways through which AI influences critical thinking skills. The study concludes that the development of metacognitive awareness, information literacy, and reflective thinking is essential for ensuring that AI functions as a tool for intellectual enhancement rather than a substitute for human cognition.
