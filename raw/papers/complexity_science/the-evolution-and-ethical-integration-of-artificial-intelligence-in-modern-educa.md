# THE EVOLUTION AND ETHICAL INTEGRATION OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN MODERN EDUCATION: A FRAMEWORK FOR PERSONALIZED LEARNING AND INSTITUTIONAL ACCOUNTABILITY

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence (AI) has come a long way from its early theoretical days in the mid -1950s to become an advanced, data-based system that can learn from experience and execute jobs automatically. At present times, AI acts as a game-changer in education by making possible customized educational experiences based on the use of machine learning frameworks that provide individual guidance to every student, taking into account their speed, behaviour, preferences, and accomplishments. The change of this technological nature means that the role of a teacher must be shifted to the one who should now only provide guidance and help in, additionally, overseeing the machines. Beyond classrooms, AI adoption is a priority for national policies. For instance, India’s National Education Policy 2020 requires the teaching of AI and computational thinking in schools. The curriculum is set to start from Grade 3 in the academic year 2026-27. However, the rise of AI may create some challenges in terms of socio-economics. While AIED researchers are usually driven by the goals of enhancing learning results, the experience from other fields of AI indicates that good intentions do not guarantee ethical use of the technology. Important issues such as data ownership, algorithm bias, transparency, and student agency still remain unresolved. There is a necessity of collaborative endeavours in the creation of a community-wide framework in addressing the difference between "doing ethical things" and "doing things ethically. The PRISMA (Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses) framework is a prominent method for ensuring transparency and repeatability in systematic reviews and can be used in the mapping of the enormous literature in this evolving sphere, while the mentioned resources mainly rely on expert surveys of leading scientists to define the major shortcomings.
