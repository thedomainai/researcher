# Participatory Organizations in the AI Era: Part IV Explanation, Participation, and Navigational Closure

**著者**: Natsue Tanaka
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

This paper examines a structural condition emerging within increasingly AI-assisted organizational environments: the possibility that expanding explanatory capacity may unintentionally weaken participatory continuity. As AI systems reduce the cost of generating procedural clarification, contextual explanation, and defensive communication, organizations may increasingly produce environments characterized not by information scarcity, but by explanatory overproduction. The paper proposes a distinction between explanation and navigation, arguing that interpretive completeness does not automatically preserve participatory orientation. In some conditions, expanding explanation may generate navigational friction, procedural hesitation, interpretive self-monitoring, and participatory destabilization. The paper introduces concepts including participatory closure through explanation, defensive explainability, and navigational saturation, while examining how participation-preserving communication may become increasingly important within AI-assisted systems. Rather than opposing explanation or transparency, the paper offers a structural observation: explanation and navigation are distinct organizational functions requiring separate forms of coordination. This paper constitutes Part IV of the “Participatory Organizations in the AI Era” series.
