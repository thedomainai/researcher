# Algorithmic Self-Organization of Complex Systems

**著者**: Jincheng Zhang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

This paper explores the potential of algorithmic self-organization – a paradigm shift from traditional engineering approaches – to generate complex, self-organizing systems from simple rules and feedback loops. We introduce a novel framework leveraging genetic algorithms to evolve systems, fostering emergent properties previously considered unattainable. The core mechanism centers around a system of "genetic algorithms" that iteratively refine systems based on the rules, creating increasingly complex and adaptive patterns. The paper details the methodology, proposes a preliminary system architecture, and discusses the implications of this approach for understanding and potentially engineering complex systems across diverse disciplines, including biology, physics, and artificial intelligence. The central claim is that this framework offers a pathway to create truly novel and dynamic systems, moving beyond predefined constraints towards emergent behavior.
