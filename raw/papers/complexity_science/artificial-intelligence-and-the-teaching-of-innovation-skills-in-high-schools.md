# Artificial Intelligence and the Teaching of Innovation Skills in High Schools

**著者**: Forouzan Khayyat
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 複雑系科学

## Abstract

The aim of this study is to analyze the role of artificial intelligence in teaching and strengthening innovation skills among high school students. The rapid expansion of artificial intelligence technologies, particularly adaptive learning systems, generative content tools, data analytics systems, and intelligent problem-solving environments, has created new capacities for transforming teaching and learning processes. In this regard, innovation skills, including creativity, critical thinking, problem-solving, collaboration, digital literacy, and the ability to turn ideas into practical solutions, are considered among the most important competencies required by students in the twenty-first century. The present study was conducted using a library-based and documentary method with a descriptive-analytical approach. Data were collected through the study and analysis of peer-reviewed scientific sources, books, policy reports, and reputable international documents in the fields of artificial intelligence, school education, and innovation skills. The findings indicate that artificial intelligence, when used purposefully and under the guidance of the teacher, can contribute to the development of students’ innovation skills through the personalization of learning, the provision of immediate feedback, support for project-based learning, the simulation of problem-oriented situations, and the expansion of opportunities for idea generation. However, realizing this potential requires improving the artificial intelligence literacy of teachers and students, observing ethical principles, protecting privacy, ensuring equitable access to technology, and designing appropriate curricula. Accordingly, artificial intelligence should not be regarded as a substitute for the teacher, but rather as a complementary tool for strengthening active learning and cultivating innovative students.
