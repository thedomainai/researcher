# The Generative AI Revolution in Digital Marketing: Opportunities, Implementation Barriers, and Strategic Future Directions

**著者**: Nafiz Mahmud Khan, Hisham Mahmood, Sumaiya Akther, S. M. Julkar Naeen Abir, Muhammad Usama Islam
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: マーケティング

## Abstract

Generative Artificial Intelligence (GAI) has changed the way brands interact with consumers and caused a paradigm shift in the digital marketing arena with its explosive growth. In this paper, we analyze the multi-faceted impact of GAI beyond automation to explore how it may be leveraged to create hyper-personalized content in real-time, to enhance brand loyalty and raise Return on Investment (ROI). Unlike prior reviews that examine artificial intelligence in marketing at a broad, conceptual level, this study’s contribution to academic knowledge is a function-based synthesis that maps GAI capabilities onto specific marketing activities and integrates recent large-scale industry survey evidence with peer-reviewed academic scholarship, an integration earlier reviews have not undertaken. Based on a thematic synthesis of contemporary literature and empirical findings, the study identifies a major transition in GAI adoption within marketing. A large-scale survey of more than 1,000 marketing professionals by the American Marketing Association and Lightricks found that 71% use GAI tools such as ChatGPT in their weekly work. However, usage remains largely productivity-focused rather than analytically oriented, highlighting a gap in the deeper integration of GAI into data-driven marketing decision-making. The results reveal that GAI achieves significant improvement in content quality (52%) and operational efficiency (50%). But these advantages come with significant implementation hurdles. Our work indicates that there are substantial concerns of algorithmic bias, as models trained on the internet tend to reproduce the biases associated with gender and race, and produce biased results for sensitive issues such as finance. The report also takes on growing issues about “hallucinations”, breaches of data privacy and the substitution of routine-based labor. To deal with these complications, the article argues for a change towards a more mature governance system. Future research should focus on conceptual models that connect GAI-driven content with long-term customer views, while addressing the legal and ethical vacuum in the AI space. This study therefore concludes that GAI offers unique chances for creativity but that its effective use requires a balance between technology efficiency, ethical openness, and strategic human oversight.
