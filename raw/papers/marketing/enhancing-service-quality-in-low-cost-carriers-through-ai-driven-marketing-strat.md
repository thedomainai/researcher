# Enhancing Service Quality in Low-Cost carriers through AI-Driven Marketing Strategies

**著者**: Mansour Said Hussein Ahmed, Farouk Abdelnabi Hassanein Attaalla, Khaled Soliman Abdelhalim
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: マーケティング

## Abstract

This paper aims to investigate the role of Artificial Intelligence (AI) Marketing strategies in enhancing service quality in low-cost carriers (LCCs) operating in Egypt. It focuses on the use of AI in personalization, customer communication, predictive systems, smart booking, customer feedback analysis, and digital customer engagement and examines their contribution to improving service delivery. A quantitative approach was adopted; the data were analyzed using IBM SPSS Statistics Version 28 and AMOS Version 26. The quantitative approach was based on a questionnaire administered to 430 passengers who had traveled with low-cost carriers. The results revealed that AI Marketing has a statistically significant positive effect on service quality and contributes to improving communication efficiency, service personalization, responsiveness, and customer interaction. Passengers reported positive perceptions of AI-supported marketing practices. The findings also revealed a high level of perceived service quality among passengers. Accordingly, the study recommends that low-cost carriers operating to and from Egypt leverage AI-driven marketing strategies to enhance digital service delivery and personalize passenger communication, while strengthening after-sales support and continuously monitoring service quality to ensure reliable and responsive services.
