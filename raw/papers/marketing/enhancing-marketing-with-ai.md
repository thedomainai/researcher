## Abstract

The adoption of artificial intelligence (AI) within the marketing domain has led to the development of innovative strategies that markedly improve consumer engagement. This investigation examines the influence of AI-driven marketing strategies, including personalization, building trust, and the perceived usefulness of AI technologies, on consumer attitudes. This study investigates the impact of AI on transforming consumer perceptions and driving engagement, focusing on factors such as hedonic motivation (enjoyment), perceived usefulness, and trust. This study employs a quantitative methodology that utilizes survey data analysed through structural equation modelling, to explain the effects of AI on current marketing approaches. The necessity for a balanced methodology is highlighted that integrates technological advancements with a commitment to consumer-centric norms, thereby fostering sustainable engagement within the dynamic digital environment. AI facilitates the production of tailored and engaging experiences that align with the specific requirements of individual consumers. This chapter offers valuable insights for marketers seeking to enhance AI strategies for future consumer engagement, emphasizing the importance of balancing innovation with ethical considerations to foster enduring consumer trust.
