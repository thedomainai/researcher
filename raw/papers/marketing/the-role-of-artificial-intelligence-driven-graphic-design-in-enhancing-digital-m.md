# The Role of Artificial Intelligence-Driven Graphic Design in Enhancing Digital Marketing Performance: Evidence from Jordanian E-Commerce Platforms

**著者**: Basim Abbas Ali AlObaydi, Derar Alqudah, Mohammad Khaleel Abukeshek, Laila I. Ibrahim, Ayman Kadry Mohammed Hamed
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: マーケティング

## Abstract

This study analyzes the effects of artificial intelligence (AI)-based graphic design on digital marketing performance on Jordanian e-commerce platforms. The study aims to understand how AI-based graphic design influences the quality of visual content, customer engagement, brand attraction and value building, purchase intention, and overall digital marketing performance. This study employs a quantitative approach by using a structured questionnaire and survey to collect data from employees and specialists of Jordanian e-commerce and digital marketing firms. The collected data were analyzed with the help of IBM SPSS Statistics and AMOS through the application of descriptive analysis, reliability analysis, confirmatory analysis, correlation analysis, and Structural Equation Modeling (SEM). The results showed that the performance of digital marketing, the quality of visual content, the engagement of customers, and the attraction of brands are positively and significantly impacted by AI-based graphic design within the context of digital marketing. This study also showed that visual content quality and customer engagement, along with brand attraction and purchase intention, significantly and positively affect digital marketing performance on Jordanian e-commerce platforms. This study also verified that AI-based visual communication has the potential to improve customer engagement, enhance brand communication, and improve online purchasing.
