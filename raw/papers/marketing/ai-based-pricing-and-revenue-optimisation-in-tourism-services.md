# AI-Based Pricing and Revenue Optimisation in Tourism Services

**著者**: Priyanka Gupta, Ravi Kumar, K. Varun, Tafese Niguse
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: マーケティング

## Abstract

Pricing and revenue management has been the core of tourism and hospitality marketing since the perishable and capacity-limited nature of these services means that all unsold seats, rooms or tours are forever lost. AI can now enable tourism companies to predict demand, segment consumers and price them with the speed, resolution, and personalisation that would have been unimaginable in the classical yield management days. This chapter explores tourism services based on AI-driven pricing and revenue optimisation using the marketing perspective. It combines the existing revenue management literature with the current work on AI in marketing. The chapter plots AI potentials along the pricing and revenue lifecycle, which include demand forecasting, customer segmentation, price optimisation, distribution and channel management, personalisation, and post purchase recovery and then examines five dimensions of marketing that define whether AI-based pricing creates or destroys value: customer experience, perceived price fairness, trust and relationship quality, brand equity and customer lifetime value. It states that AI-based pricing is only successful in tourism when it is perceived as a marketing tool within the broader customer relationship, not a stand-alone revenue generator and concludes by discussing ethics, governance and a research agenda.
