# Marketing Made Simple: The 7-Day Fast-Track Guide

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Marketing Made Simple: The 7-Day Fast Track Guide is a guide for students and entry-level practitioners on basic marketing principles, strategies, and practices. The book uses a seven-day study plan, which is meant to provide the reader with the basics of marketing, marketing strategy, consumer behavior, marketing research, market segmentation, targeting and positioning, products and brand management, pricing, distribution, promotion, digital marketing, marketing metrics, marketing trends, competitive strategy, retailing, selling, global marketing, and sustainable marketing. The book also has examples of marketing issues from Bangladesh and South Asia, marketing terms, chapter summaries, exercises, and marketing interview questions and answers. The book uses clear language to enable the reader to grasp marketing concepts, apply marketing models to businesses, and prepare for interviews and employment in marketing. The manual is an independent authoring guide for education. Information about companies, platforms, and regulations may have changed.
