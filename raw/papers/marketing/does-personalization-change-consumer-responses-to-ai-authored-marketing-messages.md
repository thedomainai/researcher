# Does Personalization Change Consumer Responses to AI-Authored Marketing Messages?

**著者**: Berra Taşdemir, Thomas Karl Alfred Woiczyk
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: マーケティング

## Abstract

This project contains the anonymized dataset, analysis code, statistical outputs, figures, and survey documentation associated with the Master’s thesis “AI Authorship, Authenticity, and Consumer Response in Marketing Communication.” The study uses a 2 × 2 between-subjects experiment crossing message authorship, human versus AI, with email content, generic versus personalized. The final sample contains 79 completed responses.
