# Ai-Driven Marketing and Passenger Satisfaction in Low-Cost Carriers the Mediating Role of Service Quality

**著者**: Mansour Said Hussein Ahmed, Farouk Abdelnabi Hassanein Attaalla, Khaled Soliman Abdelhalim
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: マーケティング

## Abstract

This paper aims to examine the impact of Artificial Intelligence (AI) Marketing on passenger satisfaction in low-cost carriers (LCCs) operating in Egypt, with particular emphasis on the mediating role of service quality. It investigates the relationships among AI Marketing, service quality, and passenger satisfaction and examines how AI-based marketing practices contribute to enhancing the overall passenger experience. A mixed-methods approach was adopted, combining quantitative and qualitative techniques, to test the proposed hypotheses, the quantitative data were analyzed by IBM SPSS Statistics Version 28 and AMOS Version 26. The quantitative approach was based on a questionnaire administered to 430 passengers who had traveled with low-cost carriers, while the qualitative approach involved interviews with 19 managers and employees of low-cost carriers. The findings revealed that AI Marketing positively affects passenger satisfaction and service quality, with service quality partially mediating this relationship. Moreover, AI Marketing was found to enhance market competitiveness and operational efficiency in low-cost carriers operating to and from Egypt. Accordingly, the study recommends that low-cost carriers operating to and from Egypt integrate AI Marketing into personalized marketing and service delivery and strengthen data protection and employee training to improve service quality and passenger satisfaction. Furthermore, low-cost carriers should enhance AI-powered customer support and continuously evaluate service quality to identify areas for improvement and strengthen passenger satisfaction and loyalty.
