# Fundamentals of Sport Branding

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Rooted in the traditional concept of branding – using interactions and experiences to shape a consumer’s perceptions – sport does so in a space that is emotionally charged, generationally transferred, community built/building, co-created by its consumer base, simultaneously produced and consumed, along with a plethora of additional distinguishing characteristics that it surpasses the complexities of branding in most other industries. Taking this into account this chapter outlines the fundamentals of sport branding, beginning by operationalizing the concept of the sport brand and its role in the sports ecosystem. This foundation provides an opportunity to examine the unique attributes of sport brands (e.g., variable quality, multiple touchpoints, fans as co-producers, emotional consumption, and ethics and identity), the core components of sport brand equity (awareness, perceived quality, associations/image, and loyalty), and the contemporary disruptions that are reshaping how sport brands are being created, communicated, and managed (e.g., digitally mediated communications, generative AI, and data-driven personalization). With this in mind, Chapter 1 will provide an introduction to the theories and practices that address how brands are shaped in the minds of our consumers.
