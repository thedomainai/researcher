# AI-Driven Reverse Logistics and Customer Experience: Empirical Evidence on Customer Satisfaction, Trust, and Repurchase Intention in E-Commerce

**著者**: Jestina G, Kamakshi S
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: マーケティング

## Abstract

In the rapid expanding e-commerce domain, efficient reverse logistics has recently come out as a key success factor for improving customers loyalty and satisfaction. The current study in empirical nature empirically examines the role of Artificial intelligence based decision-making for shaping customers’ satisfaction about e-commerce reverse logistics (RL) system, specifically return, refunds and exchange. This study has been done on 200 e-commerce consumers of Chennai Region for exploring the customer satisfaction with AI-based RL on: operational efficiency, ethical trust, transparency, user convenience, fairness and reliability. Based on the percentage analysis and factor analysis, three significant constructs on customer satisfaction with AI-based reverse logistics, i.e. AI Awareness and Operational Efficiency, Ethical Trust and Fairness, and Satisfaction and Loyalty Enhancement have been found, that explained the variance of 66.345%, suggesting the characteristics of RL is reliable. The results indicate that AI has significant contribution for ensuring the prompt, accurate, and seamless completion of RL functions and the ethical implementation of AI, data transparency, and unbiased AI decisions enhances the customer trust and satisfaction with the processes, respectively. Similarly, the AI driven personalized experiences, effective troubleshooting process and AI support system significantly impact the re-purchase intention of customers.
