# Social Media Marketing as a Catalyst for Sustainable Consumption and Circular Economy Transition: A Critical Review

**著者**: Anam Bhatti, Shamim Akhter, Zara Eman, Mariam
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: マーケティング

## Abstract

The increasing urgency concerning climate change, resource depletion and waste generation, are pushing the world towards sustainable consumption and the circular economy. In this regard, social media marketing has become a highly potent digitalcommunications tool that can shape consumer behavior and motivate the introduction of sustainability activities. Nevertheless, although the literature in this sector has been growing rapidly, the literature is fragmented on the marketing, sustainability and consumer behavior and information solutions field, and limited, about the role of social media marketing for facilitating sustainable consumption and circular economy. This article seeks to synthesize recent literature together through a critical literature review that focuses on the impact of social media marketing in promoting sustainable consumption and circular economy. Sustainable Consumption, Circular Economy, Social Media Marketing, and AI-Based Personalization in Sustainable Marketing: Four Sides. It also examines various strategies, such as influencer marketing, electronic word-of-mouth (eWOM), user-generated content (UGC), and AI-enabled personalization, as important tools to cultivate environmental awareness,to inspire consumer action and sustainable behavioral outcomes. The results of the study, though, indicate that, when the target is measured, social media marketing has a positive influence in raising awareness, trust and green purchasing intention of theconsumers, but the research is only focused on the purchase intention and not the overall circular consumption behaviour, such as reuse, repair, sharing and recycling of the products. The review also indicates methodological gaps, intellectual distance,and the failure to include new digital technology in the existing sustainability literature. Ultimately, the research recommends open research topics which cover interdisciplinary perspectives, longitudinal investigations, AI-based sustainability communication, and cross-cultural research in pursuit of theoretical and practical advances. This review contributes value by highlighting an integrated view of how digital marketing can accelerate sustainable consumption and support circular economy transition.
