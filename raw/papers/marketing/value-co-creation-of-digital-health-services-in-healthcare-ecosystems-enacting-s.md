# Value co-creation of digital health services in healthcare ecosystems – enacting sustainability of business models

**著者**: Minna Annika Pikkarainen, Irina Atkova, F. F. Wang, Elina Hyrkäs, Yueqiang Xu
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: マーケティング

## Abstract

Purpose Value co-creation of digital health services is a critical strategy to create new digital health services as a purpose to address complex healthcare challenges and achieving sustainable business models. The aim of this study is to explore how value co-creation of digital health services enables sustainable business models in healthcare ecosystems, addressing the challenges posed by technological advancements, regulatory changes, and global health issues. It was found in our study that sustainable business models integrate economic, environmental, and social aspects into value co-creation of digital health services, delivery and capture, ensuring innovations are effective, economically viable, and environmentally responsible. However, achieving sustainable business models is challenging for private companies co-creating digital health services working in healthcare ecosystems because it requires an extensive number of actions from policy makers and healthcare providers related to the regulations, public procurements, standards, and ethical rules. Design/methodology/approach This study employs a qualitative, interpretive case study approach to examine explore value co-creation of digital health services enables sustainable business models in healthcare ecosystems. The study was conducted using the European Union's Horizon 2020 research project, inDemand as a case context using 2 workshops with 15 participants and 20 individual interviews as a main data source. Thematic content analysis was used to analyze data. Findings Value co-creation of digital health services enables sustainable business models in healthcare ecosystems by fostering collaboration among stakeholders, integrating economic, environmental, and social aspects, and addressing regulatory and ethical issues. Commitment from policy and hospital management levels, continuous national funding, and legislative improvements are essential for achieving sustainable value co-creation and business model viability. Originality/value This study advances sustainability and service research by explicitly linking value co-creation to the enactment of sustainable business models at the healthcare ecosystem level. Moving beyond firm-centric perspectives, it develops and empirically validates an integrative framework – Value & Opportunity, Governance Design, Models & Principles, and Stakeholder Engagement – showing how these interdependent constructs enable or hinder sustainability. Drawing on rich, multi-actor evidence from a large EU Horizon 2020 project, the study reveals structural, regulatory, and cultural tensions that explain why co-created digital health innovations often fail to scale. The findings offer actionable insights for policymakers, healthcare organizations, and digital health firms aiming to achieve long-term, ecosystem-level sustainability.
