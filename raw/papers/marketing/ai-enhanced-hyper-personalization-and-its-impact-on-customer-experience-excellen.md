# AI-Enhanced Hyper-Personalization and Its Impact on Customer Experience Excellence in the Food and Beverage Service Industry

**著者**: Shivansu Sachan
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: マーケティング

## Abstract

Rapid advancements in Artificial Intelligence (AI) are transforming service industries by enabling hyper-personalization—real-time, data-driven customization of offerings and interactions at the individual level. In the highly competitive food and beverage (F&B) service sector, AI-powered recommendation systems, predictive analytics, and intelligent customer interfaces are increasingly deployed to enhance experiential value and foster long-term loyalty. Grounded in Service-Dominant Logic (SDL) and contemporary customer experience theory, this study investigates how AI-enhanced hyper-personalization contributes to customer experience excellence in F&B services. Adopting a sequential explanatory mixed-methods design, data were collected from 480 customers across restaurants, cafés, and quick-service outlets, followed by 18 semi-structured managerial interviews.
