# AI-Driven Personalization in Customer Engagement and Travel Marketing

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

The introduction of the artificial intelligence (AI) into the travel marketing is a game changer in the way the industry will interact, retain, and serve the customers. This chapter discusses how AI-driven personalization approaches have evolved and are used in the travel industry and how technologies like machine learning, natural language processing, predictive analytics, and recommendation systems are helping to transform customer experiences. The chapter discusses how personalization affects consumer decision-making, brand loyalty and revenue generation based on the existing empirical research and case studies in the industry. It also touches upon the ethical issues of privacy of data, algorithmic discrimination and customer permission. This chapter provides a theoretical context and practical data to travel marketers, technologists and policymakers who must work within the rapidly evolving AI landscape by looking at current applications and forecasting trends.
