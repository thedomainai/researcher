# Children as Consumers in AI-Driven Digital Environments

**著者**: Fernando Filipe Paulos Vieira
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: マーケティング

## Abstract

The integration of AI into children's digital platforms is reshaping how they engage with content, brands, and commercial environments. AI-driven personalization, recommendation systems, and persuasive design raise concerns about child development, autonomy, privacy, and digital well-being. This chapter examines how algorithmic marketing exploits cognitive immaturity and emotional sensitivity, addressing risks like data profiling, behavioral nudging, attention capture, and long-term digital footprints, while exploring strategies to promote digital literacy, parental mediation, and age-appropriate design.
