# Brand forgiveness: a scoping review and agenda for future research

**著者**: Soma Amol Dhaigude, Raiswa Saha, Naman Sreen, Sanjit Kumar Roy
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: マーケティング

## Abstract

Purpose Brand forgiveness reflects consumers’ willingness to maintain or restore relationships with brands following service failures, ethical controversies and marketplace transgressions. The purpose of this study is to conduct a scoping review of the theoretical development, conceptual foundations and future directions of brand forgiveness research. Design/methodology/approach The review synthesizes 80 studies published between 2009 and 2025 using scoping review guidelines and Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Review and Meta-analyses reporting procedures. The analysis examines theoretical foundations, methodological approaches, antecedents, mediators, moderators and outcomes. Findings Brand forgiveness literature is grounded in coping, attribution and justice-based theories of post-transgression evaluation, while relational and identity-based processes remain less developed. The literature identifies firm-, consumer- and brand/communication-related antecedents, along with cognitive, emotional and social mechanisms shaping forgiveness. Methodologically, the field relies largely on experiments and cross-sectional surveys, limiting understanding of forgiveness as a dynamic process. Research limitations/implications The review identifies theoretical, methodological and contextual gaps and proposes directions for advancing brand forgiveness research. Originality/value This review synthesizes fragmented brand forgiveness research and conceptualizes forgiveness as a process involving cognitive interpretation, emotional regulation, moral evaluation and relationship repair.
