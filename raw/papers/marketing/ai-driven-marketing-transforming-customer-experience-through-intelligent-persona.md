# AI-Driven Marketing: Transforming Customer Experience Through Intelligent Personalization and Data Analytics

**著者**: Abhishek Singh
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Artificial Intelligence is revolutionizing the marketing landscape by enabling businesses to deliver hyper-personalized experiences at scale. This research explores how AI technologies are reshaping customer engagement strategies, driving competitive advantage, and creating new opportunities for data-driven decision-making. The paper examines critical applications of AI in marketing including predictive customer behavior modeling, automated content generation, intelligent customer segmentation, and real-time campaign optimization. While AI offers substantial benefits such as improved customer lifetime value, reduced marketing costs, and enhanced targeting precision, it also presents significant challenges including algorithmic bias, data privacy concerns, and the diminishing human element in customer interactions. This study synthesizes current research, analyzes real-world implementations across ecommerce and B2B sectors, and provides evidence-based recommendations for responsible AI adoption in marketing. The conclusion emphasizes that successful AI-driven marketing requires a balanced approach integrating algorithmic efficiency with human creativity, ethical data stewardship, and customer-centric value creation. The transformation is not merely technological but represents a fundamental shift in how organizations understand, engage, and build lasting relationships with customers in an increasingly digital world.
