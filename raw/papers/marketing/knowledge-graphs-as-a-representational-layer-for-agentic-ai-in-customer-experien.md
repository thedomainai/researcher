# Knowledge Graphs As A Representational Layer For Agentic Ai In Customer Experience Decision Support

**著者**: Subisha K R, Shanmugaraja Selvakumaran, Saji K. Mathew
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: マーケティング

## Abstract

Organizations increasingly rely on behavioural, transactional, and service interaction data to understand and improve customer experience.However, enterprise analytics in this domain remain largely prediction-oriented, particularly in churn modelling, and provide limited support for diagnosis, explanation, and intervention design.This paper proposes an enterprise knowledge graph (KG) as a representational layer for agentic AI driven decision support.Drawing on Representation Theory and Organizational Information Processing Theory, the KG is conceptualized as a semantic structure that captures customer experience dynamics more faithfully than flat predictive features and supports interpretation under fragmented and evolving data conditions.A two-stage research design is proposed: simulation-based evaluation in a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) Customer Relationship Management (CRM) churn context, followed by a survey-based assessment of perceived decision support quality.The paper contributes an Information Systems perspective on graph grounded enterprise AI for decision support.
