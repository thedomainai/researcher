# From Playgrounds to Platforms

**著者**: Piali Haldar, Dev Kumar Mandal
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: マーケティング

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence, innovative applications, and platform-based ecosystems are transforming childhood into a digitally mediated, consumer-based environment. This research examines how AI-facilitated marketing constructs are reshaping child consumerism, employing a novel Tab-a structure (A3-C3) toward an amalgamated approach by products intended by techno stimulation at layer 3; techno stimulation facilitates marketing response at level one and induces cognitive-social-psychological processing from API to CDP. Hence, an algorithmically built, predictive targeting marketing practice, online platform engagement among the constituting stimuli should synergize to produce cognitive vulnerability, affect dependency, and consumption for children. It illustrates that AI-driven apparatus exacerbates erratic consumption, household brand attachment, and, above all, digital dependency, proving serious implications for fair AI governance, child protection, and digital well-being policy.
