## Abstract

In this chapter, the authors further develop on the changing face with regard to the juvenile consumer within the fast-growing realm of artificial intelligence. It also shows how AI technologies, including smart devices, social networking recommendation systems, and the Internet of Toys are shifting children as passive participants of the marketing process to active and data-generating participants in the digital economy. The paper is interdisciplinary and combines the knowledge of marketing, developmental psychology, digital ethics, and public policy to discuss four key issues, such as AI-enabled marketing practices, privacy, data ownership, digital well-being, and regulation. The authors state that, as much as AI presents opportunities of personalized learning and creative play and enhances connectivity. The growing popularity of gamification, influencer marketing, and emotionally adaptive systems makes the distinctions between entertainment and advertising and makes children less aware of when they are being sold a product.
