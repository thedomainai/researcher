# How AI-powered chatbot empathy drives online repurchase intention: the mediating role of service trust

**著者**: Tarek Tume Atua
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: マーケティング

## Abstract

AI chatbots are performing an increasing amount of emotional labor for online retailers. However, it is still unclear if the engineered empathetic nature of these AI chatbots can lead to increased repeat purchasing behavior and how they do so. This paper uses the stimulus-organism-response model to conceptualize AI chatbot emotional support as a stimulus, service trust as an organismic state (or internal experience), and repurchase intentions online as a response to those stimuli. Additionally, we examine the role that digital anxiety may play as a boundary condition. Using survey data from 447 Iraqi consumers who have purchased items online, we used Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLSSEM) to analyze our results. The results show that AI chatbot emotional support was significantly related to service trust, which in turn was significantly related to repurchase intentions. Furthermore, trust completely mediated the positive relationship between the two variables, such that all of the effects of chatbot emotional support on repurchase intentions were carried by trust. Contrary to expectations, digital anxiety did not moderate the relationship between chatbot emotional support and trust or conditionally affect the mediation of trust; thus, the process of generating trust occurs at similar rates regardless of level of digital anxiety. Overall, the findings suggest that trust is the primary pathway by which machine-generated empathy leads to customer loyalty and also demonstrate that there are no contingency conditions associated with stimulus-organism-response theory within a developing e-commerce environment.
