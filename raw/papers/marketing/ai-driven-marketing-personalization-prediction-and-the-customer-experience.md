# AI-DRIVEN MARKETING: Personalization, Prediction and the Customer Experience

**著者**: Kamna Singh
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: マーケティング

## Abstract

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has become a central force in digital marketing, reshaping how businesses understand and engage with customers. This paper investigates how AI-driven tools enable personalization, targeted advertising, predictive analytics, and automated customer service, transforming everyday digital experiences such as "You may also like" recommendations and interest-based advertisements. Adopting an investigative, observation-driven approach grounded in personal curiosity and supported by a review of existing literature, the study examines how AI systems collect and analyze customer activity—searches, clicks, views, and purchases—to detect behavioral patterns and generate marketing predictions. Real-world case studies of Amazon, Netflix, Spotify, and Coca-Cola illustrate how leading companies apply AI across recommendation engines, content personalization, and interactive brand campaigns. The paper highlights key benefits of AI-driven marketing, including improved customer understanding, more relevant advertising, faster customer support, and significant time savings for marketers. At the same time, it identifies critical challenges: privacy concerns over data collection, the risk of inaccurate predictions, discomfort caused by excessive personalization, algorithmic bias, and growing overdependence on automated systems. The findings suggest that while AI substantially enhances the scale, speed, and precision of marketing efforts, it functions most effectively as a support tool for human marketers rather than a replacement for human judgment and creativity. The study concludes that responsible, balanced AI adoption—one that respects customer privacy and choice—is essential for building trust and delivering meaningful, personalized marketing experiences in an increasingly data-driven business environment.
