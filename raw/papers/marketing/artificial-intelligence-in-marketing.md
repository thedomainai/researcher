# Artificial Intelligence in Marketing

**著者**: Ravsaheb Bhaiya Kale
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: マーケティング

## Abstract

Abstract Artificial Intelligence (AI) has emerged as one of the most influential technologies shaping modern marketing. The rapid growth of digital platforms, customer data, and automated technologies has changed how businesses understand consumers, communicate with audiences, and measure marketing performance. AI enables organizations to analyse large datasets, predict consumer behaviour, personalize communication, automate routine tasks, and generate content. This research paper examines the role of AI in marketing, its major applications, benefits, challenges, ethical concerns, and future implications. The paper argues that AI can significantly improve marketing effectiveness when combined with human creativity, strategic judgment, and responsible data practices. At the same time, concerns regarding privacy, algorithmic bias, misinformation, transparency, and employment demonstrate the need for careful and ethical implementation. The future of marketing is therefore likely to depend on a collaborative relationship between human marketers and intelligent technologies.
