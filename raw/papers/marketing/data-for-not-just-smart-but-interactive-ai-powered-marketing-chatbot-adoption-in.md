# Data for "Not Just Smart, But Interactive: AI-Powered Marketing Chatbot Adoption in E-Commerce and Digital Service Platforms"

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

This repository contains the anonymized research data supporting the study “Not Just Smart, But Interactive: AI-Powered Marketing Chatbot Adoption in E-Commerce and Digital Service Platforms.” The study examines consumer adoption of AI-powered marketing chatbots using UTAUT2 and expectation-confirmation theory. The analytical sample comprises 406 consumers with prior experience using AI-powered marketing chatbots in e-commerce and digital service platforms. The data support the fsQCA, MCMC, and IPMA analyses reported in the manuscript. Direct and indirect personal identifiers have been removed to protect participant confidentiality.
