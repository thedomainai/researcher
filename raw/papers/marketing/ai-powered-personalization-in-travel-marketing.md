## Abstract

The world travel and tourism sector is undergoing a radical change based on the rapid developments in digital technologies especially artificial intelligence (AI) big data analytics and networked digital ecosystems. Out of all these advances one of the most significant agents of change that have been altering the design and supply of travel services is the use of AI to personalize the services. With the help of smart algorithms and scale capabilities to process data organizations can deliver extremely personalized experiences that are closely in line with the preferences activities and situational demands of contemporary travellers (Valeri, 2023; Gaur et al. 2021).
