# Chatbots and Conversational AI in Customer Engagement for Travel Services

**著者**: K. Varun, Priyanka Gupta, Shashi Kant
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: マーケティング

## Abstract

Conversational artificial intelligence (CAI) and chatbots are transforming the way travel brands interact with the prospects and customers throughout the booking process. There has been a long-standing argument by marketing scholars that customer engagement is a multidimensional concept that includes cognitive, emotional, and behavioural interactions with a brand, and the interactions are becoming mediated by intelligent technologies. Based on marketing, service research, and tourism-informatics literatures, this chapter explores how travel service chatbots and CAI can generate marketing value. It discusses the theoretical basis of customer engagement and conversational AI, the history of chatbots in the travel sector, and outlines their operational applications throughout the pre-purchase, purchase, and post-purchase processes. It subsequently examines three key bases of marketing value, which include customising the customer experience, brand communication and voice, and real-time marketing intelligence based on conversational data. Challenges, such as hallucinations, channel fragmentation, and escalation failure, as well as ethical, privacy, and trust considerations are also taken into account in the chapter. The arguments are based on three illustrative cases and a research agenda is provided on the next generation of conversational-AI-enabled travel marketing. The main argument is that, designed appropriately as part of the broader marketing stack, chatbots and CAI no longer represent a cost-reduction service channel but rather a strategic customer engagement platform, full of data.
