# Enhancement of Customer Experience and Purchase Intention: Benefits of Generative Artificial Intelligence in the Retail Industry

**著者**: Kuo Cheng Chung, Chao-Hung Chiang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Generative artificial intelligence (AI) has major benefits, including the automation of data processing, the generation of content, and the optimization of decision-making processes. These capabilities substantially enhance the operational efficiency of enterprises while improving user experience and facilitating business growth through personalized recommendations. In this study, we used social support theory to explore how emotions, information, and social assistance influence individual psychology. We also used SPSS and SmartPLS 3.0 to determine the role of generative AI in the retail sector. Taken together, our findings indicate that product descriptions, visual aids, customer interaction, and personalization significantly shape customers’ experience and involvement, which in turn directly influence their purchasing behavior and intentions.
