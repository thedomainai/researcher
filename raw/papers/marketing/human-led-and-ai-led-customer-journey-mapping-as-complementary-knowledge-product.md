# Human-Led and AI-Led Customer Journey Mapping as Complementary Knowledge Production in a Living Lab

**著者**: Sirpa Toljander
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: マーケティング

## Abstract

Customer journey mapping (CJM) has fragmented into heterogeneous modelling practices, yet the epistemic consequences of that fragmentation remain poorly understood: it is unclear what each modelling logic makes visible and what it leaves out. This paper addresses that gap through a Design Science Research (DSR)–based Living Lab case study in a B2B membership organization, comparing two customer journey artefacts built on the same journey structure: a human-led, co-created journey and an AI-led journey derived from 2,379 customer comments. The two artefacts do not offer alternative pictures of the same experience; they produce different forms of emotional knowledge. The human-led artefact makes felt emotion visible: lived, situated experience articulated through narrative, meaning and identity. The AI-led artefact surfaces evaluative emotion: cumulative, judgement-based assessments of trust, fairness, tolerance and value for money that govern continuation or exit. The article therefore repositions customer journey mapping as comparative emotional knowledge production rather than the pursuit of a single accurate customer journey. For Living Lab research, it shows that human-led co-creation and AI-led synthesis are complementary participation logics, and that reading the two artefacts against each other is what renders an organization’s own assumptions about its customers visible and contestable.
