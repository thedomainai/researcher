# AI-Driven Marketing: The Role of Artificial Intelligence in Customer Engagement, Personalization and Marketing Decision-Making

**著者**: A. Das Gupta
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: マーケティング

## Abstract

Artificial Intelligence is changing business-customer connections. This paper explains AI-driven marketing. AI tools help understand customers. They create more relevant marketing campaigns. Businesses study customer data. They group customers effectively. They recommend products intelligently. Chatbots answer questions instantly. AI predicts buying behaviour. It also generates marketing content. These capabilities save significant time. Customer experience improves markedly. Costs decrease substantially. Marketers make better decisions overall. However, AI presents notable challenges. Data privacy remains a critical concern. Unfair results can emerge from algorithms. Implementation costs are often high. Human interaction may decrease substantially. This paper discusses both benefits and problems. Simple examples illustrate these points. Product recommendations demonstrate AI's effectiveness. AI chatbots showcase practical applications. The conclusion is balanced and clear. AI improves marketing capabilities considerably. However, human judgement remains essential. Safe customer data use is non-negotiable. Both elements must work together effectively.
