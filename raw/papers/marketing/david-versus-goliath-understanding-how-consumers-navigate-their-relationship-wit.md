# David versus Goliath: understanding how consumers navigate their relationship with a brand involved in online moral-panic dynamics

**著者**: Alice Venturini
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: マーケティング

## Abstract

Purpose Research on brand transgressions has primarily examined consumers’ emotional and behavioural responses at the individual level, yet little is known about how such transgressions unfold as collective phenomena on social media. This study aims to examine how consumers individually and collectively negotiate their relationships with brands during moralised online controversies, focusing on the escalation of brand transgressions into moral-panic dynamics and their implications for the consumer-brand relationship. Design/methodology/approach A nine-month netnography was conducted to analyse Reddit discussions related to the 2022 Balenciaga controversy. Approximately 800 conversational traces posted between November 2022 and August 2024 were unobtrusively collected from the 20 most active and content-rich threads across 16 relevant subreddits. Findings Findings provide evidence of both individual-level (relationship destabilisation, decoupling and rupture) and collective-level negotiation practices (relationship rupture amplification, rupture delegitimisation and reframing). A theoretically informed interpretation explains how consumer-brand relationships are simultaneously personally experienced and socially constructed. Originality/value This study advances brand transgression research by conceptualising moral-panic dynamics as a collective phenomenon unfolding across moralised brand controversies, shifting the focus from individual reactions to collective processes reshaping consumer-brand relationships in digital environments.
