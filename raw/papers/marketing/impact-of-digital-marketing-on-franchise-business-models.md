# IMPACT OF DIGITAL MARKETING ON FRANCHISE BUSINESS MODELS

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

This study explores the impact of digital marketing on franchising business models, focusing on changes in communication, brand governance, and franchise performance. Based on a literature review, the findings show that digital technologies such as social media, big data, and AI enhance customer engagement, improve brand equity, and support data-driven decision-making in franchise networks. However, challenges remain, including brand consistency issues, platform dependency, and data privacy concerns. The study also highlights the growing role of co-creation between franchisors, franchisees, and consumers in digital environments. Overall, digital marketing is found to be a transformative factor in franchising, improving efficiency while introducing new governance challenges.
