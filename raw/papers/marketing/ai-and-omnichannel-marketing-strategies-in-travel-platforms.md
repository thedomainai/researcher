# AI and Omnichannel Marketing Strategies in Travel Platforms

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

This book chapter comprises new AI-powered tools such as machine learning, data analytics, and personalisation, which together shape improved customer involvement across physical touchpoints and the digital travel industry. Coming on to create a polished and sleek experience across various channels, AI is quickly changing the mechanisms and techniques used in its marketing strategies. Taking modern insights and their examples, it further explains new techniques of natural language operation in delivering a more customised and good experience. There are also some mandates and challenges on the way, which include algorithmic bias, personal data security and limited access to digital technologies. Additionally, this book chapter also discovers the growing patterns and trends of AR, voice-enabled search and up-to-date pricing frameworks. Ultimately, it highlights that adoption of AI in cross-channel marketing is not an option but a mandate for travel portals that want to be there in the marketing; it has become a sign of competitive advantage for their customer fulfilment.
