## Abstract

This chapter discusses the combination of personalization, prediction, and persuasion into a unified system of AI-based child-centric marketing. Its fundamental aspect is the so-called personalized persuasion that automatizes psychological profiling, predictive future behaviours, and persuasive design to create cognitive and emotional reactions. Through synthesis of the literature we discuss how the AI technology uses digital exhaust or traces left by children using educational apps, games and social media to form highly personalized experiences that are at the level of the child developmental stage. On the methodological level, the research investigates the technical processes underlying such tactics by integrating the Theory of Planned Behavior (TPB) with machine-learning algorithms like Gradient Boosting and the Random Forest to forecast the purchase intentions. This roadmap is part of the theoretical reflection and practical guide to researchers, marketers, and policymakers interested in knowing how an AI-based marketing ecosystem of the next generation will appear.
