## Abstract

This chapter examines the ethical implications of AI-driven marketing, analysing how algorithmic branding shapes consumer autonomy, cultural hierarchies, and corporate accountability. Drawing on surveillance capitalism and Bourdieu&s;s cultural capital theory, it explores how platforms such as Amazon, Netflix, and Facebook convert behavioural data into commercial advantage. The chapter also evaluates governance gaps in current regulatory approaches such as GDPR and proposes reforms such as transparency mandates and data cooperatives. The chapter advances human-centric governance grounded in Floridi&s;s principles, arguing that ethical AI innovation requires embedding equity, dignity, and accountability into algorithmic systems to serve collective welfare rather than corporate agendas.
