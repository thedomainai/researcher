# AI-DRIVEN PERSONALIZATION AND USER ACCEPTANCE: THE ROLE OF PERCEIVED RELEVANCE, PRIVACY CONCERNS AND DATA CONTROL

**著者**: Kilichova Elmira Ozodovna, Worldly Knowledge Publishing Centre
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: マーケティング

## Abstract

This thesis examines the conditions under which AI-driven personalization is accepted by users, focusing on perceived relevance, privacy concerns, transparency, and data control. While personalization can make digital services more relevant and convenient, intensive use of personal data may simultaneously increase perceptions of privacy intrusion and surveillance. Recent research demonstrates that perceived relevance can serve as an important mechanism through which AI personalization generates positive behavioral responses, whereas privacy concerns may weaken this relationship. At the same time, transparency and user control can reduce uncertainty and support more favorable evaluations of personalized systems. The study develops a conceptual approach in which AI-driven personalization influences user acceptance primarily through perceived relevance, while privacy concerns operate as a potential barrier and transparency and data control function as supportive conditions. The proposed perspective is relevant to both educational technologies and digital business environments because users in both contexts increasingly interact with AI systems that collect and process behavioral data. The thesis contributes to AI marketing research by shifting attention from personalization intensity toward the quality, transparency, and perceived legitimacy of personalized experiences.
