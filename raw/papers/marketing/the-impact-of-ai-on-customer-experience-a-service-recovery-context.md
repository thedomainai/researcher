# The Impact of AI on Customer Experience: A Service Recovery Context

**著者**: Sidhanta Kumar Mishra
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: マーケティング

## Abstract

Hospitality operators now determine the integration of artificial intelligence (AI) within service-recovery encounters. The impact of this decision on customer experience (CX) remains empirically untested. Social-judgment theory identifies perceived capability, risk governance, and autonomy as antecedents of brand attitude and repurchase intention, with perceived competence serving as a mediating factor. PLS-SEM modelling of 352 consumers, utilising permutation-based multiple-group analysis across fully automated (Auto), AI-human collaborative (Collab), and AI-facilitated human recovery (Facil) conditions, demonstrates that competence mediates the effects of capability and autonomy. Additionally, risk governance enhances perceptions of competence rather than deterring engagement.
