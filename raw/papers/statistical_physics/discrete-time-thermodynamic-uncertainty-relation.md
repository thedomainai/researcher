# Discrete-time thermodynamic uncertainty relation

**著者**: Karel Proesmans, Christian Van den Broeck
**年**: 2017 | **被引用数**: 217
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 統計力学・熱力学

## Abstract

We generalize the thermodynamic uncertainty relation, providing an entropic upper bound for average fluxes in time-continuous steady-state systems (G ingrich T. R. et al ., Phys. Rev. Lett. , 116 (2016) 120601), to time-discrete Markov chains and to systems under time-symmetric, periodic driving.
