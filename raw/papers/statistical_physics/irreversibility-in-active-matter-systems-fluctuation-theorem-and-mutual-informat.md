# Irreversibility in Active Matter Systems: Fluctuation Theorem and Mutual Information

**著者**: Lennart Dabelow, Stefano Bo, Ralf Eichhorn
**年**: 2019 | **被引用数**: 288
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 統計力学・熱力学

## Abstract

A measure of irreversibility in active particle systems---such as a cluster of bacteria or a swarm of nanorobots---could be a useful metric for characterizing the out-of-equilibrium behavior of such systems.
