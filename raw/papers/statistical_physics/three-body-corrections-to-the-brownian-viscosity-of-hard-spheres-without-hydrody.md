# Three-body corrections to the Brownian viscosity of hard spheres without hydrodynamic interactions

**著者**: Julian Olszewski, Zijian Liu, Jie Xu
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 1
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 統計力学・熱力学

## Abstract

For Brownian hard spheres without hydrodynamic interactions, we study the part of the contact stress produced by three-body correlations: the third-order coefficient of the zero-shear Brownian viscosity. Kirkwood superposition gives zero for this coefficient: three nonzero pair contributions cancel exactly. An elementary radial identity exposes the cancellation, and evaluating closures with the same contact stress makes their coefficients directly comparable. Although superposition solves the bulk equation, it leaves a flux error on each shared contact. This error defines a unique finite-energy weak correction whose positive diffusive dissipation, suitably normalized, is a weak-energy coefficient. Explicit symmetric trials yield a variational lower bound and a residual upper bound, and exact integration with outward rational arithmetic proves that the weak-energy coefficient lies between 1.8266948 and 1.8267157. For the physical viscosity coefficient this is a conditional interval: the two coefficients coincide when the physical three-body correction equals the weak correction and the remote remainder of the pair adjoint identity, a surface term at large pair separation, vanishes. The interval excludes every closure coefficient compared here, including the zero of superposition. Hydrodynamic interactions contribute at the same orders in real suspensions, where rheometry measures total viscosities, so the coefficient is a quantitative benchmark for theories and simulations of dilute three-body Brownian stress in this model.
