# Nonequilibrium Equality for Free Energy Differences

**著者**: Christopher Jarzynski
**年**: 1997 | **被引用数**: 6245
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 統計力学・熱力学

## Abstract

An expression is derived for the equilibrium free energy difference between two configurations of a system, in terms of an ensemble of finite-time measurements of the work performed in parametrically switching from one configuration to the other. Two well-known identities emerge as limiting cases of this result.
