# Scale-invariant parton model

**著者**: John B. Kogut, Leonard Susskind
**年**: 1974 | **被引用数**: 123
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 超ひも理論・量子重力

## Abstract

We generalize the assumptions of the parton model to include the possibility of scale-invariant short-distance behavior. A physical picture is used to motivate our formal equations. Bjorken scaling is violated in a systematic way. We compute the asymptotic properties of $\ensuremath{\nu}{W}_{2}$ and show that its moments are power-behaved functions of ${Q}^{2}$. Arguments are presented for a nonzero value of $\frac{{\ensuremath{\sigma}}_{S}}{{\ensuremath{\sigma}}_{T}}$ in the deep-inelastic limit. The asymptotic behavior of electromagnetic form factors is found to be ${({Q}^{2})}^{\ensuremath{-}a}$, where the constant $a$ is related to properties of $\ensuremath{\nu}{W}_{2}$.
