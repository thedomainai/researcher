# The Origins of the Huqin: Competing Theories, Organology, and Cultural Transmission

**著者**: Chengcheng Zhao, Nur Atiqah Abdullah
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 超ひも理論・量子重力

## Abstract

The origins of the huqin have long been debated, with explanations emphasizing development within Han Chinese traditions, contributions from non-Han communities, and transmission from other regions.This article critically reviews these explanations through a comparison of the historical records, instrument structures, playing techniques, and transmission routes discussed in the literature.On balance, the evidence reviewed favors an account of early development within present-day China involving interaction between Han and non-Han communities.Cultural exchange provides an important framework for understanding this process, rather than attributing the instrument's formation exclusively to a single group.Nevertheless, its precise place of origin and sequence of development remain unresolved, and contributions from cross-regional transmission warrant further investigation.Further research integrating dated textual, iconographic, and archaeological evidence is needed to clarify how different instrumental traditions contributed to the emergence and development of the huqin.
