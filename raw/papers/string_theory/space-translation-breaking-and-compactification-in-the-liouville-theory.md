# Space-Translation Breaking and Compactification in the Liouville Theory

**著者**: Eric D'Hoker, R. Jackiw
**年**: 1983 | **被引用数**: 113
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 超ひも理論・量子重力

## Abstract

In the two-dimensional Liouville quantum field theory a perturbation theory may be defined for which space-translation invariance is spontaneously broken and space-time (semi)compactifies spontaneously.
