# Jordanian deformations of the AdS5 × S5 superstring

**著者**: Io Kawaguchi, Takuya Matsumoto, Kentaroh Yoshida
**年**: 2014 | **被引用数**: 263
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 超ひも理論・量子重力

## Abstract

We consider Jordanian deformations of the AdS5 × S5 superstring action. These deformations correspond to non-standard q-deformations. In particular, it is possible to perform a partial deformation, for example, of the AdS5 part only, or of the S5 part only. Then the classical action and the Lax pair are constructed with a linear, twisted and extended R operator. It is shown that the action preserves the κ-symmetry.
