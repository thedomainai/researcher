# Detection of Unmarked Non-Metallic Underground Pipelines Using Fine-Grained Strontium Ferrite-Based Powders

**著者**: I. S. Leonov, Gulnur Zakirova, A. V. Borisov, V. S. Borisova, A. V. Suslova
**年**: 2027 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 超ひも理論・量子重力

## Abstract

An obvious global trend is the spread of plastic pipelines in the construction and repair of underground utilities, including gas distribution pipelines. Despite the advantages of non-metallic pipes, their use is associated with a number of specific problems characteristic of plastic materials. One of such significant problems facing the gas industry is the problem of detecting underground non-metallic utilities and selectively identifying them from adjacent facilities. Pipelines not linked to the global coordinate system in a timely manner can lead to significant economic costs in case of abnormal situations during construction and installation works. The paper investigates an original method of searching for non-metallic utilities associated with the introduction of magnetic powder into the gas flow. It is shown that in the case of the site location in the urban area it is a low-information and ambiguous method. On the contrary, in the open area, in the absence of third-party ferromagnetic interference, this method is not only potentially applicable, but deserves close attention and further research, for which the appropriate design of the test site is proposed. Potential application of the method will increase the informative value of data on the underground location of non-metallic pipelines by 15% for gas distribution pipelines of various diameters, considering the achievement of a magnetic induction level above the pipeline in the range of 60-120 nT.
