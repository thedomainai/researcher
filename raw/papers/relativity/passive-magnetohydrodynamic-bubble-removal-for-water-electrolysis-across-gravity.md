# Passive magnetohydrodynamic bubble removal for water electrolysis across gravity regimes

**著者**: T. P. Francis, Jarosław Syzdek, Álvaro Romero-Calvo
**年**: 2027 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 相対性理論・重力

## Abstract

Magnetohydrodynamic bubble-removal methods for microgravity, lunar, and terrestrial regimes are investigated experimentally via drop-tower tests and benchtop articles. Effects are observed across cell and bubble length scales, with suggested applications for each technology.
