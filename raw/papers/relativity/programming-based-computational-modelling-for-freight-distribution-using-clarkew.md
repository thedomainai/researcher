# Programming-Based Computational Modelling for Freight Distribution Using Clarke–Wright Savings Method for Route Optimization: A Case Study in Surabaya, Indonesia

**著者**: Juang Akbardin, Dwi Novi Wulansari, Mohamad Reza Ashiddiqie, Muhammad Zahran, Muhammad Zaly Shah
**年**: 2027 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 相対性理論・重力

## Abstract

Urban freight pickup in dense metropolitan areas is constrained by short service windows, congestion, and limited vehicle capacity. This study evaluates a practical hybrid heuristic—Clarke-Wright Savings for clustering and Nearest Neighbor for sequencing—for evening pickup operations at a major Indonesian postal logistics hub in Surabaya. Using 46 pickup locations, the MATLAB implementation assesses service coverage, route-time feasibility, fleet requirement, distance efficiency, and capacity utilization under a 500-unit vehicle capacity and a 150-minute maximum route duration. The optimized plan serves all 46 pickup locations with five vehicles, compared with 22 locations served by seven vehicles in the existing operation, and reduces distance per served point from 14.57 to 4.08 km/point while keeping the longest route at 144 minutes. The case also reveals a transferable operational pattern: early routes tend to be filled efficiently in demand-concentrated areas, whereas residual spatially dispersed demand tends to create lower-utilization tail routes. A fixed-seed Genetic Algorithm benchmark further reduced total distance to 152.79 km under the same constraints, but required a longer average runtime of 60.52 seconds compared with 1.13 seconds for the proposed MATLAB-based heuristic. These findings show that transparent classical heuristics can still deliver implementable gains in urban pickup logistics when capacity and time feasibility are evaluated jointly.
