# Comparative Study of the Mechanical Behavior of Concrete with Natural and Synthetic Admixture

**著者**: Kamal Hosen, Alina Bărbulescu
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 相対性理論・重力

## Abstract

This study aims to evaluate and compare the performance of standard concrete modified with two distinct admixtures: a synthetic chemical-based additive (CWCAD) and a natural, gram flour-based additive (CWNAD), to determine if natural alternatives can provide comparable or superior mechanical and physical properties to traditional chemical enhancers.
