# Progress in Lunar Laser Ranging Tests of Relativistic Gravity

**著者**: J. G. Williams, Slava G. Turyshev, D. H. Boggs
**年**: 2004 | **被引用数**: 907
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 相対性理論・重力

## Abstract

Analyses of laser ranges to the Moon provide increasingly stringent limits on any violation of the equivalence principle (EP); they also enable several very accurate tests of relativistic gravity. These analyses give an EP test of $\ensuremath{\Delta}({M}_{G}/{M}_{I}{)}_{\mathrm{E}\mathrm{P}}=(\ensuremath{-}1.0\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}1.4)\ifmmode\times\else\texttimes\fi{}{10}^{\ensuremath{-}13}$. This result yields a strong equivalence principle (SEP) test of $\ensuremath{\Delta}({M}_{G}/{M}_{I}{)}_{\mathrm{S}\mathrm{E}\mathrm{P}}=(\ensuremath{-}2.0\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}2.0)\ifmmode\times\else\texttimes\fi{}{10}^{\ensuremath{-}13}$. Also, the corresponding SEP violation parameter $\ensuremath{\eta}$ is $(4.4\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}4.5)\ifmmode\times\else\texttimes\fi{}{10}^{\ensuremath{-}4}$, where $\ensuremath{\eta}=4\ensuremath{\beta}\ensuremath{-}\ensuremath{\gamma}\ensuremath{-}3$ and both $\ensuremath{\beta}$ and $\ensuremath{\gamma}$ are post-Newtonian parameters. Using the Cassini $\ensuremath{\gamma}$, the $\ensuremath{\eta}$ result yields $\ensuremath{\beta}\ensuremath{-}1=(1.2\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}1.1)\ifmmode\times\else\texttimes\fi{}{10}^{\ensuremath{-}4}$. The geodetic precession test, expressed as a relative deviation from general relativity, is ${K}_{\mathrm{g}\mathrm{p}}=\ensuremath{-}0.0019\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}0.0064$. The search for a time variation in the gravitational constant results in $\stackrel{\ifmmode \dot{}\else \textperiodcentered \fi{}}{G}/G=(4\ifmmode\pm\else\textpm\fi{}9)\ifmmode\times\else\texttimes\fi{}{10}^{\ensuremath{-}13}\text{ }\text{ }{\mathrm{y}\mathrm{r}}^{\ensuremath{-}1}$; consequently there is no evidence for local ($\ensuremath{\sim}1\text{ }\text{ }\mathrm{A}\mathrm{U}$) scale expansion of the solar system.
