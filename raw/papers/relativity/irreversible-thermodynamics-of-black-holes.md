# Irreversible Thermodynamics of Black Holes

**著者**: Philip Candelas, D. W. Sciama
**年**: 1977 | **被引用数**: 176
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 相対性理論・重力

## Abstract

Black holes are shown to obey the principles of irreversible thermodynamics in the form of a fluctuation-dissipation theorem for their zero-point quantum fluctuations. Moreover Hawking radiation is shown to be related to the macroscopic radiation of a nonstationary black hole in accordance with Onsager's principle.
