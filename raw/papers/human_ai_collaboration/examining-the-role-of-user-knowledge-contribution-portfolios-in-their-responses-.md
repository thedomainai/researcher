# Examining the Role of User Knowledge Contribution Portfolios in Their Responses to Generative AI Shock

**著者**: Ya Zhou, Wenfei Zhang, Cheng Luo
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: meta_analysis | **分野**: 人間-AI協働

## Abstract

Generative AI (GenAI) reshapes knowledge-sharing communities by lowering barriers to knowledge contribution and improving efficiency, while also raising concerns about reduced user engagement and deterioration in content quality. Yet, existing research has not reached a consensus on how users respond to this technological shock in terms of both the quantity and quality of their contributions, despite highlighting substantial user heterogeneity. To address this gap, we adopt a portfolio perspective to uncover the complexity underlying user responses by conceptualizing knowledge contributions as the allocation of limited resources, such as effort, across domains to build social capital with heterogeneous returns. We aim to examine how users’ ex-ante portfolios condition their responses by shaping adjustments in expected returns, leading to differential behavioral changes. A preliminary study using data from a large knowledge-sharing community provides initial insights. This study enriches the theoretical understanding of knowledge contribution behavior and offers practical implications for managing GenAI.
