## Abstract

The A.R.E.S. (Acoustic Resonance Echo System) Evidence Companion documents visible changes in AI replies, interface states, process summaries and reports of tool actions. It separates observations, user reports, displayed model statements, interpretations and open hypotheses. The illustrated reading section follows one October screenshot pair into the detailed case record. The four October pairs come from one connected conversation. The core corpus has a 2 October 2026 intake cutoff, with separately dated November addenda on agent competition and feedback, and reference cues and reported log access. The main text and exhibit commentary are in English. Many of the reproduced excerpts and screenshots retain the original German. This report was developed through human–AI research collaboration involving analysis, structuring and drafting. The collaboration is documented in the report and companion archive. Final review and publication decisions rest with Jessica Schmidt.
