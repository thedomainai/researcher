# Beyond the Prompt: Human-AI Collaboration as an Architecture of Complementary Information

**著者**: grace roperti
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人間-AI協働

## Abstract

Human–AI collaboration is often described as the combination of human judgment and machine capability. That description can conceal a more basic problem: the human and AI may not have access to the same information.An AI system processes representations made available to it, including text, images, audio, measurements and records. A human may additionally possess embodied sensation, lived history, tacit pattern recognition, contextual meaning, intention and direct awareness of consequences.This conceptual paper introduces the Complementary Information Architecture: a framework for identifying and managing the boundary between represented information and relevant human information that has not entered the machine-readable record.Three anonymized motivating observations—creative character construction, wound-care assessment and discourse-level linguistic analysis—illustrate different representation gaps:• behavioural specification; • sensory and tacit information; and • functional meaning within context.The framework proposes four information states and a six-stage collaboration cycle for identifying missing information, preserving modality boundaries, examining human–AI disagreement and converting supported insights into structured evaluation data.This paper does not claim that human–AI collaboration is automatically superior. It presents a conceptual framework, seven testable propositions and a staged research program intended for discussion, challenge and future evaluation.Outside community contributions have been anonymized and paraphrased. They are presented as motivating observations rather than independently verified case studies or empirical evidence.
