# Understanding Automation Bias in Human–AI Collaboration in Healthcare: A Scoping Review

**著者**: Binlin Wang, Huiling Hu
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: 人間-AI協働

## Abstract

This study aims to map the extent, range, and nature of evidence on automation bias in human–AI collaboration in healthcare, and to clarify how the phenomenon is defined, measured, explained, and mitigated across clinical contexts. From the review, we want to search how to assess automation bias in human–AI collaboration while analyzing how human–AI collaboration influence the attention and intellection. Besides, we expect to explore the possible intervention for automation bias.
