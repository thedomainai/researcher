# Scaling Generative AI: Building Organizational Capability for Innovation Teams

**著者**: John M. York, Paul F. Weber, Eun Sik J. Cho, William Chen, Kamrun Kanika
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人間-AI協働

## Abstract

Organizations across industries are rapidly experimenting with generative artificial intelligence (AI), yet many struggle to move beyond isolated pilots toward scalable use. This article examines how innovation teams convert generative-AI experimentation into repeatable organizational capability by operationalizing structured prompting and evaluation as process routines that standardize AI-enabled knowledge work. It introduces a practical approach that integrates structured prompting through the Task–Context–Role–Deliverable–Tone (T-C-R-D-T) schema with an iterative quality-assurance cycle – Examine–Analyze–Evaluate–Iterate (E-A-E-I). An illustrative comparison demonstrates how these routines improve consistency, traceability, and decision readiness relative to ad hoc prompting. Firms that institutionalize these routines can transform generative AI from an experimental productivity tool into a scalable innovation capability.
