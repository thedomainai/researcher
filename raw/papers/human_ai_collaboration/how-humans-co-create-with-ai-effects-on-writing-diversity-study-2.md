# How Humans Co-Create With AI: Effects on Writing Diversity (Study 2)

**著者**: Nathan Jo, Manish Raghavan
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人間-AI協働

## Abstract

Humans are increasingly using generative AI tools to aid or complete a variety of tasks. Despite the large productivity gains that we stand to gain from using these tools, there is growing concern that AI outputs "flatten" toward a common style, reducing diversity across artifacts and authors. Several recent papers have conducted user experiments showing that outputs do indeed flatten in creative writing, albeit in specific uses of AI (e.g., just for brainstorming ideas or for text completion). This study aims to simulate a more realistic model of human-AI co-creation whereby users can use AI throughout the creative writing pipeline: to write a first draft, to edit, etc. Does AI still steer toward similar-looking outputs when users have diverse preferences and have free rein on how they use the tool? What, precisely, does AI contribute at each step of co-creation?
