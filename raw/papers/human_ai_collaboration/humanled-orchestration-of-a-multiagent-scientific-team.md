# Human‑Led Orchestration of a Multi‑Agent Scientific Team

**著者**: Daniel Jeffrey
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人間-AI協働

## Abstract

Large Language Models (LLMs) have introduced a new mode of scientific inquiry in which a human scientist leads a coordinated team of specialised AI agents. This paper formalises a reproducible, falsifiable operational model for human‑directed multi‑LLM scientific orchestration. The workflow described here underpins the Precision Cascade framework and has already produced three reproducible scientific outputs across cosmology and exoplanet demographics. The paper defines the functional roles of three distinct LLM agents, outlines the human‑led governance structure that maintains conceptual coherence, and presents a cross‑validation protocol that bridges blind spots and amplifies strengths across the team. A central operational finding is that LLMs do not detect missing structure, do not define scientific standards, and do not know what “world‑class” or “audit‑grade” means. They execute. The human scientist must govern. Paper 0B therefore formalises not only the workflow but the epistemic role of the human orchestrator, providing the operational foundation for the Precision Cascade programme and demonstrating how a human scientist can act as the Principal Investigator of a multi‑agent research group to produce transparent, deterministic, and falsifiable scientific artefacts.
