# From words to work: how AI social language boosts productivity and creative self-efficacy

**著者**: Cuicui Wang, Ruyue Wang, Rui Li
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人間-AI協働

## Abstract

Purpose While AI agents enhance productivity, they may also threaten human autonomy and creativity in collaborative tasks. Balancing efficiency with individual capability development is key to advancing human–AI collaboration. Design/methodology/approach Based on Emotions as Social Information theory and Self-Determination Theory (SDT), this research conducted two studies to explore how AI social-oriented language influences perceived collaboration outcomes, specifically perceived work productivity and creative self-efficacy. Study 1 employed a questionnaire survey in general work and life collaboration contexts. Study 2 employed a simulated interaction experiment involving specific tasks. Findings Study 1 demonstrated the positive effects of AI social-oriented language on both perceived work productivity and creative self-efficacy, with perceived AI-supported autonomy mediating these effects. Study 2 further supported these relationships and revealed the boundary conditions of task type. Specifically, for creative tasks, highly social-oriented AI language enhanced both perceived AI-supported autonomy and collaboration outcomes, whereas for routine analytical tasks, these effects were not significant. Originality/value This study not only addresses current concerns regarding collaboration performance but also highlights the human-centered value of AI by emphasizing creative potential. It bridges the gap by linking AI language style to these concrete outcomes. The findings enrich the understanding of AI language design in human–AI collaboration and provide both theoretical and practical implications for optimizing AI interaction strategies to improve collaboration outcomes.
