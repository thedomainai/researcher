# INVENTIONAI: A classification for invention (or any concept) produced through successful and measurable human-AI collaboration

**著者**: Alex Hirsch
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

INVENTIONAI proposes a classification for invention produced under qualifying HUMANAI collaboration. The framework distinguishes among HUMAN work, HUMAN + AI work, and HUMANAI, and argues that HUMANAI requires three conditions: human instigation, success in beneficiary-defined terms, and measurable advancement proof in the work itself. (documented somewhere) This record includes three documents: the foundational INVENTIONAI paper, a simpler plain-language version, and a short testing note. The testing note reports a small April 2026 evaluation in which three AI systems—ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Kimi—were given six difficult cases twice, first without the framework and then after reading a short version of it. The test is best understood as a HUMANAI versus HUMAN + AI mapping test rather than a direct end-to-end test of inventionai classification. The purpose of this record is to deposit the framework as a proposal for debate, use, and refinement, together with a simpler explanation and an initial test of how the framework helps AI systems reason more consistently about difficult human-AI cases.
