# Navigating Renewable Energy Tensions Through Agentic Human–AI Collaboration

**著者**: Kenan Degirmenci, Anna Monika Wiewiora, Jacqueline Corbett, Alistair Barros
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人間-AI協働

## Abstract

Household decisions about renewable energy technologies involve persistent competing demands, including short-term versus long-term outcomes and individual versus collective interests. These tensions can be reinforced by psychological distance, which makes future and collective consequences less salient than immediate and individual considerations. This research-in-progress paper examines how agentic AI can support households in navigating such tensions rather than resolving them through simple optimization. Drawing on paradox theory, psychological distance, agentic AI, sensemaking, and human–AI collaboration, we derive five initial meta-requirements: surfacing competing demands, counteracting asymmetric salience, providing context-sensitive agentic support over time, supporting ongoing sensemaking, and calibrating human and AI agency. We outline a mixed-method, interpretive design science approach to empirically examine and refine these meta-requirements through qualitative inquiry, artifact development, and scenario-based evaluation. The study conceptualizes agentic AI as support for ongoing paradox navigation in complex sustainability decisions.
