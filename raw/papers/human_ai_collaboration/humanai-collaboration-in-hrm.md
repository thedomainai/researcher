# Human–AI Collaboration in HRM

**著者**: R Nalini, R. Amudha, C. Vijayabanu, R Gayathri
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人間-AI協働

## Abstract

The goal of Human-AI collaboration within the HRM field is to combine artificial intelligence (AI) technologies with the capabilities of people to create enhanced decision-making and worker support mechanisms, using AI-enabled workplaces in the Indian IT sector, as an illustration. A recent framework, called the HAC model, suggests that HWACs can successfully integrate human resources and artificial intelligence-based technologies through a hybrid approach to talent management, recruitment, and employee well-being via data analytics and ethical AI-based policies/procedures.
