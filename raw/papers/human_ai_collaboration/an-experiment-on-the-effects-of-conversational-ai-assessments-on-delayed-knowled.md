# An Experiment On The Effects Of Conversational Ai Assessments On Delayed Knowledge Retention

**著者**: Alexander Peter Scheuer, Thilo Tamme, Andrej Levin
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人間-AI協働

## Abstract

Gen-AI promises unprecedented efficiency and personalization in education and professional learning, but these gains may come at a hidden cost.As AI systems become ever more capable of explaining, summarizing, and solving on demand, they increasingly enable and encourage cognitive offloading, allowing users to delegate thinking in ways that may erode durable knowledge retention.At the same time, decades of work on retrieval practice show that effortful, generative retrieval is one of the most reliable mechanisms for strengthening long-term memory and transfer.This tension creates an urgent design challenge: how can we exploit the scalability and conversational richness of AI to counteract offloading and systematically rebuild retrieval, rather than further displacing it?This TREO paper outlines an experiment with master students at a university that contrasts a conversational AI-based assessment with an otherwise equivalent non-conversational generated-response assessment and hypothesizes that learners exposed to conversational probing will show higher knowledge retention.
