# From Automation to Augmentation

**著者**: Manolo Logrono Anto
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人間-AI協働

## Abstract

Organizations invest in training so that HR professionals can build skills in digital knowledge, analyzing data, and making good decisions, especially when using AI in HR. Ethical guidelines should cover issues like biased algorithms, protecting employee data, being open about how AI works, and making sure people are responsible for decisions made by AI. It's important to keep checking AI systems to ensure they treat everyone fairly during job interviews, promotions, and performance assessments. There needs to be a good mix between human and AI leadership. AI can handle tasks that involve data, while humans should focus on ethics, showing understanding and care, and planning for the future of the company. Good change management, involving employees, and being clear about how AI is used to keep trust are also key to helping people accept and use AI. Future studies should look into how AI affects company performance, how employees behave, the role of leaders, and how to make work sustainable for everyone. This way, the company can support innovation and create a welcoming place.
