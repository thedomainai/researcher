# GNU Octave Implementation of LQG-Based Human–Machine Teaming for Adaptive Closed-Loop Supply Chain Optimization under Uncertainty

**著者**: Gabriel Luca Nazar, Frédéric Andrès, Noha A. Mostafa
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人間-AI協働

## Abstract

his GNU Octave program implements a numerical simulation framework for Human–Machine Teaming (HMT) in Closed-Loop Supply Chain (CLSC) management under uncertainty. It builds upon a deterministic-equivalent Linear Quadratic Gaussian (LQG) production–inventory optimization model and extends the conventional Open-Loop No-Updating (OLN) strategy through an adaptive Model Predictive Control (MPC) mechanism. The implementation integrates manufacturing, remanufacturing, recycling, and disposal decisions while considering stochastic demand, product returns, inventory constraints, and quadratic operational costs. A simulated demand shock is introduced to evaluate the system's adaptive capabilities through AI-assisted forecasting, rolling-horizon re-optimization, and human supervisory intervention. The program compares the OLN baseline with the proposed HMT-enabled adaptive control strategy by examining inventory trajectories, production and recovery decisions, operational costs, uncertainty propagation, service-level performance, and sustainability indicators. It also provides a computational foundation for sensitivity analysis and Monte Carlo experiments to investigate the resilience and adaptability of closed-loop supply chains under changing operational conditions.
