# IOT_AI_ML Based Noise Source Identification, Monitoring and Controlling Device with Alert Mechanism

**著者**: Rajesh Bharatiya, Prakash Pagam
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人間-AI協働

## Abstract

Urban noise pollution is a growing environmental and health problem that negatively affects sleep quality, cardiovascular conditions, productivity, and general well-being. Conventional noise-monitoring systems rely on manual measurements and responses to complaints, making them predominantly passive, which limits timely responses and the attribution of the source. The current research presents a small, smart system that synthesizes the Internet of Things (IoT), Artificial Intelligence (AI), and Machine Learning (ML) to enable real-time noise monitoring, automatically determine the source, and adjust to effective control based on hierarchical alerts. Acoustic measurements obtained through geographically dispersed sensor nodes are analyzed at the network end using pre-trained machine-learning models to identify noise sources and measure adherence to regulatory rules. An IoT platform based on the cloud also allows data aggregation, visualization, longitudinal analysis, and notification to stakeholders. Field experiments carried out on residential, industrial, and construction sites showed strong performance with 89 % classification, less than three second alert response times, and statistically significant reduction in sustained noise violations. Consequently, the proposed platform is a scalable and practical solution for smart city noise regulation and active environmental control.
