# Evaluating AI-Assisted Assessment Workflows: A Governance Framework for Testing Operational Usefulness, Evidence Quality, Decision Boundaries, and Continuous Improvement

**著者**: Saheli Basu
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人間-AI協働

## Abstract

This paper presents an applied professional framework for evaluating AI-assisted assessment workflows as operational systems rather than isolated AI models. The framework examines whether an AI-assisted workflow is reliable enough to support real operational decisions by evaluating objective clarity, source fitness, task performance, evidence traceability, consistency, human oversight, and practical impact. The publication introduces a multidimensional evaluation approach that considers seven dimensions: Operational Objective, Source Fitness, Task Performance, Evidence Traceability, Consistency, Human Oversight, and Operational Impact. The framework emphasizes that trustworthy AI depends not only on model capability but on workflow design, evidence boundaries, accountability structures, and continuous evaluation. It provides methods for testing decision boundaries, identifying failure modes, and determining where human validation must remain non-negotiable. This publication is derived from professional practice in AI-assisted workflow testing, source and connector evaluation, evidence validation, operational review, human oversight, and continuous governance, generalized for public dissemination. This publication is practice-based rather than presented as peer-reviewed empirical research. It is intended for AI product and operations teams, assessment leaders, workflow designers, governance professionals, evaluators, and organizations testing AI-enabled decision support.
