# Cognitive Graph Embeddings for Human-AI Collaboration

**著者**: Jincheng Zhang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人間-AI協働

## Abstract

This paper introduces a novel approach to graph embeddings designed specifically for facilitating effective human-AI collaboration. Traditional graph embedding techniques often struggle to represent the complex, nuanced relationships that underpin human reasoning and understanding. We argue that current methods lack the ability to capture the cognitive aspects of these relationships, leading to suboptimal performance in collaborative scenarios. Our proposed solution, Cognitive Graph Embeddings (CGE), addresses this limitation by integrating cognitive models of human reasoning directly into the embedding process. CGE represents relationships not merely as connections, but as concepts, each associated with a specific mental model. This approach allows the AI system to understand the *why* behind the connections, leading to more intuitive and effective collaboration. We define the core claim, core mechanism, and rationale for this new approach. The research aims to bridge the gap between graph representation learning and cognitive science, ultimately producing embeddings that better reflect human cognitive processes.
