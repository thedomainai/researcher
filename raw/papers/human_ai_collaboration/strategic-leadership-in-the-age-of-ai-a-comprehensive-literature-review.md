# Strategic Leadership in the age of AI: A Comprehensive Literature Review

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

This paper provides a comprehensive literature review on strategic leadership in the age of Artificial Intelligence (AI), analysing recent research on how AI technologies are reshaping leadership, decision-making, and organizational performance. The review highlights that AI enables data-driven decision-making, enhances strategic foresight, and supports organizational agility, while introducing new challenges such as algorithmic bias, transparency, ethical governance, and workforce adaptation. Modern leaders are required to integrate technological fluency with human-centric values, ethical oversight, and cross-functional collaboration to navigate AI-driven digital transformation. Studies emphasize AI’s role in fostering innovation, improving forecasting precision, and aligning organizational strategies with sustainability and global development goals. Despite these advancements, gaps remain in empirical evidence regarding the long-term psychological and organizational impacts of AI-enabled leadership, as well as best practices for effective implementation. Overall, the literature underscores that future-ready leaders must balance AI adoption with ethical and strategic foresight to achieve sustainable, inclusive, and competitive organizational outcomes.
