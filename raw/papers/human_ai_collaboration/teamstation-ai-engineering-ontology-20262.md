# TeamStation AI Engineering Ontology 2026.2

**著者**: Lonnie McRorey
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人間-AI協働

## Abstract

A public research dataset containing 24 AI engineering domains maintained by TeamStation AI. Each record connects a stable concept ID to a definition, responsibilities, required skills, tools, enterprise outcomes, hiring signals, related concepts, role titles, technology affinities, and Distributed Engineering OS mappings. The dataset supports research on AI-native software delivery, human and agent team composition, engineering-capacity planning, retrieval, and knowledge graphs. It does not validate people, predict employment performance, or authorize automated employment decisions.
