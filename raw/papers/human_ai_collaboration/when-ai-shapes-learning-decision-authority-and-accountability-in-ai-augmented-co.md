# When AI Shapes Learning: Decision Authority and Accountability in AI-Augmented Consulting

**著者**: Chukui CHEN, Claude Liu, Scott Miau
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人間-AI協働

## Abstract

This case examines how an AI-based knowledge system, initially introduced as a learning support tool, gradually evolves into a decision-influencing actor in organizational settings. Drawing on a consulting firm implementing a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system, the case highlights how system design features, such as traceability and structured outputs, shape perceived authority and influence user judgment. As AI becomes embedded in onboarding and project preparation, it transforms not only knowledge access but also learning processes, cognitive engagement, and accountability structures. The case presents a strategic dilemma between restricting use, implementing governance, or scaling adoption, providing a basis for examining the role of AI in digital learning environments and its implications for decision authority and professional development.
