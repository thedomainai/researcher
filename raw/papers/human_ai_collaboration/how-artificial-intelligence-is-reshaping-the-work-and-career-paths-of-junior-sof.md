# How Artificial Intelligence Is Reshaping the Work and Career Paths of Junior Software Developers in China and the United States

**著者**: Yuxiang Zou
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人間-AI協働

## Abstract

The widespread adoption of generative AI in software development can impact junior developers as AI tools can help with coding, testing, debugging, and documentation tasks. This paper will explore how AI influences the job, the necessary skills, and the career paths of junior software engineers, and why this can vary across different countries, such as China and the USA. Experiments, job-vacancy studies, surveys, government statistics, and industry evidence are the bases for the analysis. While results have been inconsistent for different tasks and contexts, research indicates that AI can boost developer productivity, with one field experiment finding it has a greater impact on less-experienced developers. There is more code being written, more code to be reviewed, and more code to be communicated and understood because of AI's increasing automation of repetitive tasks and coding. While software developers should still be in demand, competition for jobs in this field might increase. The direct evidence of changes in junior hiring is more pronounced in the United States, while China has a massive developer community and a growing domestic AI industry. In conclusion, the evidence points to the evolution of the junior developer position, not its demise, due to the influence of AI.
