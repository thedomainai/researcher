# Physics-constrained Quantum-inspired Feature Selection for Scenario-augmented CO2 Trapping Efficiency Screening in Sandstone Saline Aquifers

**著者**: Alamgir Pathan, Mysara Eissa Mohyaldinn, Md Shokor A Rahaman
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人間-AI協働

## Abstract

Carbon capture and storage (CCS) offers an important route for lowering industrial CO2 emissions; however, storage-performance prediction becomes more uncertain when captured CO2 streams include impurities.This work introduces a physics-constrained, quantum-inspired feature-selection framework for interpretable scenario-augmented screening of CO₂ trapping efficiency under selected impurity descriptors in sandstone saline aquifers.A scenarioaugmented dataset was constructed by combining trapping-index data, sandstone residual-trapping evidence, capillarypressure information, and impurity cases comprising pure CO2, CO2 + 5 vol% H2S, CO2 + 5 vol% NO2, and CO2 + 5 vol% SO2.Residual, solubility, and total trapping efficiencies were used as prediction targets.The physics-aware feature set represented reservoir conditions, rock storage capacity, capillary behaviour, injection exposure, impurity composition, density, and buoyancy-related proxies.The proposed QIPFS approach retained target-specific compact subsets of 14-16 predictors from an initial 42-feature set, thereby reducing dimensionality while preserving or enhancing predictive performance.SHAP-based interpretation identified injection rate, pore volume, permeability, residual gas saturation, capillary effects, salinity, and storage-flow indicators as the dominant predictive contributors within the constructed dataset.In contrast, impurity-related variables primarily served as scenario identifiers rather than direct measured controls.
