# Quantum random walks: An introductory overview

**著者**: Julia Kempe
**年**: 2003 | **被引用数**: 1912
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 量子力学・量子情報

## Abstract

This article aims to provide an introductory survey on quantum random walks.Starting from a physical effect to illustrate the main ideas we will introduce quantum random walks, review some of their properties and outline their striking differences to classical walks.We will touch upon both physical effects and computer science applications, introducing some of the main concepts and language of present day quantum information science in this context.We will mention recent developments in this new area and outline some open questions. God does not play dice.
