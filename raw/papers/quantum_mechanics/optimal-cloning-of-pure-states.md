# Optimal cloning of pure states

**著者**: Reinhard F. Werner
**年**: 1998 | **被引用数**: 665
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 量子力学・量子情報

## Abstract

We construct a unique optimal quantum device for turning a finite number of $d$-level quantum systems in the same unknown pure state \ensuremath{\sigma} into $M$ systems of the same kind, in an approximation of the $M$-fold tensor product of the state \ensuremath{\sigma}.
