# A Secure and Efficient Lattice-Based Conditional Privacy-Preserving Authentication Protocol for VANETs

**著者**: Xiaopeng Zhou, Yang Xin, Haoyang Dang, Kaiyan Jin, Lei Shi
**年**: 2027 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 量子力学・量子情報

## Abstract

Vehicle ad-hoc networks (VANETs) are a research hotspot of intelligent transportation systems (ITS). With the rapid development of connected vehicles, ensuring secure, reliable and efficient information transmission in VANETs has become a critical issue for ITS. To address this problem, this paper proposes an efficient lattice-based conditional privacy-preserving authentication protocol for VANETs. The protocol resists quantum attacks, integrates fog computing to alleviate the authentication burden of trusted authority (TA) and supports batch authentication, which effectively reduces the response delay of VANETs. It is proven to be strongly unforgeable against adaptive chosen-message attacks in the random oracle model. We conducted Java and NS3 simulation experiments on the scheme. Compared with these of some known schemes, the efficiency of a single identity verification operation was improved by 28.89%, and the efficiency of batch verification of 1000 messages was increased by 21.48%. According to the Network Simulator 3 (NS-3) simulation experiments, even in a communication scenario with 400 vehicles and a high driving speed of 25 m/s, the average message loss rate is lower than 3%. The analysis shows that our scheme has obvious advantages in space and computational efficiency.
