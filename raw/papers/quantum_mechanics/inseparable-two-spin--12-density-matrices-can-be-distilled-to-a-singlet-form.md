# Inseparable Two Spin- 12 Density Matrices Can Be Distilled to a Singlet Form

**著者**: Michał Horodecki, Paweł Horodecki, Ryszard Horodecki
**年**: 1997 | **被引用数**: 493
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 量子力学・量子情報

## Abstract

A quantum system is called inseparable if its density matrix cannot be written as a mixture of product states. In this Letter we apply the separability criterion, local filtering, and Bennett et al. distillation protocol [Phys. Rev. Lett. 76, 722 (1996)] to show that any inseparable $2\ifmmode\times\else\texttimes\fi{}2$ system represents the entanglement which, however small, can be distilled to a singlet form.
