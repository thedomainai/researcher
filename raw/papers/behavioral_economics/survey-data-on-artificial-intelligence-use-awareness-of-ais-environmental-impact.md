# Survey data on artificial intelligence use, awareness of AI's environmental impact, climate anxiety and pro-climate behavioural intentions

**著者**: Patrick Hartmann
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 行動経済学

## Abstract

Overview Cross-sectional consumer survey data collected within the research project "Opportunities in the transition towards low carbon economy mitigating climate change: Stimulating low carbon consumption through behavioral incentives and nudges (TRANSCLIMA)" (ref. PID2021-123686OB-I00), University of the Basque Country UPV/EHU. The dataset supports a model in which the intensity of consumers' use of artificial intelligence services is associated with pro-climate intentions through a sequential pathway running from climate anxiety to climate commitment, with awareness of AI's environmental impact moderating the association between use intensity and climate anxiety. Data protection Personal data were collected and processed in compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD). The dataset published here has been anonymised: it contains no direct or indirect personal identifiers and cannot be used to re-identify participants.
