# Algorithmic Geopolitics in the Eastern Mediterranean

**著者**: Konstantinos Poyiadjis
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 行動経済学

## Abstract

This chapter examines how AI is reshaping strategic decision-making and security competition in the Eastern Mediterranean. Integrating structural realism, Regional Security Complex Theory, and bounded rationality, it conceptualizes the region as a transregional security subcomplex and develops algorithmic geopolitics to explain how data systems, autonomous platforms, and cyber capabilities mediate regional power. Drawing on legal, institutional, scholarly, and open-source materials, the chapter assesses maritime surveillance, critical-infrastructure protection, cyber attribution, and crisis management. It argues that AI redistributes rather than eliminates uncertainty: improved sensor fusion may strengthen tactical awareness, while automation bias, opacity, manipulation, and compressed decision cycles increase risks of misperception and escalation. It concludes that human oversight, uncertainty reporting, secure audit trails, resilient infrastructure, and stronger incident-management mechanisms are needed to ensure that AI supports rather than destabilizes strategic judgment.
