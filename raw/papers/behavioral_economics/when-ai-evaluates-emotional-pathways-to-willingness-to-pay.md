# When AI Evaluates: Emotional Pathways to Willingness to Pay

**著者**: Jin Sik Kim, T. K. Koo, Seong Dae Kim, Subin Im
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 行動経済学

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence (AI) increasingly evaluates and selects content, shaping what users ultimately encounter. We examine whether content selected through AI evaluators differs from human-selected content in downstream emotional responses and willingness to pay (WTP). Professional experts created five text-based advertising storyboards, of which 176 human evaluators and nine LLM-based AI evaluators independently rated for emotional impact. The highest-rated storyboard from each source was used in a 2 × 2 experiment crossing selection source (AI-selected vs. human-selected) and media format (text vs. visual) with 713 participants. Participants exposed to AI-selected content reported stronger emotional responses and higher WTP, and mediation analysis identified a significant indirect pathway through emotion. Media richness did not consistently enhance WTP. These findings conceptualize AI evaluation as a selection process and identify emotion as one downstream pathway linking AI-based selection to economic valuation.
