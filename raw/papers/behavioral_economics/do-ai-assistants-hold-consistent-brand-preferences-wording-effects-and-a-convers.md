# Do AI assistants hold consistent brand preferences? Wording effects and a conversational unit in AI B2B software recommendations

**著者**: Dhruv Walia
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 行動経済学

## Abstract

I am testing two things about AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude) recommending B2B software: whether the same brand pick comes back when the buying question is reworded and asked again, and whether asking for a single pick in a short conversation recovers that preference better than a single question. 22 buying situations, 5 wordings, 3 assistants at paid-tier consumer defaults with web search on, 3 reruns, 3 ways of asking. Primary outcome: a generalizability coefficient (E-rho-squared) from a binary mixed model on the picked brand, with Krippendorff's alpha as the co-primary check and the linear-model coefficient reported for comparison with prior work; primary contrast commit vs one-shot-forced, two-sided, smallest effect of interest 0.10. Controls, exclusions, coding rules, extraction gate, tier check, ethics, data release and conflict of interest are fixed in the registered text.
