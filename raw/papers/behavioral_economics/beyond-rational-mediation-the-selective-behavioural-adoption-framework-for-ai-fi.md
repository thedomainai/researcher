# Beyond Rational Mediation: The Selective Behavioural Adoption Framework for AI Financial Tool Adoption

**著者**: Chun Wing Hung, Tommy K. H. Chan
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 行動経済学

## Abstract

Robo-advisors could democratise institutional-grade wealth management, yet institutional adoption exceeding 90% coexists with individual adoption near 5%. Rational adoption frameworks yield mixed findings on this paradox, pointing to theoretical inadequacy: frameworks developed in organisational contexts with distributed risk and institutional support cannot accommodate individual high-stakes decisions where personal capital vulnerability activates affective rather than deliberative processing. We advance the Selective Behavioural Adoption Framework, positioning behavioural finance as theoretical replacement rather than supplement. Proof-of-concept PLS-SEM analysis (N = 151) indicates that trust exerts a substantial direct effect on adoption and reduces all three psychological barriers examined, yet only financial uncertainty mediates the trust-adoption relationship, with ambiguity aversion and social influence remaining psychologically active but behaviourally inert. Digital literacy demonstrates an expertise backfire effect, amplifying rather than mitigating financial uncertainty's negative influence on adoption. Full confirmatory validation with construct separation is planned.
