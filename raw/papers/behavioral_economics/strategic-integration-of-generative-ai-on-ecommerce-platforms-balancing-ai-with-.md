# Strategic Integration Of Generative Ai On Ecommerce Platforms: Balancing Ai With Consumer Aversion

**著者**: Jessica Wu, wenhui liu, 陈富赞, Harris Wu
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 行動経済学

## Abstract

This paper investigates the strategic deployment of Generative AI tools by eCommerce platforms.While AI enhances merchants' creative efficiency, consumers exhibit a natural tendency against AI-generated content (AIGC), a phenomenon rooted in "Algorithm Aversion" and manifested as the "Uncanny Valley."For platforms, the challenge is twofold: they must decide how much to invest in Generative AI tool performance and how to structure fees to ensure merchant participation while maintaining consumer trust.We model the equilibrium between platform tool performance, platform pricing, and merchant creative effort.Our findings highlight a "Subsidy Zone" where platforms must incentivize merchant adoption to counteract consumer skepticism when consumer aversion is high.We show that the optimal platform strategy is compensatory: investing in technical realism primarily to bridge the expertise gap of its users.
