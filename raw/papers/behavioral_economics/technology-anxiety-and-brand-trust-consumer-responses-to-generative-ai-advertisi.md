# Technology Anxiety and Brand Trust: Consumer Responses to Generative-AI Advertising

**著者**: Tran Duong Minh Chuyen
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 行動経済学

## Abstract

This structured integrative review examines how consumers interpret generative-AI advertising and how those interpretations shape brand trust. The review synthesizes 21 studies published between 2018 and 2025 across six evidence domains: AI advertising, AI disclosure, algorithm aversion, personalization and privacy, AI agents, and virtual influencers. Sixteen sources are primary empirical studies, three are reviews or meta-analyses, and two are conceptual articles. The analysis organizes the evidence through an extended Stimulus-Organism-Response framework. GenAI advertising cues, including disclosure, personalization, data-use transparency, human oversight, and human-likeness, are treated as stimuli. Consumers form parallel threat appraisals, such as technology anxiety, privacy concern, autonomy loss, perceived risk, and inauthenticity, and benefit or relational appraisals, such as authenticity, credibility, competence, social presence, control, and perceived agency. Brand trust is positioned as the proximal relational state that links these appraisals to approach outcomes, including ad attitude, engagement, purchase intention, data disclosure, and positive word of mouth, as well as avoidance outcomes. The synthesis indicates that AI disclosure is not intrinsically harmful or beneficial: outcomes depend on task subjectivity, data sensitivity, explanation quality, responsible brand conduct, and consumer capabilities. The article contributes a parsimonious S-O-R framework, an evidence-domain profile of the literature, and propositions that can guide future experiments and survey-based SEM in Vietnam.
