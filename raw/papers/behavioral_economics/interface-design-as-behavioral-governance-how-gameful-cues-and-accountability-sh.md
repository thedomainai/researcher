# Interface Design As Behavioral Governance: How Gameful Cues And Accountability Shape Reliance In Ai-Based Decision Support Systems

**著者**: Mario Richard Smeets
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 行動経済学

## Abstract

How do interface design and accountability cues jointly shape behavioral reliance on AI-based decision support?A 2×2 experiment (N = 190) indicates that gameful interface cues significantly increase behavioral reliance, while a justification requirement neither reduces reliance nor attenuates this effect.However, accountability decreases perceived gamefulness, revealing a governance spillover that shifts the experienced tone toward seriousness without altering behavior.Findings position interface design as a behavioral control lever in AI-supported decision routines and reveal distinct mechanisms of formal and informal control in human-AI interaction.
