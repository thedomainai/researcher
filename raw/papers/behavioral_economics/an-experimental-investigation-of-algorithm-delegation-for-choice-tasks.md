# An Experimental Investigation of Algorithm Delegation for Choice Tasks

**著者**: Fabrice Le Lec, Elina Ishmukhametova
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 行動経済学

## Abstract

Do individuals like to delegate their decisions to algorithms? This question is essential to understand the economic implications of artificial intelligence. Previous works showed an effect of “algorithm aversion”: people prefer to trust other people rather than algorithms after seeing it err. Nevertheless, the consensus on this topic has not been reached yet as contradictory results have recently been found. This project aims at investigating the attitude towards algorithmic delegation, i.e., the willingness or reluctance to delegate our choices or decisions to algorithms. In this perspective two main opposite forces can impact the decision-making: autonomy and freedom of choice or conversely choice aversion and overload. This study has an aim to reveal the potential aversion or appreciation to algorithmic delegation and its intensity.
