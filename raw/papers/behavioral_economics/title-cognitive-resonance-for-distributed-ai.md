# Title: Cognitive Resonance for Distributed AI

**著者**: Jincheng Zhang
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 行動経済学

## Abstract

Cognitive Resonance, a principle suggesting complex systems can achieve emergent behavior through subtle feedback loops, provides a novel framework for developing distributed AI models. This paper explores the implementation of a system leveraging this principle to create distributed intelligence, focusing on dynamically adjusting the influence of individual AI agents based on their internal state and external data streams. We propose a mechanism for fostering collective intelligence by establishing a core cycle of adaptive influence, aiming to surpass traditional approaches that emphasize individual decision-making. The research highlights the potential of this approach to unlock novel capabilities in AI systems, offering a pathway towards more robust and adaptable distributed intelligence.
