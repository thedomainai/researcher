# How to Detect AI Cheating in Pre-Employment Tests

**著者**: Admin Ashtolabs
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 行動経済学

## Abstract

Learn how to identify AI-assisted cheating in online pre-employment assessments while maintaining a fair and transparent hiring process. This practical guide by HireValid explores common methods candidates use to access AI assistance, browser and behavioral signals, unusual answer timing, webcam and identity checks, and answer similarity patterns. Discover how randomized question pools, timed assessments, interactive tasks, and Integrity Scores can help employers evaluate candidate results more effectively. The guide also explains the limitations of AI-text detectors, candidate privacy, consent, and the importance of human review before making hiring decisions. Designed for HR professionals, recruiters, hiring managers, and small to mid-sized businesses, this playbook provides actionable strategies for improving assessment integrity and making more informed recruitment decisions. Learn more at https://www.hirevalid.com/
