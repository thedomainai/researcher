# From Concept to Shape: An Analogue-AI Framework for Architectural Synthesis

**著者**: Bárbara Ruschel Lorenzoni, Angélica Paiva Ponzio, Ava Fatah gen. Schieck
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 行動経済学

## Abstract

Current applications of Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) in architectural design education emphasize open-ended ideation and image production, offering limited support for architectural synthesis. This paper proposes a hybrid analogue-AI framework for integrating Generative AI (GenAI) into the architectural design studio, employing GenAI across three stages — problem framing, solution generation, and informed refinement within which the design space repeatedly expands and contracts. Building on Author´s (2025a) AI-assisted workflow and Author’s (2025b) decomposition of the design process into cognitively matched steps, this framework positions GenAI as a support to design reasoning without replacing analogue representation, human agency, or architectural judgement. We argue that constraining GenAI inputs with prior analogue work may support reproducibility, informed creativity and design control, contributing to outcomes that are both original and constructible while addressing recurring AI dependency on open-ended prompting and stylistic iteration.
