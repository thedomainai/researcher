# “This is not a Human”: The Effect of Transparency Interventions on the Humanization of Relational Conversational Agents

**著者**: Lenart Motnikar, Katharina Baum, Sarah Spiekermann
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 行動経済学

## Abstract

The rapid growth in the use of conversational agents (CAs) for relational purposes, such as companionship, has raised concerns about psychological risks associated with their humanization. Policymakers increasingly mandate transparency to mitigate these effects, yet the efficacy of current approaches, such as informational disclaimers, remains unclear. This study examines how different transparency interventions influence users’ humanization of an emotional support CA. In an experiment with 1,144 participants, respondents interacted with the CA under four conditions: no intervention, a neutral disclaimer, a strong warning, or AI identity signaling. Results show that AI identity and the warning significantly reduce perceived anthropomorphism and social presence, whereas the disclaimer produced no notable effect. Linguistic analyses further revealed shifts in users’ communication patterns across conditions. These results challenge prevailing practices that rely solely on disclaimers and offer guidance on mitigating risk and ethical liabilities in the use of relational CAs.
