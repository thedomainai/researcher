# Next Generation Autonomous Well Testing: An AI-Powered Platform for Scheduling, Stability Verification, Validation, and Decision Making

**著者**: S. Tiar, M. M. Abdelrahman, S. Alarabi, F. Alshamsi, C. Kada Kloucha

## Abstract

Abstract Production well testing in mature conventional carbonate fields poses challenges that extend well beyond those encountered in unconventional shale developments. Multiphase flow behavior, highly variable GOR and water cuts, pronounced reservoir heterogeneity, and ageing surface infrastructure collectively limit the effectiveness of static, rule-based test automation. While earlier work addressed fixed-threshold scheduling and validation for single-phase unconventional wells, a fully adaptive, closed loop context aware data-intelligence-native platform spanning the entire well-test lifecycle in a large, mature conventional field has not previously been reported. This paper presents the field deployment and operational validation of such a platform. The platform is built on an integrated digital architecture that combines data driven machine learning, real-time SCADA ingestion, production-database integration, and physics-informed constraint modelling. It is implemented through five cooperating context aware data intelligent layers. Intelligent Multi-Constraint Scheduling dynamically prioritizes the well-test queue by balancing test-overdue status, production contribution, performance deviation, facility availability, and routing feasibility. Adaptive Stability Determination uses field-trained time-series models to identify valid steady-state windows across multiphase flow regimes, replacing fixed-threshold logic. Multi-Dimensional Data Validation combines dynamic production-envelope modelling, phase-ratio consistency, allocation correlation, data driven anomaly detection, and a Virtual Flow Meter that provides an independent, physics-based verification of measured rates. Contextual Anomaly Classification categorizes detected anomalies into instrumentation drift, testing-facility anomalies, slugging, separator carryover, or genuine production change, thereby reducing false-positive intervention rates. Recommendation-Driven Approval Orchestration presents confidence-scored results recommendations through an integrated dashboard, collapsing the traditional multi-step manual review into a single-screen decision interface. Field deployment delivered measurable operational and production benefits, including a 90% reduction in engineering hours for scheduling and validation, a 20% improvement in first-pass test acceptance, and an 80% reduction in test-to-approval cycle time. The platform enabled an 80% increase in testing volume without additional manpower and was associated with a measured 2% production uplift. Near-real-time approvals and higher-quality validated test data accelerated well-optimization decisions and enabled automated, field-ready production scenarios in place of manual forecasting. This work shifts industry practice from encoding static engineering rules toward AI systems that learn and reason from operational data, enabling improved allocation integrity, reservoir surveillance, and accelerated decision-making at scale.
