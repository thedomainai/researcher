# Adaptive Selection of Effective AI Assistance in AI-assisted Decision Making Using Multi-Armed Bandits

**著者**: Zhuoyan Li, Ze Ma, Shishuang He, Zhuoran Lu, Ziang Xiao
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 行動経済学

## Abstract

In AI-assisted decision making, the way that AI assistance is presented significantly influences human cognition, leading to varied reliance strategies. Current human-AI interaction designs often constrain decision makers to interact with specified static forms of AI assistance, rather than providing personalized, intelligent support that adapts to individual behavior and decision contexts. Inspired by the nudge theory in behavioral economics, in this paper, we conceptualize various forms of AI assistance as “choice architectures”. We then introduce a human-adaptive AI interaction framework in AI-assisted decision making, which is based on contextualized multi-armed bandits, to adaptively identify the most effective choice architecture for each individual that can nudge them towards relying on AI more appropriately and making more accurate decisions under different decision contexts. Results from between-subjects experiments across two types of decision making tasks show that our framework promoted more appropriate human reliance on AI compared to other default forms of AI assistance. We conclude by discussing the limitations and design implications of our findings for AI-assisted decision making.
