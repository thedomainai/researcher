# The Finfluencer-FOMO Nexus: Conceptualizing Social Media-Driven Investment Volatility in the Indian Equity Market

**著者**: 
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 行動経済学

## Abstract

The rapid rise of financial influencers, popularly termed "finfluencers," on platforms such as YouTube, Instagram, and Telegram has changed how India's retail investors discover, evaluate, and act on investment ideas.This conceptual paper examines how finfluencer-driven content interacts with the psychological state of Fear of Missing Out (FOMO) to produce herd-like trading ss and, in turn, elevated short-term volatility in the Indian equity market.Drawing on Behavioural finance theory, herding literature, and recent empirical work on social-media-informed investors, the paper synthesizes existing scholarship to identify a set of recurring research gaps: the absence of an integrated model linking finfluencer stimuli, psychological mediators, and market-level volatility outcomes; limited attention to the Indian regulatory context following the Securities and Exchange Board of India's (SEBI) 2024-2025 finfluencer guidelines; and a scarcity of studies that treat social media virality as a feedback loop rather than a one-way influence.To address these gaps, the paper proposes an original conceptual framework, the "Finfluencer-FOMO Nexus," which maps the pathway from finfluencer stimulus and platform amplification, through psychological mechanisms (social proof, herding, FOMO, and overconfidence), to investor Behaviour and aggregate market volatility, moderated by financial literacy and regulatory oversight.The paper concludes with theoretical propositions, practical implications for regulators and investors, and directions for future empirical validation.
