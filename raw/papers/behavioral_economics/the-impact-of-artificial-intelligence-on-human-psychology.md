# THE IMPACT OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE ON HUMAN PSYCHOLOGY

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 行動経済学

## Abstract

This article analyzes the impact of artificial intelligence (AI) technologies on human psyche, behavior, and cognitive processes. The study examines the role of AI systems in personal development, decision-making, and emotional states. Furthermore, the paper discusses mechanisms for preventing negative consequences such as digital dependency, cognitive offloading (laziness), and social isolation. The findings of the article provide practical recommendations for psychologists and technology specialists on maintaining human mental health in an AI-driven environment
