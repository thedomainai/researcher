# Strategic Integration of AI for Data‑Driven Decisions and Automation in Operations Management

**著者**: Lordt Becklines, Omar El-Gayar
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: オペレーション管理

## Abstract

Organizations are investing in artificial intelligence (AI) for forecasting, scheduling, quality, maintenance, and service operations, yet many initiatives stall at pilot scale because leaders lack an operations-centered strategic integration logic that links use cases to workflows, data and platform foundations, governance, and change execution. This study develops an evidence-informed framework for the strategic integration of AI through a PRISMA-guided systematic literature review and design science artifact construction. The review applied explicit inclusion and exclusion criteria, structured coding, quality appraisal, and evidence-to-design translation to derive seven decision domains and a strategy-to-execution cycle. Initial ex ante formative evaluation combines analytical traceability mapping and literature-grounded scenario demonstration in representative operations management contexts to assess clarity, completeness, internal consistency, actionability, and boundary conditions. The resulting framework offers a rigorous and practical blueprint for scalable and trustworthy AI-enabled operations while clarifying where future expert and field validation are still required.
