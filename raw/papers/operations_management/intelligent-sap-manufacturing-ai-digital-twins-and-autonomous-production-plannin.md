# INTELLIGENT SAP MANUFACTURING AI, Digital Twins, and Autonomous Production Planning

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

Intelligent SAP Manufacturing: AI, Digital Twins, and Autonomous Production Planning provides a practical framework for transforming conventional SAP-based manufacturing into an intelligent, connected, and progressively autonomous operating environment. The book explains how artificial intelligence, machine learning, digital twins, advanced planning, and real-time shop-floor data can improve production planning, asset reliability, scheduling, and operational decision-making. Using the SAP manufacturing ecosystem as its foundation, the book examines SAP S/4HANA, PP/DS, Integrated Business Planning, Digital Manufacturing, Asset Performance Management, Business Technology Platform, AI Core, and Joule. It demonstrates how these technologies can support predictive maintenance, demand forecasting, quality prediction, capacity optimization, simulation, closed-loop production planning, and agentic AI applications. Particular attention is given to the data quality, master data governance, integration architecture, explainability, cybersecurity, and human oversight required for dependable AI adoption. The book also presents implementation roadmaps, maturity models, governance principles, assessment worksheets, key performance indicators, and practical architectural patterns. Rather than advocating fully automated plants without human control, it proposes a controlled autonomy model in which planners establish decision rules, define operating boundaries, review exceptions, and gradually expand system authority based on measurable evidence. Written for SAP consultants, manufacturing leaders, enterprise architects, production planners, and data professionals, the book connects emerging intelligent-manufacturing concepts with the practical realities of enterprise implementation and shop-floor operations.
