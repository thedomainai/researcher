# AI-Powered Compression: Real-Time Monitoring and Simulation for Maximazing Gas Production

**著者**: Diego Cantor Alvarez, Abdulla AlThawadi
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーション管理

## Abstract

Abstract Machinery capacity in Oil and Gas operations is often constrained by original design specifications yet evolving field conditions present opportunities for performance optimization. This study introduces a comprehensive methodology for enhancing the output of API 618 reciprocating, multi-stage gas compressors by integrating Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) performance software with real-time Artificial Intelligence (AI) analytics. The approach involved deep analysis of design limitations and operational data to calibrate an AI model, which identified optimal compressor performance benchmarks and quantified deviations from actual field performance. Multiple simulations, executed within OEM criteria, enabled precise identification of performance gaps and informed targeted adjustments to compressor configurations. These optimizations resulted in a substantial increase in compression station output—from an average of 70.7 MMSCFD to 78.2 MMSCFD, with peak production reaching 81 MMSCFD, representing an 11% improvement. Additionally, oil production was elevated by integrating new wells, yielding an incremental revenue of $494,000 over a 32-day trial period. The findings demonstrate the transformative potential of AI-driven performance monitoring and optimization in upstream gas compression, offering a scalable framework for operational excellence and revenue enhancement without capital expenditure. The methodology and results provide actionable insights for operators seeking to maximize asset productivity and align with digital transformation initiatives. This case study establishes a benchmark for AI-enabled operational excellence in the oil and gas industry, supporting continuous improvement and strategic investment in advanced analytics.
