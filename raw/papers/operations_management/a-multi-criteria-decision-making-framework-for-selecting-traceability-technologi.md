# A Multi-Criteria Decision-Making Framework for Selecting Traceability Technologies in Food Supply Chains

**著者**: Mladen Božić, Svetlana Dabić-Miletić
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーション管理

## Abstract

Food supply chains (FSCs) are characterized by high requirements regarding quality, safety, shelf life, and the timely exchange of information among supply chain participants. These requirements are particularly pronounced in the case of milk and dairy products, which are sensitive to temperature conditions and require continuous control during storage and distribution. One of the key challenges in FSC is ensuring reliable product traceability, especially when existing systems are based on barcodes, manual data entry, and limited availability of real-time information. This paper develops a hybrid multi-criteria decision-making model for selecting the most appropriate technology to improve product traceability in FSCs, based on a specific company case. Internet of Things (IoT), radio-frequency identification (RFID), and Blockchain technologies are considered as alternatives. The evaluation is performed using 10 economic, technological, operational, and security-related criteria. The fuzzy best-worst method (BWM) is applied to determine the criteria weights. The fuzzy measurement of alternatives and ranking according to compromise solution (MARCOS) method is used for the final ranking of the alternatives. The results show that the most important criteria are related to temperature monitoring, real-time monitoring, and traceability capability. The final ranking indicates that IoT represents the most suitable solution, followed by RFID, while Blockchain ranked third. The obtained results confirm that technology selection should be based on its alignment with the specific requirements and operational priorities of the FSC.
