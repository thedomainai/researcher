# Digital Transformation Strategies for Sustainable Organizational and Workforce Development

**著者**: Lukas Müller, Anna Keller
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーション管理

## Abstract

Digital transformation has become a highly useful approach that organizations can employ to increase operational efficiency, innovation, competitiveness, and sustainable development. The introduction of technologies such as artificial intelligence (AI), cloud computing, IoT, big data analytics, automation, and digital collaboration platforms affects the way in which organizations deliver value and manage their resources. Nonetheless, digital transformation entails more than the introduction of innovative technologies. The restructuring of organizations, training of their employees, development of their digital skills, leadership, culture, cybersecurity practices, and governance are all essential aspects of digital transformation. The paper focuses on the ways in which digital transformation strategies may lead to sustainable development of organizations by enhancing the capabilities of their employees. The study deals with the interrelationships between digital technologies, organizational operations, sustainability, employee capabilities, and strategic decision-making. Special emphasis is put on such aspects of digital transformation as digital skills development, reskilling and upskilling, digital leadership, data-driven decision-making, process optimization, employee engagement, and flexibility in work organization. Major barriers are also addressed. In addition, a theoretical framework is put forth which outlines how digital transformation could have an impact on economic, social, and environmental sustainability via organizational and workforce development approaches. Finally, it is concluded in the study that organizations can ensure sustainability in their digital transformations when technology adoption is combined with human-centered approaches to workforces.
