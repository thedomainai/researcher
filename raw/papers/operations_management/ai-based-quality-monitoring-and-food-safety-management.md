# AI-Based Quality Monitoring and Food Safety Management

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

AI has transformation in the area of food quality inspection and safety management due to the capabilities that have been introduced in rapid, precise, non-destructive and predictive assessment of food quality through the production process, processing, storage, transport, retailing and consumption. These techniques are applicable in fast monitoring and quick intervention in areas of dairy products, meat products, seafood, fruits and vegetables, cereals, beverages and ready to eat meals. This chapter discusses the principles, technologies, applications, benefits, challenges, and future prospects of AI-based food quality monitoring and safety management, with particular emphasis on intelligent sensors, real-time contamination detection, automated inspection, shelf-life prediction, process optimization, traceability, and predictive risk management across diverse food sectors.
