# Industry 4.0 in Applications of Waste Management

**著者**: S. C. Anjana Devi
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーション管理

## Abstract

The amalgamation and intervention of technologies such as machine learning and Internet of Things with robotics and “cobots” in the industrial processes have resulted in exponential efficiency. These remarkable contributions are the outcomes of Industry 4.0 in various domains and operations. Industry 4.0 has tremendous influence on the society on the positive direction along with certain issues; one such concern is the management of waste generated from various terminals within the conversion drive of raw and semifinished materials into consumable products. According to sustainable development goals (SDGs), waste management is essentially a vital concern for the all the nations. Explicitly, SDG 6 focused on Clean Water and Sanitation, SDG 11 with Sustainable Cities and Communities, and SDG 12 with Responsible Consumption and Production have the objective of moving toward creating a better ecosystem. Unquestionably, Industry 4.0 is reforming the approach of waste management with the application of blockchain, big data, automation, artificial intelligence (AI), and Internet of Things, along with many more technologies. These technology revolutions condense negative environmental impact and enhance operational efficiency with optimal resource utilization. Some of the applications are in the area of smart waste bins equipped for real-time monitoring of waste levels and AI-driven sorting for separating materials. Robotics reduces workplace threats by minimising human involvement in risky waste-handling tasks, while blockchain boosts transparency, traceability, and regulatory compliance across the complete waste management lifecycle. This chapter discovers the scope of Industry 4.0 in managing waste, a significant SDG that bothers all the stakeholders. The chapter places emphasis on real-world applications with relevant cases following the future trends, obviously not forgetting the challenges. Leveraging the application of smart technologies, societies, manufacturing, business processing, and cities can evolve toward sustainable and data-driven system. Industry 4.0 in a substantial direction contributes to global sustainability development goals, thus bringing in clean and smart cities for mankind.
