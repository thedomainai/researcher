# The Synergistic Integration of AI, IoT, and Blockchain for Intelligent and Sustainable Supply Chain Management – A Comprehensive Review

**著者**: Abijith Balaji, R. Kavitha, S. Akash, S. Pavithra
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーション管理

## Abstract

The integration of technologies like IoT, Blockchain, and AI in supply chains is crucial for modern operations. This study reviews the synergistic potential of these technologies, particularly in emerging markets. It presents a comprehensive model for supply chain management, demonstrating improved visibility, security, and flexibility. The study serves supply chain experts and technology stakeholders by providing a scalable, practical approach adaptable across industries and geographical regions.
