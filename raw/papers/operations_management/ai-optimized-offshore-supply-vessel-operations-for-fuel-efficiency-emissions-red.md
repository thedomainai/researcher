# AI-Optimized Offshore Supply Vessel Operations for Fuel Efficiency, Emissions Reduction, and Operational Sustainability

**著者**: J. Alnaqbi, S. Alhmoudi, Ali Albreiki
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーション管理

## Abstract

Abstract This paper investigates the application of AI-driven optimisation to offshore supply vessel (OSV) operations in order to reduce fuel consumption, minimise emissions, and improve logistical efficiency in offshore oil and gas activities. The objective is to develop an intelligent operational framework capable of dynamically optimising vessel routing, speed profiles, and load distribution based on real-time environmental and operational conditions. An integrated optimisation framework was developed using operational vessel data, meteorological conditions, wave parameters, fuel consumption profiles, and offshore scheduling requirements. Machine learning and predictive routing algorithms were applied to continuously evaluate optimal navigation paths, vessel speeds, and cargo allocation strategies under varying offshore conditions. A real-time optimisation model was designed to dynamically respond to weather fluctuations, operational delays, and changing supply demands. Simulation studies were conducted using representative offshore logistics scenarios to evaluate fuel efficiency, emissions reduction, voyage reliability, and operational responsiveness. Comparative benchmarking was performed against conventional fixed-route offshore supply operations. Simulation studies indicate that the proposed AI-optimised framework has the potential to reduce offshore vessel fuel consumption by approximately 16% compared to conventional operations. These results, while promising, are derived from discrete-event simulation and require validation through real-world deployment. The framework demonstrates pathway viability for achieving measurable efficiency gains, with simulation suggesting proportional CO2 emissions reductions and improved voyage scheduling performance. Dynamic route optimisation enabled vessels to avoid high-resistance sea conditions, reducing unnecessary engine loading and improving overall energy efficiency. Adaptive speed control further contributed to fuel savings while maintaining operational delivery requirements.The system also demonstrated improved responsiveness to operational disruptions, including weather-related delays and fluctuating offshore demand schedules. AI-assisted cargo allocation reduced inefficient load distribution and minimised unnecessary vessel movements between offshore facilities. Comparative analysis indicates that the proposed framework outperforms traditional fixed-routing strategies in both environmental and operational performance metrics.In addition to reducing operating costs, the framework supports offshore sustainability objectives by lowering fuel usage and enhancing logistical coordination across offshore assets. The findings demonstrate strong potential for integrating AI-driven optimisation into offshore marine operations as part of broader digital transformation and decarbonization initiatives within the upstream energy sector. This work introduces a real-time, AI-assisted optimisation framework specifically designed for offshore supply vessel operations in the upstream sector. Unlike conventional marine logistics approaches based on static routing and scheduling, the proposed system integrates environmental data, operational demand, and predictive optimisation to simultaneously enhance fuel efficiency, reduce emissions, and improve offshore logistical performance within a scalable digital operations framework.
