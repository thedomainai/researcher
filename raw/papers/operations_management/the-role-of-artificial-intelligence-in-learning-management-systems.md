# THE ROLE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN LEARNING MANAGEMENT SYSTEMS

**著者**: Xujanov Elbek
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーション管理

## Abstract

This article examines the role of artificial intelligence (AI) in learning management systems (LMS) in higher education. It analyses how AI can support personalized learning, automated assessment, intelligent tutoring, learning analytics, and teachers’ instructional work. A conceptual approach is used to synthesize relevant literature and identify practical integration principles. The analysis indicates that AI-enabled LMS functions may improve the timeliness of feedback, help identify learning difficulties, and support evidence-informed educational decisions. However, these benefits are not automatic: they depend on pedagogical design, data quality, transparency, privacy safeguards, and meaningful human oversight. The article proposes a functional integration model that connects learner data, AI-based analysis, personalized recommendations, an intelligent assistant, and teacher review. Since the study is conceptual, the proposed model requires empirical evaluation before claims about its effectiveness can be made.
