## Abstract

The threat of unparalleled disruptions of international supply chains caused by health crises (example, COVID-19), geopolitical and geo economic tensions, and an increase in the risk of cyber-sabotage is growing. The conventional risk management strategies are not sufficient in handling the multi-dimensional nature of supply chains today. This chapter explores the potential uses of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in securing, stabilizing and future-proofing supply chains in the face of volatile conditions. By combining predictive analytics with blockchain, machine learning-driven anomaly forecasting, and the use of AI-driven decision-support tools, companies can also ensure resilience against uncertainty, cyberattack, and breakdowns in logistical operations. The chapter also examines how the incorporation of AI and supply chain governance connect in terms of ethics, and how to protect data security.
