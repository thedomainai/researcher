# Actual Modern Problems OF Picture Coding

**著者**: S. A. (Saydazimov) ugli
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーション管理

## Abstract

This article provides detailed information on the latest digital image encoding problems and their solutions. The software and hardware requirements for optimizing the size and quality of digital images are reviewed, as well as the role of artificial intelligence and machine learning technologies in the field. The article also discusses the future directions of digital image coding, including the need to develop new algorithms and coding standards for ultra-high resolution images. The problems of applying new technologies, which are important in improving the efficiency of coding, are also considered.
