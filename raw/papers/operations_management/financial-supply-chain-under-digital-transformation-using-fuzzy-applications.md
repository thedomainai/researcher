# Financial Supply Chain Under Digital Transformation Using Fuzzy Applications

**著者**: T.K. Nivetha, Aruna Polisetty
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーション管理

## Abstract

Digital technologies are transforming business models into supply chain finance (SCF) by improving efficiency, transparency, and risk management. Fuzzy techniques address uncertainty in financial decision-making as new technologies change SCF. This paper explores financial supply chain management, fuzzy methodologies, and digital transformation technologies like artificial intelligence (AI), blockchain, fintech, and digital finance. A systematic literature review (SLR) was conducted using Scopus database found 23 eligible English-language publications. Results show fuzzy techniques are mainly used for risk assessment, credit appraisal, and decision-support in SCF, with limited integration into advanced digital transformation tools. The study proposes a conceptual framework to reveal the antecedents, episodes and consequences (ADO) of fuzzy approaches integrated with digital technologies to improve SCF performance. The findings help organizations improve financial supply chain operations under uncertainty using a hybrid digital-fuzzy model. This review outlines the existing knowledge, identifies integration gaps and research opportunities in fuzzy and digital transformation in SCF.
