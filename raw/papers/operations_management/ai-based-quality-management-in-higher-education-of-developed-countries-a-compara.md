# AI-BASED QUALITY MANAGEMENT IN HIGHER EDUCATION OF DEVELOPED COUNTRIES: A COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS AND UZBEKISTAN'S EXPERIENCE

**著者**: Djumaniyazov Umrbek Ilkhamovich
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーション管理

## Abstract

This article presents a comparative analysis of the introduction and effectiveness of artificial intelligence (AI)-based quality management systems in the higher education systems of developed countries – the USA, the United Kingdom, Germany, Finland, and South Korea. The article examines the impact of predictive analytics, learning analytics, AI-monitoring tools, and automated accreditation systems on the effectiveness of higher education institutions. The current situation in Uzbekistan’s higher education system is assessed, and practical conclusions and recommendations are put forward for applying international experience to national conditions.
