# THE IMPACT OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN SUSTAINABLE TEXTILE SUPPLY CHAIN MANAGEMENT

**著者**: Bernadette OLUYOLE, Obere Lauretta AFATAKPA
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

The textile industry is one of the largest sectors globally, employing millions of workers and contributing significantly to global trade. However, it faces persistent challenges concerning sustainability, efficiency, and environmental impact. Traditional practices have psroven inadequate in addressing growing demands for transparency and responsible production. Artificial Intelligence (AI) is emerging as a transformative force, offering advanced tools to optimize production, manage supply chains, and align textile operations with global sustainability goals. This paper provides a detailed analysis of AI’s role in sustainable textile supply chain management, examining how machine learning, predictive analytics, and automation can enhance efficiency, transparency, flexibility, and responsiveness. Using global case examples and theoretical insights, the study demonstrates AI’s capacity to minimize waste, reduce carbon footprints, enable ethical sourcing, and strengthen collaboration across supply chains. The findings emphasize that the integration of AI into supply chains is not merely an evolution of technology but a paradigm shift that redefines how industries approach sustainability. The research concludes that the future of textile supply chains depends on harnessing AI-driven innovations to foster resilience, competitiveness, and environmental responsibility. Keywords: Artificial Intelligence, Supply Chain Management, Textile Industry, Sustainability, Resource Optimization.
