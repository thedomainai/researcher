# AI service quality in the workplace: conceptualization, scale development and validation

**著者**: Pengyi Shen, Rumeng Zhang, Demin Wan
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーション管理

## Abstract

Purpose Workplaces are increasingly adopting artificial intelligence (AI) to collaborate with employees. However, no scale currently exists that comprehensively measures the dimensions of AI service quality in the workplace from an employee perspective. This paper aims to address this gap by developing and validating a scale of AI service quality in the workplace. Design/methodology/approach This paper adopted a mixed methods approach and conducted a series of studies with China as the research context. First, scale items were generated through semistructured interviews and a grounded theory approach. Subsequently, the scale was evaluated through rigorous procedures, including exploratory factor analysis and confirmatory factor analysis. Finally, the nomological validity of the scale of AI service quality in the workplace was confirmed by examining its relationships with job satisfaction and job engagement. Findings The AI service quality scale in the workplace comprises 24 items, distributed across three core dimensions: functional quality, experiential quality and collaborative quality. Functional quality includes reliability, intelligence and security; experiential quality includes anthropomorphism and empathy; collaborative quality includes complementarity, fluency and proactiveness. Moreover, AI service quality in the workplace is significantly and positively associated with employees’ job satisfaction and job engagement. Originality/value AI represents a key technological evolution in the contemporary workplace. As this emerging technology continues to evolve, it has generated numerous research questions that remain largely unexplored. It is hoped that this work will spark scholars’ interest, drive further advancement in the fields of AI service management and information systems, and provide important practical implications for organizations to optimize AI applications and enhance AI service quality.
