# Digital Twins and Predictive Intelligence for Smart Tourism and Travel Operations

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

This chapter examines the transition from e-tourism to AI-enabled Smart Tourism 2.0, focusing on digital twins and predictive intelligence. Digital twins are virtual replicas of destinations that enable simulation, scenario testing, and decision-making. The chapter reviews deep-learning models, including Long Short-Term Memory and multi-graph convolutional approaches, which improve tourism demand forecasting compared with traditional statistical methods. It also discusses intelligent route optimization and AI-driven personalized visitor experiences. Real-time visitor-flow management, overtourism mitigation, carbon-reduction strategies, rural inclusion, and emerging metaverse and augmented-reality applications are examined. Governance challenges related to data privacy, blockchain trust models, and digital inequality are also addressed. Drawing on evidence from more than 60 Scopus-indexed studies, the chapter provides an integrated framework for the sustainable and ethical implementation of intelligent tourism systems.
