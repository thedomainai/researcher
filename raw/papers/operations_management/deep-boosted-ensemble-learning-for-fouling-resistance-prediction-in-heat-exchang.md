# Deep-Boosted Ensemble Learning for Fouling Resistance Prediction in Heat Exchangers: An Industry 4.0 Framework

**著者**: Abdul Wahid Soomro, Laiha Mat Kiah, Rafidah Md Noor, Salim Newaz Kazi, Kaleemullah Shaikh
**年**: 2027 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーション管理

## Abstract

Fouling of heat exchanger surfaces reduces thermal efficiency, increases energy consumption, and can lead to costly maintenance interventions. This study proposes a deep-boosted ensemble learning framework integrating Convolutional Neural Network-Bidirectional Long Short-Term Memory (CNN-BiLSTM) representation learning with eXtreme Gradient Boosting (XGB) and Light Gradient Boosting Machine (LGBM) residual correction, followed by constrained least-squares ensemble fusion for near-real-time estimation of contemporaneous fouling resistance in a counter-flow double-pipe heat exchanger (DPHE). An Internet of Things (IoT) sensor-equipped experimental platform was developed to acquire timestamped thermohydraulic measurements under controlled operating conditions. The evaluation comprised 9292 quality-filtered observations from the internal DPHE experiment and an external plate heat exchanger (PHE) dataset comprising 2358 observations obtained from a publicly available dataset. On the held-out internal DPHE test set, the Optimized Weighted Ensemble achieved R2=0.9824 and root mean square error (RMSE) =1.00×10−6 m2 K/W. In the supplementary cross-study comparison, the model attained the highest Normalized Performance Index (NPI = 0.9877) within the predefined comparable benchmark set. External evaluation produced closely comparable performance without recalibration, while repeated-seed ablation indicated that the clearest incremental benefit arose from ensemble-level fusion. The framework supports low-latency, near-real-time fouling-state monitoring and data-informed maintenance decision support under the investigated laboratory conditions, providing a basis for heat-exchanger monitoring in Industry 4.0 environments.
