# Life Cycle Assessment of AI-Powered Fermentation Systems

**著者**: A. Kosiha, G. Kalaiarasi, P. Gowthami, Yuvaraj Dinakarkumar, R. Suresh Babu
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーション管理

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence (AI)-powered fermentation systems integrate AI algorithms with bioprocess engineering to enhance efficiency, control, and sustainability in industrial fermentation. This study presents a comprehensive Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) of such systems, evaluating their environmental impact across key stages: system design, AI model development, operational deployment, and end-of-life disposal. The analysis compares AI-augmented fermentation with traditional systems, focusing on energy consumption, greenhouse gas emissions, raw material usage, and waste generation. AI integration significantly improves yield prediction, process optimization, and adaptive control, leading to reduced resource input and lower emissions during operation. However, the environmental cost of data infrastructure and hardware requirements introduces notable impacts during the development phase. Results highlight the trade-offs between digitalization benefits and the environmental burdens of computational infrastructure. Recommendations include leveraging green data centers, modular hardware, and eco-friendly AI model training practices. This LCA supports the sustainable development of biomanufacturing by guiding policy, innovation, and industrial adoption of intelligent fermentation technologies.
