# From Forecast Accuracy to Resilience: The Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Supply Chain Management

**著者**: Zhixian He
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーション管理

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence (AI) is increasingly presented as a general-purpose technology for supply chain management, yet the literature remains fragmented across technology families, functional areas and research designs. This paper develops an integrative account of how AI changes supply chain decisions and outcomes. Drawing on nine conceptual, empirical and review sources published between 1997 and 2024, it identifies four mechanisms through which AI affects supply chains: richer demand signal extraction and forecasting; automated evaluation and monitoring of suppliers and contracts; dynamic optimisation of inventory, production and logistics decisions; and earlier detection of, and faster response to, disruption. These mechanisms operate along a ladder of delegated authority that runs from descriptive analytics to bounded autonomous decision-making, and their realisation depends on data infrastructure, organisational absorptive capacity and governance rather than on algorithms alone. Reported performance effects are positive but conditional: survey evidence associates AI-enabled analytics with operational and environmental performance, whereas benchmarking evidence shows that machine learning does not automatically outperform established statistical methods. The paper concludes that the principal impact of AI is less the substitution of human planners than the compression of decision latency and the reallocation of managerial attention, and it identifies the data, organisational and governance conditions on which those gains depend.
