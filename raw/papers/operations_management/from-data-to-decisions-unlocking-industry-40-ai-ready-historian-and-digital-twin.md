# From Data to Decisions, Unlocking Industry 4.0: AI-Ready Historian and Digital Twin Driving Efficiency &amp; Reliability at Engro Fertilizers

**著者**: Ahmad Iqtidar, Mohsin Mukhtar
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: オペレーション管理

## Abstract

Engro Fertilizers, a leading nitrogen fertilizer producer in Asia, undertook a major operational digital transformation initiative to enhance asset reliability, energy efficiency, and process transparency across its ammonia, urea, and utilities units. The facility deployed a centralized Aspen InfoPlus.21 (IP.21) historian covering 17 plant units and ingesting over 20,000 process variables at 1-second resolution—forming a unified, real-time plant data backbone. The project addressed key pain points including data silos, delayed troubleshooting, and limited diagnostic capability. Over six years of legacy process data (~5TB) from OSI PI were successfully migrated with full integrity. In parallel, the system was effectively integrated with ERP SAP's Production Planning (PP) and Quality Management (QM) modules, as well as the LIMS platform, allowing for seamless traceability across operations, quality, and process. A key advancement was the deployment of Aspen Online, enabling a real-time digital twin that mirrors process conditions continuously and highlights deviations through AI and first-principles models. Operators and engineers now access live comparisons between actual and expected operating windows, accelerating response times to potential faults and improving decision accuracy. To ensure secure data exchange and compliance with ISA/IEC-62443 cybersecurity standards, Pakistan's first Secure Gateway, Data Diode was deployed, enabling unidirectional data transfer from OT to IT networks while isolating critical infrastructure. The architecture supports over 500 users across engineering, operations, and reliability teams, and is fully scalable for future AI/ML integration. The integrated solution not only enhances operational reliability but also positions the plant for a self-optimizing future.
