# AI Adoption and Corporate Greenwashing : Evidence from China's ESG Industry

**著者**: Zhaolu Tang, Eunmi Tatum Lee
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートガバナンス

## Abstract

This study examines whether AI adoption is associated with lower corporate greenwashing. AI may improve ESG information processing, but it may also facilitate selective disclosure and more sophisticated sustainability narratives. Integrating information asymmetry theory, agency theory, and the impression-management perspective, this study further investigates the governance conditions under which the relationship between AI adoption and corporate greenwashing becomes stronger. Using a strong two-way fixed-effects empirical design based on a comprehensive panel of Chinese non-financial A-share listed firms from 2012 to 2024, the results show a significant negative association between AI adoption and corporate greenwashing. In addition, the findings show that environmental disclosure quality provides weaker moderating evidence, while sustainability committee governance and external scrutiny significantly strengthen the negative association between AI adoption and corporate greenwashing. The findings suggest that AI adoption can reduce corporate greenwashing by improving firms’ ability to collect, process, integrate, and verify ESG-related information, thereby reducing disclosure–performance inconsistency in sustainability communication. These findings highlight the importance of disclosure quality, internal governance, and external monitoring for improving the credibility of AI-supported ESG information and offer implications for the emerging ESG industry.
