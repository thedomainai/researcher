# Policy Design for AI-Enabled Higher Institutions

**著者**: Michael Oyedele Oyenuga
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートガバナンス

## Abstract

The recent and rapid rise of artificial intelligence (AI) in higher education has moved more quickly than many institutions could have hoped for or predicted, changing the way we teach, conduct research, administer functions, and govern institutions. This chapter provides a look at higher institutions through the lens of policy design informed by governance architectures in support of responsible digital transformation in, and with AI. The chapter leverages socio-technical systems theory and international AI governance frameworks (to explore governance mechanisms, institutional control mechanisms (institutionally grounded oversight approaches), policy-design principles and incipient governance tensions. It puts forward a governance architecture combining ethical oversight, data governance, risk management, stakeholder participation and continuous evaluation to enable trustworthy consumption of inclusive sustainable AI with confidence in higher education. Finally, a brief conclusion with suggestions for policymakers and leaders of higher education institutions
