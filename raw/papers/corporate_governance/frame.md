## Abstract

This chapter presents FRAME (Framework for Responsible AI in Monitoring and Evaluation), a comprehensive practical approach for responsible AI use across the evaluation lifecycle. Drawing on socio-technical systems theory, international governance standards, including the NIST AI Risk Management Framework and the European Union AI Act, and synthesizing insights from the case studies documented throughout this volume, we argue that moral responsibility resides with human actors rather than algorithmic systems. The framework provides evaluators with actionable guidance structured around the seven evaluation phases discussed in this volume, addressing critical risks including hallucination, bias amplification, and accountability diffusion. A comprehensive checklist operationalizes five core principles: Accountability Architecture, Stakeholder Engagement, Epistemic Integrity, Transparency, and Proportionality, enabling evaluation teams to systematically assess and document responsible AI integration. The FRAME approach checklist presented in this chapter emerged from practical experience with the cases documented in this volume and ongoing engagement with practitioners in The Development CAFE’s AI for MEL e-learning course, ensuring applicability across diverse organizational contexts and evaluation types.
