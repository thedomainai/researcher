# Why Firms Diverge on ESG: Governance Reinforcement and Divergent Strategic Trajectories

**著者**: Mubanga Lackson Chipimo
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: meta_analysis | **分野**: コーポレートガバナンス

## Abstract

Why do firms exposed to similar institutional environmental, social, and governance (ESG) pressures develop divergent strategic trajectories? Existing research explains why ESG becomes organizationally salient and how firms can develop sustainability-related capabilities, but provides a less developed account of how competing ESG interpretations become sufficiently durable to shape organisational commitments over time. This paper develops a governance-centred process explanation of ESG strategic heterogeneity. The paper develops an integrative conceptual framework drawing on institutional theory, managerial cognition, corporate governance and dynamic capabilities. It conceptualizes governance reinforcement as the recursive stabilization of strategic interpretations through repeated and aligned governance attention, incentive structures, strategic oversight and resource commitments across successive governance cycles. The framework proposes that governance reinforcement increases the persistence of particular ESG interpretations, contributing to divergent organisational trajectories. Reinforced compliance- and legitimacy-oriented interpretations are more likely to remain weakly coupled to organisational learning and capability development, whereas reinforced strategic interpretations supported by sustained organisational commitment are more likely to support organisational learning and capability development. These trajectories are path-dependent but revisable as organisational outcomes feed back into subsequent interpretation and governance cycles. The paper contributes a temporal process explanation of ESG strategic heterogeneity by connecting institutional salience and managerial interpretation with sustained organisational commitment. Rather than introducing new governance mechanisms, it explains how the recurrence and alignment of established mechanisms can make particular ESG interpretations organizationally durable.
