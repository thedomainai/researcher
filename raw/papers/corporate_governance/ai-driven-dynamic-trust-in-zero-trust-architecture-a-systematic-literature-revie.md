# AI-Driven Dynamic Trust in Zero Trust Architecture: A Systematic Literature Review of Machine Learning, Federated Learning and Governance Challenges

**著者**: Melissa Sharon Tubili, Belinda Mutunhu Ndlovu
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: コーポレートガバナンス

## Abstract

Sophisticated cyber threats have exposed the limits of perimeter-based security models, and Zero Trust Architecture (ZTA) has increasingly been adopted as an alternative paradigm. Purpose: This Systematic Literature Review examines the integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) within Zero Trust frameworks, covering dynamic trust scoring, behavioural analytics and federated learning, the latter treated as one strand of AI rather than a separate paradigm. Following PRISMA 2020, 22 studies published between 2022 and 2025 were identified across IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, SpringerLink, MDPI and Nature/Scientific Reports, appraised against a five-criterion quality instrument and synthesised narratively. The studies included are few and heterogeneous, and their findings should be read accordingly. Ensemble and hybrid designs predominate (14 of 22). Twelve studies report quantitative results, of which only eight evaluate on real named datasets; reported accuracies of 95 to 99 per cent come from different datasets, tasks, threat models, and evaluation settings, and are neither directly comparable nor a field-level benchmark. Only three studies directly compare learned adaptive trust against a static rule-based baseline. No study reports live operational deployment. We identify thirteen technique categories, eleven implementation barriers, and seven emerging trends. This paper proposes an integrated eight-component framework for AI-driven dynamic trust management, along with a five-domain research agenda. The framework is conceptual and synthesis-derived and requires empirical validation before operational use.
