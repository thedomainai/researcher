# Stabilize or Destabilize? Token–Based Ownership under Legitimacy Shocks

**著者**: Kayoung Shin, Moksh Matta, Hyeokkoo Eric Kwon
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートガバナンス

## Abstract

Digital platforms often use cash-like incentives to encourage user contributions, but such rewards can make contributor supply fragile and highly sensitive to reward value. To build a more stable contribution base, some platforms grant tokens economically tied to platform value. Yet it remains unclear whether this tokenized ownership stabilizes contributions even under an exogenous legitimacy shock. We leverage two novel datasets from the mainnet and the Q&A platform to examine this question. Using a Difference-in-Differences design, we find that total weekly rewards do not change significantly, but the composition of reward-earning activity shifts. Substantive contributions—including Q&A quantity and quality and referrals—decline, while attendance check-ins increase, despite being lower-value activities that still incur reward costs. Together, these findings advance understanding of how token-based distributed ownership shapes contributor responses under legitimacy shocks, with implications for platform resilience, incentive design, and risk-sensitive regulation.
