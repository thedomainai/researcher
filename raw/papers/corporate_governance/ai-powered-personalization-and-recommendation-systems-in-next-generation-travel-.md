# AI-Powered Personalization and Recommendation Systems in Next-Generation Travel Management

**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

This chapter examines how artificial intelligence reshapes personalization and recommendation systems across next-generation travel management across global travel markets. It integrates recommender-system theory, smart tourism research, customer-experience design, and predictive analytics into an implementation-oriented framework for airlines, hotels, online travel agencies, destination managers, and corporate travel programs. The chapter explains how collaborative, content-based, context-aware, knowledge-based, and generative models can orchestrate offers, itineraries, service recovery, sustainability choices, and duty-of-care interventions. It also evaluates data governance, privacy, bias, explainability, organizational readiness, and interoperability barriers that limit adoption. Six practical tables and four TikZ figures translate the discussion into architecture, metrics, risk, and roadmap tools. The chapter concludes that successful AI personalization depends less on algorithmic novelty than on trusted data, measurable value, ethical design, and cross-functional execution.
