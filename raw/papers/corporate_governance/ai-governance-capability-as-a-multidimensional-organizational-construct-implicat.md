# AI Governance Capability as a Multidimensional Organizational Construct: Implications for Decision-Making Quality and Organizational Resilience

**著者**: Irlenys Josefina Tersek Rodriguez
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートガバナンス

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence (AI) is increasingly embedded in organizational decision-making, risk management, and value creation, particularly in highly regulated sectors. Yet research on AI governance remains distributed across normative principles, digital governance, dynamic capabilities, human–AI decision-making, and organizational resilience. This article integrates these streams by conceptualizing AI Governance Capability (AIGC) as a higher-order, multidimensional organizational capability through which organizations coordinate and continually reconfigure governance structures, governance processes, organizational competencies, strategic alignment, and continuous monitoring and improvement. Using an integrative conceptual synthesis anchored in Dynamic Capabilities Theory, the article specifies the construct domain, distinguishes AIGC from adjacent concepts, identifies the mechanisms associated with its five dimensions, and develops propositions linking AIGC to decision-making quality and organizational resilience. The framework argues that governance value is configurational rather than additive: formal structures or controls are insufficient when they are not complemented by organizational competence, strategic coherence, and adaptive learning. The article contributes a theoretically bounded construct and nomological framework for subsequent operationalization and empirical validation. For practice, it provides organizations—especially those operating under high regulatory exposure and significant AI dependence—with a capability-based lens for moving from principle-level responsible AI commitments toward coordinated, adaptive, and accountable governance in use.
