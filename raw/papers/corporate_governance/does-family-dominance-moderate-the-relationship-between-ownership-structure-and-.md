# Does family dominance moderate the relationship between ownership structure and earnings management? Evidence from Bangladesh

**著者**: Uddin Helal, Hossain Musharof, Hossain Iqbal, Uddin Mazumder Nizam, Chowhan Joy
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: コーポレートガバナンス

## Abstract

The aim of this study is to assess the relationship between company ownership structure and earnings management while also examining the moderating influence of family dominance on this association. The empirical study analyzed data from the annual reports of 118 publicly traded companies across 13 categories of non-financial organizations in Bangladesh from 2011 to 2023. The data were analyzed and hypotheses evaluated by multivariate linear regression and ordinary least squares methods. Empirical findings reveal a positive association between managerial, foreign, and state ownership and earnings management in non-financial organizations in Bangladesh. In contrast, local individual ownership effectively mitigates earnings management practices. The results indicate that the ownership structure index has a positive influence on accrual-based earnings management. Higher levels of institutional and foreign ownership in family firms are associated with a reduced likelihood of earnings management. The impact of ownership structure variables on earnings management is more significant in non-family firms than in family firms. This study contributes comprehensive evidence regarding the moderating role of family dominance on the association between corporate ownership structure and earnings management. Policymakers should be cognizant of managerial, foreign, and state ownership, and prioritize local individual ownership. These strategies could enhance the possibility of concentrating on effective ownership structures in non-financial firms, hence reducing earnings manipulation. The government should prioritize local individual ownership to guarantee transparency in financial reporting.
