# The World of *Thinai* A Multidisciplinary Theoretical Critique

**著者**: Ramar Dr. S.
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人類学

## Abstract

This research paper critically analyzes the concept of "Tinai Ulagam" (Tinai World), demonstrating that it transcends traditional literary categorization to function as a profound transdisciplinary epistemological framework deeply rooted in Tamil thought. Grounded in the Tolkappiyam and Sangam literary traditions, Tinai is reinterpreted not merely as a geographical space or literary setting, but as a relational, lived space (relational space) where landscape, temporality, culture, and human emotion (spatially produced emotion) interact seamlessly. By intersecting ancient Tamil ecological wisdom with contemporary critical discourses—such as ecological humanities, political ecology, environmental anthropology, and feminist literary criticism—the study explores the multi-layered dimensions of the five Tinai systems as holistic socio-economic-ecological modes of livelihood. Furthermore, the paper projects this indigenous knowledge system into the contemporary digital era, proposing modern paradigms like the "Digital Tinai" and examining the epistemological dialogue between Artificial Intelligence (algorithmic knowledge) and traditional ecological knowledge. Ultimately, the paper redefines Tinai as a crucial contemporary resource for climate justice and ecological ethics, mapping a comprehensive conceptual model that bridges ancient heritage with future global worldviews.
