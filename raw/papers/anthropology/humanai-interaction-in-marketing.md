# Human–AI Interaction in Marketing

**著者**: Khan Shariya Hasan Upoma, Sanath Chilakala, Surya Teja Meesala
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

This chapter comprehensively explores human–artificial intelligence (AI) interaction (HAI) in marketing, examining the intricate relationship between AI technologies, human cognition, organizational structures, and ethical considerations. The main goals are to (1) map the present state of AI adoption in marketing; (2) assess how HAI affects stakeholders, including developers, marketers, and consumers; and (3) create strategic frameworks for successful implementation. A mixed-methods approach is used, integrating quantitative data from behavioral analytics, controlled experiments, and large-scale surveys with qualitative insights from focus groups, ethnographic observations, and in-depth interviews. Important conclusions highlight key elements crucial to the success of HAI, including cooperation dynamics, ethical transparency, artificial empathy, and trust calibration. While human–AI collaboration maximizes decision-making in areas such as programmatic advertising and customer support, the study shows that customization based on AI makes customers more interested; however, it also runs the risk of being perceived as intrusive. The results also demonstrate that making things user-friendly and being open about AI might help close trust gaps. This report bridges theoretical models with real-world applications to provide practical frameworks for incorporating AI into marketing tactics ethically and successfully, advancing both industry practice and scholarly discourse.
