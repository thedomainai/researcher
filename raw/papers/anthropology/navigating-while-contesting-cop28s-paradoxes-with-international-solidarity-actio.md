# Navigating while contesting COP28’s paradoxes with international solidarity actions: a youth delegation’s collaborative and feminist auto-ethnography

**著者**: Katherine Robitaille, Laura Wilmot, Laura Fequino, Noémie Lefrançois, Mishka Caldwell-Pichette
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人類学

## Abstract

There are many paradoxes at the Conferences of the Parties (COPs) of the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC). Disillusioned by inadequate political action, many young activists view intergovernmental climate talks as ineffective and increasingly challenge existing institutions and power structures. For us, participating in COP28 meant occupying a space dominated by lobbyists to amplify marginalized voices, and navigating while contesting the many paradoxes that such a presence entails. This article explores how we navigated the paradoxes of COPs through international solidarity actions, drawing on our lived experience as young women activists within the Quebec Association of International Cooperation Organizations (AQOCI) youth delegation at COP28. The analytical framework draws on critical theories of climate justice and organizational paradoxes. The qualitative methodology is inspired by a collaborative and feminist auto-ethnography using our lived experiences at COP 28 as the primary data source, collected through observations and reflective journals between September 2023 and December 2024. The findings illustrate how the delegation engaged in international solidarity actions by navigating while contesting three paradoxes: disparities in the representation of countries, nations and populations; the overrepresentation of fossil fuel lobbyists relative to civil society; and the gaps between actors’ commitments and their actions.
