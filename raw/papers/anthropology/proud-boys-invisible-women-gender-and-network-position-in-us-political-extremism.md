# Proud Boys, Invisible Women? Gender and Network Position in U.S. Political Extremism

**著者**: Daniel Farquharson
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: 人類学

## Abstract

Most people involved in political extremism in the United States are men, but women take part too, and their roles are often overlooked. This study looked at how men and women are connected to other members of far-right and far-left extremist groups. Using public data on more than 1,400 people who were involved in extremism, the study mapped who was connected to who and compared the positions of men and women in these groups. Women in both types of groups were less connected to the rest of their group than men, and this gap was bigger in far-right groups than in far-left. Women also tended to be connected to other women, especially on the far right, though their connections to other women often went through men. These results can help law enforcement and prevention programs make sure women are not overlooked when working to combat extremist behavior.
