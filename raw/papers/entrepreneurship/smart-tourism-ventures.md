## Abstract

This chapter focuses on how artificial intelligence, sustainable technologies, and entrepreneurial innovation are changing the travel and hospitality industry. We investigate the way in which the emerging smart tourism ventures are utilizing AI-driven solutions to generate new business models responding to the challenge of sustainability through a thorough analysis. The study examines effective startup environments, platform innovations proposed through digital technology, and sustainable activities that are transforming the way tourists experience and how the business is undertaken in the industry. Through discussions of the case studies of the most successful smart tourism projects across the world, the chapter will inform on the strategic implementation of the AI technologies in combination with the practice of sustainability, offering a pathway into digital tourism innovation to the would-be entrepreneurs and already existing companies.
