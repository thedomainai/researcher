# AI and Digital Economy: Pathway to Sustainable Development and Viksit Bharat 2047

**著者**: Vijaykumar A. Patil
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: アントレプレナーシップ

## Abstract

Abstract Artificial Intelligence (AI) and the Digital Economy are increasingly shaping India's economic transformation. Their applications in agriculture, manufacturing, services, banking, education, healthcare, governance and entrepreneurship have the potential to improve productivity, reduce transaction costs, expand access to services and strengthen innovation. This study examines the economic role of AI and digitalisation with particular reference to sustainable development and the vision of Viksit Bharat 2047. The study is based on secondary data and follows a descriptive and analytical approach. Official statistics on internet connectivity, digital payments and the IndiaAI Mission are used to provide a quantitative dimension to the analysis. The evidence shows rapid expansion of India's digital ecosystem: internet subscribers reached 1,028.61 million by December 2025, while rural internet subscribers stood at 407.69 million in March 2025. UPI processed 22,716.07 million transactions worth ₹28,92,138.67 crore in June 2026. The IndiaAI Mission, approved with an outlay of ₹10,371.92 crore, has expanded compute capacity beyond its initial 10,000-GPU target. The study concludes that AI and digitalisation can support productivity, financial inclusion, entrepreneurship, sustainable resource use and public-service delivery. However, digital inequality, skill gaps, data privacy, cybersecurity, employment restructuring and environmental costs require policy attention. Inclusive infrastructure, human-capital development, responsible AI and sustainable digital practices are therefore essential for Viksit Bharat 2047.
