# Pelatihan Analisis SWOT dan MoSCoW: Penguatan Mahasiswa pada Edupreneurship di Era Digital

**著者**: Riyadhul Fajri, Iqbal Iqbal, Rudy Juli Saputra, Mukhlis Aminullah, Afriana Afriana
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: アントレプレナーシップ

## Abstract

This community service initiative aims to strengthen and enhance the objective and effective analytical skills required to identify the potential and needs of student-led entrepreneurial ventures. The training is designed to equip young entrepreneurs and aspiring entrepreneurs drawn from Generation Z and the broader student population (including millennials) with the ability to analyze the potential of their existing or planned business ventures and to develop future business strategies. The program targets students who have already begun their entrepreneurial journeys, as well as final-year students who have completed coursework in entrepreneurship and aspire to launch "edupreneurship" ventures. The methodology employs focus group discussions (FGDs), beginning with a refresher on relevant concepts and case studies, followed by collaborative analysis, and concluding with an evaluation. The results indicate that the participants—comprising both novice entrepreneurs and students interested in entrepreneurship have improved their analytical capabilities, as evidenced by their proficiency in applying the SWOT and MoSCoW methods to assess challenges. Ultimately, the training seeks to shift the participants' mindsets toward entrepreneurship, fostering a strategic, innovative, independent, and adaptive approach essential for navigating the evolving business landscape in today’s increasingly digital, globalized era and beyond.
