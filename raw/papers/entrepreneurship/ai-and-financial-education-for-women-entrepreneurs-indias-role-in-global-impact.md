# AI and Financial Education for Women Entrepreneurs: India’s Role in Global Impact

**著者**: Kanika Thakur, Satish K. Mittal, Samar Raqshin
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: アントレプレナーシップ

## Abstract

This paper presents the catalyst impact of artificial intelligence (AI) in effective financial education and entrepreneurial success of women in India and related dynamism worldwide. Despite the growing presence of AI in the education domain, its direct impact on women entrepreneurs' trajectory still remains untouched. Systematic literature review approach is adopted in this paper to collate findings from peer-reviewed research works written from 2000 to 2025 on AI-based education, financial literacy and women entrepreneurship. These findings suggest that women entrepreneurs benefit from AI-based educational tools due to the availability of scalable, customized and contextually appropriate know-how for navigating the financial domain with greater confidence and capability. Perpetuating problems facing such its impact are inequalities in access, sociocultural suitability, and ethical dilemmas, particularly amid constituting social and economic factors common in developing nations. Development of digital financial inclusion and AI training in India would set out exemplary practice for Global South in bridging the divide between financial literacy and entrepreneurship opportunities via harnessing the potential of AI. This paper calls for inclusive gender-sensitive AI tools and proposes policy frameworks that promote equitable access to AI-based educational platforms, AI value proposition in the area of financial education becoming truly enriching for the empowerment of women entrepreneurs to exercise their clout as game changers in global economies.
