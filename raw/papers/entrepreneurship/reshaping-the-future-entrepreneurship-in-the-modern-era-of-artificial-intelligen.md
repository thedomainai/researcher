# Reshaping the Future: Entrepreneurship in the Modern Era of Artificial Intelligence and Digital Transformation

**著者**: Ravee Shekhar
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: アントレプレナーシップ

## Abstract

Abstract : Entrepreneurship has always played an important role in economic growth, technological progress and social development. Today, Artificial Intelligence (AI) and digital transformation have changed the way businesses are started and managed. Technologies such as AI, cloud computing, digital platforms and data analytics have reduced the barriers to starting a business while creating new opportunities for innovation and global expansion. At the same time, entrepreneurs face challenges related to cybersecurity, ethical use of AI, data privacy, digital inequality, and changing regulations. Therefore, modern entrepreneurs must use technology effectively while building customer-focused and sustainable business models. This paper examines how AI and digital transformation are influencing entrepreneurship by creating new opportunities, changing business strategies, and improving innovation and decision-making. It explains how digital technologies help businesses become more flexible, competitive, and efficient. The study also highlights the changing role of entrepreneurial leadership in encouraging innovation, managing uncertainty, and addressing ethical issues related to AI. It concludes that long-term entrepreneurial success depends on combining technological skills with human creativity, strategic planning, responsible innovation, and continuous learning. Entrepreneurs who adapt to technological changes will be better prepared to achieve sustainable economic and social growth.
