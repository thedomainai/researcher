# THE ROLE OF STEM AND ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN ENTREPRENEURSHIP AND SECURITY FOR SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT IN JEMA’A LGA OF SOUTHERN KADUNA

**著者**: JESSE MICHAEL DAUDA, SARKI SANI
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: アントレプレナーシップ

## Abstract

This study explores the role of STEM education and Artificial Intelligence (AI) in promoting entrepreneurship and security for sustainable development in Jema’a Local Government Area (LGA), Southern Kaduna. A descriptive research design was employed to gather data from 150 respondents through structured questionnaires, interviews, focus group discussions, and observational visits. The findings reveal that while 45% of respondents are highly aware of STEM and AI applications, only 20% report full integration, with 51% experiencing partial adoption in educational, entrepreneurial, and security sectors. In security, traditional tools like surveillance systems and data analytics are widely used, whereas advanced technologies such as drones face challenges due to cost and infrastructure limitations. Qualitative insights suggest that AI tools improve operational efficiency, decision-making, and innovation, but challenges such as lack of technical training, limited resources, and resistance to change hinder their broader use. The study concludes that although there is growing awareness of STEM and AI, practical application is still in its early stages. It recommends enhancing capacity-building initiatives, improving government and institutional support, and promoting public-private partnerships to effectively integrate STEM and AI technologies for local development.
