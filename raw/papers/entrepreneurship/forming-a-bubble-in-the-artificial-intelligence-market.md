# FORMING A BUBBLE IN THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE MARKET

**著者**: Олександр Череп, Yuliia Kaliuzhna, Svitlana Markova, Maksym Tarianik
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0

## Abstract

The dynamics of the development of the artificial intelligence market has been studied, highlighting not only financial indicators, but also research and social activity. The dynamics of investments in the AI market has been analyzed. It has been established that in recent years there has been a rapid development of the artificial intelligence market. It is artificial intelligence that is becoming the main means of technological and economic development, in various areas, such as business, finance, medicine. artificial intelligence in these areas entails significant investments, due to the high level of expectations, regarding the future impact on the development of the world economy. However, along with the active development of technology, there is also an increase in fears and discussions about the possible formation of an economic bubble in the artificial intelligence market. It is substantiated that if we characterize the AI market, it is worth noting the growth in the value of technology companies, rapid and large-scale venture investments, and the constant emergence of new startups. All of this bears some resemblance to previous tech booms, as well as the dot-com bubble. The capital expenditures of companies for financing artificial intelligence and the dynamics of financing artificial intelligence infrastructure are estimated. An analysis of general trends in large investments in new technologies and AI is performed. Examples of economic bubbles in the AI market are given. A comparison of previous bubbles and the modern AI market is carried out, common characteristics and differences are highlighted. Problems and risks of the development of the AI market are indicated. In the process of research, methods of comparative analysis, generalization of scientific approaches, as well as research of modern trends in the development of the technology sector were used, analysis of international economic data and reports, as well as research of modern literature on economic and technological bubbles.
