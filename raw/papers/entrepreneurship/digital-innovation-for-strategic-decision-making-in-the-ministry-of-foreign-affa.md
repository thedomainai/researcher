# Digital innovation for strategic decision-making in the Ministry of Foreign Affairs: integrating ERP systems, data analytics, and digital governance

**著者**: Aline Elliut Gómez-González, Cláudia Vechi Torres, Fernando Núñez-Guzmán, María Isabel Camarena-Puentes

## Abstract

This article analyzes the role of digital innovation in strategic decision-making within the Secretaría de Relaciones Exteriores, highlighting the integration of ERP systems, data analytics, and digital governance as key elements for institutional modernization. The objective was to identify how the incorporation of digital technologies contributes to strengthening operational efficiency, interoperability, and data-driven management in complex public organizations. The methodology adopted was qualitative, documentary, and analytical, based on the review of scientific literature, institutional reports, and specialized documents. The results show that the implementation of ERP systems, Business Intelligence tools, and digital engagement strategies promotes process automation, improves decision-making, and strengthens institutional transparency. The study proposes and theoretically supports an integrated digital innovation framework for digital innovation that supports institutional modernization, enhances strategic decision making, and promotes public value creation.
