# Entrepreneurship and Innovation: Drivers of Sustainable Business Growth

**著者**: M.Z.M Zahin
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: アントレプレナーシップ

## Abstract

Entrepreneurship and innovation are widely recognized as engines of economic development and organizational competitiveness. This paper analyses the relationship between the two, drawing on classical theories, contemporary frameworks and case studies from global corporations and emerging economies. It explores how entrepreneurs use innovation to identify opportunities, create value and sustain growth in dynamic markets, integrating Schumpeterian economics, opportunity-recognition theory and sustainability frameworks. The paper concludes that policy support, institutional frameworks and digital transformation are needed to build entrepreneurial ecosystems that drive sustainable business growth.
