# A Study on the Key Drivers of AI Capability and Startup Performance in Qatar

**著者**: Deema Al-Mohanadi
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: アントレプレナーシップ

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence has become increasingly important to startups seeking to improve innovation, efficiency, and competitiveness. However, while prior research has examined AI capability in established organizations, limited attention has been given to how AI capability develops in startups operating under conditions of resource scarcity, uncertainty, and structural fluidity, particularly in emerging entrepreneurial ecosystems such as Qatar. This study addresses that gap by examining the factors that contribute to AI capability in startups in Qatar and by analysing the relationship between AI capability and venture performance. This study is grounded in the Resource-Based Theory and conceptualizes AI capability as a higher-order construct composed of tangible, human, and intangible dimensions. A research model was developed and tested using survey data collected from individuals affiliated with startups operating in Qatar. The data were analysed using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling, including both combined-sample analysis and subgroup analysis comparing AI-enabled and AI-native startups. The findings show that AI capability is positively influenced by AI data modeling, AI stack, agile and lean execution, and risk proclivity, while funding and investment demonstrate a significant negative relationship with AI capability. In contrast, technical AI skills and leadership in AI do not show significant effects in the combined model. The results also indicate that AI capability has a strong positive effect on venture performance. Subgroup analysis further reveals differences between AI-enabled and AI-native startups, suggesting that some capability drivers do not operate uniformly across startup types. The findings offer insights for founders, ecosystem stakeholders, and policymakers seeking to strengthen AI readiness and performance in emerging startup ecosystems.
