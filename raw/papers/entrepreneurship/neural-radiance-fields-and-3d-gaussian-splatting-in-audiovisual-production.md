# Neural radiance fields and 3D Gaussian splatting in audiovisual production

**著者**: Fiorella Luz De La Cruz Espinoza, Norma Beatriz Ríos, Carlos Jesús Canova-Barrios
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 1
**タイプ**: systematic_review | **分野**: アントレプレナーシップ

## Abstract

Generative artificial intelligence is reshaping entrepreneurship, but the empirical evidence on its role in opportunity recognition and venture creation has not been synthesized.A systematic review was conducted following PRISMA methodology, were included empirical studies of generative artificial intelligence in entrepreneurship, with a narrative synthesis organized by opportunity recognition and venture creation.Eleven studies were included.The pattern split the evidence, with studies of opportunity recognition and decisionmaking reporting augmentation and, in one field experiment, a reduction in innovation, while studies of venture creation reported faster formation and stronger competitive positioning, with effects moderated by founder skill and task location.Generative artificial intelligence both enables and complicates the entrepreneurial process, augmenting opportunity recognition and venture creation for capable founders while introducing a risk of homogenization and judgment loss, so its net effect is conditional on the founder rather than uniformly positive.
