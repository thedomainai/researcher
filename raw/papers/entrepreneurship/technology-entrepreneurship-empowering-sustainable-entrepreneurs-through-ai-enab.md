# Technology Entrepreneurship Empowering Sustainable Entrepreneurs Through AI-Enabled Support Ecosystems

**著者**: Partap Singh
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: アントレプレナーシップ

## Abstract

Technology entrepreneurship is key to addressing environmental and social challenges by driving sustainable innovation through digital tools and intelligent systems. This chapter explores how AI-enabled ecosystems empower sustainable entrepreneurs by improving decision-making, financing, and efficiency. Traits such as resilience, adaptability, ethical judgment, and vision are vital for embedding sustainability in business models, yet limited financial and technological access creates barriers. AI can streamline funding, connect entrepreneurs with impact investors, and provide real-time analytics for market adaptation. It also supports mentorship, ESG compliance, and innovation scaling. Still, cautious approaches by traditional financial institutions highlight the need for deeper AI integration in entrepreneurial finance and policy frameworks. This study calls for a strategic shift toward AI-driven ecosystems to strengthen sustainable entrepreneurship with lasting environmental and social impact.
