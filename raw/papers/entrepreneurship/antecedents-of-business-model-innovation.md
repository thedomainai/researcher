# Antecedents of Business Model Innovation

**著者**: Piotr Wójcik, Anna Morgan‐Thomas, Mariola Ciszewska‐Mlinarič
**年**: 2026 | **被引用数**: 0
**タイプ**: primary | **分野**: アントレプレナーシップ

## Abstract

This chapter examines internal and external antecedents that make business model innovation possible among Polish firms pursuing strategic renewal. Based on a survey and case study research, we develop a typology of antecedents that activate business model innovation. The conceptual map distinguishes four types of antecedents based on two dimensions: continuity (incremental or discontinuous phenomena) and locus (internal/external). Based on empirical evidence from a mixed-methods design study, we identify four categories of antecedents: (i) learning orientation, collaboration and openness, (ii) experimentation and cognitive framing, (iii) technological turbulence and market dynamism, and (iv) institutional windows of opportunity. We further demonstrate how these conditions work in combinations to become activation triggers to four different renewal pathways followed by firms.
