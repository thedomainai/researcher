# See what happens when creative legends use AI to make ads for small businesses.

Today we're launching The Small Brief, an initiative bringing together three ad industry icons to champion a local businesses they love. Their mission is to build breakthrough, studio-quality campaigns that elevate incredible businesses while highlighting the storytelling power of AI and inspiring other creatives and small businesses to explore it for themselves.
We gave the creatives unlimited access to [Flow](https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow?gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=23723143563&gbraid=0AAAABDRA0IWZIhU955BDMZqlZNWIQiOrS&gclid=CjwKCAjw-8vPBhBbEiwAoA39WgVpNYUb0769dEYsyrRzuIWQh8FcBILs42nOiujgYDD4RIh_R2jeHRoCU94QAvD_BwE), Google’s AI creative studio, to bring these brand stories to life. We’ll reveal the final campaigns and share a deeper look into each creative's process in June.
