# The new AI-powered Google Finance is expanding to Europe.

This week, the [new, AI-powered Google Finance](https://www.google.com/finance/beta/) is launching [across Europe](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/16490185?hl=en#zippy=:~:text=Google%20Finance%20availability), with full local language support. This reimagined experience offers a suite of powerful capabilities designed to help you better understand the financial world.
AI-powered research: Ask about anything, from individual stocks to broader market trends. You’ll get a comprehensive AI response, with links to learn more. For more complex questions, try [Deep Search](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/new-google-finance-ai-deep-search/), now globally available in Google Finance.
Advanced visualizations: New charting tools let you go beyond basic historical performance. View technical indicators, like moving average envelopes, or tap [key moments](https://x.com/thefox/status/2051700174582653351) on stock charts to learn why the price changed that day.
Real-time intel: A revamped news feed and expanded data for commodities and cryptocurrencies keep you informed as markets move.
Live earnings: Follow corporate earnings calls with live audio, synchronized transcripts and AI-generated insights, including annotated highlights that help you hone in on what matters.
