## 1. Investor Psychology and Security Market Under‐ and Overreactions

**著者**: Kent Daniel, David Hirshleifer, Avanidhar Subrahmanyam | **年**: 1998 | **被引用**: 5702 | **Tier**: 1 | **分野**: entrepreneurship

**Key Insight**: 自己情報の過信と自己帰属バイアスを持つ意思決定エージェントの存在は、システム全体において短期の過小反応（モメンタム）と長期の過剰反応（反転）を必然的に引き起こす。

### Abstract

ABSTRACT We propose a theory of securities market under‐ and overreactions based on two well‐known psychological biases: investor overconfidence about the precision of private information; and biased self‐attribution, which causes asymmetric shifts in investors' confidence as a function of their investment outcomes. We show that overconfidence implies negative long‐lag autocorrelations, excess volatility, and, when managerial actions are correlated with stock mispricing, public‐event‐based return predictability. Biased self‐attribution adds positive short‐lag autocorrelations (“momentum”), short‐run earnings “drift,” but negative correlation between future returns and long‐term past stock market and accounting performance. The theory also offers several untested implications and implications for corporate financial policy.

## 2. Gradient-based learning applied to document recognition

**著者**: Yann LeCun, Léon Bottou, Yoshua Bengio, Patrick Haffner | **年**: 1998 | **被引用**: 57486 | **Tier**: 1 | **分野**: finance_corporate

**Key Insight**: 空間的対称性と局所的接続性という幾何学的制約をネットワーク構造に埋め込むことで、高次元データからの効率的な特徴抽出と汎化が可能になる。

### Abstract

Multilayer neural networks trained with the back-propagation algorithm constitute the best example of a successful gradient based learning technique. Given an appropriate network architecture, gradient-based learning algorithms can be used to synthesize a complex decision surface that can classify high-dimensional patterns, such as handwritten characters, with minimal preprocessing. This paper reviews various methods applied to handwritten character recognition and compares them on a standard handwritten digit recognition task. Convolutional neural networks, which are specifically designed to deal with the variability of 2D shapes, are shown to outperform all other techniques. Real-life document recognition systems are composed of multiple modules including field extraction, segmentation recognition, and language modeling. A new learning paradigm, called graph transformer networks (GTN), allows such multimodule systems to be trained globally using gradient-based methods so as to minimize an overall performance measure. Two systems for online handwriting recognition are described. Experiments demonstrate the advantage of global training, and the flexibility of graph transformer networks. A graph transformer network for reading a bank cheque is also described. It uses convolutional neural network character recognizers combined with global training techniques to provide record accuracy on business and personal cheques. It is deployed commercially and reads several million cheques per day.

## 3. Batched Contextual Reinforcement: A Task-Scaling Law for Efficient Reasoning

**年**: 2026 | **Tier**: 1 | **分野**: tech

**Key Insight**: 共有コンテキスト内で複数タスクを並行処理する際、タスク数の増加に伴い、タスクあたりの消費リソースが単調減少しつつ高い処理精度を維持する「タスクスケーリング則」が存在する。

### Abstract

Large Language Models employing Chain-of-Thought reasoning achieve strong performance but suffer from excessive token consumption that inflates inference costs. Existing efficiency methods such as explicit length penalties, difficulty estimators, or multi-stage curricula either degrade reasoning quality or require complex training pipelines. We introduce Batched Contextual Reinforcement, a minimalist, single-stage training paradigm that unlocks efficient reasoning through a simple structural modification: training the model to solve N problems simultaneously within a shared context window, rewarded purely by per-instance accuracy. This formulation creates an implicit token budget that yields several key findings: (1) We identify a novel task-scaling law: as the number of concurrent problems N increases during inference, per-problem token usage decreases monotonically while accuracy degrades far more gracefully than baselines, establishing N as a controllable throughput dimension. (2) BCR challenges the traditional accuracy-efficiency trade-off by demonstrating a "free lunch" phenomenon at standard single-problem inference. Across both 1.5B and 4B model families, BCR reduces token usage by 15.8% to 62.6% while consistently maintaining or improving accuracy across five major mathematical benchmarks. (3) Qualitative analyses reveal emergent self-regulated efficiency, where models autonomously eliminate redundant metacognitive loops without explicit length supervision. (4) Crucially, we empirically demonstrate that implicit budget constraints successfully circumvent the adversarial gradients and catastrophic optimization collapse inherent to explicit length penalties, offering a highly stable, constraint-based alternative for length control. These results prove BCR practical, showing simple structural incentives unlock latent high-density reasoning in LLMs.

<details>
<summary>Tier 分類理由</summary>

本研究の知見は、具体的なLLMや現在のハードウェア制約を除去しても、「共通のコンテキスト（リソース）内で複数の推論タスク（N）を並列処理する際、タスク数Nの増加に伴いタスクあたりの消費リソースが単調減少し、かつ全体の精度（性能）の劣化は緩やかである」という、情報処理システム一般における『タスクスケーリング則（Task-Scaling Law）』として抽象化できる（テスト1合格）。この「共有リソースの同時利用による効率化と性能のトレードオフ」という制約は、AGI時代においても計算資源や通信帯域の有限性として永久に存続する（テスト2合格）。また、単なる現象記述にとどまらず、並列処理タスク数Nと推論コスト・精度の関係性を定式化し、なぜ効率的な推論（暗黙のトークン予算の生成）が実現するのかという動的メカニズムを明らかしている（テスト3合格）。したがって、不変原理を記述したTier 1に分類される。

## 4. Adaptation in Natural and Artificial Systems

**著者**: John H. Holland | **年**: 1992 | **被引用**: 35555 | **Tier**: 1 | **分野**: complexity_science

**Key Insight**: 非線形な環境における適応は、スキーマの再結合と並行表現による選択（遺伝的アルゴリズム）を通じて、効率的な探索と利用のバランスを最適化するプロセスとして定式化できる。

### Abstract

Genetic algorithms are playing an increasingly important role in studies of complex adaptive systems, ranging from adaptive agents in economic theory to the use of machine learning techniques in the design of complex devices such as aircraft turbines and integrated circuits. Adaptation in Natural and Artificial Systems is the book that initiated this field of study, presenting the theoretical foundations and exploring applications. In its most familiar form, adaptation is a biological process, whereby organisms evolve by rearranging genetic material to survive in environments confronting them. In this now classic work, Holland presents a mathematical model that allows for the nonlinearity of such complex interactions. He demonstrates the model's universality by applying it to economics, physiological psychology, game theory, and artificial intelligence and then outlines the way in which this approach modifies the traditional views of mathematical genetics. Initially applying his concepts to simply defined artificial systems with limited numbers of parameters, Holland goes on to explore their use in the study of a wide range of complex, naturally occuring processes, concentrating on systems having multiple factors that interact in nonlinear ways. Along the way he accounts for major effects of coadaptation and coevolution: the emergence of building blocks, or schemata, that are recombined and passed on to succeeding generations to provide, innovations and improvements. Bradford Books imprint

## 5. The Interpretation of Cultures

**著者**: R. Fenn, C. Geertz | **年**: 1973 | **被引用**: 20907 | **Tier**: 1 | **分野**: anthropology

**Key Insight**: 社会・組織システムは客観的法則ではなく「意味の記号論的ウェブ」として構成されており、その理解と設計には行動の物理的記述ではなく、文脈に埋め込まれた意図の解釈（厚い記述）が必要不可欠である。

<details>
<summary>Tier 分類理由</summary>

本論文（クリフォード・ギアツの『文化の解釈学』）は、記号表現とその解釈のネットワークとしての「文化」を定義し、それを「厚い記述（thick description）」によって読み解く方法論を提示している。
1. 抽象度テスト：人間や特定の文化記述を除去しても、「自己が織り上げた意味の網の目に囚われたシステム（エージェント群）において、その行動の背後にある記号的意味論を文脈依存的に解釈・調停するプロセス」として抽象化可能であり、AI nativeなマルチエージェント社会におけるセマンティック・プロトコルの設計に応用可能であるため合格。
2. 制約不変テスト：AGI時代、あるいは自律型AIエージェントが独自の社会・協調システムを形成する時代においても、情報伝達における「シグナル」と「コンテキスト（文脈・意図）」の乖離、およびそれらを調停・解釈する機能（意味論的レイヤー）は不変の課題として存続するため合格。
3. メカニズムテスト：単なる文化的事象の記述にとどまらず、記号と行動が「なぜ」特定の文脈において特定の意味を持ち、機能するのかという解釈学的メカニズムを説明しているため合格。

</details>
