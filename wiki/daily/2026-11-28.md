## 1. Technology Acceptance Model 3 and a Research Agenda on Interventions

**著者**: Viswanath Venkatesh, Hillol Bala | **年**: 2008 | **被引用**: 7546 | **Tier**: 1 | **分野**: sociology

**Key Insight**: 新しい技術やエージェントの受容は、主体の認知的アンカリング（不安や自己効力感）と、外部からの調整的介入（トレーニングや社会的影響）の相互作用による認知プロセスを通じて決定される。

### Abstract

ABSTRACT Prior research has provided valuable insights into how and why employees make a decision about the adoption and use of information technologies (ITs) in the workplace. From an organizational point of view, however, the more important issue is how managers make informed decisions about interventions that can lead to greater acceptance and effective utilization of IT. There is limited research in the IT implementation literature that deals with the role of interventions to aid such managerial decision making. Particularly, there is a need to understand how various interventions can influence the known determinants of IT adoption and use. To address this gap in the literature, we draw from the vast body of research on the technology acceptance model (TAM), particularly the work on the determinants of perceived usefulness and perceived ease of use, and: (i) develop a comprehensive nomological network (integrated model) of the determinants of individual level (IT) adoption and use; (ii) empirically test the proposed integrated model; and (iii) present a research agenda focused on potential pre‐ and postimplementation interventions that can enhance employees' adoption and use of IT. Our findings and research agenda have important implications for managerial decision making on IT implementation in organizations.

<details>
<summary>Tier 分類理由</summary>

Q学習は、環境の遷移確率（ダイナミクス）が未知の状況において、試行錯誤を通じて最適な行動価値関数（Q値）を再帰的に更新・収束させるモデルフリー型強化学習の基盤アルゴリズムである。1. 抽象度テスト：人間や特定の計算ハードウェア、既存制度に依存せず、任意の「状態・行動・報酬」で定義されるマルコフ決定過程（MDP）システム全体に適用可能であり合格。2. 制約不変テスト：AGI時代においても、未知の環境と相互作用しながら自律的に最適意思決定を学習する「不確実性下での価値反復」の数学的原理は存続するため合格。3. メカニズムテスト：報酬の遅延がある環境で、なぜ局所的な更新ルール（Temporal Difference誤差）の積み重ねが理論的に最適方策への収束を保証するのかという数学的メカニズムを証明しているため合格。したがって、すべてを満たすTier 1となる。

</details>

## 3. EXPANDEDINTELLIGENCE- A Research Programme on Human-AIRelations, Civilisational Change, and the Architecture of Expanded Intelligence and Cognition

**年**: 2026 | **被引用**: 4 | **Tier**: 1 | **分野**: anthropology

**Key Insight**: 知能が個体の物理的境界を超えて拡張・分散する際の、エージェンシーの変容メカニズムと、それを調停するための自己組織化的なシステム設計・統治フレームワークの提示

### Abstract

This document maps a research programme twenty years in the making. It traces the intellectual architecture of Expanded Intelligence — a body of work developed across two published books, a graduation thesis, conference presentations, a live governance methodology, and an operational AI platform — and presents its 124 distinct research contributions as a unified programme of inquiry. The programme addresses a single civilisational question from multiple directions: what becomes of human agency, identity, cognition, and social organisation when intelligence expands beyond the biological body? The answer unfolds across five interconnected domains — philosophy of technology, futures studies, human-AI relations, cognitive diversity and neurodivergence, and institutional governance methodology — producing original frameworks, named concepts, and operational proofs of concept that do not yet exist in the literature in this integrated form. The 124 entries documented here span 72 full papers, 45 concept notes, 1 creative/research object, and 2 historical records. They include frameworks such as the Seven Lenses analytical model, the Homo Expansus anthropological category, the Dyad as a relational theory of human-AI cognition, the Expanded Intelligence / ESI evolutionary lineage, the Round Table Protocol as a governance methodology, the 11-Layer AI Orchestration Framework, and The Pond as a theory of collective ethical intelligence. Together they constitute a research programme in the field of Human-Technology-World relations, with particular emphasis on the AI moment as a civilisational threshold. This overview is submitted as a programme preprint. Individual papers will follow as standalone submissions to their designated venues. All entries are timestamped here as prior art and intellectual record. Keywords: Expanded Intelligence · Homo Expansus · The Dyad · Human-AI relations · Philosophy of technology · Futures studies · Neurodivergence · ESI · The Pond · Round Table Protocol · AI orchestration · Civilisational change · Post-job economy · Cognitive sovereignty

### Abstract

I. Introduction, 65. — II. A model of long-run growth, 66. — III. Possible growth patterns, 68. — IV. Examples, 73. — V. Behavior of interest and wage rates, 78. — VI. Extensions, 85. — VII. Qualifications, 91.

<details>
<summary>Tier 分類理由</summary>

本論文（ソロー・モデル）は、資本蓄積、労働（人口）、および技術進歩という生産要素が長期的な経済成長にどのように寄与するかを定式化した、マクロ経済学・成長理論の金字塔である。1.【抽象度テスト】人間や特定の産業、現在の技術・制度を除去し、「生産主体（エージェント/AI群）」「計算資源・インフラ（資本）」「アルゴリズム・効率性（技術進歩）」へと抽象化しても、投入要素と生産量の関係式、および定常状態（Steady State）の概念は完全に成立する。2.【制約不変テスト】AGI時代においても、計算資源（資本）の蓄積限界、減価償却（インフラの陳腐化や物理的制約）、および生産性のフロンティアを押し上げる「技術進歩（アルゴリズムの自己進化）」という要素間のダイナミクスと制約は不変である。3.【メカニズムテスト】なぜ経済が定常状態に収束するのか、またなぜ持続的な1人当たり成長には「技術進歩（ソロー・残差）」が不可欠なのかという、成長の根本的な因果メカニズムを数学的に説明している。したがって、Tier 1に分類される。

</details>
