# The psychology of suicidal behaviour

**Authors**: Rory C. O’Connor, Matthew K. Nock | **Year**: 2014 | **Cited by**: 1222 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: cognition: supporting; suicide: core

## Abstract

Key recent developments in theoretical, clinical, and empirical psychological science about the emergence of suicidal thoughts and behaviours are described, and the central importance of psychological factors are emphasised.
