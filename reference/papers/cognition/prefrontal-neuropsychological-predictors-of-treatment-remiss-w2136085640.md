# Prefrontal Neuropsychological Predictors of Treatment Remission in Late-Life Depression

**Authors**: Guy G. Potter, Joshua D Kittinger, Henry Ryan Wagner, David C. Steffens, Kousik Krishnan | **Year**: 2004 | **Cited by**: 133 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: cognition: supporting

## Abstract

It is found that perseverative responses during verbal initiation tasks significantly predicted remission status (MADRS <7), and this finding is consistent with previous single-agent treatment studies suggesting a relationship between prefrontal neuropsychological function and treatment response in late-life depression.
