# Resilience to suicidality: The buffering hypothesis

**Authors**: JUDITH MOORE JOHNSON, Alex M. Wood, Patricia Ann Gooding, Peter James Taylor, Nicholas Tarrier | **Year**: 2011 | **Cited by**: 426 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: cognition: supporting

## Abstract

The buffering hypothesis is introduced, a framework for the investigation of resilience to suicidality, and strong support for the existence of psychological moderators is found and a moderating impact of attributional style, perfectionism, agency and hopelessness is indicated.
