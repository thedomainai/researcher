# Cognitive deficits are associated with functional impairment in severely depressed patients

**Authors**: William Vaughn McCall, Aaron G. Dunn | **Year**: 2003 | **Cited by**: 87 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: cognition: core

## Abstract

Antidepressant treatments that preserve or enhance global cognition in addition to relieving core depressive symptoms may lead to the best functional outcomes.
