# Cognitive disturbance in outpatient depressed younger adults: evidence of modest impairment

**Authors**: Merida M. Grant, Michael E. Thase, John A. Sweeney | **Year**: 2001 | **Cited by**: 320 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: cognition: core

## Abstract

Findings, derived from a large sample of unmedicated depressed outpatients, indicate that major depressive disorder in healthy younger ambulatory adults does not cause appreciable impairments in cognitive functioning in the absence of clinical and course-of-illness features.
