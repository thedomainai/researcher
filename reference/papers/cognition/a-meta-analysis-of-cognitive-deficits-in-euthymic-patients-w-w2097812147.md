# A meta-analysis of cognitive deficits in euthymic patients with bipolar disorder

**Authors**: Lucy J. Robinson, Jill Maria Thompson, Peter Gallagher, Utpal Goswami, Allan Hunter Young, I. Nicol Ferrier, P. Brian Moore | **Year**: 2006 | **Cited by**: 962 | **Kind**: meta_analysis | **Relevance**: cognition: core

## Abstract

Euthymic bipolar patients demonstrate relatively marked impairment in aspects of executive function and verbal memory, and it is not yet clear whether these are two discrete areas of impairment or are related to one another.
