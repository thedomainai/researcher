# Distinct Profiles of Neurocognitive Function in Unmedicated Unipolar Depression and Bipolar II Depression

**Authors**: Joana V. Taylor Tavares, Luke Clark, Dara M. Cannon, Kristine Erickson, Wayne Curtis Drevets, Barbara Jacquelyn Sahakian | **Year**: 2007 | **Cited by**: 267 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: cognition: core

## Abstract

Comparing the performance of unmedicated currently depressed MDD and BD groups on a battery of neuropsychological tests that included measures of risk taking and reflection impulsivity indicates differing profiles of cognitive impairment in unmedication depressedMDD versus BDII subjects.
