# Cognitive Mechanisms of Treatment in Depression

**Authors**: Jonathan Paul Roiser, Rebecca M. Elliott, Barbara Jacquelyn Sahakian | **Year**: 2011 | **Cited by**: 556 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: cognition: supporting

## Abstract

A ‘cognitive neuropsychological’ model of depression is proposed, suggesting that negative information processing biases have a central causal role in the development of symptoms of depression, and that treatments exert their beneficial effects by abolishing these biases.
