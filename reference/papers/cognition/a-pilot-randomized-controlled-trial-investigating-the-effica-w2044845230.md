# A pilot randomized controlled trial investigating the efficacy of MEmory Specificity Training in improving symptoms of posttraumatic stress disorder

## Abstract

The MEST group generated significantly more specific memories and had significantly fewer PTSD symptoms following training and at follow-up than the control group.
