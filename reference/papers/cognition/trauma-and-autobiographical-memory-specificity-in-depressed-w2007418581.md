# Trauma and autobiographical memory specificity in depressed inpatients

**Authors**: Dirk Hermans, Kris Van den Broeck, Gerrit Belis, Filip Raes, Guido Pieters, Paul Eelen | **Year**: 2003 | **Cited by**: 111 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: cognition: core

## Abstract

Participants who had been victim of physical abuse were less specific than participants who had not been confronted with such physical adversities and the number of specific responses was not related to depression severity, but was significantly associated with the presence and severity of reported physical abuse.
