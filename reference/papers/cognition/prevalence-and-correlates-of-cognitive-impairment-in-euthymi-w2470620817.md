# Prevalence and correlates of cognitive impairment in euthymic adults with bipolar disorder: A systematic review

## Abstract

The prevalence of cognitive impairment in euthymic adults with bipolar disorder is quantified and sociodemographic, clinical and other factors that are significantly associated with cognitive impairment are described to facilitate an improved understanding of Cognitive impairment burden in BD.
