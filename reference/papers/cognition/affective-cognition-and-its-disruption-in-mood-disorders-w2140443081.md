# Affective Cognition and its Disruption in Mood Disorders

**Authors**: Rebecca M. Elliott, Roland Zahn, J.F.W. Deakin, Ian Muir Anderson | **Year**: 2010 | **Cited by**: 338 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: cognition: supporting

## Abstract

In this review, affective cognition, responses to emotional stimuli occurring in the context of cognitive evaluation, is considered, in particular, emotion categorization, biasing of memory and attention, as well as social/moral emotion.
