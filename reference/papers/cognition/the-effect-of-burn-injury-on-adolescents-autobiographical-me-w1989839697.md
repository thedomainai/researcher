# The effect of burn injury on adolescents autobiographical memory

**Authors**: D. J. Stokes, Barbara Dritschel, Debra A. Bekerian | **Year**: 2003 | **Cited by**: 62 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: cognition: supporting

## Abstract

The Burn group experienced intrusive thoughts which interfered with normal autobiographical functioning, and produced significant correlations between sub-scales of the impact of event scale and selected measures on the autobiographical memory tasks.
