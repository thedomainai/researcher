# Examining emotion regulation in depression: A review and future directions

**Authors**: Jutta Joormann, Colin H. Stanton | **Year**: 2016 | **Cited by**: 755 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: cognition: supporting

## Abstract

This article links cognitive biases and deficits associated with depression to difficulties in the self-regulation of emotion and identifies topics in need of future attention to shed light on the relation between cognition and emotion regulation in this and other psychological disorders.
