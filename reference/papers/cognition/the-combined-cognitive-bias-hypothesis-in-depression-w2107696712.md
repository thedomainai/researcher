# The combined cognitive bias hypothesis in depression

**Authors**: Jonas Everaert, Ernst H. W. Koster, Nazanin Derakshan | **Year**: 2012 | **Cited by**: 327 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: cognition: supporting

## Abstract

This paper attempts to apply the combined cognitive bias hypothesis (Hirsch, Clark, & Mathews, 2006) to depression research and reviews competing theoretical frameworks that have guided research in this area.
