# Teasdale’s differential activation hypothesis: implications for mechanisms of depressive relapse and suicidal behaviour

**Authors**: Mark A. Lau, Zindel V. Segal, Jane M. Williams | **Year**: 2004 | **Cited by**: 234 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: cognition: supporting

## Abstract

This review demonstrates sufficient evidence to consider cognitive reactivity as a potential causal risk factor for depressive relapse/recurrence, and extends the application of this model to the problem of suicidal relapse/Recurrence including a review of preliminary support for this approach.
