# Neurocognitive deficits and disability in major depressive disorder

**Authors**: Judith Jaeger, Stefanie M. Berns, Sarah Uzelac, Sara Davis-Conway | **Year**: 2006 | **Cited by**: 493 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: cognition: core

## Abstract

Data indicate that NC deficits, at least for some MDD sufferers, play an important role in functional recovery, and new treatments may be required to help affected patients accommodate neurocognitively based performance deficits at work, at home and in the community.
