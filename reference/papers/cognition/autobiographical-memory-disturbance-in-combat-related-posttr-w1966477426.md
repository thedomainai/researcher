# Autobiographical memory disturbance in combat-related posttraumatic stress disorder

**Authors**: Richard J. McNally, Natasha B. Lasko, Michael L. Macklin, Roger K. Pitman | **Year**: 1995 | **Cited by**: 403 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: cognition: supporting

## Abstract

Difficulties remembering one's past may underlie difficulties envisioning one's future, as reflected in the PTSD symptom of 'future foreshortening', and difficulties using memory specifically may also hamper efforts to solve personal problems.
