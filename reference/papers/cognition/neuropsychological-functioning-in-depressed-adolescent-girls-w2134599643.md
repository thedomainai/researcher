# Neuropsychological functioning in depressed adolescent girls

**Authors**: Keith Matthews, David Coghill, Sinéad Rhodes | **Year**: 2008 | **Cited by**: 93 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: cognition: supporting

## Abstract

Depressive episodes in adolescent girls are associated with neurocognitive deficits similar to those previously defined in adult populations and these impairments can be detected prior to exposure to psychotropic drugs.
