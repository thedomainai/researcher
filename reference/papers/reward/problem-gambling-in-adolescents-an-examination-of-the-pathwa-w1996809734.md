# Problem Gambling in Adolescents: An Examination of the Pathways Model

**Authors**: Rina Gupta, Lia Nower, Jeffrey Lee Derevensky, Alex P. Blaszczynski, Neda Faregh, Caroline Elizabeth Temcheff | **Year**: 2012 | **Cited by**: 98 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: supporting

## Abstract

Latent class analysis concluded 5 classes, yet still provided preliminary support for three distinct subgroups similar to those proposed by the Pathways Model, adding a depression only subtype, and a subtype of problem gamblers experiencing both internalizing and externalizing disorders.
