# Reward sensitivity and anger in euthymic bipolar disorder

**Authors**: Or A. Duek, Yamima Osher, Robert Haim Belmaker, Yuly Bersudsky, Ora Kofman | **Year**: 2013 | **Cited by**: 46 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: core

## Abstract

Learning for reward and punishment were negatively correlated in the BP group, suggesting that individuals could learn well either from reward or punishment, but not both, and no correlation was found between these forms of learning in the HC group.
