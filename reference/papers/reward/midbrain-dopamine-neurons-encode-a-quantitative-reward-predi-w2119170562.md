# Midbrain Dopamine Neurons Encode a Quantitative Reward Prediction Error Signal

**Authors**: Hannah M. Bayer, Paul William Glimcher | **Year**: 2005 | **Cited by**: 1374 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: core

## Abstract

This work examined the activity of single dopamine neurons during a task in which subjects learned by trial and error when to make an eye movement for a juice reward, and found that these neurons encoded the difference between the current reward and a weighted average of previous rewards, a reward prediction error.
