# Striatal dopamine release codes uncertainty in pathological gambling

**Authors**: Jakob Linnet, Kim Mouridsen, Ericka Peterson, Arne Møller, Doris J. Doudet, ALBERT H. GJEDDE | **Year**: 2012 | **Cited by**: 85 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: core

## Abstract

The data supported an inverse U-shaped relation between striatal dopamine release and IGT performance if the pathological gambling group, but not in the healthy control group, and suggest that dopaminergic sensitivity to uncertainty is pronounced in pathological gambling, butNot among non-gambling healthy controls.
