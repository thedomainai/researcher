# A reformulated cognitive–behavioral model of problem gambling

**Authors**: Louise Sharpe | **Year**: 2002 | **Cited by**: 404 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: core

## Abstract

A review of the major research findings in the area of gambling and a biopsychosocial model that integrates diverse areas of research is provided and it is hoped it will stimulate future research work that investigates not only individual factors and their relationship to gambling, but also the interactions between different variables.
