# Pathological gambling severity is associated with impulsivity in a delay discounting procedure

**Authors**: Sheila M. Alessi, Nancy M. Petry | **Year**: 2003 | **Cited by**: 534 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: core

## Abstract

Results indicate that of the measures tested, gambling severity was the best single predictor of impulsive behavior in a delay discounting task in this sample of pathological gamblers.
