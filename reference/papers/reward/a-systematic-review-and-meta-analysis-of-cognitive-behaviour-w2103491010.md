# A systematic review and meta-analysis of cognitive-behavioural interventions to reduce problem gambling: Hedging our bets?

**Authors**: Patricia Ann Gooding, Nicholas Tarrier | **Year**: 2009 | **Cited by**: 271 | **Kind**: meta_analysis | **Relevance**: reward: core

## Abstract

There was a highly significant effect of CBT in reducing gambling behaviours within the first three months of therapy cessation regardless of the type of gambling behaviour practiced and meta-regression analyses showed that the quality of the studies influenced the effect sizes.
