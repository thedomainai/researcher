# A double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of olanzapine for the treatment of video poker pathological gamblers

**Authors**: TIMOTHY W. FONG, Ari D. Kalechstein, Bo J. Bernhard, Richard J. Rosenthal, Lori Rugle | **Year**: 2008 | **Cited by**: 160 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: supporting

## Abstract

Findings suggest that olanzapine is not an efficacious treatment for video poker pathological gamblers, but may still be an effective treatment for a specific subset of pathologicalgamblers, including those with a co-occurring psychiatric disorder.
