# Lateral habenula as a source of negative reward signals in dopamine neurons

**Authors**: Masayuki Matsumoto, Okihide Hikosaka | **Year**: 2007 | **Cited by**: 1339 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: core

## Abstract

It is shown that the primate lateral habenula, part of the structure called the epithalamus, is a major candidate for a source of negative reward-related signals in dopamine neurons, and the inhibitory input from the lateral ha benula plays an important role in determining the reward- related activity of dopamine neurons.
