# Drug Wanting: Behavioral Sensitization and Relapse to Drug-Seeking Behavior

**Authors**: Jeffery D. Steketee, Peter W. Kalivas | **Year**: 2011 | **Cited by**: 669 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: core

## Abstract

It is argued that sensitization remains a useful model for determining the neural basis of addiction, and an example is provided in which data from sensitization studies led to potential pharmacotherapies that have been tested in animal models of relapse and in human addicts.
