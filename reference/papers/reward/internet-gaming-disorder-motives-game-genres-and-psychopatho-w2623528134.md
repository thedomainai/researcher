# Internet gaming disorder, motives, game genres and psychopathology

**Authors**: Stéphanie Laconi, Sophie Pirès, Henri Chabrol | **Year**: 2017 | **Cited by**: 298 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: core

## Abstract

Assessment of the prevalence of Internet gaming disorder in a sample of French young Internet users revealed that time spent on the Internet, gaming motives and depressive symptoms were significant predictors of InternetGaming disorder scores, with differences according to gender.
