# Dissecting the diversity of midbrain dopamine neurons

**Authors**: Jochen Roeper | **Year**: 2013 | **Cited by**: 395 | **Kind**: review | **Relevance**: reward: supporting

## Abstract

Recent data supporting an unexpected degree of diversity among these midbrain DA neurons in the mammalian brain are highlighted, ranging from their developmental lineages and different synaptic connectivity to their electrophysiological properties and behavioral functions.
