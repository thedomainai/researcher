# Evidence for sugar addiction: Behavioral and neurochemical effects of intermittent, excessive sugar intake

**Authors**: Nicole M. Avena, Pedro V. Rada, Bartley G. Hoebel | **Year**: 2007 | **Cited by**: 1353 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: supporting

## Abstract

The evidence supports the hypothesis that under certain circumstances rats can become sugar dependent and may translate to some human conditions as suggested by the literature on eating disorders and obesity.
