# Sensory stimuli alter discharge rate of dopamine (DA) neurons: evidence for two functional types of DA cells in the substantia nigra

**Authors**: Louis A. Chiodo, Seymour M. Antelman, Anthony R. Caggiula, Charles G. Lineberry | **Year**: 1980 | **Cited by**: 181 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: supporting

## Abstract

It is demonstrated that dopaminergic neurons located in the substantia nigra of the rat respond to sensory stimuli, and that two functionally distinct DA cell types exist within this region.
