# Neural Mechanisms of Incentive Salience in Naturalistic Human Vision

**Authors**: Clayton Hickey, Marius V. Peelen | **Year**: 2015 | **Cited by**: 216 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: supporting

## Abstract

Using multivoxel pattern analysis in visual cortex, it is found that the encoding of Reward-associated targets was enhanced, whereas encoding of reward-associated distractors was suppressed, with the strength of this effect predicted by activity in the dopaminergic midbrain and a connected cortical network.
