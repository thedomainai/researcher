# Internet addiction in adolescents: Prevalence and risk factors

**Authors**: Daria Joanna Kuss, Antonius J. van Rooij, Gillian W. Shorter, Mark Damian Griffiths, D. van de Mheen | **Year**: 2013 | **Cited by**: 506 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: supporting

## Abstract

The use of online gaming and social applications increased the risk for Internet addiction, whereas extraversion and conscientiousness appeared as protective factors in high frequency online gamers and support the inclusion of ‘Internet addiction’ in the DSM-V.
