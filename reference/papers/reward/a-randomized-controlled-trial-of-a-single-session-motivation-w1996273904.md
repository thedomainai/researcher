# A randomized controlled trial of a single session motivational intervention for concerned gamblers

**Authors**: Katherine M. Diskin, David Carson Hodgins | **Year**: 2009 | **Cited by**: 76 | **Kind**: rct | **Relevance**: reward: supporting

## Abstract

This study tested the efficacy of a single face-to-face MI compared with a control interview for reducing gambling behaviours in individuals who expressed concerns about their gambling.
