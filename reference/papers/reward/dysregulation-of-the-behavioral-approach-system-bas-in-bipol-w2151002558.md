# Dysregulation of the behavioral approach system (BAS) in bipolar spectrum disorders: Review of theory and evidence

**Authors**: Snežana Urošević, Lyn Y. Abramson, Eddie Harmon‐Jones, Lauren B. Alloy | **Year**: 2008 | **Cited by**: 225 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: core

## Abstract

This paper reviews studies on life events, cognitive style and other psychosocial and neurobiological factors, and proposes an expansion of the BAS dysregulation model of bipolar spectrum disorders to foster further biopsychosocial investigations of bipolar disorder.
