# Regular gaming behavior and internet gaming disorder in European adolescents: results from a cross-national representative survey of prevalence, predictors, and psychopathological correlates

**Authors**: Kai W. Müller, Mari Janikian, Michael Dreier, Klaus Wölfling, Manfred Elmar Beutel, Chara K. Tzavara, Clive Richardson, Άρτεμις Τσίτσικα | **Year**: 2014 | **Cited by**: 490 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: core

## Abstract

This survey demonstrated that IGD is a frequently occurring phenomenon among European adolescents and is related to psychosocial problems, and the need for youth-specific prevention and treatment programs becomes evident.
