# Roles of “Wanting” and “Liking” in Motivating Behavior: Gambling, Food, and Drug Addictions

**Authors**: Mike J.F. Robinson, Adam M. Fischer, Aarit Ahuja, Ellen Nacha Lesser, Hannah Maniates | **Year**: 2015 | **Cited by**: 247 | **Kind**: book | **Relevance**: reward: core

## Abstract

It is argued that two separate but interconnected subcortical and unconscious processes direct motivation: "wanting" and "liking" and these two psychological and neuronal processes typically work together, but can become dissociated, particularly in cases of addiction.
