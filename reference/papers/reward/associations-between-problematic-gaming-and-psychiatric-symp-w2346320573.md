# Associations between problematic gaming and psychiatric symptoms among adolescents in two samples

**Authors**: Sofia Vadlin, Cecilia Åslund, Charlotta Hellström, Kent W. Nilsson | **Year**: 2016 | **Cited by**: 114 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: core

## Abstract

Problematic gaming was associated with psychiatric symptoms in adolescents; when problematic gaming is considered, the probability of coexisting psychiatric symptoms should also be considered, and vice versa.
