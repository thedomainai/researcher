# Individual differences in the attribution of incentive salience to reward-related cues: Implications for addiction

**Authors**: Shelly B. Flagel, Huda Akil, Terry E. Robinson | **Year**: 2008 | **Cited by**: 580 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: core

## Abstract

Preliminary data are reviewed suggesting that individual differences in the tendency to attribute incentive salience to cues predictive of reward may confer vulnerability or resistance to compulsive behavioral disorders, including addiction.
