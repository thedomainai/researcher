# Clinical predictors of gaming abstinence in help-seeking adult problematic gamers

**Authors**: Daniel Luke King, Cam Adair, JOHN B. DEC. M. SAUNDERS, Paul Delfabbro | **Year**: 2018 | **Cited by**: 36 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: supporting

## Abstract

Adult gamers with gaming disorder seeking help to reduce their gaming may benefit initially from strategies that manage withdrawal and psychoeducation about riskier gaming activities, which could help predict abstinence from or continuation of gaming.
