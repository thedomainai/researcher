# Predictors of impulsivity and reward seeking behavior with dopamine agonists

**Authors**: William G. Ondo, Dejian Lai | **Year**: 2007 | **Cited by**: 196 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: core

## Abstract

Three hundred consecutive patients taking DA either for Parkinson's disease, restless legs syndrome, or both were interviewed about changes in gambling, spending, sexual activity, or other impulsive activities subsequent to DA, and regression models identified risk factors for impulsivity.
