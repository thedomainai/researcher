**Authors**: Halley M. Pontes, Mark Damian Griffiths | **Year**: 2014 | **Cited by**: 1152 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: core

## Abstract

The nine items of the IGDS-SF9 are valid, reliable, and proved to be highly suitable for measuring IGD, which is a new nine-item short-form scale to assess Internet Gaming Disorder.
