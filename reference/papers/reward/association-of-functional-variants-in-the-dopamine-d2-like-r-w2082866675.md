# Association of functional variants in the dopamine D2-like receptors with risk for gambling behaviour in healthy Caucasian subjects

**Authors**: Daniela S. S. Lobo, Renan Pedra de Souza, Ryan P. Tong, David M. Casey, David Carson Hodgins, Garry John Smith, Rob J. Williams, Don Schopflocher, et al. | **Year**: 2010 | **Cited by**: 117 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: supporting

## Abstract

It is found that none of the single polymorphisms investigated on DRD1 and DRD3 were associated with CPGI scores in healthy subjects, and the role of dopamine D2-like receptor in addiction susceptibility is provided.
