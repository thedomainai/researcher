# Structural plasticity associated with exposure to drugs of abuse

**Authors**: Terry E. Robinson, Bryan E Kolb | **Year**: 2004 | **Cited by**: 1123 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: supporting

## Abstract

Evidence is summarized that exposure to amphetamine, cocaine, nicotine or morphine produces persistent changes in the structure of dendrites and dendritic spines on cells in brain regions involved in incentive motivation and reward, and judgment and the inhibitory control of behavior.
