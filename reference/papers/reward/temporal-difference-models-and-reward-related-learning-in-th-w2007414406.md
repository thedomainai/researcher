# Temporal Difference Models and Reward-Related Learning in the Human Brain

**Authors**: John P. O’Doherty, Peter Dayan, Karl John Friston, Hugo Critchley, Raymond J. Dolan | **Year**: 2003 | **Cited by**: 1571 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: core

## Abstract

Regression analyses revealed that responses in ventral striatum and orbitofrontal cortex were significantly correlated with this prediction error signal, suggesting that, during appetitive conditioning, computations described by temporal difference learning are expressed in the human brain.
