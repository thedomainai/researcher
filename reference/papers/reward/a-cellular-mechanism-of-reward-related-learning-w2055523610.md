# A cellular mechanism of reward-related learning

**Authors**: John N. J. Reynolds, Brian I. Hyland, Jeffery R. Wickens | **Year**: 2001 | **Cited by**: 915 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: supporting

## Abstract

It is proposed that stimulation of the substantia nigra when the lever is pressed induces a similar potentiation of cortical inputs to the striatum, positively reinforcing the learning of the behaviour by the rats.
