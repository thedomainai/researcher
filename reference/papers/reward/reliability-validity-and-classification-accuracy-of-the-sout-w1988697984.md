# Reliability, validity, and classification accuracy of the South Oaks Gambling Screen (SOGS)

**Authors**: Randy D. Stinchfield | **Year**: 2002 | **Cited by**: 573 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: supporting

## Abstract

The South Oaks Gambling Screen was found to have satisfactory reliability with coefficient alphas of .69 and .86 in the general population and gambling treatment samples, respectively and to have good to excellent classification accuracy in the gambling treatment sample, but had poorer accuracy inthe general population sample with a 50% false positive rate.
