# Responses of monkey midbrain dopamine neurons during delayed alternation performance

**Authors**: Tomas Ljungberg, Paul Apicella, Wolfram Schultz | **Year**: 1991 | **Cited by**: 131 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: core

## Abstract

Of 88 DA neurons, 65% and 52% showed phasic responses to the trigger light and reward, respectively, suggesting that DA neurons respond to the salient attentional and motivating stimuli guiding task performance.
