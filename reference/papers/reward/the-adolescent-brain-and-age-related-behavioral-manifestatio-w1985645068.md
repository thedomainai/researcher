# The adolescent brain and age-related behavioral manifestations

**Authors**: LINDA PATIA SPEAR | **Year**: 2000 | **Cited by**: 5518 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: supporting

## Abstract

Developmental changes in prefrontal cortex and limbic brain regions of adolescents across a variety of species, alterations that include an apparent shift in the balance between mesocortical and mesolimbic dopamine systems likely contribute to the unique characteristics of adolescence.
