# Exploring Behavioral Activation and Inhibition Sensitivities Among College Students at Risk for Bipolar Spectrum Symptomatology

**Authors**: Björn Meyer, Sheri Lynn Johnson, Charles S. Carver | **Year**: 1999 | **Cited by**: 218 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: supporting

## Abstract

Cross-sectionally the roles in bipolar spectrum symptomatology of two broad motivational systems that are thought to control levels of responsiveness to cues of threat and reward are explored, the Behavioral Inhibition System (BIS) and the Behavioral Activation system (BAS).
