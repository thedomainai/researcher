# The principal features and mechanisms of dopamine modulation in the prefrontal cortex

**Authors**: Jeremy Keith Seamans, Charles R. Yang | **Year**: 2004 | **Cited by**: 1626 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: supporting

## Abstract

Certain principles of DA mechanisms are identified by drawing on published, as well as unpublished data from PFC and other CNS sites to shed light on aspects of DA neuromodulation and address some of the existing controversies.
