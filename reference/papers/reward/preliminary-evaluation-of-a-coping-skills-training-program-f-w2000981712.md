**Authors**: Robert G. Rychtarik, Neil B. McGillicuddy | **Year**: 2006 | **Cited by**: 79 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: reward: supporting

## Abstract

CST shows promise as an effective treatment for individuals distressed as a result of a partner’s gambling problem and appears to mediate a corresponding large significant reduction in depression and anxiety relative to DTC.
