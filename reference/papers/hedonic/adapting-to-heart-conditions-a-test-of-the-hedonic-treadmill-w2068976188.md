# Adapting to heart conditions: a test of the hedonic treadmill

**Authors**: Stephen Wu | **Year**: 2001 | **Cited by**: 50 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: hedonic: core

## Abstract

The results show that those who have had a heart condition in the past are less likely to report worse self-assessed health and emotional health due to the onset of a new condition than those who have not previously had exposure to heart trouble.
