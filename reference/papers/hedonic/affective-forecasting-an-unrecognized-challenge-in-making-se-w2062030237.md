# Affective Forecasting: An Unrecognized Challenge in Making Serious Health Decisions

**Authors**: Jodi Halpern, Robert M. Arnold | **Year**: 2008 | **Cited by**: 190 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: hedonic: supporting

## Abstract

Three specific mechanisms for affective forecasting errors that may influence health decisions are described: focalism, in which people focus more on what will change than onWhat will stay the same; immune neglect; failure to predict adaptation, inWhich people fail to envision shifts in what they value.
