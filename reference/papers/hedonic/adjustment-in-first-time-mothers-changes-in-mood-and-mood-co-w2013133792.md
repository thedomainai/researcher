# Adjustment in first-time mothers: Changes in mood and mood content during the early postpartum months.

**Authors**: Alison S. Fleming, Diane N. Ruble, Gordon L. Flett, Vicki Van Wagner | **Year**: 1990 | **Cited by**: 87 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: hedonic: supporting

## Abstract

This longitudinal study investigated changes in the content of mood and in mood levels of primiparous mothers. Interview responses were collected from a core sample of 32 women during pregnancy and at 1, 3, and 16 months postpartum
