# The thought control questionnaire: A measure of individual differences in the control of unwanted thoughts

**Authors**: Adrian Wells, Mark I. Davies | **Year**: 1994 | **Cited by**: 643 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: avoidance: core

## Abstract

The development and validation of a questionnaire that assesses strategies for controlling unpleasant and unwanted thoughts and significant associations were found between the punishment and worry subscales of the TCQ and various measures of emotional vulnerability and perceptions of impaired control over cognition.
