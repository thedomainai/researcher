# Paradoxical effects of thought suppression: a meta-analysis of controlled studies

**Authors**: Jonathan S. Abramowitz, David F. Tolin, Gordon P. Street | **Year**: 2001 | **Cited by**: 570 | **Kind**: meta_analysis | **Relevance**: avoidance: core

## Abstract

A small to moderate rebound effect of thought suppression that varied in magnitude depending on the nature of the target thought and the method by which thought frequency was measured is indicated.
