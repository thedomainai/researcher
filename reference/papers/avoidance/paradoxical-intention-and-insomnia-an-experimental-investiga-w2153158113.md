# Paradoxical intention and insomnia: an experimental investigation

**Authors**: L. Michael Ascher, Ralph McMillan Turner | **Year**: 1979 | **Cited by**: 99 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: avoidance: supporting

## Abstract

Results indicated that subjects exposed to the paradoxical intention procedure reported significant improvement on several measures of sleep behavior when compared with reports of subjects in either placebo or no-treatment control groups.
