# Effects of suppression and acceptance on emotional responses of individuals with anxiety and mood disorders

**Authors**: Laura Campbell‐Sills, David Harrison Barlow, Timothy A. Brown, Stefan G. Hofmann | **Year**: 2005 | **Cited by**: 854 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: avoidance: core

## Abstract

Although both groups reported similar levels of subjective distress during the film, the acceptance group displayed less negative affect during the post-film recovery period, and the suppression group showed increased heart rate and theaccept group decreased heart rate in response to the film.
