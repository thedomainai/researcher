# Specificity of cognitive emotion regulation strategies: A transdiagnostic examination

**Authors**: Amelia Aldao, Susan Nolen–Hoeksema | **Year**: 2010 | **Cited by**: 859 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: avoidance: supporting

## Abstract

It is suggested that the use of maladaptive strategies might play a more central role in psychopathology than the non-use of adaptive strategies and provide support of a transdiagnostic view of cognitive emotion regulation.
