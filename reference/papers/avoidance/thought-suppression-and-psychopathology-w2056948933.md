# Thought suppression and psychopathology

**Authors**: Christine L. Purdon | **Year**: 1999 | **Cited by**: 408 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: avoidance: core

## Abstract

A review of Wegner's seminal investigations of effects of thought suppression on later thought frequency, including the findings and their relevance for existing models of specific disorders is provided.
