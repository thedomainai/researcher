# Thought suppression failures in combat PTSD: A cognitive load hypothesis

**Authors**: Deane E. Aikins, Douglas Clark Johnson, Jessica L. Borelli, David H. Klemanski, Paul M. Morrissey, Todd L. Benham, Steven Mark Southwick, David F. Tolin | **Year**: 2009 | **Cited by**: 31 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: avoidance: core

## Abstract

Non-specific skin conductance responses indicated that the suppression task was related to similar levels of increased sympathetic activity for both the PTSD and Pre-Deployed groups, whereas the Combat Equivalent group showed no increased activation during thought suppression.
