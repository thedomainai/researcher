# The efficacy of Acceptance and Commitment Therapy: An updated systematic review and meta-analysis

**Authors**: Lars‐Göran Öst | **Year**: 2014 | **Cited by**: 482 | **Kind**: meta_analysis | **Relevance**: avoidance: core

## Abstract

An evidence-base evaluation showed that ACT is not yet well-established for any disorder, but it is probably efficacious for chronic pain and tinnitus, possibly efficaciousFor depression, psychotic symptoms, OCD, mixed anxiety, drug abuse, and stress at work, and experimental for the remaining disorders.
