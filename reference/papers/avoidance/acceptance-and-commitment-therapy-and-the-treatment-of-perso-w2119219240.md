# Acceptance and commitment therapy and the treatment of persons at risk for long-term disability resulting from stress and pain symptoms: A preliminary randomized trial

**Authors**: JoAnne C. Dahl, Kelly G. Wilson, Annika K. Nilsson | **Year**: 2004 | **Cited by**: 455 | **Kind**: rct | **Relevance**: avoidance: core

## Abstract

Improvements in sick leave and medical utilization could not be accounted for by remission of stress and pain in the ACT group as no between-group differences were found for stress or pain symptoms.
