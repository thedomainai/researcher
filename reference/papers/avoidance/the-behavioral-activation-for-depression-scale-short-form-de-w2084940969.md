# The Behavioral Activation for Depression Scale–Short Form: Development and Validation

**Authors**: Rachel C. Manos, Jonathan William Kanter, Wen Luo | **Year**: 2011 | **Cited by**: 291 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: avoidance: core

## Abstract

Four studies were conducted in order to develop and provide initial evaluation of a short form of the Behavioral Activation for Depression Scale that demonstrated good item characteristics as well as acceptable internal consistency reliability, construct validity, and predictive validity.
