# A meta-analysis of emotional reactivity in major depressive disorder

**Authors**: Lauren M. Bylsma, Bethany H. Morris, Jonathan A. Rottenberg | **Year**: 2007 | **Cited by**: 944 | **Kind**: meta_analysis | **Relevance**: avoidance: supporting

## Abstract

In omnibus analyses of 19 laboratory studies comparing theotional reactivity of healthy individuals to that of individuals with MDD, MDD was characterized by reduced emotional reactivity to both positively and negatively valenced stimuli, with the reduction larger for positive stimuli than for negative stimuli.
