# Current theoretical models of generalized anxiety disorder (GAD): Conceptual review and treatment implications

**Authors**: Evelyn Behar, Ilyse Dobrow DiMarco, Eric B. Hekler, Jan Mohlman, Alison M. Staples | **Year**: 2009 | **Cited by**: 514 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: avoidance: core

## Abstract

Five contemporary models of GAD are critiques: the Avoidance Model of Worry and GAD, the Emotion Dysregulation Model, mindfulness/acceptance-based approaches with existing cognitive behavioral models, an integrated model (AMW), and each model's corresponding evidence-based therapeutic interventions are discussed.
