# Acceptance and change in the context of chronic pain

**Authors**: Lance M. McCracken, James W. Carson, Christopher Eccleston, Francis J. Keefe | **Year**: 2004 | **Cited by**: 316 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: avoidance: core

## Abstract

It may be useful to frame the treatment of chronic pain practically, as a problem of acceptance and change, to encourage change efforts to be directed away from struggles that keep the person stuck, toward situations that yield overall better results.
