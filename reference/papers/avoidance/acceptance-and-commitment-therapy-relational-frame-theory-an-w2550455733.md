**Authors**: Steven C. Hayes | **Year**: 2016 | **Cited by**: 808 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: avoidance: core

## Abstract

The third-wave treatments are characterized by openness to older clinical traditions, a focus on second order and contextual change, an emphasis of function over form, and the construction of flexible and effective repertoires, among other features.
