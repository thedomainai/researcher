# An experimental investigation of thought suppression

**Authors**: David M. Clark, Suzanne Ball, David Pape | **Year**: 1991 | **Cited by**: 270 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: avoidance: core

## Abstract

An experiment investigating the hypothesis that trying to suppress a thought will lead to an immediate and/or delayed increase in its occurrence is reported, and results supported the delayed (rebound) hypothesis.
