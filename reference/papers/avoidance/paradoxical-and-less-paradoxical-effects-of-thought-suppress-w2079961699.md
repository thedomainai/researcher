**Authors**: Eric Rassin | **Year**: 2000 | **Cited by**: 163 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: avoidance: core

## Abstract

It is concluded that research concerned with the psychopathological consequences of thought suppression would benefit from development of better taxonomies of intrusive thinking and cognitive avoidance strategies.
