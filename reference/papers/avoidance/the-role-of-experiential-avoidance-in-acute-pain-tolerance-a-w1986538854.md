# The role of experiential avoidance in acute pain tolerance: A laboratory test

**Authors**: Matthew T. Feldner, Hamid M. Hekmat, Michael J. Zvolensky, Kevin E. Vowles, Zachary S. Secrist, Ellen W. Leen‐Feldner | **Year**: 2005 | **Cited by**: 95 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: avoidance: core

## Abstract

Results indicated that individuals reporting higher levels of experiential avoidance had lower pain endurance and tolerance and recovered more slowly from this particular type of aversive event.
