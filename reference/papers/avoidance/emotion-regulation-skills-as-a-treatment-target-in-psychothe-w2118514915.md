# Emotion-regulation skills as a treatment target in psychotherapy

**Authors**: Matthias Berking, Peggilee Wupperman, Alexander Reichardt, Tanja Pejic, A. Louis Dippel, Hansjörg Znoj | **Year**: 2008 | **Cited by**: 546 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: avoidance: supporting

## Abstract

Replacing parts of the standard CBT treatment with the emotion-regulation training enhanced the effects of theCBT treatment on skills application and on other measures of mental health.
