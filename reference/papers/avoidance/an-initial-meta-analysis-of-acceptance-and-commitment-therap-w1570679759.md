# An initial meta-analysis of Acceptance and Commitment Therapy for treating substance use disorders

**Authors**: Eric B. Lee, Woolee An, Michael E. Levin, Michael P. Twohig | **Year**: 2015 | **Cited by**: 272 | **Kind**: meta_analysis | **Relevance**: avoidance: core

## Abstract

A significant small to medium effect size was found favoring ACT relative to active treatment comparisons following treatment, and ACT appears to be a promising intervention for substance use disorders.
