# Emotion-regulation strategies across psychopathology: A meta-analytic review

**Authors**: Amelia Aldao, Susan Nolen–Hoeksema, Susanne Schweizer | **Year**: 2009 | **Cited by**: 6726 | **Kind**: meta_analysis | **Relevance**: avoidance: core

## Abstract

A large effect size is found for rumination, medium to large for avoidance, problem solving, and suppression, and small to medium for reappraisal and acceptance in the relationship between each regulatory strategy and each of the four psychopathology groups.
