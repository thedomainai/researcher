# Avoidance and depression: the construction of the Cognitive–Behavioral Avoidance Scale

**Authors**: Nicole D. Ottenbreit, Keith S. Dobson | **Year**: 2003 | **Cited by**: 461 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: avoidance: core

## Abstract

The findings from this study suggest that avoidance may be an important construct in the psychosocial conceptualization of depression and this integrated measure of avoidance has potential utility for depression researchers.
