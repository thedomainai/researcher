# A preliminary investigation of acceptance and commitment therapy and habit reversal as a treatment for trichotillomania

**Authors**: Michael P. Twohig, Douglas W. Woods | **Year**: 2004 | **Cited by**: 142 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: avoidance: core

## Abstract

Self-monitoring data showed that treatment was successful in decreasing the numbers of hairs pulled to near-0 levels for 4 of the 6 participants, with results being maintained for 3 of the 4 participants at the 3-month follow-up.
