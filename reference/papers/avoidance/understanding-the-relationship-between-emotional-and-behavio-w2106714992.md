# Understanding the relationship between emotional and behavioral dysregulation: Emotional cascades

**Authors**: Edward A. Selby, Michael D. Anestis, Thomas E. Joiner | **Year**: 2008 | **Cited by**: 437 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: avoidance: supporting

## Abstract

This study proposes the role of emotional cascades, an emotional phenomenon that occurs when an individual intensely ruminates on negative affect, thus increasing the magnitude of that negative affect to the point that an individual engages in a dysregulated behavior in order to distract from that rumination.
