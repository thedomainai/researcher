# Acceptance-based treatment for smoking cessation

**Authors**: Elizabeth V. Gifford, Barbara S. Kohlenberg, Steven C. Hayes, David O. Antonuccio, Melissa M. Piasecki, Mandra L. Rasmussen-Hall, Kathleen M. Palm Reed | **Year**: 2004 | **Cited by**: 505 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: avoidance: supporting

## Abstract

There were no differences between conditions at posttreatment; however, participants in the ACT condition had better long-term smoking outcomes at 1-year follow-up, and results were consistent with the functional acceptance-based treatment model.
