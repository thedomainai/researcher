# Solving the puzzle of deliberate self-harm: The experiential avoidance model

**Authors**: Alexander L. Chapman, Kim L. Gratz, Milton Z. Brown | **Year**: 2005 | **Cited by**: 1485 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: avoidance: supporting

## Abstract

The purpose of the present paper is to outline a framework-the Experiential Avoidance Model (EAM) of DSH, which integrates a variety of research on emotions, experiential avoidance, and DSH within a clinically useful framework that sparks novel research directions.
