# Thought suppression produces a rebound effect with analogue post-traumatic intrusions

**Authors**: Mark I. Davies, David M. Clark | **Year**: 1998 | **Cited by**: 158 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: avoidance: core

## Abstract

Attempts to suppress traumatic material may be involved in the development and maintenance of post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) and there was a rebound effect for analogue traumatic intrusions although not for polar bear thoughts.
