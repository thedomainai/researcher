# The effects of acceptance versus control contexts on avoidance of panic-related symptoms

**Authors**: Georg H. Eifert, Michelle Heffner | **Year**: 2003 | **Cited by**: 378 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: avoidance: core

## Abstract

Compared to control context and no-instruction participants, acceptance context participants were less avoidant behaviorally and reported less intense fear and cognitive symptoms and fewer catastrophic thoughts during the CO(2) inhalations.
