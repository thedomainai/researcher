# A systematic review and meta-analysis of psychological interventions to improve mental wellbeing

## Abstract

Mindfulness-based and multi-component positive psychological interventions demonstrated the greatest efficacy in both clinical and non-clinical populations, and Meta-analyses found that singular positivepsychological interventions, cognitive and behavioural therapy-based, acceptance and commitment therapy- based, and reminiscence interventions were impactful.
