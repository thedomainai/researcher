# A comparison of acceptance- and control-based strategies for coping with food cravings: An analog study

**Authors**: Evan M. Forman, Kimberly Lynn Hoffman, Kathleen B. McGrath, James D. Herbert, Lynn L. Brandsma, Michael R. Lowe | **Year**: 2007 | **Cited by**: 298 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: avoidance: core

## Abstract

Results suggested that the effect of the intervention depended on baseline PFS levels, such that acceptance-based strategies were associated with better outcomes (cravings, consumption) among those reporting the highest susceptibility to the presence of food, but greater cravings among those who scored lowest on the PFS.
