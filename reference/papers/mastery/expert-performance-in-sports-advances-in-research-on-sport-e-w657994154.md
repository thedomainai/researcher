# Expert Performance in Sports: Advances in Research on Sport Expertise

**Authors**: K. Anders Ericsson, Janet L. Starkes | **Year**: 2003 | **Cited by**: 352 | **Kind**: book | **Relevance**: mastery: core

## Abstract

Part 1 The magic and the science of sport expertise - introduction to sport expertise research and this volume. Part 2 Where are we and how did we get there?: expert performance in sport - current perspectives and critical issues the development of elite performance and deliberate practice - an update from the perspective of the expert-performance approach. Part 3 Developing and retaining sport expertise: from play to practice - a developmental framework for the acquisition of expertise in team sport a search for deliberate practice - an examination of the practice environments in figure skating and volleyball tactics the neglected attribute of expertise - problem representations and performance skills in tennis expertise in sport judges and referees - circumventing information processing limitations expert athletes - an integrated approach to decision-making perceptual expertise - development in sport retaining expertise - what does it take for older expert athletes to continue to excel?. Part 4 Novel ways of examining the characteristics of expertise and related theories: the development of expertise - the role of coaching, families and cultural contexts memory and expertise - what do experienced athletes remember? expert performance in sport - views from the joint perspectives of ecological psychology and dynamical systems theory. Part 5 The great debate - is a general theory of export performance achievable?: constraints and issues in the development of general theory of expert perceptual motor performance - a critique of the deliberate practice framework how the expert-performance approach differs from traditional approaches to expertise in sports - in search of a shared theoretical framework for studying expert performances.
