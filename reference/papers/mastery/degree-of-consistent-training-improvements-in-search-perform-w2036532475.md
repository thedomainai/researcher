# Degree of consistent training: Improvements in search performance and automatic process development

**Authors**: Walter Schneider, Arthur D. Fisk | **Year**: 1982 | **Cited by**: 198 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: mastery: supporting

## Abstract

Experiments showed that detection performance improvement with practice was a monotonic function of the degree of consistency, decreasing to zero as the target-to-distractor ratio increased from 10:0 to 10:20, as consistency decreased, detection performance asymptoted earlier and at a lower level.
