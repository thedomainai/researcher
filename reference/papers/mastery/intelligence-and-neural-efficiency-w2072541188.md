# Intelligence and neural efficiency

**Authors**: Aljoscha C. Neubauer, Andréas Fink | **Year**: 2009 | **Cited by**: 902 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: mastery: supporting

## Abstract

It is concluded that neural efficiency might arise when individuals are confronted with tasks of (subjectively) low to moderate task difficulty and it is mainly observable for frontal brain areas.
