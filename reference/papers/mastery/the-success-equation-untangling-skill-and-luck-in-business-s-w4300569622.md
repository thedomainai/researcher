# The Success Equation: Untangling Skill and Luck in Business, Sports, and Investing

**Authors**: Michael J. Mauboussin | **Year**: 2013 | **Cited by**: 68 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: mastery: core

## Abstract

Demonstrating investment skill by investment managers requires persistent and predictable performance. Expectations investing is a process for identifying attractively priced stocks by first estimating the market’s expectations of key value drivers and then determining the likelihood that the company can or cannot meet those expectations.
