# The Routledge International Companion to Gifted Education

**Authors**: (unknown) | **Year**: 2013 | **Cited by**: 123 | **Kind**: book | **Relevance**: mastery: supporting

## Abstract

The Routledge International Companion to Gifted Education is a ground-breaking collection of fully-referenced chapters written by many of the most highly-respected authorities on the subject from around the world. These fifty contributors include distinguished scholars who have produced many of the most significant advances to the field over the past few decades, like Joseph Renzulli and Robert Sternberg, alongside authorities who ask questions about the very concepts and terminology embodied in the field
