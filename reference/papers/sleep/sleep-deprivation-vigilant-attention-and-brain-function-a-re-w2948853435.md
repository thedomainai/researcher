# Sleep deprivation, vigilant attention, and brain function: a review

**Authors**: Amanda N. Hudson, Hans P. A. Van Dongen, Kimberly A. Honn | **Year**: 2019 | **Cited by**: 384 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: core

## Abstract

A bottom-up, neuronal pathway-dependent mechanism involving use-dependent, local sleep may be the main driver of response variability and explain the dissociation between cognitive processes with regard to trait vulnerability to sleep loss.
