# Melatonin add-on in manic patients with treatment resistant insomnia

**Authors**: Giuseppe Bersani, Alessandra Garavini | **Year**: 2000 | **Cited by**: 85 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: supporting

## Abstract

The results of this pilot clinical study suggest that MLT add-on can be useful in antimanic therapy to treat resistant circadian sleep alterations as well as consequently exert a global therapeutic action on the manic state.
