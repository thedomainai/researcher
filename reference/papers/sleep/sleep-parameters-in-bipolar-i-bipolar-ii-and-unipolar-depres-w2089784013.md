## Abstract

Compared to both bipolar I and unipolar disorders, bipolar 11 disorder was associated more often with alcoholism, antisocial personality disorder, premenstrual syndrome in the index patient, as well as with phobic and obsessivecompulsive disorders in first-degree relatives.
