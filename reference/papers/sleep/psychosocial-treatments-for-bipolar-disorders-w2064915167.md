# Psychosocial Treatments for Bipolar Disorders

**Authors**: Jan L. Scott, Francesc Colom | **Year**: 2005 | **Cited by**: 66 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: supporting

## Abstract

Given the reduction in relapse rates and hospitalizations associated with the use of psychologic therapy as an adjunct to medication, it is likely that these approaches will prove to be clinically and cost effective and provide a significant improvement in the quality of life of individuals with BP.
