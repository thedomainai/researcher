# Significant association of the arylalkylamine N-acetyltransferase (AA-NAT) gene with delayed sleep phase syndrome

**Authors**: Hirohiko Hohjoh, Miwa Takasu, Kaori Shishikura, Yasurou Takahashi, Yutaka Honda, Katsushi Tokunaga | **Year**: 2003 | **Cited by**: 114 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: supporting

## Abstract

The data suggest that AA-NAT could be a susceptibility gene for DSPS, and there is a significant difference in allele positivity at the single nucleotide polymorphism involved in an amino acid substitution from alanine to threonine at position 129 between patients with DSPS and healthy controls.
