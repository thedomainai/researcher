# Delayed sleep phase syndrome response to melatonin

**Authors**: M. Dahlitz, Blanco Álvarez, J. David Parkes, J. English, Joséphine Arendt, Jean Vignau | **Year**: 1991 | **Cited by**: 429 | **Kind**: rct | **Relevance**: sleep: core

## Abstract

Melatonin may act as a phase-setter for sleep-wake cycles in subjects with a delayed sleep phase syndrome and is investigated by means of a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial.
