# A practical approach to circadian rhythm sleep disorders

**Authors**: Bjørn Bjorvatn, Ståle Pallesen | **Year**: 2008 | **Cited by**: 236 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: core

## Abstract

This review will focus on how to estimate circadian phase based on a careful patient history and recommend appropriate timing of bright light and/or melatonin in the different circadian rhythm sleep disorders.
