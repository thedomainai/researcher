# Frontal lobe function, sleep loss and fragmented sleep

**Authors**: Kelly Jones, Yvonne D. Harrison | **Year**: 2001 | **Cited by**: 391 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: core

## Abstract

The overall picture is one of a disparate range of impairment following sleep loss and sleep fragmentation, with executive impairment appears to be more closely related to hypoxaemic events than daytime sleepiness.
