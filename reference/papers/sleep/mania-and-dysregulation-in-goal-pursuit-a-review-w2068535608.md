# Mania and dysregulation in goal pursuit: a review

**Authors**: Sheri Lynn Johnson | **Year**: 2004 | **Cited by**: 387 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: supporting; reward: core

## Abstract

Evidence for deficits in goal regulation in bipolar disorder is reviewed, finding that people with a history of mania demonstrate intense mood reactivity, particularly in response to success and reward.
