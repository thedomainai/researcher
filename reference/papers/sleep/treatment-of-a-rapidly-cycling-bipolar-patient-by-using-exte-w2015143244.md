# Treatment of a Rapidly Cycling Bipolar Patient by Using Extended Bed Rest and Darkness to Stabilize the Timing and Duration of Sleep

**Authors**: Thomas A. Wehr, Erick H. Turner, Jeffrey M. Shimada, Catherine H. Lowe, Charles N. Barker, Ellen Leibenluft | **Year**: 1998 | **Cited by**: 165 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: core

## Abstract

Fostering sleep and stabilizing its timing by scheduling regular nightly periods of enforced bed rest in the dark may help to prevent mania and rapid cycling in bipolar patients.
