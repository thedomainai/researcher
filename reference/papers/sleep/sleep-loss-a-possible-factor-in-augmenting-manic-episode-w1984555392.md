# Sleep loss, a possible factor in augmenting manic episode

**Authors**: Barbara Barbini, Sara Bertelli, Cristina Colombo, Enrico Smeraldi | **Year**: 1996 | **Cited by**: 150 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: core

## Abstract

The correlation between night-time sleep duration and the intensity of manic symptomatology rated consecutively for 3 days with the Young Rating Scale for Mania and the Nurses' Observation Scale for Inpatient Evaluation (NOSIE) was found.
