# Use of Melatonin in the Treatment of Phase Shift and Sleep Disorders

**Authors**: Debra Jean Skene, Steven W. Lockley, Joséphine Arendt | **Year**: 1999 | **Cited by**: 60 | **Kind**: book | **Relevance**: sleep: core

## Abstract

Both in field and simulated studies of jet lag and shift work suitably timed melatonin improved sleep and, in some cases, hastened readaptation of the circadian rhythms following the phase shift.
