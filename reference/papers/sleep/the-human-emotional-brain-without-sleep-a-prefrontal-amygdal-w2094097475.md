# The human emotional brain without sleep — a prefrontal amygdala disconnect

**Authors**: Seung-Schik Yoo, Ninad Gujar, Peter T. Hu, Ferenc Andras Jolesz, Matthew P. Walker | **Year**: 2007 | **Cited by**: 1321 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: core

## Abstract

Functional magnetic resonance image (fMRI) scans have obtained evidence that a lack of sleep inappropriately modulates the human emotional brain response to negative aversive stimuli.
