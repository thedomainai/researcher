# Sleep disturbance and psychiatric disorders: A longitudinal epidemiological study of young Adults

**Authors**: Naomi Breslau, Thomas Roth, Leon D. Rosenthal, Patricia M. Andreski | **Year**: 1996 | **Cited by**: 1850 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: core

## Abstract

Prior insomnia remained a significant predictor of subsequent major depression when history of other prior depressive symptoms was controlled for, and complaints of 2 weeks or more of insomnia nearly every night might be a useful marker of subsequent onset of major depression.
