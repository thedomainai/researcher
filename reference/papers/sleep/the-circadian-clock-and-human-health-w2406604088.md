# The Circadian Clock and Human Health

**Authors**: Till Roenneberg, Martha W. Merrow | **Year**: 2016 | **Cited by**: 1029 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: supporting

## Abstract

This work attempts to provide a systematic approach to understanding the connection between the circadian clock and health and introduces a taxonomy of (mis)alignments to expose how little the authors know about entrainment within any organism and which 'eigen-zeitgeber' signals are used for entrainments by the different cells and tissues.
