# Primary insomnia: a risk factor to develop depression?

**Authors**: Dieter Wilhelm Riemann | **Year**: 2002 | **Cited by**: 647 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: core

## Abstract

It would be worthwhile to investigate if early adequate treatment is able to prevent psychiatric sequelae of primary insomnia, as insomniac symptoms alone seem to be of predictive value for the development of depression in the succeeding years.
