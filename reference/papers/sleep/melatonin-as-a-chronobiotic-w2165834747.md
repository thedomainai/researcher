# Melatonin as a chronobiotic

**Authors**: Joséphine Arendt, Debra Jean Skene | **Year**: 2004 | **Cited by**: 630 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: core

## Abstract

Successful use of melatonin's chronobiotic properties has been reported in other sleep disorders associated with abnormal timing of the circadian system: jetlag, shiftwork, delayed sleep phase syndrome, some sleep problems of the elderly.
