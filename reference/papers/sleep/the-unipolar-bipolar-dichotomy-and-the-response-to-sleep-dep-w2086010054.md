# The unipolar–bipolar dichotomy and the response to sleep deprivation

**Authors**: Barbara Barbini, Cristina Colombo, Francesco Benedetti, Euridice Campori, Laura Bellodi, Enrico Smeraldi | **Year**: 1998 | **Cited by**: 117 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: supporting

## Abstract

TSD caused better clinical effects in bipolar and single-episode patients; in particular, unipolar patients lacked effects in perceived mood after the first TSD and showed worse Hamilton ratings in respect to the other groups after the three TSD treatments.
