# Human Responses to the Geophysical Daily, Annual and Lunar Cycles

**Authors**: F. Foster, Till Roenneberg | **Year**: 2008 | **Cited by**: 413 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: supporting

## Abstract

Despite human isolation from seasonal changes in temperature, food and photoperiod in the industrialised nations, the seasons still appear to have a small, but significant, impact upon when individuals are born and many aspects of health.
