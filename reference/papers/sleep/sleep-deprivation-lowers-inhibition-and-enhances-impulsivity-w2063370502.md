# Sleep deprivation lowers inhibition and enhances impulsivity to negative stimuli

**Authors**: Clare Anderson, Charlotte R. Platten | **Year**: 2010 | **Cited by**: 332 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: core

## Abstract

It is reported how one night of sleep loss led to increased impulsivity to negative stimuli, such that sleep deprived individuals had an increased failure to inhibit a response and faster incorrect responses.
