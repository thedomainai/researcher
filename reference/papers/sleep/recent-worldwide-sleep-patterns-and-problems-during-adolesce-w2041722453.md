# Recent worldwide sleep patterns and problems during adolescence: A review and meta-analysis of age, region, and sleep

**Authors**: Michael Gradisar, Greg Gardner, Hayley Dohnt | **Year**: 2011 | **Cited by**: 1216 | **Kind**: meta_analysis | **Relevance**: sleep: supporting

## Abstract

Findings from a meta-analysis of 41 surveys of worldwide adolescent sleep patterns and problems published in the last decade suggest a worldwide delayed sleep-wake behavior pattern exists consistent with symptoms of Delayed Sleep Phase Disorder.
