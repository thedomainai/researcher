# Relationship between sleep and mood in patients with rapid-cycling bipolar disorder

**Authors**: Ellen Leibenluft, Paul Albert, Norman E. Rosenthal, Thomas A. Wehr | **Year**: 1996 | **Cited by**: 181 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: core

## Abstract

Of the three sleep parameters, decreased sleep duration was the best predictor of mania or hypomania the next day, followed by wake onset time, and the association between sleep duration and subsequent mood was less consistent for depression than for mania/hypomania.
