# Sleep and circadian rhythm disruption in psychiatric and neurodegenerative disease

**Authors**: Katharina Wulff, Silvia Gatti, Joseph G. Wettstein, F. Foster | **Year**: 2010 | **Cited by**: 1085 | **Kind**: review | **Relevance**: sleep: core

## Abstract

It is proposed that brain disorders and abnormal sleep have a common mechanistic origin and that many co-morbid pathologies that are found in brain disease arise from a destabilization of sleep mechanisms.
