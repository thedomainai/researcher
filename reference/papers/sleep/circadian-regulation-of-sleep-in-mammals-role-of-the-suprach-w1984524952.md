# Circadian regulation of sleep in mammals: Role of the suprachiasmatic nucleus

**Authors**: Ralph E. Mistlberger | **Year**: 2005 | **Cited by**: 322 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: supporting

## Abstract

The behavioral and neurobiological evidence suggests that in rodents and humans, the circadian clock actively promotes both wake and sleep, at different phases of the circadian cycle.
