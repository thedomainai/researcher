# Delayed sleep phase syndrome in adolescents

**Authors**: Michael J. Thorpy, Elisa Korman, Arthur J. Spielman, Paul B. Glovinsky | **Year**: 1988 | **Cited by**: 215 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: core

## Abstract

The delayed sleep phase syndrome is characterized by difficulty in falling asleep at a socially acceptable time of night and an inability to be easily aroused in the morning and may contribute to the behavioral and educational difficulties seen in patients.
