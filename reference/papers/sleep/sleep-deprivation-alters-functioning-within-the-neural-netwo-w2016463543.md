# Sleep deprivation alters functioning within the neural network underlying the covert orienting of attention

**Authors**: Bryce A. Mander, Kathryn Jean Reid, Vijay K. Davuluri, Dana Melanie Small, Todd B. Parrish, M-Marsel Mesulam, Phyllis C. Zee, Darren R. Gitelman | **Year**: 2008 | **Cited by**: 60 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: core

## Abstract

Seven healthy, young adults underwent functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI) following normal rest and 34-36 h of SD while performing a task in which attention was shifted in response to peripheral targets preceded by spatially informative, misleading, or uninformative cues.
