# Lack of sleep affects the evaluation of emotional stimuli

**Authors**: Daniela Tempesta, Alessandro Couyoumdjian, Giuseppe Curcio, Fabio Moroni, Cristina Marzano, De Gennaro Luigi, Michèle Ferrara | **Year**: 2010 | **Cited by**: 220 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: core

## Abstract

The results indicate that sleep is involved in regulating emotional evaluation and the emotional labeling of neutral stimuli biased toward negative responses was not mediated by the increase of negative mood.
