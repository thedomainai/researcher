# Effects of sleep deprivation, lunch, and personality on performance, mood, and cardiovascular function

**Authors**: Andrew Paul Smith, Andrea Maben | **Year**: 1993 | **Cited by**: 48 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: supporting

## Abstract

Individual differences in the effects of sleep deprivation were apparent, with trait anxiety being related to the changes in subjective alertness produced by sleep deprivation, and sleep-deprived extraverts showing greater performance impairments than the sleep- Deprived introverts.
