# Poor sleep as a potential causal factor in aggression and violence

**Authors**: Jeanine Kamphuis, Peter Meerlo, Jaap M. Koolhaas, Marike Lancel | **Year**: 2012 | **Cited by**: 411 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: core

## Abstract

An overview of existing literature on the relation between poor sleep and aggression, irritability, and hostility is given and individual variation within these neurobiological systems may be responsible for amplified aggressive responses induced by sleep loss in certain individuals.
