# Frequency of delayed sleep phase syndrome among hospitalized adolescent psychiatric patients

**Authors**: Yaron Dagan, Dan J. Stein, M. Steinbock, Iftah Yovel, Dana Hallis | **Year**: 1998 | **Cited by**: 65 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: sleep: core

## Abstract

It is suggested that there may be an interrelationship between DSPS and personality disorders, and may lend some support to the hypothesis that inborn peculiarities in the sleep-wake rhythm lead to the social and functional difficulties characteristic of personality disorders.
