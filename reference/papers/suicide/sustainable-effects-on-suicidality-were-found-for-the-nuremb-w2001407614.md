# Sustainable effects on suicidality were found for the Nuremberg alliance against depression

**Authors**: Ulrich Hegerl, Roland Mergl, Inga Havers, Armin Schmidtke, Hartmut Lehfeld, Günter Niklewski, David Althaus | **Year**: 2009 | **Cited by**: 99 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: suicide: supporting

## Abstract

1 year after the end of the main intervention, preventive effects on suicidality of the NAD remain at least stable and the four-level intervention concept appears to be cost-effective and is presently implemented in many European regions.
