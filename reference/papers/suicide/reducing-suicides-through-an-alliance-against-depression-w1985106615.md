# Reducing suicides through an alliance against depression?

**Authors**: Bettina Hübner‐Liebermann, Tanja Neuner, Ulrich Hegerl, Göran Hajak, Hermann Spießl | **Year**: 2010 | **Cited by**: 40 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: suicide: supporting

## Abstract

An intensive community-based campaign could be effective in lowering suicide rates and the combination of continuing medical educations for general practitioners focusing on 'male depression' and low-threshold campaigns for the general public seems to reach male depressive patients.
