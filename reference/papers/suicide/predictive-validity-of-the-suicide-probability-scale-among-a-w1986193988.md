# Predictive Validity of the Suicide Probability Scale among Adolescents in Group Home Treatment

**Authors**: Robert E. Larzelere, Gail L. Smith, LISA M. BATENHORST, DOUGLAS B. KELLY | **Year**: 1996 | **Cited by**: 65 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: suicide: supporting

## Abstract

This is the first known evidence of predictive validity of any measure of suicide risk in adolescents, and it can prevent some suicide attempts when combined with effective suicide precautions.
