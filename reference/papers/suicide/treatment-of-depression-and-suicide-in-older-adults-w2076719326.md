# Treatment of Depression and Suicide in Older Adults

**Authors**: Sunil Singh Bhar, Gregory K. Brown | **Year**: 2011 | **Cited by**: 63 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: suicide: supporting

## Abstract

A 12-session CBT protocol for reducing depression, suicide ideation, and other risk factors of late-life suicide and other behavioral and cognitive strategies that are commonly associated with CBT interventions for depression are described.
