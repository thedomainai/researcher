# Family Intervention for Adolescents With Suicidal Behavior: A Randomized Controlled Trial and Mediation Analysis

**Authors**: Jane Pineda, Mark R. Dadds | **Year**: 2013 | **Cited by**: 171 | **Kind**: rct | **Relevance**: suicide: supporting

## Abstract

Preliminary evidence for the use of family-focused treatments for adolescent suicidal behavior in outpatient settings is provided and changes in adolescent's suicidality were largely mediated by changes in family functioning.
