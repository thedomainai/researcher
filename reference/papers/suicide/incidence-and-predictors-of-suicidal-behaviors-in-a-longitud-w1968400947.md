# Incidence and Predictors of Suicidal Behaviors in a Longitudinal Sample of Young Adolescents

**Authors**: Robert E. McKeown, Carol Z. Garrison, Steven P. Cuffe, Jennifer L. Waller, Kirby L. Jackson, Cheryl L. Addy | **Year**: 1998 | **Cited by**: 213 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: suicide: supporting

## Abstract

While there are a number of predictors of suicidal behaviors, the false-positive rate is high and focusing on proximal risk factors, particularly stressors in adolescent development, may overlook the fundamental role of underlying mental disorder and familial factors.
