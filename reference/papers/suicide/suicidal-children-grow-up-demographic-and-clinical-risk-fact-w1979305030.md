# Suicidal Children Grow Up: Demographic and Clinical Risk Factors for Adolescent Suicide Attempts

**Authors**: Cynthia R. Pfeffer, Gerald L. Klerman, Stephen W. Hurt, MARTIN L. LESSER, JOAN R. PESKIN, CAROL A. SIEFKER | **Year**: 1991 | **Cited by**: 179 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: suicide: supporting

## Abstract

Adolescents who attempted suicide in the follow-up period were seven times more likely to have a mood disorder during the following period than those who did not attempt suicide.
