# Safety Planning Intervention: A Brief Intervention to Mitigate Suicide Risk

**Authors**: Bárbara Stanley, Gregory K. Brown | **Year**: 2011 | **Cited by**: 1280 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: suicide: core

## Abstract

An innovative and brief intervention, the Safety Planning Intervention (SPI), is described, which can be administered as a stand-alone intervention and consists of a written, prioritized list of coping strategies and sources of support that patients can use to alleviate a suicidal crisis.
