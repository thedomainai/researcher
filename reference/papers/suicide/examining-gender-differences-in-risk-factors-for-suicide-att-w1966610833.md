# Examining Gender Differences in Risk Factors for Suicide Attempts Made 1 and 7 Years Later in a Nationally Representative Sample

**Authors**: Martie P. Thompson, Laney S. Light | **Year**: 2010 | **Cited by**: 76 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: suicide: supporting

## Abstract

These results indicate similar risk factors for nonfatal suicide attempts among males and females, however, younger age and somatic symptoms were reported to berisk factors for females but not for males, suggesting the need for targeted interventions with young females with somatic complaints.
