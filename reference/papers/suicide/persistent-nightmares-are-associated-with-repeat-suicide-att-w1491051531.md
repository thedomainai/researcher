# Persistent nightmares are associated with repeat suicide attempt

**Authors**: Nils Sjöström, Jerker E. Hetta, Margda Wærn | **Year**: 2009 | **Cited by**: 175 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: suicide: supporting

## Abstract

It is suggested that nightmares might constitute a marker for increased risk of suicidal behavior, after adjusting for sex, axis-I DSM-IV diagnoses, and self-reported depression and anxiety symptom intensity.
