# Practice Parameter for the Assessment and Treatment of Children and Adolescents With Suicidal Behavior

**Authors**: David Shaffer, Cynthia R. Pfeffer | **Year**: 2001 | **Cited by**: 466 | **Kind**: guideline | **Relevance**: suicide: supporting

## Abstract

These guidelines review what is known about the epidemiology, causes, management, and prevention of suicide and attempted suicide in young people and present suggestions on how the clinician may interface with the community.
