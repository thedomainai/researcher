# Is there evidence for negative effects of antidepressants on suicidality in depressive patients?

**Authors**: Hans‐Jürgen Möller | **Year**: 2006 | **Cited by**: 67 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: suicide: supporting

## Abstract

There seems to be only a small amount of evidence from different research approaches that antidepressants, not only serotonin reuptake inhibitors (SSRIs), might induce, aggravate or increase the risk of suicidal ideation and suicide attempts.
