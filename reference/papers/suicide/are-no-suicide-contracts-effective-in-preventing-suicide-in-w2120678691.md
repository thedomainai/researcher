# Are No-Suicide Contracts Effective in Preventing Suicide in Suicidal Patients Seen by Primary Care Physicians?

**Authors**: Kary Kelly | **Year**: 2000 | **Cited by**: 40 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: suicide: core

## Abstract

A search of the literature was performed with MEDLINE, HlthStar, Curr Cont, Cinahl, and PsychInfo databases using the keywords “suicide,” “no-suicide contracts,’ “noharm contracts, ” and “Suicide prevention/control” with limits to primary care, psychology, and the English language, and thirty-two articles were identified.
