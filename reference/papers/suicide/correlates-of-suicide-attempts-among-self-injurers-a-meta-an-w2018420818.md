# Correlates of suicide attempts among self-injurers: A meta-analysis

**Authors**: Sarah E. Victor, Elisha David Klonsky | **Year**: 2014 | **Cited by**: 399 | **Kind**: meta_analysis | **Relevance**: suicide: supporting

## Abstract

After suicidal ideation, the strongest predictors of SA history were NSSI frequency, number of N SSI methods, and hopelessness, while Demographic characteristics, such as gender, ethnicity, and age, were weakly associated with SA history.
