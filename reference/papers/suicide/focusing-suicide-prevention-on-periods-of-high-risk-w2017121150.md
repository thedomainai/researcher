# Focusing Suicide Prevention on Periods of High Risk

**Authors**: Mark Olfson, Steven C. Marcus, Jeffrey A. Bridge | **Year**: 2014 | **Cited by**: 105 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: suicide: core

## Abstract

Progress in the prevention of suicide has been limited by the large number, high prevalence, and wide distribution of suicide risk factors and the inherent challenges associated with financing and mounting large-scale, coordinated suicide prevention programs.
