# Health Care Contacts in the Year Before Suicide Death

**Authors**: Brian Kenneth Ahmedani, Gregory E. Simon, Christine C. Stewart, Arne L Beck, Beth E. Waitzfelder, Rebecca C. Rossom, Frances L. Lynch, Ashli A. Owen‐Smith, et al. | **Year**: 2014 | **Cited by**: 682 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: suicide: supporting

## Abstract

This study indicates that opportunities for suicide prevention exist in primary care and medical settings, where most individuals receive services prior to death, and may target improved identification of mental illness and suicidal ideation.
