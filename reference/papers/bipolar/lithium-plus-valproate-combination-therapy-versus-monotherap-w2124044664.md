# Lithium plus valproate combination therapy versus monotherapy for relapse prevention in bipolar I disorder (BALANCE): a randomised open-label trial

**Authors**: ‡ | **Year**: 2010 | **Cited by**: 532 | **Kind**: rct | **Relevance**: bipolar: core

## Abstract

Both combination therapy with lithium plus Valproate and lithium monotherapy are more likely to prevent relapse than is valproate monotherapy, and this benefit seems to be irrespective of baseline severity of illness and is maintained for up to 2 years.
