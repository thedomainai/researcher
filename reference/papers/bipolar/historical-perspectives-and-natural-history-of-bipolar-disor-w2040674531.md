# Historical perspectives and natural history of bipolar disorder

**Authors**: Jules Angst, Robert Sellaro | **Year**: 2000 | **Cited by**: 397 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: bipolar: core

## Abstract

Given the poor outcome of bipolar disorders found in naturalistic follow-up studies and the lifelong investigation, intensive antidepressant, antimanic, and mood-stabilizing treatments are required in most cases.
