# Chronic Restlessness With Antipsychotics

**Authors**: Irene M Bratti, John Michael Kane, Stephen R. Marder | **Year**: 2007 | **Cited by**: 51 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: bipolar: core

## Abstract

A 34-year-old man with a 13-year history of schizophrenia has upper and lower extremity tremor and profound motor restlessness, especially of the legs, which led him to pace the house and neighborhood until the soles of his feet were bleeding.
