# Psychosocial intervention development for the prevention and treatment of depression: promoting innovation and increasing access

## Abstract

The National Institute of Mental Health Psychosocial Intervention Development Workgroup recommends systematic study of the mediating mechanisms that drive the process of change and the moderators that influence their effects in psychosocial interventions.
