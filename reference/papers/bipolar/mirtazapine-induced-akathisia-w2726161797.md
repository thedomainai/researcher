# Mirtazapine‐induced akathisia

**Authors**: Boregowda Girishchandra, Liana Johnson, Rebecca M Cresp, Kenneth Orr | **Year**: 2002 | **Cited by**: 30 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: bipolar: core

## Abstract

It is suggested that mirtazapine’s adrenoceptor action might be the basis for the occurrence of akathisia in patients with chronic depression, and two cases of acute akath isia associated with mirtzapine are reported.
