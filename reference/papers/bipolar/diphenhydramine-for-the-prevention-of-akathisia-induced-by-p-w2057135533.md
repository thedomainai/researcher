# Diphenhydramine for the prevention of akathisia induced by prochlorperazine: A randomized, controlled trial

**Authors**: David R. Vinson, Daniel L Drotts | **Year**: 2001 | **Cited by**: 96 | **Kind**: rct | **Relevance**: bipolar: supporting

## Abstract

Adjuvant diphenhydramine reduces the incidence of akathisia induced by prochlorperazine and is associated with an increase in sedation.
