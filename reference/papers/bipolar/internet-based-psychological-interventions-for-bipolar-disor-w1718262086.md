# Internet-based psychological interventions for bipolar disorder: Review of the present and insights into the future

**Authors**: Diego Hidalgo‐Mazzei, Ainoa Mateu, María Reinares, Aleksandar Matic, Eduard Vieta, Francesc Colom | **Year**: 2015 | **Cited by**: 99 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: bipolar: supporting

## Abstract

Internet-based technologies for bipolar disorder interventions represent a potential highly feasible and acceptable method of delivering this kind of interventions to bipolar patients, considering the high rates of retention and compliance reported.
