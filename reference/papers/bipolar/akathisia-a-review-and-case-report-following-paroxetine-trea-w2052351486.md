# Akathisia: A review and case report following paroxetine treatment

**Authors**: Claudia Frances Baldassano, Christine J Truman, Andrew Alan Nierenberg, S. Nassir Ghaemi, Gary S Sachs | **Year**: 1996 | **Cited by**: 45 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: bipolar: core

## Abstract

A case of akathisia secondary to paroxetine in an 18-year-old female is presented and given the potential untoward effects of this syndrome, early diagnosis is essential.
