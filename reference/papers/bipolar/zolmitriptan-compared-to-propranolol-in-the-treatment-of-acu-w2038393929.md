# Zolmitriptan compared to propranolol in the treatment of acute neuroleptic-induced akathisia: A comparative double-blind study

**Authors**: Ayelet Avital, Ruth Gross‐Isseroff, Rafael Stryjer, Haggai Hermesh, Abraham Weizman, Roni Shiloh | **Year**: 2009 | **Cited by**: 33 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: bipolar: supporting

## Abstract

Zolmitriptan was found to be as effective as propranolol for the treatment of NIA and showed improvement of akathisia (BARS) along the treatment period, with significant effect for time but not for group.
