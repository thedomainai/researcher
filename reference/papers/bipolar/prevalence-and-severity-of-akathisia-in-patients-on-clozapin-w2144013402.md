# Prevalence and severity of akathisia in patients on clozapine

**Authors**: Bruce M. Cohen, Paul E. Keck, Andrew Satlin, Jonathan O Cole | **Year**: 1991 | **Cited by**: 93 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: bipolar: core

## Abstract

Patients receiving clozapine, like patients receiving standard antipsychotic drugs, should be monitored for akathisia, which is observed to be similar in prevalence and severity in patients treated with clozAPine and those receiving standard neuroleptic antipsychotics.
