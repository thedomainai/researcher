# Refining the evaluation of bipolar II: beyond the strict SCID-CV guidelines for hypomania

**Authors**: Franco Benazzi, Hagop Souren Akiskal | **Year**: 2002 | **Cited by**: 282 | **Kind**: guideline | **Relevance**: bipolar: core

## Abstract

Systematic probing for all past Hypomanic symptoms and behaviors, independently of the answer to the screening question on mood, can elicit hypomanic features that would otherwise be discarded by strict adherence to the SCID-CV.
