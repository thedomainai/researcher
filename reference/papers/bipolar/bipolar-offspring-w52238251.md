# Bipolar offspring

**Authors**: Kiki Chang, Hans Steiner, Kimberly Dienes, Nancy E. Adleman, Terence A. Ketter | **Year**: 2003 | **Cited by**: 72 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: bipolar: supporting

## Abstract

The evidence for establishing bipolar offspring as a high-risk cohort, the studies which point to possible prodromal states in bipolar offspring, biological findings inipolar offspring which may be indicators of even higher risk for bipolar disorder, initial attempts at early intervention in prodromic pediatric bipolar disorder are reviewed, and implications for future research are reviewed.
