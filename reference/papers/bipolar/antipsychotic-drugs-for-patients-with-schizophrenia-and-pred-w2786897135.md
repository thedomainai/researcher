# Antipsychotic drugs for patients with schizophrenia and predominant or prominent negative symptoms: a systematic review and meta-analysis

**Authors**: Marc Krause, Yikang Zhu, Maximilian Huhn, Johannes Schneider‐Thoma, Irene Bighelli, Adriani Nikolakopoulou, Stefan Leucht | **Year**: 2018 | **Cited by**: 231 | **Kind**: meta_analysis | **Relevance**: bipolar: supporting

## Abstract

Amisulpride is the only antipsychotic that outperformed placebo in the treatment of predominant negative symptoms, but there was a parallel reduction of depression.
