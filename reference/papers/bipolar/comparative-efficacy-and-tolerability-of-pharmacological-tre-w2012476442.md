# Comparative efficacy and tolerability of pharmacological treatments in the maintenance treatment of bipolar disorder: a systematic review and network meta-analysis

**Authors**: Tomofumi Miura, Hisashi Noma, Toshi A. Furukawa, Hiroshi Mitsuyasu, Shiro Tanaka, Sarah Stockton, Georgia Salanti, Keisuke Motomura, et al. | **Year**: 2014 | **Cited by**: 376 | **Kind**: meta_analysis | **Relevance**: bipolar: core

## Abstract

Lithium should remain the first-line treatment when prescribing a relapse-prevention drug in patients with bipolar disorder, notwithstanding its tolerability profile, according to the better quality of the supporting evidence.
