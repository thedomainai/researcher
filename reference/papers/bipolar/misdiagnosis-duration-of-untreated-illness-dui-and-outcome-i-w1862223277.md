# Misdiagnosis, duration of untreated illness (DUI) and outcome in bipolar patients with psychotic symptoms: A naturalistic study

**Authors**: A. Carlo Altamura, Massimiliano Buoli, Alice Caldiroli, Lea Caron, Claudia Cumerlato Melter, Cristina Dobrea, Michela Cigliobianco, Francesco Zanelli Quarantini | **Year**: 2015 | **Cited by**: 168 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: bipolar: supporting

## Abstract

DUP (but not DUP) is a predictor of outcome in bipolar patients with psychotic symptoms, indicating that an early diagnosis and proper treatment with a mood stabilizer (or an atypical antipsychotic with mood stabilizing effects) may improve long-term outcome of these patients.
