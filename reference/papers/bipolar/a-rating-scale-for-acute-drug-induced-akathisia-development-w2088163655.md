# A rating scale for acute drug-induced akathisia: development, reliability, and validity

**Authors**: Perminder Singh Sachdev | **Year**: 1994 | **Cited by**: 71 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: bipolar: core

## Abstract

This paper describes the process of developing a new rating scale for acute neuroleptic-induced akathisia, and a preliminary examination of its construct validity was performed by calculating the correlations with the ratings on the analogue and global scales.
