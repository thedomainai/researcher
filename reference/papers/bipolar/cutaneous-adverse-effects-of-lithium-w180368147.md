# Cutaneous Adverse Effects of Lithium

**Authors**: Chi Keung Yeung, Henry H. Chan | **Year**: 2004 | **Cited by**: 89 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: bipolar: supporting

## Abstract

Patients treated with lithium developed more cutaneous reactions, particularly acne and psoriasis, than patients receiving other psychotropics, with a prevalence in lithium-treated patients as high as 45%.
