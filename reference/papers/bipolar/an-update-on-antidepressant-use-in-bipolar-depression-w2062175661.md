# An Update on Antidepressant Use in Bipolar Depression

**Authors**: Michelle M. Sidor, GLENDA M. MacQUEEN | **Year**: 2012 | **Cited by**: 57 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: bipolar: core

## Abstract

Future studies should explore the patient characteristics and response patterns that predict a more favorable response profile to antidepressants amongst patients with bipolar disorder so that the medications can be rationally used in those who are most likely to benefit.
