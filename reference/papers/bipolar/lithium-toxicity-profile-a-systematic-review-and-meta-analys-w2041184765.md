# Lithium toxicity profile: a systematic review and meta-analysis

**Authors**: Rebecca F. McKnight, M. Adida, Katie Budge, Sarah Stockton, Guy M. Goodwin, John Geddes | **Year**: 2012 | **Cited by**: 884 | **Kind**: meta_analysis | **Relevance**: bipolar: core

## Abstract

There is little evidence for a clinically significant reduction in renal function in most patients, and the risk of end-stage renal failure is low, but the balance of risks should be considered before lithium is withdrawn during pregnancy.
