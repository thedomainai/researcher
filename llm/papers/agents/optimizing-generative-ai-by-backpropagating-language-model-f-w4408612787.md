# Optimizing generative AI by backpropagating language model feedback

**Authors**: Mert Yüksekgönül, Federico Bianchi, Joseph Boen, Sheng Liu, Pan Lu, Zhi Huang, Carlos Guestrin, James Zou | **Year**: 2025 | **Cited by**: 78 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: agents: core

## Abstract

Generative artificial intelligence systems can be optimized using TextGrad, a framework that performs optimization by backpropagating large-language-model-generated feedback to improve AI systems, which enables optimization across diverse tasks, including radiotherapy treatment plans and molecule generation.
