# Accelerating long-context inference of large language models via dynamic attention load balancing

**Authors**: Jie Ou, Jinyu Guo, Shuaihong Jiang, Xu Li, Ruini Xue, Wenhong Tian, Rajkumar Buyya | **Year**: 2025 | **Cited by**: 2 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: foundations: core

## Abstract

Large language models (LLMs) have demonstrated exceptional performance across various natural language processing tasks. However, their quadratic complexity of attention mechanisms results in ineﬃciency during long-context inference. Although existing research has employed sparse attention techniques to enhance LLM eﬃciency in long-context scenarios, we observe that these methods introduce heterogeneous attention computation patterns across diﬀerent heads, leading to GPU load imbalance and resource idling during practical deployments.
