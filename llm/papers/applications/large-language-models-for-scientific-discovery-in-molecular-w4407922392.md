# Large language models for scientific discovery in molecular property prediction

**Authors**: Yizhen Zheng, Huan Yee Koh, Jiaxin Ju, Anh T. N. Nguyen, Lauren Therese May, Geoffrey I. Webb, Shirui Pan | **Year**: 2025 | **Cited by**: 81 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: applications: core

## Abstract

LLM4SD, a framework designed to harness LLMs for driving scientific discovery in molecular property prediction by synthesizing knowledge from literature and inferring knowledge from scientific data, is introduced.
