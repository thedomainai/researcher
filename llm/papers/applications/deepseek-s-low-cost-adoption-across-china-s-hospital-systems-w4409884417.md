# DeepSeek’s “Low-Cost” Adoption Across China’s Hospital Systems

**Authors**: Dian Zeng, Yiming Qin, Bin Sheng, Tien Yin Wong | **Year**: 2025 | **Cited by**: 69 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: applications: supporting

## Abstract

This Perspective examines the rapid adoption of DeepSeek, a large language model, across Chinese hospitals and the regulatory and governance concerns surrounding the use of artificial intelligence–enabled models in health care.
