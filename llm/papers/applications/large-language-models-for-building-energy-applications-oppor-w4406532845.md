# Large language models for building energy applications: Opportunities and challenges

**Authors**: Mingzhe Liu, Liang Zhang, Jianli Chen, Wei-An Chen, Zhiyao Yang, L. James Lo, Jin Wen, Zheng D. O’Neill | **Year**: 2025 | **Cited by**: 101 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: applications: supporting

## Abstract

A development roadmap built on in-context learning, domain-specific fine-tuning, retrieval augmented generation, and multimodal integration is proposed to enhance LLMs’ customization and practical use in this field.
