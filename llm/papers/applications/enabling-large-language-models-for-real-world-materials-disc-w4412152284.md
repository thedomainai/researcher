# Enabling large language models for real-world materials discovery

**Authors**: Santiago Miret, N. M. Anoop Krishnan | **Year**: 2025 | **Cited by**: 39 | **Kind**: article | **Relevance**: applications: core

## Abstract

This Perspective shows relevant failure cases of LLMs in materials science that reveal the current limitations of LLMs related to comprehending and reasoning over complex, interconnected materials science knowledge, and outlines a framework for developing materials science LLMs (MatSci-LLMs) that are grounded in domain knowledge.
